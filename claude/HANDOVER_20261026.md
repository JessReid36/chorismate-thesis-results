## 3. How jobs are run on hpc1

### Environment every PBS script needs

```bash
export PATH="/apps/openmpi/4.1.1/bin:$PATH"

export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1
```

ORCA must be called by **absolute path** (`"$ORCA/orca" job.inp > job.out`) for its parallel launcher to work.

### Checking state — only one thing counts as evidence

```
qstat -u 18660916
```

**Plain and unfiltered. Nothing else is evidence of job state** — not `qstat -x`, not a folder listing, not an
inference from elapsed time. `S` column: `R` running, `Q` queued, `H` held on a dependency, `F`/absent finished.
For a finished job's exit status, `qstat -xf <id>` works but is history, not state.

### The known failure mode

**comp036 kills jobs instantly.** Exit status −2, `00:00:00` walltime, **no output file at all**. Seven of
sixteen submissions were lost to it in one session, with the failing inputs byte-identical to their working
twins. If a job vanishes with no `job.out`, check `exec_host` before suspecting the input. Resubmit, or pin:

```
qsub -l select=1:ncpus=8:mem=24gb:host=comp043 job.pbs
```

### Patterns used here

**Restartable single-job batches** (Stage 6: `field.pbs`, `qscan.pbs`). One PBS job runs many single points in
sequence, each guarded by `grep -q "ORCA TERMINATED NORMALLY" job.out` so a resubmission skips what is done. A
`mkdir .running` lock with a `trap ... EXIT` rmdir prevents two jobs colliding. **If a job dies without
clearing `.running`, the result bundle refuses to run** — remove it manually with
`ssh 18660916@hpc rmdir <dir>/.running` only after a plain `qstat` shows nothing queued.

**Dependency chains** (Phase 1 SP package). One `submit.sh` per frame folder, run **once**, on hpc1:

```
bash /home/18660916/system_development/05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers/f<frame>_r8_a1/submit.sh
```

It uses PBS `-W depend=afterok:<id>`, so a job starts only if its predecessor exited 0 — every internal check
passing is a precondition for the next stage. It is guarded by three sentinels in the folder: `SUBMITTED` (holds
the job ids; refuses to re-run), `SUBMITTED.partial` (an earlier submission stopped part-way — **tell Jessica,
do not re-run**), and `DO_NOT_SUBMIT`. It also refuses if any `job_*.pbs.out` already exists.

### Taking care before aborting

Do not abort HPC runs. Stop one only when it is clearly not working, and say why first.

### After every finished step

Download the outputs and **commit them as physical files before anything is measured from them** — a sha256
manifest of every file, verified against hpc1 before the copy is trusted, committed in its own commit, and only
then analysed. Summaries are not a substitute for the files.

## 5. Phase 2.2 Stage 6 — the finding, stated once

A positive point charge with no Pauli wall for electrons will capture electron density when the well it creates
is deep enough. **Finding 4** established the missing wall: the Lennard-Jones term never enters the QM
Hamiltonian, so our sites stop nuclei and nothing stops electrons. Marefat Khah et al., JCTC 2020,
doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b01162 report exactly this as "unphysical leaking of the electron density", "in
particular, when diffuse basis functions are required", and state that smearing does not fix it because "these
methods do not capture the effects of the Pauli exchange repulsion on the electronic wavefunction".

The threshold is **basis-dependent**, which is the whole explanation of why the representation passed its own
tests and then failed (`DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt`):

```
   basis         collapsed single points   number of charges in them
   def2-SVP                54              14 to 76, median 64
   ma-def2-SVP              2              20
   r2SCAN-3c                7              42 to 76, median 48
   def2-SVPD               11              1 to 20, median 1
   B97-3c                   0              none
```

Every test that validated the representation used **one to three** charges and was CLEAN — the 108 additivity
runs, the 16 A-matrix finite-difference runs, the q-scan below 0.65 e. Every design was evaluated at **five to
twenty**, and the earlier shell-ladder work at 42 to 88. **The representation was validated in the regime where
it works and used in the regime where it fails.** It is not a diffuse-basis artefact — r2SCAN-3c collapses in
the same charge range as def2-SVP.

The breakdown was recorded in the original def2-SVP frozen outputs from the first design run, in the SCF cycle
count (12→15→15→16→12→36→42 for 0→1→2→3→5→10→20 charges) and the gap (3.40→3.19→2.29→2.13→1.76→0.85→0.61 eV).
Diagnostics 1–5 read energies only.

## 9. Methodology — the literature rules

- **A claim from a paper must come from the full paper in `chorismate-refs/`.** Not an abstract, not a search result, not memory. If a paper is missing, give its checked DOI and **wait** until it is committed before drawing any conclusion from it.
- **One paper is not a standard.** A practice cited in a single paper does not count as standard practice — **three or more independent sources** are needed.
- **Use published methods and what has already worked here.** Re-read the repos before proposing a fix.
- **Never shape a calculation or a theory to get a lower barrier.** Report what the outputs say.
- **Pre-registrations and criteria are never edited.** Changes go into a new dated amendment or erratum. This applies to `STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt`, `STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt`, `PHASE22_PLAN.txt`, `SP_PLAN.txt`, `C4_CRITERIA.txt` and every `*_AMENDMENT*`/`*_ERRATUM*`.
- **A result's own criteria are fixed before the runs go in** and applied unchanged afterwards, even when the answer is unwelcome. Where a threshold is a near miss, say so (λ_quad: 10.3% against a 10.0% threshold, three tenths of a point, and it moves the implied bound by a factor of two).
- Commit messages end with a status tag — `[retained-final]`, `[exploratory]`, `[archived]` — and the session's attribution lines.
- **A fabricated or unverifiable quotation is the worst failure available here.** It has happened once (§10).
