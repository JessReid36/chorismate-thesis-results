## 3. The finding, stated once

A positive point charge with no Pauli wall for electrons will capture electron density when the well it creates
is deep enough. Finding 4 established the missing wall: the Lennard-Jones term never enters the QM Hamiltonian,
so our sites stop nuclei and nothing stops electrons. Marefat Khah et al., JCTC 2020,
doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b01162 report exactly this as "unphysical leaking of the electron density", "in
particular, when diffuse basis functions are required", and state that smearing does not fix it because "these
methods do not capture the effects of the Pauli exchange repulsion on the electronic wavefunction".

The threshold is **basis-dependent**, which is the whole explanation of why the representation passed its own
tests and then failed:

```
   basis         collapsed single points   number of charges in them
   def2-SVP                54              14 to 76, median 64
   ma-def2-SVP              2              20
   r2SCAN-3c                7              42 to 76, median 48
   def2-SVPD               11              1 to 20, median 1
   B97-3c                   0              none
```

Every test that validated the representation used **one to three** charges and was CLEAN — the 108 additivity
runs, the 16 A-matrix finite-difference runs, the 93-point q-scan below 0.65 e. Every design was evaluated at
**five to twenty**, and the earlier shell-ladder work at 42 to 88. The representation was validated in the
regime where it works and used in the regime where it fails.

It is not a diffuse-basis artefact. r2SCAN-3c collapses in the same charge range as def2-SVP.

## 7. Honesty record — read this before trusting anything above

These are errors made *in producing the work above*. They are here because a summary would lose them.

- **A library subagent returned a fabricated quote** attributed to Ranaghan 2004 about diffuse functions. The word "diffuse" does not appear in that paper, nor anywhere in `chorismate-refs/`. It was removed and recorded in `STAGE6_ASSESSMENT_20261022.md`. **Treat any quotation not read from the full paper as unverified.**
- **Diagnostic 1 was misdiagnosed twice** — first as the reference being broken, then as the known `b·q²` term. Both killed by arithmetic. Recorded in the assessment rather than dropped.
- **An append in a negative test wrote through `cp -al` hardlinks** into a mirror clone and modified `STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt`. Restored and re-verified. Rehearsals now use `git clone --local` against bare origins.
- **Five of eight diffuse jobs produced nothing**; the first hypothesis (the plain-QM control being the odd one out) was wrong — all failures were comp036, exit −2, zero walltime, byte-identical inputs.
- **D6's thresholds are post-hoc.** They were chosen after the Stage 6 outputs had been inspected. D6.0 discloses this first. Mitigation: anchored to in-corpus controls, distributions printed, specificity check printed (0 of 352 controls flagged).
- **Four further corrections** are in `STAGE6_ERRATUM3_20261026.txt` E3.5: the single-charge collapse was at def2-SVPD not def2-SVP; def2-SVPD collapses from 1 to 20 charges not only at 1; the total-field reading of D4 was not supported; a proposed electrostatic predictor was tested and failed; and D6's own first classifier called 616 of the 832 def2-SVP runs then in the corpus collapsed, including healthy polarised ones.
- **`cs6_field.py` carries a grammar defect** in its "does not survive" branch ("so the frozen half of Stage 6 to be rebuilt"). Not fixed: it is the committed generator of the D4 inputs and the result bundle checks it byte-for-byte. Fix in a dated erratum if that branch is ever quoted.

## 8. How work is done here

Bundles. Each is a tar.gz with a `MANIFEST.sha256`, a script that refuses to run unless both repos are clean,
level and carry the expected prerequisites, commits code **before** running it, generates into a scratch
directory so every check passes before any results commit, and ends by printing the explicit `git push` lines.
Three passes of checking before anything is handed over: a full clean run, a set of negative tests that each
stop fires, and a final clean run with the mirrors verified untouched. The bundles so far: `x97y` (D4 inputs),
`x97z` (D4 result), `x98` (D5), `x99` (D6).

`qstat -u 18660916`, plain and unfiltered, is the only evidence of job state. For every finished HPC step the
outputs are committed as physical files with a sha256 manifest, before anything is measured from them.
