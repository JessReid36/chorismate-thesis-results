Stationary-point barriers (SP) - AMENDMENT 3, ERRATUM 1. Written 21 October 2026, the same day as SP_AMENDMENT3.txt
and after it was applied. SP_AMENDMENT3.txt stays as committed (results 77f5d23); this file corrects one factual
sentence in it and changes no decision, no rule, no number and no result.

THE ERROR

SP_AMENDMENT3.txt A3.3 reads:

   "That covers a folder with a job still running - at 11:12 SAST on 21 October, frame 20000 (job_ts 419762, job_r0
   419768) and frame 21634 (job_ts 419770) - and a folder in which any job has begun to write."

A3.1 carries the same mistake, calling 419770 "frame 21634's job_ts".

419770 is not frame 21634. Its PBS job name is sa200008tsc, which qstat abbreviates to "sa200008t*": frame 20000,
radius 8, job_ts, with the trailing "c" that spa_prepare.py adds for a surroundings-check run (S = ci). It belongs to
f20000_r8_ci_a1. Frame 21634's job_ts was 419778 and was queued, not running.

The reading came from the abbreviated job name in the qstat output of 11:12 SAST; the "c" was in the truncated part.
The first wave's run folders were submitted in alphabetical order (f20000_r8_a1, f20000_r8_ci_a1, f21634_r8_a1, ...),
not with the three surroundings-check folders last, so the second block of eight ids is frame 20000's ci folder.

WHAT IS TRUE

The two folders deferred when the amendment was applied are f20000_r8_a1 and f20000_r8_ci_a1, both of frame 20000:

   f20000_r8_a1      job_ts 419762 (running), job_r0 419768 (running), its other six held
   f20000_r8_ci_a1   job_ts 419770 (running), its other seven held or queued

This is what package_waves/x94/QSTAT_BEFORE.txt and X94_RECORD.txt record, both committed with the applied
amendment (results e23c85f): those files were written from what PBS said at the time and are correct. Only the two
sentences quoted above are wrong.

WHY IT CHANGES NOTHING

A3.3's rule is stated on the state of a folder, not on the identity of any frame: "a run folder is handled only if it
is untouched: every one of its eight jobs is in state Q or H, and the folder holds no job output and no step record",
and the state "is read from PBS when the change is applied, not from the state recorded above". The bundle read the
state itself and deferred the two folders that were not untouched. The named ids in A3.1 and A3.3 are illustrative.
Frame 21634 was handled as an untouched folder, which was correct: all eight of its jobs were queued.

A note worth keeping with this correction, which the wrong reading obscured: frame 20000 is the alignment reference
for Stage 5 ("the lowest-numbered complete frame (20000, as D3, when it is complete)", STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt), and the
other deferred folder is that same frame's surroundings check (A2.4). So both deferred folders belong to the one
frame Stage 5 aligns on, and neither has been resubmitted yet. They are resubmitted by the bundle that follows, once
their running jobs have ended, under SP_AMENDMENT3.txt A3.3 unchanged.

DIRECTION

None. No decision, rule, threshold, input, calculation or reported quantity differs.
