A2.1 THE REACTANT: B_sp AND B_eff (DECISIONS Q1)
B_sp = E(TS) - E(R_IRC) stays the primary barrier (SP_PLAN.txt QUANTITIES). Added for every complete frame and for the
ensemble: the effective barrier B_eff = E(TS) - min(E(R_IRC), E(R0)). Where R0 lies below R_IRC, B_sp is reported as a
lower bound of the barrier from the lowest reactant found. R0 is the package's only second reactant search, so B_eff
captures only what R0 finds.
Added here:
- R0 enters only when it passed P7 (with A1.2's checks) and P8 shows it a minimum (no counted imaginary mode), the test
  R_IRC must also pass; otherwise B_eff = B_sp and the frame is listed. Direction: this can only lower B_eff, towards
  B_sp.
- Frame 42436's B_eff in the ensemble uses its package R0 (f42436_r8_a1/r0), so it equals its B_sp, 14.03. Smoke test
  1's R0 (16.88 from it, above; DECISIONS Q1 names that value) comes from SP_PLAN.txt's protocol, not Amendment 1's, so
  it is reported beside the ensemble, not in it. Direction: compared with entering 16.88, the B_eff mean is lower by
  2.85/n kcal/mol (about 0.1 at n = 30).
- The name: Li & Hartke 2013 call the lower barrier, measured from the reactant connected to the TS, "effective" (it is
  "lowered by this amount" when the cluster rearranges along the path). Here B_eff is the higher one, measured from the
  lowest reactant found; the name follows DECISIONS, and the definition above is what counts.
Why: B_sp was fixed before any result and measures from the reactant on the TS's own path, as the chorismate-mutase
studies do (Claeyssens et al. 2005 and 2011; Senn et al. 2009; Ranaghan et al. 2004). Where a distinct lower reactant
is known, the literature puts its cost into the barrier (Li & Hartke 2013; Claeyssens et al. 2011, p. 1583; Kastner et
al. 2006, p. 457), which B_eff reports.
Direction: B_eff >= B_sp in every frame.

A2.2 ATTRITION AND RE-RUNS (B2; DECISIONS Q3)
The 30 frames are those of 05_qmmm/23_c4_reactant/c4_bands.tsv, fixed in advance; none is replaced in the ensemble
(A2.4's replacement concerns only the surroundings check). A frame is complete as SP_PLAN.txt QUANTITIES defines it (TS,
TS frequencies, IRC, R_IRC and R_IRC frequencies passed). A frame that is not complete is reported with the step that
failed, its reason and the values it reached: the failing step's record and, for an alternation, its cycle history
(spa_package.py), and spa_report.py's full report of every run folder, committed beside the package's output.
Re-runs:
- A machinery stop (SP_AMENDMENT1.txt A1.5) in a step of the B_sp chain is handled as A1.5 states: the run folder is
  renamed with _stop1, generated again and resubmitted once; a second machinery stop is a failure.
- Added here: a machinery stop in a step outside the chain (P_IRC, R0 or their frequencies) re-runs only that job and
  the job that depends on it, once, after the stopped step's folder and its job's output are renamed with _stop1 (the
  dependent job never ran, PBS afterok, so its folder is only replaced); a second machinery stop fails that step. The
  step folders and job scripts are taken from a fresh generation of the run folder into a scratch folder, checked byte
  for byte against the committed inputs' sha256 (GENERATOR_LOG.txt); R0 starts from the same r0/start.xyz (checked
  against starts.sha256), P_IRC from the same IRC end (spa_stage.py prep, as in the first run). Re-running the whole
  folder would replace a complete B_sp, and the chain's steps and records stay as they are.
- An input error is not re-run as a machinery stop: A1.5 has the input corrected in a disclosed amendment.
- Added here: a job that has left the queue without its last line and without a record for its step is a machinery
  stop. The bundle that collects the outputs checks qstat and records such jobs in the run folder's LEFT_QUEUE.txt,
  which spa_package.py reads; until then the job is "not finished". When only that job is re-run, its line moves from
  LEFT_QUEUE.txt into the renamed step folder.
The ensemble needs at least 20 complete frames (SP_PLAN.txt PACKAGE); with fewer it is reported but not used as the
Phase 1 barrier. C4's "more than 3 of the 30 frames dropped" rule belonged to C4's step 3 and is retired with it
(C4_CLOSURE.txt); this rule replaces it for Phase 1. Twenty is a floor (Grossfield & Zuckerman 2009: fewer than about
20 statistically independent configurations "should be considered unreliable"), not a guarantee of precision; the
confidence interval (A2.3) shows the precision reached. The frames lie at least 650 ps apart, against 485 ps (5 tau of
the slowest observable, DECISION_SAMPLING.md).
Selectivity check (DECISIONS Q3 names it; its test and threshold are added here): whenever at least 2 frames have
failed, the band barriers barrier_klow (c4_bands.tsv) of the failed frames against those of the complete frames, as the
difference of means with its 95% confidence interval (Welch); with one failed frame, the rank of its band barrier among
the complete frames'. If the interval excludes zero, the report states that failure is related to the band barrier and
the direction in which the complete frames' mean may then be biased (higher band barriers among the failed frames:
biased low). It changes no number.
Direction: none; the check detects non-random failure.

A2.3 STATISTICS AND THE PHASE 1 BARRIER (B3, audit item C3; DECISIONS Q4; replaces SP_PLAN.txt PACKAGE's "Ensemble"
line and its "decided separately, after the package", and audit item C3's mean +- sem and C4 step 4's statistics, for
the SP barrier)
Primary, over the complete frames with S = md: the arithmetic mean of B_sp with the sd, the sem, the 95% confidence
interval of the mean (t-distribution, n - 1 degrees of freedom) and n; every frame's value tabulated; the median and the
range. It is compared, as the chorismate-mutase studies compare theirs, with the experimental activation enthalpy, 12.7
+- 0.4 kcal/mol, and with Claeyssens et al. 2011's enzyme barrier, 11.3 +- 1.8 kcal/mol (the barrier of the average of
their 16 potential-energy profiles, with the sd of the 16 pathways; B3LYP/6-31G(d)/CHARMM27). The same statistics are
given for B_eff.
Comparisons between ensembles (the enzyme against water, or against a design): the difference of means with its 95%
confidence interval (Welch-Satterthwaite).
Secondary, as estimates of the free-energy barrier, compared with the experimental activation free energy, 15.4
kcal/mol, at 298.15 K (SP_PLAN.txt): the exponential average -RT ln(mean exp(-B/RT)) (SP_PLAN.txt; Cooper & Kastner
2014, eq. 4) and the cumulant estimate mean - sd^2/(2RT) (Ryde 2017, eq. 5). The sd in kJ/mol is stated against Ryde
2017's Table 2 (the number of energies needed for 10 and 20 kJ/mol at 95% confidence) and against n; the table is for
300 K, so (added here) the sd is scaled by 300/298.15 for the look-up, and the row at or above it is used (the tabulated
numbers rise with sigma), the row below shown beside it. The cumulant estimate assumes a Gaussian distribution, and Ryde
2017 asks for evidence of it ("it becomes more important to provide evidence that the distribution actually is
Gaussian"): the histogram and normal plot (as Ryde 2017's SI Figure S1, here in text and in the table) and, added here,
the Shapiro-Wilk test, the skewness and the excess kurtosis. p < 0.05 is reported as evidence against a normal
distribution, and so against the cumulant estimate.
The experimental values are Kast, Asif-Ullah & Hilvert 1996's, as Ranaghan et al. 2004 and Claeyssens et al. 2011 quote
them.
The Phase 1 barrier (audit item C3; added here, beyond DECISIONS, before any package value exists, in place of SP_PLAN's
"decided separately, after the package"): when the package's conditions are met (at least 20 complete frames, A2.2; the
surroundings check passed, A2.4; every record agrees with its output; nothing undecided), the Phase 1 barrier is the
B_sp ensemble mean with its 95% confidence interval and n, B_eff given beside it. Frame 820's +16.00 kcal/mol
(BARRIER_FINAL.md) is named as the earlier single-frame characterisation and is not averaged in. When the conditions are
not met, the package gives no Phase 1 barrier and C3 stays open: after a failed surroundings check, A2.4's remedies
follow; with fewer than 20 complete frames, whatever is done next is a new decision, written in an amendment before its
runs and disclosed as made after the package's results.
Direction: the mean is the highest of the three estimators; as an estimate of the free-energy barrier it is biased high
(Cooper & Kastner 2014; Ryde 2017), and this is stated with it. C4 step 4 used Ryde's 300 K; at 298.15 K the cumulant
estimate is about 0.03 kcal/mol lower at an sd of 2.4 kcal/mol (not material; stated). Settling C3 now moves nothing.

A2.4 THE SURROUNDINGS CHECK (B4; DECISIONS Q5; replaces "and both sets are carried forward" in SP_PLAN.txt PACKAGE)
The check is SP_PLAN.txt's: frames 20000, 44388 and 59999 again with S = ci at 8 A. Per frame the difference
B_sp(ci) - B_sp(md) is reported; a frame exceeds when |B_sp(ci) - B_sp(md)| > 2 kcal/mol, and the check fails if at
least two of the three exceed (B_eff(ci) - B_eff(md) reported only).
Added here (DECISIONS Q5 did not cover it): if a check frame's md or ci run fails (finally, as A2.2 defines it), the
check uses instead the nearest frame later in frame-number order (earlier, for 59999) whose md run is complete and which
is neither a check frame nor already a replacement, assigned in the order 20000, 44388, 59999; a nearer frame whose md
run is not yet decided is waited for, and a frame whose md run failed is passed over. The replacement is run once with
S = ci (f<FRAME>_r8_ci_a1; its inputs generated and committed like the package's before it runs), and it is run even if
the verdict is already reached, so that every place reports a difference. If it fails too, or no frame is left, that
place has no difference. If neither outcome can then be reached, the check cannot be decided, and the ensemble is
reported but not used as the Phase 1 barrier until a further amendment, written before any further result, settles it.
Direction: none; the replacement depends on which runs completed, not on any barrier value.
If the check fails, the B_sp ensemble is reported but not used as the Phase 1 barrier, and a further amendment, written
before its runs, applies these remedies in this order, each re-checked on the same three frames:
  (a) a larger relaxed region, towards the chorismate-mutase studies' 16-20 A (Ranaghan et al. 2004: atoms beyond 16 A
      fixed; Claeyssens et al. 2005 and 2011: a 25 A sphere, the outer 5 A fixed; Senn et al. 2009: protein within 17 A
      free). The check varies only atoms beyond 8 A, so this is the direct remedy;
  (b) if the dependence persists, a larger QM region (Ryde 2016, section 4, item 7: "the QM system needs to be extended
      until the energies are stable"), Arg90 first;
  (c) free energies (Rod & Ryde 2005; Ryde 2016), outside the thesis's present scope.
Putting (a) before (b) is DECISIONS' reading; Ryde 2016 prescribes (b). Each element is from the literature.
Direction: (a) unknown. (b) is expected to lower the barrier, stated now: in the library a larger QM region lowered it
each time - Lee et al. 2002 as Ranaghan et al. 2004 report it (chorismate mutase, RHF/4-31G: 19.7 -> 16.6 kcal/mol
with Glu78 and Arg90 in the QM region); Kulik et al. 2016 (catechol O-methyltransferase: activation energies "decrease
nearly monotonically from 24 kcal/mol ... to an asymptotic limit of 16 kcal/mol"); Li & Chan 2025 (chorismate mutase:
rate constants 5-27 times higher with Arg7 and Glu78 added to a QM region that already held Arg90; rates, not
barriers). A check that passes changes nothing.

A2.5 THE DESIGNS' BARRIER (B5; DECISIONS Q6)
Every barrier compared with the enzyme's, the bare substrate's (the uncatalysed reference) and every design's, is
computed by the same chain with the enzyme's settings (substrate-only QM region, B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX,
TightSCF, the same embedding): a TS optimised in that system with one counted imaginary mode (P4's test); an IRC in both
directions whose ends are the reactant and the product of the same reaction (P5's test); the reactant and product
minimised from the IRC ends; B = E(TS) - E(R_IRC); and B_eff from the lowest reactant found by a stated conformer search
that includes the pseudo-diequatorial substrate conformer, reported beside B as in A2.1. Quasi-atoms are held fixed. The
catalytic effect is the barrier difference (ddE) against the reference in the same medium; a flexible medium (explicit
water) is averaged over configurations with A2.3's statistics. An NEB-CI may supply the TS start; frozen-field scores
stay screening numbers. The medium is decided in Phase 2.2; the definition holds either way.
Added here: in a medium of explicit molecules, "the same chain" includes the enzyme's region and surroundings - whole
residues and waters within 8 A of the substrate relaxed by A1.1's alternation with A1.3's test, everything beyond held
at that configuration's MD structure - so that design and enzyme barriers are computed alike. Every water within 8 A
moves. 23_c4_reactant/pilot_a2/C4_WAY_FORWARD_20261011.md section 3.5 ("Freezing is a sensitivity test, never the
production protocol for designs", after Dubey et al. 2020 and Rod & Ryde 2005) is superseded for the molecules beyond 8
A, for that reason; for the enzyme, A2.4's check measures the effect of that boundary. Whether and how a design's frozen
boundary is checked (a design may have no band, and so no climbing image to freeze around) is fixed in Phase 2.2's plan
before its runs. In a medium without explicit molecules (gas phase, continuum) every atom but the quasi-atoms is
optimised.
This replaces, for every barrier compared with the enzyme's, the "relaxed NEB-CI barriers" of C4_CRITERIA.txt (WHAT THIS
DOES TO THE DESIGN YARDSTICK).
Direction: B is like for like by construction. B_eff is not quite: the enzyme's second reactant is R0 (from the band's
image 0), a design's comes from the conformer search, and this is stated with the results. B_eff stops a design from
looking better because its field favours a non-reactive conformer.

A2.6 THE CYCLE LIMIT (DECISIONS Q7; changes SP_AMENDMENT1.txt A1.1's "K = 1, 2, ..., 8" and A1.3's "Not converged
after cycle 8")
The limit rises from 8 to 20 cycles; A1.3's convergence test (a)-(c) is unchanged. In the code: spa_prepare.py (the job
scripts' cycle loop and messages), spa_stage.py (KMAX = 20: alt_prep's bounds, alt_decide's limit and its message "the
alternation did not converge in 20 cycles") and spa_report.py (KMAX = 20, so that it reads 20 cycles; its smoke verdict
keeps Amendment 1's text, which decided the region, and its docstring says so). Nothing else in them changes: the
difference from the committed A1.8 versions is these lines and their docstrings. Frame 42436's package runs (smoke test
2) ran under the 8-cycle limit and converged within 7 cycles, so the change does not touch them.
Why: no paper sets a cycle cap; the schemes iterate until their criteria are met (Zhang, Liu & Yang 2000; Kastner et al.
2006 and 2007; Murphy et al. 2000; Senn & Thiel 2009). Zhang et al.'s "no more than eight cycles" is an observation on
one reactant minimisation, not a limit. A cap is a resource guard, like MaxIter. In smoke test 2's 8 A TS the core
energy change shrank by factors of 0.45-0.79 per cycle and the substrate shift by 0.67-0.86. Assuming a change of 1.5
kcal/mol at cycle 2 (the largest seen was 1.14, 6 A TS; 0.93 at 8 A), 20 cycles reach A1.3's 0.10 kcal/mol for factors
up to about 0.86. Later cycles took up to 3.4 h (R_IRC at 8 A, cycle 3), so 20 cycles take about 68 h. The walltime
(176 h) can bind before the cycle limit: a 5000-step first environment step takes about 73 h at the median rate of 53 s
per step and about 96 h at the pilot's 69 s, the whole job then about 140-160 h, and at the slowest rate seen (145 s,
one environment step) a 5000-step first step alone would exceed the walltime. A1.3 counts reaching the walltime as a
failure. Smoke test 2's first environment steps took at most 493 optimiser steps. The number 20 is this amendment's;
the literature supplies its role, not its value.
Reported: every stationary point's cycle count, and which needed more than 8 (spa_package.py).
Direction: the limit decides which frames finish, not their values (A2.2's check covers that). A2.11 adds the
limitation.

A2.9 THE PACKAGE SCRIPT (B7)
spa_package.py, with spa_package_stats.py (Python 3.6, standard library; it reads only, apart from an optional new TSV
file), computes from the run folders and c4_bands.tsv everything A2.1-A2.6 report:
- per frame: its state (complete; failed, with step, reason and values reached; a machinery stop not yet re-run, with
  A2.2's instruction; an input error; not finished), B_sp, B_eff, dR0, E(TS) - E(R0), dE_rxn, the TS imaginary mode,
  the cycle counts, the last cycle's core change, the state of P_IRC and R0 and their frequencies, and the band barriers
  (descriptive);
- attrition and the selectivity check; the ensemble statistics of B_sp and B_eff; the cycle counts; the surroundings
  check with A2.4's replacement and its verdict; frame 42436's smoke-test-1 R0;
- the Phase 1 barrier when A2.3's conditions are met, or why not.
It first runs spa_report.py's report on every run folder and prints as PROBLEM every record that differs from its ORCA
output; such an output is PROVISIONAL, and its numbers are not used until the record or the transfer is put right and
the script run again. While anything is undecided (a job not finished, a machinery stop not yet re-run, a surroundings
place without its difference yet, a PROBLEM) it marks its output PROVISIONAL. It is run once every package job has ended
and again after every re-run; each output is committed whatever it shows, and the result is the first output with
nothing undecided:
  python3 spa_package.py SD 05_qmmm/23_c4_reactant/c4_bands.tsv SP_PACKAGE_TABLE.tsv
Checks before this commit: the statistics agree with scipy 1.18.1 to 1e-9 on 53 reference values embedded in
spa_package_stats.py (python3 spa_package.py selftest), and to 1e-10 or better over 3000 random samples of 3-40 values
(the independent reviewer: 4000 samples, 3.2e-12). Ryde 2017's Table 2 is embedded as printed (PDF page 15 of the
library's manuscript) and was checked entry by entry against the PDF's text. The script was run on frame 42436 rebuilt
from the committed archives (B_sp 14.03, B_eff 14.03, dR0 +1.85; the smoke-test-1 line 2.85 and 16.88) and on
synthetic packages built from it: all frames complete; failures of every kind; machinery stops in and outside the chain,
with and without _stop1; an input error; a job not finished; surroundings checks that pass, fail, use a replacement and
cannot be decided; a record altered against its output.

Run folders (SP_PLAN.txt PACKAGE; 8 A; Amendment 1's alternating scheme with A2.6's limit): the other 29 frames of
c4_bands.tsv with S = md, f<FRAME>_r8_a1; frames 20000, 44388 and 59999 with S = ci, f<FRAME>_r8_ci_a1; frame 42436's

replaced by this amendment's versions and spa_package.py and spa_package_stats.py added, each checked against the sha256
below (the job scripts call SD's spa_stage.py). The inputs, generator logs and sha256 records are committed before any
job is submitted.
Expected cost: about 2,000 job-hours if each new run folder costs what frame 42436's 8 A run did (61.3; 950 atoms, the
largest 8 A region), more where an alternation needs more cycles.
Disk (added here): at most three alternation jobs of one run folder run at once - job_ts and job_r0 start at submission,
job_r_irc and job_p_irc after job_ts_freq_irc, while job_r0 may still run (A1.6's "four jobs start together" overstated
this) - and each environment step may write up to 3.3 GB of trajectory before it passes (A1.6), so a run folder may need
up to about 11 GB at once, and A1.6's check wants 20 GB free before each alternation step. Before submission the bundle
measures the free space and the quota on hpc1 and submits at most (the smaller of the two, less 20 GB) / 11 GB run
folders at a time, in frame-number order, the next wave when a wave's alternation jobs have ended; the waves are
reported.
Outputs: before anything is deleted from hpc1, every run folder's files are downloaded to the PC archive (sha256
checked) and the readable outputs, geometries, Hessians, final wavefunctions and a manifest are committed (Jessica's
rule of 18 October 2026; the x77 collection extended to the package's folders). C4's 35 runs and any M1 continuations
are handled the same way (C4_CLOSURE.txt).
