Stationary-point barriers per frame (SP) - PLAN. Written 12 October 2026; committed before any SP input is generated,
and the inputs before any SP run. Results folder 05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers; code phase1_system_dev/sp_prepare.py,
sp_stage.py, sp_report.py (tier1-realism; sha256 at the end). Retained-final: this plan fixes the protocol, the smoke
test's region rule and the reporting of the 30-frame package before any of it has run.

DECISION (Jessica, 12 October 2026)
Apply frame 820's stationary-point protocol (steps 18c-18f: transition-state optimisation with a hybrid Hessian,
frequency check, IRC, reactant and product re-optimised in the same region and frozen surroundings) to every frame of
the ensemble, with an extended region chosen by distance; smoke-test it on one frame, then run the 30 frames as one
package.
The draft C4 Amendment 2 (TS-anchored forth and back) is not pursued; its pilot stays [exploratory]. This departs from
PILOT_A2_ADDENDUM1.txt, whose rule (fewer than 3 of 6 frames not met -> Amendment 2 written) the pilot met; the pilot's
results (6 of 6 met) were seen before this decision. Reason: the literature review of 12 October (LITERATURE below)
found stationary points in one region and one set of frozen surroundings to be the standard route to a QM/MM barrier;
the TS-anchored cycle is one half of the alternating scheme (Senn & Thiel 2009, section 3.2). C4_CRITERIA.txt and
Amendment 1 are unchanged; C4's running jobs finish and are reported as planned.

LITERATURE (full papers in the library; DOIs below)
Region. Chorismate-mutase QM/MM studies relax large regions but trace the path by restrained minimisation along
r = d(C-O) - d(C-C), not by a saddle-point search: Ranaghan et al. 2004, atoms beyond 16 A fixed, waters 10-16 A
restrained (their AM1 test: the restraints raised the barrier by 3.0 kcal/mol); Claeyssens et al. 2005, 25 A sphere,
outer 5 A fixed; Senn et al. 2009, protein within 17 A and waters within 30 A free, "depending on the snapshot ... about
9850 atoms". For saddle points the Thiel/Kastner protocol is a distance region: Cooper & Kastner 2014, "Whole residues
(amino acids or water molecules) with at least one atom within 8 A of the proline N atom were optimized (about 360
atoms), all other atoms were frozen to their positions from the MD. This is the same protocol as we typically employ";
Kastner et al. 2007 (fluorinase), "All residues or water molecules within 8 A of SAM or F- were included in the
optimization" (3960 degrees of freedom). Senn & Thiel 2009 (section 3.2): quasi-Newton optimisers scale as O(N^2) to
O(N^3) and become "impractical for large systems"; the TS searches they describe use a core Hessian with
microiterations. The ORCA 6.0 manual's QM/MM chapter describes microiterations only through its GROMACS interface;
ORCA's OptTS works in redundant internal coordinates, which the manual warns are slow beyond about 2000 primitives (18c:
1506 internal coordinates for the 181 atoms ORCA optimised, its 102 active atoms and their fixed neighbours; an 8 A
region holds about nine times as many active atoms). Our outputs: 18b (OptTS, 1959 active atoms) was killed at 221 GB
(limit 120 GB); 18c (102 atoms) converged in 21 cycles. The 8 A distance rule is therefore the largest literature-backed
region in which ORCA's TS search is plausible; 6 A is the fallback; the smoke test decides (below).
Frozen surroundings. Cooper & Kastner 2014: the frame's MD positions for every atom outside the region, for every state.
Senn et al. 2009 (BsCM): reactant-state snapshots, the outside fixed. Ryde 2016 (section 4, item 7): "optimize the MM
system for one state and employ this MM structure for all the other states"; if the results depend on that state, the
model must be extended. Rod & Ryde 2005: with the environment fixed entirely, environments from reactant and product
simulations gave forward barriers of 59 and 26 kJ/mol. Claeyssens et al. 2005 took snapshots from TS-restrained MD.
Reactant. Ryde 2016 (section 2.5): all distant groups must stay in the same local minimum through a reaction sequence
("a single water molecule at the periphery" can change the energy by 20 kJ/mol). Taking the reactant as the minimum
reached from the TS (IRC, then minimisation) keeps reactant and TS in one basin of the surroundings.
Frequencies. ORCA 6.0 manual: partial Hessian vibrational analysis (PHVA) "should be used for QM/MM optimized structures
only to verify the nature of the stationary point"; with non-active atoms present ORCA selects PHVA itself.

PROTOCOL (per frame F, radius d, surroundings S; ORCA 6.0.1, B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX, TightSCF, QM atoms
6207-6230, charge -2, singlet, ff14SB/GAFF/TIP3P force field complex_solvated.ORCAFF.prms; atom indices 0-based)
P1 Region: every residue (amino acid, water, ion, the substrate) with at least one atom within d of any atom 6207-6230,
   whole, measured on the frame's MD structure (19_ensemble/frame_F/frame_F_CHA2.pdb). Primary d = 8 A.
P2 Surroundings, shared by every state of the frame: S = md, the MD structure (primary); S = ci, the final band's
   climbing image (the surroundings check under PACKAGE).
P3 TS: start = region atoms from the band's climbing image (neb_MEP.allxyz), every other atom from S. OptTS TightOpt,
   Calc_Hess true with Hybrid_Hess over the 24 QM atoms (as 18c), MaxIter 500. Change from 18c: TightOpt (max gradient
   1e-4, RMS 3e-5 Eh/bohr, C4's thresholds) for every stationary point, because the region is about nine times larger.
P4 TS check: NumFreq with only the 24 QM atoms active (PHVA, 144 displacements; the environment as optimised). Pass:
   exactly one imaginary mode of magnitude >= 20 cm-1 (smaller imaginary values are listed, not counted). Change from
   18c (NumFreq over all 102 active atoms): at ~950 atoms that would be ~5700 displacements.
P5 IRC: from the TS, the 24 QM atoms moving and the environment held at the TS geometry, Hessian from P4 (InitHess read,
   hessMode 0), both directions, MaxIter 100 (18d's settings). Pass: one end reactant-like (O-C 6214-6215 <= 1.80 A and
   C-C 6207-6219 >= 2.40 A) and the other product-like (C-C <= 1.80 A, O-C >= 2.40 A). Change from 18d: 18d moved all
   102 region atoms and read the full 102-atom Hessian.
P6 R_IRC and P_IRC: Opt TightOpt of the region from the reactant-like and product-like IRC ends, MaxIter 500; pass:
   converged and still reactant-like / product-like. Change from 18e/18f: those started from the step-15/17 minima.
P7 R0 (check): Opt TightOpt of the region from the band's image 0 (the Phase 1 reactant) region atoms in S, MaxIter 500;
   pass: converged and still reactant-like.
P8 Frequencies of R_IRC, P_IRC, R0: as P4; a minimum has no counted imaginary mode.
Energies: the last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY (QM/MM) of each optimisation (as BARRIER_FINAL.md); kcal/mol = Eh x
627.5094740631. Every check (sp_stage.py) also verifies that no atom outside the active atoms moved (2e-6 A).

QUANTITIES (per frame, reported whatever they show)
B_sp = E(TS) - E(R_IRC), the primary barrier. Complete when P3 converged, P4 passed, P5 passed, R_IRC converged (P6) and
R_IRC has no counted imaginary mode (P8); otherwise the frame is reported with the step that failed and has no B_sp.
dR0 = E(R0) - E(R_IRC) (the C4 question: is there a lower reactant than the one connected to the TS?); E(TS) - E(R0) is
reported alongside, never in place of B_sp. dE_rxn = E(P_IRC) - E(R_IRC). The TS imaginary frequency; every counted and
uncounted imaginary value; cycles, wall time, memory and file sizes of every job.

SMOKE TEST (before the package)
Frame 42436, chosen by a rule: the largest 8 A region of the 30 frames (950 atoms), so the worst case for cost. Both
radii, S = md: f42436_r8 and f42436_r6, all eight jobs each (submit.sh). Region rule, fixed here: the 30 frames use 8 A
if, at 8 A, every job ended inside its 176 h walltime with ORCA terminating normally, the TS, R_IRC, P_IRC and R0
optimisations passed their checks (P3, P6, P7), the TS has exactly one counted imaginary mode (P4) and the IRC ends are
one reactant and one product (P5); the frequencies of the three minima (P8) are results, not part of the rule; otherwise
6 A if the same holds at 6 A; otherwise stop and report. sp_report.py smoke prints the verdict. The barrier values play
no part in the rule. Frame 42436's runs at the chosen radius are that frame's package runs (same code, same rules); the
other radius is reported as a region-size check on that frame.

PACKAGE (after the smoke test meets the rule; one bundle)
The other 29 frames at the chosen radius with S = md, all eight jobs each. Surroundings check: frames 20000, 44388 and
59999 (the 1st, 15th and 30th by frame number) again with S = ci at the chosen radius; reported per frame as B_sp(ci) -
B_sp(md). If the difference exceeds 2 kcal/mol in at least two of the three, the report states that the barrier depends
on which state's surroundings are frozen (Ryde 2016, item 7), and both sets are carried forward.
Ensemble (complete frames, S = md): count; mean and standard deviation (as Phase 1 and Claeyssens et al. report them);
median; range; the exponential average -RT ln(mean exp(-B_sp/RT)) at 298.15 K (Cooper & Kastner 2014, their eq. 4).
Per frame, beside B_sp: the band's barrier_image0 and barrier_klow (c4_bands.tsv), descriptive only. If fewer than 20 of
30 frames are complete, the ensemble is reported but not used as the Phase 1 barrier until this plan is revisited.
Whether B_sp becomes "the Phase 1 barrier" (audit item C3) is decided separately, after the package.

EXPECTED SIZES (estimates from frame 820's files; the smoke test records the real ones in FILES_BEFORE_CLEANUP.txt)
Per run folder at 8 A, while running: full-system trajectories 2.7 MB per optimisation cycle and per IRC step (18c:
57.6 MB for 21 cycles; 18d: 233 MB for its whole path, 29 + 59 steps), up to ~1.4 GB per optimisation at 500 cycles;
Hessian files of the TS optimisation over the whole region, likely several hundred MB each (18c: 12.4 MB with 102 active
atoms; 18b: 1.19 GB with 1959); ORCA output text ~0.1-0.4 GB per optimisation. After each passing step the job deletes
full-system trajectories, *.tmp and the TS step's Hessians and xz-compresses any output over 50 MB; expected ~0.1-0.3 GB
kept per run folder (two start/end full-system xyz of 1.3-4 MB per step, compressed outputs). On a failure nothing is
deleted. Disk check before each step: 10 GB free for the TS, 5 GB for IRC and minimisations, 3 GB for frequencies.

LIMITATIONS (stated with the results)
The 8 A region is smaller than the chorismate-mutase literature's 16-20 A (P1). Surroundings frozen at the
reactant-state MD structure may favour the reactant (Rod & Ryde 2005); the surroundings check on three frames measures
it. PHVA tests only the substrate block of the Hessian. The IRC holds the environment at the TS geometry; the
minimisations then relax it. Potential energies only (no zero-point or thermal terms), one functional and basis, as
frame 820.

FILES AND CODE
sp_prepare.py (generator, hpc1 login node): checks every source against the repos' sha256 records; writes the inputs,
the start files of the TS and R0, eight PBS jobs and submit.sh per run folder; a second generation into a scratch folder
must give byte-identical files. sp_stage.py (in the jobs): starts of later steps, checks, STAGE_RESULT.txt; a failed
check stops the job, and PBS afterok stops every job that depends on it. sp_report.py: read-only report and the smoke
verdict; it prints no B_sp for a frame that is not complete. The package's ensemble statistics and the surroundings
check are computed by a script committed with the package's inputs, before any package result exists. Jobs: 16 cores,
250 GB (TS; TS frequencies + IRC) or 180 GB (minimisations, frequencies), walltime 176 h, e-mail on abort only.
