C4 Amendment 1: the reference energy of a run's start image (written 11 October 2026, committed while most of the 35
runs were still running; C4_AMENDMENT1_STATE.txt lists which runs had finished when it was committed). It changes two
energy references in C4_CRITERIA.txt and the sentences built on them. No threshold, test, frame, run or input changes.
Analysis: c4_analyse_a1.py (the pre-registered c4_analyse.py stays as committed and is run as well).

WHAT WAS FOUND
The criteria assumed that ORCA writes neb_MEP.allxyz and neb_MEP.QMRegion_trj.xyz one NEB iteration before the
converged band, so that a geometry taken from them has the energy written beside it (Ew_k, the comment-line energy,
equal to the last block of neb.NEB.log) rather than the converged band's (E_k, the PATH SUMMARY). The runs show the
opposite. Each run's first energy is a fresh single point on its start geometry, and it matches E_k, not Ew_k: the
pre-registered analysis, run on 11 October at 12:32 while most runs were going (C4_REPORT_PROVISIONAL.txt, committed
with this amendment), gave E_start - Ew from -0.443 to +0.026 kcal/mol and failed M2 for S2_42436 and S2_24883, the
two frames whose labels lag most (Ew_klow - E_klow = +0.446 and +0.141, c4_bands.tsv), by about their lags. Before
this amendment was committed, the amended analysis was run on the same outputs (x68 stops otherwise) and found
E_start - E_k within 0.10 kcal/mol of its median for every run; its M2 lines are in C4_AMENDMENT1_STATE.txt. So the
geometries on disk ARE the converged band's; only the energy labels written beside them are one iteration old.
ORCA's own final single point at the end of each finished run equals its optimiser's last energy within 0.002
kcal/mol, so the optimiser's energy and the band's are the same quantity.

WHAT CHANGES
1. M2: E_start is compared with E_(start image) of the final band (E_0 or E_klow, PATH SUMMARY), not with Ew. The rule
   is unchanged (every run within 0.10 kcal/mol of the median). The pre-registered values against Ew are still
   printed, marked as superseded.
2. A1: dE_reopt = E_min - E_klow. The pre-registered formula, (E_min - E_start) + (Ew_klow - E_klow), added the label
   lag on the assumption that E_start = Ew_klow. Since E_start = E_klow, that term only shifted dE_reopt by the lag.
   With dE_reopt negative (a minimisation goes down), removing it makes A1 harder to pass in every frame whose lag is
   positive (by up to 0.45 kcal/mol, frame 42436) and easier by at most 0.03 kcal/mol in the one frame whose lag is
   negative (50892). The barrier from the re-optimised reactant is E_ci - E_min (= B - dE_reopt, as before).
3. Text built on the wrong premise, superseded: in BANDS AND ENERGIES, "A re-optimisation starts from a written
   geometry, so its starting energy is Ew_k"; in STEP 6, that stab_TS "pairs each in vacuo energy with the QM/MM energy
   of the same geometry on disk (the written band, Ew)", and the sentence on its difference to B. Corrected: the
   geometries on disk are the final band's, so stab_TS pairs each in vacuo energy with E (PATH SUMMARY), exactly a
   barrier difference at one set of geometries, with no lag to report. The plan's 10 October note (i) is corrected in
   the same way. Phase 1's harvested stab_TS (PATH SUMMARY barriers, in vacuo energies at the geometries on disk) was
   consistent all along.

WHAT EXISTED WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN (disclosed)
When this was written (11 October, about 13:00) six runs had finished: S2_41786, S2_43087, S2_26495, S2_34991 (step 2)

under the pre-registered analysis are in C4_REPORT_PROVISIONAL.txt. For the four step-2 frames the change in dE_reopt

S2_42436 and S2_24883 only their start energies (M2) were known, not their results.
