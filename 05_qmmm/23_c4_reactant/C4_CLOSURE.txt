C4 CLOSURE: how C4 of the Phase 1 audit ends. An amendment to C4_CRITERIA.txt and C4_AMENDMENT1.txt, written 18 October
2026 and committed with 05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers/SP_AMENDMENT2.txt, before C4's runs are collected or analysed and before
any input of the stationary-point (SP) package exists. It is not the draft "Amendment 2" of pilot_a2/PILOT_A2.txt (a
TS-anchored forth and back), which SP_PLAN.txt dropped and whose pilot stays exploratory. C4_CRITERIA.txt and
C4_AMENDMENT1.txt stay as committed (sha256 at the end) and govern everything this file does not change. It settles
item B1 of 05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers/SP_ERRATUM1.txt from question 2 of
05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers/PACKAGE_DECISIONS_LITERATURE_20261018.md (DECISIONS Q2), which gives the literature.
Retained-final.

WHAT HAS BEEN SEEN BEFORE THIS AMENDMENT (disclosed: the decision follows C4's provisional step-2 results)
- C4_REPORT_PROVISIONAL.txt (the pre-registered analysis on 11 October, committed with C4 Amendment 1): every one of
  the 13 frames assessed at step 2 fails A1 (dE_reopt -1.84 to -11.56 kcal/mol) and passes A2 and A3; step 0 finds
  "incomplete optimisation" in all five S0 frames; 17 runs were then still running or waiting for an M1 continuation.
- The C4 Amendment 2 pilot (pilot_a2, exploratory): its first-cycle test met in 6 of 6 frames (dR -0.02 to +0.01);
  B3 - B = +0.84 to +3.00 kcal/mol.
- SP_PLAN.txt (12 October) applied a stationary-point protocol to every frame, leaving whether its barrier becomes the
  Phase 1 barrier to audit item C3 (settled in SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.3), and left C4 to finish ("C4's running jobs
  finish and are reported as planned"). SP Amendment 1's smoke test 2: frame 42436 at 8 A, B_sp 14.03
  kcal/mol, dR0 +1.85; smoke test 1's R0 lies 2.85 kcal/mol below that R_IRC (SP_AMENDMENT2.txt).
- All 35 C4 jobs have ended (SP_ERRATUM1.txt A9: queue listings of 13 and 14 October). Their outputs have not been
  collected, and neither c4_analyse.py nor c4_analyse_a1.py has been run on them since 11 October.

WHAT C4 STILL DOES (as pre-registered)
1. Collect the 35 runs (S0 x 5, S2 x 30) under Jessica's rule of 18 October 2026: every file downloaded to the PC
   archive (sha256 checked) and the readable outputs, geometries, energy files, any Hessians, final wavefunctions and a
   manifest committed, before anything is deleted from hpc1.
2. M1 as C4_CRITERIA.txt states it: a run not finished is continued once in <run>_c, with its inputs committed before
   it runs; still not finished, the frame fails step 2. The continuations' outputs are collected as in 1.
3. Run the pre-registered c4_analyse.py and the amended c4_analyse_a1.py (C4_AMENDMENT1.txt) on every run, and report
   machinery (G, X, M0, M1, M2), step 0 (the kind of dip), step 1 (B = E_ci - E_klow, with the image-0 barrier beside
   it) and step 2 (A1, A2, A3 per frame, and how many frames are accepted), whatever they show.
Step 2 answers the audit's own question for the write-up: whether k_low, the band's lowest image at or before the
climbing image, is a reactant of that frame.

WHAT C4 DOES NOT DO (steps 3-6 of C4_CRITERIA.txt are not run)
- Step 3 (forth and back) is a check some authors use, not the standard route to an enzyme barrier: Ryde 2016 (p. 130)
  gives it as one of the approaches "suggested to detect and avoid" the local-minimum problem, calls it "tedious and
  time consuming", and names QM/MM structures for several snapshots as the "more common approach"; Senn et al. 2009 scan
  each chorismate-mutase snapshot in both directions as a check. The SP package is the several-snapshots route.
- A step-3 repair of the bands would carry their flaw forward: every band held the first-shell waters fixed (audit item
  A8), and a band rebuilt in the same setup keeps it.
- The concern step 3 targets, a reactant lower than the band's, is carried on the SP route by B_eff
  (SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.1).
- Step 4 (the result over the retained frames) is not C4's result: it was defined over the frames retained after step
  3, and without step 3 it would be the mean over only the frames step 2 accepts, a subset chosen by the test itself.
  The analysis scripts still print their step-4 block (marked PROVISIONAL, "final after step 3") and label failing
  frames "-> step 3"; both are reported as printed and read as "no C4 result" and "fails step 2". The band barriers
  stay descriptive (SP_PLAN.txt prints them beside B_sp). The Phase 1 barrier is settled by SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.3.
- C4's rule "if more than 3 of the 30 frames are dropped, stop" belonged to step 3 and is retired with it;
  SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.2 sets Phase 1's attrition rule.
- Step 5 (the 6 A sensitivity test) is not replaced. On the SP route the region's effect rests on one frame: frame
  42436's B_sp at 6 A and 8 A differ by 1.07 kcal/mol, above step 5's own 1.0 kcal/mol tolerance; this is reported as
  a limitation of the SP barrier. The surroundings check (SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.4) tests the frozen boundary on three
  frames.
- Step 6 (downstream: the in vacuo single points, grid, A matrix, s17, stab_TS, the variance split and the regression
  on the new reactants) belongs to the Phase 2 redesign. C4_CRITERIA.txt's WHAT THIS DOES TO THE DESIGN YARDSTICK is
  replaced by SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.5.

DIRECTION
Not running step 3 removes a band-repair candidate that step 2's provisional data (re-optimised reactants 1.84-11.56
kcal/mol below k_low, pre-registered formula) suggest would give higher barriers than the bands'. On the SP route that
direction is represented by B_eff, which is reported beside B_sp for every frame.
