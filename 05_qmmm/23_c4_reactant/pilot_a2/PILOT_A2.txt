C4 Amendment 2 pilot - EXPLORATORY. The TS-anchored forth and back tried on one finished frame. Written 11 October 2026
(evening) while 17 of the 35 C4 runs were still running; committed before any pilot input is generated, and the inputs
before any pilot run. Not part of C4's result; C4_CRITERIA.txt and Amendment 1 are unchanged by it.

WHY
C4_WAY_FORWARD_20261011.md (this folder; the literature review, also in the project as claude/C4_WAY_FORWARD.md) proposes
replacing step 3's NEB-CI "back" leg by a TS-anchored forth and back (Amendment 2, draft, its section 5). Before that is
pre-registered, one frame tests what the review could not settle from the literature:
 1. machinery: ORCA 6.0.1 L-Opt with the substrate (the QM atoms) left out of the active atoms, so that the environment
    relaxes around a fixed climbing-image substrate. ORCA's L-Opt (its MD-module optimiser) states that "frozen
    coordinates, fragments are ignored", so the active-atom list is the only way to hold the substrate;
 2. cost: steps and wall time of each leg;
 3. a first look at the quantities Amendment 2 would test (below).

FRAME - chosen by a rule fixed before any pilot result
The earliest-finished step-2 run whose start image is not image 0: S2_43087 (ended 11 October 05:03; S2_41786 ended
earlier but starts from image 0, which has no environment to carry from the climbing image). The bundle checks the rule
on hpc1 before it writes anything. Band 19_ensemble, climbing image 5, k_low 1.

RUNS - the step-2 template, thresholds and machinery (TightSCF, TolMaxG 1.0e-4, TolRMSG 3.0e-5 Eh/bohr, MaxIter 2000)
  T1_43087  start: the final band's climbing image (neb_MEP.allxyz image 5; the converged geometry, Amendment 1). Active
            atoms: C4's 1959 minus the substrate (6207-6230): 1935. Input otherwise S2_43087/reopt.inp, line for line.
  R2_43087  generated only after T1 has finished (gradients met, energy plateau as M1, c4_extract ok): T1's last geometry
            with the substrate (6207-6230) taken from band image 1 (k_low). All 1959 atoms active. Input file:
            S2_43087/reopt.inp byte for byte. Not generated if a substrate atom would start closer than 1.0 A to any other
            atom (the generator reports the closest contact either way).
  A second cycle is not part of the pilot.

QUANTITIES (kcal/mol; the optimiser's energies, as in C4), reported whatever they show
  T1  E_start - E_ci (the start is the converged climbing image: must be within 0.01); fall_T = E_ci - E_min(T1);
      steps; environment displacement and most-displaced atom; substrate displacement (must be 0; c4_extract.py checks
      every atom outside T1's active atoms, the substrate included)
  R2  dR = E_min(R2) - E_min(S2_43087), against the draft test |dR| <= 1.0; substrate RMSD to its MD structure (A2)
  B = E_ci - E_klow (band); B3 = E_min(T1) - E_min(R2); and, as a check, E_min(T1) - E_min(S2_43087)

FIXED BEFORE THE PILOT - the draft Amendment 2 values, which the pilot must not be used to choose
The step-2 thresholds for every leg; the 1.0 kcal/mol test; at most two cycles; more than 3 of 30 unresolved frames ->
stop and revisit; the three-frame NEB check on 23268, 42436 and 44388. If any of these changes after the pilot, the change
and its reason are stated in the amendment.

HOW THE PILOT RELATES TO C4
If Amendment 2 is committed with rules under which frame 43087's T1 and R2 inputs are byte-identical to the pilot's (their
sha256 decide), the pilot runs are that frame's step-3 runs; otherwise the frame is run again under the amendment. The
pilot's inputs, outputs and report are committed [exploratory] whatever they show.
