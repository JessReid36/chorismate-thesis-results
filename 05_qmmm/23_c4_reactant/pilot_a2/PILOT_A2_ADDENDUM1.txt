WHY SIX
One frame tests the machinery; it says little about how often the reactant re-optimised from the relaxed transition-state
environment fails the draft test |dR| <= 1.0 kcal/mol on the first cycle. With six frames the decision rule below
gives a false alarm with probability 0.016 if 10% of frames would fail, and catches the method with probability 0.66 if
half would fail and 0.93 if 70% would (binomial).

FRAMES - by the rule of PILOT_A2.txt extended to six
The six earliest-finished step-2 runs whose start image is not image 0, ordered by the end of their job (the time stamp
of reopt.pbs.out on hpc1). From the end times in C4_AMENDMENT1_STATE.txt (committed 11 October, 17:15):
  S2_43087 05:03 (the pilot's first frame), S2_26495 10:37, S2_34991 11:10, S2_48292 13:38, S2_20000 15:17, S2_58698 15:18
(S2_41786 03:01 and S2_33320 15:39 start from image 0). The bundle recomputes the six on hpc1 and stops unless they are
these six, each finished cleanly (gradients met, c4_extract ok).

PILOT DECISION RULE - fixed now
After R2 of all six has finished (T1 and R2 each finished: gradients met, energy plateau, c4_extract ok; one
continuation allowed as in C4's M1):
  - any machinery failure (a substrate atom moved in a T1, ORCA rejecting the input, a leg not finishing after one
    continuation) -> fix the procedure before Amendment 2 is written;
  - 3 or more of the six with |dR| > 1.0 kcal/mol -> the TS-anchored design is revisited (C4_WAY_FORWARD section 4,
    option D, or a second cycle as standard) before Amendment 2 is written;
  - otherwise Amendment 2 is written with the values PILOT_A2.txt fixed, and each frame enters step 3 as soon as its own
    step-2 run has finished (to be stated in the amendment).
B3 - B over six frames is reported but decides nothing: six frames cannot estimate a mean shift to better than about
+-2-3 kcal/mol, and B stays the provisional barrier until step 4.

REUSE
As PILOT_A2.txt: a pilot frame's T1 and R2 are its step-3 runs only if Amendment 2's inputs for that frame are
byte-identical (sha256) to the pilot's; otherwise the frame is run again.
