C4 of the Phase 1 audit: the reactant end of each NEB band (PHASE1_AUDIT_CHECKLIST.md, item C4, protocol accepted
6 October 2026). Pre-registered 10 October 2026: these criteria and the band table c4_bands.tsv (which fixes the
frames and images below) are committed before any input is generated, and the inputs before any run. Band table:
c4_bands.py (PC, committed files only). Generator: c4_prepare.py (hpc1; every source checked against its sha256 in the
results repo). In-job extraction: c4_extract.py. Analysis: c4_analyse.py.

THE PROBLEM
In 27 of the 30 reported frames (bands after C5) an image before the climbing image lies more than 0.5 kcal/mol
below image 0, by up to 12.81 kcal/mol (frame 23268). Image 0 is the Phase 1 reactant, optimised to ORCA's
large-system thresholds (L-Opt: max gradient 1.0e-3, RMS 2.0e-4 Eh/bohr), and every tabulated barrier is measured from
it. Over the 30 frames (c4_bands.tsv): from image 0, 13.22 +- 4.36 (sd) kcal/mol; from the lowest image at or before the
climbing image, 17.10 +- 2.57.

BANDS AND ENERGIES
Final band of each frame: 22_c5_nebci for the eight C5 frames, 19_ensemble for the other 22 (all NEB-CI).
  E_k   image k's energy on the final band (last PATH SUMMARY of neb.out); every tabulated barrier uses these.
  Ew_k  image k's energy on the band as written to disk (neb_MEP.QMRegion_trj.xyz, neb_MEP.allxyz). ORCA writes these
        files at the last iteration in neb.NEB.log, one before the converged band. The end points are fixed. A
        re-optimisation starts from a written geometry, so its starting energy is Ew_k (they differ from E_k by -0.03
        to +0.45 kcal/mol at the images used; c4_bands.tsv, lag_klow).
  k_low the lowest image of the final band at or before the climbing image (image 0 included). The same image is
        lowest on the written band in all 30 frames.
  E_start, E_min  the optimiser's energy at its first and its last step (reopt-minimize-ener.csv; one run, one
        definition). Phase 1's optimisations end 0.16-0.19 kcal/mol below their own final single point (c4_bands.tsv,
        opt_offset), so the optimiser's energies are never compared directly with single-point energies: every
        criterion uses differences within one run, within one band, or the run's start against its own start image.
All energies kcal/mol (x 627.5094740631).

RUNS (35; ORCA 6.0.1; 8 cores, 120 GB and 176 h walltime, as Phase 1's reactant optimisations)
Input: each frame's Phase 1 reactant_opt.inp (QMMM B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX L-Opt; QM atoms 6207-6230, charge
-2, singlet; the same 1959 active atoms; the same force field) with three changes: TightSCF (as the bands); %geom
MaxIter 2000 TolMaxG 1.0e-4 TolRMSG 3.0e-5 end (ORCA's TightOpt gradient thresholds; see DEVIATION); the geometry read
from start.xyz (the written image, full precision, no PDB rounding). 2000 steps at the slowest Phase 1 rate (180 s
per step, frame 42436) take 100 h, so a run that reaches the step limit still ends inside the walltime and writes its
last geometry.
  S0_<frame>  step 0: image 0 re-optimised, for the five frames with the deepest dips: 23268, 42436, 44388, 54795,
              49592.
  S2_<frame>  step 2: image k_low re-optimised, all 30 frames. For 33320 and 41786 k_low is image 0, so their S2 run
              is also their step-0 run (the two frames without a dip).

DEVIATION FROM THE ACCEPTED PROTOCOL (made now, before any input exists)
The protocol named ORCA's Normal gradient thresholds (TolMaxG 3e-4, TolRMSG 1e-4). L-Opt stops on gradients alone, and
every Phase 1 reactant optimisation was still falling in energy when it stopped: by 2.8-7.5 kcal/mol over its last 50
steps (median 4.1) and 0.16-0.62 over its last 10 (reactant_opt-minimize-ener.csv, 30 frames). ORCA's own Normal
criteria also require the energy to settle (TolE 5e-6 Eh per step), which L-Opt does not test. A run stopped on a
slope would make A1 and step 0 depend on where L-BFGS happened to stop. So the gradient thresholds are tightened to
ORCA's TightOpt values and an energy plateau is required (M1). This is stricter than the protocol: a later stop can
only lower a re-optimised energy (the optimiser goes downhill), which makes A1 harder to pass, not easier.

MACHINERY (a run failing G, X, M0 or M2 is not assessable and is reported; its frame waits for a corrected run)
  G   generator, before any run: every source matches its sha256 in the results repo; image 0 of neb_MEP.allxyz
      equals reactant.pdb within 6e-4 A (PDB rounding); in image k_low every atom outside the active region equals
      reactant.pdb within 6e-4 A; image k_low's substrate equals the committed written QM-region path within 2e-6 A,
      and its energy, if the allxyz comment carries one, equals Ew_klow within 1e-6 Eh; the active-atom list is the
      same in reactant_opt.inp and the band's neb.inp; atoms 6207-6230 are the substrate in reactant.pdb and in the
      frame's MD structure. Logged: substrate RMSDs to the MD structure (a start above 0.25 A is flagged), and the
      RMS displacement of the environment (active atoms other than the substrate) from image 0 and from k_low to the
      climbing image, for all 30 frames: how far the surroundings change on the way to the TS (Ryde 2016).
  X   in each job, c4_extract.py: every atom outside the active region is where start.xyz put it (2e-6 A).
  M0  the input ORCA echoes is the committed reopt.inp; the optimiser's printed thresholds are the input's (1e-4 and
      3e-5 Eh/bohr read 0.496 and 0.149 kJ/mol/A); its energies start at step 1. Once the gradients are met, also:
      118 electrons, charge -2, singlet, and the final active-region geometry extracted.
  M1  finished: ORCA terminated normally, "The minimization has converged.", and the energy over the final 50 steps
      fell by at most 2.5e-4 Eh (ORCA's Normal energy criterion, 5e-6 Eh per step, on average; a run of 50 steps or
      fewer is judged on its gradients). A run not finished is continued once, in <run>_c, from its last geometry
      (reopt.xyz, or the last frame of reopt_trj.dcd if the walltime killed it) with the same settings, except that a
      run which met the gradients without the plateau continues at ORCA's VeryTightOpt gradients (TolMaxG 3e-5,
      TolRMSG 8e-6); its inputs are committed before it runs, and the two parts are one optimisation (E_start from the
      first, everything else from the second). Still not finished: the frame fails step 2.
  M2  every run's E_start - Ew_(start image) lies within 0.10 kcal/mol of the median over all runs: each run began from
      the intended image, and the median measures any constant offset between the optimiser's energy and the band's
      (0 if the two are the same quantity; about -0.18 if they differ as Phase 1's opt_offset suggests). The values are
      reported.

STEP 0 - which kind of dip (diagnosis, reported; it changes nothing below)
For each S0 frame: fall F0 = E_start - E_min; depth d = E_0 - E_klow.
  F0 >= d - 1   "incomplete optimisation": re-optimising image 0 alone reaches the dip (within 1 kcal/mol)
  otherwise     "separate local minimum": the band found a lower minimum that image 0's optimisation does not reach
Also reported for these five frames: the S0 against the S2 end point (energy, and active-region RMS displacement),
which shows whether the two starts reach one structure. For 33320 and 41786, F0 is reported (no dip to reach).

STEP 1 - the barrier
B = E_ci - E_klow, from the final band. (From image 0: E_ci - E_0, reported beside it once.)

STEP 2 - is k_low a reactant? (S2 run of each frame; accepted only if all three hold)
  A1  |dE_reopt| <= 1.0 kcal/mol, where dE_reopt = (E_min - E_start) + (Ew_klow - E_klow) is the re-optimised energy
      relative to image k_low of the final band (Claeyssens et al. 2011: unrestrained re-optimisation of the reactant
      from the lowest restrained structure gave very similar structures, energy differences below 1 kcal/mol).
  A2  substrate RMSD between the final geometry and the frame's own MD structure, after superposition, <= 0.30 A
      (the Phase 1 optimisation moved substrates by median 0.170, max 0.219 A; the written k_low images already lie
      0.128-0.249 A from their MD structures, median 0.194, from the committed .rst7 files; the generator recomputes
      both).
  A3  active-region displacement from image 0 (1959 atoms, no superposition: the rest of the system is fixed)
      <= 0.20 A RMS per atom (the band distance to k_low, spread over the active atoms, is at most 0.152 A per atom,
      frame 23268; an upper bound on the dips' own displacement); the largest single-atom displacement and its atom
      are reported.
Accepted: the frame's step-1 barrier B stands (protocol step 1; Claeyssens et al. 2011 measure from the lowest point
of the path and use the re-optimisation as the check). The barrier from the re-optimised reactant, B - dE_reopt, is
reported beside it, with the mean dE_reopt. dE_reopt is usually negative (a minimisation goes down), so B is usually
the lower of the two, by at most 1 kcal/mol in an accepted frame; the report states the mean difference and its sign.

STEP 3 - forth and back (Ryde 2016), for each frame failing step 2
Forth: the frame's product substrate placed in the S2 end point's surroundings and optimised there (step-2
thresholds), so both end points share one environment; back: NEB-CI between the S2 end point and that product
(NEB-CI thresholds as C5); then steps 1 and 2 on the new band.
At most two cycles; a frame still failing is dropped and recorded with its numbers. If more than 3 of the 30 frames
are dropped, stop and revisit the protocol before any number is used. Step 3's inputs are generated after step 2's
results and committed before they run; this rule is fixed now.

STEP 4 - the result
Over the retained frames: the arithmetic mean +- sem of B is the result, with the sd in kcal/mol and kJ/mol; the
image-0 values are shown once beside it; the barriers from the re-optimised reactants and the mean dE_reopt are shown
(informative). Ryde's
(2017) second-order cumulant estimate, mean - sd^2/(2RT) at his T = 300 K, is reported with his Table 2's number of
energies needed for +-20 and +-10 kJ/mol at that sd (95% confidence), stated against n.

STEP 5 - sensitivity (Behrens & Hartke 2021; Ryde 2016), fixed now, inputs committed later and before they run
Frames 23268, 42436, 44388 (the three deepest dips). Active region reduced to whole residues and waters with an atom
within 6.0 A of the substrate in image 0, everything else fixed at image 0's coordinates; reactant (from image 0) and
product (the frame's product with the frozen atoms set to image 0's coordinates) optimised to the step-2 thresholds,
then NEB-CI as step 3. Holds if the barrier from the lowest point at or before the highest to the highest is within
1.0 kcal/mol of the frame's
B in at least two of the three frames; otherwise the largest difference is reported with the result as a stated
uncertainty of the barrier definition. It does not change B.

STEP 6 - downstream
Every frame whose reactant geometry changes (k_low not image 0, or a new band from step 3) gets in vacuo single
points at the new reactant's substrate geometry and goes through the alignment, grid, A matrix and s17 again (Stage
5); stab_TS, the variance split and the regression are recomputed. B and stab_TS use the same reactant, image k_low.
stab_TS pairs each in vacuo energy with the QM/MM energy of the same geometry on disk (the written band, Ew), so it is
exactly a barrier difference at one set of geometries; the difference between that QM/MM barrier and B (from the
written band's one-iteration lag; at most 0.45 kcal/mol at the images used, frame 42436) is reported once. Methods
states that the barrier definition was settled by this audit and shows both definitions once.

WHAT THIS DOES TO THE DESIGN YARDSTICK (stated before any result)
Measured from k_low, the enzyme's barrier is higher and its spread smaller, so its TS stabilisation (D5 reference 2)
is smaller in magnitude, and a design is judged against a smaller enzyme number. The definition was accepted on 6
October 2026 on the literature, before any Phase 2.2 design was validated (the one design run so far, s14, is a
frozen-surrogate screen). Claeyssens et al. 2011 plot and average their pathways from the lowest point on the reactant
side; Behrens & Hartke 2021 measure the barrier from the lowest point to the highest; Ryde 2016: all distant groups
must stay in one local minimum throughout a reaction sequence. The same definition applies to every barrier compared
with the enzyme (the bare substrate's and the designs' relaxed NEB-CI barriers), so the comparison is like for like.
Within C4 the barrier from k_low is usually below the barrier from the re-optimised reactant (step 2); both are
reported, and the protocol's choice (k_low) was fixed on 6 October, before any re-optimised energy existed.

SCOPE
Thirty frames, one model chemistry (B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP/Amber), potential energies. The re-optimisations test the
reactant end only; the climbing images are C5's and Phase 1's.
