Stage 4c, Amendment 1 (9 October 2026) - committed before the zero-charge runs.

What and why. The pre-registered quantity is ddE from the QM energy, with the sphere's steric term (LJ) excluded
from L1 and reported separately. In the pseudopotential levels (L0, L2, L3) the Ne-type pseudopotential adds a
charge-independent reactant/TS term of its own, which the analysis did not remove: a design error
(exploratory/STAGE4C_EXPLORATORY.txt, item 1). Amendment 1 removes it as the criteria intended: for each site set,
the same spheres at zero charge, and ddE_corr = ddE - ddE(zero charge). L1 needs no correction: at zero charge a
bare sphere leaves the QM energy equal to the bare substrate's.

What had been looked at when this was written: everything - STAGE4C_REPORT.txt, all 70 energies, orbital and Loewdin
diagnostics and the exploratory analysis. Unlike Stage 4b's Amendment 1, this one is written with the results known.
It therefore changes no threshold, leaves the original verdict on record (STAGE4C_REPORT.txt), and reports the
corrected comparisons separately (STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1_REPORT.txt).

Runs (cs4c_amend1_prepare.py; 36). Each input is a committed input with every Ne> charge set to 0.000000 and nothing
else changed (checked by the generator). Site sets: S1 (E1, E2), S3 (E3), S4 (E4), S5 (E5, E7), S6 (E6), S8 (E8);
levels L0, L2, L3; R and TS. The inline pseudopotential at zero charge ran in Stage 4b (B2_inline_*_q0).

Analysis (cs4c_amend1_analyse.py). Check 0 for the new runs; corrected ddE; the pre-registered comparisons with
thresholds unchanged. Added: a representation check, L1 against L0 corrected (same basis, only the representation
differs), judged by the same criteria and used in the decision rule below; informative: L1 against L2 corrected, L0
against L2, first order against L1; and, chosen after E5's def2-TZVPD reactant was seen, the same comparisons without E5.

Decision rule, fixed now, before the zero-charge runs. It replaces the pre-registered consequence of RANKING ONLY
("magnitudes are judged at L3, and D5's reference 2 is recomputed at L3") for these reasons: (i) L3 equals L2 where
both are well-behaved (within 0.59 kcal/mol for seven of eight) and its one large difference is a change of electronic
state, not basis convergence; (ii) the design-versus-enzyme comparison (D5 reference 2) is like-for-like only at one
model chemistry, and Phase 1's enzyme values are def2-SVP with bare MM charges, the same representation as L1;
(iii) Ryde 2016 recommends SVP-level structures and diffuse functions for single-point energies when anions are
involved - a diffuse single-point check, not a change of production level; (iv) like-for-like, diffuse functions
change the barrier change by about 6%.
  1. Design and relaxed validation stay at def2-SVP with bare LJ spheres (L1).
  2. If the representation check is PASS, every final design also gets a def2-SVPD single point with Ne-type
     pseudopotential spheres, corrected by the same spheres at zero charge; both numbers are reported. Otherwise no
     diffuse check is made in this representation, and the basis uncertainty is stated from Stage 4c and this amendment.
  3. Every final design is screened electronically at each level used: HOMO-LUMO gap, and the shift of substrate atomic
     charges against the bare substrate (Hirshfeld; Davidson & Chakravorty 1992). A design whose state at the diffuse
     level differs from its def2-SVP state (the E5 signature) is flagged, and its barrier change is reported as
     unresolved at this level of theory. Thresholds are fixed when design validation is pre-registered.
  4. D5's reference 2 moves to def2-SVPD only if a final design's catalytic verdict differs between the two levels.
  5. The corrected S4c verdict sets the stated basis uncertainty. Only a FAIL (ranking or sign not kept after the
     correction) changes the protocol: the final ranking of top designs is then made at def2-SVPD.

Inputs. AMENDMENT1_INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 lists the 36 inputs, batch_Z_svp.pbs, batch_Z_svpd.pbs, batch_Z_tzvpd.pbs
and this note; INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 and every pre-registered and committed file are untouched.
