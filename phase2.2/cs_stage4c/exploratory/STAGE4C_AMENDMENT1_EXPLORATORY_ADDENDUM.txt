Stage 4c Amendment 1 - EXPLORATORY note, addendum (10 October 2026). Not pre-registered; verdicts unchanged. Two
additions to STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1_EXPLORATORY.txt that a revised commit script carried but the version run did not.
Numbers: STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1_DIRECTION_REPORT.txt (cs4c_amend1_direction.py on the committed outputs).

1. Item 1, more exactly worded. "Removing it works" means: removing it passes the representation check (L1 against
   L0, corrected: residual 0.284, max 0.739 kcal/mol). The correction is computed independently, from the same
   spheres at zero charge in separate runs; nothing is fitted to L1.

2. The correction is not a lever on the result. It has no adjustable part, it does not touch L1 (the production
   values all design work uses), and its direction is mixed: at each pseudopotential level it makes three of the six
   catalytic arrangements look more barrier-lowering (E1, E2, E8) and three less (E3, E5, E6). Summed over the six,
   it makes them look less barrier-lowering, by +2.80 (L0), +0.88 (L2) and +0.45 (L3) kcal/mol.
