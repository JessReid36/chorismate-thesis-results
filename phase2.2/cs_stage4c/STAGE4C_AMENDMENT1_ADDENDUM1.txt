Stage 4c, Amendment 1 - Addendum 1 (9 October 2026). Committed after the 36 zero-charge runs were submitted and
before their results were seen or analysed.

Change. Rule 4 of the decision rule in STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1.txt ("D5's reference 2 moves to def2-SVPD only if a final
design's catalytic verdict differs between the two levels") is replaced: D5's reference 2 - the enzyme's differential
TS stabilisation, QM/MM barrier minus in vacuo barrier at the same frames and geometries - will be computed at
def2-SVPD as well as at def2-SVP, whatever the designs show, and every design-versus-enzyme comparison will be
reported at both levels. It is not run now. Rules 1-3 and 5 and every threshold are unchanged.

Why. Rule 4 made the level of the enzyme reference depend on the designs' results; fixing it in advance removes that
dependence. It also puts the headline comparison at the level recommended for anion energies (Ryde 2016: diffuse
basis sets for single-point energies when anions are involved), the level of the planned check on final designs (rule 2).

How, to be pre-registered as its own stage when it is built:
- single points at the existing def2-SVP QM/MM reactant and TS geometries of the D5 frames; no re-optimisation (Ryde
  2016: SVP-level structures, larger-basis single points). Phase 1's reported def2-SVP barriers are unchanged;
- bare MM charges cannot be used with def2-SVPD: Stages 4 and 4b showed a diffuse basis traps density at a nearby
  positive charge, and in the enzyme Arg90's nitrogens sit 2.71 and 2.83 A from O2 and O3 (frame 41786,
  CHARGE_MODEL_DECISIONS.md, D-WP4). The MM atoms near the substrate therefore carry the Marefat Khah et al. 2020
  pseudopotentials with their element mapping (He-type for H, Ne-type for B-F, Ar-type for Al-Cl), the rest stay point
  charges. Marefat Khah et al. put them on all MM sites; a shell around the substrate is this project's deviation, so
  its radius is tested for convergence. Atom types outside their mapping are settled when the stage is built;
- the pseudopotentials' zero-charge reactant/TS term is removed exactly as for the designs (Amendment 1); the steric
  contribution stays classical (LJ), carried over from the def2-SVP QM/MM energies, which at fixed geometry do not
  depend on the basis;
- validated first at def2-SVP: on a subset of frames, the pseudopotential-embedded, corrected value must reproduce the
  existing bare-charge value before any def2-SVPD value is used;
- electronic screens as for final designs (rule 3).

Conditions and timing. After C4, whose re-optimisations change some frames and on which the def2-SVP reference already
waits; before design validation. It requires Amendment 1's representation check to pass. If that check fails, the
pseudopotential embedding is not available and another way to reach the diffuse level must be found and assessed
first; the commitment to report the enzyme at both levels stands and is revisited only with written reasons.
