PHASE 2.2 — PLAN AND RATIONALE
==============================
Written 2026-09-30, after Phase 1 was audited step by step and the Hartke-group
literature was re-read in full. Supersedes the Phase 2 / 2b approach.

Every claim below is traceable to a committed output file or to a paper that has
been read. Where a source is cited, the specific setting it belongs to is named,
because the three Hartke-group sources use THREE DIFFERENT settings and conflating
them has already caused errors in this project.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. THE THREE SOURCES, KEPT APART
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[D2018]  Dittner & Hartke, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2018, doi 10.1021/acs.jctc.8b00151
         "Globally Optimal Catalytic Fields - Inverse Design of Abstract Embeddings
         for Maximum Reaction Rate Acceleration"
         refs: acs.jctc.8b00151.pdf
         SETTING: abstract POINT CHARGES on a vdW surface. Reaction path is FIXED
         and pre-optimised ("static" or "vertical" mode). Menshutkin SN2 at PM7,
         with gas-phase and COSMO(water) reference paths. Charges are optimised so
         that the bare substrate + charges reproduces what the continuum does.
         NO continuum is present during the optimisation itself.

[DTHESIS] Dittner, PhD thesis, "Globally Optimal Catalysts"
         refs: gocat_2.pdf
         Contains D2018 reprinted as a chapter, PLUS material not in D2018:
           Ch. 2 (Theory)  - the vdW surface constraint algebra, constraint values
           Ch. 3.6         - the Coulomb-implosion problem; Algorithm 3.1 (static)
                             versus Algorithm 3.2 (ADAPTIVE, fully relaxes the MEP
                             inside the GOCAT)
           Ch. 7           - the adaptive scheme applied, on a Diels-Alder reaction
                             (maleic anhydride + cyclopentadiene), NOT the SN2
         So the thesis DOES relax the path; D2018 does not. Do not cite D2018 for
         path relaxation, and do not cite the thesis Ch.7 system as an SN2.

[B2021]  Behrens & Hartke, Topics in Catalysis 2021, doi 10.1007/s11244-021-01486-1
         "Globally Optimized Molecular Embeddings for Dynamic Reaction Solvate
         Shell Optimization and Active Site Design"
         refs: s11244-021-01486-1.pdf
         SETTING: REAL MOLECULAR FRAGMENTS, not point charges. MEP recomputed
         inside every fitness evaluation. GFN-xTB for QM, OPLS-AA for MM.
         Two systems: SN2 in 20 explicit acetones; and KSI active-site design from
         crystal structure 1OH0 with ALL WATERS REMOVED and 17 capped side chains.
         Fitness has THREE terms here, not D2018's five.

Claeyssens 2011 (doi 10.1039/c0ob00691b) is the Phase 1 primary comparator.
Claeyssens 2005 (doi 10.1039/b508181e) is the comparator ONLY for the enzyme-only
barrier-versus-stabilisation regression. See 00_crosscutting/comparator_decision.txt
in consolidated_works.

1. THE GOAL, RESTATED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Catalyse the chorismate-to-prephenate Claisen rearrangement by placing charges in
the ABSENCE of the enzyme - what [D2018] achieve for the Menshutkin SN2 - but with
a NON-BLACK-BOX optimiser. [D2018] and [B2021] both use a genetic algorithm: no
optimality guarantee, ten independent runs to gain confidence, and in [D2018] a
database of 5388 solutions clustered after the fact to understand the answer.

The novelty claim is therefore: SAME PHYSICS, PROVABLE OPTIMUM. Not a different
approximation with a provable optimum.

2. WHAT CHANGES FROM PHASE 2 / 2b, AND WHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 THE DIFFERENCE-POTENTIAL MAP IS REBUILT ON POST-CUT FRAMES
    The committed dv_grid_ensemble_mean.tsv states its own provenance in its
    header: 10 frames, 02450 04085 09025 09900 10775 11630 12485 14155 17505
    19185. ALL TEN are below the 20,000 ps equilibration cut, all ran at the 400 kJ
    spring, and Phase 1's finalised position is that the pilot set must not be used
    for reported results. 28 post-cut frames now have barriers.
    -> the map, the grid and the B6 frame-sensitivity conclusion all rebuild.

2.2 NO CONTINUUM ALONGSIDE THE CHARGES
    Phase 2 as it stood used point charges PLUS CPCM eps = 4. That matches neither
    published setting: [D2018] uses COSMO only to DEFINE the target path, never
    alongside the charges; [B2021] uses explicit molecules and no continuum at all.
    A continuum next to the charges supplies the same electrostatic stabilisation
    the charges are being optimised to provide.
    -> charges only. A continuum may define the target, as [D2018] do.

2.3 SMEARED CHARGES, NOT POINT CHARGES         <-- the main physical change
    [DTHESIS] Ch. 3.6 states the failure mode in its own words:
      "by using a vdW surface for the point charge entities, Coulomb implosion is
       an issue due to the non-convexity around the singularity of the charge
       center without any other repulsions such as dispersion interactions of real
       atoms"
    and in its footnote, that without compensating fields "even stationary points
    of higher order are possible".
    Our own step_7_relaxed_validation_and_point_charge_collapse.md reproduces this.
    It is a published pathology of the model, not a bug in our implementation.
    [DTHESIS] MANAGES it by rejection - gradient-norm thresholds and angle checks
    that detect and discard imploded solutions.
    -> we REMOVE IT AT SOURCE by replacing the point charge with a finite-width
       (Gaussian-smeared) charge distribution. This is a physical improvement on
       the source model, it is defensible from first principles, and it makes the
       constraint set smoother, which helps a MILP rather than hurting it.
    RISK, to be tested not assumed: smearing reduces the attainable field gradient
       near the surface. Too wide and the designed effect is lost. The width must
       be calibrated against something physical - see section 4.

2.4 A CHEAP METHOD FOR THE DESIGN LOOP, BENCHMARKED NOT ASSUMED
    [B2021] run GFN-xTB inside the loop and justify it as: "As long as the relative
    energies are accurate enough between different solution candidates, our global
    optimization scheme will be able to find optima that will be transferable to
    higher quality ab initio calculations."
    That warrant is about RELATIVE energies, so it must be tested on ours.
    The literature is specifically unfavourable for this reaction. Claeyssens 2011
    p.10: "both AM1 and HF overestimate the reaction barrier severely, while
    standard non-hybrid density functional techniques (e.g. PBE and SCC-DFTB)
    underestimate the reaction barrier". p.11: AM1 "overestimates substrate
    compression in chorismate mutase" - a GEOMETRY error. Woodcock's SCC-DFTB
    barrier is 6.0 against Claeyssens' 11.3 at B3LYP. GFN-xTB is in the same
    tight-binding family.
    -> s10_cheap_method_benchmark.py tests XTB2, HF-3c, B97-3c and r2SCAN-3c
       against the committed barrier_vac column, frame by frame, plus a field-
       response test. Verdict rules were fixed before running. PBS 414079.

2.5 SEPARATION CONSTRAINT REVISITED
    [DTHESIS] Ch. 2 gives their typical values: charge bounds [-1, +1] e and a
    minimum charge-charge separation r_min = 1 Angstrom.
    Phase 2 used 3.5 Angstrom, which is 3.5x tighter, and that is why "can two
    charges even be placed in a 4-5 Angstrom favourable region?" became a problem.
    The constraint causing the difficulty is ours, not inherited.
    -> revisit. A smeared charge has a physical width that can set r_min instead
       of an arbitrary choice.

3. WHAT STAYS THE SAME, AND WHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 THE SURFACE IS THE UNION OF ATOM-CENTRED vdW SPHERES
    [DTHESIS] Ch. 2, Eq. 2.16: each charge satisfies
        || r_i - R_{J,Voronoi} ||_2 - d_J = 0
    where d_J are ATOM-DEPENDENT vdW radii. No probe rolling, no solvent-accessible
    offset, no uniform shell. Assignment is Voronoi-like: the atom minimising
    (||r_i - R_J|| - d_J). A charge inside any sphere is projected back onto it.
    The surface encloses ALL PATH FRAMES at once - "common vdW surface exposed by
    all atoms of all frames" - not one geometry.
    -> keep. One layer, as agreed.

3.2 GOCAT'S PER-FRAME VALIDITY TERMS
    [D2018] uses five: minimal barrier; TS stabilised versus the reference; no new
    intermediate minima; a penalty if R, TS or P shifts more than 2 frames; and
    gradient norms at R, TS and P below threshold. They are explicit that without
    the fifth, "the reaction profile could be transformed into any arbitrary path
    ... even into an equi-potential contour line, with no barrier at all".
    [B2021] simplifies to three: RMS gradient at R/TS/P; sum of all barriers in the
    path; and sum of RMSD between frames, favouring pre-organised candidates.
    -> adopt. Note which source each term comes from when writing up.
    NOTE: the gradient-norm term exists BECAUSE the path is fixed in [D2018].
    [DTHESIS] Ch. 3.6 says a full path relaxation "would eliminate the need for
    additional gradient thresholds". If we ever relax the path, that term changes
    meaning.

3.3 ARITHMETIC MEAN PENALISED BY SPREAD, ACROSS FRAMES
    Ryde (2016) records the consensus for combining snapshot energies as an
    exponential average, since the rate depends on exp(-dG/RT). At our barrier
    spread that average is dominated by a single frame and is badly conditioned.
    Dutta Dubey, Stuyver, Kalita & Shaik (jacs.9b13029) show solvent organises
    against an applied field and screens it in proportion to polarity, so a design
    that works in some conformers and not others is screened away where it is
    weakest. Robustness across conformers is a condition of surviving solvation,
    not a refinement.
    -> mean penalised by spread. State it as a considered choice.

4. THE DESIGN TARGET, AND THE CALIBRATION IT GIVES US
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Burschowsky et al., PNAS 2014 (pnas.1408512111, edited by Warshel) replaced Arg90
in B. subtilis chorismate mutase with CITRULLINE: isosteric, but the cationic
guanidinium becomes a neutral urea. Their result:

    "the positively charged arginine contributes up to 5.9 kcal/mol to transition
     state stabilization but only 0.6 kcal/mol to the binding energy of the ground
     state"

with the citrulline NH2 3.2 Angstrom from the ether oxygen of the TS analogue.

Against our own measurement: mean stab_TS = -4.8 kcal/mol over the frames with
both a barrier and an in vacuo barrier. ONE cationic centre at 3.2 Angstrom
accounts for more than the entire measured stabilisation.

Three consequences:
  - The target is not a subtle distributed field. It is approximately ONE positive
    charge about 3 Angstrom from the ether oxygen, with a roughly 10:1 differential
    between transition state and reactant.
  - It is a BENCHMARK: 5.9 kcal/mol is what one well-placed cation achieves in a
    real enzyme.
  - It is a CALIBRATION for the smearing width of section 2.3: choose the width so
    that a single smeared +1 at 3.2 Angstrom from the ether oxygen reproduces
    5.9 kcal/mol of differential stabilisation. That ties our charge model to a
    measured experimental result instead of an arbitrary parameter.

5. NUMBER OF CHARGES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[D2018] scanned N_Ch and report: "We have tried several different N_Ch values but
present here only the N_Ch = 10 size. This is already a converged end point of a
general trend, starting at N_Ch = 1, reaching N_Ch = 3 (SI), and not changing
qualitatively at N_Ch = 20 (not shown)." They also ran a full N_Ch = 1 case.

They further observe that at N_Ch = 10 the solution database "might even be reduced
to one (essential) dimension (due to gauge freedom)", i.e. extra charges buy
alternative arrangements of the SAME field, not new physics.

-> scan N_Ch = 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 and stop when the objective stops improving. Start at
   1, because Burschowsky makes it the physically motivated case and because
   recovering the Arg90 position from a one-charge optimisation would be a
   validation obtainable no other way.

-> GAUGE FREEDOM IS AN OPPORTUNITY FOR A CERTIFIED OPTIMISER. If many arrangements
   give the same field, the optimum is degenerate. A GA returns whichever member it
   lands on; [D2018] had to cluster 5388 solutions by MDS to discover the
   degeneracy after the fact. A MILP can CHARACTERISE the optimal set directly -
   solve, add a no-good cut, re-solve, enumerate. That is a stronger statement than
   "the GA found this one", and it falls out of the certificate rather than being
   bolted on.

7. OPEN QUESTIONS NOT YET SETTLED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - FIXED PATH OR RELAXED PATH. [D2018] fixes it and needs gradient thresholds.
    [DTHESIS] Ch. 7 relaxes it (Algorithm 3.2) on a Diels-Alder system. [B2021]
    recomputes the MEP every fitness call. A MILP over a fixed dv map is the
    [D2018] setting. Whether an outer loop - solve the certified inner problem,
    recompute the MEP, rebuild the map, repeat - is worth building is undecided.
    That would be sequential linearisation with a certified inner step.
  - EXPLICIT WATER. [B2021] remove all waters for the enzyme case and name it as a
    deficiency of their own work, wanting "the inclusion of explicit water
    molecules". Phase 1 has 139 mobile waters in the active region. Whether the
    design environment includes them is undecided.
  - TARGET REFERENCE. [D2018] use COSMO(water) to define the target the charges
    must reproduce. Whether our target is gas-phase-referenced or water-referenced
    is undecided.
  - PRECISION FLOOR. The standard error on the mean barrier sets the smallest
    design effect separable from conformational noise. Recompute on the post-cut
    frames before claiming any effect.
