Stage 4e of the charged-sphere development: does the sign of the barrier change beside the carboxylates depend on
the electronic-structure method? (pre-registered 10 October 2026, committed before the inputs are generated and
before any run; plan: PHASE22_PLAN_UPDATE_20261009.md, "Update: Stage 4e"). This is the re-examination that Stage
4d's PC2 failure requires (STAGE4D_CRITERIA.txt). Generator: cs4e_prepare.py (on the PC, from committed files only);
analysis: cs4e_analyse.py.

Why. At B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, a cation at Arg7's place (beside the enolpyruvyl carboxylate, O1/O2) or at Arg63''s
place (beside the ring carboxylate, O5/O6) raises the barrier: Arg7 alone +2.57 kcal/mol, Arg63' alone +5.98 (QM
part, Stage 4d), and the first-order field at the nearest grid sites is positive in 29-30 of 30 frames
(STAGE4D_EXPLORATORY.txt). Szefczyk et al. 2004 find both residues barrier-lowering at MP2/6-31G(d), Arg7 at every
level they report (point-charge potential to MP2), on an RHF/6-31G(d)/CHARMM22 structure. Stage 4d ruled out the
residues' places, the frozen environment and frame 41786 alone as the cause; the method is untested. The design's
field (the A matrix) is built at B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, so its sign beside the carboxylates decides where an optimiser
puts anions and cations.

Design. Frame 41786's reactant and TS, fixed, in their own (Phase 1) coordinates, exactly as Stage 4d. Five levels:
  L1  B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, def2/J, RIJCOSX   the production level; its inputs are byte-identical to Stage 4d's
  L2  B3LYP/6-31G(d)                          Claeyssens et al. 2005's level
  L3  HF/6-31G(d)                             Szefczyk et al. 2004's wavefunction level
  L4  MP2/6-31G(d) (frozen core)              Szefczyk et al. 2004's reference level - the reference here
  L5  wB97X-D3/def2-SVP, def2/J, RIJCOSX      a second functional family (range-separated hybrid; keyword WB97X-D3)
All TightSCF, ORCA 6.0.1. ORCA uses pure d functions for every basis, including 6-31G(d), which was defined with
Cartesian d (ORCA 6.0 manual); this does not bear on signs. Dispersion acts between QM atoms only, so it cancels from
every quantity below.
At each level, 34 single points: the bare substrate (R, TS); each of Stage 4d's six residues alone, with Stage 4d's
point charges unchanged (Arg90, Arg7, Glu78, Arg116, Arg63', Lys60'; R, TS); and a +1 and a -1 probe charge at five
grid_v2 sites, taken back to frame 41786's own coordinates: 37 (nearest Arg90's charge centre), 52 and 87 (nearest
Arg7's), 209 and 211 (nearest Arg63''s) (R, TS). 170 runs.
Quantities. ddE(X) = [E_TS(X) - E_TS(bare)] - [E_R(X) - E_R(bare)] x 627.5094740631, QM energy, kcal/mol, negative
lowers the barrier. For a probe site, the first-order part a = [ddE(+1) - ddE(-1)]/2 (what reverses with the charge;
the quantity the A matrix holds) and the polarisation part [ddE(+1) + ddE(-1)]/2. The steric (LJ) terms are those
of Stage 4d and do not depend on the level; totals are reported for the residues.
  carboxylate set C: Arg7 alone, Arg63' alone, a(52), a(87), a(209), a(211)
  control set K:     Arg90 alone, a(37) - barrier-lowering in every model so far and in the literature
  informative:       Glu78, Arg116, Lys60' alone; the polarisation parts

Checks that must pass for the stage to be assessable:
  check 0  all 170 runs terminate normally; 118 electrons; one basis-function count per level; the number of point
           charges ORCA reads equals runs.tsv's.
  R0       L1's bare and residue-alone energies equal Stage 4d's (same inputs) within 1e-6 Eh.
  controls K negative at L4 (the reference). If not, the stage is not assessable.

Verdict (L1 against L4, sign compared wherever |L4| >= 0.5 kcal/mol, as in Stage 4c):
  SIGN ROBUST       every C and K value at L1 has L4's sign. The sign beside the carboxylates does not follow the
                    method at this geometry; Stage 5 builds the A matrix at L1, and the disagreement with Szefczyk
                    et al. is recorded as structural (their structure against ours), not resolved.
  METHOD-DEPENDENT  some C or K value at L1 has the opposite sign to L4's. L1 is not used to build the A matrix. Stage
                    5 builds it at the DFT level (L2 or L5) whose C and K values all have L4's sign and whose fit to
                    L4 over all eleven values (six residues alone, five first-order parts) has the smallest residual
                    (fit L4 = k x level through the origin); if neither does, from MP2 densities. Every Stage 4c and
                    4d value beside a carboxylate is then marked as level-dependent.
  Any level whose K values are not both negative is reported and is not eligible.
Informative, not verdicts: k and residual of each level against L4; L3 against L4 (does correlation change the
signs?); L3's signs for the six residues against Szefczyk et al.'s HF-level values (Table 1, dSCF) - if HF at this
geometry disagrees with their HF at theirs, the structures differ; L1's first-order parts against A_v2's a for frame
41786 at the same sites (the A matrix's own values); HOMO-LUMO gaps (rule 3 of STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1.txt: a run below
1.0 eV is flagged, and every value that uses it is unresolved and left out of the sign comparison).
Scope: one frame, fixed geometries, vacuum, five levels with modest basis sets. It decides which level builds the
A matrix; it does not by itself say which structure, ours or Szefczyk et al.'s, is right.

References: Szefczyk, Mulholland, Ranaghan & Sokalski, J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2004, 126, 16148 (doi 10.1021/ja049376t);
Claeyssens, Ranaghan, Manby, Harvey & Mulholland, Chem. Commun. 2005, 5068 (doi 10.1039/b508181e); ORCA 6.0
manual, basis sets section (pure d and f functions); wB97X-D3: Lin, Li, Mao & Chai, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2013,
9, 263 (doi 10.1021/ct300715s).
