Stage 4d of the charged-sphere development: positive and negative controls for the design quantity
(pre-registered 10 October 2026, committed before the inputs are generated and before any run; plan:
PHASE22_PLAN_UPDATE_20261009.md, "Update: Stage 4d"). Generator: cs4d_prepare.py (runs on hpc1, from the
Phase 1 files of frame 41786, each checked against its sha256 in the results repo); analysis: cs4d_analyse.py.

Why. Every design number so far is ddE: the change in barrier that fixed charges cause at the fixed reactant and TS
of frame 41786, from the QM energy with the steric (LJ) term beside it. The sizes are large: one +1 sphere at
site 117 gives -9.3 kcal/mol with LJ, three spheres (E5) -32.3, while the whole enzyme at this frame stabilises the
TS by only -0.8 (Phase 1, stab_TS) and by -5.0 +- 4.2 over 43 frames (Claeyssens et al. 2005: 4.2 on average for
the same quantity). Nothing so far has applied the design calculation to the real enzyme. This stage does: it
measures the enzyme, single residues, and spheres standing in for residues, all with the design calculation, and
compares them with Phase 1 and the literature. It tests the yardstick; it does not test any design.

Method (as Stage 4c L1). B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, def2/J, RIJCOSX, TightSCF; QM = the chorismate dianion (24 atoms,
-2, singlet). The environment enters as fixed point charges (%pointcharges), which reproduce the QM part of ORCA
QM/MM to 1e-12 Eh (Stage 4b). Frame 41786 in its own (Phase 1) coordinates, not the aligned frame.
  substrate R  = 05_qmmm/20_invacuo/41786_R.xyz  (NEB image 0)
  substrate TS = 05_qmmm/20_invacuo/41786_TS.xyz (NEB climbing image)
  env_R  = every other atom of 19_ensemble/frame_41786/reactant.pdb (the NEB's image 0; coordinates to 0.001 A)
  env_TS = every other atom of 19_ensemble/frame_41786/neb_NEB-CI_converged.xyz (the climbing image, full system)
  charges and LJ parameters = 19_ensemble/frame_41786/complex_solvated.ORCAFF.prms (the Phase 1 force field)
Quantity, per environment X: ddE_QM(X) = [E_TS(X) - E_TS(bare)] - [E_R(X) - E_R(bare)] x 627.5094740631;
dE_LJ(X) = LJ(TS) - LJ(R) between the substrate and X's atoms, computed classically with ORCA's rule (WP1: rule A,
R_ij = (R_i + R_j)/2, eps_ij = sqrt(eps_i eps_j), E = eps_ij[(R_ij/r)^12 - 2(R_ij/r)^6]) over all pairs, no
cutoff; total = ddE_QM + dE_LJ (reporting commitment 2). Negative lowers the barrier. A sphere is a +1 point
charge with Amber N's LJ (R_min 3.648, eps 0.170), as in Stages 1-4c.

Runs (42; runs.tsv lists each with its environment and expected number of point charges):
  bare             no environment (R, TS)
  NC0              env_R atoms, every charge 0
  ENZ              the whole enzyme as point charges, env_R
  ENZTS            the whole enzyme, env_TS
  REV              the whole enzyme with every charge reversed, env_R
  KO_<res>         the whole enzyme with one residue's charges set to 0 (its LJ kept), env_R
  ALONE_<res>      that residue's charges only, env_R
  SPH_R90CZ        one +1 at Arg90's CZ (its guanidinium charge centre: WP4, 0.11-0.19 A away)
  SPH_R7CZ         one +1 at Arg7's CZ
  SPH_G37          one +1 at grid_v2 site 37, the design-grid site nearest Arg90's CZ (0.65 A), at contact distance
  SUB_R90          the whole enzyme with Arg90's charges replaced by one +1 at its CZ (Arg90's LJ kept)
  <res> = R90, R7, E78, R116 (chain B, residues 217, 134, 205, 243 in the system's numbering) and R63p, K60p
  (Arg63', Lys60' of chain A, residues 63, 60): the residues Szefczyk et al. 2004 analyse that carry a formal
  charge. The generator stops unless each has the expected name, a whole-residue charge of +1 or -1 and an
  atom within 10 A of the substrate. Residues are whole (backbone included); Szefczyk et al. used side-chain
  fragments.

Checks that must pass for the stage to be assessable (machinery):
  check 0  all 42 runs terminate normally; 118 electrons; 264 basis functions; the number of point charges ORCA
           reads equals runs.tsv's.
  R0a      bare R and TS reproduce Phase 1's in vacuo single points (20_invacuo/sp_41786_R.out -836.142634941140,
           sp_41786_TS.out -836.116074935829; same method, settings and geometry file) within 1e-6 Eh.
  R0b      ENZ's R reproduces Phase 1's QM part of the QM/MM reactant (19_ensemble/frame_41786/reactant_opt.out,
           -836.914536233376) within 5e-4 Eh. The tolerance allows for reactant.pdb's 0.001 A rounding of every
           coordinate (estimated effect about 5e-5 Eh); a wrong charge set or a misplaced atom misses by far more.
  NC0      NC0's R and TS equal bare's within 1e-6 Eh: point charges of zero change nothing.
If any fails, the stage is not assessable and the cause is found before anything else uses it.

Controls with verdicts (thresholds fixed now):
  PC1  The whole enzyme, measured with the design yardstick. PASS if ENZ's total lies within the Phase 1 ensemble's
       stab_TS mean +- 2 sd, [-13.3, +3.3] kcal/mol (43 frames, 05_qmmm/19_ensemble_barriers/ensemble_barriers.tsv
       at results commit 5fa30a2: -4.99 +- 4.15). Reported beside it: ENZTS, the "own environment" value (R in
       env_R, TS in env_TS) against frame 41786's stab_TS (-0.81; the difference is mainly the protein's own MM
       energy change, which point charges do not contain), and Claeyssens et al.'s 4.2.
  PC2  Single residues, measured with the design yardstick, against Szefczyk et al. 2004 (Table 1, residue-TS and
       residue-substrate pairs cut from a QM/MM model of the same enzyme). PASS if ALONE's total has Szefczyk's
       sign for each residue whose sign is the same at all seven of their levels (point-charge potential to MP2):
       Arg90, Arg7, Arg116 negative (catalytic), Lys60' positive (anti-catalytic). Glu78 is reported but not
       judged: its value changes sign with the orientation of the substrate's hydroxyl (their Tables 2-3, -8.70 to
       +7.33). Arg63' is reported but not judged: its sign differs between their levels. A value within 0.05
       kcal/mol of zero counts as a mismatch. Magnitudes are compared, not judged: their structures, method and
       side-chain fragments differ from ours.
  PC3  A sphere standing in for a residue. PASS if SPH_R90CZ and SPH_R7CZ each have the sign of ALONE for the same
       residue (QM part). Recorded, not judged, and carried forward as the stated representation scale:
         r90 = SPH_R90CZ / ALONE_R90 and r7 = SPH_R7CZ / ALONE_R7 (QM part): a point charge against the
               residue's own distributed charges at the same place;
         g   = SPH_G37 / SPH_R90CZ (QM part): moving the sphere from Arg90's place to the grid at contact distance;
         SUB_R90 - ENZ (QM part): the error of replacing one residue by a sphere inside the enzyme.
  NC1  REV (informative): the odd part (ENZ - REV)/2 is what reverses with the charges (first order); the even part
       (ENZ + REV)/2 is polarisation, which does not. Their sizes show how much of the enzyme's effect a
       first-order surrogate would miss.

Consequences (they change how numbers are used, never a threshold already fixed):
  machinery check fails        stop; the cause is found before Stage 5 or the optimiser use frame 41786's yardstick.
  PC1 PASS                     the frozen-geometry yardstick gives the enzyme an enzyme-sized number. The enzyme's
                               own frozen value (ENZ total) becomes the screening reference that designs are
                               compared with; the catalytic verdict stays with the relaxed NEB-CI barrier against
                               D5's reference 2 (reporting commitments 1 and 3).
  PC1 FAIL                     the frozen yardstick does not measure even the enzyme realistically: frozen values
                               rank designs only and are never compared with an enzyme number.
  PC2 FAIL                     the yardstick misjudges the sign of a residue's effect; the design objective is
                               re-examined before the optimiser stage.
  PC3 FAIL                     a sphere does not even keep the sign of the residue it stands for; the sphere
                               representation is re-examined before Stage 5.
  PC3 ratios                   quoted beside every design magnitude from here on.

Electronic screen (rule 3 of STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1.txt): every run's HOMO-LUMO gap and largest shift of a substrate
Hirshfeld charge against bare are reported; a run with a gap below 1.0 eV is flagged and its values are unresolved.
Scope: one frame (41786, untouched by C4 and C5), frozen geometries, def2-SVP only. It measures the yardstick on
known environments; it says nothing about whether any design is catalytic.

References: Claeyssens, Ranaghan, Manby, Harvey & Mulholland, Chem. Commun. 2005, 5068 (doi 10.1039/b508181e);
Szefczyk, Mulholland, Ranaghan & Sokalski, J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2004, 126, 16148 (doi 10.1021/ja049376t);
Burschowsky et al., PNAS 2014, 111, 17516 (doi 10.1073/pnas.1408512111), Arg90 "up to 5.9 kcal/mol" of TS
stabilisation in water (a free energy; informative only).
