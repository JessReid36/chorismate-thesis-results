Two things to keep in mind when reading TIME_DEV.txt, neither of them a fault in the code:

1. ROWS MARKED 'lp' CARRY A GAP, AND AT LARGE n IT EXCEEDS THE GAIN. Rows marked 'full' are exact (gap 0). Where
   the full search cannot close the gap within its probe time, the row falls back to the LP-guided search and
   reports the gap against the whole grid's bound. Where that happens depends on the scipy build: in Claude's
   workspace (scipy 1.18.1) the full search was exact to n = 9; on scipy 1.11.4 it was exact to n = 6, and n = 7-9
   came back 'lp' with the same objectives and gaps of 0.012-0.086. At B = 2.0 the gap grows to about 0.49 kcal/mol
   by n = 20, while the improvement from one n to the next falls to about 0.08. Consecutive n are therefore NOT
   separable at those sizes: n = 20 is not "better than" n = 19 by 0.08 when each carries a gap of about 0.5.
   Decision O6's stopping rule asks for a gain beating both the frame spread and the method uncertainty, and the
   frame spread alone (the semi-deviation is about 1.6 here) should stop the sweep in the single digits. Where the
   comparison falls on 'lp' rows, the gaps are part of it and are stated.
2. TIMINGS DO NOT CARRY ACROSS SCIPY VERSIONS. The same LP-guided solve at n = 12 took 78 s under scipy 1.18.1 and
   377 s under scipy 1.11.4. The designs were identical in every digit; only the time differed. The timings in this
   file are this PC's, with the version named above.
