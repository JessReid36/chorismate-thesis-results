Stage 5 development build - exploratory, not pre-registered, not a result (19 October 2026)

What this is. The Stage 5 code (cs5_floors.py, cs5_grid.py, cs5_align.py, cs5_amatrix.py, cs5_report.py) run and
tested before STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt was written, on inputs that are not Stage 5's production inputs: the 30 Phase 1 band
frames (R, TS and P of 05_qmmm/20_invacuo) as s16_align_frames.py aligned them onto frame 20000's reactant
(phase2.2/aligned/). Its purposes: test the code, show what each floor rule does, and give the optimiser stage a grid
and L to develop against before the SP package ends. Its sites and L are not used for any reported design.

THE DROP RULE, CHANGED BEFORE THE CRITERIA WERE WRITTEN
  First version: drop every cation-layer site that clears the q <= 0 floors ("the anion/neutral layer holds it").
  That dropped 360 sites: 83 on the anion/neutral surface (duplicates at the grid's resolution) and 277 beside H8,
  outside that surface by up to 1.16 A, on the +1 floor of H8 (3.68 A, the closest a +1 sphere resting at an oxygen
  came to H8), where the anion/neutral surface lies nearer (its H8 floor is 2.331 A; its sites there lie 2.52 A or
  more from H8) and admits no positive charge; 266 of the 277 were more than 0.5 A from any anion/neutral site that
  admits a positive charge. So cations had no place beside the
  hydroxyl. Rule adopted: drop a cation site only if it lies on the anion/neutral surface (clearance to the q <= 0
  floors zero within 1e-6 A) and an anion/neutral site within 0.5 A admits every charge it admits. Here all 83
  on-surface sites meet the second condition (the nearest anion/neutral site is within 0.496 A).

RADIAL TEST (cs5_grid.py check; Stage 2's rigid model: Coulomb in vacuum with the substrate's charges, plus LJ)
  At every site that admits a positive charge, the force on the sphere along the site's outward normal, at all 90
  geometries: for the lowest admitted positive charge and for +1, it pushes the sphere outward at 10 of 2552 sites
  (largest 6.21 kcal/mol/A; radial minimum at most 0.04 A farther out); everywhere else the dianion draws it inward,
  so a sphere held on that line would come at least as near as the site. Beside H8, at the 217 anion/neutral sites
  that admit no positive charge (2.52-3.68 A from H8), a +0.25 sphere is drawn inward at every one, a +1 sphere at all
  but one: the H8 floors for positive charges keep cations out of a region the rigid model would let them reach.
