Stage 5 of the charged-sphere development: the design grid, the spheres' Lennard-Jones term L and the A matrix, on
Phase 1's final geometries (pre-registered 19 October 2026; committed before any Stage 5 production input exists and
before any SP package result other than frame 42436's (smoke test 2, already in SP_AMENDMENT2.txt) is read; plan:
PHASE22_PLAN_UPDATE_20261009.md, section 5, "Stage 5", with the update appended on this date). Code (tier1-realism,
phase2.2/): cs5_floors.py, cs5_grid.py, cs5_align.py, cs5_amatrix.py, cs5_report.py (sha256 at the end). This file was
checked by its author in three passes and by an independent reviewer before commit; the reviewer's findings were
addressed before commit.

WHAT STAGE 5 PRODUCES
The inputs of the optimiser stage, for every complete frame f of the SP package and every grid site i:
  - the site's position (aligned frame of reference), layer and the charges it admits (sites.tsv);
  - L[i,f], the sphere's Lennard-Jones energy at the TS minus at the reactant, kcal/mol (L.tsv);
  - a[i,f] = V_TS,f(r_i) - V_R,f(r_i), the first-order barrier change per unit charge, kcal/mol per e (A.tsv); and
    ap[i,f] = V_P,f(r_i) - V_R,f(r_i) (AP.tsv), kept for the optimiser stage's open question on path terms and not
    used here.
For a design with charges q_i on occupied sites, the surrogate barrier change of frame f is
    ddE_f = sum_i ( a[i,f] q_i + L[i,f] )   (+ sum_i b_i q_i^2, the polarisation term, left to the optimiser stage)
(approach document, "How a sphere's energy enters the design"; PHASE22_PLAN_UPDATE_20261009.md section 2). This is
Sokalski's first-order model with a repulsive term: "A = E_EL,MTP(1) + E_EX(1)" (Sokalski 1985, Eq. 17, p. 404), whose
electrostatic part for point charges is "A = sum_C q_C (V_TC - V_SC)" (Eq. 19, p. 404). Here the Lennard-Jones sphere
stands for the exchange repulsion (and adds dispersion). The design and validation decisions themselves (budget,
number of spheres, spread penalty, preorganisation term, re-scoring, null distribution) belong to the optimiser stage
and are not fixed here.

WHEN IT RUNS
Production starts only when spa_package.py's FINAL output (SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.9: nothing undecided) states that the
Phase 1 barrier conditions are met (A2.3: at least 20 complete frames, the surroundings check passed, every record
agrees with its output). cs5_align.py extract enforces this: it takes that run's saved report and stops unless it
reads "RESULT: FINAL (nothing undecided)" and "THE PHASE 1 BARRIER (A2.3; audit item C3): the conditions are met",
and records the report's and the table's sha256. If they are not met, Stage 5 waits for the amendment that follows
(A2.3, A2.4), because remedy (a) of A2.4 would change the geometries. Nothing in this file uses a package result.

2. ALIGNMENT (cs5_align.py align; as s16_align_frames.py, D3)
One rotation and translation per frame, fitted (Kabsch, reflection-guarded; s16's kabsch() unchanged) on the rigid
core (C2, O3 and the six ring carbons; indices 3, 7, 8, 10, 12, 13, 15, 17) of the frame's R onto the reference R,

frames before building the surface ("All frames are maximally compact or aligned for the reaction path", p. 10).

3. FLOORS (fixed now: cs_stage5/floors.tsv, from cs5_floors.py, minima=all)
How close a sphere (Amber N, R* 1.824 A, eps 0.170 kcal/mol) of charge q may sit to substrate atom J:
  q >= +0.25, per level 0.25/0.50/0.75/1.00: min(LJ contact, the closest approach to J of any Stage 2 minimum at that
       level) over Stage 2's 240 minima per level (four oxygen starts x 30 frames x R and TS), own basin and migrated:
       each is a place where a sphere of that charge rests (gradient below 1e-5 kcal/mol/A). Made monotone in q (a
       larger charge never has a larger floor; 3 entries raised). Rule B and "the smallest distance seen" are
       Jessica's decisions of 19 October 2026. A charge between levels takes the lower level's floors. A floor is one
       atom's closest approach, applied around that atom in every direction (section 4 tests what that allows).
  0 < q < 0.25: the LJ contact (no Stage 2 data), with H8 at the +0.25 level.
  q <= 0: the LJ contact. Stage 2 found no resting place for a negative sphere (1920 of 1920 starts escaped);
       STAGE2_PROTOCOL.txt: "where a sign of charge has no contact minimum, the floor for it comes from the
       Lennard-Jones pair minimum and Stage 3". H8 (GAFF ho) has no LJ: its q <= 0 floor is Stage 3's smallest
       H8-sphere distance, 2.331 A (n1_41786_qm1, a -1 sphere in QM/MM, no artefact).
  H8 for q > 0: the closest approach of positive minima, 3.68 A (+1) to 4.02 A (+0.25). These spheres rest at oxygens
       (O4 and the carboxylates), so the values are distances seen from there, not a limit set by H8; Stage 2's rigid
       model draws a +0.25 sphere inward at every one of the 217 anion/neutral sites beside H8 that therefore admit no
       positive charge (2.52-3.68 A from H8; at +1, all but one). The floor jumps at q = 0 (2.331 A below, 4.016 A
       above), and some sites admit two separate ranges, e.g. q in [-1, 0] and [0.5, +1]; the optimiser must allow
       that (section 4, sites file columns q_min_neg and q_min_pos).
  LJ contact = R_min,ij = (R_min,sphere + R_min,J)/2 on the full R_min (ORCA's rule; Stage 1 S1.4), i.e. R*_sphere +
       R*_J, the sum of the two van der Waals radii.
Migrated minima set 8 of the 24 +1 floors (floors.tsv, column closest_p100_at; H7's listed minimum lies at its LJ
contact). STAGE2_REPRODUCIBILITY.txt (x46) described "own-basin rows, which are the distances the report and the grid
floor use", written when the floor was to be the approach document's contact distance by atom type; rule B replaced
that rule, and this is a deviation from that statement (section 9 (vi)). With own-basin minima only (cs5_floors.py
minima=own), the floors that change are (+1 / +0.25 level, A; H1's does not): O3 3.508 / 3.508 against 2.745 / 3.036;
C2 3.300 / 3.679 against 3.050 / 3.234; H4 3.283 / 3.283 against 3.054 / 3.283; O2 2.638 / 2.932 against 2.574 /
2.887; H5 2.540 / 2.659 against 2.441 / 2.618; H2 2.481 / 2.606 against 2.435 / 2.593; O6 2.607 / 2.900 against 2.588
/ 2.900. O3, the ether oxygen whose bond breaks, has no own-basin +1 minimum at all (every ether start migrates, WP5
and Stage 2); its 2.745 A comes from a +1 sphere started at O3 in frame 48292's TS that came to rest 2.745 A from it.
cs5_floors.py recomputes Stage 2's minima with Stage 2's own code and stops unless every one reproduces its
cs2_contacts.tsv row: the same outcome, contact atom and nearest atom, and the contact, nearest and H8 distances
within 1e-3 A for own-basin and migrated minima alike (x46's criterion, extended to the migrated minima because they
set floors here). 960 of 960 do, all within 5e-5 A (the table's rounding). A rerun gives floors.tsv byte for byte.
The floors were measured on the 30 Phase 1 band frames and are applied, per atom, to the package's geometries. Between
frames the +1 contact distance varies with sd 0.034 A at carbA's oxygens (range 2.591-2.781 A) and 0.084 A at carbB's
(2.610-2.872 A) (WP5_RIGID_REPORT.txt, all 30 frames), and each floor is the smallest over 60 geometries, so it
already spans that variation; a package geometry whose own contact lies farther out admits sites nearer than its own
contact by up to about that spread (towards a lower barrier), checked radially on the production geometries (section
4) and guarded in validation by the screen "no sphere closer than its contact distance minus a tolerance" (approach
document, validation protocol). Stage 3's QM/MM contacts agreed with the rigid model within 0.07 A.

4. THE GRID (cs5_grid.py grid; then cs5_grid.py check)
Each layer is Dittner's surface (thesis, Eq. 2.16, p. 18: a charge on the sphere of radius d_J about one atom J,
outside every other; "a common vdW surface exposed by all atoms of all frames", p. 10; Dittner & Hartke 2018, p. 8:
"The GOCAT consists of partial charges placed on a van der Waals surface. This surfaces encloses all frames along the
whole reaction path"), with d_J the floor for the sphere's charge instead of a van der Waals radius. A sized, charged
sphere comes to a distance that depends on its charge (Behrens' thesis, section 5.2, p. 60: the quasi-atoms are
"locally optimized to their equilibrium distance"; Stage 2), so there are two layers, each one shell for its charges:
  ANION/NEUTRAL layer  radii = the q <= 0 floors (LJ contact);
  CATION layer         radii = the q = +1 floors: no nearer to atom J than any resting +1 sphere came (nearer than LJ
                       contact to every atom but H3 and H7; 3.68 A from H8).
A floor is applied around its atom in every direction, so the cation layer is an envelope: its sites are not all
places where a +1 sphere rests (those are at the oxygens). What it allows is tested radially, with Stage 2's rigid
model (Coulomb in vacuum with the substrate's charges, plus the LJ of section 5; wp5_rigid_lj_scan.py): at a site
admitting positive charges, the force on the sphere along the site's outward normal, at every geometry, for the lowest
admitted positive charge and for +1. An inward force means a sphere held on that line would come at least as near.
Development grid: pushed outward at 10 of 2552 such sites, whose radial minimum lies at most 0.04 A farther out; at
every other site and geometry the sphere is drawn inward (the dianion's -2 attracts it). Reported by cs5_grid.py check
on the production grid (informative: the rigid model has no polarisation or solvent).
Negative and neutral spheres at LJ contact rest nowhere (Stage 2); they are held there as Dittner's charges are held
on the vdW surface, and as fixed spheres are in validation (A2.5).
Union over every atom of every geometry: states R, TS and P of every complete frame, aligned. Per layer: candidate
points on a Fibonacci lattice, density = 48 points per A^2 of each atom's sphere; kept if outside every sphere of
every geometry; enclosed pockets removed by a flood fill from the box boundary (voxel vox = 0.25 A, box margin 6.0 A;
a point is kept if its voxel, 1.5 voxels out along its sphere's normal, is reached); greedy Poisson-disk thinning at
res = 0.5 A (Jessica, 19 October 2026) in a fixed order (frame by number, state R, TS, P; atom; lattice index); every
open candidate point then lies within res of a site of its layer. A cation-layer site that lies on the anion/neutral
surface (clearance to the q <= 0 floors zero within 1e-6 A) is dropped when an anion/neutral site within res admits
every charge it admits: there the two surfaces coincide and the anion/neutral layer holds that place at the same
resolution (so every candidate point lies within 2 res of a site admitting its charges). Cation sites inside that
surface (at the oxygens) and outside it (beside H8) are kept. Each site admits every charge whose floors it clears at
every geometry: q in [-1, 0] if it clears the q <= 0 floors; q in [q_min_pos, +1] for the smallest positive level
whose floors it clears; an empty site (no sphere, no L) is always allowed. The charge bounds [-1, +1] e are Dittner's
typical values (thesis p. 19: "[q_low, q_up] = [-1, +1] e"). Resolution and separation are separate
(GRID_DESIGN_DECISIONS.md at the repo root, section 1): the minimum distance between two spheres, 3.25 A (approach
document, Stage 5: sigma of two Amber N spheres, 3.648 A / 2^(1/6), the analogue of Behrens' sigma = 3.55 A threshold,
p. 60), is the optimiser's constraint, over both layers; it is not a grid parameter. cs5_grid.py check must PASS: it
re-derives, from the geometries and floors.tsv alone, every site's admitted charges, clearance, on_atom/on_state and
distance columns; checks every admitted charge clears its floors at every atom of every geometry; checks the frame set
and the sha256 of the geometries, floors and force field against the sites file's header; and that two sites of one
layer are at least res apart. cs5_grid.py lj and cs5_amatrix.py stop unless the aligned geometries are those the sites
file records (sha256).

5. L (cs5_grid.py lj)
L[i,f] = E_LJ(sphere at i, substrate at TS_f) - E_LJ(sphere at i, substrate at R_f), with the substrate's committed LJ
parameters (05_qmmm/13_bridge/complex_solvated.ORCAFF.prms, rows 6208-6231), combined as ORCA does (arithmetic mean
of R_min, geometric mean of eps; Stage 1 S1.4, WP1), E = eps_ij [(R_min,ij/r)^12 - 2 (R_min,ij/r)^6]. ORCA
force-switches LJ from 10 A, which below 10 A adds one constant per pair (WP1: about 1-4e-4 kcal/mol) that cancels in
TS - R; the header reports the summed |E_LJ| of pairs at 10 A or more, where the switch acts (development grid: at
most 0.003 kcal/mol per site). L enters every barrier
change quoted as a result (reporting commitment 2). Sphere-sphere terms are equal at R and TS (spheres fixed) and
cancel. cs5_grid.py ljcheck must PASS: the same formula against ORCA's own MM energies in Stage 4c's eight L1
arrangements (dE_LJ(L1), STAGE4C_REPORT.txt, 3 decimals), within 0.0015 kcal/mol (seen: 0.0005).

6. THE A MATRIX (cs5_amatrix.py prepare; stage5_a.pbs on hpc1; cs5_amatrix.py assemble)
New in vacuo single points of the bare dianion at every complete frame's aligned R, TS and P:
  ! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF / %maxcore 3000 / %pal nprocs 8 end / %scf MaxIter 300 end /
  * xyzfile -2 1 substrate.xyz   (as 05_qmmm/20_invacuo and s17_verify_amatrix.sh; ORCA 6.0.1)
then orca_vpot of each density at every site (points in Bohr with ORCA's factor, 1/0.5291772083). The level is the
production level that Stage 4e kept (SIGN ROBUST) and the package's QM level (A2.5). In vacuo densities, because the
design environment is the bare substrate plus spheres (s15_dv_matrix.py, point 3; D1). a = (V_TS - V_R) x
627.5094740631; a < 0: a positive charge there lowers the barrier (s15's convention, validated by s1_signcheck.py
and by Stage 4e's probes, whose first-order parts equal A_v2's within 4%).
Checks in assemble (all must PASS, or A.tsv is not written):
  (a) every run: ORCA TERMINATED NORMALLY, SCF CONVERGED, 118 electrons, echoed coordinates equal substrate.xyz
      (1e-5 A); every vpot.out holds one value per site at the points written (1e-6 Bohr), so the row order holds;
  (b) rigid-motion invariance: the frame with the largest alignment rotation is run again in its own coordinates with
      the sites taken back by the inverse transform; max |V_own - V_aligned| <= 1e-4 Eh per e (0.06 kcal/mol per e);
  (c) finite difference: at the first frame, at the sites nearest O3, O5 and O1 at R, ORCA point charges of +0.05
      and -0.05 e at R and TS give a_fd = [E_TS(+) - E_TS(-) - E_R(+) + E_R(-)] / 0.1 x 627.5094740631, which must
      equal a within 0.01 kcal/mol per e + 1% of |a| (Hellmann-Feynman; at 0.05 e the third-order part is about 1e-4
      of a, from Stage 4e's 3-4% at 1 e);
  (d) every input is unchanged since prepare (INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256); runs.tsv and row_order.tsv are what prepare
      writes for these geometries and sites; each run's substrate.xyz is the aligned geometry byte for byte (the
      invariance run's, its inverse transform within 1e-8 A); the sites file was built on these geometries.
A_REPORT.txt also tabulates each frame's in vacuo energies at R, TS and P and its in vacuo barrier E(TS) - E(R), for
the downstream quantities that need them (C4_CLOSURE.txt step 6: stab_TS).
About 3n + 13 single points of about 70 s each on 8 cores (Stage 4e's L1 runs), one PBS job (8 cores, 24 GB, 24 h).

7. IF A CHECK FAILS

is read. No threshold in this file is changed after the result it applies to has been seen.

9. DEVIATIONS FROM THE APPROACH DOCUMENT AND THE PLAN, each judged against the alternative
  (i) Geometries: the SP package's TS, R_IRC and P (C4_CLOSURE.txt step 6; A2.5) instead of the "C4/C5 geometries".
      C4 closed without step 3 and the package supersedes the band barriers; the design must be built on the
      geometries whose barriers it will be compared with. More likely to succeed: yes; there is no other final set.
  (ii) Two layers with floors by charge, instead of one floor "at the most strongly attracted charge for that atom
      type" (approach document, Stage 5) and instead of one layer (PHASE22_PLAN.txt 3.1: "keep. One layer, as agreed";
      PHASE22_PLAN_UPDATE_20261009.md section 1: "one shell to start"). Those statements were made for a surface of
      bare point charges and set one surface rather than several shells (Phase 2's 3, 4 and 5 A shells); here each
      layer is the first shell for its own charges. As worded, the approach document's rule puts every charge at the
      +1 distances: an anion 2.6 A from a carboxylate oxygen, a neutral sphere inside its LJ wall, where no such
      sphere comes (Stage 2: no negative sphere rests anywhere; weaker cations rest farther out, e.g. +0.25 at 2.85 A
      against +1 at 2.59 A from carbA's oxygen). Stage 2's protocol already sends negative charges to the LJ contact.
      Jessica chose two layers on 19 October 2026 ("so that we depend on the charge as we will be varying them", her
      message that day). More likely to succeed: yes. A design that relies on contacts no sphere of its charge makes
      (a like charge pressed on a carboxylate) is the kind the substrate's relaxation in validation undoes (WP6, plan
      section 4 item 5), and the one-layer alternative at LJ contact for every charge would hold cations 0.76-0.91 A
      farther from the oxygens than they come (O3 and the carboxylate oxygens).
  (iii) P in the union, where grid_v2 used R and TS. Dittner's surface encloses every frame of the path, product
      included (thesis p. 10; Dittner & Hartke 2018, p. 8 of 60). A fixed sphere must not sit inside the product's
      floors, because the validation chain runs the IRC to the product (A2.5).
  (iv) Resolution 0.5 A, where grid_v2 used 1.0 A (Jessica, 19 October 2026). Dittner's charges move continuously on
      the surface; a finer grid is closer to that.
  (v) A from new single points on the aligned geometries rather than s15's reuse of densities in each frame's own
      coordinates: the package's densities in vacuo do not exist yet, and running on the aligned geometries removes
      the per-frame back-transform that once scrambled A_v1. Check (b) measures the invariance this relies on.
  (vi) Floors from every Stage 2 minimum, migrated ones included, where STAGE2_REPRODUCIBILITY.txt (x46) described the
      grid floor as using own-basin rows (section 3). Rule B ("as close as Stage 2's spheres were seen to rest")
      counts a migrated minimum as what it is, a place where a sphere of that charge rests; x46's sentence described
      the earlier floor rule. Own-basin only would put no cation nearer the ether oxygen O3 than LJ contact (3.508 A),
      although a +1 sphere rests 2.745 A from it, and although Arg90's side chain sits near 3 A from it in the enzyme
      (Burschowsky et al. 2014, p. 17520, the isosteric Arg90Cit variant: "Cit90 is positioned within 3.2 A (average
      over four active sites) from the ether oxygen of 4", the TS analogue, "and within 3.0 A from the corresponding
      O7 of chorismate"). More likely to
      succeed: all minima, because the alternative removes the region the enzyme itself uses. The migrated minima are
      made reproducible by the extended stop rule (section 3).

10. DIRECTION OF EACH CHOICE (towards or away from a lower designed barrier)
  - Floors for q <= 0 at LJ contact (not the +1 distances): negative and neutral spheres sit farther out, so their
    effects are smaller in size than the approach document's rule would give: away.
  - Positive floors from the closest approach of any minimum of the level ("smallest distance seen"), migrated minima
    included: towards. Against own-basin minima only, the development grid has 1228 cation sites instead of 1109, and
    117 of them lie nearer O3 than 3.508 A (66 nearer than 3.0 A; closest 2.745 A) instead of none.
  - Floors applied around each atom in every direction (an envelope): towards, since sites lie at an atom's floor in
    directions where no sphere was seen to rest. The radial test bounds it: a sphere held on each site's normal comes
    at least as near at all but 10 of 2552 sites, and those 10 lie at most 0.04 A inside their radial minimum.
  - H8's floors for q > 0 (3.68-4.02 A, distances seen from spheres resting at oxygens): away; 217 sites beside H8
    admit no positive charge although the rigid model draws a +0.25 sphere inward at every one of them.
  - Monotonicity raised 3 entries (farther out): away.
  - Floors measured on the Phase 1 frames, applied to the package's: where a package contact lies farther out than the
    floor, sites may be nearer than that frame's own contact by up to the frame-to-frame spread (sd 0.03-0.08 A):
    towards; tested radially on the production grid.
  - The two-layer grid offers positive charges, beside the cation layer, the LJ-contact positions of the anion/neutral
    layer: more choices can only keep or lower the best surrogate barrier: towards.
  - P in the union: removes sites: away.
  - Resolution 0.5 A: more sites; can only keep or lower the best surrogate barrier: towards (closer to Dittner's
    continuous surface).
  - R_IRC, not the lowest reactant: none by construction (B_eff is reported in validation; A2.5).
  - L without ORCA's force switch: at most about 0.003 kcal/mol per site (header): none material.
  - New single points (v): none expected; (b) and (c) test it.
