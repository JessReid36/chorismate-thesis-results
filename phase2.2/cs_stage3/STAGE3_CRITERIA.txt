Why these runs. Stage 2 found positive spheres hold at the carboxylates and every negative sphere escapes the
dianion in the rigid model, which cannot move H8. The open questions are (a) whether the rigid contact holds in
QM/MM at a fractional charge, and (b) whether a frozen negative sphere next to the hydroxyl draws H8 onto itself
when H8 can move, since nothing repels H8 except electrostatics.

S3.1 (p1) PASS if: converged (or the s13 stability rule: contact within 0.05 A over the last third); no collapse
     (closest substrate atom >= 2.0 A); every bond within 0.15 A of its start; and the nearest oxygen within 0.3 A
     of the rigid own-basin contact for its group at q +0.5 in frame 41786 R (Stage 2: carboxylate A 2.744 A,
     carboxylate B 2.970 A). A nearest oxygen at the ether or hydroxyl has no rigid own-basin value (Stage 2) and
     counts as outside. Informative: the change from q +1 (WP4 armb_anch, 2.711 A to O2).
