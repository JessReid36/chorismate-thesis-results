STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 13, PRE-REGISTERED                                       27 October 2026

DOES THE OVERPOLARISATION ARTEFACT DEPEND ON THE CHARGE MAGNITUDE?

Written and committed before the runs. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. No continuum anywhere,
per STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027. Diagnostic 12 is not amended by this document.


D13.1  WHY

Diagnostic 11 measured r_safe = 3.50 A and Diagnostic 12 is measuring which site property predicts
the artefact. BOTH RUN AT |q| = 1. Diagnostic 12 says so itself, in D12.4: "any screen issued here
is a screen AT |q| = 1, the worst case the grid admits, and applies to smaller charges only as an
upper bound."

Diagnostic 2 has already measured, at site 1690 and in exactly this condition, that the artefact
may not be present at smaller charges at all. Its committed C3 table (ma-def2-SVP, in vacuo), read
as the linear coefficient odd/q:

   q        0.05      0.25      0.50      1.00
   odd/q   -7.1956   -7.2017   -7.2160   -4.8293

The coefficient is flat to 0.28 per cent from q = 0.05 to q = 0.50 and then collapses by 33 per
cent at q = 1.00. def2-SVPD (C5) shows the same shape, smaller: flat to 0.64 per cent, then
-9.3 per cent. def2-SVP (C1, C2) is flat throughout, which is the basis D11's C-A1.5 showed
conceals the artefact. Site 1690 sits at 2.96 A, INSIDE r_safe.

If that behaviour is general, r_safe is not a distance - it is a distance AT A CHARGE, and the
grid's admissible region is a (distance, |q|) region rather than a floor. That matters now because
the project's own calibration (STAGE6_ASSESSMENT_20261022.md section 5) points at designs of many
weak charges rather than few strong ones, and the per-site cap D2 measured for the linear surrogate
is q* = 0.25 e in three of its five conditions.

This diagnostic asks whether D2's single-site observation holds across distance.

C-A13.1, THE WALL MUST BE INERT AT EACH SITE. An ECP centre carrying no charge must give

THE OBSERVABLE. For each site s and charge q,

   ddE(arm, s, q) = [ (E_TS - E_R)(arm,s,q) - (E_TS - E_R)(ctl) ] x 627.5094740631
   d_shield(s, q) = ddE(bare, s, q) - ddE(wall, s, q)
   PASS(s, q)  iff  |d_shield(s,q)| <= max( 0.10 x |ddE(bare,s,q)| , 0.05 kcal/mol )

Diagnostic 11's tolerance, unchanged, and relative for its stated reason.

THE SCALING EXPONENT. For each site, fit log|d_shield| against log q by least squares over the
three magnitudes and report the exponent p, with the two-point exponents 0.25->0.50 and
0.50->1.00 beside it so that a pure power law can be told from a switch-on. A site whose
|d_shield| is below 0.02 kcal/mol at two or more magnitudes is reported as BELOW NOISE and
contributes no exponent: a log fit through numerical noise is not a measurement.

THE READINGS.

THE DIRECT QUESTION, reported alongside. Of the eight sites, how many PASS at q = 1.00, at 0.50

exponent does.


D13.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

Additivity, and it is the limitation that matters most here. Every run carries ONE charge. A
design of twenty-six charges at 0.25 e is not twenty-six independent probes, and nothing in this
diagnostic says the artefact of such a design is the sum of theirs. STAGE6_FINDINGS_20261022.md
Finding 2 measured ddE additivity at def2-SVP and found it holds to 3.1 per cent at n = 2 and
6.1 per cent at n = 3; the ARTEFACT's additivity has never been measured at any n, and this does
not measure it either. Any statement that weak charges are safe in a many-charge design is
therefore not supported by this diagnostic alone.

Eight sites, not the grid. The sites span D12's distance range but are a sample of a sample.

The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7).

The environment: in vacuo. The product geometry. And whether any of these positions is catalytic -
they are probes, and seven of the eight are positions the grid would refuse to a 0.25 charge.
