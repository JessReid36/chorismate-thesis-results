All in kcal/mol, at the frozen geometries of frame 42436, in vacuo, for the ten-site vector at
sum|q| = 1.3000 e. f_closed is the fraction of the bare-basis gap that the quantum wall closes,
exactly as D26.2 defines it.

   A   ddE at def2-SVP,  bare        -5.6165     DIAGNOSTIC 24 Note 1, four decimal places
   B   ddE at def2-SVPD, bare        -6.134121   d16/def2-SVPD, FULL precision
       the bare-basis gap |B - A|     0.517621

   set   ddE(wall) = C     d_shield        |C - A|     f_closed   D26.4 verdict
   Ne      -5.891135      -0.242985       0.274635      0.4694    MIXED
   Ar      -5.757698      -0.376423       0.141198      0.7272    SPILL-OUT
   He      -5.989230      -0.144890       0.372730      0.2799    NOT SPILL-OUT

   median f_closed 0.4694, which on its own reads MIXED
   spread 0.2799 to 0.7272 across three sets of the same method

THE VERDICT IS SET-DEPENDENT. D26.4 required that outcome to be declared if the sets straddle a
boundary, and they straddle BOTH: Ar clears 0.70 and He falls below 0.30. One pseudopotential says
the gap is spill-out, another says it is not, and the third says both mechanisms contribute. No
single D26.4 verdict is formed.


R26.2  WHAT THAT MEANS FOR def2-TZVPD, WHICH IS THE QUESTION THAT PROMPTED THIS

def2-TZVPD IS NOT AVOIDABLE. The cheap test was run precisely to find out whether the expensive one
is necessary, and the answer is that it cannot substitute for it. The reason is not that the wall
fails to close the gap - on one parameter set it closes nearly three-quarters of it - but that the
amount closed is not a property of the physics at the precision the decision needs.

This is DIAGNOSTIC 23's finding arriving a second time, at a question it was not asked about. D23
measured that d_shield moves 37 to 42 per cent between Ne and He and concluded that no fine-grained
claim may rest on it. f_closed is a fine-grained claim built on d_shield, so it inherits exactly
that sensitivity. The present result is the quantitative confirmation that the inheritance is fatal
to this particular use.

WHAT IS STILL WORTH HAVING FROM IT. The three sets agree that the wall closes a SUBSTANTIAL part of
the gap - the smallest value is 28 per cent, not 3 per cent. So spill-out onto unwalled point
charges is established as a real contributor to the def2-SVP / def2-SVPD disagreement, even though
its size is not. That is a weaker claim than the diagnostic hoped for and a stronger one than
nothing.


R26.3  THE PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTATION, AND HOW IT FARED

D26.4 recorded the expectation MIXED, leaning spill-out, with the reasoning that the committed
d_shield magnitudes (0.17 to 0.44 kcal/mol) are the same order as the 0.5183 gap but not obviously
larger.

The median is 0.4694, which is MIXED. The expectation is therefore met on the statistic the
pre-registration named. It is recorded here that the expectation did NOT anticipate the set spread
being wide enough to straddle both boundaries; what anticipated that was D26.4's SET-DEPENDENT
clause, which was written because DIAGNOSTIC 23 had already reversed three verdicts on margins of
0.0068 to 0.0119 kcal/mol. The clause earned its place.


R26.4  THE GATES, AND THE TWO THAT ARE WORTH THE SPACE

Twenty gates pass. Two of them are checks the diagnostic could have failed and did not, and they
are what licenses the arithmetic above.

GATE 6, REPRODUCTION OF DIAGNOSTIC 23. The reader derives d_shield from raw ORCA energies by its own
route and must reproduce D23's committed differences. It does:

   dd(Ar)  computed -0.133437   D23 prints -0.1334
   dd(He)  computed +0.098095   D23 prints +0.0981

Two independent paths to the same quantity agreeing to 3.7e-05 and 5.0e-06. Had the sign convention
or the arm pairing been wrong, this is where it would have shown.

GATE 5, READABILITY THROUGH THE COMMITTED CLASSIFIER. All sixteen arms - control, bare, and wall and
wall0 for three sets - are read by the COMMITTED phase2.2/cs6_audit.py, so the thresholds are that
file's and not this one's. None is STRAINED or COLLAPSED. SCF cycles run 15 to 17 against a control
of 16 to 17, nowhere near D6.3's factor-of-two rule.

GATE 4, control reproduction: the def2-SVPD control barrier is 18.332256 kcal/mol against the
committed record's 18.3323.


R26.5  GATE 7 DID NOT PASS, AND WHAT IT FOUND IS A FINDING RATHER THAN A FAULT

GATE 7 required B, computed at full precision from d16, to round to DIAGNOSTIC 24's printed
-6.1348. It does not:

   B computed          -6.134121
   D24 prints          -6.1348
   difference          +0.000679 kcal/mol  =  +1.083e-06 Eh

THE INPUTS ARE IDENTICAL AND THIS WAS CHECKED, NOT ASSUMED. d16/def2-SVPD/S6-10_bare_F1_R/pc.pc and
d24/pc_1.3000e_n10.pc are identical token for token. The substrate geometries are identical. Both
runs declare the same keyword line, "! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF". So the
difference cannot be in the charge vector, the geometry or the method, and must lie in the reference
each diagnostic subtracted. The control barrier implied by D24's figure is 18.332936 against d16's
18.332256.

ONE PART IN A MILLION HARTREE IS WHAT TWO INDEPENDENT RUNS OF THE SAME CONTROL COST. It is not
diagnosable further from this repository, because DIAGNOSTIC 24's inputs and raw outputs were never
committed - only its log. That is a third consequence of D24 being exploratory, after its
inadmissibility and its four-decimal precision ceiling.

IT DOES NOT CHANGE THE VERDICT. Recomputing f_closed with D24's B instead of d16's shifts every set
by at most 0.0010:

   Ne 0.4694 -> 0.4688      Ar 0.7272 -> 0.7263      He 0.2799 -> 0.2795

and the set-dependence, the median's class and the conclusion about def2-TZVPD are all unchanged.

A SMALLER DISCREPANCY INSIDE DIAGNOSTIC 24 ITSELF, found while reading it. Its log prints the 1.3000
e gap as 0.5184 and its Note 1 table prints 0.5183. The arithmetic -5.6165 - (-6.1348) gives 0.5183
exactly, so the table's column is the difference of two rounded prints while the log computed it
before rounding. DIAGNOSTIC 26 quotes 0.5183, from Note 1. This result computes the gap from the
full-precision B as 0.517621. None of the three figures changes any verdict here, and this paragraph
is the record of the discrepancy rather than a correction to either document.


R26.6  WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE

1. IT CANNOT ESTABLISH BASIS CONVERGENCE, and now it cannot bound the spill-out contribution either.
   Only a higher-zeta basis can do the first. D5.4's requirement stands undiminished.
2. THE THREE ECP SETS ARE ONE LINEAGE, from one paper by one group fitted by one recipe, as
   ECP_CONVENTION_20261102.md records. The 0.2799 to 0.7272 spread therefore bounds the sensitivity
   FROM BELOW. A set from another source could lie outside it.
3. ONE FRAME, 42436, ONE CHARGE VECTOR at sum|q| = 1.3000 e, frozen geometries, in vacuo. That field
   strength is the one inside D5.3(d)'s passing region, which makes it the least demanding place the
   question could have been asked. The 2.8800 e and 5.6433 e cells have no matching DIAGNOSTIC 23 arm.
4. A IS A FOUR-DECIMAL INPUT and no committed def2-SVP run of this vector exists, so f_closed cannot
   be computed to better than about one part in a thousand whatever is done to the other terms.
5. THE MULTIREFERENCE QUESTION IS UNTOUCHED. DIAGNOSTIC 19's Reading 3 returned DISAGREE and still
   does.
6. NO BARRIER IS QUOTED and no design is accepted or rejected. The catalytic verdict remains
   SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.5's chain.
7. THIS RESULT CANNOT ENTER THE THESIS UNTIL DIAGNOSTIC 24 IS RE-REGISTERED AND RE-RUN, as D26.5
   item 7 stated in advance. Its A and its B both originate there. R26.5 has now added a reason of
   its own: D24's figure for a quantity the committed record also holds cannot be reconciled with it,
   and the inputs that would explain the difference are not in the repository.
