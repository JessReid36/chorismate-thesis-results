D18.1  THE QUESTION

Does a Pauli wall open the HOMO-LUMO gap of S6-20 at full strength at def2-SVP?

DIAGNOSTIC 17 could not read S6-10 or S6-20 at def2-SVPD. Their bare arms were COLLAPSED and
their walled arms STRAINED on gap, and two of its three readings returned NOT READABLE. Three
candidate mechanisms were carried forward for that failure. They have different consequences
and only one of them is fixable by changing the representation:

   (a) density spilling out onto the bare point charges        fixable by the representation
   (c) a genuine field-driven near-degeneracy                  not fixable by the representation
   (d) double-counted polarisation                             undetermined

The committed `field` tree already bears on this. It is S6-20's own 20 charges, bare, scaled
from lambda 0.25 to 1.00, at def2-SVP and at def2-SVPD, on the same geometry everything else
uses. At def2-SVP:

   lambda   sum|q| e   gap R eV   gap TS eV   cyc R   cyc TS   maxlw R   dip R
   0.25     1.4400     2.7258     2.0341      16      16       0.3978    7.115
   0.50     2.8800     1.8545     1.3725      14      16       0.4179    6.543
   0.75     4.3200     1.0510     1.0188      14      33       0.4374    7.037
   1.00     5.7600     0.6099     0.9236      42      47       0.4554    9.155

   [measured, field/outputs/field_outputs.tar.xz, read with cs6_audit.read() at code 365e6c5]

The committed def2-SVP control row is nb=264, n=94: cyc 15, gap 3.2780 eV, maxlw 0.3870, dip
8.12. So at lambda 1.00 the gap has fallen to 0.19 of the control's and the SCF takes 2.8 times
the control's cycles, while the largest Loewdin deviation sits 0.0684 e above the control
median against D6.3's D_LW of 0.25, and the dipole is 1.127 times the control median against
R_DIP of 2.0. This basis carries no diffuse functions and this arm carries no ECP.

The gap closes where there is little sign that the density has moved. That is the case for (c)
over (a), and it has one hole. Loewdin populations are computed on basis functions centred on
the substrate atoms. A compact basis has no functions at the point-charge sites at all, so
density accumulating there is not something a Loewdin analysis on this basis is able to report.
The dipole is the better witness and it does move, by 12.7% at R and 46.1% at TS.

A Pauli wall settles it. If 20 walls at the charge sites leave the gap closed, then nothing
about how the charges are represented is responsible for closing it.

The wall is known to be active at this basis. The committed standoff tree carries a bare and a
walled arm at def2-SVP for a single +1 e charge:

   point   ddE bare   ddE wall   d_shield
   dref    -7.6757    -7.6088    -0.0669
   d450    -3.2955    -3.2956     0.0000

   [derived from committed files, kcal/mol. UNCORRECTED: no wall-at-zero arm exists at
    def2-SVP in that tree, so the zero-charge wall term is not removed from these.]

At the reference site the wall changes the barrier by 0.0669 kcal/mol, above DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's
0.05 floor and 6.3% of its 1.067 budget. At 4.50 A it changes nothing. So a null result in
READING 1 cannot be read as a wall that is inert at this basis.

READING 1, does the wall open the gap?

   The walled arm's HOMO-LUMO gap at R and at TS, against D6.3's committed GAP_ABS of 1.0 eV.
   The gap is read as cs6_audit reads it at code 365e6c5, which prefers TRAH's converged gap

   WALL DOES NOT OPEN THE GAP   the walled gap is below 1.0 eV in at least one state.
   WALL OPENS THE GAP           the walled gap is at or above 1.0 eV in both states.

   The bare gaps, 0.6099 eV at R and 0.9236 eV at TS, and the full D6.3 class of both arms, are
   reported whatever the verdict.

D18.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

THE LARGEST LIMITATION, STATED FIRST. If the gap stays closed with the wall, this diagnostic
does not establish that the near-degeneracy is physical. It establishes only that it is not
produced by how the charges are represented. A gap of 0.6 eV is itself a warning that
single-reference B3LYP is outside the range it can be trusted in, and the quantity this project
would then be quoting for this design - ddE(bare) = -24.6898 kcal/mol at this lambda, derived
from committed files, 23 times DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's budget - is a number the method is not
entitled to report. The follow-up to that outcome is a multireference diagnostic, not another
representation.

One design, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, in vacuo, no continuum
(STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). One basis, and the one at which the failure is mildest. Nothing
about def2-SVPD, where gap closure and density displacement occur together and where this
diagnostic therefore cannot say which drives which. Nothing about S6-10 or any other
arrangement. Nothing about whether the arrangement is sound; only about whether it can be read.

The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7), so the
walled arm is a contrast and not a truth. A wall that fails to open the gap could in principle
be a wall that is not repulsive enough. The standoff evidence quoted in D18.1 is the committed
argument against that reading, it is for a single +1 e charge and not for twenty, and it is
uncorrected for the zero-charge wall term.

Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain unmeasurable until the OptTS / coordsys cartesian
conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028 is decided.


D18.5  DIRECTION

If READING 1 returns WALL DOES NOT OPEN THE GAP, then the representation route is closed for
the designs the optimiser actually produces, and no stronger charge representation reaches the
problem. The thesis's claim then has to be made inside a characterised readable window rather
than at the strength the optimiser produces, and the honest contribution is a method with a
stated domain of validity. E2's real-ion calibration and E4's separation of total field from
local field remain worth running; work on the representation does not.

If it returns WALL OPENS THE GAP, then the def2-SVP closure was a representation artefact after
all, the def2-SVPD failure is plausibly the same thing amplified by diffuse functions, and
strengthening the representation becomes the right next step rather than a detour.

D18.3 fixes which answer means which before anything is computed.
