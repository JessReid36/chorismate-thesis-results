================================================================================================
WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY (section 7 of the pre-registration, in short; its own words govern)
================================================================================================

   It is a preliminary. It fixes no distance, no size and no rule, adopts no level, rebuilds no
   grid and runs no search. Section 6 of the pre-registration says what each outcome sends the
   next step to.

   S is an estimate: one electron, the bare charge treated as a nucleus in a complete basis,
   potentials to first order, nothing relaxed. Where it is borne out here, that is at the few
   points run.

   Parts B and C and most of part D1 are run at the reactant only. A run NOT FLAGGED at the
   reactant says nothing of the transition state.

   The fifth leg is not read. No monoanion with a charge is run.

   The ghost centre carries the def2-TZVPD functions of hydrogen. They describe an electron
   bound to a charge of 0.75 or 1.00 e well, one bound to 0.50 e less well, and one bound to
   0.25 e hardly at all; no run at CAMTZG is made at 0.25 e.

   CAMQZ is one step. A design that holds there is not thereby shown to hold in every basis
   set.

   One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, bare point charges that stand for no chemical group.
