STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 33
THE FIRST BARRIER EFFECT AT THE CORRECTED LEVEL OF THEORY, ON CONFIGURATIONS MEASURED RELIABLE
Pre-registered 9 November 2026, before any input exists and before any run is submitted.

STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. AMENDMENTS 1 to 5 stand. No budget is reinstated: DIAGNOSTIC 29
and DIAGNOSTIC 31 rule out a cap written as a function of the charges, the withdrawn
Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 stays withdrawn, and nothing below depends on one.


D33.1  WHY

Three facts, each from the committed record, do not currently sit together.

  (i)   The optimiser's objective is at the retired level of theory. The A matrix is
        a_if = V_TS,f(r_i) - V_R,f(r_i), the difference of the BARE substrate's electrostatic
        potential at site i, from in vacuo B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP densities (A_REPORT.txt). DIAGNOSTIC
        28 retired B3LYP. Its committed result places the dianion's highest occupied orbital at
        +1.2307 eV under B3LYP and -0.5918 eV under CAM-B3LYP, against a target of -0.5123 eV set
        by Probe 3's measured detachment energy of +0.5123 eV. So B3LYP sits 1.7430 eV high and
        CAM-B3LYP 0.0795 eV low - both differences DERIVED here from those committed figures, not
        stated in D28.

  (ii)  The reliability envelope is at the corrected level of theory. DIAGNOSTIC 31 and DIAGNOSTIC
        32 are CAM-B3LYP/def2-SVPD. So the envelope and the objective are not on the same footing,
        and no design has ever been both optimised and certified at one level of theory.

  (iii) Which configurations break cannot be predicted. DIAGNOSTIC 29 settled it with two runs
        carrying byte-identical charge vectors and opposite verdicts; DIAGNOSTIC 31 confirmed it on
        purpose-built iso-total families. So a candidate cannot be screened. It can only be chosen
        from inside the region already measured, and then measured again.

This diagnostic puts the objective on the same footing as the envelope, and measures the barrier
effect of the configurations that are inside it. It is the smallest job that produces a barrier
effect which is simultaneously at the corrected functional, inside a basis window confirmed by
DIAGNOSTIC 30, and on a configuration measured reliable.


D33.2  WHAT IS RUN

Two stages. Geometry is the committed aligned frame 42436 throughout; nothing is relaxed and no
geometry is generated. The grid is unchanged: the site floors come from floors.tsv at the classical
Lennard-Jones wall and L_i is that wall's own energy difference, both independent of the electronic
method, so only a_i changes.

D33.3  THE QUANTITIES, AND THE CRITERIA THAT DECIDE WHAT MAY BE QUOTED

ddE = [E_TS(design) - E_TS(control)] - [E_R(design) - E_R(control)], kcal/mol, negative lowers the
barrier. H2KCAL = 627.5094740631, as everywhere in Stage 6.

RELIABILITY is the committed classifier phase2.2/cs6_audit.py at its committed thresholds -
D_LW 0.25 e, R_DIP 2.0, GAP_ABS 1.0 eV, R_GAP 0.5 - read against d31's charge-free control, with
two rules that are already settled and are restated here so they are not re-decided later:
   - the DIPOLE leg is NOT applied to a walled arm. ORCA counts an ECP centre as a nucleus, so the
     dipole there is bookkeeping. D32.3 stated this in advance for DIAGNOSTIC 32 and it holds here.
   - STABILITY is judged on the LAST analysis in the run, per AMENDMENT 5, that being the solution
     every reported number is computed from. A corrected first-pass instability is recorded, with
     its first verdict, its negative-eigenvalue count and its lowest eigenvalue, and is not a flag.

A design's ddE IS QUOTABLE only if BOTH hold:
   (1) the design is RELIABLE on the criterion above, at both states; and
   (2) its Sigma|q| <= 2.88 e, the largest vector DIAGNOSTIC 30 confirmed converged against
       def2-TZVPD under this functional (gap_DT 0.2139 kcal/mol at 2.8800e_n10).
A design failing (1) or (2) has its ddE REPORTED AND LABELLED, and may not be quoted as a Stage 6
barrier effect. The two designs listed as OUTSIDE in D33.2 are expected to fail (2); they are run
because the comparison is informative and the cost is four runs.

R33.2  WHICH OF THE SIX DESIGNS ARE RELIABLE, each with its flags and its per-state figures.

R33.3  ddE FOR EACH DESIGN, with the quotable subset named under D33.3, and each design's sites,
       charges and Sigma|q| printed in full.

R33.4  THE LINEAR ESTIMATE'S CALIBRATION. score = sum a_i^new q_i + sum L_i against the measured
       ddE, for every design. The record has two such pairs, both at B3LYP/def2-SVP and both at the
       unconstrained optimum: S6-2 score -11.2184 against ddE -12.3835, ratio 1.10, and S6-20
       score -19.6537 against ddE -24.8261, ratio 1.26. Whether that ratio survives the change of
       level of theory and the move to constrained per-sphere charges is read, not assumed.


D33.5  CONTROLS AND STOPS, FIXED HERE

G1  THE NEW A MATRIX MUST PASS THE COMMITTED A MATRIX'S OWN CHECKS, at the same three sites and
    with the same per-site limits: 0.0728, 0.0611 and 0.0237 kcal/mol per e for sites 1678, 1954
    and 1612. If any fails, a_i^new is not used and the diagnostic STOPS after stage A. A
    finite-difference check that fails is not re-scored with a wider limit.

G2  THE INVERSE-TRANSFORM CHECK must give max |dV| <= 1e-4 Eh/e, the committed limit. Fail: STOP.

G3  THE CONTROL MUST BE THE COMMITTED ONE. Both d31 control outputs must match the sha256 above
    byte for byte. Fail: STOP, and nothing is read.

G4  EVERY RUN must terminate normally, report a FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY, run a stability
    analysis, and - for the walled arms - report the same basis-function count and electron count
    as its bare counterpart, the wall adding neither. Fail on any: that design is not read.

G5  SIGMA|Q| IS COMPUTED FROM EACH INPUT'S OWN CHARGES, never assumed from its (n, q) label, and
    the band under D33.3(2) is assigned from the computed value.

G6  IF THE SITE RANKING MOVES SO FAR THAT NO BASIS-CONFIRMED DESIGN SURVIVES - that is, if for
    every (n, q) inside the window the greedy selection with a_i^new admits fewer than n sites -
    the diagnostic STOPS and reports that, rather than substituting designs.

G7  THE WALL-ONLY CONTROLS AT n = 2 AND n = 3 MUST NOT MOVE DENSITY: each must have a Loewdin rise
    below D_LW against d31's control. If either moves, the walled designs at that n are reported
    and not quoted, because the wall would then not be a neutral addition there.


D33.6  WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY, STATED BEFORE IT IS RUN

1. NOTHING HERE ESCAPES r_safe. Every site in this diagnostic sits at 2.85-2.96 A from the
   substrate, inside the 3.50 A that DIAGNOSTIC 11 measured, and D11.3 holds that no design
   measured inside r_safe is interpretable. The choice between moving the surface out and changing
   the charge model is recorded as open in D11's Erratum 1 and is not settled here. So a quotable
   ddE from this diagnostic is a barrier effect WITHIN the current grid, not a claim that the
   current grid is admissible.

2. THE WALL IS NOT ADOPTED AND IS NOT CALIBRATED. Route (b) has no amendment. ERRATUM7 records the
   Ne parameters used outside their published octet assumption; DIAGNOSTIC 23 found swapping
   He/Ne/Ar moves d_shield by 37 to 42 per cent of its own value, and records a count of
   independent alternatives of the same class of zero. So the two walled designs' ddE values are
   reported for comparison and no production number may rest on them.

3. FROZEN GEOMETRIES, ONE FRAME, IN VACUO, NO CONTINUUM. Stage 6's relaxed half has no committed
   result, two amendments block every future relaxation, and Finding 4 holds that the wall's entire
   value lies at the relaxed geometry. No activation free energy follows from this diagnostic.

4. THE GREEDY IS NOT THE MILP. The selection rule in D33.2 is the committed greedy pipeline, which
   reproduces S6-2 and S6-3 exactly and is therefore the optimum at n <= 3, but at n = 11 and
   n = 20 the MILP may find better designs. The ddE values are for the designs actually run.

5. SIX DESIGNS ON ONE GRID AND ONE FRAME IS NOT AN OPTIMISATION. This measures the configurations
   at the edge of a mapped envelope. It does not search, and it does not establish that the best
   design in that envelope has been found.

6. NO ENVELOPE IS EXTENDED. DIAGNOSTIC 31's envelope and DIAGNOSTIC 32's n <= 3 rescue are used as
   given. If a design measured reliable here sits where D31 said it would, that is consistency, not
   a new reliability result, and the envelope is not widened by it.
