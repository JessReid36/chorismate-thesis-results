STAGE 6 - ERRATUM 3, 2026-10-26
Three readings withdrawn or re-read in the light of DIAGNOSTIC 6

Pre-registrations and criteria are never edited. This erratum does not alter STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt,
STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt, PHASE22_PLAN.txt or any pre-registration, and it does not alter any committed result file.
DIAGNOSTIC2_AMENDMENT1_RESULT.txt, DIAGNOSTIC3_RESULT.txt, DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt and DIAGNOSTIC5_RESULT.txt
all stand exactly as committed. What changes is what may be concluded from them.

The basis for all three items is phase2.2/cs_stage6/DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt, which read the SCF cycle count, the
HOMO-LUMO gap, the dipole magnitude and the largest Loewdin charge out of every committed ORCA output on this
dianion. Diagnostics 1 to 5 read energies only. Nothing was recalculated.


E3.1  q_quad = 0.80 e IS WITHDRAWN

DIAGNOSTIC2_AMENDMENT1_RESULT.txt reported q_quad = 0.80 e: the largest per-site magnitude at which the
quadratic model holds within 10 per cent, offered as Stage 7's per-site charge cap.

DIAGNOSTIC 6 classifies BOTH runs that define it as COLLAPSED. They are one positive charge at site 1690 at
def2-SVPD:

   qp0800_R    Loewdin +0.461 e over control   gap 0.472 eV   36 SCF cycles, 2.6x control
   qp0800_TS   Loewdin +0.377 e over control   gap 0.629 eV   31 SCF cycles, 2.2x control

q_quad is therefore NOT a saturation bound on the chemistry. It is the magnitude at which this charge model's
density collapses, measured by two calculations that had already collapsed. THE PER-SITE CAP OF 0.80 e MUST NOT
BE USED, in Stage 7 or anywhere else, and no result may be quoted as resting on it.

What survives, and why. b = -1.532 kcal/mol per e^2 stands. It was derived from q = +-0.50 e, and DIAGNOSTIC 6
classifies both of those runs CLEAN: qp0500_R gap 2.781 eV in 15 cycles, qm0500_R gap 3.448 eV in 16 cycles,
Loewdin within 0.05 e of control. The amendment's decision not to use the printed q = 1.00 value was correct.
The reason recorded there - that q = 1.00 lies inside the saturated region - is SUPERSEDED: it lies inside the
collapsed region, which is a different and more serious objection.

The largest per-site magnitude at which def2-SVPD is CLEAN at this site and this distance is +0.65 e
(qp0650_R, gap 1.535 eV, 16 cycles). That is an upper bound on any future per-site cap. It is NOT a measured
cap: the gap has already more than halved from its control's 3.573 eV by that point, and no run was done
between 0.65 and 0.80.

Distance matters as much as magnitude and is not yet separated from it. In phase2b_charge_design/06b_inner_shell
a SINGLE positive charge at def2-SVPD is COLLAPSED at 1.5 A (sp_reactant_inner_1.5_p, Loewdin +1.798 e, gap
0.489 eV, 65 cycles) and still COLLAPSED at 2.0 A (sp_ts_inner_2.0_p, Loewdin +1.171 e, gap 0.719 eV, 34
cycles), while at site 1690's 2.96 A it takes +0.80 e. Any future cap must be stated as a magnitude AND a
distance, never as a magnitude alone.


E3.2  DIAGNOSTIC 3 IS WITHDRAWN

DIAGNOSTIC3_RESULT.txt returned INCONCLUSIVE, and its closing note attributed that to a specification defect:
the ghost control had been written "at either basis", so def2-SVP's failure carried def2-SVPD down with it.
That defect is real but it is NOT the reason the diagnostic should have been inconclusive.

Diagnostic 3's substantive comparison was between a bare point charge and a REAL Na+ or K+ ion at site 1690,
the ion carrying its own electrons and therefore a genuine Pauli wall. The committed outputs record those ion
runs as near-degenerate, against a charge-free control at 8.12 D and a 3.278 eV gap:

   def2-SVP    M2_Na_R   gap 0.387 eV   29 cycles   dipole 17.05 D
   def2-SVP    M2_Na_TS  gap 0.352 eV   29 cycles   dipole 17.55 D
   def2-SVP    M3_K_R    gap 0.361 eV   25 cycles   dipole 21.84 D
   def2-SVP    M3_K_TS   gap 0.396 eV   27 cycles   dipole 22.40 D
   def2-SVPD   M2_Na_R   gap 0.383 eV   37 cycles   dipole 21.07 D
   def2-SVPD   M3_K_R    gap 0.437 eV   26 cycles   dipole 27.31 D

Diagnostic 3 concluded that a real ion saturates about as much as a bare point charge does, and therefore that
the charge bound is a property of the dianion rather than a defect of the model. That conclusion rested on
comparing two unwell calculations. Two sick calculations agreeing is not evidence that the physics is real, and
the conclusion is withdrawn. Every later statement resting on it is withdrawn with it - in particular the claim
that Pauli repulsion is a small effect, which was measured as 0.8 and 8.1 per cent in exactly these runs.

What is NOT settled here: whether those ion runs are an artefact or a genuine charge-transfer ion pair. This
dianion's HOMO sits at +1.231 eV in vacuo at def2-SVPD, so transferring an electron to Na+ may well be
thermodynamically real - in which case M2 and M3 are a different electronic state and still not a control for
Pauli repulsion. Either way they cannot do the job Diagnostic 3 asked of them.

A LIMIT OF DIAGNOSTIC 6 THAT MUST BE STATED HERE: the M2 and M3 runs carry 128 and 136 electrons, and
cs6_audit.py filters to 118. DIAGNOSTIC 6 DID NOT CLASSIFY THEM. The figures above are read directly from the
committed pauli_outputs.tar.xz and are not D6 verdicts. Extending the audit to those electron counts is an open
item. The ghost controls M4 and M5 do carry 118 electrons and no point charges, and D6 classified them CLEAN.


E3.3  lambda_quad = 0.25 IS RE-READ, NOT CORRECTED

DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt stands as committed, lambda_quad = 0.25 included. Its meaning changes.

lambda_quad was the largest lambda at which def2-SVPD's ddE lies within 10 per cent of the quadratic model.
DIAGNOSTIC 6 classifies the def2-SVPD field runs at lambda = 0.75 and 1.00 - all four, both states - as
COLLAPSED, and the def2-SVP runs at lambda = 0.75 and 1.00 as STRAINED. So lambda_quad marks where the
REPRESENTATION fails, not where the chemistry saturates. It is a bound on the model.

Note for the record that the def2-SVP lambda = 1 field runs are the same wavefunctions as the frozen S6-20 runs
and D6 gives them the same verdict and the same numbers - gap 0.610 and 0.924 eV, 42 and 47 cycles - which is
the consistency Finding 4 requires and is reported here because it is a check that passed, not a problem.

THE DEFENSIBLE DOMAIN IS lambda <= 0.50, AND IT NOW RESTS ON TWO UNRELATED CRITERIA THAT COINCIDE.

   DIAGNOSTIC5_RESULT.txt D5.3(d): the two bases agree on ddE to within 1.0 kcal/mol up to lambda = 0.50,
   |Delta| = 0.9757 there.
   DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt: every field run at lambda = 0.25 and 0.50 is CLEAN at BOTH bases, and nothing at
   lambda >= 0.75 is.

Energy agreement between two bases and wavefunction health within each basis are independent questions. They
give the same answer. Inside that domain:

   lambda = 0.50   sum |q| = 2.880 e   largest single charge 0.275 e
                   ddE = -10.657 kcal/mol (def2-SVP)   -11.633 kcal/mol (def2-SVPD)

For scale, a pocket of six charged residues at +-1 carries sum |q| of about 6 e, so this is roughly three
charged residues' worth. THIS WAS MEASURED ON ONE DESIGN, S6-20, at one frame, at fixed geometry. Whether the
domain transfers to another arrangement is untested, and Stage 7 must either test it or say on its face that it
has not been tested.

One precision note carried forward from DIAGNOSTIC5's D5.2 so that it is not lost: lambda_quad came out at 0.25
rather than 0.50 because def2-SVPD's S at lambda = 0.50 is 10.3 per cent against a pre-registered threshold of
10.0. The threshold was fixed before the runs and is not moved. The near miss is three tenths of a point and it
moves the implied bound by a factor of two, so the bound must never be quoted as sharply determined.

E3.5  CORRECTIONS TO THE RECORD OF THIS SESSION'S OWN REASONING

Recorded because they were stated and were wrong, and a reader of the commit history would otherwise inherit
them.

   A single point charge was said to have collapsed the density at def2-SVP. IT DID NOT. The run in question,
   sp_reactant_inner_1.5_p, is at def2-SVPD. At def2-SVP a single +1.00 e at 2.96 A is CLEAN, gap 3.190 eV in
   15 cycles.

   def2-SVPD was said to collapse ONLY at a single charge. It does not: DIAGNOSTIC 6 finds it COLLAPSED from 1
   to 20 charges. The earlier claim was made against an incomplete copy of the corpus that predated the field
   outputs archive.

   The divergence in DIAGNOSTIC 4 was first read as a TOTAL-FIELD effect bounded at sum |q|. That reading was
   not supported: at lambda = 0.75 the largest single charge is 0.4125 e, below the +0.50 e that is CLEAN as a
   single charge, and def2-SVPD still collapsed. A single electrostatic scalar was then proposed to predict the
   collapses and TESTED AND FAILED - evaluated on a 1 A shell around each positive site it gives S6-P 14.40 eV,
   which is CLEAN, against S6-10's 9.01 eV, which is STRAINED, so it does not order the data. No predictor is
   offered.

   The DIAGNOSTIC 6 classifier itself was wrong on its first construction. A Loewdin criterion applied alone
   called 616 of the 832 def2-SVP point-charge single points then in the corpus collapsed - the
   corpus has since grown by the field outputs archive, so that denominator is a record of the development
   test and not of the committed table - including runs with a 3.85 eV gap
   converging in 14 cycles, which is ordinary polarisation and not a failure. COLLAPSED now requires relocation
   AND an unwell SCF together, which gives 54, and POLARISED exists precisely so that a healthy polarised
   density is never mistaken for a fault. An absolute Loewdin threshold also had to be abandoned, because the
   B97-3c and r2SCAN-3c charge-free controls natively sit at 0.743 and 0.659 e.
