STAGE 6 - AMENDMENT 6: binding, the fifth leg of the reliability criterion
17 November 2026. Proposed on 16 November 2026 in the assessment Jessica asked for, and agreed
by her on 17 November 2026. It takes effect when she commits it.

Nothing already committed is edited by this amendment and no committed verdict is rewritten by
it. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. AMENDMENTS 1 to 5 stand. It changes what a design must
pass from here on, and states its scope exactly.


A6.1  WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS

The criterion has four legs, read on the dianion against the charge-free control at the same
level and state: the Loewdin rise, the dipole ratio, the gap, and the stability of the last
analysis (AMENDMENT 5). None of them reads whether the dianion still holds its second electron
when the charges are present.

Probe 2's result, vde_probe/PROBE2_RESULT.txt, wrote the rule for that case:

   "Any ddE measured at such a condition is not a barrier effect and must not be reported as
   one."

The condition it speaks of is a detachment energy at or below zero.

DIAGNOSTIC 40 measured the detachment energy, at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD and both states, for
the five arrangements whose lowering the record held quotable. All five had passed the four
legs. All five are METASTABLE at both states. D40.2: "It does not make binding a leg of the
reliability criterion. That is an amendment."

DIAGNOSTIC 42 measured it for the 13 conditions of DIAGNOSTIC 31 that pass the four legs. Ten
are BOUND at both states and three are METASTABLE at both. D42.2: "It does not make binding a
leg of the reliability criterion, and it does not take the decision on a neutrality constraint
for the optimiser. Each is an amendment."

So the record holds 18 arrangements that passed the four legs and have a detachment energy, and
8 of them, the five of D40 and three of D42, are not bound dianions (DERIVED here by counting
the two results; neither prints the total).

This is the amendment D40.2 and D42.2 name.


A6.2  THE RULE FROM HERE

A fifth leg, BINDING, is added to the four.

For a design measured with external charges, the detachment energy is read at each state from
which its ddE is formed, at the level of theory and in the basis of that ddE, with the same
external charges, by D40's convention, which is Probe 2's:

   VDE = E(monoanion) - E(dianion), at the same geometry with the same external charges. The
   monoanion is charge -1, multiplicity 2, and carries the stability analysis. Each energy is
   the last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY of its output. The conversion is 27.211386245988 eV per
   Eh.

   BOUND        VDE above zero, and the monoanion's last stability analysis STABLE
   METASTABLE   VDE at or below zero

   A value above zero whose monoanion is not shown to be STABLE is reported with its number as
   "ABOVE ZERO, MONOANION NOT STABLE" and is not counted as BOUND. This is section 2 of D42's
   pre-registration, and it can only take a BOUND away: a monoanion solution that is not the
   lowest one makes the detachment energy too high.

A design PASSES THE FIFTH LEG if it is BOUND at both states.

From here on, a ddE may be reported as a barrier effect only if its design passes the four
legs and the fifth. Where either state is METASTABLE, Probe 2's sentence applies to the ddE,
and the number is reported with that word beside it. Where a state is not measured, or is
above zero with the monoanion not shown stable, the fifth leg is NOT PASSED and the ddE is
reported as such.

THE WORD RELIABLE KEEPS ITS COMMITTED MEANING, which is the four legs. It is not redefined,
because the committed verdicts that use it use it in that sense. The fifth leg is reported
beside it in its own words, BOUND and METASTABLE.

A reader that applies this rule says that it applies AMENDMENT 6.


A6.3  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TOUCH, AND WHY

IT IS NOT RETROSPECTIVE. The verdicts of DIAGNOSTICS 31, 32, 33 and 34 were issued without
this leg and stand as committed.

IT IS NOT THE ERRATUM D40 ASKS FOR. D40.1 says of a quotable number its result reaches: "what
becomes of that number is a dated erratum and a decision for Jessica." That holds for the five
lowerings. This amendment withdraws none of them and restricts none of them. It only fixes the
rule for what comes after.

IT DOES NOT MAKE THE TEN CONDITIONS OF D42 QUOTABLE. D42.8 stands as written: "A ddE LISTED
UNDER R42.6 IS NOT MADE QUOTABLE BY D42." Every one of them sits inside r_safe.

phase2.2/cs6_audit.py IS NOT AMENDED. It holds no detachment energy. The rule lives in the
readers that apply it.


A6.4  WHAT IS NOT DECIDED HERE

1. NO RULE ON THE CHARGES IS ADOPTED. The fifth leg is a measurement made on each design. A
   constraint that the charges of a design sum to zero or more is not written into the
   optimiser by this amendment. D42 did not falsify that rule on 8 conditions, and D42.3 says
   of it: "NOT FALSIFIED IS NOT ESTABLISHED."

2. NO MARGIN IS SET. BOUND is any value above zero. A thin margin is reported as a number and
   is not a third word. The thinnest among D42's ten is +0.1591 eV, at the reactant of
   n10_q0.25.

3. THE TEST IS IN VACUO, AS THE RECORD IS. Whether an in vacuo detachment energy is the right
   test for a charge model that stands for an environment is not decided here. AMENDMENT 2
   took continuum solvation out of the protocol. If a later amendment brings a continuum back,
   that amendment must say what becomes of this leg.

4. ONE LEVEL AT A TIME. BOUND at one functional and one basis is BOUND at that functional and
   that basis. D40's part A found the bare dianion's detachment energy at the reactant to lie
   between +0.5013 and +1.3185 eV across eight functionals at def2-SVPD. Which further levels
   a design is taken to is AMENDMENT 7.

5. VERTICAL, AND UNRESTRICTED KOHN-SHAM. Spin contamination is reported and not corrected.
   D40.4 stands: "where a detachment energy is negative, its sign is the reading and its size
   is not."
