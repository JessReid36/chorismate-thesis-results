Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 4: the TOTAL-FIELD scan. One design, scaled. Written 23 October 2026, after
DIAGNOSTIC2_AMENDMENT1_RESULT.txt and before Stage 7 is written.

THIS IS A DIAGNOSTIC AND NOT A METHOD CHANGE. No criterion, threshold, tolerance, arrangement or reported number
changes. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt and STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt are untouched and PHASE22_PLAN.txt section 2.2 stays in force.

D4.1 WHY, AND WHAT THE PREVIOUS THREE DIAGNOSTICS LEFT OPEN

DIAGNOSTIC2_AMENDMENT1_RESULT.txt measured the per-site bound: at site 1690 the quadratic model a q + b q^2 is
within 10 per cent of the measured response up to q = 0.80 e. That settles four of the six committed designs,
whose charges sit at exactly 1.00 e and are therefore outside it:

   S6-P  S6-2  S6-3  S6-5     max |q| = 1.0000 e     outside the measured bound
   S6-10 S6-20                max |q| = 0.5500 e     inside it

IT DOES NOT SETTLE S6-20, and S6-20 is the design DIAGNOSTIC1_RESULT.txt found worst: every one of its twenty
charges is at or below 0.55 e, comfortably inside the per-site bound, and its ddE still moved from -24.8261 at
def2-SVP to +4.5746 at ma-def2-SVP, 118 per cent with a sign reversal.

The reason the per-site bound cannot settle it is that every measurement so far applied ONE charge. A bound on the
response to one charge says nothing about the response to twenty, and S6-20 is the proof that it does not
transfer. The variable none of the three diagnostics has varied is the TOTAL field, which is what the assessment's
sum |q| correlation of r = +0.9955 pointed at before any of them ran - and that correlation was measured across
four DIFFERENT designs, so design and total field were confounded in it.

There is a second reason this matters now. At the measured per-site cap the LINEAR optimum under the pre-registered
budget sum q^2 <= 2.00 never reaches 0.80 e: water-filling over the 30 to 35 mutually separated sites the grid
admits gives a largest charge of 0.56 to 0.70 e. So the measured cap would not bind on a Stage 7 design at all,
and Stage 7 written on it alone would be a pre-registration that constrains nothing.

D4.2 WHAT IS RUN

ONE design, scaled. S6-20's twenty sites and charges exactly as relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt records them, with every
charge multiplied by the same lambda:

   lambda = 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00        sum |q| = 1.44, 2.88, 4.32, 5.76 e
                                          sum q^2 = 0.128, 0.512, 1.152, 2.048 e^2
                                          largest single charge = 0.1375, 0.275, 0.4125, 0.55 e

so that at every lambda EVERY individual charge is inside the measured per-site bound of 0.80 e and only the total
field varies. That is the confound the assessment's correlation could not remove, removed by construction.

   BASES   def2-SVP and def2-SVPD, both in vacuo. def2-SVPD because
           STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC3_NOTE1_20261022.txt N3.2 measured def2-SVP to be about fifteen times more sensitive
           to empty basis functions placed at a design site, so it cannot show saturation whether or not it is
           there; def2-SVP as well, because the inter-basis DIFFERENCE at each lambda is one of the quantities
           D4.3 names. ma-def2-SVP is deliberately NOT used, for N3.2's reason and because it is the one condition
           whose single-charge response was not even monotone at 1.00 e.

   GEOMETRIES  frame_42436's committed reactant and transition state, byte for byte.

   CHARGES as BARE POINT CHARGES, twenty in one file, no Lennard-Jones. Forced, not convenient: Finding 4
           measured the LJ sphere and the bare charge at the same site to differ by exactly L with identical
           electronic deviation, necessarily, since the LJ term never enters the QM Hamiltonian. L is a classical
           quantity already committed in L_f42436.tsv and is added arithmetically where it is needed.

   16 single points of a few minutes. The bare references are NOT re-run: they come out of the committed qscan
   outputs archive, so the comparison is against the same bare energies every earlier diagnostic used.

D4.3 WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

The prediction at each lambda, from quantities already committed and NOT refitted:

   predicted(lambda)  =  lambda * sum_i a_i q_i   +   lambda^2 * b * sum_i q_i^2

with a_i and q_i read out of relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt (sum a q = -19.51748 kcal/mol, sum q^2 = 2.0476 e^2, which
reproduces the committed score of -19.65373 to 3e-5 once sum L is added) and b = -1.532 kcal/mol per e^2 from
DIAGNOSTIC2_AMENDMENT1_20261022.txt. THE USE OF ONE b FOR ALL TWENTY SITES IS AN APPROXIMATION AND IS STATED AS
ONE: b has been measured at site 1690 only, at 2.96 A from the nearest substrate atom, and the other nineteen
sites sit at various distances. If the scan's residual is large, a site-dependent b is one of the explanations and
D4.4 says so.

   S(lambda) = | measured - predicted | / | predicted |

  THE TOTAL-FIELD BOUND is lambda_quad, the largest lambda with S(lambda) <= 10 per cent, at def2-SVPD. The
  corresponding bound on total field is lambda_quad * 5.76 e, and THAT is the number Stage 7 needs and does not
  have. If lambda_quad = 1.00 there is no total-field bound inside the range a design can reach under the
  pre-registered budget, and the per-site cap is the only bound required.

  THE sum |q| HYPOTHESIS. Delta(lambda) = ddE(def2-SVPD) - ddE(def2-SVP) at each lambda. The assessment claimed
  the inter-basis movement scales with sum |q| at r = +0.9955, across four different designs. Here sum |q| is
  proportional to lambda and the design is fixed, so:
     CONFIRMED  if Delta is linear in lambda with r >= 0.98 over the four points and an intercept within
                0.3 kcal/mol of zero.
     NOT CONFIRMED otherwise, in which case the correlation was a property of comparing different designs and the
                assessment's section 4 must be read as describing those four designs and not as a scaling law.

  AND THE ONE THAT MAY VINDICATE S6-20. DIAGNOSTIC1_RESULT.txt measured S6-20 at ma-def2-SVP as +4.5746, against
  -24.8261 at def2-SVP. This scan measures lambda = 1.00 at both bases, the same design and geometries differing
  only in basis.

     FIRST, A CONSISTENCY STOP THAT TIES THIS SCAN TO THE COMMITTED MEASUREMENT. The committed frozen -24.8261 is
     a QM/MM number and includes S6-20's classical sum L of -0.13622; this scan uses bare charges, so its
     def2-SVP lambda = 1 point must equal -24.8261 - (-0.13622) = -24.6899, which is what Finding 4 requires since
     the bare charge and the LJ sphere differ by exactly L and nothing else. analyse STOPS unless they agree
     within 0.05 kcal/mol. If they do not, this scan is not measuring the committed design and nothing else in it
     may be read.

     THEN the decision is this scan's def2-SVPD lambda = 1 against ITS OWN def2-SVP lambda = 1, which is like for
     like; NOT against the committed frozen number, which carries sum L.
     If ddE(def2-SVPD, lambda = 1) is NEGATIVE and within 15 per cent of this scan's def2-SVP value, then
     Diagnostic 1's sign reversal was an ma-def2-SVP artefact, consistent with N3.2, and S6-20 survives. Finding 6
     and the magnitudes of Findings 1 and 5 would then need re-reading at def2-SVPD rather than withdrawing.
     If it is POSITIVE, or far from the def2-SVP value, the sign reversal is real at any diffuse basis and the
     frozen half of Stage 6 has to be rebuilt at def2-SVPD before any of it informs anything.

No threshold here is new: the 10 per cent is Diagnostic 2's own convention and the 15 per cent is decision O6's
method-and-basis figure.

D4.4 WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

One design, so the result is about S6-20's particular arrangement of twenty charges and not about total field in
general; a different arrangement with the same sum |q| could behave differently, and that is exactly the confound
this diagnostic removes in one direction only. One frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry throughout. Whether b
varies between sites, which is the first explanation to reach for if the residual is large and which would need
the charge-magnitude scan repeated at two or three further distances. Negative total field - every lambda here
scales a design whose net charge is -0.24 e. And nothing about the relaxed geometry or about Loss.

DIRECTION

None. The outcome supplies one number Stage 7 needs, tests one claim the assessment made, and decides whether
S6-20's Diagnostic 1 result stands or was an artefact of the basis it was measured in. D4.3 fixes all three
readings before the runs go in.
