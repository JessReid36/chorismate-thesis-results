was taken, when, at which levels, and what was computed from it. This is that note.

N1.4  WHAT WAS COMPUTED FROM THE TWO PRINTOUTS, AND BY WHAT

A script of Claude's, inspect_d44.py, kept beside this note. It is not the committed reader
and applies none of its gates. From the printed energies it formed only what the
pre-registration defines from energies: ddE at each level, with that level's own control; its
difference from D43's working-level ddE, as a share of it; the detachment energy where both
energies were printed; the control against the committed energy of the same input; and the
energy of each stability search minus the energy of the run it started from. THE FOUR LEGS
WERE NOT READ: no Loewdin charge, no dipole and no gap was looked at, so nothing was known of
whether charge had moved.

N1.5  WHAT WAS DONE WITH THOSE NUMBERS BEFORE THE OFFICIAL READ

They were given to Jessica in the chat, each time as unofficial and preliminary. From them
Claude made four preliminary figures, a file of figures for her meeting of 19 November, and
paragraphs for that meeting. None of it went into a committed file.

On the first printout, of three levels, Claude told her that the barrier change moved little
with the basis set and that the working level looked defensible as it stood. The second
printout, of CAMTZ, came 44 minutes later and overturned both, and Claude told her so the same
morning.

On the second printout Claude told her two more things, each as an inference and not as a
reading: that in the larger basis set electron density is probably drawn onto the bare
positive charges; and that the coupled-cluster reference of AMENDMENT 8 should not be built on
B_n05_q0.50 as designed before that was understood.


N1.6  WHAT WAS NOT CHANGED, AND THE ONE THING THAT WAS DONE

No input, keyword, level, tolerance or reading of D44 was changed between the looks and the
official read. D44's result says so of the inputs in its own gates: G44.1, "all 568 files of
INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 are the ones listed", and G44.6, "every output that exists prints, as
the input it ran, the committed input beside it." The code repository stood at e4b44208, its
commit of 18 November 2026 at 17:32:24 +0200, before the looks and when the reader ran.

One job was submitted again after the looks. The RDTZ job was cut by its limit of 24 hours:
"=>> PBS: job killed: walltime 86429 exceeded limit 86400" (run_RDTZ.pbs.out.part1). It was
submitted again as it was on 19 November 2026 (run_RDTZ.pbs.out: "host=comp050.hpc start=Thu
Nov 19 10:49:39 SAST 2026"), as the pre-registration's F3 says: "A job that was cut short is
submitted again as it is." No energy of RDTZ had been seen.
