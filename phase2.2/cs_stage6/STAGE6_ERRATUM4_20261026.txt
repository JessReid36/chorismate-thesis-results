STAGE 6 - ERRATUM 4, 2026-10-26
Six defects in DIAGNOSTIC 6's instrument and in what three Stage 6 documents say about it. Every one measured.

Pre-registrations and criteria are never edited. This erratum does not alter STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt,
STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC4_20261023.txt, STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC6_20261026.txt, STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC7_20261026.txt,
STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt, PHASE22_PLAN.txt, STAGE4_CRITERIA.txt, STAGE4B_CRITERIA.txt, STAGE4C_CRITERIA.txt or any
other pre-registration, and it does not alter any committed result file. DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt stands exactly as
committed, and so does every verdict in it: that is measured below, not assumed. STAGE6_ERRATUM3_20261026.txt is
itself never edited either; E4.2 corrects a sentence in it by record, not by amendment.

It also does not change cs6_audit.py. Four of the six items below are defects in that program. Changing it would
change DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt, and re-running a diagnostic is a decision with a pre-registration of its own.
What to do about them is set out at the end and is Jessica's to decide.

HOW THE SIX WERE ESTABLISHED. Four read-only probes, no new calculation, no network, nothing written into either
repo by them. Each reuses cs6_audit.py's own parser, cs6_audit.read(), unchanged and imported rather than
retyped, so no threshold and no field is re-expressed. Two of them re-run cs6_audit's verdict() as well,
transcribed structurally because it is a closure inside main(). The first probe reproduces DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt
exactly - 1680 completed runs on this dianion, 352 controls, 1264 point-charge single points, 47 geometry
optimisations, 63 QM/MM - so it is reading the same corpus the committed result read, and every difference below
is a real difference and not an artefact of a second parser. The probes are
WORKLOG_20261026.md section Q; they are scratch and are not committed.

A NOTE ON QUOTATION. Every quotation is verbatim. Where a source's line wrapping differs from this file's, the
quotation is reflowed to this width and nothing else is changed; where the wrap itself is worth seeing, it is
kept and said so. The one multi-line block reproduced line for line is DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt's counts
paragraph in E4.4(ii), checked against the file character by character.

A NOTE ON DIRECTION, because it is the thing that matters most here. Four of these six corrections run AGAINST
the charge representation: they make the committed picture of its failure earlier, broader, or less well
supported than it looked. Two run in the flattering direction. Each item below states which, in its own words,
and no item is softened or omitted on account of it.


E4.1  "IT WAS RECORDED AND NEVER READ" IS TOO BROAD. STAGE 4 AND STAGE 4B READ IT, AND REPORTED FAILURES ON IT,
      EIGHTEEN DAYS EARLIER

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC6_20261026.txt D6.0 closes its table of the frozen def2-SVP runs with:

   "The cycle count triples and the gap falls below 1 eV. It was recorded and never read."

and cs6_audit.py's own docstring puts it more broadly still:

   "The breakdown was recorded in the original outputs from the
    first design run onward and was never read."

   (quoted across its two source lines, cs6_audit.py lines 11-12)

The narrow claim is true. Diagnostics 1 to 5 read energies, not one of them read the cycle count or the gap, and
nothing read either quantity for the Stage 6 design runs. The broad claim is not true. Two committed reports read
exactly these indicators and recorded failures on them on 8 October 2026, eighteen days before Diagnostic 6:

   phase2.2/cs_stage4/STAGE4_REPORT.txt, commit 7785dbf, 2026-10-08
      "S4.2 polarisation term at contact: b(SVPD) -14.2406 vs b(SVP) -9.8413 kcal/mol, change 44.7%
       (criterion < 10%) -> FAIL"
      and, in the same file, the density indicator in all but name:
      "informative Mulliken charge on O2 (nearest the sphere): q=0 -> q=+1 change -0.0231 e (SVP) vs
       -1.4809 e (SVPD)"

   phase2.2/cs_stage4b/STAGE4B_REPORT.txt, commit 1382b07, 2026-10-08
      "q=+1.00  SVPD gap 0.55 eV, deviation from trend -17.179 kcal/mol (SVP 3.34 eV, -0.272) -> NOT safe"
      and the conclusion of Arm A:
      "safe distance by charge (smallest tested distance from O2 at which that charge, and every larger tested
       distance, is safe):
          q=+0.50: none of the tested distances
          q=+0.75: none of the tested distances
          q=+1.00: none of the tested distances"

So the HOMO-LUMO gap was read, at def2-SVPD, over four distances and three positive magnitudes, and reported with
a verdict, before any Stage 6 arrangement existed. A reader of D6.0 as written would not know that.

WHAT D6 MAY PROPERLY CLAIM, and what is withdrawn. The claim that survives is the narrower one: these indicators
were never read AT def2-SVP AGAINST A SAME-BASIS CHARGE-FREE CONTROL, which is the comparison D6 introduced, and
were never read for the Stage 6 design runs at all. That is a real and sufficient novelty. The unqualified
sentences above are withdrawn; neither may be quoted.

WHAT IS NOT WITHDRAWN. Nothing in D6's measurement depends on the observation being first. Its thresholds,
controls, specificity check and verdicts are unaffected.

DIRECTION: flattering, and recorded for that reason. This correction says the project caught the gap collapse
earlier than D6.0 implies, which reflects better on the record, not worse. Nothing is claimed from it. It is
written down because it was stated and was wrong.

WHAT IT RAISES AND DOES NOT SETTLE. Stage 4b's own resolution, recorded in CHARGE_MODEL_DECISIONS.md's dated
section "Update 9 October 2026 - after Stage 4b", was "with def2-SVP the bare sphere stands; a diffuse basis
needs the Ne-type pseudopotential". Stage 6 then ran its designs at def2-SVP under that decision. D6 is the
finding that def2-SVP's own wavefunction health fails there too - a question Stage 4 and 4b did not ask, since
both compared def2-SVP against def2-SVPD rather than either against a charge-free control. The decision was
therefore taken on the evidence available, and D6's finding is new against it. Whether the def2-SVP leg of that
9 October decision must now be revisited is a decision, not an erratum item, and it is Jessica's.


E4.2  E3.3's def2-SVP CLAUSE OVERSTATES BY ONE RUN. THREE OF THE FOUR ARE STRAINED; lam075_R IS CLEAN

STAGE6_ERRATUM3_20261026.txt E3.3 says:

   "DIAGNOSTIC 6 classifies the def2-SVPD field runs at lambda = 0.75 and 1.00 - all four, both states - as
    COLLAPSED, and the def2-SVP runs at lambda = 0.75 and 1.00 as STRAINED."

The def2-SVPD clause is right: four runs, all four COLLAPSED, and DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt lists them. The
def2-SVP clause reads as four and the committed result lists three. Read out of DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt's own
"EVERY COLLAPSED OR STRAINED SINGLE POINT" table, the nb 264 field rows are 33, 42 and 47 cycles - three rows,
not four. Identified in the committed archive field_outputs.tar.xz, with the nb 264 control medians D6 printed
(15 cycles, gap 3.278 eV, maxLoewdin 0.387 e, dipole 8.12 D):

   def2-SVP/lam075_R    14 cycles   gap 1.051 eV   maxLw 0.437 e   dip  7.04 D   ->  CLEAN
   def2-SVP/lam075_TS   33 cycles   gap 1.019 eV   maxLw 0.435 e   dip  8.87 D   ->  STRAINED (cycles)
   def2-SVP/lam100_R    42 cycles   gap 0.610 eV   maxLw 0.455 e   dip  9.15 D   ->  STRAINED
   def2-SVP/lam100_TS   47 cycles   gap 0.924 eV   maxLw 0.454 e   dip 11.86 D   ->  STRAINED

lam075_R is CLEAN on both tests and not marginally: 14 SCF cycles is BELOW the control median of 15, and its
1.051 eV gap is above the 1.0 eV absolute floor the STRAINED test requires. The sentence is corrected to: the
def2-SVP field runs at lambda = 1.00, both states, and the TRANSITION STATE at lambda = 0.75, are STRAINED; the
REACTANT at lambda = 0.75 is CLEAN.

THE CONCLUSION OF E3.3 IS UNCHANGED, and it is important to be exact about why, because this correction runs in
the flattering direction and must not be allowed to do any work it cannot do.

   (a) The defensible domain is a property of the WORSE state, not the better one. A barrier needs both. At
       lambda = 0.75 the transition state is STRAINED, so lambda = 0.75 does not yield a usable barrier however
       the reactant is classified.
   (b) E3.3's leading criterion is the def2-SVPD quartet, all four COLLAPSED at lambda = 0.75 and 1.00. That is
       untouched.
   (c) lam075_R's margin is 0.051 eV above an absolute threshold of 1.0 eV that D6.0 itself discloses was chosen
       after the outputs had been inspected. A verdict resting on 0.05 eV against a hand-chosen floor is not
       something to lean on in either direction, and this erratum leans on it only to refuse a claim, never to
       support one.

DIRECTION: flattering, by one run, and explicitly denied any consequence. lambda <= 0.50 stands.


E4.3  DIAGNOSTIC 4 CALLS sum q^2 <= 2.00 "THE PRE-REGISTERED BUDGET". IT IS AN UNCALIBRATED CODE DEFAULT, AND
      THE ARGUMENT RESTING ON IT IS NOT ESTABLISHED

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC4_20261023.txt uses the phrase twice:

   "At the measured per-site cap the LINEAR optimum under the pre-registered budget sum q^2 <= 2.00 never
    reaches 0.80 e: water-filling over the 30 to 35 mutually separated sites the grid admits gives a largest
    charge of 0.56 to 0.70 e. So the measured cap would not bind on a Stage 7 design at all, and Stage 7 written
    on it alone would be a pre-registration that constrains nothing."

   "If lambda_quad = 1.00 there is no total-field bound inside the range a design can reach under the
    pre-registered budget, and the per-site cap is the only bound required."

Three committed sources say that value is not a pre-registered design budget:

   phase2.2/cs_stage6/STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, in the S6-2 arrangement rule - "charges at the budget's optimum for
   those two fixed sites (B as the code's default, 2.0 e^2, which is a code-test value and not the calibrated
   B)". So the criteria fix B, but only as the value at which S6-2's and S6-3's charges are assigned once their
   sites are chosen, and they name it a code-test value in the same breath.

   phase2.2/cs_opt_milp.py (code repo), the optimiser's own parameter table -
   '"budget": 2.0,  # B in e^2, sum_i q_i^2 <= B (O3). Production B comes from the screens.'

   phase2.2/OPTIMISER_CODE_README.md - "that the calibration of B (decision O3) should have in view", written of
   a calibration still to be done.

So B = 2.0 e^2 is a code default awaiting calibration, fixed by STAGE6_CRITERIA for two arrangements of one
stage. It is not a pre-registered bound on what a design may reach. The phrase "the pre-registered budget" is
withdrawn in both places.

WHAT FOLLOWS. DIAGNOSTIC 4's MEASUREMENT is untouched - lambda_quad is read from the committed runs and does not
involve B. What is withdrawn is the ARGUMENT at the first quotation: that the measured per-site cap "would not
bind on a Stage 7 design at all". That argument needs a budget, and there is no pre-registered one, so it is NOT
ESTABLISHED. Under a smaller calibrated B the per-site cap may well bind. Stage 7 must not be written on the
assumption that it cannot.

FLAGGED, NOT ASSERTED, because I have not recomputed it: the water-filling figure of 0.56 to 0.70 e assumes
charges may be chosen freely on the admitted sites. OPTIMISER_CODE_README.md records that "1228 of the 2769
development sites cannot carry zero charge" and that "1064 sites admit only |q| = 1". On those sites a sphere
costs the budget at least its floor squared, so at B = 2.0 e^2 a cation-layer design can hold about two spheres,
not thirty. Whether that changes the 0.56 to 0.70 e figure depends on which grid and which layer D4 meant, and I
have not established that. It is an open question, not a finding.

DIRECTION: against the representation, and against the convenience of the plan. Withdrawing this removes a
reason that had been offered for NOT writing a per-site cap into Stage 7. The cap may be needed after all.

Note for the record: STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC7_20261026.txt, already committed at 03edda7, mentions no sum q^2 budget
anywhere. It is unaffected and needs no change.


E4.4  TWO BOOKKEEPING DEFECTS IN cs6_audit.py. ONE CHANGES NO VERDICT; THE OTHER MAKES A PUBLISHED TABLE MEAN
      SOMETHING OTHER THAN ITS HEADING

(i) THE CONTROL SET IS NOT FILTERED THE WAY THE CLASSIFIED SET IS. cs6_audit.py builds the controls every
threshold is anchored to from the charge count alone -

   g = [d for d in good if d["nb"] == nb and d["npc"] == 0]

- while the single points it classifies are filtered on the optimisation count as well -

   sp = [d for d in good if 0 < d["npc"] <= MM_NPC and d["nopt"] == 0]

- and the result file states the reason the two should match: "a charge-free control is NOT like-for-like once
the geometry has moved". So a charge-free GEOMETRY OPTIMISATION enters the control medians, which is exactly what
that sentence forbids.

MEASURED. 2 of the 352 controls are geometry optimisations. Both are at nb 264, both in
phase2_charge_design/08_validate_relaxed/hpc_text_outputs.tar.xz. Filtering them out moves one published number,
the nb 264 gap median, from 3.2780 to 3.2600 eV; the cycle, Loewdin and dipole medians do not move at all.
ZERO of the 1680 classifications change. The reason is structural rather than lucky: the STRAINED gap test is
"gap below 1.0 eV AND below half its own control's gap", and half of either median is about 1.63 eV, so the
absolute 1.0 eV floor binds and the control gap is never the operative threshold at this basis.

So this is a real defect in the program with no effect on any committed verdict. DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt's
specificity check, control table and every classification stand as committed.

(ii) TWO OF THE FOUR CATEGORIES OVERLAP BY 46 RUNS, AND ONE PUBLISHED TABLE IS ALMOST ENTIRELY THE RUNS THE SAME
PARAGRAPH SAYS ARE EXCLUDED. The categories are

   mm = [d for d in good if d["npc"] > MM_NPC]
   go = [d for d in good if d["npc"] > 0 and d["nopt"] > 0]

so any QM/MM run that is also an optimisation is in both. Measured: 46 runs are in both, and none is in no
category, which is exactly why the published counts sum to 1726 against a corpus of 1680 -

   "1680 completed single points and optimisations on this dianion (118 electrons, 59 occupied)
    352 carry no point charges and are the controls; 1264 are point-charge single points; 47 are
    point-charge geometry optimisations, reported separately because a charge-free control is NOT
    like-for-like once the geometry has moved. A further 63 runs carry more than 200 point charges and
    are QM/MM calculations whose charges are an MM environment rather than a designed arrangement;
    they are excluded from every table below."

The arithmetic is the smaller half. The table headed "THE POINT-CHARGE GEOMETRY OPTIMISATIONS, REPORTED APART"
is built from go and prints

   CLEAN         46
   POLARISED      1

Measured: of those 47 runs, 46 carry 55656 point charges - they are the QM/MM runs the paragraph above says are
excluded from every table below - and exactly ONE is a design-scale arrangement:
phase2_charge_design/08_validate_relaxed/ropt_charged.out, 2 point charges, nb 264, 6 SCF cycles, gap 3.378 eV,
POLARISED. The two charge-free optimisations of item (i) are that run's charge-free counterparts in the same
archive.

SO THE "CLEAN 46" IS 46 MM-ENVIRONMENT QM/MM RUNS AND IS NOT EVIDENCE ABOUT DESIGNED CHARGES AT ALL. It must not
be quoted as reassurance that point-charge geometry optimisations are healthy. The evidence that table actually
holds on designed charges is one run, and it is POLARISED. Nothing in the committed record quotes that table, so
nothing downstream needs withdrawing.

DIRECTION: against the representation. A reading that looked like 46 clean optimisations is one polarised one.

   for a in sorted(p for p in root.rglob("*.tar.xz") if ".git" not in p.parts):

tarballs and that tarfile never sees, of which 588 are ORCA outputs (file names ending .out.xz; the rest are

MEASURED, with cs6_audit.read() unchanged over those files: 415 of them are completed single points on this

scope is all of it. 119 carry no point charge file; 257 would enter the published single-point tables; 24 are
QM/MM.

DOES THE PUBLISHED RESULT CHANGE? NO, AND THIS WAS CHECKED RATHER THAN ASSUMED. Re-forming every control median
from the fuller control set and re-running cs6_audit's verdict() over the 1264 single points it published, ZERO
change category. The medians move as follows, and no run crosses a threshold on any of it:

      nb   basis         controls        median cycles        median gap (eV)

WHAT THEY WOULD ADD, AND THE ONE READING THAT CHANGES. 257 single points: 250 CLEAN, 7 STRAINED, 0 COLLAPSED.
The seven are

   def2-SVP   npc 3   35 cycles  gap 1.402 eV   phase2.2/cs_stage4c/L1_E5_TS/job.out.xz

DIRECTION: against the representation, and firmly. def2-SVP fails earlier than the committed table shows.


E4.6  CHARGE DELIVERED ON AN ECP CENTRE READS AS NO CHARGE, SO THE OBVIOUS FIX FOR E4.5 IS THE WRONG ONE

cs6_audit.py detects charges from one printed line only:

   pc = re.search(r"Reading point charge file\s*\.*\s*ok \((\d+) point charges\)", s)

Stage 4b's Arm B and the whole of Stage 4c's L0, L2 and L3 levels do not use a point-charge file. They place the
charge on a coreless Ne> ECP centre - Marefat Khah's Ne-type pseudopotential, which is the Pauli wall itself.
Such a run prints no point-charge line at all, so it reads npc = 0 and would be taken for a CHARGE-FREE CONTROL.

VERIFIED IN THE OUTPUTS, not inferred. phase2.2/cs_stage4c/L0_E5_R/job.out.xz echoes

   | 30>   Ne>  1.000000     47.48070500    24.05965300    54.92320200  NewECP
   | 42>   Ne> -1.000000     52.34180500    30.42611100    53.41665400  NewECP
   | 54>   Ne>  1.000000     44.86287900    24.52784100    57.08792800  NewECP

and contains zero "Reading point charge file" lines. It is a three-charge arrangement that reads as a control.

MEASURED over all 119 loose charge-free-looking runs, by reading the Ne> charge out of each: 59 carry a charged
Ne> centre - 48 in cs_stage4c, 11 in cs_stage4b, including B_ecp_svp_qp1 and B_ecp_svpd_qp1 at q = +1. The other
60 are genuine (42 have Ne> centres at zero, 18 have no Ne> centre). By basis, the controls the fuller corpus
would add and how many are charged: nb 264, 45 of which 22; nb 402, 38 of which 21; nb 682, 30 of which 16.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE COUNT. Charged runs have lower gaps, more cycles, larger Loewdin charges and
larger dipoles than charge-free ones. Putting them in the control pool RAISES the cycle, Loewdin and dipole
medians and LOWERS the gap median, and every threshold is a multiple of a control median. The classifier becomes
MORE PERMISSIVE and under-reports collapse. So pointing the walker at the loose files without fixing charge
detection first would bias the audit in the flattering direction - which is the single worst thing that could be
done to it.

DIAGNOSTIC 6 AS COMMITTED IS THEREFORE THE CONSERVATIVE READING. Having opened none of these files, it was
contaminated by none of them. Checked rather than assumed: all 352 of its controls were re-opened from the
tarballs and NOT ONE contains an Ne> ECP centre at all, charged or otherwise, so none of them is a charged
arrangement reading as a control. This is the second of the two flattering items and it is not a reason for
comfort: E4.5 and E4.6 happen to cancel in D6's favour, and that is luck, not design.

AND ONE SUBSTANTIVE CONSEQUENCE, which is the most important thing in this erratum. D6.5 says:

   "It does not establish a usable charge model - a Pauli wall for electrons is the fix and it is untested here."

The Pauli wall is not untested in this project. STAGE4B_CRITERIA.txt pre-registered the test and the decision
that follows from it:

   "B1 PASS: with def2-SVPD and the ECP at contact, the gap at q = +1 is at least 2.0 eV, the deviation from the
    trend at q = +1 is within 0.5 kcal/mol, and b agrees between def2-SVP and def2-SVPD within 10%."

   "B1 fails: Arm A's safe distances become the rule for positive spheres in the grid, and Gaussian-smeared
    spheres (PySCF) are the next option to assess."

B1 FAILED. STAGE4B_REPORT.txt: "B1 (SVPD, ECP sphere at contact): gap at q=+1 3.64 eV; deviation from trend at
q=+1 -1.432 kcal/mol (+0.75: -0.739); b SVP -9.681 / SVPD -12.835 (32.6%) -> FAIL".

Read directly out of the two committed outputs, at IDENTICAL coordinates (the Ne> centre of B_ecp_svpd_qp1 and
the sphere of sp_svpd_qp1 both sit at 40.83200000 18.75000000 42.55100000, the contact position), the same basis
and the same charge:

   bare sphere, point charge +1, def2-SVPD    gap 0.550 eV   26 SCF cycles   cs_stage4/sp_svpd_qp1
   Ne> ECP sphere, q = +1, def2-SVPD          gap 3.637 eV   14 SCF cycles   cs_stage4b/B_ecp_svpd_qp1

So the mechanism claim is RIGHT, and measured: a Pauli wall recovers 3.09 eV of gap at the same geometry and
takes the SCF from 26 cycles to 14. What it does not do is fix the energies. B1 required the deviation from the
trend within 0.5 kcal/mol and b within 10 per cent, and got -1.432 kcal/mol and 32.6 per cent.

THE WALL FIXES THE INDICATOR DIAGNOSTIC 6 MEASURES AND NOT THE QUANTITY A BARRIER NEEDS. "A Pauli wall for
electrons is the fix" is therefore withdrawn as written. What may be said is: a Pauli wall demonstrably restores
wavefunction health, and the one committed test of whether it also restores the energetics failed its own
pre-registered criterion. A CLEAN verdict from Diagnostic 6 is necessary and is not sufficient.

DIRECTION: against the plan, and this is the item to carry forward. The fix Stage 7 was expected to rest on is
already measured as not sufficient, in a committed report, by a pre-registered criterion it failed.


WHAT THIS ERRATUM DOES NOT DO, AND WHAT IS LEFT TO DECIDE

It changes no pre-registration, no criteria file, no result file and no code. Six corrections to the record, four
of them against the charge representation.

Open, and Jessica's to decide, each needing a dated pre-registration of its own rather than an edit:

   1. Whether to fix cs6_audit.py's four defects - the control filter, the category overlap, the tarball-only
      walker and the point-charge-only charge detection - and re-run it. A re-run produces a NEW result file; it
      does not edit DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt. E4.6 is the binding constraint: charge detection must be fixed in the
      same change as the walker, or the audit gets more permissive rather than more complete.
   2. Whether the def2-SVP leg of the 9 October 2026 decision in CHARGE_MODEL_DECISIONS.md must be revisited in
      the light of D6, now that E4.5 puts the def2-SVP onset at three charges.
   3. What follows from B1's failure for Stage 7. STAGE4B_CRITERIA.txt's own pre-registered consequence was that
      "Arm A's safe distances become the rule for positive spheres in the grid", and Arm A recorded none at
      q >= +0.50, with Gaussian-smeared spheres named as the next option and recorded elsewhere as unavailable in
      ORCA. Whether that consequence was carried out is not established here and is not asserted.
   4. The electron-count limit E3.2 already flagged: cs6_audit filters to 118 electrons, so the 128- and
      136-electron ion runs are unclassified. Unchanged by this erratum and still open.
