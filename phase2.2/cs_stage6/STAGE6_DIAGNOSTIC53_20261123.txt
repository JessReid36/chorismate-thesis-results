3. THE READINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every run is read by the committed reader of outputs (cs6_d40.py), the committed reading of
stability (cs6_d33b.py) and the committed classifier (cs6_audit.py), as D48 and D50 read theirs.
D50's pre-registration: "the four legs against a control, at D_LW 0.25 e, R_DIP 2.0, GAP_ABS 1.0
eV, R_GAP 0.5, stability on the last analysis. LEFT THE ATOMS: the 24 atoms' Loewdin charges sum
to 0.25 e or more above the control's." The functions of cs6_d53.py that form these readings are
D50's. Before this was written they were run on D50's committed outputs of the design and of its
control, at R and TS, and gave back D50's committed table: 52 values compared, no difference.
They also read the committed outputs of the dianion at def2-TZVPD of D37 (wB97X-V, R and TS) and
of D36 (Hartree-Fock, R and TS) as MEASURED; D37's print a count (117.9996 at R) and D36's print
none.

MEASURED: the run ended normally, its SCF converged, it reports 118 electrons and a final
energy, it read the point charges its folder holds (or none, if it holds none), and the
committed classifier reads its Loewdin charges, its dipole and its number of basis functions.
DID NOT CONVERGE: ORCA says the SCF did not converge. Any other run is NOT MEASURED, and the
result gives ORCA's words.

R53.1  THE RUNS, AND THE COUNT. Each of the 82 runs: measured or not. THE COUNT of a run of CAM
       or WXV is the number of electrons its grid integrates, the last N(Total) ORCA prints. THE
       COUNT HOLDS if it lies within 0.02 e of 118. A measured run of CAM or WXV that prints no
       count does not hold. Hartree-Fock has no count. In D50, on ORCA's default grid, the
       design with functions counted 117.9918 at R.

R53.2  CAN THE SECOND LEVEL BE READ. THE SECOND LEVEL IS READ if the four controls of WXV (GN
       and GF, at R and TS) are all measured, the stability analysis of each ran and was read,
       and each is STABLE on its last analysis. Otherwise THE SECOND LEVEL IS NOT READ, and
       every word of R53.6 is NOT DECIDED.

R53.3  THE CONTROLS. The plain control GN of a level and state is STABLE if it is measured, its
       analysis ran and its last analysis is STABLE; if it is not, the GF and N runs of that
       level and state are NOT READ. The control with functions GF is read against GN as D50
       read its controls, by the three legs that do not divide the density among atoms. D50's
       pre-registration: "THE CONTROL HOLDS if it is measured, none of the three fires and none
       is withheld; DOES NOT HOLD if one fires or its SCF does not converge." Otherwise it is
       not decided. Reported beside the word and not part of it: the Loewdin charge missing from
       the 24 atoms, the Loewdin charge of the six ghost atoms, and the energy of GF against GN.

R53.4  EACH ARRANGEMENT AT EACH LEVEL AND STATE. The F run is read against its GF control
       exactly as D48 and D50 read theirs. It is NOT READ if GF is not measured or not read, or
       if the analysis of GF was not read or is UNSTABLE on its last analysis. D50's
       pre-registration: "TOOK ELECTRONS the SCF converged and either a leg fires or the run
       LEFT THE ATOMS"; "DID NOT TAKE: measured, no leg fires, none withheld, and it did not
       leave the atoms. Otherwise not decided."; "ONTO THE FUNCTIONS if it took electrons and
       left the atoms, A LEG ONLY if a leg fired and it did not." Reported beside it: the charge
       that left the 24 atoms, and the change of the Loewdin charge of the six ghost atoms. THE
       GAIN, in eV: (F minus GF) minus (N minus GN). Below zero, the functions lower the energy
       more when the charges are there. It is formed if all four runs are measured and, at CAM
       and WXV, all four counts hold; at Hartree-Fock no N is run and no gain is formed. The N
       run is read against GN by the four legs and reported.

R53.5  THE BAR, read only for a run that TOOK ELECTRONS. At CAM: ABOVE L if the gain is below
       minus L, WITHIN L otherwise, with L the value of RULE C in the committed d52_rules.tsv,
       0.312704 eV, which D52's result prints as 0.3127. At WXV the same with L2, three times
       wB97X-V's largest distance from the limit in that table: 3 x 0.038367 = 0.115101 eV,
       printed as 0.1151. L2 was written after D52's result was known.

R53.6  THE TWO LEVELS TOGETHER, for each arrangement and state:

       NOT DECIDED                           if THE SECOND LEVEL IS NOT READ; or if, at CAM or
                                             at WXV, the F run is not decided, its control GF
                                             does not HOLD, or the count of F or of GF does not
                                             hold; or if the word below needs a bar and the
                                             gain is not formed
       TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS        TOOK ELECTRONS at CAM and at WXV; marked WITHIN L2
                                             or ABOVE L2 by the gain at WXV
       TAKEN AT THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL ONLY  TOOK ELECTRONS at CAM, DID NOT TAKE at WXV; marked
                                             WITHIN L or ABOVE L by the gain at CAM
       TAKEN AT THE SECOND LEVEL ONLY        DID NOT TAKE at CAM, TOOK ELECTRONS at WXV
       HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS                  DID NOT TAKE at CAM and at WXV

       The rows are taken in that order: NOT DECIDED first. Also reported: how many of the 14
       (arrangement, state) have each word, the fewest charges in an arrangement that TAKES
       ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS, and the words of the design.

R53.7  HARTREE-FOCK, THE BRACKET. What each F run did at Hartree-Fock against its own control,
       by the reading of R53.4. Reported beside R53.6. It changes no word of it.

R53.8  THE DESIGN AGAINST D50. For d11 at CAM: what the run did, the charge that left and the
       gain here (IntAcc 12 for hydrogen) beside D50's (ORCA's default grid): TOOK ELECTRONS,
       +0.5205 e and -0.0898 eV at R; DID NOT TAKE, +0.0392 e and -0.0057 eV at TS. Reported.

No ddE is formed and no design is named good or bad. D53 adopts nothing.

5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The outcomes are taken in this order; the first that occurs decides the next step.

   (a) THE SECOND LEVEL IS NOT READ. D52's pre-registration already says what then holds for a
       capture within L: it is "reported NOT SETTLED AT THIS LEVEL ALONE and stays so until a
       dated amendment names what judges it." The cause is written up first. What CAM and
       Hartree-Fock gave is reported and decides nothing.

   (b) AN ARRANGEMENT TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS, at either state. Bare charges of 0.25 e
       together take electrons from this dianion in this test. The smallest arrangement that
       does so, and whether the design does, are stated. D52's pre-registration: "If they take
       electrons, the wall is the fallback of her order of 17 November 2026, as a test arm under
       AMENDMENT 10, with one electron at walled charges run first." If every such gain at WXV
       is WITHIN L2 the result is said with that mark, and whether a third level is asked before
       the wall is for the amendment.

   (c) NONE OF (a) OR (b), AND AN ARRANGEMENT IS TAKEN AT THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL ONLY AND ABOVE
       L, OR TAKEN AT THE SECOND LEVEL ONLY. The two levels disagree by more than the errors for
       one electron measured in D52 account for. Nothing is concluded about capture; a dated
       document on the cause comes before anything else.

   (d) NONE OF (a) TO (c), AND AN ARRANGEMENT IS TAKEN AT THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL ONLY, WITHIN L.
       The second level does not support the capture, and its gain is within three times the
       working functional's own error for one electron. It is not counted as a capture and not
       counted as holding. The amendment that AMENDMENT 10 leaves open then says at which level
       the test with functions is read from then on, since the working functional reports
       captures that the level nearer the limit does not. D50's capture by the design is read
       the same way if the design falls here.

   (e) EVERY ARRANGEMENT HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS at both states, the design among them. Bare
       charges of 0.25 e together hold in this test. D52's pre-registration: "If, in the
       diagnostic of the dianion, bare charges together hold, the wall is left and a comparative
       run may follow later, as Jessica said." The amendment that AMENDMENT 10 leaves open (what
       a survivor must pass) is then written, and the safe surface is next.

   (f) NONE OF THE ABOVE: at least one (arrangement, state) is NOT DECIDED and every other one
       HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS. Nothing is said of those not decided. If the design is among them
       at either state, the question of this diagnostic is open, and a dated note says what is
       done about the runs that failed (a run is not made again in its folder).

6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, bare charges of one size, seven arrangements at sites
   of one old design. The fifth leg (the monoanion) is not run.

   D52 measured one side of a capture: how strongly each functional holds ONE WHOLE electron at
   the charges. The other side is how firmly each has the dianion hold its own. For the bare
   dianion at R in def2-SVPD the committed detachment energies are +1.2486 eV (CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ)
   and +1.2763 eV (wB97X-V), and D39's coupled cluster gives +1.7702 eV as the difference of two
   CCSD(T) energies and +1.0590 eV as the lowest IP-EOM-CCSD root, 0.7 eV apart. So two
   functionals that agree are not thereby right.

   What D50 measured at R was not one whole electron: 0.5205 e left the atoms, the first
   analysis was UNSTABLE, and the solution found after it has <S**2> = 0.53. Neither D51 nor D52
   measured the error of a functional for a part of an electron shared between the substrate and
   the charges. L and L2 are bars for one whole electron and are used here because they are the
   only measured ones. Hartree-Fock is run as the bracket for this reason and is not a judge.

   A Loewdin charge of a diffuse function divides the density; it does not count electrons that
   moved. That is why each run is read against a control with the same functions, and why the
   gain is given beside it.

   The grid: hydrogen and the ghost atoms have IntAcc 12, carbon and oxygen ORCA's default. THE
   COUNT says whether the grid integrates the 118 electrons; it does not say that an energy is
   converged with the grid to better than D52 found for one electron.
