The captions, quoted, because they are not the same for the three sets:
   Table S1: He ecp-all. All-electron ECP were fitted to reproduce with zero electrons the orbital energies of the 2s2p3s3p shells in the d-aug-cc-pV5Z basis within 6 digits (and for other basis sets with a less digits)
   Table S2: Ne ecp-all. All-electron ECP were fitted to reproduce with zero electrons the orbital energies of the 3s3p3d4s4p4d shells in the d-aug-cc-pwCV5Z basis within 6 digits (and for other basis sets with a less digits)
   Table S3: Ar ecp-all. All-electron ECP were fitted to reproduce with zero elec- trons the orbital energies of the 4s4p4d5s5p5d shells in the aug-cc-pV(6+d)Z basis.

Table S3 is the only one of the three that states no precision, names a singly
augmented basis, and gives a d target skipping the first unoccupied d level.
EXPERIMENT 0 could not validate the Ar block better than 3.067e-03 Eh; D23.5 C23.5
is the consequence.
