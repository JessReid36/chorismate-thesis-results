A11.5  WHAT IS DECIDED

1.  THE OLD ARRANGEMENTS ARE SET ASIDE. A10.3, item 1, stands: "B_n11_q0.25 IS NOT CARRIED
    FORWARD AS A DESIGN THAT HOLDS." No further run is made on it or on any other arrangement of
    D31 to D54, bare or walled. Their committed results stand as committed.

2.  THE SAFE SURFACE IS NOT MEASURED FIRST. Step 3 of A9.4 (v) reads: "The safe surface is
    measured, the grid is rebuilt and a search is run on it, each under its own pre-
    registration." It is replaced by: a search is run on the committed grid of frame 42436 under
    its own pre-registration (DIAGNOSTIC 56), and its survivors are named by A9.4 (i). The grid
    is not rebuilt. The reason is A11.2: single charges of this size have shown no sign in any
    run, and what has failed is size and charges together, which a surface measured with one
    charge does not test. A draft diagnostic that walked one charge outward (numbered 55) was
    built and not committed; the number 55 is not used.

3.  THE CHARGES. Bare point charges, no basis function at a charge, no wall. No charge at a site
    is larger than 0.25 e. This is the region of A11.2 in which no run of the record shows a
    sign. It is a cap on each charge, which D29 does not speak against: in D29's set the
    smallest 'largest charge' among the runs that moved is 0.4125 e.

4.  NO RULE FOR ARRANGEMENTS IS WRITTEN, AND EVERY DESIGN IS MEASURED INSTEAD. D50's pre-
    registration, as A10.1 quotes it, has "the search is not run at 0.25 e before a rule for
    arrangements exists". By D29 no such rule can be a limit on the charges alone, and none has
    been found. That sentence is replaced: the search is run, and each design it places is read
    by the five legs at the working level and at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD before it is called
    anything. A design that fails is dropped; none is rescued.

5.  THE WORD SURVIVOR IS NOT CHANGED. A10.4 (iii): "No search is pre-registered before a later
    dated amendment has said whether it changes (A10.5, item 2)." This is that amendment, and it
    says: A9.4 (i) stands as written, "only if it passes the five legs at the working level AND,
    at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD, is RELIABLE on four legs, passes the fifth leg, and has a ddE
    that is WITHIN the tolerance of its ddE at the working level." A10.5, item 2, says "That a
    run with functions at the positive charges should be part of it follows from A10.2." That is
    not made part of the word. D52 to D54 found the test with functions to turn on 0.03 to 0.09
    eV where the second level's own limit L2 is 0.1151 eV, which those levels do not settle.
    Instead: THE SURVIVORS, at most the ten with the largest lowering, ARE RUN WITH FUNCTIONS AT
    THEIR CHARGES AND BEHIND THE HELIUM WALL under a pre-registration of their own, and what is
    found is reported beside each. A survivor that takes electrons there is reported as such;
    whether it may then be quoted is for the amendment that follows that result.

6.  THE CHARGES OF A DESIGN SUM TO ZERO OR MORE. D43's H9: "a RELIABLE arrangement whose charges
    sum to zero or more keeps it bound"; D43's result: "H9 IS NOT FALSIFIED BY THESE 12
    CONDITIONS." The search is given this as a rule, so that it does not spend its designs on
    arrangements the fifth leg is known to take away. It is not neutrality, which the decisions
    of 20 October leave free. It acts against a lower barrier.

7.  A BUDGET WITH CHARGES OF DIFFERENT SIZES IS FOR LATER, under an amendment of its own. The
    program takes the cap from one number. Until then the budget of decision O3 is set where it
    cannot bind.

8.  THE STOPPING RULE. No further diagnostic on the charge model, the safe distance or electron
    capture is run before the search. D54's own stopping rule stands.
