STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 54 - PRE-REGISTRATION
The design at the second level, from two starts
================================================================================

Written 24 November 2026, after D53's result (results commit 42e776db) and before any run of
D54. D53 NOTE 1, committed with this document, records why the design is NOT DECIDED there.
Jessica, 24 November: "okay do d54".

It adopts no rule for designs and opens or closes no size, no distance and no design. It names
no design good or bad and forms no ddE. D53's result stands as committed whatever this shows.

THIS DOCUMENT AND ITS READINGS WERE WRITTEN WITH D53'S RESULT KNOWN, every number of the design
at every level among them. No run of D54 exists.

THE STOPPING RULE. No diagnostic on electron capture follows D54. Whatever D54 shows, the next
document is the amendment that AMENDMENT 10 leaves open (what a design must pass, and at which
level the test with functions is read), and after it the safe surface. A cell that D54 leaves
NOT DECIDED is judged by that amendment and not by another run. Claude put this rule to Jessica
with D54 on 24 November; her answer is the one quoted above.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D53 (outcome (f)): "If the design is among them at either state, the question of this diagnostic
is open, and a dated note says what is done". Two things are open for the design B_n11_q0.25 at
wB97X-V, the second level.

   (1) The gap leg could not be read. In both runs ORCA set a level shift at the first step of
       the SCF, and the committed rule then withholds the leg (D53 NOTE 1, N1.2). No other leg
       fired: +0.0265 e left the atoms at R and +0.0335 e at TS, and both runs were STABLE on
       their first analysis.

   (2) A capture has not been looked for at the second level from where it was found. At
       CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ the run at R took electrons (+0.5258 e left, <S**2> = 0.5320, a gain of
       0.0917 eV, WITHIN L). The run at wB97X-V started from ORCA's own guess and never from
       that solution. And D53 showed that the stability analysis can report STABLE where a lower
       solution exists (NOTE 1, N1.6). So D53's numbers do not establish that there is no
       capture at the second level.

Every design a search finds with negative charges near the substrate can meet the same two
things. D54 is run to settle how such a design is tested, on the one arrangement where a capture
has been seen.

2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five runs. Frame 42436, the dianion (118 electrons), def2-TZVPD, in vacuo, the eleven charges of
B_n11_q0.25 and the functions of D50 (set X2, treatment A) at the six positive sites, as in
D53's runs of the design.

   run                level  state  starts from D53's   what it is
   DI7_WXV_O_d11_R    WXV    R      DI6_WXV_F_d11_R     the second level from its own start
   DI7_WXV_O_d11_TS   WXV    TS     DI6_WXV_F_d11_TS    the same at TS
   DI7_WXV_S_d11_R    WXV    R      DI6_CAM_F_d11_R     the second level from the capture
   DI7_CAM_S_d11_R    CAM    R      DI6_CAM_F_d11_R     the control of the way of starting
   DI7_HF_S_d11_R     HF     R      DI6_CAM_F_d11_R     the bracket

'Starts from': the run of D53 whose converged orbitals the run reads. DI6_CAM_F_d11_R is the run
of D53 in which the electrons were taken. Its orbital file holds separate orbitals for the two
spins: after its first analysis ORCA wrote "Restart requested: Orbitals will be transformed and
reconvergence attempted". The three runs that start from it therefore ask for separate orbitals
of the two spins.

Each input is the committed input of D53's run of that level and state with two changes and no
other. The first line gains the word MORead, and for the three runs above also UKS (UHF at
Hartree-Fock, in place of HF), spelled as D52's committed inputs spell them. One line is added,
which names the file the orbitals are read from: "start.gbw". The first lines:

   DI7_WXV_O_d11_R    "! wB97X-V def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF MORead"
   DI7_WXV_O_d11_TS   "! wB97X-V def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF MORead"
   DI7_WXV_S_d11_R    "! UKS wB97X-V def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF MORead"
   DI7_CAM_S_d11_R    "! UKS CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF MORead"
   DI7_HF_S_d11_R     "! UHF def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF MORead"

THE START FILES. start.gbw is a copy of the orbital file (job.gbw) that the named run of D53
left in its folder on hpc1. The harvest of D53 deleted nothing there. These files are not in the

anything is submitted, copies them into place and checks each against that list. It changes

   job  name       its runs, in the order made                          cap of one  at most

ESTIMATE of the cost, not a measurement: 2 to 8 hours for the two jobs together, one to five

3. THE READINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every run is read by the functions of D53's committed reader, which are D50's: MEASURED, DID NOT
CONVERGE or NOT MEASURED as there; the four legs against D53's committed control with the same
functions and no charge; the charge that left the 24 atoms; the gain with D53's committed
partner without functions and its plain control. D50's pre-registration: "TOOK ELECTRONS the SCF
converged and either a leg fires or the run LEFT THE ATOMS"; "DID NOT TAKE: measured, no leg
fires, none withheld, and it did not leave the atoms. Otherwise not decided." The controls and
the partners are not run again: they are D53's outputs, which the reader holds to the committed
files.

THE SAME SOLUTION: two runs are the same solution if their energies lie within 0.0010 eV of each
other and the charge that left the atoms within 0.02 e. These two are absolute and are set here,
not measured: the first is about a hundredth of the gain of the capture at the working
functional (0.0917 eV), the second is the bound the count of electrons is held to. R54.2 reports
how closely a run started again from its own solution returns to it.

R54.1  THE RUNS. Each of the five: measured or not; the count of electrons at CAM and WXV, which
       HOLDS within 0.02 e of 118; whether ORCA printed that it set a level shift; and whether
       the run STARTED FROM ITS FILE: the energy of its first SCF step lies less than half as
       far above the lowest of the run as in D53's run of the same input, which started from
       ORCA's own guess (there 3.205 Eh at WXV at R, 3.256 at WXV at TS, 0.600 at CAM and 0.780
       at Hartree-Fock). A run that did not start from its file is NOT READ.

R54.2  THE WAY OF STARTING. DI7_CAM_S_d11_R starts the working functional from its own capture
       solution. THE WAY OF STARTING IS REPRODUCED if that run is measured, its count holds, it
       STARTED FROM ITS FILE, and it TOOK ELECTRONS with 0.25 e or more leaving the atoms.
       Whether it is THE SAME SOLUTION as D53's DI6_CAM_F_d11_R is reported beside it. If the
       way of starting is not reproduced, the runs from the capture solution at wB97X-V and at
       Hartree-Fock are NOT READ.

R54.3  THE SECOND LEVEL FROM ITS OWN START, at R and at TS: what the run did by the four legs,
       as D53 read its own run; and whether it is THE SAME SOLUTION as D53's run of the same
       input.

R54.4  THE SECOND LEVEL FROM THE CAPTURE SOLUTION, at R. The run is held against DI7_WXV_O_d11_R
       (against D53's DI6_WXV_F_d11_R if that run is not measured or not read).

       NOT READ                          R54.2 is not reproduced, or the run is not measured,
                                         or it did not start from its file, or the run it is
                                         held against is not measured
       RETURNED                          it is THE SAME SOLUTION as the run it is held against
       A CAPTURE SOLUTION                it is another solution and 0.25 e or more left the
                                         atoms
       ANOTHER SOLUTION WITHOUT CAPTURE  it is another solution and less than 0.25 e left the
                                         atoms

       Another solution is BELOW, ABOVE or LEVEL by its energy against that run: BELOW if lower
       by more than 0.0010 eV, ABOVE if higher by more than that, LEVEL otherwise. Reported with
       it: <S**2>, the last stability analysis, the four legs, and the gain.

R54.5  THE DESIGN AT THE SECOND LEVEL. At R the word follows the lowest solution found. TAKES
       ELECTRONS if the run from its own start TOOK ELECTRONS; or the run from the capture
       solution is A CAPTURE SOLUTION that is BELOW or LEVEL; or it is ANOTHER SOLUTION WITHOUT
       CAPTURE that is BELOW or LEVEL and itself reads TOOK ELECTRONS by the four legs. DOES NOT
       TAKE if the run from its own start DID NOT TAKE and the run from the capture solution
       RETURNED, or is a solution ABOVE, or is ANOTHER SOLUTION WITHOUT CAPTURE that is BELOW or
       LEVEL and itself reads DID NOT TAKE by the four legs. NOT DECIDED otherwise, and if a
       count does not hold. At TS no capture solution is known at any level to start from; the
       word is that of the run from its own start. THE TWO LEVELS TOGETHER are then formed with
       the cells of the working functional in D53's committed table (at R TOOK ELECTRONS, WITHIN
       L; at TS DID NOT TAKE), by the words of R53.6: TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS (marked
       WITHIN L2 or ABOVE L2 by the gain of the run in which the electrons were taken; L2 =
       0.1151 eV), TAKEN AT THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL ONLY (WITHIN L; L = 0.3127 eV), TAKEN AT THE
       SECOND LEVEL ONLY, HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS, NOT DECIDED.

R54.6  HARTREE-FOCK FROM THE CAPTURE SOLUTION. DI7_HF_S_d11_R against D53's DI6_HF_F_d11_R, by
       the words of R54.4. Reported. It changes no word.

No ddE is formed and no design is named good or bad. D54 adopts nothing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Taken in this order; the first that occurs decides. In every case the stopping rule holds: no
further run on capture.

   (a) THE DESIGN TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS at R. The lowest solution found at wB97X-V
       takes electrons. Bare charges of 0.25 e in this arrangement take electrons at both levels
       in this test; the mark by L2 is said with it. As D53's pre-registration has it for this
       outcome, the wall is then the fallback, as a test arm under AMENDMENT 10, and the
       amendment says what a design with negative charges must pass.

   (b) TAKEN AT THE SECOND LEVEL ONLY at TS. Not expected by any number of D53. Nothing is
       concluded; the amendment records it as unexplained.

   (c) AT R TAKEN AT THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL ONLY (WITHIN L), AND AT TS HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS. The
       second level does not support the capture from either start, and the gain at the working
       functional is within three times its own error for one electron. It is not counted as a
       capture and not counted as holding. The amendment then says at which level the test with
       functions is read from then on, and whether a design is also started from a capture
       solution where one is known.

   (d) ANYTHING ELSE: a cell is NOT DECIDED (the way of starting not reproduced, a run not
       measured or not started from its file, a leg withheld again, a count that does not hold).
       It stays NOT DECIDED, the result gives the reason, and the amendment says what judges
       such a cell. No run is made again.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, one old design, charges of one size. The design is
   not carried forward as a design that holds (AMENDMENT 10) whatever D54 shows.

   A run started from the capture solution finds a solution near that start. If it RETURNED, no
   capture solution was found from this start. That is stronger than D53's evidence and is still
   not a proof that none exists.

   The stability analysis is reported with every run and is not a proof (D53 NOTE 1, N1.6).

   L and L2 are bars for one whole electron (D52). The solution at the working functional has
   about half an electron on the functions; no bar was measured for that.

   Hartree-Fock was not a clean bracket in D53. Its run here is reported and decides nothing.
