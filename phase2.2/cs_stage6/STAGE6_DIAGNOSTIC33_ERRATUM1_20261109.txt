WRITTEN BEFORE STAGE A HAS BEEN READ. No output of this diagnostic has been examined. This is not
a tolerance being revised to accommodate a result; it is a fault in the pre-registration, found
while building the reader, recorded before the reader was run.


E1.1  WHAT G1 SAYS

   "G1  THE NEW A MATRIX MUST PASS THE COMMITTED A MATRIX'S OWN CHECKS, at the same three sites and
   with the same per-site limits: 0.0728, 0.0611 and 0.0237 kcal/mol per e for sites 1678, 1954 and
   1612."


E1.2  WHY THAT IS WRONG

The committed check is RELATIVE. phase2.2/cs5_amatrix.py computes

   tol = 0.01 + 0.01 * abs(a)

and the three numbers G1 pins are simply that formula evaluated at the COMMITTED a: site 1678 has
a = -6.2779 giving 0.0728, site 1954 has a = +5.1138 giving 0.0611, site 1612 has a = +1.3716
giving 0.0237. All three reproduce exactly.

Stage A computes a new a at each of those sites. The committed formula therefore gives a new
tolerance at each, and G1's pinned numbers are the tolerance for a quantity this diagnostic is not
measuring. Pinning them also contradicts WORKING_RULES.md guard rail 4, TOLERANCES RELATIVE, NOT
ABSOLUTE, which exists for exactly this failure.


E1.3  HOW IT IS RESOLVED, AND WHY THIS CANNOT LOOSEN ANYTHING

G1 is read as requiring BOTH tolerances, and a site passes only if it passes both:

   |a_fd - a_new|  <=  min( G1's pinned limit , 0.01 + 0.01 * abs(a_new) )

Taking the minimum means the stricter of the two governs at every site, whichever that is. So this
resolution cannot admit a finite-difference result that either tolerance alone would have rejected.
It is strictly at least as demanding as G1 as written, and strictly at least as demanding as the
committed formula.

Both numbers are reported at every site, with which one bound the test.

This is also the faithful reading of G1's own first clause. It says the new A matrix "MUST PASS THE
COMMITTED A MATRIX'S OWN CHECKS" - and the committed check is the relative formula. The pinned
numbers are that formula's output at the old a, quoted as if they were the check itself.


E1.4  WHAT IS NOT CHANGED

G1's stop is unchanged: if any site fails, a_i^new is not used and the diagnostic stops after stage
A. G1's last sentence is unchanged and is the reason this erratum exists at the time it does - a
finite-difference check that fails is not re-scored with a wider limit, and nothing here widens
one.

No other gate, criterion or reading of DIAGNOSTIC 33 is affected. The pre-registration is not
edited.
