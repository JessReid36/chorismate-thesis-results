E2.1  THE CLAIM

The pre-registration, D32.1, states

   "DIAGNOSTIC 23 found the argon ECP wall cut the Loewdin relocation from +0.875 e to
   +0.093 e at the strongest field then available, and argon was chosen because its repulsive
   wall matches the validated Amber nitrogen Lennard-Jones wall more closely than neon or
   helium at the 10, 1 and 0.1 kcal/mol contours."

The second half of that sentence - the reason argon was chosen - is correct and is NOT
withdrawn. Only the measurement in the first half is.

and the result repeats it as

   "DIAGNOSTIC 23 measured a factor of nine at the strongest field then available."

Both are WITHDRAWN.


E2.2  WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS

+0.875 e IS REAL, AND IS NOT WHAT THE CLAIM USES IT FOR. It is the Loewdin rise at
Sigma|q| = 5.6433 e in def2-SVPD, a BARE condition. It is committed and it is correctly used
elsewhere - D28's reading of it across three functionals (+0.875 -> +0.750 -> +0.750 e) is
sound and is not touched by this erratum.

+0.093 e IS IN NO OUTPUT AND NO RESULT. Before this erratum the string "0.093 e" occurred
exactly once in the whole committed Stage 6 record, and that occurrence is line 50 of D32's own
pre-registration. There is no measurement behind it. This erratum quotes the figure in order to
withdraw it, so from here the string occurs twice and in no other file.

DIAGNOSTIC 23 MEASURED NEITHER QUANTITY. D23 measured d_shield in kcal/mol and the propagation
factor f. It has no bare arm at all: all 50 of its runs are ctl, wall0, wall_F1, wall_F2,
wall_A or wall_M. A bare-against-walled Loewdin comparison cannot be formed from it. Reading
its committed outputs with the committed classifier phase2.2/cs6_audit.py
(sha256 1dda394a134b3c7265df1898c9b5dc507d01c41d73aa5e59274495a1e7005dea), the Loewdin rise
over d23's own control, across every argon arm, has a maximum of

   +0.0422 e     ar_d16_S6-3_wall_F1_R

THE ONE DIAGNOSTIC THAT DID COMPARE BARE AGAINST WALLED FOUND NO EFFECT. DIAGNOSTIC 18 put a
complete 20-sphere design at full field at def2-SVP and reports, in d18/DIAGNOSTIC18_RESULT.txt

   bare_R   maxlw 0.4554   excess 0.0684
   wall_R   maxlw 0.4559   excess 0.0689

a change of +0.0005 e, under its own verdict NEITHER ARM MOVES - because at that basis there
was no relocation for the wall to remove.

So the withdrawn figure was a composite: a real bare number from one diagnostic, paired with a
walled number that exists nowhere, attributed to a third diagnostic that measured neither of
them. It entered the pre-registration from working notes rather than from the committed
record, which is the failure the full-record rule exists to prevent.


E2.3  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT AFFECT

IT CHANGES NO DIAGNOSTIC 32 VERDICT. The grid, the charge-free control, the pairing against
DIAGNOSTIC 31 and all five criteria are internal to DIAGNOSTIC 31 and DIAGNOSTIC 32. The
result itself states of the factor comparison that it is "reported for comparison and gate[s]
nothing", and that is accurate: no gate, no reading and no verdict consumes it. R32.1's bare
and walled rises and the factors computed from them are measured from committed outputs
against a committed control and stand unchanged.

WHAT IT DID AFFECT is the motivation. D32.1 presented a factor of nine as an existing
measurement and framed the diagnostic as a test of whether it "carries over". It was not a
measurement, so there was nothing to carry over, and the expectation it set was not evidence.
That the expectation was borne out does not retrospectively source it.

THE ARGON CHOICE IS UNAFFECTED. Argon was selected in D23 by matching the validated Amber
nitrogen Lennard-Jones wall at the 10, 1 and 0.1 kcal/mol contours more closely than neon or
helium. That is separately recorded and is not withdrawn. D32.8's limitation that this is one
ECP species, never validated against anything physical, stands as written.


E2.4  THE REPLACEMENT STATEMENT

For any future document that needs it:

   Before DIAGNOSTIC 32 the record contained NO measurement of the argon wall's effect on
   Loewdin relocation against a bare arm. The only committed bare-against-walled comparison,
   DIAGNOSTIC 18, found no effect at def2-SVP, where no relocation was present to remove.
   DIAGNOSTIC 32's own R32.1 is the first such measurement in this project: at def2-SVPD, with
   relocation present, the wall reduces it by a factor of 7.8 to 20.7 across eleven of twelve
   conditions, and by 2.3 at B/n20_q1.00.


E2.5  WHERE ELSE IT APPEARS

The claim occurs in three places. Two are corrected by this erratum: D32's pre-registration
and D32's result. The third is the project document D31_FINDINGS_20261108.md, which is not
part of the committed Stage 6 record and is corrected in place.

No other committed document carries it. D28_FINDINGS_AND_WITHDRAWAL_20261105.md cites +0.875 e
correctly, as a bare relocation figure compared across functionals, with no walled pairing and
no attribution to DIAGNOSTIC 23, and needs no change.
