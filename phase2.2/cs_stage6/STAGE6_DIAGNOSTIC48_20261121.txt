STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 48
Is the estimate S of DIAGNOSTIC 47 a rule? A test on more sites and on arrangements, with basis
functions at every positive charge.

Pre-registered 21 November 2026, before any D48 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.
No committed file used the number 48 when this was written.
It is read under AMENDMENT 5 (stability), AMENDMENT 6 (the detachment energy) and AMENDMENT 9. It
adopts no rule, rebuilds no grid, runs no search, and opens or closes no size, no distance and no
design.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D47 (result of 21 November 2026) ran bare positive charges with and without basis functions placed
at the charge. With them, +0.75 e at site 1124 and +1.00 e at 9 A each took an electron from the
substrate, though plain def2-TZVPD flags neither; +0.50 e at site 1124 did not. D47's
pre-registration had written down an estimate, S, of whether a bare charge binds an electron more
strongly than the dianion holds its own, and what S predicted for those four runs before they were
made. The result: "predictions: borne out 4, NOT borne out 0, none made 0, not decided 0."

D47's pre-registration also fixed what follows from that outcome: "Every prediction made is borne
out: S may be put forward, in a pre-registration of its own, as a rule to be tested on more sites
and on arrangements. Nothing is built on it before that." This is that pre-registration. Claude
asked on 21 November 2026: "shall I write the D48 pre-registration, the test of S as a rule?"
Jessica: "yes".

WHY IT MATTERS. S is formed from two maps that are already committed and costs nothing for an
arrangement. If it holds, whether a positive charge is safe from this failure can be asked of every
arrangement a search looks at. If it does not, the only test of an arrangement stays a run with
functions at its charges.

WHAT FOUR RUNS DID NOT SHOW, and this diagnostic asks:
   (a) WHERE THE ZERO OF S LIES. D47's four runs were far from it: S was +1.07, +0.73, -2.57 and
       -10.04 eV. Part A runs the sizes on both sides of it, 0.05 e apart.
   (b) OTHER SITES. The four runs were on one line. Part A takes, beside site 1124, three more
       outer sites, picked by a rule that spreads them over what S says.
   (c) ARRANGEMENTS. The four runs were single charges. In S the other charges enter twice: through
       the detachment energy, and through the potential at the charge. Part B runs five
       arrangements, among them the two designs that D44 finds RELIABLE.
   (d) WHAT THE FUNCTIONS AT THE CHARGE CAN BIND. D47.4: "The ghost centre carries the def2-TZVPD
       functions of hydrogen. They describe an electron bound to a charge of 0.75 or 1.00 e well,
       one bound to 0.50 e less well, and one bound to 0.25 e hardly at all." Near the zero of S the
       charges are 0.40 to 0.70 e. Part C measures what those functions bind at each size.
   (e) WHAT HOLDS THE LAST ELECTRON ONCE CHARGES ARE PRESENT. S takes that detachment energy to
       first order. Results already committed show the first-order value too high for the designs
       of D43 (section 8). Part V measures it for the two new pairs of part B.

2. THE CONVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Frame 42436, the committed geometries of d33a at the reactant (R) and the transition state (TS),
in vacuo, bare point charges. Every run is the dianion, but for the four monoanions of part V.
27.211386245988 eV per Eh, 13.605693122994 eV per Rydberg, 0.5291772083 A per bohr, as D47.

THE LEVEL is CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD, the method line and the body of the committed inputs of
D44, with the stability analysis: "! CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF".

   THE PLAIN CONTROL of a job is the committed input d44/CAMTZ_C_ctl_R/job.inp, byte for
      byte, with the committed geometry beside it. It is the first run of every job but E1. A job
      is USED only if that run gives back the committed energy within 1e-6 Eh and its last
      stability analysis is STABLE. Otherwise every run of the job is NOT READ.
   A RUN WITH A CHARGE is the committed input d44/CAMTZ_B_n02_q0.50_di_R/job.inp with its last
      line replaced by the committed geometry written out and, after it, one ghost-atom line
      "H: x y z" for EACH POSITIVE CHARGE, in the order of the point-charge file. A ghost atom is
      the def2-TZVPD functions of a hydrogen atom with no nucleus and no electron. Its centre is
      0.01 A beyond the charge, on the line from the substrate atom nearest the charge (over both
      states) through the charge. This is D47's level CAMTZG and D47's rule; for site 1124 it gives
      D47's centre, and the inputs of +0.50 e at site 1124 and of its control are D47's, byte for
      byte. Negative charges carry no ghost atom.
   A GHOST CONTROL is the committed control input with the same ghost atoms and no charge, at the
      same state. A run with a charge is read against the ghost control that carries its ghost
      atoms. If that control is not measured, or its last stability analysis is not STABLE, the run
      is NOT READ.
   A RUN OF PART V is a committed input of D44, byte for byte: for a dianion,
      d44/CAMTZ_B_n02_q0.50_di_R/job.inp; for a monoanion,
      d44/CAMTZ_B_n02_q0.50_mo_R/job.inp. Beside it the pair's point-charge file and the
      committed geometry. No functions at the charges.

THE LEGS are those of D43, D44 and D47, read by the committed classifier cs6_audit.py at its
committed thresholds: the Loewdin rise (D_LW 0.25 e), the dipole ratio (R_DIP 2.0), the gap (GAP_ABS
1.0 eV and R_GAP 0.5; withheld in a run that engaged TRAH or a level shift, or whose control did)
and the LAST stability analysis (AMENDMENT 5). A dianion run whose SCF did not converge is flagged
(D31.3). The word of a run:
   FLAGGED        a leg fires, or the SCF did not converge
   NOT FLAGGED    none of those, the gap readable and the stability analysis read
   HELD           none of those, and the gap withheld or an analysis not read
   NOT READ       the run's job or its control cannot be used
   NOT MEASURED   no readable output
The fifth leg is read in part V only, for the two pairs and without functions at the charges.

WHAT THE RUN DID is the reading D47's pre-registration made for runs with functions at the charge,
unchanged:
   LEFT THE ATOMS   the sum of the 24 atoms' Loewdin charges is above its control's by D_LW
                    (0.25 e) or more
   TOOK ELECTRONS   the SCF converged and either a leg fires or the run LEFT THE ATOMS
   DID NOT TAKE     the run is NOT FLAGGED and did not leave the atoms
   not decided      anything else
With several ghost atoms the result also names the positive charge whose functions gained most, and
by how much. That is reported; it enters no word.

HOW A RUN TOOK THEM, a mark this diagnostic adds beside D47's reading and not in place of it:
   ONTO THE FUNCTIONS   the run TOOK ELECTRONS and LEFT THE ATOMS
   A LEG ONLY           the run TOOK ELECTRONS because a leg fired, and it did not leave the atoms
S estimates whether an electron goes to a charge. A run that is A LEG ONLY did something else: a
leg fired while the 24 atoms kept their charge. It is known to happen: D47's pre-registration set
out, read after the fact from D44's committed outputs, that in the four designs D44 finds
UNRELIABLE, with no functions at the charges, the charge of one substrate atom, O3, falls by 0.89
to 1.73 e at R. B_n05_q0.50 is one of the four. Such a run is not safe, and the result names it;
but it says nothing for or against S. So the counts and the separation of R48.5 are given by D47's
reading and, where a run is A LEG ONLY, again with those runs set aside. THE OUTCOMES OF SECTION 6
ARE READ WITH THOSE RUNS SET ASIDE.

S, AS D47 WROTE IT. From the committed maps of D47 at def2-TZVPD (orca_vpot on the charge-free
dianion and monoanion, d47/T1_C_ctl and d47/T1_C_bare, at R and TS) and the committed energies
under them. V is in Eh per e; v = (V_monoanion - V_dianion) x 27.211386245988, eV per e. For a
positive charge q_i at site i among charges q_j, at a state:
   VDE1 = VDE_bare + SUM_j q_j v_j
   S_i  = VDE1 - 27.211386245988 x [ V_monoanion(i) + SUM_(j not i) q_j / r_ij ] - 13.605693122994 x q_i^2
eV, r_ij in bohr. VDE_bare is the committed detachment energy of the bare substrate, +1.1687 eV at
R and +0.6892 eV at TS. VDE1 is what holds the last electron on the dianion with the charges
present, to first order. The bracket is the potential that the monoanion and the other charges make
at charge i. The last term is the energy with which a bare charge q_i binds one electron in a
complete basis. S of an arrangement is the smallest S_i over its positive charges; the result says
which charge that is. THE ZERO SIZE of a site at a state is the size at which S of one positive
charge alone there is zero.

S IS FORMED NOW. cs6_d48_prepare.py forms S for every condition from the committed files and writes
it, with what it predicts, into d48/CONDITIONS.tsv, which is committed with this document before
any job is submitted. The script refuses to write anything unless the S it forms for the
single-charge conditions of D47 is the S that d47.tsv holds. The reader forms S again on its own.

WHAT S PREDICTS, with the band D47 fixed:
      DID NOT TAKE     if S is +0.5 eV or more at that state
      TOOK ELECTRONS   if S is -0.5 eV or less
      nothing          between them
A PREDICTION is BORNE OUT if the run did what S predicted; NOT BORNE OUT if it did the other; NOT
DECIDED if the run is not decided. Where S predicts nothing, none is made, and the run is reported
all the same.

S SEPARATES what the runs did if the largest S at which any run TOOK ELECTRONS is below the smallest
S at which any run DID NOT TAKE. Both numbers are reported, over every run with a charge, whether a
prediction was made for it or not. If S does not separate, the runs that lie between the two
numbers are listed.

THE CALIBRATION (part C). One electron, the functions of one ghost atom, and a bare charge q
0.01 A from their centre: no substrate and no nucleus. B(q) is minus its energy: what these
functions bind. In a complete basis B is 13.605693122994 x q^2. It is run at two levels:
   HF    Hartree-Fock ("! UHF def2-TZVPD TightSCF"), which is exact for one electron but for the
         functions. 13.605693122994 x q^2 minus B(HF) is what the functions lose.
   CAM   the functional and the integral approximations of the runs; the dispersion term is left
         out, being zero for one centre ("! UKS CAM-B3LYP def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF").
Both start from the core-Hamiltonian guess (Guess HCore in the input): there is no atom from which
ORCA's default guess could take a density. They run on one core.
S' is S with 13.605693122994 x q_i^2 replaced by B(CAM) at q_i: what S becomes when its last term is
what these functions and this functional give. S' PREDICTS NOTHING. It is set beside S, and the
same question, whether it separates what the runs did, is asked of it. It is formed only for an
arrangement whose every positive size has a measured B(CAM).

THE DETACHMENT ENERGY, MEASURED (part V). VDE = E(monoanion) - E(dianion) at the same state with
the same charges, positive means the dianion holds its last electron (AMENDMENT 6). Its words,
BOUND and METASTABLE, are D42's, by its committed word_of(), as D44 used them: BOUND is a VDE above
zero with the monoanion's last stability analysis STABLE; METASTABLE is a VDE at or below zero. The
dianion of part V is read on the four legs against the committed control of its state. Job V1 is
USED, like every job, only if its plain control gives back the committed energy.

S_m, WHERE THE DETACHMENT ENERGY IS MEASURED. S takes the detachment energy with the charges
present to first order (VDE1). S_m is S with a detachment energy measured at def2-TZVPD, from the
monoanion and the dianion with the charges and no functions at them, in place of VDE1. It exists:
   for the designs, from D44's measurement (d44.tsv), where D44's four legs find the design
      RELIABLE: B_n02_q0.50 and B_n11_q0.25;
   for the pairs, from part V, at a state where the pair's dianion without functions is NOT FLAGGED,
      both energies are measured and the monoanion's last stability analysis is STABLE.
S_m PREDICTS NOTHING; it is set beside S. One reading is fixed on it. For a run that TOOK ELECTRONS
where S predicted DID NOT TAKE, the first-order detachment energy
   ACCOUNTS FOR IT            if S_m of that run is below +0.5 eV
   DOES NOT ACCOUNT FOR IT    if S_m is +0.5 eV or more
   NOT KNOWN                  if the run has no S_m.

4. READINGS, FIXED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R48.1  THE CONTROLS. Every plain and ghost control: its energy, the committed energy of the plain
       control it is set against, the stability analysis, the number of basis functions, and the

       state: the largest size that DID NOT TAKE and the smallest that TOOK ELECTRONS, with S at
       both and the zero size; or NOT ORDERED, if a smaller charge took electrons and a larger one
       did not, or a run is not decided.
R48.4  PART B. For each arrangement and state: S, S', S_m where it exists, the charge S names, VDE1,

       what the run did, and whether the prediction was borne out. For each pair: what the single
       charge did beside what the pair did.

       control, at R and TS): this energy minus D47's committed one.
R48.8  THE OTHER HALF, ON RESULTS ALREADY COMMITTED. A charge can also fail the other way: negative

S AS A RULE.
   EVERY PREDICTION MADE IS BORNE OUT, AND S SEPARATES what the runs did: an amendment may propose
      S as a further test of a design (every positive charge at or above a threshold, at both
      states) and as the condition a search holds positive charges to, in place of a distance. The
      threshold it proposes may not lie below +0.5 eV, nor below the largest S at which a run of
      D48 took electrons. The amendment must say how part C enters it and, for arrangements, where
      the detachment energy comes from: R48.8 and R48.9 say how far VDE1 can be trusted.
   A RUN TOOK ELECTRONS WHERE S PREDICTED IT WOULD NOT (S at +0.5 eV or more): S as it is written
      would pass an arrangement that is not safe. It is not proposed as a test. The result gives
      the largest S at which a run took electrons; a threshold above that is tested, in a
      pre-registration of its own, before anything is built on it.
   A RUN DID NOT TAKE WHERE S PREDICTED IT WOULD (S at -0.5 eV or less), and no run failed the other
      way: S errs toward caution there. If S' of that run is above -0.5 eV, the functions at the
      charge and the functional account for it, and the run does not speak against S in a complete
      basis. If S' is at -0.5 eV or less too, S overstates the binding for a reason this diagnostic
      does not name. Either way S may still be proposed as above: it would refuse more than it
      must, and the amendment says so.
   S DOES NOT SEPARATE though no prediction failed: the runs between the two numbers are ones S did
      not decide. A threshold can then only be proposed at or above the largest S at which a run
      took electrons.
   A RUN OF AN ARRANGEMENT TOOK ELECTRONS WHERE S PREDICTED IT WOULD NOT, and no single charge did:
      the fault lies in what S does with the other charges. If the first-order detachment energy
      ACCOUNTS FOR IT (section 2), S as written is kept for single charges only, and what may be
      put forward for arrangements, in a pre-registration of its own, is S with the detachment
      energy measured: two more runs for each arrangement. If it DOES NOT ACCOUNT FOR IT, or that
      is NOT KNOWN, S is not put forward for arrangements at all until the cause is found.
   FEWER THAN HALF of the predictions made are decided: nothing is concluded about S.

7. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D48.1  One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, bare point charges that stand for no chemical group.
       Four sites for single charges and five arrangements.
D48.2  The functions at a charge are the def2-TZVPD functions of hydrogen. They bind an electron to
       a small charge less well than a complete basis would. So a run that DID NOT TAKE shows that
       these functions describe no electron leaving; it does not show that none would in a complete
       basis. A run that TOOK ELECTRONS has no such weakness. Part C measures the size of the
       shortfall and removes none of it.
D48.3  LEFT THE ATOMS is set at 0.25 e, the committed threshold D_LW, used on a quantity it was not
       written for (D47). A run that moves less than that onto the functions at a charge, and fires
       no leg, counts as DID NOT TAKE; the amount is printed for every run. With several ghost atoms
       the sum counts what all of them hold together, so a sum made of small parts at many charges
       can reach 0.25 e with no electron on any one of them; the charge whose functions gained most
       is printed to tell the two apart. Loewdin charges divide electrons among centres by a
       convention, and an approximate functional can spread a fraction of an electron where an exact
       one would not.
D48.4  The fifth leg is read for the two pairs only, and without functions at the charges. Negative
       charges are run only inside arrangements. R48.8 compares an estimate with results that were
       committed before this was written; it tests nothing new. Part V is four values at one site
       pair: it can show VDE1 wrong there, and cannot show it right elsewhere.
D48.5  Every charge is at an outer site. Nothing is said of a charge closer than 3.50 A to an atom,
       where the legs of the working level, and not this failure, set the limit so far.
D48.6  Three kinds of input have not run before in this record: several ghost atoms in one run;
       and the two inputs of the calibration, which hold one electron and no nucleus. If ORCA
       refuses one of them its run is NOT MEASURED and the others are unaffected. A calibration run
       that does not end is stopped after 900 seconds.
D48.7  S is an estimate: one electron, nothing relaxed, potentials to first order, the charge
       treated as a nucleus. Where it is borne out, that is at the sites, sizes and arrangements
       run. The sizes are 0.05 e apart: where S's zero lies is known no more closely than that.
D48.8  The band of 0.5 eV was fixed by D47 before any run. D48 does not fit it and does not move
       it.
