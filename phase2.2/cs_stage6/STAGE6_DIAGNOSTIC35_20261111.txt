STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 35
Stage 4e's five levels of theory, on frame 42436's stationary points.

Pre-registered 11 November 2026, before any D35 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The table of reference barriers has two halves that cannot be compared. Ten rows are
frame 42436 and five are frame 41786, and the five at 41786 are Stage 4e's levels -
B3LYP/6-31G(d), HF/6-31G(d), MP2/6-31G(d) and wB97X-D3/def2-SVP - which exist at no
other frame. A barrier measured at one geometry may not be set beside a barrier
measured at another, so the method comparison those five were run for cannot be read
against anything in Stage 6.

The geometries are not merely different, they are of different kinds.

Frame 42436's reactant and transition state are the SP package's own optimised
stationary points, r_irc/final.xyz converged in six cycles and ts/final.xyz in seven.
STAGE6_DECISIONS_20261021.md decision S1 chose them for Stage 6 and says why it did
not choose the development set, verbatim:

   Its "reactant" is NEB band image 0 and its "TS" is the climbing image. Neither is a
   stationary point.

Frame 41786 is the development set. Checked while writing this: all 24 atoms of
phase2.2/cs_stage4e/L1_bare_R/substrate.xyz are identical, to six decimal places in
every coordinate, to atoms of
05_qmmm/23_c4_reactant/frames/frame_41786/image0_active.xyz.xz. Stage 4e's reactant
is band image 0.

C4 then measured whether a band image is a sound reactant. C4_A1_REPORT.txt step 2
requires |dE_reopt| <= 1.0 kcal/mol; frame 41786 gives -4.58 and fails, frame 42436
gives -5.12 and fails, and the report records "accepted 0 of 30". Steps 3 to 6, which
would have repaired them, were not run (C4_CLOSURE.txt). So no frame's band reactant
was ever repaired, and frame 42436's Stage 6 geometries avoid the problem by coming
from the SP package's optimisations rather than from the band.

How large the difference is, at the one level that exists at both frames, both values
recomputed here from the runs' own outputs:

   B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP   frame 41786, band image 0          16.666655 kcal/mol
   B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP   frame 42436, stationary points     18.461014 kcal/mol
   the geometry alone                                       +1.794359 (derived)

For scale, from D27 and D33 stage A: the basis def2-SVP -> def2-SVPD moves the
barrier -0.1288 and the functional B3LYP -> CAM-B3LYP moves it +8.4925 (both derived).
The geometry is fourteen times the basis effect and about a fifth of the functional's.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ten single points. For each of Stage 4e's five levels, the reactant and the transition
state of frame 42436.

THE INPUT FILE IS COPIED BYTE FOR BYTE FROM STAGE 4e AND NOTHING IN IT IS CHANGED.
Checked while writing this: within each level, Stage 4e's bare reactant and bare
transition-state job.inp are the same file by sha256. The only thing that distinguishes
one run from another is the substrate.xyz beside it. So D35 changes exactly one thing,
the geometry, and the method line, the auxiliary basis, the SCF settings, the Hirshfeld
print block and the charge and multiplicity are all Stage 4e's own:

   level  source input                             method line, as Stage 4e wrote it
   L1     cs_stage4e/L1_bare_{R,TS}/job.inp        ! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
   L2     cs_stage4e/L2_bare_{R,TS}/job.inp        ! B3LYP 6-31G(d) TightSCF
   L3     cs_stage4e/L3_bare_{R,TS}/job.inp        ! HF 6-31G(d) TightSCF
   L4     cs_stage4e/L4_bare_{R,TS}/job.inp        ! MP2 6-31G(d) TightSCF
   L5     cs_stage4e/L5_bare_{R,TS}/job.inp        ! WB97X-D3 def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF

Note that L2, L3 and L4 carry no D3BJ and no RIJCOSX. That is how Stage 4e ran them and
it is not corrected here; correcting it would change two things at once.

The geometries are the committed bare substrate of frame 42436,
d33a/sp_42436_R/substrate.xyz and d33a/sp_42436_TS/substrate.xyz, which are the same
coordinates as the Stage 6 control relax/S6-C/{R,TS}_sp/combined.xyz.

L1 IS THE CONTROL AND IT RUNS FIRST. Its level already exists at this frame, so it must
reproduce a number the record already holds. See G35.3.

ORDER. L1 R, L1 TS, then L2 to L5 in order, reactant before transition state. Walltime
24:00:00, which routes to day. No -q. Stage 4e's five batches of 34 runs each took
between 37 and 98 minutes at this size, so ten runs is well inside it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

R35.2  Each beside its frame 41786 counterpart, with the difference. This is the

       is preserved, Stage 4e's method comparison is at least ordinally robust to the

       nothing here recomputes it.

4. GATES. ANY FAILURE STOPS THE DIAGNOSTIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G35.1  Every D35 job.inp is byte-identical by sha256 to its Stage 4e source.
G35.2  Every D35 substrate.xyz is byte-identical by sha256 to its d33a source.
G35.3  CONTROL. L1's two energies equal the committed frame 42436 B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP
       values to within 1e-6 Eh each, and its barrier equals 18.461014 kcal/mol to
       within 0.0010. The committed values, recomputed here from
       frozen/sp_outputs.tar.xz, are R -836.1397955187 Eh and TS -836.1103760199 Eh.
       If L1 does not reproduce them, the geometry or the input is not what this
       document says it is and nothing else here may be read.
G35.4  Every run terminates normally, converges its SCF, and reports 118 electrons.
G35.5  The geometry in each output's echo matches frame 42436's substrate and does NOT
       match frame 41786's, tested on the coordinates and not on the file name.
G35.6  No file under cs_stage4e, d33a or frozen is modified by this diagnostic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D35.1  It does not transfer Stage 4e's conclusion. That conclusion is about eleven
       residues at five levels, 170 runs, and those residues sit in frame 41786's
       coordinates. Repeating it at frame 42436 is a rebuild and not a lift, and D35
       does not attempt it. Stage 4e's verdict stands where it stands, at its own
       frame and on its own geometries.
D35.2  It does not revisit C4. The band reactant failed step 2 for all 30 frames and
       steps 3 to 6 were not run; that is recorded here as the reason the two frames
       are of different kinds, and it is not reopened.
D35.3  One frame, frozen geometries, bare substrate, in vacuo, no solvation, no
       sampling, no free energy. No barrier here is a rate.
D35.4  MP2/6-31G(d) is Stage 4e's chosen reference at a small basis. It is not a
       benchmark and D35 does not treat it as truth. 6-31G(d) has no diffuse functions,
       and D30 found even def2-SVPD insufficient for this dianion above 2.88 e of
       applied charge; no charge is applied here, but the caution about compact bases
       on a dianion stands.
D35.5  No charges, no design, no wall. Nothing here bears on reliability, on r_safe, or
       on any barrier lowering.
D35.6  Nothing here is shaped toward a lower barrier. The expected direction is that
       every level rises at the stationary-point geometry, as L1 does by +1.794359, and
       if a level falls instead that is the result.
