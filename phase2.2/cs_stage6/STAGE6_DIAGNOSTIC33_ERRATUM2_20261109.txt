E2.1  WHAT THE RESULT SAYS

R33.3 reports

   QUOTABLE: bare_n05_q0.50 -14.3612 kcal/mol, bare_n11_q0.25 -13.0007 kcal/mol,

That is correct under D33.3, which makes a ddE quotable on two conditions only: the design is
RELIABLE at both states, and its Sigma|q| <= 2.88 e. wall_n02_q1.00 satisfies both - RELIABLE, and
Sigma|q| = 2.00 e.


E2.2  WHAT THE PRE-REGISTRATION ALSO SAYS, AND THE RESULT DOES NOT CARRY

D33.6 item 2, fixed before any run existed:

   THE WALL IS NOT ADOPTED AND IS NOT CALIBRATED. Route (b) has no amendment. ERRATUM7 records the
   Ne parameters used outside their published octet assumption; DIAGNOSTIC 23 found swapping
   He/Ne/Ar moves d_shield by 37 to 42 per cent of its own value, and records a count of
   independent alternatives of the same class of zero. So the two walled designs' ddE values are
   reported for comparison and no production number may rest on them.

The reader implements D33.3 and not D33.6(2), so its QUOTABLE list includes a walled design and
its "largest quotable lowering" names one. Both statements are true in D33.3's sense. Neither
carries D33.6(2)'s restriction, and a reader taking -15.2781 as this project's barrier effect would
be doing what D33.6(2) forbade in advance.

This is a defect in the reader and the document it wrote, not in the measurement.
