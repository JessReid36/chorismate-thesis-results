## y08 — Diagnostic 10 read, and the gate that was wrong about S6-P (27 October 2026)

- **07:06.** Job 422122 finished. Sixteen single points, all terminated normally. Jessica scp'd

  Nothing was committed by that command, by design.

- **Gate 1 passed.** The control reproduced Diagnostic 1's committed `−836.227913439` and
  `−836.199397816` Eh to 2.3e-10 and 4.7e-10 Eh. The `%pointcharges` re-expression of the control

- **Gate 2 reported FAIL on S6-P** and stopped the reading, as D10.2 requires. S6-2 and S6-20
  agreed with Finding 4 to −0.0000 and +0.0000 kcal/mol; S6-P was out by +0.5629.

- **Read the failure rather than the chemistry.** The measured ddE for S6-P equalled D1's QM/MM
  ddE *exactly* (−3.8579 both), and the reported discrepancy equalled, to four decimals, the
  `sum L` the gate had subtracted. A real breakdown of Finding 4 would not do that for one design
  and vanish for the other two. The suspect was the gate, not the run.

- **Confirmed on three committed records, all predating the runs.**
  `relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt` header: `S6-P   1 sphere(s), BARE point charge, no LJ wall`.
  `diffuse/DIFF-S6-P/{R,TS}_sp/site.ORCAFF.prms` atom 25: `N 1.000000 0.000000 0.000000 0.000000
  0.000000` — epsilon and sigma zero in both geometries, against `-0.170000 3.648000` for S6-2's
  and S6-20's spheres. And the pre-registration's own D10.1 line 43: "S6-P — one **bare** +1".
  The pre-registration contradicts itself: it calls S6-P bare, then assigns it a wall offset.

- **Root cause.** `L = sum(r[6] for r in A[arr])`. L is a property of a *site*; site 1690 carries
  L = +0.56294 and is walled in S6-2 and S6-3 but bare in S6-P. The L printed under S6-P records
  what the site would cost if walled. Correct `sum L` for S6-P is 0.

- **Reproduced the bug before fixing it.** The superseded script, run against synthetic outputs
  carrying D1's control energies and the three measured ddE values, reproduced the 27 October
  gate-2 table line for line, +0.5629 included. The diagnosis is demonstrated, not asserted.

- **`cs10_cpcm.py` corrected.** `sum L` now comes from the force field that ran, not from the
  summary table: a sphere with four zero Lennard-Jones parameters contributes nothing. The new
  `deployed_L()` refuses *in both directions* if `ARRANGEMENTS.txt`'s bare flag and
  `site.ORCAFF.prms` disagree, and refuses if a design's R and TS force fields differ.
  sha256 `8d2a22bc…` → `25e0e7f7…`.

- **GATE 0 added.** All forty-four committed input files are checked byte for byte against

## y09 — CPCM removed, and the charge model put on trial (27 October 2026)

- **Jessica's question drove this.** "Does it not defeat the point of having the charges?" Chasing
  it produced a sharper answer than the artefact argument I had given, and then a second finding
  that matters more than the continuum question.

- **CPCM is removed** — `STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027.txt`. Three independent grounds, all
  grep-verified from full papers held in `chorismate-refs`: it is the wrong *kind* of field
  (Fried & Boxer's preorganisation-versus-reorganisation, Warshel's preorganisation term being
  identically zero in a randomly-oriented medium — five sources); the geometry has no precedent
  (Barone & Cossi put explicit charges *inside* the cavity; four sources use that topology; the
  strings "inside the cavity", "outside the cavity" and "dielectric boundary" occur in **zero** of
  the 77 held papers); and it is not a small constant (Claeyssens Table 4: PCM 53.4 against IPCM
  36.0 kcal/mol on *this* reaction at identical QM level). `STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt` untouched.

- **What the amendment deliberately does not claim.** That combining them is formally
  double-counting — no held source says so. That D10's CPCM numbers are wrong as measurements —
  they reproduce D2's independently generated C4 condition to 3.4e-10 Eh. And ground 3's
  −6.6/+5.9/+20.2 figures are marked corroborative, not decisive, because the dielectric
  differential is not an exact decomposition of the total and does not match for S6-2.

- **Diagnostic 11 pre-registered** — the charge model and the minimum stand-off. One charge walked
  outward along a fixed axis at 2.50 / 2.9596 / 3.50 / 4.50 / 6.00 / 15.00 Å, bare point charge
  against the same charge behind a coreless-ECP Pauli wall, 52 single points, no continuum.
  Anchored on Senn & Thiel: the artefact "becomes more severe the closer the point charge
  approaches the QM density and the more spatially flexible the density is", worse with diffuse
  functions. Stage 6 sits at 2.85–2.96 Å in ma-def2-SVP — the worst case on both axes at once.

- **The `dref` rung is byte-identical to D10's S6-P.** Same `substrate.xyz`, same `pc.pc`, same
  `job.inp`, verified by diff. Gate 2 is therefore a true reproduction, not a near one. Getting
  there needed two fixes caught in rehearsal: the committed coordinate lines are now carried
  through verbatim rather than reformatted at `%18.8f`, and the reference rung is placed at site
  1690's committed coordinates directly rather than at `o + u·2.96`, which missed by 3.7e-4 Å.

- **The `r_safe` rule was broken in the plan and is fixed here.** `|Δ_shield| ≤ 0.25 kcal/mol` is
  absolute, so at 6 Å — where every quantity is small — it passes automatically and would have
  certified almost any separation as safe. It is now relative (10 % of |ddE(bare)|, with a
  0.05 kcal/mol numerical floor), requires monotone convergence, and requires two successive rungs.

- **Probe 1 pre-registered** — the explicit-water cost-and-binding probe. Bare substrate only: no
  designed charges, no arrangements, no ddE, so nothing in it can be read as a result about a
  design. Shell sizes 0, 6, 12, 20 waters (24 / 42 / 60 / 84 QM atoms). N = 6 is not a free choice:
  ranks 1–6 lie between 2.592 and 2.949 Å and rank 7 is at 4.192 Å, a 1.243 Å gap.

- **Stated in the probe, because it would otherwise mislead:** the real first shell in the enzyme
  is mostly *protein* — 63 non-water against 17 water atoms within 3.5 Å at frame 42436. Substrate
  plus waters in vacuo is a device for binding the density, not a model of the active site.

- **Input gates tightened.** Five directories named `frame_42436` exist in the repo, so resolving
  the geometry by filename was wrong; it is now the explicit Stage 6 path, gated on the two sha256
  values `ARRANGEMENTS.txt` records in its INPUTS block. GATE 0 carries over from D10: every input
  checked byte for byte against the committed manifest before anything is read.

- **Cost, from the committed D10 outputs rather than guessed:** vacuum single points average
  5.7 min, control 3.2, the 20-charge run 11.2. D11's 52 runs are one charge or none, so ~4 h.
  Probe 1's cost is the thing being measured.
