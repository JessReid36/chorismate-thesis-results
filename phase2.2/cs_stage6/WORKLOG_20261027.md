- **Read the failure rather than the chemistry.** The measured ddE for S6-P equalled D1's QM/MM

  `relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt` header: `S6-P   1 sphere(s), BARE point charge, no LJ wall`.

## y09 — CPCM removed, and the charge model put on trial (27 October 2026)

- **Jessica's question drove this.** "Does it not defeat the point of having the charges?" Chasing
  it produced a sharper answer than the artefact argument I had given, and then a second finding
  that matters more than the continuum question.

- **CPCM is removed** — `STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027.txt`. Three independent grounds, all
  grep-verified from full papers held in `chorismate-refs`: it is the wrong *kind* of field
  (Fried & Boxer's preorganisation-versus-reorganisation, Warshel's preorganisation term being
  identically zero in a randomly-oriented medium — five sources); the geometry has no precedent
  (Barone & Cossi put explicit charges *inside* the cavity; four sources use that topology; the
  strings "inside the cavity", "outside the cavity" and "dielectric boundary" occur in **zero** of
  the 77 held papers); and it is not a small constant (Claeyssens Table 4: PCM 53.4 against IPCM
  36.0 kcal/mol on *this* reaction at identical QM level). `STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt` untouched.

- **What the amendment deliberately does not claim.** That combining them is formally
  double-counting — no held source says so. That D10's CPCM numbers are wrong as measurements —
  they reproduce D2's independently generated C4 condition to 3.4e-10 Eh. And ground 3's
  −6.6/+5.9/+20.2 figures are marked corroborative, not decisive, because the dielectric
  differential is not an exact decomposition of the total and does not match for S6-2.

- **Diagnostic 11 pre-registered** — the charge model and the minimum stand-off. One charge walked
  outward along a fixed axis at 2.50 / 2.9596 / 3.50 / 4.50 / 6.00 / 15.00 Å, bare point charge
  against the same charge behind a coreless-ECP Pauli wall, 52 single points, no continuum.
  Anchored on Senn & Thiel: the artefact "becomes more severe the closer the point charge
  approaches the QM density and the more spatially flexible the density is", worse with diffuse
  functions. Stage 6 sits at 2.85–2.96 Å in ma-def2-SVP — the worst case on both axes at once.

- **The `dref` rung is byte-identical to D10's S6-P.** Same `substrate.xyz`, same `pc.pc`, same
  `job.inp`, verified by diff. Gate 2 is therefore a true reproduction, not a near one. Getting
  there needed two fixes caught in rehearsal: the committed coordinate lines are now carried
  through verbatim rather than reformatted at `%18.8f`, and the reference rung is placed at site
  1690's committed coordinates directly rather than at `o + u·2.96`, which missed by 3.7e-4 Å.

- **The `r_safe` rule was broken in the plan and is fixed here.** `|Δ_shield| ≤ 0.25 kcal/mol` is
  absolute, so at 6 Å — where every quantity is small — it passes automatically and would have
  certified almost any separation as safe. It is now relative (10 % of |ddE(bare)|, with a
  0.05 kcal/mol numerical floor), requires monotone convergence, and requires two successive rungs.

- **Probe 1 pre-registered** — the explicit-water cost-and-binding probe. Bare substrate only: no
  designed charges, no arrangements, no ddE, so nothing in it can be read as a result about a
  design. Shell sizes 0, 6, 12, 20 waters (24 / 42 / 60 / 84 QM atoms). N = 6 is not a free choice:
  ranks 1–6 lie between 2.592 and 2.949 Å and rank 7 is at 4.192 Å, a 1.243 Å gap.

- **Stated in the probe, because it would otherwise mislead:** the real first shell in the enzyme
  is mostly *protein* — 63 non-water against 17 water atoms within 3.5 Å at frame 42436. Substrate
  plus waters in vacuo is a device for binding the density, not a model of the active site.

- **Input gates tightened.** Five directories named `frame_42436` exist in the repo, so resolving
  the geometry by filename was wrong; it is now the explicit Stage 6 path, gated on the two sha256
  values `ARRANGEMENTS.txt` records in its INPUTS block. GATE 0 carries over from D10: every input
  checked byte for byte against the committed manifest before anything is read.

- **Cost, from the committed D10 outputs rather than guessed:** vacuum single points average
  5.7 min, control 3.2, the 20-charge run 11.2. D11's 52 runs are one charge or none, so ~4 h.
  Probe 1's cost is the thing being measured.

- **Three documents committed as notes**, per Jessica's instruction that the reasoning be
  recoverable: `TESTING_PLAN_20261027.md`, `CPCM_APPROPRIATENESS_20261027.md`, and this section.

- **One correction I owe the record.** I told Jessica that a subagent had fabricated the quotation
  "coarsened, nondynamic approximations to COSMO embeddings". It had not. The phrase is in
  `acs.jctc.8b00151.pdf` hyphenated as "non-dynamic", and `gocat_2.pdf` reprints it unhyphenated
  exactly as reported. My grep missed it on hyphenation and I announced absence from a failed
  search — the same error as the Dittner ligature case in Erratum 7. **A failed grep is
  inconclusive, not evidence of absence**, and spelling variants are checked before any claim that
  something is not in a paper.

## y10 — Probe 1's binding reading withdrawn; the right observable was already measured (27 October 2026)

- **Probe 1 ran and the cost answer is good news.** 24 / 42 / 60 / 84 QM atoms in 1m38 / 3m23 /
  5m03 / 7m24, peak 2.2 GB against a 24 GB request, exit status 0. Near-linear in atom count —
  3.5× the atoms for 4.5× the time. Explicit water at this level is cheap: eight single points in
  a twenty-water shell is about an hour. That was what Jessica asked the probe for and it stands.

- **Its binding column is withdrawn.** `STAGE6_PROBE1_ERRATUM1_20261027.txt`. I headed a column
  `bound?` and filled it with the sign of the B3LYP HOMO. That is not the test.

- **Diagnostic 2 had already answered the question, five days earlier, on the right quantity.**
  Committed 22 October, vertical detachment energy at the reactant geometry: −0.3167 eV at
  def2-SVP (metastable), **+0.5123 eV at ma-def2-SVP (BOUND)**, +0.5013 eV at def2-SVPD. Its own
  words underneath: *"A positive VDE means the species is bound despite a positive HOMO, and
  Finding 8's second half must be withdrawn as an artefact of reading Koopmans' theorem off a DFT
  orbital."* So **a finding had already been withdrawn in this project for precisely this error**,
  and `STAGE6_ERRATUM7` E7.10 had restated the authority for it the day before Probe 1 was written.

- **The consequence is larger than the probe.** At the basis Stage 6 actually runs in, the bare
  dianion is bound, and there was never a binding problem to solve. Three statements in the record
  rest on the HOMO instead and are corrected, not deleted: `SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md` §2's
  "electronically unbound in vacuum" (29 September, pre-dates D2); D10's reading that "the
  continuum did its job"; and the testing plan's defect 3. D10's **verdict** is unaffected — it
  turns on sign reversal and ranking — and Amendment 2's three grounds for removing CPCM are
  unaffected, because none of them is a binding argument.

- **A2 stops being blocking.** The environment step was blocking only because the density had to
  be bound by something once CPCM was removed. It does not. A gas-phase reference is independently
  defensible here — Mulholland: "We use the gas-phase profiles as a convenient and meaningful
  reference" — and Li & Chan 2025 practise it. Explicit water remains wanted on Shaik's screening
  argument, which is a different case, and is now known to be affordable.

- **A sting worth keeping.** The condition the surrogate's `a` coefficients were fitted in —
  def2-SVP in vacuo — is the one of the three where the dianion is **metastable** (−0.3167 eV).
  Diffuse functions make it more bound, not less.

- **Probe 2 pre-registered** — the question that is actually open. D2 measured the VDE of the
  *bare* substrate; nobody has measured it with a designed charge present. Dreuw & Cederbaum:
  "at short distances the monoanion-point charge system represents a resonance state that can in
  principle not be correctly computed with bound state methods". Eight single points at the
  reactant geometry: no charge, +1 at `dref`, −1 at `dref`, −1 at `d250` (2.50 Å, deliberately
  closer than any committed design), each with its own dianion and monoanion run so the probe is
  self-contained. Gate 2 reproduces D2's +0.5123 eV, which makes the comparison legitimate rather
  than asserted.

- **Pre-registered as not a clearance.** If every condition comes back bound, that is one charge at
  −1.00 e. S6-20 carries ten negative charges totalling −3.00 e and cannot be inferred from it.
  The reading rule says so before the runs.

- **The standing lesson, recorded in the erratum.** Two errors of the same shape in two days: a
  failed grep read as absence, and now a disqualified observable used because it was the
  convenient one in the output. Both were already documented in this project's record before they
  were repeated. **Before a diagnostic is written, the observable it turns on is checked against
  the errata.** A quantity this project has withdrawn a finding over does not re-enter as a
  verdict column.

## y11 — Diagnostic 11's verdict committed, its erratum, and Probe 3 (27 October 2026)

- **Diagnostic 11: `r_safe` = 3.50 Å.** The committed arrangements sit at 2.85–2.96 Å, inside it.
  On D11.3 the grid must be rebuilt at that stand-off and every `a` recomputed, and no design
  measured inside `r_safe` is interpretable as a design evaluation. That **suspends** Diagnostic
  10's reading — the sign reversal and the ranking inversion — without touching its measurements.

- **Gate 2 was an exact reproduction.** The `dref` rung was byte-identical to D10's S6-P and
  returned −3.857852 to +0.0000. The harness has not drifted between bundles.

- **The bare ladder is not monotone and the walled one is.** Bare: −6.59, −3.86, −5.59, −3.59,
  −1.97 across 2.50–5.90 Å, where the classical estimate falls smoothly. A bare charge at 2.96 Å
  is *less* catalytic than the same charge at 3.50 Å, which no electrostatic model produces.
  `dref` — S6-P's exact position — is the worst rung, `d_shield` +3.96. Diagnostic 2 saw the same
  anomaly at the same site five days earlier from independently generated inputs.

- **C-A1.5 is the clincher.** Bare-minus-walled is **+3.964 at ma-def2-SVP and −0.067 at
  def2-SVP** — a ratio of **0.017**. Senn & Thiel's prediction, confirmed, and the explanation for
  why the surrogate appears to work in def2-SVP and nowhere else: that basis **conceals** the
  artefact. **C-A1.4**: the artefact is specific to positive charges; the q = −1 arm shows almost
  none.

- **Erratum 1 corrects a control against myself.** A3.4 required the far-field point to equal a
  classical estimate that omits polarisation *by construction* — and that polarisation is a roughly
  constant 1.7–1.9× enhancement at every separation, 15 Å included. The oracle behaved; the
  criterion tested the wrong thing. **A3.4 is not re-scored as a pass**: a control rewritten after
  seeing its number is not a control. The far-field question is carried forward unanswered, with a
  properly-specified replacement pre-registered for the next diagnostic that needs one.

- **Also in the erratum:** C-A1.1 passed at `dref` by **0.018 kcal/mol**, which is not a margin, so
  the walled `dref` figure carries ~+0.28 of wall inside it; and the wall is **not inert at
  2.50 Å** (+1.31), correctly voiding that rung.

- **What `r_safe` is, written down because the verdict invites the wrong reading.** It bounds
  **model** validity, not where a catalytic charge may sit. Jessica's objection was right: real
  active sites do not keep to a stand-off. **Arg90's guanidinium carbon sits 3.19 Å from the
  substrate at R and 3.27 Å at the TS** in this project's own frame 42436 — inside `r_safe` — and
  it is the catalytic residue. A guanidinium has electrons and Pauli repulsion; a point charge has
  neither. What fails at 2.96 Å is the representation.

- **Two routes, one pre-registered.** (a) Keep bare charges, move the surface to ≥3.50 Å — the
  branch D11.3 specified, at a cost of ~25 % of the field per charge. (b) Keep the distances and
  change the charge model, which D11's own wall-corrected ladder supports. **Route (b) is not
  adopted**: D11.3 did not specify it, and it needs the Ne ECP calibrated first, since Erratum 7
  records those parameters are used outside their published octet assumption. Recorded as an open
  scientific decision, not a correction.

- **Post hoc, labelled as such.** Subtracting each rung's own wall contribution gives 1.877, 1.918,
  1.911, 1.801, 1.693 × classical — one enhancement factor at every separation. And the direction
  is the surprise: at S6-P's position bare gives −3.858 against a wall-corrected −8.104, so the
  artefact **suppresses** the apparent effect by ~4.2 kcal/mol, landing within 0.9 of D2's
  independent small-q extrapolation of −7.20. Stage 6 has been under-estimating that charge. One
  charge, one site, additive-wall assumption unproven; nothing is built on it.

- **Probe 2 passed both gates**, reproducing D2's +0.5123 eV exactly. A +1 at 2.96 Å makes the
  dianion *more* bound (+3.4366 eV); a −1 unbinds it (−2.1005 eV at 2.96 Å, −2.3338 at 2.50 Å).
  Two distinct failure modes on opposite signs: overpolarisation for positive charges, loss of
  boundedness for negative ones — and the wall does nothing for the second.

- **Probe 3 pre-registered.** S6-2 places −1.00 e at 3.485 Å; S6-20 places −2.70 e across eight
  sites inside 3.5 Å — both **derived** in the code from `ARRANGEMENTS.txt`, not asserted. Four
  monoanion runs; the dianion partners are D10's committed outputs, each monoanion copied byte for
  byte from its partner differing only in the charge line. If either design is metastable its ddE
  is not a barrier effect at all, which would be a stronger statement than uninterpretability — and
  would imply a constraint the optimiser lacks, since `Σqᵢ² ≤ B` is **blind to sign**.

- **Every result committed here was re-measured from the committed outputs by this bundle**, not
  copied from `/tmp`, so the committed result is provably what the committed outputs produce.

## y12 — Probe 3: all four bound; the resonance hypothesis is dead (27 October 2026)

- **Probe 3 passed both gates** — the control dianion reproduces D1 to 2.3e-10 Eh and its VDE
  reproduces Diagnostic 2's committed **+0.5123 eV exactly**.

- **Every committed design is electronically bound at the reactant geometry.**

      S6-C    0 sites,  0.00 e    VDE +0.5123 eV
      S6-P    0 sites,  0.00 e    VDE +3.4366 eV
      S6-2    1 site,  -1.00 e    VDE +1.1058 eV
      S6-20   8 sites, -2.70 e    VDE +0.3016 eV

- **The prediction was wrong and the arithmetic says exactly where.** I estimated S6-2 at about
  −1.7 eV. The Coulomb scaling of its negative charge was fine — predicted −2.22 eV for the −1 at
  3.485 Å against a value of −2.33 implied by S6-2, **4.8 % out**. The error was omitting the +1
  charge in the same design, which contributes **+2.92 eV**. It is the additivity error this project
  has been documenting all day, made in prose, with both terms already written down an hour earlier.

- **Three claims withdrawn, none of which had been committed.** That the resonance explained
  Diagnostic 10's pattern; that a sign-aware bound on the budget was the constructive consequence;
  and the framing that two of three designs were measuring nothing. All three lived only in
  conversation. The committed record contains no assertion that S6-2 or S6-20 is metastable — the
  pre-registrations stated it conditionally and the condition did not fire. **The discipline of
  committing only pre-registered conditionals is what kept the record clean while the reasoning
  was wrong.**

- **The sign-aware constraint is not supported as an active one.** S6-20 holds −2.70 e inside
  3.5 Å and stays bound. The direction is real and measured — a lone −1 at 2.96 Å gives −2.10 eV —
  but a design's positive charges compensate. If a guard is wanted it belongs on **net local
  charge**, not on negative charge, and it is insurance rather than a binding constraint.

- **Diagnostic 11's `r_safe` is now the only open defect in the evaluation.** The designs' ddE
  values remain uninterpretable for that reason and no other.

- **A claim corrected before it was committed.** The first draft of this section said "the VDE
  reconstructs additively to 4.8 %". It does not, and the arithmetic offered for it —
  `0.5123 + 2.9243 − 2.3308 = +1.1058` — is an **identity**, not a test: the −2.3308 was derived
  *from* the S6-2 measurement, so the reconstruction returns it by construction. What is actually
  shown is narrower: an independent 1/r scaling of the measured −1 shift at 2.96 Å predicts −2.2192
  for 3.485 Å, against −2.3308 implied by S6-2 **assuming additivity**. The 4.8 % agreement tests
  additivity and the distance scaling **jointly and cannot separate them** — either could be off
  with the other compensating. Measuring the −1 at 3.485 Å *alone* is one run and would separate
  them. Caught by guard rail 1 on re-reading, before the bundle was built.

- **One genuine cross-check.** Probe 2's `dref_p1` and Probe 3's `S6-P` are the same calculation —
  +1 at site 1690, same geometry, same basis — generated by different scripts and submitted as
  separate jobs. Both give **+3.4366 eV**, agreeing to 0.0e+00.

- **Still untested, as the pre-registration said:** this is the reactant geometry only. A design
  bound at R could be metastable at the transition state.

- **Guard rails added to `WORKING_RULES.md`**, at Jessica's instruction and in her wording, with
  the provenance of each of the seven recorded against the specific error it came from. They sit
  under the rule that has always headed that file — "do not tell me what is true, show me the
  command that establishes it" — because every failure today was a way of breaking that rule while
