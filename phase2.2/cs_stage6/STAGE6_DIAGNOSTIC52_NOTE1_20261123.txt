look was made, of the 68 runs of 104 that had ended. Claude told her from it which way the rules

This note says what was looked at, when, what was said from it, and why it could not change the
result. Two things that Claude derived from the look were wrong; they are corrected in N1.5.

UNOFFICIAL", that:

   ORCA and the second program agreed at all 12 Hartree-Fock runs, and the functions held the
   electron;

   rule A leaned to set X2 at IntAcc 12, with the runs on IntAcc 40 still to come;

   CAM-B3LYP lay 0.05 to 0.104 eV above the limit, most at the two charges that lie farthest
   apart, as computed beforehand, so that rule C would give L of about 0.31 eV;

   the grid asked for hydrogen alone gave the energy of IntAcc 12 for every atom;

At 17:06 Jessica asked "summarse the findings for me. so it is a fail? or what?" and Claude
answered "No, it is not a fail. The job is running as written, and D52 has no pass or fail for

The committed result gives rule A as set X2 with treatment A and IntAcc 12, rule B as wB97X-V,
rule C as "L = 0.3127 eV", and the grid for hydrogen alone as "THE SAME AS IntAcc 12 FOR EVERY
ATOM". That is what the look leaned to. The look is the source of none of it: every number of
the result was formed by the committed reader from the outputs, under rules fixed before the job
began. The look changed no input, no rule and no run.


N1.5  TWO THINGS CLAUDE DERIVED FROM THE LOOK THAT WERE WRONG

   1. In that reply Claude said: "Run 69 did not end normally and most probably used its full
      one-hour cap (derived from the printed times, not printed itself)." Run 69 is
      ONE6_WMV_X3_i20_s1. It did not use the cap. The committed log has "ONE6_WMV_X2_i20_x6
      orca_exit=0 normal=1 Mon Nov 23 15:42:20 SAST 2026" and then "ONE6_WMV_X3_i20_s1
      orca_exit=0 normal=0 Mon Nov 23 16:35:31 SAST 2026": the run took 53 minutes and ended by
      ORCA's own stop, and its output says "SCF NOT CONVERGED AFTER 4972 CYCLES". The six runs
      of wB97M-V in set X3 took 42 to 53 minutes each (derived from the log); none was stopped
      by the 3600 s that the job script allows a run.

   2. Claude's estimate in the same reply: "if each of the five remaining wB97M-V runs uses its
      full hour, they finish at about 21:45." The whole job ended at 20:42:21.

Neither statement entered the result, a rule or a decision. They are corrected here because they
were said.
