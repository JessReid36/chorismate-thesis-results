23 November 2026. The note that AMENDMENT 10 lists as owed (A10.6: "Not yet done: a dated note
on D44."). Written by Claude after D44's result (results 3f6765ac), its NOTES 1 and 2 and

N3.1  WHY THIS NOTE

D44's pre-registration, of its jobs: "Every job script is restartable: submitted again, it
skips every run that ended normally." and, in outcome F3: "A job that was cut short is
submitted again as it is." D44 NOTE 1 records that this was done for job RDTZ: "It was
submitted again as it was on 19 November 2026". None of the three says what ORCA starts a run
from when the run is made a second time in its folder. D50 NOTE 1, N1.6, came upon it while
looking at something else: "ORCA then starts from it." This note says which runs of D44 that
concerns and what of D44's result rests on them.


N3.2  WHAT HAPPENED (MEASURED)

THE FIRST SUBMISSION, run_RDTZ.pbs.out.part1: "host=comp054.hpc start=Wed Nov 18 10:36:55 SAST
2026". Three monoanion runs ended without ORCA's line of normal termination:

   RDTZ_B_n02_q1.00_mo_R orca_exit=0 normal=0 Wed Nov 18 22:15:01 SAST 2026
   RDTZ_B_n02_q1.00_mo_TS orca_exit=0 normal=0 Wed Nov 18 22:46:33 SAST 2026
   RDTZ_B_n03_q0.75_mo_TS orca_exit=0 normal=0 Thu Nov 19 09:52:21 SAST 2026

The last run the log reports is RDTZ_B_n05_q0.50_di_TS, at 10:20:08 on 19 November. The run
after it in the script is RDTZ_B_n05_q0.50_ds_R, and then: "=>> PBS: job killed: walltime
86429 exceeded limit 86400". DERIVED from the start and that walltime: the job was killed at
about 10:37, so that run was cut about 17 minutes after it began.

THE JOB SCRIPT, run_RDTZ.pbs: "Restartable: submitted again, it skips every run that ended
normally." Its test is ORCA's line of normal termination in the run's job.out. Every other
run is made again in the same folder, and its job.out is written over.

THE SECOND SUBMISSION, run_RDTZ.pbs.out: "host=comp050.hpc start=Thu Nov 19 10:49:39 SAST
2026". It made 13 runs. Four of them had been started before:

   RDTZ_B_n02_q1.00_mo_R orca_exit=0 normal=0 Thu Nov 19 16:54:55 SAST 2026
   RDTZ_B_n02_q1.00_mo_TS orca_exit=0 normal=0 Thu Nov 19 17:13:24 SAST 2026
   RDTZ_B_n03_q0.75_mo_TS orca_exit=0 normal=0 Thu Nov 19 21:45:29 SAST 2026
   RDTZ_B_n05_q0.50_ds_R orca_exit=0 normal=1 Thu Nov 19 22:12:43 SAST 2026

WHAT ORCA DID. In each of the four folders the first attempt had left a file of orbitals,
job.gbw. The inputs neither ask ORCA to read it nor forbid it. Each of the four outputs
prints "Checking for AutoStart:", "The File: job.gbw exists", "=> Guess is set to MORead" and
"INITIAL GUESS: MOREAD". The nine other runs of the second submission print "INITIAL GUESS:
MODEL POTENTIAL".

THE WHOLE STAGE. d44_note3/restarted_runs.py reads every job.out under phase2.2/cs_stage6,
2229 of them. These four are the only ones that print "The File: job.gbw exists".

THE FIRST ATTEMPTS ARE NOT KEPT. The committed job.out of each of the four is its second
attempt. D44's README says of the archive on Jessica's PC that it holds the files "as they
stood on hpc1 after the jobs had ended", so it holds the second attempts too. Of the first
attempts the record has the log lines above and nothing else.


N3.3  WHAT D44'S RESULT READS FROM THE FOUR (MEASURED, d44.tsv)

THE THREE MONOANION RUNS did not end normally the second time either. ORCA's words:
   RDTZ_B_n02_q1.00_mo_R    "SCF NOT CONVERGED AFTER 1941 CYCLES"
   RDTZ_B_n02_q1.00_mo_TS   "Stability equations did not converge!"
   RDTZ_B_n03_q0.75_mo_TS   "SCF NOT CONVERGED AFTER 1811 CYCLES"
The result has each as NOT MEASURED, and the fifth leg of B_n02_q1.00 and of B_n03_q0.75 at
RDTZ as "NOT PASSED: NOT MEASURED".

RDTZ_B_n05_q0.50_ds_R ended normally and is read as measured. It is the dianion of
B_n05_q0.50 at R with the stability analysis. From it the result takes, in R44.2: the search
UNSTABLE at its first analysis and STABLE at its last, <S**2> 1.264665, a Loewdin rise of
+1.1586 e, a dipole of 5.98 times the control's, an energy +2.69e-03 Eh above the run
without the analysis, the word CHARGE MOVED, the two flags "the solution the search ended
on: relocation: Loewdin rise +1.1586 e" and "the solution the search ended on: dipole 5.98x
control", and one entry under held, "the solution the search ended on: gap withheld".


N3.4  WHAT RESTS ON THEM, AND WHAT DOES NOT

Read on the result's own rules. R44.2: "UNRELIABLE: a flag at either state." R44.5: "It FAILS
if it is UNRELIABLE, or METASTABLE at a state, or its ddE is OUTSIDE." The header: "VDE =
E(monoanion) - E(dianion)", and R44.4: "ddE = (E(TS) - E(TS, control)) - (E(R) - E(R,
control)) on the dianion". At a double hybrid the dianion's energy in both is that of the run
WITHOUT the stability analysis (di); the result says of the other, "the energy used is the
restricted one".

B_n05_q0.50 AT RDTZ. Its word on four legs is UNRELIABLE and its word at the level is FAILS.
   - The run without the analysis at R, begun from ORCA's own guess, is flagged by itself:
     "relocation: Loewdin rise +1.1202 e; dipole 5.38x control". So is the one at TS (+1.0105 e,
     5.02x). UNRELIABLE therefore does not rest on the restarted run.
   - DERIVED by the script from E_di, E_mo and E_control alone: ddE -4.7191 kcal/mol and VDE
     +3.8724 eV at R, +4.4330 eV at TS, the numbers of the table. Its ddE is OUTSIDE, its
     fifth leg PASSED. None of these uses a number of the restarted run.
   - WHAT DOES REST ON THE RESTARTED RUN is its own cell of R44.2 as listed in N3.3, and
     nothing else in the result. AMENDMENT 9, A9.1, quotes this design's flags at RDTZ at R
     as they "begin", which is the flag of the run without the analysis.

B_n02_q1.00 AND B_n03_q0.75 AT RDTZ. Each FAILS because it is UNRELIABLE and its ddE is
OUTSIDE, both from runs begun from ORCA's own guess. What the three monoanion runs add is the
fifth leg's "NOT PASSED: NOT MEASURED". A measured monoanion could have changed that leg and
could not have changed FAILS.

SO NO WORD OF D44 ON A DESIGN, A LEVEL OR THE CROSS-CHECK RESTS ON A RUN THAT BEGAN FROM
LEFT-OVER ORBITALS. D44's result, its NOTES 1 and 2 and AMENDMENTS 9 and 10 stand as they are.


N3.5  DID THE START CHANGE WHAT THE ONE FINISHED RUN FOUND? (POST HOC)

MEASURED. The first step of a run with the stability analysis is the same SCF as the run
without it. In RDTZ_B_n05_q0.50_ds_R that first SCF ended after 2 cycles ("SCF CONVERGED
AFTER 2 CYCLES") at -834.6780772807833 Eh. RDTZ_B_n05_q0.50_di_R, begun from ORCA's own
guess, ended its SCF at -834.678077280831 Eh. DERIVED: they differ by 4.8e-11 Eh. In the 11
other pairs of level RDTZ, where both runs began from ORCA's own guess, the two differ by
8.2e-11 Eh at most (the script's table).

Everything the result reads from the restarted run came after that point and was computed in
the second attempt: the first analysis, the search (85 cycles), the last analysis ("The
stability analysis shows that the wavefunction is stable") and the MP2 step.

THE READING. The left-over orbitals were those of the first SCF, already converged when the
job was cut, and the run went into its stability analysis from a solution of the same energy
as the one a run from ORCA's own guess reaches. That is an inference from two energies. The
same energy to 1e-10 Eh is not a proof of the same orbitals, and what the search would have
ended on from a fresh start has not been measured.

OF THE THREE MONOANION RUNS nothing of the kind can be read. None converged in either attempt
and the first attempts' outputs are gone, so why each failed the first time is not known.


N3.6  WHAT IS DONE FROM HERE

Nothing is run again. No word rests on these runs (N3.4), and for the one that is read the
evidence of N3.5 is that the start did not matter.

D44's sentence "A job that was cut short is submitted again as it is" is not carried into a
later pre-registration as it stands. D50 was handled otherwise (D50 NOTE 1, N1.6: collected
as it stood). Claude's practice from here, which is not a criterion: a pre-registration that
lets a run be made a second time says what that run starts from, and its job script makes
it in a folder that holds no file of the earlier attempt.
