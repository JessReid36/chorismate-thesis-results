Two distinct refusals of two features the criteria's TS specification requires together: a
transition-state optimisation in Cartesian coordinates, and a frequency calculation on a system
whose active atoms are a subset.

WHY THE SPECIFICATION WAS NEVER TESTED

STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, THE RELAXATION, names its source: "WP4's protocol
(phase2.2/wp4_s13_rerun/armb_anch/job.inp), which is committed and has run". That file's keyword
line is

   ! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF QMMM Opt

An Opt. WP4 relaxed a reactant and never optimised a transition state, so the criteria's TS side -
"The TS side uses OptTS from ts/final.xyz followed by frequencies" - was written by extension from
a protocol that had only ever done the reactant half, and its Cartesian coordinates were never
tested against OptTS. The reactant side is unaffected and is running now, in Cartesians, exactly as
WP4 did.

WHAT REPLACES IT, AND WHERE IT COMES FROM

Not invented here. This project has already optimised a transition state of THIS frame with QM/MM
and ORCA 6.0.1, successfully, twice, and both are committed:

   05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers/f42436_r8_a1/ts/core.inp - the run that produced the ts/final.xyz these
   relaxations start from:

      ! QMMM B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX OptTS TightOpt TightSCF
      %geom
        Calc_Hess true
        Hybrid_Hess {the 24 QM atoms} end
        MaxIter 500
      end

   05_qmmm/18c_reduced_region/ts_reduced.out, whose echoed input adds the frequencies and which
   produced the 313.30 cm-1 imaginary mode of ts_frequencies_summary.txt:

      ! QMMM B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX OptTS NumFreq TightSCF
      %geom
        Calc_Hess true
        Hybrid_Hess {the 24 QM atoms} end
      end

THE THREE CHANGES, AND NOTHING ELSE

Two directories are added: relax/S6-C/TS_opt2 and relax/n10_A/TS_opt2.


   REFUSED, relax/<arm>/TS_opt/job.inp              ADDED, relax/<arm>/TS_opt2/job.inp
   ... TightSCF QMMM OptTS Freq                     ... RIJCOSX OptTS NumFreq TightSCF QMMM
   %geom                                            %geom
     MaxIter 1000                                     MaxIter 1000
     coordsys cartesian                               Calc_Hess true
     ProjectTR false                                  Hybrid_Hess {0 .. 23} end
   end                                              end

1. coordsys cartesian and ProjectTR false are dropped, so the optimisation runs in redundant
   internal coordinates. This is what ORCA's own message asks for and what both committed runs used.
2. Calc_Hess true with Hybrid_Hess over the 24 substrate atoms supplies the Hessian a
   transition-state search needs, in the hybrid form ORCA will compute, which is what the second
   refusal was about.
3. Freq becomes NumFreq, numerical frequencies, which is what works with a hybrid Hessian and what
   the committed reduced-region run used.

UNCHANGED, and checked rather than asserted by cs6_y58.py: combined.xyz and site.ORCAFF.prms are
the committed TS_opt files BYTE FOR BYTE; the %qmmm block, its QMAtoms and its ActiveAtoms are
identical; both arms get the same treatment, so the control and the design are still computed alike;
MaxIter, maxcore, nprocs and the SCF settings are untouched; and the submission script differs only
in its job name and its two paths.

THE REFUSED INPUTS ARE LEFT EXACTLY AS COMMITTED. relax/S6-C/TS_opt and relax/n10_A/TS_opt are not
edited and not removed. They are the record of what was specified and refused, and TS_opt2 is what
runs. Any reader of the relaxation results must be able to see both.

DIRECTION

Redundant internals with a computed hybrid Hessian is the more permissive choice in the sense that
it can converge where Cartesians cannot even start; it is also the only executable one. Nothing
about the coordinate system or the Hessian provision favours a lower barrier: the TS energy is a
property of the stationary point, not of the coordinates used to reach it, and the criteria's test
on it - exactly one imaginary mode - is unchanged and is stricter under NumFreq than under no
frequency calculation at all.

COST, so that it is not a surprise

Calc_Hess plus NumFreq over 24 atoms is of order two times 144 QM/MM gradients on top of the
optimisation cycles. WP4's analogue ran 46 cycles in 5 h 58 m on these same 8 cores and 24 GB, so
roughly eight minutes a gradient, which puts these two jobs at the order of forty hours each. Both
sit on the week queue's 168 hours, and the 1000-cycle MaxIter is unchanged.

ONE RESIDUAL RISK, STATED BEFORE IT IS RUN

Hybrid_Hess is how ORCA expresses "exact over these atoms" when part of the system is molecular
mechanics, and the control has no MM region at all: its ActiveAtoms are simply all 24 atoms it has.
Listing all of them in Hybrid_Hess therefore asks for the full Hessian, which is what the control
needs, and the two arms keep an identical %geom block so that they are computed alike - which is
the whole reason the criteria insist on a control. If ORCA refuses Hybrid_Hess in a job with no
QM/MM block, it will say so in a second, as it did today, and the fix is to delete that one line
from the control's input only. That would make the two %geom blocks differ by a line that is a
no-op for the control, and it would be recorded here as NOTE 1's own second entry rather than
quietly applied.

WHAT THIS NOTE CANNOT SETTLE

1. Whether either TS search converges at all, and whether what it reaches is a first-order saddle.
   The criteria's own test applies: a TS that does not return exactly one imaginary mode is reported
   as such and is not used for a relaxed barrier.
2. The imaginary-mode count may not be one. The committed reduced-region run of this system returned
   TWO, at 313.30 and 18.65 cm-1, and WRITEUP_INDEX.md already flags the second as needing a decision.
   If that recurs here it is reported, not resolved by this note.
3. Nothing about the reactant side, which is running under the committed protocol.
4. Nothing about the catalytic verdict, which remains SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.5's chain.
