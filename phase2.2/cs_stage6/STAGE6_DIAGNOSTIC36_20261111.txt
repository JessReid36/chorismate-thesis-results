STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 36
A correlated reference for the in vacuo barrier: DLPNO-CCSD(T) on frame 42436.

Pre-registered 11 November 2026, before any D36 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The production level of theory was never adopted by an amendment. D28 made the case
for CAM-B3LYP and closed with "the production functional is NOT changed by this
document. NOTHING IS DECIDED HERE." Checked while writing this: Stage 6's Amendments 1
to 5 are the A matrix, the removal of CPCM, A3, the coordinate system and Hessian, and
the stability criterion. None of them touches the functional. D30 onward nevertheless
all run CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD, and so do D34's inputs.

The evidence for that choice is one property. D28 measured where the bare dianion's
highest occupied orbital sits, against a target of -0.5123 eV derived from Probe 3's
measured detachment energy of +0.5123 eV: B3LYP +1.2307 eV, CAM-B3LYP -0.5918,
wB97X-D3 -1.3424. B3LYP puts the electron above the vacuum level and is qualitatively
wrong for this dianion; between the two range-separated functionals that test prefers
CAM-B3LYP by 0.0795 eV against 0.8301 eV.

It says nothing about the barrier, and on the barrier the two disagree. Recomputed here
from D28's own control runs:

   functional   def2-SVPD    def2-TZVPD
   CAM-B3LYP      26.8248      25.7003
   wB97X-D3       29.0441      27.9689

and B3LYP gives 18.4610 at def2-SVP, 18.332256 at def2-SVPD and 17.304571 at
def2-TZVPD (D27). The spread between the two range-separated functionals is 2.2 at
double zeta and 2.3 at triple zeta, and B3LYP sits more than eight below either.

Nothing in the record can arbitrate that. The experimental anchors the record holds -
STAGE6_ERRATUM7 E7.11 records 24.5 and 20.7 kcal/mol in water - are a free energy and
an enthalpy in solution, and E7.11 is explicit that "Any comparison of a computed
barrier with an experimental one must state which quantity is which". Ours is an in
vacuo electronic barrier on frozen geometries. It is not either of them.

A correlated wavefunction method computes that same quantity without choosing a
functional. That is what D36 is for. It does not make a functional correct; it measures
how far each one sits from a correlated treatment of the same electrons at the same
geometry and the same basis.

THERE IS NO COUPLED-CLUSTER PRECEDENT IN THIS RECORD. Checked while writing this: no
committed input anywhere in either repository contains DLPNO, CCSD, an /C auxiliary
basis or AutoAux. Every convention below is therefore stated here for the first time
rather than inherited, and that is itself a reason to run the control in section 3.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Six single points on the committed bare substrate of frame 42436,
d33a/sp_42436_{R,TS}/substrate.xyz, charge -2, multiplicity 1, frozen geometries.

ARM C, the control, 2 runs, FIRST.
   D28's own input, copied byte for byte:
      ! CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
   Its geometry is already identical to d33a's by sha256, checked while writing this,
   so this is a repeat of a committed calculation and must return the committed number.
   See G36.3. Nothing else in D36 may be read if it does not.

ARM H, the reference determinant, 2 runs, SECOND.
      ! HF def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
   DLPNO-CCSD(T) is built on a Hartree-Fock reference, and for a dianion that reference
   may be poorly behaved. Arm H measures it directly rather than leaving it to be dug
   out of arm B's output: the HF barrier, and the HF highest-occupied orbital energy at
   each leg, reported beside D28's three DFT values.

ARM B, the benchmark, 2 runs, LAST.
      ! DLPNO-CCSD(T) def2-TZVPD AutoAux TightSCF TightPNO
   with %maxcore 5000 and 8 cores.

   THE BASIS IS def2-TZVPD AND NOT A LARGER ONE, DELIBERATELY. The comparison that
   matters is against CAM-B3LYP/def2-TZVPD at 25.7003 and wB97X-D3/def2-TZVPD at
   27.9689, which are committed at exactly this basis. Running the benchmark at a
   larger basis would change two things at once and would answer a different question.
   The consequence is stated in D36.2: this is a comparison of methods at a fixed
   basis, not a converged absolute barrier.

   THE AUXILIARY IS AutoAux, FOR A STATED REASON. ORCA ships /C correlation-fitting
   sets for the def2-TZVPP family; whether one exists for def2-TZVPD is not something
   this record establishes, and guessing a basis-set name that may not exist is how a
   job fails at three in the morning. AutoAux is generated from the orbital basis and
   is always available. If ORCA rejects it, that is reported as a failure and a
   different auxiliary needs its own amendment; it is not swapped silently.

   Frozen core is ORCA's default and is not overridden.

ORDER. Arm C's two runs, then arm H's two, then arm B's two, reactant before transition
state in each. Cheapest and most diagnostic first, so a walltime or memory failure in
arm B costs nothing already measured.

RESOURCES. 8 cores, 48 GB, walltime 24:00:00, which routes to day. No -q. The DFT and
HF runs at this size take minutes; the two DLPNO-CCSD(T) runs are the unknown, and 24
hours is requested for that reason. If arm B exceeds the walltime or the memory, that
is reported as a failure with the scheduler's own words, arms C and H stand, and a
larger request needs its own amendment.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

       correlation components separated as ORCA reports them.
R36.2  Each functional's barrier minus the DLPNO-CCSD(T) barrier, at def2-TZVPD and at
       the same geometry: CAM-B3LYP (25.7003), wB97X-D3 (27.9689), B3LYP (17.304571).
       This is the ranking the diagnostic exists to produce.

       If neither can be read, that is stated and the benchmark is reported with the
       reference-quality question open rather than silently assumed closed.
R36.5  Whether the double-zeta to triple-zeta shift the functionals show - CAM-B3LYP
       -1.1245 and wB97X-D3 -1.0752, both derived from the table in section 1 - is a

       about one kcal/mol. The answer may be yes whatever the benchmark says; D36 does
       not run CCSD(T) at def2-SVPD and cannot measure the method's own basis shift.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

G36.2  Arm C's input is byte-identical by sha256 to D28's
       CAM-B3LYP/def2-TZVPD/ctl_{R,TS}/job.inp.
G36.3  CONTROL. Arm C reproduces D28's committed energies, -837.20938385 Eh at the
       reactant and -837.16842778 Eh at the transition state, to within 1e-6 Eh each,
       and its barrier equals 25.7003 kcal/mol to within 0.0010. If it does not, the
       geometry or the pipeline is not what this document says and nothing else is
       readable.
G36.4  Every run terminates normally, converges its SCF, and reports 118 electrons.
G36.5  Arm B's output reports a converged coupled-cluster calculation and prints the
       final CCSD(T) energy; a run that terminates normally without one is a failure.
G36.6  The charge is -2 and the multiplicity 1 in all six inputs.
G36.7  No file under d28, d33a or frozen is modified by this diagnostic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D36.1  It does not make any functional correct. It measures distance from one
       correlated treatment at one basis on one geometry. A functional that sits
       closest here is the closest here, and nothing more.
D36.2  IT IS NOT A CONVERGED ABSOLUTE BARRIER. def2-TZVPD is a modest basis for
       CCSD(T) and the basis was chosen to match the committed DFT arm rather than to
       converge the correlation energy. The number is a reference for comparison at
       this basis, and must not be quoted as the true in vacuo barrier.
D36.3  It is still an in vacuo electronic barrier on one frozen frame. It is not the
       experimental 24.5 or 20.7 kcal/mol, which are a free energy and an enthalpy in
       water, and E7.11's rule applies to every sentence that puts them near each
       other.
D36.4  DLPNO is an approximation to CCSD(T). TightPNO is requested, and the residual
       PNO error is not measured here.
D36.5  A dianion is a hard case for a single-reference method. If the HF reference
       places the highest occupied orbital above the vacuum level, or if the T1
       diagnostic is large, the benchmark is reported WITH that caveat attached
       wherever it is used, and a multireference check becomes an open item.
D36.6  No charges, no design, no wall, no reliability claim. Nothing here bears on
       r_safe, on any barrier lowering, or on which designs survive.
D36.7  It does not adopt a production level of theory. That is an amendment and a
       decision for Jessica, and D36 only supplies evidence for it.
D36.8  Nothing here is shaped toward a lower barrier. No outcome is preferred: if
       CCSD(T) lands nearest B3LYP, which would overturn D28's direction, that is the
       result and it is reported as plainly as the opposite.
