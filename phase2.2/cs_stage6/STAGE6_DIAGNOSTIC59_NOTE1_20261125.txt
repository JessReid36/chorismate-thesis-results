STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 59 - NOTE 1: the 60 runs with the charges and the functions together stopped
in ORCA's start-up; the cause; what follows

25 November 2026. Written after D59's jobs ended and before its outputs were collected, from the
job logs and one output read on hpc1 (Jessica's printouts of 17:26, 17:30 and 17:32); no number
of D59 was read. Claude drafted this text; it takes effect when Jessica commits it, with
DIAGNOSTIC 61's pre-registration. Nothing committed is edited by it.


N1.1  WHAT HAPPENED

All 24 jobs of D59 ran to their end, and 226 of its 286 runs ended normally. The 60 that did not
are exactly the runs F (the design's charges and the functions together) at CAM, WXV and HF, for
the ten designs, at R and at TS. Each ended with "ORCA finished by error termination in
Startup". The one output read, d59/CAM_F_S_n05_k2_R/job.out, gives the reason while it computes
the nuclear repulsion with the point charges: "Error (SHARK/DRIVER/Startup): Zero distance
encountered between atoms 24 and charge 2". Atom 24, counting from zero, is the first ghost atom
after the 24 atoms of the substrate; charge 2, counting from zero, is the first positive charge
of the run's pc.pc, and the two stand at the same position. Every run N, GF and GN, and every
run of the wall, ended normally.


N1.2  THE CAUSE

cs6_d59_prepare.py wrote each ghost centre at the position of its positive charge as D56's pc.pc
writes it ("pos = [x for q, x in Q if q > 0]"). D59's pre-registration asks for "the functions
of D50 (set X2, treatment A) as a ghost hydrogen at each positive charge, D53's committed ghost
block word for word." D53's committed inputs, whose head and ghost block D59 copies, put each
ghost centre 0.01 A beyond its charge, by the rule of the committed cs6_d48_prepare.py ("the
ghost centre sits this far beyond the charge, on the line from the nearest atom") that D50, D53
and D54 used; cs6_d53_prepare.py stops when "a ghost centre does not lie 0.01 A from the
charge". THE ERROR IS CLAUDE'S: the words of D59's pre-registration were met and the distance
D53's inputs keep was not. It was not caught because the rehearsal's stand-in for ORCA printed
committed outputs and did not read the geometry, and because Claude's comparison of D59's inputs
with D53's noted that the positions of the ghost centres differ without testing their distance
from the charges.


N1.3  WHAT IT CHANGES

R59.1 cannot be formed for any of the ten designs; D59's committed reader reports its runs F as
they are. R59.2 and R59.3 are not touched: no run of the wall carries a ghost atom. D59's runs
GF ended normally, but their ghost centres stand at the positions of the charges, so they are
the control of no run with the charges and the functions together; DIAGNOSTIC 61 forms its own.
D59's runs N and GN carry no ghost atom and are read by DIAGNOSTIC 61 as D59's pre-registration
reads them.


N1.4  WHAT FOLLOWS

DIAGNOSTIC 61, pre-registered with this note, makes the runs F and GF again in new folders
(d61/), each ghost centre 0.01 A beyond its charge by D48's rule, and reads R59.1 as D59's
pre-registration fixed it, with D59's committed runs N and GN. No run of D59 is made again in
its folder: "No run is made again in its folder." Jessica chose that D61 is built before the
relaxation pilot: "d61".


N1.5  DIRECTION

None on any barrier reported as a result. The test that could report a design as taking
electrons with functions at its positive charges has not been made yet; nothing is rescued or
dropped by this note.
