   - A runs (16): S_n20_k1 with every charge so walled, both sets, the same states and levels

   - Z runs (32): the controls of the wall alone, A10.3 item 3 (c): the same centres with no

   - I runs (48): the measurement of item 4 at site 1690 of sites_f42436.tsv, DIAGNOSTIC 3's

     neon set and the published capping block of A12.3 item 5; each of the three with no charge;

Every geometry and every position of a charge is copied from a committed file: the substrate and
point charges from D56's committed inputs, the helium and neon blocks from d23 and additivity as
FORMS TEST 1 copied them, the capping block as A12.3 item 5 transcribes it. The method line and
the stability analysis are D56's. Three forms these runs use were not among FORMS TEST 1's: a
walled centre that carries a negative charge (the A runs), a helium or neon centre that carries
no charge (the Z and Y runs, He0 and Ne0), and the capping block with no charge (Shi0). In the
committed record, 14 runs with a negative helium centre and 10 with an uncharged one (d23), and
134 runs with a negative neon centre and 216 with an uncharged one (d16, d22, additivity,
sitescan and others), all ended normally with ORCA 6.0.1, all at B3LYP-D3BJ and none at the
method of D56. They show only that ORCA takes these forms and ends normally; no number of theirs
is used here. B3LYP is not this work's method since DIAGNOSTIC 28, whose result placed the bare
substrate's highest occupied orbital at +1.2307 eV with B3LYP and -0.5918 eV with CAM-B3LYP and
said: "Probe 3's measured detachment energy is +0.5123 eV, so for the exact functional this
orbital should sit near -0.5123 eV." The capping block with no charge has not run before. A run
that ORCA refuses is NOT MEASURED and is not made again, and the readings that use it are NOT
FORMED; no other run waits on it. 144 runs in 9 jobs; the caps are D56's (1800 s at def2-SVPD,
5000 s at def2-TZVPD); a run that reaches its cap is stopped and not made again.


3. THE READINGS, FIXED NOW

R57.1 THE WALLED DESIGNS. Each run is read by D56's committed functions (cs6_d56.read_run and
cs6_d53.legs, with cs6_d40, cs6_d33b and cs6_audit), against D56's controls of the same level,
and its four legs, fifth leg, ddE and the agreement of the two levels are formed exactly as D56
formed them, with one change that D32 made before it: "So the dipole ratio is RECORDED AND
REPORTED FOR EVERY CONDITION AND GATES NOTHING." So a walled run is RELIABLE, UNRELIABLE or
INCONCLUSIVE on the Loewdin leg, the gap and the stability analysis, and its dipole ratio is
printed beside. The word of A9.4 (i) is then formed for each set and printed as BEHIND THE
HELIUM WALL or BEHIND THE NEON WALL, beside D56's committed word for the design. ddE is also
given against the wall-alone control of the same set, which is the effect of the charges with
the walls already in place. ORCA prints each walled centre in the Loewdin block under the symbol
Q, with the charge given to it and not a population; the committed cs6_audit leaves those rows
out of the largest Loewdin charge, and cs6_d53 compares the 24 atoms only. Before this was
written Claude read FORMS TEST 1's committed SV_wallpos_R with these functions against D56's
SV_C_ctl_R: it was read with no stop, its row Q carried +0.250000, and the Loewdin charges of
the 24 atoms summed to those of the control within 0.000001 e.

R57.2 THE WALL ALONE. For each Z run, the Loewdin rise over the plain control (D32.5's use of
it) and the change of the barrier the centres make with no charge on them. A wall-alone control
whose Loewdin rise reaches D_LW is reported as a wall that perturbs the density by itself.

R57.3 THE WALL AGAINST REAL IONS, at each level separately. ddE(M) is the change of the barrier
with M present against the substrate alone. As DIAGNOSTIC 3 defined it, "ion(X) = ddE(real X+) -
ddE(ghost X) is what the ion does BEYOND supplying basis functions." and D3's own result asked
for it: "A counterpoise treatment is required before this question can be answered." With T the
mean of ion(Na) and ion(K) and s half their difference, the distance of each model M (the bare
+1 e, and +1 e behind each of the three walls) from the real ions is d_M = |ddE(M) - T|. A
WALLED CHARGE IS NEARER THE IONS THAN THE BARE CHARGE if d_wall + max(s, 0.30) < d_bare; IT IS
NOT NEARER if d_wall > d_bare + max(s, 0.30); otherwise NOT DISTINGUISHED. 0.30 kcal/mol is the
size D3's pre-registration fixed for a basis-set effect too small to count: "If |ddE(ghost)|
exceeds 0.30 kcal/mol at either basis, the basis-set contribution is not negligible". It is a
size chosen before any run, not a number a functional computed, and it was chosen for that
question and not for this one. s is how far the two real ions disagree with each other, and no
model can be said to be nearer them than that. Each ion(X), each ddE(M), the walls with no
charge and the ghosts are printed.

THE HELIUM WALL IS SUPPORTED BY THIS MEASUREMENT if, at both levels, the helium-walled charge is
NEARER THE IONS THAN THE BARE CHARGE; otherwise it is NOT SUPPORTED. AMENDMENT 13 NOTE 1 sends
the pilot's walled arm here: "a second arm behind the helium wall only if D57's measurement
against real ions supports the wall by the reading its own pre-registration fixes" A tie gives
no reason to add that arm.

5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW

Whatever R57.1 finds, no design is named, kept or dropped on it and none is rescued: "The word
SURVIVOR is read on bare charges, as D56's pre-registration fixed it." R57.3 decides one thing,
the pilot's walled arm, by section 3. "Whether a walled reading may ever count for a design is
for a dated amendment written after that result." No run is made again in its folder.


6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

R57.3 is one site, one frame, a whole charge, frozen geometries, in vacuo. "WHAT IT CANNOT SAY:
anything of a charge of 0.25 e, for which no real ion exists." The walls on 0.25 e stand for no
atom; their readings are of a model, beside the bare one.
