STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 36 - AMENDMENT 1
Arm B exceeded its walltime. The resources change; the calculation does not.

Written 13 November 2026, after attempt 1 was killed and before attempt 2 is submitted.
STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC36_20261111.txt is not edited. This amendment records what happened,
what is changed, and what is deliberately left alone.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHAT HAPPENED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The job ran on comp040 from 15:14:44 on 11 November and was killed by the scheduler in
its own words:

   =>> PBS: job killed: walltime 86516 exceeded limit 86400

Arms C and H finished and are valid:

   C_R    18 min 31 s      C_TS   19 min 00 s
   H_R    11 min 25 s      H_TS   11 min 09 s

Arm B did not. B_R ran about 23 hours and was killed inside the triples correction;
B_TS never started. What B_R did reach, read from its archived output:

   E(0), the Hartree-Fock reference        -832.744852436 Eh   measured
   E(CORR)(strong-pairs)                     -3.023507483 Eh   measured
   E(CORR)(weak-pairs)                       -0.004618489 Eh   measured
   E(CORR)(corrected)                        -3.028125973 Eh   measured
   E(CCSD) = E(0) + E(CORR)                -835.772978409 Eh   DERIVED here
   T1 diagnostic                              0.015649748      measured

So the Hartree-Fock reference and the full CCSD converged, and the run died in the
(T) correction: of 11618 triples, five batches completed at roughly 1900 to 1980
seconds each, about 2.7 hours in all. The remaining twenty hours went to the SCF and
the CCSD iterations before the triples began.

THE T1 DIAGNOSTIC ADDRESSES PART OF D36.5. The pre-registration named a large T1 as a
reason the benchmark would have to be reported with a multireference caveat. At
0.0157 the reactant sits below the conventional 0.02 guide. That guide is not this
project's and was not pre-registered; the number is the measurement. The transition
state has not been measured and the question stays open for it.

NONE OF THIS IS COMMITTED AS A RESULT. A failed job is not committed; a note about it
is, and this amendment is that note. The archived output lives outside git at
chorismate-hpc-archive/d36_attempt1_walltime with a sha256 for every file.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHY IT WAS SLOW. TWO CAUSES, BOTH OURS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FIRST, THE MEMORY REQUEST STARVED IT ONTO DISK. The input asked %maxcore 5000 on 8
cores, 40 GB, on a node holding 126 GB. ORCA reported

   PNO-Integral Storage                    ... ON DISK

and wrote 36 GB of intermediates: job.PNO3.tmp at 10.9 GB, job.PNO4.tmp at 3.8 GB and
six VABPAO.tmp files at 1.8 GB each. Those were written to /home, which is NFS from
comp047 and 90 per cent full, and streamed through every CCSD iteration. Node-local
/scratch is 50 GB with 27 GB free and so cannot hold them either; the answer is to
give ORCA enough memory that it does not go to disk at all.

SECOND, THE NODE WAS SHARED. comp040 has 64 cores and 126 GB and was running seven
other jobs. Arm C is a repeat of D28's CAM-B3LYP/def2-TZVPD control, which took 9.7
and 9.2 minutes when D28 ran it; here it took 18.5 and 19.0. Plain density functional
theory with no PNO files at all ran at half speed, so the contention is real and is
not an artefact of the coupled-cluster arm.

THE WALLTIME CEILING WAS NEVER THE CLUSTER'S. Checked while writing this: the queues
are day at 24:00:00, week at 176:00:00 and month at 744:00:00, and routing is by
walltime with no queue requested. Attempt 1 asked for 24 hours because that is what
every earlier diagnostic in this record asked for. That was a carried-over habit and
not a constraint.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                           attempt 1              attempt 2
   job layout              both legs, one job     one job per leg
   PBS ncpus               8                      16
   PBS mem                 48gb                   240gb
   %pal nprocs             8                      16
   %maxcore                5000                   12000
   walltime                24:00:00               168:00:00, routing to week

TWO LINES INSIDE THE INPUT CHANGE, NOT ONE. Attempt 1's input carried %pal nprocs 8,
so asking PBS for 16 cores without changing it would have left ORCA using eight and
the rest idle. Both %pal and %maxcore are resource directives: they decide how the
work is divided and where integrals are held, not what is computed.

One job per leg so that B_TS starts whatever B_R does. 16 cores and 240 GB so the job
lands on one of the 364 GB nodes with room to spare. %maxcore 12000 on 16 cores is
192 GB inside a 240 GB request, which should keep the PNO integrals in memory; whether
it does is reported, because the output states ON DISK or otherwise and the result
will quote it. The job script also prints nproc and the free memory it actually got,
so a job that lands on a contended node says so in its own output.

The runs go in a new directory, d36a1, so attempt 1's committed inputs stay exactly as
they are and remain the record of what was tried.

EVERY ONE OF THESE IS A RESOURCE AND NOT A METHOD. Cores, memory, walltime and the
job layout cannot change a converged energy. %maxcore changes where integrals are
stored, not what they are.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The method line is untouched, byte for byte:

   ! DLPNO-CCSD(T) def2-TZVPD AutoAux TightSCF TightPNO

TightPNO stays. Loosening it would make the job faster and would also change the
number, and a reference that has been loosened to fit a queue is not a reference. If
attempt 2 also fails, loosening the thresholds or dropping the (T) are the next
options and each needs its own amendment and its own justification; neither is taken
here in advance.

AutoAux stays. Attempt 1 cleared the suspicion against it: the three integral
transformations took 50.7, 65.6 and 37.7 seconds, so the auxiliary basis is not where
the time went.

def2-TZVPD stays, for the reason D36 section 2 gives: it matches the committed DFT arm
so the comparison changes one thing and not two.

The geometry stays, byte for byte from d33a/sp_42436_{R,TS}/substrate.xyz.

Arms C and H are NOT re-run. They finished, they are valid, and their outputs on hpc1
are the ones the harvest will read.

Every reading R36.1 to R36.5, every gate G36.1 to G36.7 and every limitation D36.1 to
D36.8 stands unchanged.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. A NEW GATE, FROM THE FAILURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G36.8  Attempt 2's B_R must reproduce attempt 1's Hartree-Fock reference and CCSD
       correlation energy, to within 1e-6 Eh each:

          E(0)                 -832.744852436
          E(CORR)(corrected)     -3.028125973

       Nothing that section 3 changes can alter either number, so if they move, the
       calculation is not the one attempt 1 ran and nothing downstream is readable.
       This is a control that cost nothing: it exists only because the first attempt
       failed where it did.

G36.9  Attempt 2's input differs from the committed d36/B_{R,TS}/job.inp in exactly
       two lines, %maxcore and %pal nprocs, and in no other byte, and its geometry is
       byte-identical to d33a's.

G36.10 The result reports whether ORCA kept the PNO integrals in memory or on disk,
       and the wall-clock time of each leg, so the next person knows what this cost.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS AMENDMENT DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A36.1  It does not adopt a production level of theory. D36 supplies evidence for that
       amendment; it is not that amendment.
A36.2  It does not make the benchmark more likely to favour any functional. Nothing
       changed can move the number.
A36.3  It does not promise attempt 2 will finish. If it does not, the next step is a
       method change and that needs its own amendment, written before it is run.
A36.4  It does not withdraw or revise anything in STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC36_20261111.txt.
A36.5  It does not treat the partial reactant numbers in section 1 as a result. They
       are a note on a failed job, and the only use made of them is as G36.8's
       control.
