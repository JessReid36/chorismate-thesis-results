R29.1  THE RESULT

  scalar       C (highest not moved)   B (lowest moved)    margin      verdict
  Sigma q^2          1.1381625             1.1381625       +0.0000000  OVERLAPS
  Sigma |q|          3.2850000             3.2850000       +0.0000000  OVERLAPS
  max |q|            0.6500000             0.4125000       -0.2375000  OVERLAPS

  VERDICT   NO SCALAR IN THE SET ORDERS RELOCATION.

This is the outcome D29.3 named in advance as admissible: the diagnostic sets no limit, and that
is its result. The monotonicity and cross-functional checks do not run, because both are defined
on a preferred scalar and there is none.

For Sigma q^2 and Sigma |q| the two bounds are not merely close. They are the same number, to
every digit a double holds, and they are the same number for one reason.


R29.2  WHY, IN ONE PAIR OF RUNS

  transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_R    Sigma |q| 3.2850000000   Sigma q^2 1.138162500000
                                 Loewdin rise +0.3181   dipole ratio 1.4769   MOVED
  transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_TS   Sigma |q| 3.2850000000   Sigma q^2 1.138162500000
                                 Loewdin rise +0.1136   dipole ratio 1.3071   did not move

THE TWO CHARGE VECTORS ARE IDENTICAL. Not the same totals - the same ten charges, on the same ten
sites, read from pc.pc files whose charge columns compare equal element by element. The two runs
differ in the substrate geometry alone, R against TS, and the density relocates in one and not in
the other.

So no function of the charges can order relocation, because the same charges give both answers.
That holds for the three scalars tested and for any other function of the charge vector, since
such a function is constant across this pair by construction. It is not a statement about where a
threshold lies; it is a statement that a threshold of this kind does not exist.

max |q| fails differently and more coarsely, running backwards: the highest value among
conditions that did not move, 0.6500 e, is above the lowest among those that did, 0.4125 e.


R29.3  THE SET THAT WAS READ

  182  conditions in a diffuse basis carrying both a Loewdin and a dipole block
  170  primary set, functional B3LYP
   12  held back for the cross-functional check, CAM-B3LYP and wB97X-D3
   35  distinct arrangements in the primary set, counting by site coordinates and not by charge
   26  charge-free controls, in 12 (functional, basis, state) keys, every key's spread 0.000000
   15  primary conditions moved; 155 did not; the 15 fall in 2 arrangements

D29.2's availability counts of 110 conditions and 16 controls were both wrong. The reader that
produced them walked a glob over directory names and silently excluded every top-level directory
not beginning with "d". STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC29_ERRATUM1_20261105.txt records this, and it is the
reason this result exists in the form it does: on the restricted set both Sigma q^2 and Sigma |q|
appeared to separate cleanly, and that reading was wrong.

A CONDITION is a directory carrying a pc.pc. A directory WITHOUT one is not thereby charge-free:
the ECP-walled arms carry their charges as Ne> lines in job.inp, and 606 job.inp files in this
tree do so. The reader requires charges absent from both places before calling a directory a
control. The two representations are disjoint: no directory has both.

OF THE 15 THAT MOVED, 14 satisfy both legs of the criterion and one satisfies the Loewdin leg
alone - transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_R, the run above. None rests on the dipole leg alone, which is
the leg the project already knows to be artefact-prone where ECP centres are present.

THE HELD-BACK CONDITIONS ARE REPORTED DESCRIPTIVELY, there being no boundary to test them
against. Under both CAM-B3LYP and wB97X-D3 the Sigma |q| = 5.6433 e conditions move and the
1.3000 e and 2.8800 e conditions do not, which is what DIAGNOSTIC 28 found.


R29.4  WHAT THIS CHANGES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

ERRATUM 5 IS CONFIRMED AND STRENGTHENED. Erratum 5 found, of the CLEAN/STRAINED verdict at
def2-SVP, that "S6-5 AND E5 CARRY THE SAME TOTAL CHARGE AND THE SAME LARGEST SINGLE CHARGE, ON
THE SAME BASIS, AND GET OPPOSITE VERDICTS. They differ in how the charge is arranged, not how
much of it there is." The same conclusion now holds for the relocation leg alone, on diffuse
bases, and by a stronger instance: not two designs sharing two scalars, but one charge vector
placed against two substrate geometries.

THE WITHDRAWN Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 IS NOT REPLACED. There is nothing to replace it with. Any
limit written into the optimiser would have to be a function of something other than the charge
vector - the arrangement, the geometry, or both - and this diagnostic does not supply one.
D29.4.1 said in advance that this diagnostic does not set the budget, and it does not.

THE SET STILL CONTAINS NO UNIT CHARGE. The primary set's max |q| runs from 0.0190 to 0.6500 e.
Every design carrying a +-1.00 e charge - S6-P, S6-2, S6-3, S6-5, E5, E7 - was run only at
def2-SVP, the basis DIAGNOSTIC 27 showed to be blind to relocation for want of diffuse functions.
Nothing in the record measures relocation for a +-1 e design. This was not foreseen in D29.4 and
is recorded here.

The six items of D29.4 stand as written.
