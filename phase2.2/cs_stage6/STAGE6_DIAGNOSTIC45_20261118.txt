STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 45
The approximations of the coupled-cluster benchmark, measured: the triples, the PNO
threshold and the basis set, on the bare substrate of frame 42436.

Pre-registered 18 November 2026, before any D45 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.
A search of the tracked file names and of the text files of both repositories found no
diagnostic that uses the number 45. The directories standoff/d450_* are a distance of
4.50 A and not a diagnostic.
It is read beside AMENDMENT 8, which is committed with it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every comparison of a functional with a correlated barrier in this record rests on two
numbers: D36's DLPNO-CCSD(T) barrier at def2-TZVPD, "THE BARRIER IS 23.8603 kcal/mol.",
and D41's at def2-SVPD, "THE BARRIER AT def2-SVPD IS 22.8968 kcal/mol." The documents
that give them say what was not measured.

   The PNO threshold. D36's result: "TightPNO was requested; the residual PNO error is
   not measured here." D41.4: "DLPNO is an approximation; TightPNO is requested and its
   residual error is not measured."
   The basis set. D36's result: "23.8603 is a reference for comparison at this basis and
   must not be quoted as the true in vacuo barrier." D41's result: "THE CORRELATED BARRIER
   SHIFTS BY +0.9635 kcal/mol from def2-SVPD to def2-TZVPD." D41.5: "Two basis sets do not
   make an extrapolation, and none is attempted."
   The triples. No document of the record says which treatment of the triples the
   benchmark used. The four committed outputs say it themselves: each prints a time under
   "Total time for T0" and "Total time for Iterative (T)    ...     0.000 sec". The
   benchmark's triples are the non-iterative ones. ORCA 6.0's manual lists the keyword
   that was used, DLPNO-CCSD(T), as "DLPNO-CCSD with perturbative triple excitations" and
   a second one, DLPNO-CCSD(T1), as "DLPNO-CCSD with iterative perturbative triple
   excitations".

AMENDMENT 7 left this open, A7.4: "WHICH BENCHMARK IS NEARER THE TRUTH." It also said
what no agreement among functionals can stand in for: "Nothing in the record measures it.
Agreement of three functionals is not that measurement."

On 18 November 2026 Jessica was shown the three things above as shortfalls of the
benchmark against what the literature asks of a reference, with the cost of each, asked
that the choice not be the easiest one but a balance of accuracy, standard and cost, and
agreed to these checks: "okay yes you can proceed". The papers that are the reason for
each check are named here as the reason and not as evidence for any number:
   - the triples: Guo, Riplinger, Becker, Liakos, Minenkov, Cavallo and Neese, J. Chem.
     Phys. 2018, 148, 011101, which introduces the iterative treatment (T1) beside the
     non-iterative one (T0);
   - the PNO threshold: Altun, Neese and Bistoni, J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2020, 16, 6142,
     for the two-point formula from TCutPNO 1e-6 and 1e-7; and Gray and Herbert, J. Chem.
     Phys. 2024, 161, 054114, for the warning that with diffuse basis functions the
     behaviour of DLPNO energies with the threshold has to be looked at and not assumed.

D45 measures the three on the bare barrier. It measures nothing with charges: that is
DIAGNOSTIC 46, which AMENDMENT 8 describes.

2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Fourteen single points, each at the reactant (R) and the transition state (TS) of frame
42436, on d33a's committed geometries. No charges, no MM region, no wall. Every input is
the committed input of the def2-SVPD benchmark, d38/N/job.inp, which d41/T/job.inp
repeats byte for byte,

   ! DLPNO-CCSD(T) def2-SVPD AutoAux TightSCF TightPNO
   %maxcore 6000, %pal nprocs 16, %scf MaxIter 300, charge -2, multiplicity 1

with ONE thing changed:

   runs             what is changed
   T1_R, T1_TS      DLPNO-CCSD(T) becomes DLPNO-CCSD(T1)
   P6_R, P6_TS      a block  %mdci / TCutPNO 1e-6 / end  is added
   P8_R, P8_TS      a block  %mdci / TCutPNO 1e-8 / end  is added
   M_SV_R, M_SV_TS  the method line becomes  ! RI-MP2 def2-SVPD AutoAux TightSCF
   M_TZ_R, M_TZ_TS  the same with def2-TZVPD
   M_TP_R, M_TP_TS  the same with def2-TZVPPD
   M_QZ_R, M_QZ_TS  the same with def2-QZVPPD

The MP2 method line is the committed one with the method and the basis set changed and
TightPNO, which belongs to the DLPNO method, taken off. TightPNO stays in the P6 and P8
inputs, so its other thresholds stay; only TCutPNO is changed in the input, and whatever
ORCA derives from it is printed (R45.1).

THE COMMITTED LEGS ARE NOT RUN AGAIN. The series of the PNO threshold has the committed
runs as its middle point, 1e-7: d38/N (R) and d41/T (TS). The triples are compared with
the same two. The coupled-cluster barrier at def2-TZVPD is d36a2/B_R and B_TS.

WHY THESE BASIS SETS. def2-SVPD and def2-TZVPD are the record's two. def2-TZVPPD and
def2-QZVPPD are the triple-zeta and quadruple-zeta sets of the same family with the same
diffuse functions: a pair one step apart, which is what a two-point extrapolation needs.
The basis functions of the dianion number 402 and 682 in the first two, by the committed
outputs. In the last two they number 746 and 1290 by a count from the basis set
definitions (the basis_set_exchange library); that count is not from a committed file,
and the reader prints what ORCA reports.

WHY MP2 FOR THE BASIS SET. A coupled-cluster run at def2-QZVPPD was costed and is not
made: the committed def2-TZVPD legs took 4 h 08 and 6 h 42 on 16 cores with 240 GB asked
for and up to 80 GB of scratch, at 682 functions, and the cost grows steeply with the
number of functions. The standard way round it is used instead: the dependence on the
basis set is measured with a cheaper correlated method and added to the coupled-cluster
number. What that assumes is said in section 3 and tested as far as the record allows.

THE ORDER. M_SV, M_TZ, M_TP, P6, T1, M_QZ, P8, each R then TS: the cheap and the certain
first, so that a job that is cut short has lost the least.

RESOURCES. One job, cm_d45, 16 cores, 120 GB, walltime 48:00:00, no -q. The job script is
D41's, for fourteen runs: it works in node-local scratch, copies each output home as the
run ends, samples the scratch in use every 60 s while ORCA is running, and removes the
scratch of a run when the run has ended. It can be submitted again as it is: it skips
every run that ended normally. A run that fails does not stop the runs after it.
   Scratch. The def2-SVPD benchmark needed 31 to 46 GB (D38 Erratum 1; D41, R41.6). What
   a tighter threshold needs is not measured. 250 GB is asked for and 120 GB is the least
   a location may offer; D41 found 1541 GB free where it ran. Those figures are not
   defended as sufficient. If a run fails on a write, that is a measurement of the
   footprint and not a failure of the method.
   Time. ESTIMATES, none of them measured: the MP2 runs a few minutes to an hour each at
   the first three basis sets and 3 to 8 hours each at def2-QZVPPD; P6 about an hour
   each or less; T1 2 to 4 hours each; P8 4 to 10 hours each. The sum is between about
   one day and about two. The three longest kinds of run have never been made in this
   project, and a job that reaches its walltime is submitted again as it is.

3. THE CONVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A BARRIER is E(TS) minus E(R) at 627.5094740631 kcal/mol per Eh, on the last FINAL SINGLE
POINT ENERGY of each output, with its parts as ORCA prints them: for a coupled-cluster
run E(0), E(CORR)(corrected), the triples correction and E(CCSD(T)); for an MP2 run the
SCF total energy, the RI-MP2 correlation energy and the MP2 total energy.

   b6, b7, b8   the DLPNO-CCSD(T) barrier at def2-SVPD at TCutPNO 1e-6, 1e-7 and 1e-8.
                b7 is the committed 22.8968.
   d67, d78     b7 - b6 and b8 - b7.

THE SAME REFERENCE. Every new def2-SVPD run, and the MP2 run at def2-TZVPD, starts from a
Hartree-Fock calculation that a committed run has already made. A new run is used only if
its Hartree-Fock energy is within 1e-6 Eh of the committed run's at the same basis set
and state. That figure is the one D44 uses for an input that repeats a committed one. It
is a test that two runs are the same calculation and not a tolerance on a result.

THE TRIPLES. T1 minus T0 on the barrier is the barrier of the T1 runs minus b7. It is
reported with the part of it that is the triples' own. No threshold is set on it,
because the record holds none.

THE PNO SERIES. With both differences formed,

   CONVERGING        |d78| is smaller than |d67|
   NOT CONVERGING    |d78| is |d67| or larger
   NOT ESTABLISHED   b6 or b8 is not formed

   The rule has no number in it. It asks one thing: does the second tightening move the
   barrier less than the first. It is blunt on purpose, and it does not pass by itself
   when the differences are small (WORKING_RULES.md, guard rail 4): two small differences
   of which the second is the larger read NOT CONVERGING. If that happens the numbers are
   printed and what is made of them is a dated amendment that says it was written with
   them in view. Whether the two steps go the same way, and whether the correlation
   energy recovered at each state is larger in size at each tighter threshold, are
   reported beside the word.

   THE TWO-POINT ESTIMATE is b6 + 1.5 x (b7 - b6), the formula of Altun, Neese and
   Bistoni with their factor for the pair 1e-6 and 1e-7. It is an ESTIMATE. b8 is a
   measurement, and the two are printed side by side.

THE BASIS SET. The MP2 barrier at each of the four basis sets and the steps between them
are measurements. THE EXTRAPOLATION uses the two formulas of ORCA 6.0's manual for its
automatic extrapolation, on def2-TZVPPD (X = 3) and def2-QZVPPD (Y = 4):

   SCF            E(X) = E(inf) + A exp(-alpha sqrt(X))             alpha = 7.88
   correlation    E(inf) = (X^beta E(X) - Y^beta E(Y)) / (X^beta - Y^beta)   beta = 2.97

   The manual gives those two exponents for def2-TZVPP and def2-QZVPP. THE SETS USED HERE
   CARRY DIFFUSE FUNCTIONS AND THE EXPONENTS WERE NOT MADE FOR THEM. The extrapolated
   barrier is an ESTIMATE on that ground alone, and the step it adds beyond def2-QZVPPD
   is printed so that its size can be seen.

   THE PREMISE of adding an MP2 basis step to a coupled-cluster barrier is that CCSD(T)
   minus MP2 does not depend on the basis set. The record allows one test of it: that
   difference at def2-SVPD and at def2-TZVPD, and its change. It is reported.

THE COMPOSITES. Two sums are printed, and both are ESTIMATES:

   COMPOSITE A   23.8603 + (T1 minus T0) + d78 + (MP2/def2-QZVPPD - MP2/def2-TZVPD)
   COMPOSITE B   23.8603 + (T1 minus T0) + 0.5 x d67 + (MP2 extrapolated - MP2/def2-TZVPD)

   A adds only steps that were measured. B replaces two of them with the extrapolated
   forms the literature uses. Each adds corrections measured one at a time, two of them
   at the smaller basis set and one at MP2; whether they add is not measured. By
   WORKING_RULES.md, guard rail 6, an estimate never carries a conclusion. Neither
   replaces D36's number.

5. GATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A failure of G45.1 to G45.4 stops the reader and nothing is written.

G45.1  Every file listed in d45/INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 has the sha256 listed there, the
       list is exactly the files of the diagnostic, and every input, RUNS.tsv and run.pbs
       are what cs6_d45_prepare.py writes when the reader runs it again on the committed
       files.
G45.2  Every file listed in d45/SOURCES.sha256 has the sha256 listed there, and no
       committed file under d33a, d36a2, d38 or d41 is modified.
G45.3  THE COMMITTED LEGS. d38/N, d41/T, d36a2/B_R and d36a2/B_TS end normally with a
       converged SCF and 118 electrons, their parts add up to their E(CCSD(T)) within
       2e-9 Eh, each prints TCutPNO: 1.000e-07, a time for T0 and 0.000 sec for the
       iterative (T), and they give 22.8968 and 23.8603 kcal/mol, +0.9635 apart. The two
       legs of each basis set print one DLPNO SETTINGS block and one number of basis
       functions. d37.tsv and d37a1.tsv give nine functionals at def2-TZVPD, and
       CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ's barrier minus D36's is the +1.8400 that AMENDMENT 7 prints.
G45.4  The nine sentences this document quotes from D36's result, D41 and its result, and
       AMENDMENT 7 are in those documents word for word, and in this one.

G45.5  PER RUN. A run is measured only if it terminates normally, converges its SCF,
       reports 118 electrons, ran on the committed coordinates of its state, prints as
       the input it ran the committed input beside it, reports the number of basis
       functions of the committed runs where its basis set is one of theirs, and passes
       the test of the same reference where it has a committed counterpart; and
       - a coupled-cluster run: prints every part of a CCSD(T) energy, each label with
         one value; the parts add up within 2e-9 Eh; its last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY is
         its E(CCSD(T)); it prints TCutPNO as asked, which for a T1 run is the committed
         1.000e-07, and TCutPairs, TCutMKN and TCutDO as the committed run prints them;
         a T1 run prints a time above zero for the iterative (T); a P6 or P8 run prints
         a time for T0 and 0.000 sec for the iterative (T);
       - an MP2 run: prints an SCF total energy, an RI-MP2 correlation energy and an MP2
         total energy that add up within 2e-9 Eh; its last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY is
         that total; and it correlates orbitals 16 to 58, which is the frozen core of the
         benchmark, 32 electrons.
       A run that is not measured is NOT READ, or NOT RUN if it left no output, with
       every reason. A reading that needs it is NOT FORMED. Nothing else stops.

    22.8968 are not edited. A composite is an estimate. The list of cross-check levels in

    benchmark may be used is a dated document. D46 measures the same two approximations

7. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D45.1  ONE FRAME, FROZEN GEOMETRIES, IN VACUO, NO CHARGES. It is not the experimental
       number and not a prediction of a physical barrier (E7.11; D36.2, D36.3).
D45.2  IT NAMES NO DESIGN, SAYS NOTHING ABOUT ddE AND ADOPTS NO LEVEL OF THEORY.
D45.3  (T1) IS STILL A PERTURBATIVE, LOCAL TREATMENT OF THE TRIPLES. Nothing here
       measures the error of CCSD(T) itself, and no excitation beyond the triples is
       run. The T1 diagnostics of D36 and D41 are the record's only statement on that.
D45.4  THE TRIPLES AND THE PNO THRESHOLD ARE CHECKED AT def2-SVPD ONLY, and they are
       carried to def2-TZVPD in a composite as if they were the same there. TCutPairs,
       TCutMKN and TCutDO are not varied.
D45.5  MP2 IS USED FOR ITS DEPENDENCE ON THE BASIS SET AND FOR NOTHING ELSE. Its barrier
       is not a result. The two exponents were made for def2-TZVPP and def2-QZVPP,
       without diffuse functions.
D45.6  THE AUXILIARY BASIS IS AutoAux AND THE CORE IS FROZEN IN EVERY RUN, as in the
       benchmark. Neither error is measured, and no relativistic or core correction is
       made.
D45.7  A COMPOSITE IS AN ESTIMATE AND NEVER CARRIES A CONCLUSION.
D45.8  NOTHING HERE IS SHAPED TOWARD AN OUTCOME. Every reading is printed whichever way it
       comes out, a correction that raises the benchmark as one that lowers it.

8. WHAT WAS KNOWN WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Known: the results of D36, D37, D37 Amendment 1, D38 and D41, so every committed number
above, among them the nine functionals' distances from D36's barrier. So it is known
which functionals a correction of either sign would bring nearer to the benchmark. No
correction is known, in size or in sign.
Known of D44: what AMENDMENT 8, A8.8, lists, and no energy.
NOT KNOWN: the energy of any run of D45. None exists.

WHAT HAS NO PRECEDENT IN THE RECORD. No committed output is a DLPNO-CCSD(T1) run, a run
at a TCutPNO other than 1e-7, or a pure RI-MP2 run. The reader's patterns for the lines
of such outputs were written from the committed non-iterative outputs and from the
double hybrid d37/PWPB95_SV_R, whose output prints the lines of ORCA's RI-MP2 step.
Whether ORCA 6.0.1 accepts the three changed inputs, and how long and how much scratch
they take, is not known. The keywords are the manual's.

The reader was tested before this was committed, on outputs that are not D45's:
committed outputs of d38/N, d41/T and d36a2, copied into D45's run directories and
altered where a test needed it, with the lines of an RI-MP2 step written in the form in
which d37/PWPB95_SV_R prints them. Every number seen that way is one of those
diagnostics' own or was made up for the test. The job script was tried with a stand-in
for ORCA: once with too little scratch, where it refused and left nothing behind but its
log; once through to the end; once with a run made to fail, where the runs after it went
on; and once cut short and submitted again, where it skipped the runs that had ended.
