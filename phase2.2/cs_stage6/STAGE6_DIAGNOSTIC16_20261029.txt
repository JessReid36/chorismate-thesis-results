Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 16 (29 October 2026).
Committed before any of its runs exist. Nothing of this diagnostic has been computed.


D16.1  THE QUESTION

Does the overpolarisation artefact grow with the NUMBER of charges, at a fixed total field?

DIAGNOSTIC 15 measured the artefact of one design: S6-10, ten charges, sum |q| 2.1900 e, def2-SVPD.
Its d_shield is 0.4140 kcal/mol, 3.88% of that design's own ddE, and it is NOT BOUNDED by the sum
of its sites - 1.656 times the signed sum of the ten measured alone. D15.4 records what that does
not settle: whether 0.41 is typical, and whether it grows with n.

It matters because the optimiser is intended to run from n = 3 up to about 20. If the artefact
grows with n, the designs the optimiser prefers are the ones where the representation is worst,
and the per-design correction D15 leaves as the way forward gets larger exactly where it is least
affordable to be wrong.

The committed arrangements of relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt span n = 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20 at essentially
one budget (sum q^2 between 2.00000 and 2.04760). They are compared at a MATCHED TOTAL FIELD, so
that n is what differs and not the field.

   PRIMARY, sum |q| = 1.3000 e, def2-SVPD

      design   n    lambda     max |q|

      S6-5     5    0.433333   0.4333
      S6-10    10   0.296804   0.1632

   SECONDARY, sum |q| = 2.1900 e, def2-SVPD

   TERTIARY, the cooperativity at n = 20

      S6-20's twenty sites, each alone, at lambda 0.380208 - the 2.19 e condition, so that the

D16.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS COMPUTED

d_shield, the zero-charge correction, AMENDMENT 1 A1.2's dipole correction and D6.3's
classification are all as committed. No tolerance is new. Where a tolerance is needed it is
DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's two constants, 0.10 and 0.05 kcal/mol, applied to the quantity being compared -
as DIAGNOSTIC 14.3 applied them to Dsum:

   tol = max( 0.10 x (the largest Dset among the readable designs), 0.05 kcal/mol )

A design is READABLE when its six runs are CLEAN or POLARISED by D6.3 with A1.2's correction.

READING 1, the primary condition:

   NO EFFECT TO COMPARE   every readable Dset <= tol. There is no artefact at this field for n to
                          act on, and no verdict is given. AMENDMENT 2's lesson, applied in advance.
   GROWS WITH n           Dset rises with n over the readable designs without exception, and the
                          largest exceeds the smallest by more than tol.
   FALLS WITH n           falls without exception, by more than tol.
   FLAT                   largest minus smallest <= tol.
   NOT MONOTONIC          neither rises nor falls without exception, and the spread exceeds tol.
   NOT READABLE           fewer than three designs readable.

   Dset is reported for every readable design whatever the verdict, with n and its lambda.

READING 2, the secondary condition:

   CONSISTENT       S6-20's Dset at 2.19 e stands in the same relation to S6-10's committed 0.4140
                    as Reading 1 found at 1.30 e.
   INCONSISTENT     it stands in the opposite relation, by more than tol.
   WITHIN TOLERANCE the two differ by <= tol, so the field does not decide it.
   NOT READABLE     either is STRAINED or COLLAPSED.

READING 3, the cooperativity at n = 20 against n = 10:

   excess(n) = Dset - | signed sum of that design's singles |, both at sum |q| 2.19 e.
   DIAGNOSTIC 15's committed numbers give excess(10) = 0.4140 - 0.2500 = 0.1640 kcal/mol.

   COOPERATIVITY GROWS   excess(20) > excess(10) + tol
   FLAT                  | excess(20) - excess(10) | <= tol
   COOPERATIVITY FALLS   excess(20) < excess(10) - tol
   NOT READABLE          any of S6-20's twenty singles, or the design itself, is unreadable

   Dsum, Dset, the signed sum, the excess and the ratio Dset / |signed sum| are all reported for
   n = 20 beside DIAGNOSTIC 15's for n = 10. DIAGNOSTIC 14.3's BOUNDED rule is also applied to
   S6-20 unchanged and reported, so the two designs can be compared on it directly.


D16.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

THE LARGEST LIMITATION, STATED FIRST. The five designs are not one arrangement with charges added.
They are the optimiser's best arrangement at each n, on different sites. So n and the arrangement
vary together, and a difference in Dset across them cannot be attributed to n alone. This
diagnostic measures whether the artefact is larger for the designs this project would actually
choose at larger n. It does not isolate n as a variable, and no nested mixed-sign series exists
that would.

One frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, in vacuo, no continuum (STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027).
Two fields, both below the budget: nothing at lambda = 1. Nothing at def2-SVP, where DIAGNOSTIC 15
already found no artefact to measure for a whole design. Nothing about WHY the artefact is
cooperative, or why it is basis-dependent; A1.4 located that and did not explain it. The Ne ECP
parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7), so the walled arm is a
contrast and not a truth. Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain unmeasurable until the
OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028.txt is decided.


D16.5  DIRECTION

DIAGNOSTIC 15 left the per-design correction as the route forward: the artefact cannot be
predicted from a design's sites, but it can be measured for the design itself in six runs. That
route is affordable only if the correction stays small as n grows. This diagnostic measures
whether it does, over the range of n the optimiser is intended to search, and D16.3 fixes which
answer means which before anything is computed.
