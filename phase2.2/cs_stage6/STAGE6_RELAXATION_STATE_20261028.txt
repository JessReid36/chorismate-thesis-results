R2  WHY THE TRANSITION-STATE RELAXATION STOPPED

Verbatim from S6-C/TS_opt/job.out:

    WARNING: You cannot use transition state optimization for a Cartesian optimization!!!
             Please choose optimization with redundant coordinates (!OPT)
      ===>   Skipping actual calculation

    [file orca_main/main_input_check.cpp, line 8049]: Error (ORCA_MAIN): ... aborting the run

This is an input incompatibility, caught by ORCA before any calculation began. It is NOT a
convergence failure, a walltime overrun or a resource problem. Nothing was computed.

It is the conflict already on record as an open item: HANDOVER_20261026.md section 8, item 10,
"OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict - still no erratum". What was not on record is that this
conflict is what stopped the relaxations.

R4  WHAT IT MEANS FOR Loss, G0 AND kappa

Loss = ddE(frozen) - ddE(relaxed), so every design's Loss is measured against the control's
relaxed reactant and transition state. The reactant exists. The transition state does not, and
cannot be produced by the protocol as written, because OptTS requires redundant internal
coordinates and the protocol specifies Cartesian.

Cartesian coordinates are how the frozen surroundings are held, so moving to redundant internals
is a change to the protocol and not a correction to it. Until that is decided, Loss, G0 and kappa
are not merely unmeasured - they are unmeasurable, and no amount of queue time changes that.

Deciding it requires a dated amendment, as HANDOVER_20261026.md section 8 item 10 already says.
This note does not propose one and does not decide it.
