D17.1  THE QUESTION

Is the overpolarisation artefact linear in the scale factor lambda on a FIXED arrangement, and
does it still depend on n once the largest single charge is held equal?

DIAGNOSTIC 16 read FALLS WITH n at a matched total field of 1.3000 e, over four readable designs.
D16.4 states the limitation that makes that reading hard to use: the five designs are the
optimiser's best arrangement at each n, on different sites, so n and the arrangement vary
together. A third variable moves with them. At a matched TOTAL field a design with more charges
needs a smaller lambda, because its unscaled sum |q| is larger - 2.4125, 3.0000, 4.3800 and
5.7600 e at n = 3, 5, 10 and 20. So Dset falling with n may be Dset falling with lambda.

Two committed measurements already bear on this, each on ONE arrangement at two lambdas:

   S6-10   lambda 0.296804 -> 0.500000  (x1.6846)   Dset 0.2430 -> 0.4140  (x1.7037)
   S6-20   lambda 0.225694 -> 0.380208  (x1.6846)   Dset 0.1702 -> 0.2919  (x1.7150)

Both are consistent with Dset proportional to lambda. Two points cannot distinguish a line from
the start of a curve, and that is what this diagnostic is for.

It matters because every number this project has measured for a whole design was taken at a
fraction of that design's strength. DIAGNOSTIC 15 ran S6-10 at lambda 0.500000 and DIAGNOSTIC 16
ran the five designs at lambda 0.2257 to 0.6500. The optimiser's output is lambda = 1. If Dset is
linear in lambda, S6-10 and S6-20 at full strength carry an artefact near 0.81 and 0.75 kcal/mol,
which is 76% and 70% of DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's budget of 1.067 and rate factors of 3.90 and 3.52. No
measurement exists anywhere near there.

D17.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS COMPUTED

d_shield, the zero-charge correction, AMENDMENT 1 A1.2's dipole correction and D6.3's
classification are all as committed. No tolerance is new: DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's 0.10 and 0.05
kcal/mol, applied to the quantity being compared.

   tol = max( 0.10 x (the largest Dset entering that reading), 0.05 kcal/mol )

A design is READABLE at a lambda when its six runs - the two committed wall-at-zero, and the
bare and walled arms at that lambda - are CLEAN or POLARISED by D6.3 with A1.2's correction.

READING 1, is Dset linear in lambda on a fixed arrangement?

   For S6-10 and S6-20 separately, three points: the two committed and the new one. The line
   through the origin is fitted to the two COMMITTED points alone, so the new point is a
   prediction and not a fit. Those predictions are fixed here before the runs:

      S6-10   c = 0.8234 kcal/mol per unit lambda   predicted Dset(0.979746) = 0.8067
      S6-20   c = 0.7609 kcal/mol per unit lambda   predicted Dset(0.979746) = 0.7455

   LINEAR          | measured - predicted | <= tol, for both designs.
   SUBLINEAR       measured < predicted - tol, for both. The artefact saturates; the
                   extrapolation to full strength overstates it.
   SUPERLINEAR     measured > predicted + tol, for both. It accelerates, and the budget is
                   exhausted sooner than the line says.
   SPLIT           the two designs disagree in direction by more than tol. No verdict on
                   linearity; both are reported.
   NOT READABLE    either design is unreadable at the new lambda.

   The measured Dset, the prediction, the difference, tol, and the two-point exponent
   log(Dset2/Dset1) / log(lambda2/lambda1) over all three points are reported whatever the
   verdict.

READING 2, does the artefact depend on n at equal largest charge?

   Dset for S6-2, S6-3, S6-5, S6-10 and S6-20, all at max |q| = 0.538860 e.

   NO EFFECT TO COMPARE   every readable Dset <= tol.
   GROWS WITH n           rises with n over the readable designs without exception, largest
                          exceeds smallest by more than tol.
   FALLS WITH n           falls without exception, by more than tol.
   FLAT                   largest minus smallest <= tol. The artefact is set by the largest
                          single charge and not by how many charges carry the field.
   NOT MONOTONIC          neither, and the spread exceeds tol.
   NOT READABLE           fewer than three designs readable.

   FLAT is the outcome that would make DIAGNOSTIC 16's FALLS WITH n an artefact of lambda
   rather than of n, and it is named here so that it cannot be claimed afterwards.

READING 3, where does a single charge stop being usable?

   S6-2's bare arm was STRAINED at 0.6500 e in DIAGNOSTIC 16. It is run here at 0.538860 e.

   READABLE AT 0.5389      the usable bare single-site charge lies between 0.5389 and 0.6500 e.
   STILL UNREADABLE        it lies below 0.5389 e, and the class and the failing quantity are
                           reported.

   This bounds one number and nothing more. One site in one design at one geometry.


D17.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

THE LARGEST LIMITATION, STATED FIRST. Reading 2 holds the largest charge equal and lets
everything else about the arrangement vary - the number of charges, where they sit, their signs,
and the total field, which runs from 1.0777 e at n = 2 to 5.6433 e at n = 20. It removes one
confound from DIAGNOSTIC 16 and introduces another in its place. Read together with D16's
matched-total-field series the two bracket the question; neither settles it alone, and no nested
series exists that would.

S6-10 and S6-20 are run here at total fields of 4.29 and 5.64 e, far above anything this project
has measured. If they strain, the reading says so and that is itself the finding, but it is not
evidence that the ARRANGEMENT is unsound - only that the representation cannot be read there.

Reading 1 fits a line through two points and tests a third. Three points cannot distinguish
linear from weakly curved over this range either, and the verdicts above are stated against a
stated tolerance rather than as a claim about the functional form.

One frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, in vacuo, no continuum
(STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). Nothing at def2-SVP, where DIAGNOSTIC 15 found no artefact to
measure for a whole design. Nothing about WHY the artefact is cooperative or basis-dependent.
The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7), so the
walled arm is a contrast and not a truth. Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain
unmeasurable until the OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028
is decided.


D17.5  DIRECTION

Every whole-design artefact this project has measured was taken at a fraction of the design's
strength. If Reading 1 returns LINEAR, the per-design correction DIAGNOSTIC 15 left as the route
forward is three to four times larger at the strength the optimiser actually produces than any
number now on record, and the budget question has to be reopened at that size. If it returns
SUBLINEAR the extrapolation is wrong in the direction that helps and the correction stays
affordable. If Reading 2 returns FLAT, the design rule is a cap on the largest single charge and
not a preference for many charges, and that is a rule the grid can carry, where DIAGNOSTIC 13
concluded a per-site screen could not be.
