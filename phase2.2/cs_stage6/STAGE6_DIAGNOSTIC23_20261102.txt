Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 23 (2 November 2026).
Committed before any of its runs exist. Nothing of this diagnostic has been computed.


D23.1  THE QUESTION

Does the choice of pseudopotential parameter set reach d_shield?

Every walled arm in the Stage 6 record uses one parameter set: Marefat Khah et al.'s Ne ecp-all
(acs.jctc.9b01162, SI Table S2). EXPERIMENT 0 (ECP_CONVENTION_20261102.md, committed 2 November)
established that the operator ORCA was given is the authors' operator: the radial-power column is
NWChem's column, so a 2 means r^0, and the published parameters reproduce their own stated fitting
target to 7.670e-07 Eh under that reading and fail by 1.495e-01 Eh under the other. What EXPERIMENT 0
did not and could not establish is how much a CHANGE of that operator would move the quantity the
thesis reports.

ECP_BRIEF_20261102.md assumed the movement was close to one for one. That is an upper bound, not a
measurement. Writing the quantity as cs6_d17.py and cs6_additivity.py form it,

   d_shield  =  ddE(bare)  -  ddE(wall, raw)  +  ddE(wall0)

the parameters enter BOTH ddE(wall, raw) and ddE(wall0), and those two enter d_shield with opposite
signs. If a parameter change moves them by the same amount, d_shield does not move at all. The
propagation factor therefore lies between 0 and 1 and is undetermined.
DSHIELD_DECOMPOSITION_ERRATUM1_20261102.md E1.3 withdrew a proposed experiment that would have run
the zero-charge arms alone, because such a design measures the change in ddE(wall0) and then assumes
the factor it is meant to measure.

The record already contains both regimes. At def2-SVP the raw discrepancy and the chargeless term
cancel to within 0.0152 kcal/mol in every collective arm, so d_shield is indistinguishable from zero
there, which is what DIAGNOSTIC 14 AMENDMENT 1 and DIAGNOSTIC 15's def2-SVP reading report. At
def2-SVPD they do not cancel, and that non-cancellation is the reported d_shield. Changing the basis
is not changing the parameters, so this is an analogue and not the factor.

This diagnostic runs BOTH arms at two alternative parameter sets and measures the factor directly.


D23.2  WHAT IS MEASURED

THE ALTERNATIVES. The reference library contains exactly one published parameterisation of this
class of object - a centre contributing no electrons and no basis functions, placed near a QM region
to supply Pauli repulsion - and it has three members: He, Ne and Ar ecp-all. ECP_ASSESSMENT_20261102.md
section 3 records the sweep that establishes this and names the eight cited-but-absent papers where
an independent alternative could be. The two alternatives run here are therefore NOT INDEPENDENT of
Ne: one paper, one group, one fitting recipe. That is stated in advance and D23.5 says what it costs.

THE BLOCKS, AS THEY WILL BE WRITTEN. SI Tables S1 and S3 print coefficient / power / exponent;
ORCA's NewECP primitive line is index / exponent / coefficient / radial power.

   He>  <q>  x y z  NewECP              Ar>  <q>  x y z  NewECP
     N_core 2                             N_core 18
     lmax d                               lmax f
     s 1                                  s 1
       1  0.50980    2.42000  2             1  1.64100  275.00000  2
     p 1                                  p 1
       1  0.49165   -0.43590  2             1  0.27330    1.90000  2
     d 1                                  d 1
       1  1.00000    0.00000  2             1  0.44000   -3.40000  2
     end                                  f 1
                                            1  1.00000    0.00000  2

In each block the lmax channel is the local one and carries coefficient zero, exactly as the Ne block
does. EXPERIMENT 0 validated the He block against its own published fitting target to 1.381e-06 Eh
and could not validate the Ar block better than 3.067e-03 Eh on any of eight basis and target
combinations; C23.5 states what follows from that.

THE ELEMENT LABEL. ORCA's own output legend reads "> coreless ECP center with (optional) point
charge", and the committed walled outputs confirm it: a run with three centres at 0.538860,
-0.410881 and -0.350259 prints "Sum of atomic charges: -2.2222800" against a QM charge of -2, which
is -2 plus exactly those three charges and nothing else. The centre therefore introduces no nuclear
charge whatever element label and N_core it is given. Each set is nevertheless written on its own
element label with its own N_core, so that each block is exactly what its authors published and no
reliance is placed on how ORCA treats a mismatched N_core. GATE 3 checks the consequence rather than
the reasoning.

CONSTRUCTION. Each new input is the committed job.inp copied VERBATIM with only the ECP tail
replaced - the element label, N_core, lmax and the channel lines. The head, charge, multiplicity,
geometry, centre positions and centre charges are byte-identical to the committed input. This is
DIAGNOSTIC 22's construction (copy and swap, with a gate that the swap reverses) and not
DIAGNOSTIC 21's (rebuild from the generator), for the reason D22.2 gives.

WALL-AT-ZERO IS SHARED WHERE THE POSITION SET IS SHARED. A wall-at-zero arm depends only on the
positions, so the seven readable conditions need five such arms, not seven: S6-10 at 1.3000 e and
S6-10 at 2.1900 e share a position set, and so do S6-20 at 1.3000 e and S6-20 at 2.1900 e. The five
sets are n = 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20, and their committed arms are

   d16/def2-SVPD/S6-2_wall0      d16/def2-SVPD/S6-3_wall0      d16/def2-SVPD/S6-5_wall0
   d16/def2-SVPD/S6-10_wall0     d16/def2-SVPD/S6-20_wall0

The S6-2 arm lives in the d16 tree although the condition it serves is DIAGNOSTIC 17's, because
DIAGNOSTIC 17 changed the charge scale and not the positions.

WHAT IS RUN, per parameter set:

   wall-at-zero       5 position sets  x 2 states  =  10 single points
   charged walled     7 conditions     x 2 states  =  14 single points

D23.3  THE GATES, WHICH RUN BEFORE ANY NUMBER IS READ

GATE 0  PROVENANCE. Every committed input from which a new input is derived is byte-identical to
        what hpc1 ran, checked against that tree's INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256. A mismatch stops the
        reading and names the file.

GATE 1  THE SWAP REVERSES. Each new input, with its ECP tail swapped back to the committed Ne block,
        is byte-for-byte its committed source. A difference stops the reading and prints the byte
        offset of the first differing byte. This is what licenses the claim that nothing but the
        pseudopotential changed.

GATE 2  THE CONTROL. The re-run charge-free control reproduces the committed
        d16/def2-SVPD/ctl_R and ctl_TS FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY to GATE_EH = 1e-6 Eh, the figure
        cs11_vde.py, cs6_d20.py and cs6_d21.py already use. This is what licenses comparing a new
        run with a committed one.

GATE 3  THE CENTRE IS STILL EMPTY. Every new walled output reports Number of basis functions = 402
        and Number of Electrons NEL = 118, both equal to the control's, and lists its centres as
        basis group "Type Q,> : contracted to pattern {}". For each charged arm, the printed Sum of
        atomic charges equals -2 plus the sum of that arm's centre charges, to 1e-4 e. If any of
        these fails, the centre is no longer the electron-free and basis-free object the record's
        other 659 walled outputs describe, and no number from that run is read.

GATE 4  TERMINATION. Every run contains ORCA TERMINATED NORMALLY. A run that does not is not
        committed; a note about it is.


D23.4  WHAT IS READ, IN THIS ORDER

READING 1  CLASSIFICATION BEFORE ANY ENERGY. Every new run is classified by D6.3 with AMENDMENT 1
           A1.2's dipole correction, whose own words are that where a run carries charge on an
           inline ECP centre the dipole test is applied to the printed total dipole with
           sum_i q_i (r_i - R_com) subtracted, and that a run with no charge is unaffected. So A1.2
           applies to the fourteen charged walled arms per set and not to the ten wall-at-zero arms.
           Thresholds exactly as cs6_audit.py has them and no others: D_LW 0.25 e, R_DIP 2.0,
           GAP_ABS 1.0 eV, R_GAP 0.5, R_CYC 2.0. A condition with any arm that is STRAINED or
           COLLAPSED is NOT READABLE for that parameter set, and no d_shield is reported for it.

READING 2  THE TWO DIFFERENCES, per readable condition and per set X in {He, Ar}:

              dz(X)  =  ddE(wall0, X)  -  ddE(wall0, Ne)
              dd(X)  =  d_shield(X)    -  d_shield(Ne)

           with the Ne terms the committed values, which for the seven conditions are

              condition        ddE(wall0, Ne)   d_shield(Ne)
              S6-2  (D17, M)        -0.0026        -0.3468
              S6-3  (D16, F1)       -0.1815        -0.4379
              S6-5  (D16, F1)       -0.4407        -0.4311
              S6-10 (D16, F1)       -0.7187        -0.2430
              S6-10 (D15, A)        -0.7187        -0.4140
              S6-20 (D16, F1)       -0.6190        -0.1702
              S6-20 (D16, F2)       -0.6190        -0.2919

READING 3  THE PROPAGATION FACTOR, per readable condition:

              f  =  dd / dz        reported with its sign

           Reported, with no criterion attached. NOT reported where |dz| <= 0.001 kcal/mol: that is
           five orders above the numerical floor and is the point below which the ratio of two small
           differences stops carrying information. A large f at small dz is not evidence of
           anything and is not read as such.

READING 4  THE FOUR COMMITTED VERDICTS, RECOMPUTED. Each is recomputed with the X numbers and
           printed beside the committed verdict. No tolerance is new; each rule is quoted from the
           result file that set it.

           D16 READING 1   Dset monotone over n = 3, 5, 10, 20 at the matched field 1.3000 e;
                           tol = max(0.10 x largest Dset, 0.05) = 0.0500.
                           Committed: FALLS WITH n. Thinnest step, n = 3 to n = 5: 0.0068.

           D16 READING 2   Dset(S6-20 at F2) - Dset(S6-10 at A) = -0.1221 against tol 0.0500.
                           Committed: CONSISTENT. Margin 0.0721.

           D16 READING 3   excess(n = 20) - excess(n = 10) = -0.057022 from the raw energies
                           against tol 0.0500, where excess = |Dset| - |signed sum of the
                           single-site d_shield|.
                           Committed: COOPERATIVITY FALLS. Margin 0.0070.

           D15 READING 1   Dset 0.4140 - Dsum 0.3521 = +0.0619 against tol 0.0500.
                           Committed: NOT BOUNDED. Margin 0.0119.

           The three margins 0.0068, 0.0070 and 0.0119 kcal/mol are what makes this diagnostic
           worth running: the term being subtracted to obtain those Dset values is 0.18 to 0.72
           kcal/mol, and a position-dependent change of order 0.01 in any one arm would flip them.

READING 5  REPORTED, NO CRITERION. Whether the He factors are consistent with the only measured
           analogue in the record. Going from def2-SVP to def2-SVPD on arm B, the chargeless term
           moved by -0.5988 (n10) and -0.7785 (n6neg) while d_shield moved by -0.3426 and -0.2777,
           so the charged arm absorbed 43 and 64 per cent of the movement. That is a basis change
           and not a parameter change, so agreement is suggestive and disagreement is not a fault.


D23.5  CRITERIA, FIXED BEFORE THE RUNS EXIST

The only tolerance used is SHIELD_ABS = 0.05 kcal/mol, the absolute floor cs11_standoff.py,
cs12_sites.py and cs13_qscale.py already carry. No new number is introduced.

C23.1   If max |dd| <= 0.05 over every readable condition and both sets:
        PARAMETER CHOICE DOES NOT REACH d_shield at this project's absolute floor.

C23.2   If 0.05 < max |dd| and no verdict of READING 4 changes:
        VALUES PROVISIONAL ON THE PARAMETER SET, VERDICTS STAND. Every reported d_shield is then
        quoted with the parameter set named.

C23.3   If any verdict of READING 4 changes:
        that verdict is A PROPERTY OF THIS PARAMETER SET and is reported as such in the write-up,
        naming which set changes it and by how much.

C23.4   A condition that is NOT READABLE for a set contributes to no maximum and to no recomputed
        verdict for that set, and the verdict line names it. A set for which fewer than four of the
        seven conditions are readable returns INSUFFICIENT READABLE CONDITIONS for that set and no
        C23.1 to C23.3 verdict is formed from it.

C23.5   HE AND AR ARE REPORTED SEPARATELY AND NEVER POOLED. EXPERIMENT 0 validated the He block
        against its own published fitting target to 1.381e-06 Eh. It could not validate the Ar block
        better than 3.067e-03 Eh on any of eight basis and target combinations, and excluded the
        coarser quoted precision as the cause; SI Table S3 is also the only one of the three to
        claim no precision at all. An Ar result is therefore a SENSITIVITY PROBE and not a
        comparison of published operators. C23.1 to C23.3 are evaluated TWICE: once on He alone and
        once on both sets. Where the two disagree, the He-only reading is the one quoted and the
        difference is reported.


D23.6  WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

- HE AND AR ARE NOT INDEPENDENT OF NE. One paper, one group, one fitting recipe, one ansatz. A flat
  response to both bounds the sensitivity FROM BELOW; it does not measure it. It says nothing about
  a parameter set from outside this lineage, and the library contains none (ECP_ASSESSMENT section 3,
  count of independent alternatives of the same class: 0).

- A RESPONSE TO AR ALONE is not demonstrably a response to the published Ar operator (C23.5).

- THIS DOES NOT TEST WHETHER THE WALL IS RIGHT. d_shield has no third arm. It measures how much the
  projector changes what the charge does; it cannot say whether the bare arm or the walled arm is
  nearer the truth, because the truth for an object with no physical referent is not defined without
  choosing one. The comparison the remedy literature uses - deviation from a reference containing the
  environment's electrons explicitly - exists in this record for one site, one basis pair and one
  integer charge (DIAGNOSTIC 3), without a walled arm. Building that third arm is a separate
  diagnostic and exists only at integer charge, because no atom carries 0.12 to 0.65 e.

- FROZEN GEOMETRY, ONE FRAME, ONE BASIS, IN VACUO. No barrier and no activation free energy follows
  from this diagnostic.

- THE FACTOR f IS A RATIO OF TWO DIFFERENCES, each of which may be small. READING 3 withholds it
  below |dz| = 0.001 kcal/mol for that reason.

- THIS DOES NOT REVISIT DIAGNOSTIC 19's multireference reading, which returned DISAGREE and still
  does, nor DIAGNOSTIC 20's instability onset at 4.32 e.

- NOTHING HERE LICENSES A CLAIM ABOUT THE PARAMETERS BEING CORRECT FOR A FRACTIONAL POINT CHARGE.
  STAGE6_ERRATUM7_20261026.txt E7.8 records that the published set is used outside the domain its
  authors validated; EXPERIMENT 0 narrowed that to a question of applicability rather than
  transcription, and this diagnostic does not touch it.
