D30.1  WHY BOTH OF THESE, AND WHY NOW

DIAGNOSTIC 28 established CAM-B3LYP as the accurate functional against a measured anchor: the
bare dianion's highest occupied orbital sits at -0.5918 eV under CAM-B3LYP against a detachment
energy of -0.5123 eV, where B3LYP puts it at +1.2307 eV, above zero. It also showed that the gap
collapse at Sum|q| = 2.8800 e was a B3LYP artefact: the same condition reads 14 per cent of its
control's gap under B3LYP and 45 per cent under CAM-B3LYP.

TWO THINGS THE RECORD THEREFORE DOES NOT ESTABLISH.

A. DIAGNOSTIC 27's basis verdict was measured entirely under B3LYP. It found def2-SVPD converged
   at 1.3000 e, NOT CONVERGING at 2.8800 e with gap_DT = 6.946128 kcal/mol, and NOT CONVERGED at
   5.6433 e. Since the wavefunction artefact at 2.8800 e was B3LYP's, the basis divergence at that
   vector may be B3LYP's too. DIAGNOSTIC 28 cannot settle it: it ran six charged arms per
   functional and all six are def2-SVPD. At def2-TZVPD it ran controls only, so no charged arm
   exists at def2-TZVPD under any range-separated functional.

B. No stability analysis exists under any range-separated functional. Every run in this record
   carrying %scf STABPerform is B3LYP: twelve of them, in d20. This is not a formality. DIAGNOSTIC
   19 Reading 2 found the B3LYP lambda = 1.00 solution UNSTABLE with two negative eigenvalues in
   each state, and ORCA reconverged 0.8564 kcal/mol lower at R and 3.7976 kcal/mol lower at TS.
   Those two figures are recorded in STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC19_NOTE1_20261031.txt
     sha256 ffc6bdb67e3a26e9fb174e49f903b2faab8dbe2ab6cc0afdd06fa08f2f4a88b6
   as energy changes of -0.8564 and -3.7976 kcal/mol; the sign is the lowering. An instability of
   that size moves a barrier by more than TOL.

Both are cheap, and both bear on whether charge-placement optimisation can proceed, because the
optimiser's scores are only as good as the method used to judge the designs it proposes.

Twenty-two new single points. The keyword line is identical to DIAGNOSTIC 27's and DIAGNOSTIC 28's
in every respect but the functional, the basis and Arm B's stability block:

    %maxcore 3000
    %pal nprocs 8 end
    %scf MaxIter 300 end                      (Arm B adds STABPerform true inside this block)
    %pointcharges "pc.pc"                     (omitted for a control)

ARM A, THE BASIS SEQUENCE UNDER CAM-B3LYP.  14 jobs.
    def2-SVP     1.3000e_n10, 2.8800e_n10, 5.6433e_n20, each R and TS      6
                 ctl_R, ctl_TS                                             2
    def2-TZVPD   the same three vectors, each R and TS                     6
    def2-SVPD    NOT RE-RUN. The six charged arms and the two controls are already committed in

    def2-TZVPD controls are likewise already committed in d28/CAM-B3LYP/def2-TZVPD and re-read.

ARM B, THE STABILITY OF THE CAM-B3LYP SOLUTION.  8 jobs.
    def2-SVPD with STABPerform true
                 the same three vectors, each R and TS                     6
                 ctl_R, ctl_TS                                             2
    def2-SVPD is the production basis and the basis d28's arms used, so Arm B's energies are
    directly comparable to committed ones without a basis change in between.

THE CHARGES AND THE GEOMETRIES ARE NOT RETYPED. Every pc.pc is copied from the committed
d27/def2-SVPD arm of the same name and every substrate.xyz from the committed control of the same
state, and GATE 2 requires each to be byte-identical to its source. The three vectors are
therefore the same three vectors DIAGNOSTIC 27 and DIAGNOSTIC 28 measured, on the same geometries.

NO GEOMETRY IS RE-OPTIMISED. The relaxation, the optimiser and the grid are untouched. No
continuum appears anywhere, as Amendment 2 A2.1 requires.

COST, STATED BEFORE THE RUN SO THAT AN OVERRUN IS VISIBLE AS ONE. Measured on this system's own
committed outputs: CAM-B3LYP costs 1.40x B3LYP at def2-TZVPD on the controls (9 min 40 s and
9 min 13 s against 6 min 45 s) and 1.52x at def2-SVPD on the hardest arm (12 min 03 s against
7 min 56 s). B3LYP's worst def2-TZVPD arm took 29 min 30 s, so CAM-B3LYP's is budgeted at 45 min.
Arm B's stability pass is budgeted at 3x its own measured plain run. Expected total 6.2 h.
Walltime 24:00:00 is 3.9x that. A point that fails to converge in 300 cycles is stopped by ORCA,
not by walltime, and is caught by GATE 5. run.pbs is restartable: a job that ends on walltime is
resubmitted and skips what finished.


D30.3  ARM A: WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE IT RUNS

This is DIAGNOSTIC 27's rule, unchanged, with its own tolerance, applied to CAM-B3LYP.

    barrier(basis, arm)  = E(TS) - E(R), from the last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY of each
    ddE(basis, v)        = barrier(basis, v) - barrier(basis, control of that basis)
    gap_SD(v)            = | ddE(def2-SVPD, v) - ddE(def2-SVP,  v) |
    gap_DT(v)            = | ddE(def2-TZVPD, v) - ddE(def2-SVPD, v) |

  CONVERGED AT def2-SVPD    if gap_DT(v) <= TOL for all three vectors AND gap_DT(v) < gap_SD(v)
                            for all three. def2-SVPD is then the production basis under CAM-B3LYP.

  NOT CONVERGED             if gap_DT(v) > TOL for any vector. def2-SVPD is then not sufficient at
                            that vector under CAM-B3LYP, and every CAM-B3LYP quantity at or above
                            that Sum|q| is provisional until re-run. That consequence is accepted
                            in advance.

  On the third vector, where the two smaller bases disagree under B3LYP by 13.5154 kcal/mol, the
  arbitration is DIAGNOSTIC 27's, stated now:
      def2-SVP IS THE ARTEFACT    if | ddE(def2-TZVPD) - ddE(def2-SVPD) | <= TOL
      def2-SVPD IS THE ARTEFACT   if | ddE(def2-TZVPD) - ddE(def2-SVP)  | <= TOL
      BOTH ARE ARTEFACTS          if neither holds.

  If the three vectors do not give the same verdict, the result is reported PER VECTOR and no
  single verdict is formed, as DIAGNOSTIC 26 and DIAGNOSTIC 27 both did.

  REPORTED BESIDE IT, NOT GATED: DIAGNOSTIC 27's B3LYP gap_SD and gap_DT for the same three
  vectors, re-read from d27 rather than quoted, so that the two functionals' sequences sit side by
  side. Whether CAM-B3LYP's sequence converges where B3LYP's did not is the question; the
  comparison is the answer's context and does not enter the verdict.


D30.4  ARM B: WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE IT RUNS

This is DIAGNOSTIC 19 Reading 2's rule, applied to CAM-B3LYP at def2-SVPD.

  For each of the eight conditions, from its own output:
      STABLE      the stability analysis reports no instability of the restricted solution
      UNSTABLE    it reports one, with the number of negative eigenvalues recorded

  For any condition reported UNSTABLE, the energy ORCA reconverges to is recorded, and the
  lowering is reported in kcal/mol. Where both states of a vector are available, the change in
  that vector's barrier is reported too:
      MATERIAL        | barrier change | >  TOL
      IMMATERIAL      | barrier change | <= TOL

  ARM B's VERDICT is the set of conditions reported UNSTABLE, with MATERIAL or IMMATERIAL against
  each vector for which a barrier change can be formed.

      ALL STABLE              no condition reports an instability. The committed CAM-B3LYP
                              energies stand as they are.
      UNSTABLE, IMMATERIAL    instabilities are reported but no vector's barrier moves by more
                              than TOL. The instability is recorded and the energies stand.
      UNSTABLE, MATERIAL      at least one vector's barrier moves by more than TOL. Every
                              CAM-B3LYP barrier at or above that vector is then provisional until
                              the reconverged solution is used, and that consequence is accepted
                              in advance.

  THE CONTROL CARRIES THE TEST. If the charge-free control itself reports an instability, the
  finding is about the method and the bare dianion rather than about the designed field, and the
  result says so rather than attributing it to the charges. This is why the control is run.

  D19's READING 3 IS NOT FORMED HERE. Reading 3 combines the stability analysis with the FOD
  multireference indicator, and D19 measured FOD on the d20 lambda ladder, not on these three
  vectors. Combining the two across different conditions would be the comparison D19 forbade when
  it named DISAGREE in advance. Arm B forms Reading 2's verdict only.

D30.6  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT +-1 e DESIGNS. All three vectors are partial charges: the largest single
   charge among them is 0.5389 e. The question of whether a unit charge is usable is untouched
   here, and the record's unit-charge conditions sit at ma-def2-SVP, a basis this diagnostic does
   not run.
2. IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT RELOCATION. DIAGNOSTIC 29 found that no scalar function of the charges
   orders density relocation. Nothing here revisits that, and a basis or stability verdict does
   not bound relocation.
3. ONE FRAME, ONE GRID, IN VACUO, FIXED GEOMETRIES. No barrier reported here is a prediction of a
   physical barrier.
4. ARM B TESTS ONE INSTABILITY. STABPerform tests the restricted solution for an instability
   toward an unrestricted one. It is not a multireference diagnostic, and a stable result does not
   establish single-reference character, as D19.4 already recorded of the same instrument.
5. THE TWO ARMS DO NOT VALIDATE EACH OTHER. Arm A reads total energies and Arm B reads a stability
   block. A clean Arm A does not license Arm B's energies, and an unstable Arm B does not
   invalidate Arm A's basis comparison, which is a difference of differences at fixed method.
6. IT DOES NOT MAKE CAM-B3LYP THE PRODUCTION FUNCTIONAL BY ITSELF. D28 chose it on one measured
   anchor. A second anchor, and a barrier computed under it, are separate work.
