A7.2  THE WORKING LEVEL FOR THE SEARCH

A SEARCH DIAGNOSTIC is one that places designs or screens them: it asks which arrangements pass
the legs of the criterion and what their ddE is. Its working level is

   CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD, with the stability analysis,

by the keyword lines of DIAGNOSTIC 31's committed inputs. This is the level the design record
already stands at, so a new design can be set beside the old ones without a change of level
between them. That, and not a claim that the level is right, is the reason it is named.

A NUMBER AT THE WORKING LEVEL IS A NUMBER AT THE WORKING LEVEL. A design that passes the five
legs there (AMENDMENT 6) is a SURVIVOR OF THE SEARCH and nothing more. No ddE of a survivor is
quoted as more than a working-level number until it has been through A7.3.


A7.3  THE CROSS-CHECK LEVELS

A survivor is measured, before any number of it is quoted as more than a working-level number,
at these five further levels:

   CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ / def2-TZVPD        method line of d37/CAM_TZ_R/job.inp
   PWPB95-D3BJ / def2-SVPD            method line of d37/PWPB95_SV_R/job.inp
   PWPB95-D3BJ / def2-TZVPD           method line of d37/PWPB95_TZ_R/job.inp
   revDSD-PBEP86-D4 / def2-SVPD       method line and %mp2 block of d37a1/revDSD_RI_SV_R/job.inp
   revDSD-PBEP86-D4 / def2-TZVPD      method line and %mp2 block of d37a1/revDSD_RI_TZ_R/job.inp

HOW THEY WERE CHOSEN. They are the functionals of the table in A7.1 whose def2-TZVPD barrier
lies within 3 kcal/mol of D36's benchmark and whose highest occupied orbital is BOUND in D37's
test, each in the basis of the search and in the larger basis. PW6B95-D3BJ lies within 3
kcal/mol and is left out because its orbital is NOT BOUND. THE LINE AT 3 kcal/mol IS DRAWN
HERE WITH ALL NINE NUMBERS KNOWN. It is used to name these levels and for nothing else. What
makes the list worth fixing is not how it was drawn but when: before any survivor exists.

WHAT IS MEASURED AT EACH. The dianion with the survivor's charges and the charge-free dianion,
at both states, which give a ddE; and the monoanion with the same charges at both states,
which gives the fifth leg at that level.

WHAT IS FIXED NOW AND WHAT IS LEFT. The five levels and their method lines are fixed. All five
are run for every survivor and all five are reported, whichever way they come out. None is
dropped. A sixth is added only by a dated amendment written before any output at it exists.
What is added to a method line for the point charges, the monoanion and the stability
analysis, which of the four legs can be read at a double hybrid, and the tolerance within
which a survivor's ddE must agree across the levels, are fixed by the pre-registration of the
cross-check, before any of its outputs exists. That pre-registration may not change a method
line.


A7.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT DECIDE

1. THE PRODUCTION LEVEL OF THEORY. That decision stays where D36.7 and D41.7 leave it.
2. WHICH BENCHMARK IS NEARER THE TRUTH. D41.5: "two basis sets do not make an extrapolation,
   and none is attempted."
3. WHETHER A FUNCTIONAL'S ERROR ON THE BARRIER CANCELS IN ddE. Nothing in the record measures
   it. Agreement of three functionals is not that measurement.
4. ANYTHING ABOUT DIAGNOSTIC 39. Its result is committed and is not used here.
5. WHAT A SURVIVOR THAT PASSES THE CROSS-CHECK MAY BE CALLED. That is a dated document of its
   own and a decision for Jessica.


A7.5  COST

None now. For each survivor, later: four single points at each of the five levels. The
charge-free dianion points of each level are committed in d37 and d37a1; whether they can
serve as the controls depends on what the cross-check's pre-registration adds to the method
lines.
