D29.1  WHY THIS IS RUN, AND WHAT IT REPLACES

DIAGNOSTIC 28's committed result (sha256
aa9e4d8ca65c177c7ec0717362f1dc8ece4c4bd98e2e7e21b48eb281eec4ee6f)
established that B3LYP places the bare dianion's highest occupied orbital well above where its
measured detachment energy puts it, and that correcting the long-range exchange moves it. The
consequence for this stage is that the two indicators the readability classifier combines behave
very differently when the functional changes. At one fixed condition, sum|q| = 2.8800 e:

   the HOMO-LUMO gap moved from 0.4934 eV under B3LYP to 2.8223 and 4.4177 eV under the two
   range-separated functionals, flipping that condition's verdict from STRAINED to CLEAN

   the Loewdin relocation at the strongest field moved from +0.875 e to +0.750 and +0.750 e,
   changing no verdict at all

The gap is fragile under a change of functional and the relocation is robust. They also disagreed
about which conditions were bad: the condition flagged on gap turned out to be fine, and the one
flagged on relocation did not.

WHAT IS WITHDRAWN, AND BY THIS DOCUMENT RATHER THAN QUIETLY. A figure of Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 was
stated on 5 November, together with a claim that the optimiser's committed budget of B = 2.00 e^2
is about 3.4 times too high, in an uncommitted note and in conversation. Both rested on a
separation drawn across committed conditions whose verdicts used B3LYP gaps. One of those verdicts
has now flipped. NEITHER FIGURE IS QUOTABLE AND THIS DIAGNOSTIC DOES NOT ASSUME EITHER. Whether
Sigma q^2 is even the ordering variable is also not assumed; it is one of three tested below.


D29.2  WHAT IS READ

Every committed condition in a diffuse basis (402 or 682 basis functions) that carries both a
Loewdin and a dipole block and has a charge-free control in the same basis, same state and same
functional. The judgement is the committed classifier's RELOCATION criterion and nothing else:

   MOVED  if  (maxlw - maxlw of its control) >= D_LW   OR   dip / dip of its control >= R_DIP

with D_LW and R_DIP taken from phase2.2/cs6_audit.py
  sha256 1dda394a134b3c7265df1898c9b5dc507d01c41d73aa5e59274495a1e7005dea
by loading that file and reading its constants, not by copying their values into this document.

WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT READ: the HOMO-LUMO gap and the SCF cycle count. Both enter the
classifier's full verdict and both are what DIAGNOSTIC 28 showed to be functional-dependent. A
reader that consults them is not doing what this diagnostic says it does, and GATE 4 checks that.

THE PRIMARY SET is the B3LYP conditions, which are the bulk of the record. The 20 DIAGNOSTIC 28
conditions under CAM-B3LYP and wB97X-D3 are held back as a CROSS-FUNCTIONAL CHECK: once a boundary
is drawn on the B3LYP conditions, does any d28 condition fall on the wrong side of it? That check
can fail even when the primary separation succeeds.


D29.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS READ

For each candidate scalar s in { Sigma q^2 , Sigma |q| , max |q| }, over the primary set, let

   C(s) = the largest value of s among conditions that did NOT move
   B(s) = the smallest value of s among conditions that DID move

  s SEPARATES          if B(s) > C(s). Report C(s), B(s) and the margin B(s) - C(s).
  s OVERLAPS           if B(s) <= C(s). Report both values and the extent of the overlap.

  VERDICT: the set of scalars that separate.
     If NONE separates, this diagnostic reports that no scalar in the set orders relocation, sets
     no limit, and names that as its result. That is an admissible outcome and is not a failure.
     If MORE THAN ONE separates, the one with the largest RELATIVE margin (B - C) / C is reported
     as the preferred ordering variable and the others are reported beside it.

  MONOTONICITY, checked separately and able to fail on its own: within each group sharing a basis,
  a state and a functional, is the Loewdin rise non-decreasing in the preferred scalar? If it is
  not, the separation is reported WITHOUT a monotone relationship behind it.

  CROSS-FUNCTIONAL CHECK, also separate: with the boundary drawn on the B3LYP conditions, every
  d28 condition is classified and reported as CONSISTENT or INCONSISTENT with it. A single
  inconsistent condition is reported as such and is not smoothed over.


D29.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. IT DOES NOT SET THE OPTIMISER'S BUDGET. It reports where relocation begins under one criterion.
   Changing B in the optimiser is a decision that needs an amendment, and it would also need an
   argument that relocation is the right thing to bound, which this diagnostic does not supply.
2. RELOCATION IS ONE INDICATOR. A design can be unusable for reasons it does not see, and a design
   that does not relocate density is not thereby catalytically useful.
3. LOEWDIN CHARGES IN A DIFFUSE BASIS ARE NOT A CLEAN OBSERVABLE. That is why the criterion is a
   disjunction with the dipole ratio, and why both are reported per condition rather than only the
   verdict.
4. THE CONDITIONS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT. Several are field-scalings of one arrangement, so a clean
   separation across them is weaker evidence than the same separation across independent designs.
   The count of distinct arrangements is reported alongside the count of conditions.
5. ONE FRAME, ONE GRID, IN VACUO. No barrier is quoted and no design is accepted or rejected.
6. IT SAYS NOTHING ABOUT THE GAP. DIAGNOSTIC 28 left the gap's own field-dependence unresolved
   beyond the three vectors it ran, and this diagnostic does not touch it.
