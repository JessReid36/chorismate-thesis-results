E10.1  WHAT WAS WRONG

The job array finished at 07:06 on 27 October. Gate 1 passed to 5e-10 Eh. Gate 2 reported:

   design   ddE vac    D1 ddE(ma)   sum L      expected   diff
   S6-P      -3.8579     -3.8579  +0.56294    -4.4208   +0.5629  FAIL
   S6-2      -1.0250     -0.4094  +0.61564    -1.0250   -0.0000  OK
   S6-20      4.7108      4.5746  -0.13622     4.7108   +0.0000  OK

and stopped the reading, as D10.2 requires. That stop was correct behaviour on a wrong number.

sum L for S6-P is not +0.56294. It is ZERO. S6-P deploys no Lennard-Jones wall, so there is no
wall energy for a bare-point-charge run to differ from. The first cs10_cpcm.py summed the L
column of ARRANGEMENTS.txt for every design without asking whether the design deploys a wall:

    L = sum(r[6] for r in A[arr])

L is a property of a SITE, not of a design. Site 1690 carries L = +0.56294 and appears in S6-2
and S6-3, which do wall it, and in S6-P, which does not. The L listed under S6-P records what
that site would cost if walled. The gate subtracted a term that was never in the calculation.

    The pre-registration contradicts itself. D10.1 calls S6-P bare and then, fifteen lines

E10.3  WHY THIS IS A CORRECTION AND NOT A CONVENIENCE

A gate that fails and is then adjusted until it passes is the exact shape of a result being
shaped, and it deserves the suspicion. Four things distinguish this from that:

a.  The correction is read off committed files, not chosen. The new code takes sum L from the
    force field that actually ran, and refuses - in BOTH directions - if ARRANGEMENTS.txt and
    site.ORCAFF.prms disagree about whether a sphere is walled. It cannot be satisfied by
    preferring whichever record gives a better answer.

b.  The error left a signature. The reported discrepancy for S6-P was +0.5629 kcal/mol, equal
    to four decimal places to the quantity wrongly subtracted, while the two walled designs
    agreed to within 1e-4 kcal/mol. A genuine failure of Finding 4 would not land on that
    number for one design and vanish for the other two.

c.  It was reproduced before it was fixed. The superseded script, run against synthetic outputs
    carrying D1's control energies and the three measured ddE values, reproduces the 27 October
    gate-2 table line for line, including the +0.5629.

d.  The change tightens the instrument. A new GATE 0 was added, checking all forty-four
    committed input files byte for byte against cpcm/MANIFEST.sha256 before anything is read.
    Gate 0 can only withhold a verdict; it can never produce one.

With sum L corrected, all three designs satisfy Finding 4 to <= 1e-4 kcal/mol, forty times
inside gate 2's 0.05 tolerance. The identity on which this whole diagnostic rests is therefore
not assumed but measured, on three designs, which is what gate 2 existed to do.

    are what ran, and none of ORCA's scratch.


E10.5  THE STANDING LESSON

A per-site quantity in a table is not a per-design quantity, and a design that lists a site does
not necessarily deploy everything that site carries. Where a derived number can be read either
from a summary table or from the file that was actually fed to the program, the program's own
input is the authority, and the two should be cross-checked rather than one of them trusted.
