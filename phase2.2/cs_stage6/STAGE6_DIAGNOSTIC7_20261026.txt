D7.1  WHY, AND THE ONE SENTENCE IN THE RECORD THAT ASKS FOR IT

STAGE6_ERRATUM3_20261026.txt E3.3 states the defensible result and then states its own limit:

   "THIS WAS MEASURED ON ONE DESIGN, S6-20, at one frame, at fixed geometry. Whether the domain transfers to
    another arrangement is untested, and Stage 7 must either test it or say on its face that it has not been
    tested."

This is that test. The domain in question is lambda <= 0.50 of S6-20, which is sum |q| <= 2.880 e, and E3.3
rests it on TWO unrelated criteria that coincide:

   (i)  inter-basis agreement - DIAGNOSTIC5_RESULT.txt D5.3(d), the two bases agree on ddE to within
        1.0 kcal/mol up to lambda = 0.50, |Delta| = 0.9757 there;
   (ii) wavefunction health - DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt, every field run at lambda = 0.25 and 0.50 is CLEAN at
        BOTH bases.

A domain resting on two criteria must be tested on both, so D7.3 tests both.

WHAT HAPPENS ABOVE THE DOMAIN IS NOT THE SAME AT THE TWO BASES, AND THIS FILE STATES IT AS THE RESULT FILE DOES.
At def2-SVPD all four runs at lambda = 0.75 and 1.00 are COLLAPSED. At def2-SVP only THREE of the four are
flagged: DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt's "EVERY COLLAPSED OR STRAINED SINGLE POINT" table lists exactly three field
runs at nb 264, at 33, 42 and 47 SCF cycles, and the lambda = 1.00 pair accounts for the 42 and 47 (E3.3 records
that those are the same wavefunctions as the frozen S6-20 runs, which DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt lists at 42 and
47). So ONE def2-SVP run at lambda = 0.75 is not flagged at all.

   A CORRECTION TO THE RECORD, FLAGGED HERE AND DELIBERATELY NOT MADE HERE. E3.3 reads "the def2-SVP runs at
   lambda = 0.75 and 1.00 as STRAINED", which implies all four; its own source file flags three. The def2-SVPD
   clause of that same sentence is exact ("all four, both states"); the def2-SVP clause overstates.
   HANDOVER_20261026.md section 5 carries the same reading. THIS CHANGES NOTHING ABOUT THE DOMAIN, whose
   criterion is that everything at lambda <= 0.50 is CLEAN at both bases, and that holds - D6 flags nothing
   below lambda = 0.75. What it changes is the sharpness of the contrast ABOVE the domain: sharp at def2-SVPD,
   not sharp at def2-SVP. Pre-registrations and errata are never edited, so E3.3 stands exactly as committed
   and this belongs in a dated erratum of its own, which this file does not write and must not be read as
   writing. It is recorded here because D7.3 reading 1(ii) applies criterion (ii) to a second design, and it
   must apply the criterion the result file supports rather than the prose that overstated it.

WHAT D4 LEFT OPEN, IN ITS OWN WORDS. DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt "WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST": "One design, so this is
about S6-20's particular arrangement of twenty charges and not about total field in general." D4 removed the
design/total-field confound in one direction, by holding the design fixed and varying lambda. The remaining
direction is to hold the total field fixed and vary the design, which needs a second design at matched sum |q|.

A SECOND BOUND IS IN PLAY AND THE SAME RUNS TEST IT. DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt reading 1 gives lambda_quad = 0.25,
a total-field bound of sum |q| <= 1.440 e: the largest total field at which the quadratic model a q + b q^2 is
within 10 per cent of the measured response at def2-SVPD. E3.3 re-read what that bound means - "lambda_quad
marks where the REPRESENTATION fails, not where the chemistry saturates. It is a bound on the model." So the
record holds two bounds of different kinds, both stated in sum |q|, and Stage 7 has to carry both:

   sum |q| <= 1.440 e   the quadratic model a q + b q^2 describes the measured response to within 10 per cent
                        (D4 reading 1, at def2-SVPD);
   sum |q| <= 2.880 e   the two bases agree on ddE to within 1.0 kcal/mol AND every run is D6-CLEAN at both
                        (E3.3, criteria (i) and (ii) above).

Whether either is a property of the model or of S6-20's particular twenty charges is unknown, and these runs
answer it for both. D7.3 reading 2 asks it for the first.

WHAT THIS IS NOT, STATED BECAUSE IT WOULD BE EASY TO ASSERT AND IS FALSE. It is NOT a claim about the
optimiser's objective. Decision 1 of OPTIMISER_DECISIONS_20261020.md settled that the MILP scores on the LINEAR
term only - polarisation is "Not in the MILP score. The pool is re-scored with full def2-SVP single points,
which contain b and the cross terms exactly" - so the quadratic model is a diagnostic prediction in this series
and NOT the pipeline's scoring function. The pipeline's own disclosed error is a separate thing and is already
handled: Decision 1 records frozen magnitudes as "10-31 % too small before re-scoring", never quoted as
results, with "no direction as measured" on ranking across eight widely separated arrangements and near-ties
re-ordering, which is why the pool is re-scored at all. Nothing here revisits that.

THE EXPOSURE THAT IS REAL, AND WHICH READING 4 BEARS ON. Decision 1's re-scoring instrument is "full def2-SVP
single points". DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt classifies def2-SVP single points as COLLAPSED at 14 to 76 charges, and
E3.4 classifies the committed S6-10 and S6-20 as STRAINED at def2-SVP, gaps 0.610 to 0.980 eV at 2.4 to 3.1
times the control's cycles. So the instrument Stage 7 would re-score with is itself inside the region
DIAGNOSTIC 6 flags, for designs of the size Stage 7 will search. This diagnostic does not fix that. It is the
reason reading 4 classifies every run here, and the reason the D6 gate Jessica fixed on 26 October has to apply
at the re-scoring step and not only to what is finally quoted.

NEITHER BOUND IS SHARP AND THIS FILE DOES NOT TREAT THEM AS SHARP. E3.3: lambda_quad "came out at 0.25 rather
than 0.50 because def2-SVPD's S at lambda = 0.50 is 10.3 per cent against a pre-registered threshold of 10.0 ...
The near miss is three tenths of a point and it moves the implied bound by a factor of two, so the bound must
never be quoted as sharply determined." Every reading below is reported as lying between two grid points.

D7.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

Two designs, so a result either way is about S6-10 and S6-20 and not about arrangements in general; a third
arrangement at the same total field could behave differently again. One frame, one geometry pair, fixed
geometry throughout, so nothing here is about Loss or about a relaxed stationary point. Whether b varies
between sites, which is the first explanation to reach for if a residual is large and which needs the
charge-magnitude scan repeated at two or three further distances (D4.4). NO CAP AS A FUNCTION OF DISTANCE: the
per-site magnitude is bounded here but never varied against distance, so Stage 7's requirement that a cap be
stated as a magnitude AND a distance is NOT met by this file and is not claimed to be. Negative total field -
both designs scaled here carry a net charge of one sign each and the question of a negative total field is
untouched. Nothing about the designs at 1.0000 e per site, which are four of the six committed designs.
Nothing about ma-def2-SVP. And no charge model with a Pauli wall for electrons is tested or offered: E3.2,
Finding 4, DIAGNOSTIC5_RESULT.txt D5.3(c) and D6.5 leave that open and this diagnostic does not touch it.


DIRECTION

NOT NONE. It is declared per outcome below, because one of the four is towards a lower barrier and saying
"none" would be the exact failure the project's rule against shaping anything towards a lower barrier exists to
prevent. The runs supply the one thing E3.3 says Stage 7 needs and does not have, test whether D4's
quadratic-model bound is a property of the model or of one design, and settle D4.4's remaining confound. Every
reading is fixed above before the runs go in.

   Reading 1 TRANSFERS          NEUTRAL. The defensible region stays exactly where it is, at sum |q| <= 2.880 e
                                and ddE of about -10.7 kcal/mol. Nothing is gained and nothing is permitted.
   Reading 1 PARTIAL, or
   DOES NOT TRANSFER            AGAINST a lower barrier. The defensible region shrinks towards one design and
                                Stage 7 is more constrained, not less.
   Reading 2 AGREES, TIGHTER
   or NOT MONOTONE              NEUTRAL or against. A tighter or unstatable bound constrains Stage 7 further.
   Reading 2 LOOSER             TOWARDS A LOWER BARRIER. THIS IS THE ONE TO WATCH, AND IT IS NAMED NOW RATHER
                                THAN FOUND LATER. DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt reading 1 does not merely describe the
                                1.440 e bound - it says "Stage 7 imposes that". So a LOOSER result is evidence
                                for admitting larger total fields into Stage 7's search than the record now
                                admits, and a larger total field is towards a lower frozen barrier.
   Reading 3, either way        NEUTRAL. It establishes whether a bound stated in sum |q| alone can transfer
                                between designs at all. NOT MATCHED is the more constraining answer.
   Reading 4                    AGAINST or neutral by construction. It can only add verdicts that restrict what
                                may be quoted; it cannot license anything.

WHAT NO OUTCOME HERE RELAXES. This diagnostic produces evidence; it changes no pre-registration. The 1.440 e
constraint D4 reading 1 placed on Stage 7 stays in force whatever reading 2 returns, and only Stage 7 itself - a
pre-registration written before its own runs, and disclosing on its face that it was written after this result -
can alter it. Reading 1's two criteria likewise stay in force. No outcome of this diagnostic makes any design's
barrier lower than it is now measured to be, and none changes what may be quoted from the existing record.
