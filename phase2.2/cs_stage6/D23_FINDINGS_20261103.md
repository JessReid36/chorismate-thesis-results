**3 November 2026.** The reading itself is `d23/DIAGNOSTIC23_RESULT.txt`, written by
`cs6_d23.py analyse` against a pre-registration committed before any run existed. This note
records what follows from it. Every figure here was re-derived from the committed outputs
independently of that reader.

```
VERDICT: A PROPERTY OF THE PARAMETER SET
         D15 READING 1  NOT BOUNDED          -> BOUNDED

         D16 READING 3  COOPERATIVITY FALLS  -> FLAT
```

## 1. The question is answered, and the answer is yes

Against the He set every one of the seven readable conditions moves by more than the committed
0.05 kcal/mol floor, by 0.0646 to 0.1727 kcal/mol, which is **37 to 42 per cent** of each
condition's own reported `d_shield`. Against Ar the movement is larger and of the opposite sign,
0.0747 to 0.2805. All seven conditions were readable for both sets, so no condition was dropped
and the comparison is on equal footing.

## 2. Three committed verdicts are properties of the Ne parameter set

The three readings that `STAGE6_RECORD_CORRECTIONS_20261102.md` C1 identified as resting on
margins of 0.0068, 0.0070 and 0.0119 kcal/mol all reverse. The fourth, at 0.0721, survives at
0.0126. Recorded in full in `STAGE6_D15_D16_NOTE1_20261103.txt`; nothing in DIAGNOSTIC 15's or
DIAGNOSTIC 16's result files is edited, because nothing in them is wrong for the conditions it was
computed under. What changes is the scope of three readings.

The consequence that reaches furthest: under He both of D16 READING 3's excesses go **negative**,
so the collective Dset is smaller than the sum of its single-site parts. Under Ne it exceeded
them, and that excess was this project's evidence of cooperativity between charges. **No claim of
cooperativity may rest on it.**

## 3. The propagation factor is not between 0 and 1

The pre-registration's own D23.1, this project's `DSHIELD_DECOMPOSITION_ERRATUM1` E1.3, and the
Results draft's Section 6 all assert that the factor lies between 0 and 1. Measured:

```
He   -9.314, +0.235, +0.294, +0.413, +0.517, +0.704, +2.279
Ar   -1.542, -0.453, +0.088, +0.139, +0.155, +0.245, +0.613
```

`f = 1 − d(wall,raw)/dz` is bounded by 0 and 1 only if the charged arm responds in the same
direction as, and no more strongly than, the zero-charge arm. At S6-2 under He the zero-charge arm
moves −0.0145 and the charged arm −0.1500, ten times as far; at S6-3 they move in opposite
directions. The ratio of their responses runs from −1.28 to +10.31.

**This is physical, not bookkeeping.** The pseudopotential's response to a change of its own
parameters depends on the charge sitting on the centre. That is the assumption
`STAGE6_ERRATUM7_20261026.txt` E7.8 flagged, the set having been fitted on a neutral atom.
EXPERIMENT 0 established that the operator is the authors' operator; this establishes that
putting it on a charged centre is not a neutral act. Corrected in
`STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC23_ERRATUM1_20261103.txt` and `DSHIELD_DECOMPOSITION_ERRATUM2_20261103.md`.

## 4. One assumption the build could not check, now measured

`cs6_audit.py` excludes ECP centre rows from max|Löwdin| by the symbol `Q`. Had ORCA labelled a
coreless `He>` or `Ar>` centre by its element, those rows would have entered max|Löwdin| and every
classification would have been wrong with no error appearing. GATE 3 refused to read a number
until it had checked, and it passed: **all 440 centres across the 48 walled arms are labelled
`Q`**, nbasis 402 and NEL 118 throughout, equal to the charge-free control, and every printed
charge sum is exactly −2 plus that arm's centre charges.

## 5. What this does not say

- **He and Ar are not independent of Ne**: one paper, one group, one fitting recipe. This bounds
  the sensitivity **from below**. `ECP_ASSESSMENT_20261102.md` Section 3 records that the
  reference library holds no set from outside that lineage.
- **Ar is a sensitivity probe, not a published operator.** EXPERIMENT 0 validated He against its
  own fitting target to 1.381e-06 Eh and could not validate Ar better than 3.067e-03 Eh. C23.5
  evaluated every criterion twice; both readings gave the same verdict, and the He figures are
  quoted, which is the conservative choice since Ar moves everything further.
- **Nothing here says the wall is wrong, or right.** `d_shield` has no third arm.
- **READING 5's mean f of −0.696 is not to be quoted.** Averaging a quantity spanning −9.3 to
  +2.3 summarises nothing and the "30 per cent absorbed" figure derived from it is not
  interpretable. The per-condition factors are the result.
- Frozen geometry, one frame, one basis, in vacuo. DIAGNOSTIC 19's multireference reading returned
  DISAGREE and still does.

## 6. Every bare-arm quantity is unaffected

No bare arm was run in DIAGNOSTIC 23. The barrier lowerings of 4.9965 to 10.6714 kcal/mol, the
frozen barriers of 7.6609 to 13.3357 against the 18.3323 control, the readability window, D17's
per-site ceiling and the stability verdicts of DIAGNOSTICS 20 to 22 all stand untouched. What has
moved is the shielding metric built on top of them, and the three arrangement-comparing statements
drawn from it.
