READING 4: is ORCA's radial-power column the same column as NWChem's?
==============================================================================
   ORCA's own manual (6.0, section 7.5) illustrates NewECP with a published
   potential, V with SD(10,MDF). Its s channel reads [quoted]:
        1  14.4900000000  178.4479710000  2
        2  6.5240000000  19.8313750000  2

   basis-set-exchange carries those primitives in 3 set(s): Stuttgart RSC 1997, Stuttgart RSC 1997 ECP, Grimme vDZP
   Taking Stuttgart RSC 1997, which lists the potential in NWChem format:
        2  14.490000000  178.447971000
        2  6.524000000  19.831375000

   NWChem documents its radial form as
        U_l(r) = r^(-2) * sum_k A_lk * r^(n_lk) * exp(-B_lk r^2)
   and states the shift is 'in accordance with most of the ECP literature
   and implementations', so n = 2 means r^0. [quoted, NWChem ECP manual]

   Same potential, same exponents, same coefficients, and the power column
   reads 2 in both listings. READING 4: ORCA's column is NWChem's column,
   so the project's blocks are r^0 blocks.
