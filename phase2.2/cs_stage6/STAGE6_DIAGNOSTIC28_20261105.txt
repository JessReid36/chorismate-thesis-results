IS B3LYP PLACING THIS DIANION'S HIGHEST OCCUPIED ORBITAL TOO HIGH, AND IF SO DOES THAT ACCOUNT FOR
THE GAP NARROWING DIAGNOSTIC 27 FOUND?

D28.1  WHY THIS IS RUN

DIAGNOSTIC 27 (STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC27_RESULT_20261105.txt, sha256
7e7e9106ba7663cc7ba26bf2c6081b4919966016250a6fe1b4eff1c7a86f8fc8) found that the barrier change
stops converging with basis above the weakest field tested, and that the HOMO-LUMO gap narrows
severely as the field rises - at def2-SVPD the bare substrate's gap is 3.5375 eV at the reactant and
0.4935 eV at sum|q| = 2.8800 e.

There is a known reason to suspect the functional is part of this, and it is visible in DIAGNOSTIC
27's own charge-free control runs. B3LYP places the highest occupied orbital of the BARE substrate,
with no charges present at all, at

   def2-SVPD    ctl_R   +1.2307 eV      ctl_TS  +1.3881 eV
   def2-TZVPD   ctl_R   +1.2756 eV      ctl_TS  +1.4371 eV

All four are POSITIVE, which places the orbital above the vacuum level and so says the second
electron is not bound. Probe 3 (vde_designs/PROBE3_RESULT.txt, sha256
f040ab41dd22387fe7f3d357cf62379c02fd5c3135922e87ff7a5a57a288f5f1) measured the vertical detachment
energy of the same bare dianion at +0.5123 eV, which says it IS bound. Those two statements
disagree. For the exact functional the highest occupied orbital energy equals minus the detachment
energy, so it should sit near -0.51 eV rather than +1.23 eV.

That is the standard signature of self-interaction error in a global hybrid, and it matters here
because the gap narrowing DIAGNOSTIC 27 measured is the occupied level rising toward the empty one.
If B3LYP starts the occupied level roughly 1.7 eV too high, the field needed to close the gap is
underestimated and the usable field window is wider than DIAGNOSTIC 27's numbers imply. If B3LYP is
not the cause, the narrowing is real and DIAGNOSTIC 27's conclusion is strengthened. Both outcomes
are useful, which is why this is worth four of the twenty-two jobs on its own.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ASSUME. DIAGNOSTIC 27's committed result stands as written. This diagnostic does
not revisit its energies, its basis verdict or its density-relocation finding, none of which depend
on an orbital energy.

A NOTE ON WHAT DIAGNOSTIC 27 DID NOT ESTABLISH. Three of DIAGNOSTIC 27's runs engaged ORCA's TRAH
converger, and in those three the printed orbital energies are level-shifted and the
negative-gap warnings ORCA emitted precede SCF convergence rather than describing the converged
state. No converged gap is therefore available for the sum|q| = 5.6433 e condition in the diffuse
bases, and none is quoted here or treated as a reference below. The conditions this diagnostic
compares against - the controls and sum|q| = 1.3000 and 2.8800 e - did not engage TRAH and their
printed orbital energies are the converged ones.


D28.2  WHAT IS RUN

Twenty-two ORCA jobs. Two range-separated functionals, chosen so that the fraction of exact exchange
at long range increases across the three functionals now in hand:

   B3LYP       0.20 global, no long-range correction     already run, DIAGNOSTIC 27
   CAM-B3LYP   0.19 short range rising to 0.65 long range
   wB97X-D3    rising to 1.00 at long range

That ordering is the point: if the long-range exchange is the mechanism, the occupied orbital should
fall monotonically along it. One functional moving proves much less than three in order.

   2   keyword probes, one per functional: a single water molecule at def2-SVP, a few seconds each,
       whose only purpose is to establish that ORCA accepts the keyword before twenty real jobs run
   8   controls: 2 functionals x 2 bases (def2-SVPD, def2-TZVPD) x 2 states, charge-free.
       THESE CARRY THE PRIMARY READING.
  12   charged arms: 2 functionals x 3 vectors x 2 states, at def2-SVPD only

The charge vectors and geometries are the same committed files DIAGNOSTIC 27 used, copied byte for
byte and not regenerated:

   6e8f41c7da3bcf4ed9f5c650dc9fcf12c2a36e5af7a900e164892c276497aa88  d24/reactant_qm.xyz
   2babeb9000ca5220a908a70eaec80ec4a64358c8f5677e9a16cb104e0dffc5bf  d24/transition_state_qm.xyz
   daf4793d1ec9b3c328046c8f0e80d2697f7286216462bce11934af0c4f199e28  d24/pc_1.3000e_n10.pc
   345f61f854fa7e6fc53940355f856c4d1eb9672ada465cc90df0dd14f85bc49e  d24/pc_2.8800e_n10.pc
   fe151bdd3b5ca12b124f8e0a153790fd552c8216ff8b73ae2cdab402dcc6aa6e  d24/pc_5.6433e_n20.pc

def2-TZVPD is run for the controls only. The occupied-orbital question is answered by the controls,
and B3LYP places that orbital within 0.05 eV of the same value in the two bases (+1.2307 against
+1.2756 at the reactant), so the second basis is a check on that agreement rather than a separate
measurement. If the answer is that B3LYP was the cause, the charged arms at def2-TZVPD become worth
running and that is a separate pre-registration.

LEVEL. Every setting other than the functional and its dispersion token is the one DIAGNOSTIC 27
used: def2/J, RIJCOSX, TightSCF, %maxcore 3000, %pal nprocs 8, %scf MaxIter 300, charge -2,
multiplicity 1, in vacuo, frozen single points, ORCA 6.0.1, eight cores. GATE 3 verifies that
against DIAGNOSTIC 27's own committed job.inp files rather than taking it on trust.

DISPERSION. wB97X-D3 carries its own D3 correction, so no D3BJ token is added to it; adding one
would count dispersion twice. CAM-B3LYP has no built-in dispersion, so D3BJ is added, matching
B3LYP D3BJ. GATE 5 checks that each output reports exactly one dispersion correction.

COST, stated before the run. Measured B3LYP wall times on hpc1 for these exact conditions: 39.0 min
for the eight def2-SVPD jobs, and 405 and 441 s for the two def2-TZVPD controls. Range-separated
hybrids cost somewhat more per cycle because the long-range exchange is evaluated separately;
taking that at 1.3 to 1.8x gives roughly 2.0 to 2.8 h for all twenty-two, and the figure is
ESTIMATED because no range-separated functional has ever been run on this system. Walltime
24:00:00. One run that fails to converge in 300 cycles is stopped by ORCA, not by walltime.


D28.3  THE GATES, WHICH RUN BEFORE ANY NUMBER IS READ

GATE 0  PROVENANCE. Every copied geometry and charge file is byte-identical to the committed d24
        file it came from, by the five sha256 values above.

GATE 1  THE PROBES PASSED. Both keyword probes terminated normally. If either did not, ORCA does
        not accept that keyword in this build and no reading is attempted for that functional.

GATE 2  THE INPUTS ARE THE SAME CONDITIONS. For every run that has a DIAGNOSTIC 27 counterpart, the
        two job.inp files are identical once the keyword line is removed - same %maxcore, %pal,
        %scf, %pointcharges, charge, multiplicity and geometry reference.

GATE 3  AND THE KEYWORD LINE DIFFERS ONLY IN THE FUNCTIONAL. Each generated keyword line equals
        DIAGNOSTIC 27's with "B3LYP D3BJ" replaced by this functional's own token and nothing else:
        basis, def2/J, RIJCOSX and TightSCF all still present and in the same order.

GATE 4  CONTROL AND CHARGED ARM DIFFER ONLY BY THE CHARGES. Each charged job.inp equals its control
        plus exactly the line %pointcharges "pc.pc", and no control directory contains a pc.pc.

GATE 5  EXACTLY ONE DISPERSION CORRECTION PER RUN, and the functional ORCA reports is not B88/LYP,
        which is what it prints for B3LYP. A run reporting two dispersion corrections, or reporting
        B3LYP's functional, is not read.

GATE 6  TERMINATION AND CONVERGENCE. Every job reports ORCA TERMINATED NORMALLY and SCF convergence.
        A run that does not is not committed; a note about it is.

GATE 7  THE BASIS AND ELECTRON COUNT ARE UNCHANGED. Every substrate run reports 118 electrons, and
        402 basis functions at def2-SVPD and 682 at def2-TZVPD, the values DIAGNOSTIC 27 recorded.

GATE 8  TRAH IS DECLARED. For every run, whether ORCA engaged TRAH is recorded, and any orbital
        energy taken from a run that did is reported as unavailable rather than quoted. This gate
        exists because DIAGNOSTIC 27 did not have it and three of its runs needed it.

GATE 9  EVERY FIGURE IN THE RESULT DOCUMENT MATCHES THE RUN AT FULL PRECISION.

A FAILED GATE IS COMMITTED AS A FAILED GATE.


D28.4  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE IT RUNS

THE PRIMARY READING is the highest occupied orbital energy of the BARE substrate - the controls, no
charges - against B3LYP's +1.2307 eV (def2-SVPD, R) and the -0.5123 eV that Probe 3's detachment
energy implies. Write H(f) for that energy under functional f, at the reactant, def2-SVPD.

  CONFIRMED            if H(wB97X-D3) < 0.0 eV. The orbital is then bound, as the detachment energy
                       says it should be, and B3LYP's placement was a material error. The field
                       window DIAGNOSTIC 27 implies is then too pessimistic and the charged arms at
                       def2-TZVPD become worth running.

  PARTIAL              if H(wB97X-D3) falls by more than 0.5 eV from B3LYP's value but stays at or
                       above 0.0 eV. The error is real but does not by itself account for the
                       orbital being unbound, and no claim is made about the size of the correction
                       to the field window.

  NOT CONFIRMED        if H(wB97X-D3) falls by 0.5 eV or less, or rises. B3LYP's placement is then
                       not the explanation, DIAGNOSTIC 27's narrowing stands as measured, and the
                       functional is removed from the list of things to try.

  MECHANISM CHECK, which is separate and can fail even when the primary reading CONFIRMS:
                       H(B3LYP) > H(CAM-B3LYP) > H(wB97X-D3), at both states and both bases. If the
                       three are not in that order, the long-range exchange fraction is not the
                       mechanism, and the primary verdict is reported WITHOUT a mechanism.

THE SECONDARY READING is the gap at sum|q| = 2.8800 e, def2-SVPD, which B3LYP gives as 0.4935 eV at
the reactant and 1.2586 eV at the transition state. Taking TOL = 1.0 eV:

  NARROWING IS REAL    if the gap at that condition remains below TOL under wB97X-D3. The narrowing
                       is then not a B3LYP artefact whatever the primary reading says.
  NARROWING WAS EXAGGERATED  if it rises above TOL. The usable field window is then wider than
                       DIAGNOSTIC 27's numbers imply, and by how much is a question for a further
                       diagnostic, not an answer from this one.

RECORDED, NOT GATED: whether the sum|q| = 5.6433 e runs still engage TRAH under either functional,
and the Loewdin and dipole values at every condition. These are reported as measured and no
threshold is set for them here.

If the two functionals disagree on the primary reading, the result is reported per functional and no
single verdict is formed.


D28.5  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. It does not establish which functional is correct. It establishes whether B3LYP's placement of
   one orbital is anomalous against a measured detachment energy, and whether correcting that
   changes the gap. Choosing a production functional is a separate decision needing an amendment.
2. It compares orbital energies across functionals, which is weaker than comparing them within one.
   The detachment-energy comparison is the anchor precisely because it is an energy difference.
3. It says nothing about solvation, which addresses the same headroom by a different route.
4. It does not re-run the sum|q| = 5.6433 e condition at def2-TZVPD, so it cannot say whether the
   basis sequence converges under a different functional.
5. Three vectors, one frame, one grid, in vacuo. No barrier is quoted and no design is accepted.
6. It cannot recover a converged gap for the conditions where DIAGNOSTIC 27's runs engaged TRAH. If
   that is wanted it needs a run with the level shift controlled, which is not this diagnostic.

MACHINERY. phase2.2/cs6_d28_prepare.py writes the inputs on the PC and is committed with them. The
run is one restartable PBS job on hpc1, no -q. The reading is a separate script and document built
after the outputs are downloaded. Every output file is downloaded and kept as a physical file, with
a PC archive outside git carrying a sha256 per file, readable outputs in the results repository, and
nothing removed from hpc1 until both are done.
