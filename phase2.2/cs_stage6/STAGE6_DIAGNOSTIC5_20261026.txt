STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 5, PRE-REGISTERED 2026-10-26
Where Diagnostic 4's divergence lives: a decomposition of the sixteen points already on disk

This file is pre-registered. It is never edited. Changes go into a dated amendment or erratum.


D5.0  WHY

Diagnostic 4 measured ddE = (E_TS - E_TS,bare) - (E_R - E_R,bare) for S6-20 scaled by lambda at two bases, and
reported this:

      lambda   S|q|    largest |q|   predicted    def2-SVP     S      def2-SVPD    S       Delta
       0.25   1.440     0.1375       -5.0754    -5.0969    0.4%    -5.5601    9.5%   -0.4633
       0.50   2.880     0.2750      -10.5430   -10.6573    1.1%   -11.6329   10.3%   -0.9757
       0.75   4.320     0.4125      -16.4026   -17.0581    4.0%   -10.2346   37.6%   +6.8235
       1.00   5.760     0.5500      -22.6544   -24.6898    9.0%    -6.1422   72.9%  +18.5477

Two things in that table need stating plainly before anything is built on it.

First, def2-SVP is an almost exact quadratic in lambda. A free quadratic fit leaves a maximum residual of 0.0586
kcal/mol across the four points, and the lambda = 1 point reproduces the committed frozen S6-20 value minus its
classical sum L to 0.0001 kcal/mol. Nothing about the def2-SVP series is anomalous.

Second, def2-SVPD is NOT monotone. Its ddE runs -5.5601, -11.6329, -10.2346, -6.1422: it reaches its largest
magnitude near lambda = 0.5 and then returns towards zero, so that beyond half field adding charge makes the
catalysis worse, continuously. Delta changes sign near lambda = 0.53 and then grows roughly as lambda^3.5. A
free quadratic fit to def2-SVPD has a POSITIVE curvature coefficient, +40.7, the opposite sign from
polarisation, and a residual of 0.72 kcal/mol, twelve times def2-SVP's.

Diagnostic 4 could not say whether that turnover lives in the reactant, in the transition state, or in both,
because ddE is a difference and D4 reported only the difference. The sixteen single points already contain the
two state energies separately, and ORCA printed orbital energies, a dipole and Loewdin charges into the same
files. All of it is committed, sha256-manifested, in field/outputs/field_outputs.tar.xz. This diagnostic reads
it back. NO NEW CALCULATION IS PERFORMED.

The question matters because two explanations fit the table and they lead opposite ways.

  (i) REAL DIFFERENTIAL SATURATION. The dianion's response to the field genuinely saturates, and the reactant
      and transition state saturate by different amounts, so their difference turns over. Then Diagnostic 4's
      lambda_quad is chemistry, Stage 7 caps the total field, and the achievable barrier reduction for this
      substrate is bounded near 10 to 12 kcal/mol.

  (ii) ELECTRON SPILL-OUT. Finding 4 established that our charge sites have a wall for nuclei and NONE for
      electrons. Marefat Khah et al., JCTC 2020, doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b01162, report that this causes
      "unphysical leaking of the electron density", "in particular, when diffuse basis functions are required",
      and that smearing does not fix it because "these methods do not capture the effects of the Pauli exchange
      repulsion on the electronic wavefunction". def2-SVPD supplies exactly the diffuse flexibility that lets
      density pile up toward a positive site; def2-SVP does not have it. Then the def2-SVPD high-field points
      are not physical barriers at all, the bound is a bound on the model, and what has to change is the charge
      model - not the basis and not the budget.

Neither is assumed. DIAGNOSTIC3_NOTE1 N3.2 measured def2-SVP to be about fifteen times less sensitive than
def2-SVPD to empty basis functions at a design site, which says the diffuse basis is the one with the extra
freedom, and it does not say which way that freedom is used here. DIAGNOSTIC3's finding that a real Na+ ion with
its own Pauli wall saturated about as much as a bare point charge was measured at ONE charge at ONE site, in
the low-field regime where the two bases agree in this very table - so it does not extend to lambda > 0.5 and
its reassurance does not cover this. That limitation of D3 is recorded here rather than left implicit.


D5.1  WHAT IS READ, AND FROM WHERE

Every number comes out of two committed archives, each member checked against that archive's own sha256
manifest before it is parsed:

  field/outputs/field_outputs.tar.xz     the sixteen runs, ./<basis>/lam<NNN>_<state>/job.out
  qscan/outputs/qscan_outputs.tar.xz     the four bare references, ./C1/bare_<state>/job.out and ./C5/...

Per run: the final single point energy, the basis-function count, the full orbital-energy block, the dipole
magnitude and the Loewdin atomic charges. Every field is required; none is defaulted. A run is refused unless
ORCA terminated normally, the SCF converged, the electron count is 118, there is exactly one final energy, ORCA
confirmed reading exactly 20 point charges, and there are 59 occupied orbitals. A bare reference is refused if
it carries a point-charge file at all.

THE TIE TO THE PUBLISHED RESULT IS A STOP, NOT A REMARK. The eight ddE values this script reconstructs from the
decomposition must reproduce the committed DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt table to better than 1e-3 kcal/mol. If they
do not, the decomposition does not belong to the published result and nothing below it is readable.

Definitions used throughout:

  dE_R(basis, lambda)  = E(R  + charges) - E(R  bare)
  dE_TS(basis, lambda) = E(TS + charges) - E(TS bare)
  delta dE_R           = dE_R(def2-SVPD) - dE_R(def2-SVP)        and likewise for the TS
  Delta                = delta dE_TS - delta dE_R, identically Diagnostic 4's Delta


D5.2  WHAT IS NOT DONE

No calculation is submitted. No criterion, threshold, tolerance, method, arrangement or reported number
changes. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt and PHASE22_PLAN.txt section 2.2 are untouched.
DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt stands exactly as committed, including lambda_quad = 0.25.

On that number, one fact belongs in the record because omitting it would overstate its precision: lambda_quad
came out at 0.25 rather than 0.50 because def2-SVPD's S at lambda = 0.50 is 10.3 per cent against a
pre-registered threshold of 10.0 per cent. It is a near miss by 0.3 percentage points, and the bound it implies
therefore differs by a factor of two - sum |q| <= 1.440 e rather than 2.880 e - on three tenths of a point. The
threshold was fixed before the runs and IS NOT MOVED. The near miss is stated so that the bound is quoted with
its real precision rather than as though 1.440 e were sharply determined.


D5.3  THE FOUR READINGS, FIXED BEFORE ANY OF THIS WAS READ

  (a) WHERE THE DIVERGENCE LIVES. Report delta dE_R and delta dE_TS at every lambda. Because their difference
      is identically Delta, they are not independent, and the well-posed question is how large the common shift
      is beside Delta. Let lo = min(|delta dE_R|, |delta dE_TS|) at lambda = 1.
        - lo >= 3 * |Delta|: CANCELLATION-DOMINATED. Both states move far more than their difference does, so
          ddE at def2-SVPD is a small difference of large numbers and is numerically fragile. Neither basis can
          be quoted at that field, and the charge model rather than the basis is what has to change.
        - otherwise: NOT A CANCELLATION. The turnover is a real differential response, and the state carrying
          the larger shift is named as where to look.

  (b) HOW NON-QUADRATIC EACH BASIS IS. Fit a quadratic in lambda to each of the four series - two bases by two
      states - and report the maximum residual. Let svpq and svpdq be the larger residual over the two states
      at each basis.
        - svpq <= 0.3 and svpdq > 0.3 kcal/mol: the diffuse basis introduces a higher-order response the small
          basis does not have at all, reproducing N3.2's single-site result at twenty sites.
        - both <= 0.3: neither basis carries a higher-order response, so the turnover lives wholly in the
          difference and this decomposition does not explain it.
        - otherwise: the higher-order response is not specific to the diffuse functions, and the basis is not
          on its own the explanation.

      THIS READING DOES NOT CLAIM TO LOCALISE AN OUTLIER AND IS NOT WRITTEN TO. Four equally spaced lambda
      values against a three-parameter quadratic leave one degree of freedom. Holding each lambda out in turn
      and predicting it from the other three gives errors locked in a ratio of about 3 to 1 between the
      endpoints and the interior, fixed by the spacing alone, and a single bad point perturbs all four in that
      same ratio - so the pattern cannot distinguish one outlier from a smooth non-quadratic response. The
      leave-one-out errors are printed AS DATA, not as a test. Localising an outlier needs a fifth lambda;
      0.875 is the natural one, and that is recorded as an open item below rather than asserted here.

  (c) SPILL-OUT SIGNATURES. Report, for all sixteen runs, the HOMO energy, the HOMO-LUMO gap, the dipole
      magnitude and the largest Loewdin charge shift between the two bases. Spill-out is SUPPORTED if any of:
        - the COMMON inter-basis shift, (delta dE_R + delta dE_TS)/2, grows faster than lambda^2.5. Delta's own
          shape is already known from Diagnostic 4, so the common part is the new information; ordinary
          polarisation is second order in the field, so an exponent near 2 is expected and a steeper one is
          response the small basis cannot represent at all.
        - any HOMO-LUMO gap at def2-SVPD falls below 1.0 eV.
        - a dipole grows more than 1.5 times as fast with lambda at def2-SVPD as at def2-SVP.
        - a HOMO departs from its def2-SVP counterpart by more than 0.5 eV at lambda = 1 and by more than twice
          its departure at lambda = 0.25.
      A RISING HOMO IS DELIBERATELY NOT ONE OF THESE. This dianion's HOMO is already POSITIVE in vacuo at every
      basis tested - +2.278 eV at def2-SVP, +1.227 at ma-def2-SVP, +1.231 at def2-SVPD for the bare reactant -
      which is DFT self-interaction and is why Finding 8 was withdrawn, the VDE of +0.5123 eV at two diffuse
      bases being the test that settled it. The design's mostly positive charges push orbital energies DOWN, so
      a rise would not be expected even if density were leaking, and a test for one would have been vacuous.
      If spill-out is supported, the def2-SVPD high-field points are not usable as physical barriers and a
      charge model with a Pauli wall for electrons is needed before any high-field number means anything. If it
      is not supported, real differential saturation becomes the leading explanation and Diagnostic 4's bound
      should be read as chemistry.

  (d) THE RANGE IN WHICH THE TWO BASES AGREE. Report the largest lambda at which |ddE(def2-SVPD) -
      ddE(def2-SVP)| <= 1.0 kcal/mol, and the corresponding sum |q|. That is the honest domain of validity of
      the committed def2-SVP numbers, and it holds whichever of the two explanations is right. If no lambda
      qualifies, there is no field range in which the committed def2-SVP numbers are basis-converged, and that
      must be said of all of them.


D5.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT TEST

Nothing new is computed, so this cannot test any hypothesis the sixteen existing points do not already contain.
It cannot localise an outlier in lambda, for the reason given in D5.3(b). It does not test a charge model with
a Pauli wall for electrons, which is the fix if spill-out is supported. It does not test def2-TZVPD, so it
cannot say the def2-SVPD numbers are themselves converged. One design, one frame, fixed geometry. Nothing about
Loss. It says nothing about whether b varies between sites, which remains the first explanation to reach for if
the residuals are large - b was measured at site 1690 only.


D5.5  WHAT IS TRUE REGARDLESS OF THE OUTCOME

Recorded here so that it is not read as a consequence of whichever way D5.3 falls.

Every committed Stage 6 design barrier was computed at def2-SVP. At the design point the two bases disagree by
75 per cent. Whether that is saturation or spill-out, NO COMMITTED STAGE 6 DESIGN BARRIER CAN BE QUOTED AS
BASIS-CONVERGED. What D5 decides is which basis, or which charge model, the frozen half of Stage 6 has to be
rebuilt against - not whether it has to be rebuilt.

The per-site cap is not the binding constraint. S6-20's largest single charge is 0.55 e, comfortably inside the
0.80 e the amendment measured, and its barrier moved anyway. Whatever bounds this system, it is not a per-site
magnitude.

Diagnostic 4's reading 2 came back NOT CONFIRMED, r = +0.9195 with an intercept of -10.22 kcal/mol. The
assessment's r = +0.9955 across four designs was therefore a property of those four designs and is not a
scaling law in sum |q|. Section 4 of STAGE6_ASSESSMENT_20261022.md must be read that way. For the record, the
prediction offered before the runs - that an intermediate lambda_quad would come with a NOT CONFIRMED reading -
did land, but the reason given for it was incomplete: it argued Delta would have to be superlinear, whereas the
measured Delta changes sign. The prediction was right for a reason that was not the stated one.
