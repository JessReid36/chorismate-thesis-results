A2.1  A DEFECT IN A1.5's READING 1, FOUND BEFORE ANY DATA

A1.5 reads ONE SITE when a single site's | d_shield | is within tol of Dset, with

   tol = max( 0.10 x Dsum, 0.05 kcal/mol )

carried over unchanged from DIAGNOSTIC 11.3. The 0.05 is an absolute floor. When Dset and every
single site lie far below that floor - which is what DIAGNOSTIC 14 already measured at def2-SVP,
where the six negatives together give d_shield -0.0017 - every site satisfies the test, and
Reading 1 returns ONE SITE naming whichever site the harness happens to reach first.

Demonstrated on the rehearsal harness before any input of A1.4 was generated: with all six sites
at -0.0003 and Dset at 0.0017, Reading 1 returned "ONE SITE (400)". Site 400 carries nothing. The
verdict is vacuous, and in a thesis it would be worse than vacuous, because it names a site.

This is a defect in the criterion as A1.5 states it, not in the harness. It is recorded here
rather than fixed in place, and it is recorded before any A1.4 run exists.


A2.2  THE PRECONDITION

Reading 1 is read only where there is an effect to locate:

   Dset <= tol      READING 1 IS NOT READ.  The reported answer is NO EFFECT TO LOCATE, with Dset
                    and tol printed. No site is named. This is not a failure of anything: it says
                    the negatives are inert at that basis, which is the thing DIAGNOSTIC 14
                    already reported there.
   Dset >  tol      Reading 1 is read exactly as A1.5 states it, unchanged.

No new number is introduced. tol is A1.5's tol, which is DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's, unchanged.


A2.3  READINGS 2 AND 3 ARE NOT CHANGED

Reading 2 is DIAGNOSTIC 14.3's own rule and A1.5 carries it over unchanged; it is left unchanged
here, including where it is trivially satisfied. Where Dset <= tol the harness prints BOUNDED and
states on the same line that the verdict is trivially satisfied at that basis because nothing
measured there exceeds the tolerance. That is a disclosure, not a criterion.

Reading 3 compares differences against tol and does not have the defect: a difference below
tolerance returns NEITHER, which is the correct reading of "no difference".


A2.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE

Not the runs: A1.4's 76 runs are as A1.4 specifies them, and none had been generated when this was
written. Not Readings 2 or 3. Not DIAGNOSTIC 14's verdict, which stands at NO VERDICT. Not A1.2's
dipole correction. Not any tolerance anywhere.
