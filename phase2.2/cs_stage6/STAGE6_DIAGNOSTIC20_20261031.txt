D20.1  THE QUESTION

Where along the field does the production wavefunction stop being a minimum, and what does the
stable solution do to the barriers this project has reported?

DIAGNOSTIC 19 measured SCF stability at two points only: def2-SVP, bare, lambda = 0.00 and
lambda = 1.00. At zero field both states are minima - every eigenvalue of the stability matrix
positive. At full field BOTH states carry TWO NEGATIVE eigenvalues, so the restricted solution
is a saddle point, and ORCA transformed the orbitals, reconverged and found a lower stable
solution each time.

   stab_lam000_R    STABLE     E(0..2) +0.05617271  +0.09119369  +0.09759674
   stab_lam000_TS   STABLE     E(0..2) +0.01728066  +0.04008551  +0.05376300
   stab_lam100_R    UNSTABLE   E(0..2) -0.01083954  -0.00844369  +0.00317256
   stab_lam100_TS   UNSTABLE   E(0..2) -0.03473872  -0.01509792  +0.00240913

   [measured, DIAGNOSTIC 19, as corrected by STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC19_NOTE1_20261031.txt]

D19 could not take this further and said so in advance. D19.3 states of the restart that the
reconverged energy "is not the production energy, is not compared with one, and is used for
nothing but this verdict". That sentence was written before the result existed and it is
honoured: D19 draws no barrier from the stable solution and no claim rests on one.

THIS DIAGNOSTIC IS WHAT LICENSES THAT COMPARISON, and it earns the licence with GATE 3 rather
than asserting it. If adding STABPerform leaves the first converged energy identical to the
committed production energy, then the first solution IS the production solution, and the
difference between it and the stable one is the correction the production number is missing.

Three things are unmeasured and this diagnostic measures them: where in lambda the instability
begins, whether it also occurs at def2-SVPD, and whether the Pauli wall changes it.

D20.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS COMPUTED

The verdict of a run is the FIRST stability analysis, which by GATE 3 is the production
solution. A second analysis, where ORCA reconverged, is reported with it.

READING 1, where does the instability begin?

   Per basis, over the bare arm, the lambda ladder 0.00 / 0.25 / 0.50 / 0.75 / 1.00.

   STABLE THROUGHOUT     every lambda's first analysis is stable.
   ONSET AT lambda*      stable below lambda*, unstable at lambda* and at every larger lambda.
   UNSTABLE THROUGHOUT   unstable at the smallest non-zero lambda and above.
   NOT MONOTONIC         stability returns at a larger lambda than one where it was lost. No
                         onset is claimed; every lambda is reported.

   The three lowest eigenvalues at every lambda are reported whatever the verdict, and the two
   bases are reported separately and never pooled.

READING 2, what does the stable solution do to the barrier?

   For each basis and each lambda where an instability was found, with ddE computed against the
   charge-free control of the same basis:

      ddE(first)   from the first converged solution, which is the production solution
      ddE(stable)  from the reconverged solution
      correction = ddE(stable) - ddE(first)

      tol = max( 0.10 x |largest correction entering this reading| , 0.05 kcal/mol ),
      DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's tolerance, applied to the quantity being compared.

   CORRECTION NEGLIGIBLE   every |correction| <= tol.
   CORRECTION MATERIAL     any |correction| > tol. Each is reported in kcal/mol and as a
                           percentage of D11.3's 1.067 budget.

   THE CONSEQUENCE IS STATED NOW SO THAT IT CANNOT BE SOFTENED LATER. If any correction exceeds
   1.067 kcal/mol, then every committed barrier, Dset and d_shield measured at or above that
   lambda at that basis is provisional until recomputed on a stable solution, and must be
   quoted as provisional or not quoted. That applies to DIAGNOSTIC 18's -24.6898 kcal/mol and to
   every whole-design number at comparable field strength.

READING 3, does the Pauli wall change stability?

   def2-SVP, lambda = 1.00: the walled arm here against the bare arm from DIAGNOSTIC 19.

   WALL DOES NOT CHANGE STABILITY   both unstable, or both stable.
   WALL STABILISES                  bare unstable, walled stable.
   WALL DESTABILISES                bare stable, walled unstable.

   DIAGNOSTIC 18 measured that the wall moves the gap by under 1% and displaces no density
   here. If it also leaves the stability unchanged, that is a third quantity on which the wall
   makes no difference at this basis, and it is reported as such and not as a new finding about
   the wall.


D20.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

THE LARGEST LIMITATION, STATED FIRST. The reconverged solution is another RESTRICTED solution,
reached by rotating orbitals. It is not a multireference treatment and it does not make the
system single-reference. A stable RKS solution at a 0.6 eV gap is still a single-reference
description of a near-degenerate system. DIAGNOSTIC 19's READING 3 returned DISAGREE and that
stands: whether the near-degeneracy is multireference remains open, and the CASSCF or NEVPT2
calculation D19.4 named is not replaced by anything here. This diagnostic asks only whether the
production number is the lowest single-reference number, which is a narrower question and a
prerequisite to the other one.

ORCA's restart is a heuristic. It attempts a bounded number of reconvergences from transformed
orbitals, so a solution it reports as stable is stable against the rotations it tried. Nothing
here establishes that it is the global minimum of the SCF, and READING 2's correction is
therefore a lower bound on the correction, not the correction.

One design, S6-20, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, in vacuo, no continuum
(STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). The corrections measured here are for this arrangement at these
lambdas and DO NOT transfer to DIAGNOSTIC 16's or 17's designs without measuring them. Those are
148 and 18 runs and are not in this diagnostic.

The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7), so the
walled arm of READING 3 is a contrast and not a truth.

Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain unmeasurable until the OptTS / coordsys cartesian
conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028 is decided.


D20.5  DIRECTION

If READING 1 returns an onset and READING 2 returns CORRECTION MATERIAL, then this project has
been quoting high-field barriers from saddle points, and the readable-window claim that
DIAGNOSTIC 18 left as the honest contribution has to be restated on corrected numbers. The
correction is cheap for the designs that matter and the work is bounded: it is a re-run with one
extra keyword, not a change of method.

If READING 2 returns CORRECTION NEGLIGIBLE, the instability is real but costs nothing
quantitatively, and the committed barriers stand as reported with a recorded caveat.

If READING 1 returns STABLE THROUGHOUT at def2-SVPD while def2-SVP has an onset, the
instability is a property of the compact basis and not of the field, and that would be a finding
about the basis rather than about the design.

D20.3 fixes which answer means which before anything is computed.
