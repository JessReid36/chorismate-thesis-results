==============================================================================
STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 38, RESULT.  ERRATUM 1
R38.9 REPORTED THE RESIDUE AND CALLED IT THE PEAK
Written 15 November 2026.
==============================================================================

A result is never edited. This erratum sits beside it.

WHAT IS WRONG

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC38_RESULT_20261115.txt reports, under R38.8 AND R38.9:

   peak over 5 runs 11 MB

and then:

   "def2-SVPD figure was unmeasured and that R38.9 was how it would stop
   being unmeasured. The measured peak above is that figure, and it is what
   a later job should use rather than the number carried over."

The figure is not the peak, and the instruction built on it would under-size a later
job by more than three orders of magnitude.

THE EVIDENCE IS IN THE SAME COMMIT

d38/PROGRESS.txt was committed alongside the result. Its watcher sampled the scratch
directory every ten minutes while the job was alive. Its own lines:

   2026-11-14 21:55:20  used=27G  storage=ON DISK  running=N
   2026-11-14 22:05:21  used=31G  storage=ON DISK  running=N
   2026-11-15 00:05:33  used=29G  storage=ON DISK  running=B1
   2026-11-15 02:25:48  used=28G  storage=ON DISK  running=A1

Every one of the five runs shows the same shape: 27 to 31 GB in the first twenty
minutes, settling to 13 to 14 GB for the rest of the run. So the record already
contained the right order of magnitude, in the same commit, and the two halves
contradict each other. The half carrying the instruction is the wrong half.

WHY IT HAPPENED

In d38/run_a1.pbs the measurement is taken after ORCA returns:

   ( cd "$S/$r" && "$ORCA/orca" job.inp > job.out 2>&1 </dev/null )
   ...
   u=$(du -sm "$S" 2>/dev/null | cut -f1)

By that point ORCA has deleted its own temporary files. What `du` measured was the
residue a finished run leaves behind, not the space the run needed while it was
running. The gate that checked R38.9 checked that a peak was reported, not that the
measurement was taken at a time when a peak could exist. A check by the claim's own
method is not a check.

THE CORRECTED STATEMENT

   sampled maximum        31 GB, from d38/PROGRESS.txt, 10-minute sampling
   free space available   43 GB, and the job completed
   therefore              31 GB <= true peak < 43 GB, per run, closed-shell
                          def2-SVPD, 24 atoms, 16 cores, %maxcore 6000
   residue after a run    11 MB, which is what R38.9 actually measured

The true peak is bounded, not measured: a ten-minute sampling interval can miss a
spike, and the only upper bound is that 43 GB sufficed.

WHAT IS WITHDRAWN

The sentence "it is what a later job should use" is withdrawn. 11 MB must not be used
to size anything. Any job sized from it would run out of scratch partway through.

WHAT IS UNAFFECTED

Every energy, every T1 diagnostic, every orbital energy, every gate and the verdict.
Those come from the five job.out files, which are archived, hash-verified and
unaffected by a mis-timed `du`. R38.8 stands in full: the node, the chosen scratch
path, the 43 GB free and the REDUCED margin are all correct, and the candidate
refusals and the ENOSPC correction are unaffected.

WHAT D39 DOES DIFFERENTLY

Two changes, both in Diagnostic 39's job script:

   The peak is sampled while the run is alive, by the same background watcher that
   writes PROGRESS.txt, and the maximum it sees is carried into the reading. The
   post-run `du` is still taken but is reported as the residue under its own name, so
   the two quantities cannot be confused again.

   The free-space floor is set from the corrected figure rather than from 11 MB.
   D38 ran at a REDUCED margin of 43 GB against a true requirement of at least 31 GB,
   which is a 12 GB margin that nobody had measured at the time. The ORCA manual puts
   open-shell runs at "about twice as expensive as the corresponding closed-shell
   versions", so D39's floor is set well above twice the corrected figure and a
   candidate that cannot meet it is refused at the start of the job rather than
   discovered three hours in. On this cluster that rules out $TMPDIR, which held 38 GB
   on comp050 and 43 GB on comp056, and means D39 needs /scratch-small-local to be
   genuinely available on whichever node PBS gives it.
