--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The optimiser has never been run on the committed grid, and no design of it has been read by the
five legs at def2-TZVPD (A11.3). D56 does both. It asks: of the designs the search ranks best at
first order, which are SURVIVORS by A9.4 (i), and up to how many charges?

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE SEARCH, FIXED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The program is the committed cs_opt_milp.py, called by cs6_d56_prepare.py. Nothing below is
chosen with a design in sight.

   sites and admitted signs   sites_f42436.tsv, as Stage 5 built it (2626 sites)
   coefficients               A_f42436_d33a.tsv, the working level, one frame; L is zero
   largest charge at a site   0.25 e (A11.5, item 3); 1488 sites then admit a charge
   spacing                    3.25 A between any two charges (Stage 5)
   budget                     set where it cannot bind (A11.5, item 7)
   sum of the charges         zero or more (A11.5, item 6)
   numbers of charges         3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20
   designs kept at each       3, best first, each with at least one charge more than 1.5 A from
                              every charge of each better one (decision O7)
   optimality                 a design is taken only if the solver proves it optimal

The numbers of charges and the three designs at each are Claude's proposal, to keep the cost
near that of D44; they are Jessica's to change before she commits this. With a linear score and
a cap every charge goes to +0.25 or -0.25 e unless the rule on the sum holds one back, so the
designs differ in where the charges stand and in their signs. The score grows with the number of
charges by construction; that is why each number is read by itself.

THE ORDER OF EVENTS. This document, AMENDMENT 11, the three programs and a11/ are committed and
pushed. Only then is the search run, by Jessica, from the committed program; it writes d56/ with
DESIGNS.tsv and every input, and that is committed and pushed before anything is copied to hpc1.
The reader checks that the designs were committed after this document.

3. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each of the 27 designs: the dianion and the monoanion with its charges, at R and TS, at the
working level CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD and at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD (8 runs). The eight
controls without a charge. 224 runs in 10 jobs: C0 with the controls, and one job for each
number of charges. Every input is a committed input, unchanged: d31/ctl_R/job.inp,
d40/B_bare_R/job.inp, d43/B_n11_q0.25_di_R/job.inp, d43/B_n11_q0.25_mo_R/job.inp,
d44/CAMTZ_B_n02_q0.50_di_R/job.inp, d44/CAMTZ_B_n02_q0.50_mo_R/job.inp,
d44/CAMTZ_C_bare_R/job.inp, d44/CAMTZ_C_ctl_R/job.inp. The job script is D53's committed one
with its names, its runs and its caps changed: 8 cores, 48 GB, the limit of 24 hours, no queue
named, no run made a second time in its folder. The cap of one run is 1800 s at the working
level and 5000 s at def2-TZVPD; at its caps a job would run 22.7 hours.

ESTIMATE of the cost, not a measurement: 45 to 90 job-hours on 8 cores, 6 to 12 hours by the
clock if the ten jobs start together. By ORCA's own run times in the committed outputs, D43's
runs at the working level took 266 to 1030 s and D44's and D53's runs of 0.25 e designs at
def2-TZVPD 453 to 1416 s. Two of Claude's estimates this week were too low. Disk on hpc1: about
5 GB.

4. THE READINGS, FIXED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   R56.1  THE RUNS AND THE CONTROLS. A run is measured as D53's reader measures a dianion; a
          monoanion is read for its energy and its last stability analysis. Each control is said
          to reproduce the committed run of the same input if its energy lies within 1e-6 Eh of
          it. The count of electrons (D53's bound, 0.02 e) is reported and is not a leg.

   R56.2  THE FOUR LEGS, at each level, against the control of that level and state, by the
          committed functions of cs6_d53.py and the committed thresholds of cs6_audit.py, in
          D43's words: RELIABLE, no flag at either state and the gap and the stability analysis
          read at both; UNRELIABLE, a flag at either state, where not converging is a flag;
          INCONCLUSIVE, no flag and a gap withheld or a stability analysis not read; NOT
          CLASSIFIED, no flag and a state not measured.

   R56.3  THE FIFTH LEG, by AMENDMENT 6, at each level: the detachment energy is the monoanion's
          energy minus the dianion's with the same charges; BOUND if above zero and the
          monoanion's last stability analysis is STABLE, METASTABLE if at or below zero. "A
          design PASSES THE FIFTH LEG if it is BOUND at both states."

   R56.4  ddE AND THE TOLERANCE, by D44's convention: WITHIN if the def2-TZVPD ddE differs from
          the working-level ddE by less than 15 % of it, OUTSIDE if by 15 % or more in either
          direction, NOT ASSESSABLE if the working-level ddE is below 3.2 kcal/mol, NOT FORMED
          if a number is missing. At def2-TZVPD a design PASSES if it is RELIABLE, passes the
          fifth leg and is WITHIN; FAILS if it is UNRELIABLE, METASTABLE at either state or
          OUTSIDE; is OPEN otherwise.

   R56.5  THE WORD. A9.4 (i): a design is a SURVIVOR OF THE SEARCH, and nothing more, "only if
          it passes the five legs at the working level AND, at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD, is
          RELIABLE on four legs, passes the fifth leg, and has a ddE that is WITHIN the
          tolerance of its ddE at the working level." Otherwise it is NOT A SURVIVOR, with FAILS
          or OPEN beside it. The survivors are listed by their working-level ddE, and for each
          number of charges the best-ranked survivor is named.

6. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   (a)  THERE ARE SURVIVORS. At most the ten with the largest lowering go to the runs of A11.5,
        item 5 (functions at the charges, and the helium wall), under their own pre-
        registration, then to the cross-check of AMENDMENT 7. No ddE of D56 is quoted as a
        result.

   (b)  SURVIVORS UP TO SOME NUMBER OF CHARGES AND NONE ABOVE IT. That number is reported as
        where designs of 0.25 e stop holding on this grid, by these twenty-seven designs. It is
        not a rule for arrangements.

   (c)  NO DESIGN IS A SURVIVOR. It is reported. No setting of section 2 is changed with this
        result in hand except by a dated amendment that says what is changed and in which
        direction.

   (d)  A design is OPEN because a run is not measured. It stays OPEN. No run is made again in
        its folder.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo. No barrier here is a prediction of a physical
   barrier.

   The charges are bare points and stand for no residue. No basis function stands at a charge
   and no wall is in any run of D56.

   Twenty-seven designs ranked at first order by one set of coefficients. A design the search
   did not keep is not measured, and the ranking at first order is not the ranking of the
   measured ddE.

   A SURVIVOR is not a result: the cross-check and the reference have not been run on it.

   The cap of 0.25 e is where the record shows no sign; it is not a measured limit.
