   C39.4  A1
      A1's detachment energy is negative, which is what the
      pre-registration expected. D38 measured its dianion's highest
      occupied orbital at +0.3539 eV and G38.10 called it NOT
      INTERPRETABLE on that ground. The observable agrees with the
      proxy.

      This number is a bound-state method's description of what the
      record's citation of Dreuw and Cederbaum calls a resonance, one
      that 'can in principle not be correctly computed with bound state
      methods'. Basis-set dependence of it is expected rather than
      anomalous. IT IS NOT PLACED BESIDE THE BOUND VALUES WITHOUT THIS
      LABEL, and it does not retract G38.10.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
==============================================================================

   NO BARRIER (D39.1). Six single points at one geometry. This script carries
   no conversion out of hartree but the one to electron volts that the
   committed convention requires. G39.12 fails the bundle if another appears.

   IT REVISITS NO D34 VERDICT (D39.2). ADD-s1690_qp10000 and ADD-s678_qm10000
   keep whatever reliability classification D34 gave them.

   NO DESIGN CONCLUSION (D39.3). Nothing here says either arrangement is a good
   design or one worth pursuing. They were chosen because D38 already ran their
   dianions.

   IT DOES NOT LICENSE THE ddE TEST (D39.4). D38 licensed that. D39 neither
   extends nor withdraws it.

   ROUTE X CANNOT PROMOTE ITSELF (D39.5). It is a control on route M's
   open-shell reference. Route X's number is not substituted for route M's and
   route M's is not adjusted toward route X's.

   ONE GEOMETRY, AND IT IS THE REACTANT (D39.6). Nothing here says whether
   boundedness changes along the reaction coordinate.

   ONE BASIS (D39.7). def2-SVPD only, on a quantity Probe 1 already showed
   flipping sign between def2-SVP and def2-SVPD.

   IT DOES NOT COMPARE TO EXPERIMENT (D39.8). E7.11 forbids it and nothing here
   is a free energy or a barrier in any case.

   THE PARSER WAS TESTED BEFORE IT READ THESE OUTPUTS. Neither an open-shell
   nor an EOM output had ever been read by this project, so this script was
   exercised against nine synthetic ORCA outputs covering every branch above:
   the clean case, open-shell triples absent, route X refusing its auxiliary
   basis, an SCF that does not converge, A1 coming out bound, a lowest root
   below the singles bar, no roots at all, no spin-contamination block, and a
   badly contaminated reference. Three real bugs were found that way, the worst
   of which made every energy read as absent while the document still reported
   a conclusion. The fixtures are NOT committed: nothing fabricated belongs in
   this record, and they are in the bundle instead.

   NOTHING WAS SHAPED (D39.9). Method, basis, auxiliary treatment, SCF settings
   and geometry are D38's. The two arrangements are D38's. The convention and
   the constant are the committed probes'. The comparators are quoted from
   committed documents with their bases named. The 90 per cent singles bar was
   set in Amendment 1 before these outputs were read.
