A1.1  WHAT WAS FOUND, AND HOW IT WAS VERIFIED

Every POLARISED verdict in DIAGNOSTIC 14 fell on a walled run, every one on the dipole, none on
the Loewdin charge, the gap or the cycle count. No bare run was POLARISED. Every zero-charge wall
run was CLEAN.

ORCA's printed nuclear dipole contribution is identical for the charge-free control, the
zero-charge wall and the bare point charges, and differs only for the walled arm (def2-SVPD,
reactant, atomic units):

   ctl_R            -3.465273  -2.192771   0.557956
   n6neg_wall0_R    -3.465273  -2.192771   0.557956
   n6neg_bare_A_R   -3.465273  -2.192771   0.557956
   n6neg_wall_A_R   -6.000796  -2.861954  14.639237

External charges given through %pointcharges do not enter the printed dipole. Charges carried on
inline Ne> ECP centres do, because those centres are part of the molecular specification. The
difference is exactly the classical dipole of those charges about the substrate's centre of mass:

   nuclear(wall) - nuclear(ctl)   -2.535522  -0.669183  14.081281
   sum_i q_i (r_i - R_com) / a0   -2.535522  -0.669183  14.081281
   residual                        0          0          0

R_com is the mass-weighted centroid of the 24 substrate atoms of the committed frame_42436
geometry for that state, with no contribution from the Ne> centres. The origin is not arbitrary:
the six negatives carry a net -1.34 e at arm A, so the sum depends on it, and only the centre of
mass reproduces ORCA's number.

Removing that term from all 54 of DIAGNOSTIC 14's walled runs: 22 trip D6.3's dipole rule as
printed, and NONE does after. Every corrected magnitude lies between 6.6 and 9.4 Debye, against
the committed control medians of 10.23 (nb 402) and 8.12 (nb 264). The corrected walled dipole of
n6neg at arm A is 8.520 Debye against the bare arm's 8.522 Debye.


A1.2  THE CORRECTION, FROM NOW ON

Where a run carries charge on an inline ECP centre, D6.3's dipole test is applied to the printed
total dipole with sum_i q_i (r_i - R_com) subtracted, R_com as above. No tolerance changes: R_DIP
stays 2.0 and every other D6.3 threshold stays as cs6_audit.py has it. This removes a term that is
exactly computable and is not a property of the density; it does not introduce a new observable.

A run whose charges are external point charges is unaffected, and so is every run with no charge.


A1.3  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CHANGE

It does not change DIAGNOSTIC 14's verdict, and DIAGNOSTIC 14 is not re-read.

Under the corrected rule the n6neg control would have been CLEAN at both bases. At def2-SVP, where
d_shield is -0.0017 kcal/mol, the control would then have passed. At def2-SVPD it would still have
failed, on |d_shield| of 0.2255 (arm A) and 0.2794 (arm B) against a tolerance of 0.05 - an energy
difference, which the dipole does not enter. D14.3 requires the control to hold at BOTH bases, so
the diagnostic stops either way. The correction is recorded here because it is true, not because
it changes the outcome; it changes nothing, which is why it can be adopted without reopening a
reading that has already reported.

Nothing committed before today is overturned. DIAGNOSTIC 14 is the first diagnostic to apply D6.3's
classification to runs carrying charge on inline ECP centres. DIAGNOSTIC 11 and DIAGNOSTIC 13 carry
no CLEAN / POLARISED / STRAINED verdicts at all. In DIAGNOSTIC 6, all 68 verdicts that cite the
dipole also cite the Loewdin charge, the gap or the cycle count, so none rests on the dipole alone,
and its audited sources do not include the Stage 4b or Stage 4c walled runs.

Separately, POLARISED never made a run unreadable: D14.3 withholds a ratio point only when a run is
STRAINED or COLLAPSED. The artefact's only effect was on the n6neg control, which requires CLEAN.


A1.4  WHAT IS MEASURED NEXT, AND WHY

The open question is not additivity. It is why the six negative charges are inert behind the wall
at def2-SVP and not at def2-SVPD. The effect appears only with diffuse functions, it is 4.5 to 5.6
times D11.3's tolerance, and DIAGNOSTIC 14 ran no negative singles - D14.4 records that - so there
is nothing committed that says whether it comes from one site or from six.

The six negative sites of S6-10, each measured alone, at arm B (lambda 0.657534, the larger of the
two effects), bare and behind the wall, at both bases:

   sites      400, 592, 605, 650, 652, 756      the six negatives of the committed ARRANGEMENTS.txt
   arms       bare / Ne-ECP walled / wall-at-zero
   bases      def2-SVPD and def2-SVP
   states     R and TS, Diagnostic 1's committed geometries, nothing moved, in vacuo

   6 sites x 3 arms x 2 states x 2 bases = 72, plus 2 controls per basis = 76 runs.

def2-SVP is carried here, unlike in D14 where it was a control only, because the contrast between
the two bases IS the question. The positive singles are not re-run: DIAGNOSTIC 14 measured them and
they are committed. n6neg is not re-run for the same reason.

GATE. The two controls per basis must reproduce DIAGNOSTIC 14's committed control energies to
1e-6 Eh. If they do not, the harness has drifted and nothing below is read.


A1.5  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

d_shield and the zero-charge correction are as D14.3 defines them, unchanged. Classification is
D6.3 with A1.2's correction. A run that is STRAINED or COLLAPSED makes its site unreadable.

   Dsum = sum over the six of | d_shield({i}) |      each measured alone
   Dset = | d_shield(n6neg) |                        from DIAGNOSTIC 14, committed
   tol  = max( 0.10 x Dsum, 0.05 kcal/mol )          D11.3's tolerance, unchanged

READING 1, per basis - where does it come from?

   ONE SITE   a single site's | d_shield | is within tol of Dset. That site carries the effect.
   SPREAD     no single site is, and Dsum is within tol of Dset or above it.
   NEITHER    Dsum + tol < Dset. The six together do more than the six apart, and the negative
              set is cooperative. This is reported as measured and is not explained here.

READING 2, per basis - does the per-site sum bound the negative set?

   This is D14.3's own rule, unchanged, applied to n6neg against its six singles: BOUNDED if
   Dset <= Dsum + tol, NOT BOUNDED otherwise, NOT READABLE if any of the seven is STRAINED or
   COLLAPSED. DIAGNOSTIC 14 could not reach its Reading 1; this reaches the same question on the
   negative set only, and it does not stand in for the positive one.

READING 3, across the bases - is the basis difference per-site or collective?

   PER-SITE     at least one site's | d_shield | differs between the bases by more than tol.
   COLLECTIVE   no site does, yet Dset differs between the bases by more than tol.
   NEITHER      neither differs by more than tol, which would contradict DIAGNOSTIC 14's own
                n6neg numbers and would mean this harness disagrees with that one.

Each site's nearest-atom distance d_near at the reactant geometry is REPORTED beside its
d_shield, and no criterion is placed on it. ERRATUM3 E3.1 records that distance and magnitude are
not yet separated, and this amendment does not separate them.


A1.6  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

One arrangement, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, one scaling. Nothing about the
positives, which DIAGNOSTIC 14 measured and which are not re-run here. Nothing about mixed-sign
subsets. Nothing about why a basis-set difference should appear at all - this locates the effect,
it does not explain it. The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption
(ERRATUM7), so the walled arm is a contrast and not a truth. In vacuo, no continuum
(STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain unmeasurable until the
OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028.txt is decided.
