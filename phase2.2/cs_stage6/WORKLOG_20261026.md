### Question 1 — are the charges even or saturated

- Neither. Distinct |q| magnitudes per design: S6-2 has 1, S6-3 has 3, S6-5 has 3, S6-10
  has 5, S6-20 has 7. Only S6-2 is uniform, and only because both its sites sit at a bound.
- They are **proportional to −a**, which is the analytic interior optimum of decision 3's
  L2 budget. q/(−a) over S6-20's twenty sites runs 0.0905 to 0.1413, mean 0.1062; the
  closed form predicts the ratio at 0.1047 and Σaq at −19.5611 against the committed
  −19.51748, i.e. **0.22 per cent**. The designs where it agrees least (S6-2 at 12.9 per
  cent, S6-5 at 17.6) are exactly those with a site pinned at a bound, as the derivation
  says to expect.
- **But at the best sites the charge is not a decision variable at all.** 18 of the 20
  top-ranked single sites in `ARRANGEMENTS.txt` admit `[1.00, 1.00]` — lower bound equal to
  upper. That is why S6-P, S6-2, S6-3 and S6-5 all carry a +1.00 e charge; the site admits
  nothing else. S6-10 and S6-20 use none of the top twenty, because Finding 5's one pocket
  plus the 3.25 Å spacing admits at most one of them.
- Consequence, and it changed the plan: a per-site cap below 1.00 e makes those eighteen
  sites infeasible rather than restrained, and `STAGE5_GRID_DEV` records the floors as
  monotone in q, so the cap and the layer cannot be varied independently.
- Noted from the same closed form: the best score is −√(B·Σa²) over the chosen sites, so
  any site with a ≠ 0 improves it. **Under a pure L2 budget "more charges is better" is an
  algebraic identity, not a chemical finding.**

### Question 2 — why n > 5 goes STRAINED

- The gap falls monotonically with Σ|q| (3.40 → 0.61 eV from n = 0 to 20) while Σq² is flat
  from n = 2 (2.000 → 2.048). So Σq² cannot be what moves it.
- Mechanism read off the raw file rather than inferred: with a single +0.80 e at 2.96 Å the
  HOMO falls 2.94 eV and the LUMO falls **6.05 eV**. The LUMO is pulled down twice as hard
  and the gap closes from 3.573 to 0.472 eV. That is the second-order Stark response — the
  low-lying virtuals have most amplitude where the Coulomb well is deepest — amplified by a
  point charge with no Pauli wall for electrons.

### The field calibration — the part that does not depend on Diagnostic 6

- Computed E = 14.39965·q/r² V/Å at every substrate atom from the committed sphere centres,
  and projected onto each reacting bond. The script identifies the bonds from the geometry
  rather than by index and recovers the breaking O–C at 1.495 Å and the forming C–C at
  3.225 Å, matching Finding 7.
- Along the breaking C–O the designs give 0.82 to 1.21 V/Å. Siddiqui & Dubey
  (doi:10.1039/d1cp03978d) measure **wild-type BsCM at 0.20 to 0.53 V/Å** on the same bond,
  with R90A at 0.133 and R90G at 0.095, so Arg90's own share is roughly 0.1 to 0.2.
- Site 1690 is modelled on Arg90 and puts +1.00 e at 2.96 Å in vacuo: **1.64 V/Å, about
  eight times Arg90's share.** Cross-check: a guanidinium +1 e at 4–5 Å gives 0.58–0.90 V/Å
  in vacuum, or 0.14–0.22 at ε_r = 4, which brackets the measured value — so the enzyme
  number and Coulomb's law agree and the difference is distance plus screening, both of
  which this model removes.
- **The useful field saturates and the total does not.** From S6-5 to S6-20 the charge
  nearly doubles, E∥(C–O) falls from 1.214 to 1.152, E∥(C–C) rises one per cent, and the
  mean over all 24 atoms climbs from 1.244 to 1.633.

### Where the judgment went wrong, twice, and what corrected it

- **First recommendation: Σ|q| ≤ 3.0 e.** Built on Σ|q| appearing to order the def2-SVP
  verdicts — CLEAN to 3.000, STRAINED from 4.380 — together with the field saturation and
  the enzyme comparison.
- **Erratum 4 E4.5 broke it.** `cs6_audit.py` opens only tarballs, so 415 completed runs in
  loose `.xz` files were never classified, and four Stage 4c runs at three point charges
  are STRAINED at def2-SVP. The onset is three charges, not ten.
- Read `STAGE4C_CRITERIA.txt`: E5 is "three sites by |a|, charges opposing a, ×1" and E7 is
  E5 reversed. So both carry **Σ|q| = 3.000 at |q| = 1.00** — the same total and the same
  peak as S6-5, which is CLEAN, on the same basis, with the opposite verdict.
- The charges were obtained without a new measurement: the three sphere positions in
  `L1_E5_R/combined.xyz` match, to all eight decimals, the `Ne>` centres Erratum 4 E4.6
  quotes verbatim from `L0_E5_R`, giving **+1, −1, +1**.
- With those four runs included, **Σ|q| does not separate** (CLEAN reaches 3.000, STRAINED
  starts at 3.000); **max|q| runs backwards** (every CLEAN design carries a +1.00, the
  STRAINED ones top out at 0.55); **n does not separate** (E5 is STRAINED at three, S6-5 is
  CLEAN at five). Σq² separates only in a window of 0.013 e², which is the six Stage 6
  designs clustering at their shared budget rather than evidence.
- **Recommendation withdrawn.** No global scalar on the charge vector orders these eight
  points.
