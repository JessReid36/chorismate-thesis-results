HOW FAR MUST A DESIGNED CHARGE STAND OFF BEFORE A BARE POINT CHARGE IS A FAITHFUL MODEL OF IT?

Written and committed before the runs. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. No continuum anywhere,
per STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027.


D11.1  WHY

Everything downstream of the charge model assumes a point charge at about 2.9 A is a valid stand-in
for a catalytic group: the a coefficients, the grid's admitted sites, the budget, the search. Senn
& Thiel (anie.200802019, held) state the hazard in terms Stage 6's designs meet on both axes at
once:

   "While this artifact is always present to some extent when a point charge interacts with a
    polarizable charge distribution, it becomes more severe the closer the point charge approaches
    the QM density and the more spatially flexible the density is."

   "Larger basis sets, which include polarization and possibly diffuse functions, are more flexible
    and hence more prone to overpolarization."

Stage 6's closest sphere sits at 2.85 to 2.96 A and the diagnostics run in ma-def2-SVP. Five further
held sources corroborate the phenomenon (1.1462041 Laio; ct050123f Laino; acs.jctc.9b01162 Marefat
Khah; ct4c01698 Li & Chan; bs.mie.2016.05.014 Ryde). The Lennard-Jones walls do not shield anything:
Finding 4 is that the LJ term never enters the QM Hamiltonian, so to the electrons every sphere is
a bare point charge.

Diagnostic 10 found, in the continuum-free arm, that the substrate's response OPPOSES the designed
field and grows with the number of charges - +0.37, +8.19 and +20.71 kcal/mol against classical
estimates of -4.23, -9.21 and -16.00 for S6-P, S6-2 and S6-20. Whether that response is physics or
overpolarisation is the question here, and nothing downstream is interpretable until it is settled.


D11.2  WHAT IS RUN

One charge, walked outward along one axis, in two charge models. 52 single points, no geometry
moves, all on Diagnostic 1's committed R and TS.

THE AXIS, derived from committed files alone. The substrate atom nearest to site 1690 at R (atom 7,
an O, at 2.9596 A), and the unit vector from it to site 1690. Rungs:

   d250   2.50 A      inside the committed arrangements
   dref   2.9596 A    site 1690's committed coordinates EXACTLY - this rung is S6-P
   d350   3.50 A
   d450   4.50 A
   d600   6.00 A
   d1500  15.00 A     the far-field zero control

The same positions are used at R and at TS, because a designed charge does not move with the
substrate.

THE TWO MODELS.  "bare" places the charge with %pointcharges, as every Stage 6 design does.
"wall" places it as a coreless ECP centre in the coordinate block, copied byte for byte from the
committed, already-run phase2.2/cs_stage4c/L0_E5_R/job.inp - ORCA's own description is "coreless
ECP center with (optional) point charge", and the ECP's projectors supply the short-range repulsion
a real catalytic group would have. STAGE6_ERRATUM7 records that these are Marefat Khah's published
Ne parameters used OUTSIDE the octet assumption they were published under. That caveat is carried
here, not resolved; it is a reason to treat the walled arm as a contrast, not as truth.

  main ladder     5 rungs x {bare, wall} x {R, TS}, q = +1, ma-def2-SVP        20 runs
  C-A1.1          5 rungs x {R, TS}, ECP centre carrying q = 0                 10 runs
  C-A1.4          dref and d450 x {bare, wall} x {R, TS}, q = -1                8 runs
  C-A1.5          dref and d450 x {bare, wall} x {R, TS}, q = +1, def2-SVP      8 runs

D11.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

GATE 0.  Every input file must match the committed MANIFEST.sha256 byte for byte.

GATE 1.  The ma-def2-SVP control must reproduce Diagnostic 1's committed -836.227913439 and
-836.199397816 Eh to 1e-6.

GATE 2.  The dref bare rung is byte-identical to Diagnostic 10's S6-P_R_vac and S6-P_TS_vac inputs -
same substrate.xyz, same pc.pc, same job.inp - so it must reproduce D10's ddE of -3.857852 kcal/mol
to 0.01. If it does not, the harness has drifted and nothing below is read.

C-A1.1, THE WALL MUST BE INERT ON ITS OWN. An ECP centre carrying no charge must give
|ddE| <= 0.30 kcal/mol at every rung. Diagnostic 9 found the wall inert in general (all thirteen
wall-only runs CLEAN); this re-establishes it on this axis. A rung that fails has its walled result
VOIDED and reported as such, not reinterpreted.

THE READING.  Let d_shield(d) = ddE(bare, d) - ddE(wall, d).

   r_safe is the SMALLEST rung at which BOTH hold, AT THAT RUNG AND AT THE NEXT ONE OUTWARD:
     (i)   |d_shield| <= max( 0.10 x |ddE(bare)| , 0.05 kcal/mol )
     (ii)  |d_shield| is non-increasing outward

   (i) is RELATIVE by design. An absolute tolerance would be met automatically at large separation,
   where every quantity is small, and would certify almost any distance as safe. The 0.05 kcal/mol
   term is a numerical floor, not the test. Requiring two successive rungs stops a single crossing
   from counting.

   A THIRD CONDITION WAS CONSIDERED AND DEMOTED, before any of these runs existed. The ratio
   ddE(bare)/ddE(classical) - "classical" being the same charge acting on the control's frozen
   Loewdin density - was going to be required to plateau to within 10 % between the two rungs. It
   is instead REPORTED AS A QUALIFIER. The reason is that it measures whether the physics has
   settled with distance, which is a different question from whether the charge model is faithful;
   AND-ing it would let a poor classical reference at short range void a genuine r_safe, and the
   rehearsal showed that failure mode is easy to reach. Shielding is what r_safe is named for and
   what it is decided by. The qualifier is printed with r_safe, and if the ratio has not plateaued
   the result says so and says the magnitude at r_safe is not to be treated as converged.

   The guard the third condition was meant to supply - bare and walled agreeing while both are
   wrong - is carried by C-A1.1 (the wall must be inert on its own), by the two-successive-rung
   requirement, and by A3.4 (the oracle must return the classical answer at 15 A).

   r_safe <= 2.9596 A    The committed arrangements sit at or beyond it. Overpolarisation is not
                         the explanation for Stage 6's behaviour and the surrogate's failure is
                         physics. The grid stands.
   r_safe > 2.9596 A     The grid must be rebuilt at that stand-off and every a recomputed. No
                         design measured inside r_safe is interpretable.
   r_safe not reached    Bare point charges are not a usable model at any separation tested, and
                         the charge model - not the grid spacing - is what has to change.

C-A1.4, THE SIGN CONTROL. Dreuw & Cederbaum (cr0104227) warn that at short range "the monoanion-
point charge system represents a resonance state that can in principle not be correctly computed
with bound state methods". Chorismate is a dianion and S6-2 and S6-20 carry negative charges. If the
pathology is that, it appears on the q = -1 arm and not on q = +1. Reported, not scored - three
designs cannot separate it from the sphere count.

C-A1.5, THE BASIS CONTROL. Senn & Thiel predict d_shield SHRINKS with a compact basis. If it does,
def2-SVP conceals the artefact rather than being free of it, and the surrogate's apparent success
in that one condition is explained. Reported, not scored.

A3.4, THE FAR-FIELD ZERO. A charge at 15 A must agree with its classical estimate to 0.1 kcal/mol.
This is the control that catches an oracle which fails to return nothing when nothing is there.


D11.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

The environment. These runs are in vacuo and the control's density is unbound, HOMO +1.2269 eV;
STAGE6_PROBE1_20261027.txt begins on that separately. Additivity, which needs more than one charge.
Anything about whether a design is catalytic - one charge at one site is not a design, and the dref
rung reproduces an arrangement already measured rather than proposing a new one. And the Ne ECP
parameters remain used outside their published assumption.
