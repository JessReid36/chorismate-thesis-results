The design is NOT DECIDED: why, what was measured, and what is done
================================================================================

Written 24 November 2026, after D53's result (results commit 42e776db, 24 November, 07:01). A
note. It changes no pre-registration, no result, no input and no output, and it gives no reading
that D53's result does not give.

N1.1   THE OUTCOME. D53's result: 82 of 82 runs measured; "THE SECOND LEVEL IS READ."; of the 14
       (arrangement, state), HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS 12 and NOT DECIDED 2. The twelve are every
       arrangement of positive charges only (one to six charges of 0.25 e) at R and TS. The two
       are the design (d11) at R and at TS. By section 5 of D53's pre-registration this is
       outcome (f): "NONE OF THE ABOVE: at least one (arrangement, state) is NOT DECIDED and
       every other one HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS." and "If the design is among them at either state,
       the question of this diagnostic is open, and a dated note says what is done about the
       runs that failed (a run is not made again in its folder)." This is that note. No run of
       D53 failed: all are measured.

N1.2   WHY THE DESIGN IS NOT DECIDED. In 3 runs, all of the design at wB97X-V (DI6_WXV_F_d11_R,
       DI6_WXV_F_d11_TS, DI6_WXV_N_d11_TS), ORCA printed at the first step of the SCF "Small
       HOMO/LUMO gap" and, under it, "LevelShift set to 1.000 Eh" (in the third, 0.445 Eh). The
       reader, which is D50's line for line, withholds the gap leg where ORCA prints that it set
       a level shift, and D50's pre-registration reads: "DID NOT TAKE: measured, no leg fires,
       none withheld, and it did not leave the atoms. Otherwise not decided." So the two cells
       of the design at wB97X-V are not decided, and with them the two levels together. The
       result says so: "d11 at R: WXV: gap withheld." No other leg fired in those runs and none
       other was withheld.

N1.4   POST HOC, AND NOT A READING. A shift of 1.000 Eh is 27.2 eV. The gaps those two runs
       print are 2.2618 and 2.8901 eV, so the printed orbital energies do not carry the shift;
       both are above the 1.0 eV of the gap leg. Every one of the 82 outputs prints "CNVShift
       .... on" with "LevelShift .... 0.2500": level shifting is on in every run by ORCA's
       default, and the committed rule keys on the further message only. None of this changes
       the word: by the committed rule the cells are not decided.

N1.5   CHECKS MADE AFTER THE RESULT (Claude, 24 November, on clean checkouts of the pushed
       commits). The committed reader, run again on the committed outputs at the commit the
       harvest read from (5278a535), gives the result and the three tables byte for byte. 199 of
       199 input checksums and 374 of 374 output checksums hold. The independent reading written
       from the pre-registration before any run existed differs in 4 places, all on the two
       cells of N1.2: it lacked the clause on the level shift. With the clause it differs
       nowhere. The committed reader is right by its rule. The rehearsal of D53 had no case in
       which ORCA sets a level shift; it should have had one.

N1.6   HARTREE-FOCK IS NOT A CLEAN BRACKET. The Hartree-Fock control with functions lies ABOVE
       the plain control: +0.1287 eV at R and +1.0725 eV at TS (d53_controls.tsv). More basis
       functions cannot raise the energy of the same solution. The plain controls were UNSTABLE
       on their first analysis and went to solutions with <S**2> = 0.53 (R) and 1.44 (TS); the
       controls with functions were STABLE on theirs and stayed. At TS all seven Hartree-Fock
       runs with charges ended with <S**2> between 1.34 and 1.43 and are read against a control
       that did not. The words of R53.7 stand as committed; the comparison behind them is not
       like for like, and it changes no word of R53.6. It also shows that the stability analysis
       can report STABLE where a lower solution exists.

N1.7   wB97X-V AT THE TRANSITION STATE. Both controls at TS and s1 at TS were UNSTABLE on their
       first analysis and STABLE on their last, with <S**2> = 0.0608 (plain control), 0.0605
       (with functions) and 0.0241 (s1). The two controls agree with each other.
