STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 58: the force each survivor of D56 adds to the substrate, and the
first-order maps of that force and of the hold on the last electron. PRE-REGISTRATION.

25 November 2026. Claude drafted this text; it takes effect when Jessica commits it, with the
inputs of d58/ and the programs cs6_d58_prepare.py, cs6_d58.py and cs6_d58_harvest.sh, before
any job of it is submitted. It is hers to change before she commits it, and the tolerances of
section 3 are hers in particular.

It names no design a result, changes no word of D56 and adopts nothing. Nothing committed is
edited by it.


1. WHY

AMENDMENT 13, A13.4 item 2: "THE FORCE IS MEASURED FOR EVERY SURVIVOR." Item 3: "THE FORCE MAP
(F) IS MEASURED AND CHECKED." Item 4: "THE MAP OF THE HOLD ON THE ELECTRON (E) IS FORMED AND
CHECKED." The forms these runs need were tried by FORMS TEST 1, whose result is committed before
these inputs are.


2. WHAT IS RUN

All at the working level, CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD, on frame 42436, in vacuo, with D56's own
geometries and D56's own point charges. 186 runs in eight jobs; each job makes its runs in order
under the cap given, and no run is made a second time in its folder.

   - G (38 runs, jobs G1 to G4, cap 3600 s): an energy with its gradient for each of the 18
     survivors that the committed d56_survivors.tsv names, at R and at TS, with the design's
     committed pc.pc; and the substrate without charges at R and at TS. The input is FORMS TEST
     1's SV_engrad_R with D56's charges.
   - M (144 runs, jobs M1 to M3, cap 900 s): the substrate without charges at R with one of its
     24 atoms moved 0.01 bohr (0.005292 A) along x, y or z, either way; each run is followed by
     orca_vpot at the 2626 sites (d33a/points.xyz). The input is D33's committed sp_42436_R; the
     potential step is D33's committed one.
   - E (4 runs, job E1, cap 1800 s): D56's committed inputs SV_C_ctl_R, SV_C_ctl_TS, SV_C_bare_R
     and SV_C_bare_TS, unchanged, each followed by orca_vpot at the 2626 sites.

THE STEP. 0.01 bohr: small enough that the error of a central difference is far below the
tolerance of section 3, large enough that the noise of a converged density at TightSCF divided
by twice the step stays below it too. Claude's reasoning, not a measurement; the check of
section 3 is what decides whether the map holds.


3. THE READINGS, FIXED NOW

R58.1 THE RUNS. For each, whether it ended normally, its final energy, and for M and E whether
orca_vpot gave 2626 potentials. The controls G_C_R, G_C_TS and the four E runs are set against
D56's committed energies of the same inputs, REPRODUCES within 1e-6 Eh. The potentials of
E_ctl_R are set against D33's committed sp_42436_R/vpot.out and the largest difference is
reported.

R58.2 THE GRADIENT EACH SURVIVOR ADDS, at R and at TS: STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt's "G_add(X) = sqrt(
(1/3N) sum_i |g_i(D) - g_i(0)|^2 )" computed by the committed cs6_analyse.g_add from the
design's gradient and the control's; the largest single component; and beside them the gradient
the sphere's Lennard-Jones shell would add at the design's sites, by the derivative of the
committed cs5_grid.lj_energy, and G_add with it. Reported; it decides nothing, as A13.4 item 2
has it.

R58.3 THE MAP F. F[i, c] = (V_i(atom moved +) - V_i(atom moved -)) / (2 x step), for site i and
coordinate c, from the potentials of the M runs. The first-order added gradient of a design is
the sum over its charges of q_i F[i, :]. THE CHECK, at R, for each of the 18 survivors, against
the direct added gradient of R58.2 (the design's gradient minus the control's, the charges' part
alone): the relative RMS, RMS(map - direct) / RMS(direct) over the 72 components, and the
relative difference of the largest component. THE MAP PASSES if both are at most 0.25 for every
survivor; FAILS otherwise; NOT FORMED if an M run, its potentials or a direct gradient is
missing.

R58.4 THE MAP E. E[i] = V(r_i) of the control with one electron removed minus V(r_i) of the
dianion control, at R and at TS, from the E runs, in eV per e; to first order the energy to
remove the last electron with a design is VDE0 + sum_i q_i E[i], VDE0 from the E runs. THE
CHECK: against the 54 energies to remove the electron that D56 measured at the working level for
its 27 designs at R and TS (committed d56_designs.tsv). THE MAP PASSES if the largest |predicted
- measured| is at most 0.15 eV; FAILS otherwise; NOT FORMED if a run or its potentials is
missing or a control does not reproduce D56's.

THE TOLERANCES, AND THEIR DIRECTION. 0.25 for F: D56's measured lowerings were 1.03 to 1.17
times the first-order score, and a force is expected to stray from first order by about as much
or more. 0.15 eV for E: between the two basis sets D56's energies to remove the electron for the
designs of ten and twelve charges differ by 0.06 to 0.07 eV (AMENDMENT 13, A13.1), and a map
should not be held to less than that twice. Both are Claude's proposal. A looser tolerance makes
it more likely that a map is used and its rule enters the second search, which acts against a
lower frozen barrier; a tighter one, the reverse.

THE TABLES. The reader writes d58.tsv (the runs), d58_forces.tsv (R58.2), and, when formed,
F_f42436_d58.tsv and E_f42436_d58.tsv with the word of each map in their headers.


4. THE GATES

GD.1 the pre-registration given is this committed document; GD.2 the programs are the committed
ones (cs6_d58_prepare.py, cs6_d58.py, cs6_d58_harvest.sh, and the committed cs6_analyse.py,
cs5_grid.py and cs5_amatrix.py whose functions it uses); GD.3 every input of d58/ is the file
the committed cs6_d58_prepare.py forms from the committed files; GD.4 every output that exists
echoes the committed input beside it; GD.5 the committed cs5_grid.lj_energy, with D56's
geometries, gives the committed L_f42436.tsv within 1e-5 kcal/mol at all 2626 sites; GD.6 the
analytic gradient of the shell equals the central difference of that function. If a gate fails
the reader writes nothing.


5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW

A map that PASSES may be used by the second search of A13.4 item 6, with a limit or a margin
that the search's own pre-registration fixes. A map that FAILS or is NOT FORMED is reported and
not used, and no rule on that quantity enters a search; A13.4 item 6 says what follows if
neither passes. R58.2 decides nothing. No run is made again in its folder; a run that is not
measured stays so.


6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, at the working level. The gradients are of bare charges;
the shell's part is computed, not run. A map that passes agrees with direct runs on D56's 18
survivors (F) and 27 designs (E); it is first order, and it is not a measurement of any design
the second search places. No relaxation is made here.


7. WHAT WAS KNOWN WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN, WHAT CLAUDE EXPECTS, AND THE COST

Known: every committed result to D56, AMENDMENT 13 and its NOTE 1, and FORMS TEST 1's result.
Not known: any gradient of a D56 design; any potential at a displaced geometry; any potential of
the control with one electron removed. CLAUDE EXPECTS the map F to pass, the direct gradient
being somewhat larger than first order as the lowering was, and is less sure of the map E:
removing an electron changes the substrate more than a charge of 0.25 e does, and the designs of
twelve charges sit where the first order may be poorest. COST, AN ESTIMATE: G runs of 5 to 20
minutes, M runs of about 2 minutes and their potentials seconds, by D33's and D34's committed
logs; eight jobs that run side by side, each of a few hours. Claude's estimates have been too
low. DIRECTION of the diagnostic itself: none on any barrier.
