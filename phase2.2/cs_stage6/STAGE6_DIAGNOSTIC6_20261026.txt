STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 6, PRE-REGISTERED 2026-10-26
A wavefunction-health audit of every committed ORCA output on the chorismate dianion

This file is pre-registered. It is never edited. Changes go into a dated amendment or erratum.


D6.0  WHY, AND A DISCLOSURE THAT BELONGS FIRST

Diagnostics 1 to 5 read energies. Not one of them read the SCF cycle count or the HOMO-LUMO gap. Those two
fields were in the committed frozen outputs from the first design run, and they say this, at def2-SVP, with no
diffuse function anywhere in the calculation:

   design   charges   SCF cycles R/TS   gap R / TS (eV)   max |Loewdin|   dipole R
   S6-C        0        12 / 16          3.400 / 2.286       0.384          8.39
   S6-P        1        15 / 16          3.190 / 2.134       0.384          6.88
   S6-2        2        15 / 16          2.287 / 1.589       0.394          6.49
   S6-3        3        16 / 16          2.129 / 1.508       0.398          7.31
   S6-5        5        12 / 21          1.756 / 1.205       0.430          6.68
   S6-10      10        36 / 40          0.854 / 0.980       0.448          8.21
   S6-20      20        42 / 47          0.610 / 0.924       0.455          9.15

The cycle count triples and the gap falls below 1 eV. It was recorded and never read.

THE DISCLOSURE: the four thresholds D6.3 fixes were chosen AFTER those outputs had been inspected. This is
therefore not a blind test, and it is not presented as one. Three things are done about it instead of claiming
otherwise. Every threshold is anchored to the charge-free control runs of the SAME basis-set size in the same
corpus, not to an absolute value picked freely. The control distributions are printed in the result, so the
separation can be checked by eye. And a specificity check is printed: how many of the charge-free controls the
classifier itself flags, which should be zero and is reported whatever it is.


D6.1  WHAT IS READ, AND FROM WHERE

Every *.tar.xz committed in the results repo is opened and every ORCA output inside it is read. NO CALCULATION
IS PERFORMED AND NO NETWORK IS USED. A run is kept only if it is this system and completed: a final single
point energy present, ORCA's own printed electron count equal to 118, exactly 59 occupied orbitals, terminated
normally, and no "SCF NOT CONVERGED". Files named *.pbs.out are excluded - they are batch logs, and an earlier
draft of this audit mistook twenty of them for failed calculations.

Per run, only fields ORCA itself printed: the last SCF cycle count, the HOMO-LUMO gap from the orbital-energy
block, the dipole magnitude, the largest Loewdin charge on a LIGHT atom (Na, K, Mg, Ca, Li, Zn, Fe, Cl, Br, I
excluded, because a metal may legitimately carry a large one), the number of point charges ORCA confirmed
reading, and the number of geometry-optimisation cycles.

Three separations are made before anything is classified.

  - Runs carrying more than 200 point charges are QM/MM calculations whose charges are an MM environment, not a
    designed arrangement. One such run reads 55,656 of them. They are excluded from every table.
  - Geometry optimisations are reported apart from single points. Once the geometry has moved, a charge-free
    control is not like-for-like, so their verdicts carry much less weight and must not be quoted as collapses.
  - Controls are grouped by basis-set size, which separates def2-SVP (264 functions), ma-def2-SVP (328),
    def2-SVPD (402) and the composite methods B97-3c (358), r2SCAN-3c (374) and HF-3c (88). A Loewdin charge is
    basis-dependent, so it is used ONLY as a difference against a same-basis control and never as an absolute.


D6.2  WHAT IS NOT DONE

No calculation is submitted. No criterion, threshold, tolerance, method, arrangement or reported number
changes. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt and PHASE22_PLAN.txt section 2.2 are untouched. Every
committed result file stands as committed, DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt and DIAGNOSTIC5_RESULT.txt included.

This diagnostic does not say what the correct barrier is for any run. It says only which committed numbers
should not be quoted.


D6.3  THE CLASSIFICATION, FIXED BEFORE THE CORPUS WAS SCANNED IN FULL

For each run, against the MEDIAN of the charge-free controls at the same basis-set size:

   MOVED   max |Loewdin| at least 0.25 e above the control, OR dipole at least 2.0 times it
   SICK    gap below 1.0 eV AND below half the control's own gap, OR at least 2.0 times the control's cycles

   CLEAN       neither
   POLARISED   MOVED but not SICK. THIS IS NOT A FAULT. It is what a strong external field is supposed to do,
               and it is named so that it is not mistaken for one.
   STRAINED    SICK but not MOVED. A near-degeneracy: single-reference closed-shell DFT is not reliable there.
   COLLAPSED   both. The density has moved and the SCF is unwell - the signature of density captured by a
               positive point charge with no Pauli wall for electrons, which is Finding 4's defect and the
               effect Marefat Khah et al. JCTC 2020 doi:10.1021/acs.jctc.9b01162 call unphysical leaking of
               the electron density.

The conjunction matters and was arrived at by testing. A Loewdin criterion applied ALONE classified 616 of 832
def2-SVP point-charge single points as collapsed, including runs with a 3.85 eV gap converging in 14 cycles -
that is ordinary polarisation, not a failure, and a classifier that condemns it is wrong. Requiring relocation
AND an unwell SCF reduces that to 54 and leaves the healthy-but-polarised runs correctly named.

The gap test carries both an absolute floor and a control-relative one for the same reason: the B97-3c controls
sit at a median gap of 1.468 eV and the r2SCAN-3c controls at 1.777 eV, so an absolute 1.0 eV floor alone would
condemn those methods for behaving the way they natively behave.


D6.4  THE READINGS

  (a) THE SPECIFICITY CHECK. How many charge-free controls are classified anything but CLEAN. Zero is the only
      result that licenses the rest of the table; any other number is reported and said to mean the thresholds
      are too tight.

  (b) VERDICTS BY BASIS SET, for point-charge single points.

  (c) AT HOW MANY CHARGES DOES EACH BASIS COLLAPSE. The number of point charges in the collapsed runs of each
      basis. This is the reading that answers why the representation passed its own tests and then failed, and
      it is the reason this diagnostic exists.

  (d) INDICATORS AGAINST CHARGE COUNT AT def2-SVP, banded. def2-SVP has no diffuse functions and is the basis
      every committed Stage 6 design was evaluated at, so its quiet failure is the one that matters most.

  (e) EVERY COLLAPSED OR STRAINED SINGLE POINT, named, with its archive, charge count, basis and reason.


D6.5  WHAT THIS CANNOT TEST

Nothing is computed, so no hypothesis the committed outputs do not already contain can be tested. It cannot say
what the right answer is for a run it flags. It does not establish a usable charge model - a Pauli wall for
electrons is the fix and it is untested here. The thresholds are post-hoc, as D6.0 states. The geometry
optimisations are classified against controls that are not like-for-like. And a run called CLEAN is clean only
on these four indicators: it is not thereby converged, nor basis-converged, nor right.
