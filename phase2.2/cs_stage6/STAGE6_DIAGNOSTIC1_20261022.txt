Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 1: does a diffuse basis change what the frozen single points say? Written 22 October 2026,
after the 22 frozen single points were committed and measured and before any relaxation input is regenerated.

THIS IS A DIAGNOSTIC AND NOT A METHOD CHANGE. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt fixes B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP for everything Stage 6
reports and that stays fixed. No number these eight runs produce replaces a reported number, enters a fit, or
changes a criterion, tolerance or threshold. They exist to say whether a reported number can be believed.

D1.1 WHY

STAGE6_FINDINGS_20261022.md Finding 8: the bare chorismate dianion has a HOMO of +2.278 eV at the reactant geometry
in vacuo at def2-SVP. A positive HOMO means the highest occupied orbital is not bound - the species is metastable to
electron detachment - and the calculation only looks well-behaved because def2-SVP has no diffuse functions for an
electron to leave through. Every design the optimiser produced makes it worse, to +6.404 eV at S6-10, except the
single positive sphere at Arg90's own position, which binds it at -0.243 eV.

The SCF energy of a basis-set-confined species is still a well-defined variational minimum within that basis, and
differences at the same geometry and basis are still meaningful as such. What is in doubt is the reading. An applied
field acts on loosely-held density exactly where a too-small basis misrepresents it, and the designs differ in
precisely that applied field. So part of what Finding 1 reads as polarisation - the 10 to 26 per cent by which every
design beats its own linear score, growing with n - may instead be the basis holding unbound density differently in
different fields. That is a question about the numbers already in hand, and it can be settled for the cost of eight
single points.

D1.2 WHAT IS RUN

The committed frozen single points of four arrangements at both geometries, byte-identical except for the basis:

   def2-SVP  ->  ma-def2-SVP

minimally augmented def2-SVP, which adds diffuse s and p functions on the heavy atoms; the standard cheap choice
for anions, and the smallest change that lets loosely-held density go. def2/J remains the auxiliary, which is what
ORCA documents for the ma-def2 sets. Everything else is copied byte for byte - the same geometry, the same spheres,
the same charges, the same force field - so the basis is the only thing that differs. cs6_diffuse.py checks that:
it requires each generated job.inp to differ from its source in exactly the basis token and nothing else, and each
combined.xyz and site.ORCAFF.prms to be copied byte for byte, against relax/INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256.

The four arrangements, chosen to span the range rather than to be representative:
   S6-C    the bare dianion, the worst case for an unbound HOMO and the reference every effect is measured against
   S6-P    the only arrangement that binds it (HOMO -0.243 eV), a bare +1 at Arg90's position
   S6-2    the smallest sphere pair, +1 and -1, HOMO +2.809 eV
   S6-20   the largest design, the most added gradient, HOMO +4.550 eV
Their sum q*V_R spans -120 to +44 kcal/mol, which is the full range of Finding 9.

Eight single points of a few minutes each, against the fourteen six-hour relaxations they are meant to inform.

D1.3 WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

The quantity is ddE(frozen) for S6-P, S6-2 and S6-20, each against the S6-C of the same basis, and the HOMO of all
four.

  THE FINDINGS STAND if the ddE values move by less than the 15 per cent decision O6 already allows for
  method-and-basis uncertainty, and if the ORDER and the SIGN of the measured-minus-predicted gap are unchanged -
  that is, if every design still beats its own linear score and still does so more at larger n. The frozen numbers
  are then a basis-set-converged enough reading of a metastable species, the polarisation interpretation of
  Finding 1 holds, and Findings 1 to 6 can be used as they are.

  THE FINDINGS ARE QUALIFIED if the ddE values move by more than 15 per cent but the order and sign survive. The
  magnitudes are then basis-dependent and would be reported with that stated, while the qualitative conclusions -
  conservative score, additivity, L exact, the sphere buying nothing at fixed geometry - stand.

  THE FINDINGS ARE NOT USABLE AS THEY STAND if the order or the sign changes: if a design's gap reverses, or the
  growth with n disappears. Finding 1's polarisation reading would then be wrong, part of it being basis artefact,
  and the frozen half would need rerunning at a basis that binds the species before any of it informs G0 or kappa.

No threshold here is new: the 15 per cent is decision O6's existing method-and-basis figure, used as a yardstick
rather than as a criterion, and nothing is accepted or rejected on the strength of it.

D1.4 WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

The geometries. These are single points at frame 42436's committed R and TS, which are stationary points of the
full QM/MM enzyme system and not of the bare substrate; Finding 7's 33.8 kcal/mol descent is untouched by a basis
change and is a separate problem. Nor does a larger basis make a gas-phase dianion bound in reality - it makes the
calculation honest about it, which may mean a worse-looking HOMO rather than a better one. That is the point: the
diagnostic is to find out, not to improve the number.

DIRECTION

None. No decision, threshold, tolerance, criterion, arrangement or reported quantity changes, and the stage's method
remains B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP. What changes is whether the numbers already reported are read as settled or as
provisional, and D1.3 fixes which before the runs go in.
