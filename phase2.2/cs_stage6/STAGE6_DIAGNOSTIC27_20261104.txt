D27.1  WHY THIS IS RUN

Three things in the committed record point at the same gap and none of them closes it.

(a) DIAGNOSTIC 26's result (STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC26_RESULT_20261104.txt, sha256
    f97e9ec18f6ccd4620b9d9da4c8cb116b495660672cdf856de5c58a5a6ebfcfd) found the fraction of the
    def2-SVP / def2-SVPD gap closed by a quantum wall to be SET-DEPENDENT - 0.2799 with He, 0.4694
    with Ne, 0.7272 with Ar - straddling both of its own decision boundaries. Its verdict is that
    def2-TZVPD is NOT avoidable. This diagnostic is that run.

(b) DIAGNOSTIC 26's GATE 7 FAILED, committed as a failure. Its quantity B, from the committed
    def2-SVPD run, is -6.134121 kcal/mol; DIAGNOSTIC 24's print of the same vector and the same
    basis is -6.1348; the difference is +0.000679 kcal/mol, which is 1.083e-06 Eh. The cause is
    identified here and is not a defect in either run: DIAGNOSTIC 24 was computed in PYSCF
    (d24/d24_smear.py, "from pyscf import gto, dft", dft.RKS(m).density_fit(), xc = 'b3lyp',
    conv_tol = 1e-8), and every production run of this project, d16 among them, is ORCA 6.0.1 with
    B3LYP D3BJ def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF. The two programs do not agree on the absolute energy of
    this molecule and are not expected to: on byte-identical geometries they differ by -241.11
    kcal/mol, which is a functional-variant and dispersion and integral-treatment difference, not
    an error. What matters is how much of that survives subtraction, and the answer is stated here
    because it is the reason DIAGNOSTIC 26's reading was not invalidated by the mixed engines:

        absolute energy, reactant                 -241.11      kcal/mol apart
        barrier, TS minus R                         +1.900848  kcal/mol apart
        ddE, barrier minus control barrier          -0.000679  kcal/mol apart

    The double difference at fixed geometry cancels the program change by about five and a half
    orders of magnitude. The ratio is not quoted more precisely than that on purpose: its
    denominator is DIAGNOSTIC 24's four-decimal print, so the third significant figure of any such
    ratio is not real. The
    D3BJ term cancels exactly, because the two arms share a geometry and because a point charge
    carries no dispersion coefficient. DIAGNOSTIC 26's reading therefore stands as committed; the
    mixed-engine comparison contributes 0.13 per cent of the 0.517621 kcal/mol gap it measures.
    This diagnostic nonetheless re-runs the def2-SVP arm in ORCA, so that no quantity entering a
    thesis figure is a comparison across two programs.

(c) The third vector in DIAGNOSTIC 24's own log is the one that is not a small correction.
    Quoted from d24/d24_smear.log (sha256
    a79a9ccc18cbe6ba42482dd6a8354eb7d5d2b583a26cf3fb4e94c3bdff24cb8a), point-charge rows:

        vector         ddE_SVP     ddE_SVPD   |inter-basis|
        1.3000e_n10    -5.6165      -6.1348        0.5184
        2.8800e_n10   -13.1924     -14.2771        1.0847
        5.6433e_n20   -24.0704     -10.5550       13.5154

    The first two scale together. The third does not: the two bases disagree by 13.5154 kcal/mol
    and by a factor of 2.28, and they do not agree on the size of the effect at all. S6-20 carries
    Sum|q| = 5.7600 e, which is that regime. Until one of -24.07 and -10.56 is shown to be the
    artefact, no quantity at the top of the ladder can be quoted at either basis.
    (The first row's gap is 0.5184 in the log and 0.5183 in STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC24_NOTE1_20261103.txt.
    Both are correct prints of the same run: the log subtracts before rounding and the note
    subtracts two figures already rounded to four places. This diagnostic quotes the log, and the
    discrepancy is one more reason the quantity wants recomputing at full precision in ORCA.) DIAGNOSTIC 24 is
    marked EXPLORATORY and unquotable (STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC24_EXPLORATORY_20261103.txt, sha256
    097cfdf131cc782a293fb4dc5b087524dbec3edd8e62673ceda38facad14162c, "Nothing here may be quoted
    as a Stage 6 result in its present form"), so this is not a committed finding and is cited
    here only as the reason to run.

D27.3  THE GATES, WHICH RUN BEFORE ANY NUMBER IS READ

GATE 0  PROVENANCE. Every copied input is byte-identical to the committed file it came from, by
        sha256 against the five values above. A mismatch stops the reading and names the file.

GATE 1  THE GROUPS DIFFER ONLY IN THE BASIS. For each of the eight run names, the three job.inp
        files are identical after the basis token is removed. A difference stops the reading and
        prints the first differing line. This is what licenses calling the result a basis effect.

GATE 2  THE CONTROL AND THE CHARGED ARM DIFFER ONLY BY THE CHARGES. Within each basis, each
        charged job.inp equals its control job.inp plus exactly the line %pointcharges "pc.pc", and
        no control directory contains a pc.pc file.

GATE 3  THE COMMITTED PRODUCTION NUMBER REPRODUCES. The def2-SVPD / 1.3000e_n10 arm reproduces the
        committed d16 ddE of -6.134121 kcal/mol to GATE_KCAL = 1.0e-4 kcal/mol, computed as
        (barrier of that arm) minus (barrier of the def2-SVPD control). This is the strongest gate
        available, because the charge file is byte-identical to d16's and the geometry, basis,
        program and keyword line are the same; it validates the whole input chain against a number
        already in the repository. A failure stops the reading entirely and nothing else is read.

GATE 4  THE CONTROL BARRIER REPRODUCES. The def2-SVPD control reproduces the committed d16 control
        barrier of 18.332256 kcal/mol to GATE_KCAL, and its two FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY values
        reproduce d16/def2-SVPD/ctl_R and ctl_TS to GATE_EH = 1e-6 Eh, the figure cs11_vde.py,
        cs6_d20.py and cs6_d21.py already use.

GATE 5  TERMINATION AND CONVERGENCE. Every one of the 24 runs contains ORCA TERMINATED NORMALLY and
        reports SCF convergence. A run that does not is not committed; a note about it is. If any
        def2-TZVPD run fails to converge, the def2-TZVPD column is not read and the diagnostic
        reports that instead of a verdict.

GATE 6  THE BASIS IS THE ONE ASKED FOR. Each run's printed Number of Electrons NEL equals 118 in
        all 24, and each run's printed Number of basis functions equals 264 in the def2-SVP group
        and 402 in the def2-SVPD group. Both of those are ORCA's own printed values from committed
        runs of this system, so they are asserted. The def2-TZVPD count is NOT asserted: it is
        PREDICTED as 682 from a pyscf count of the same 24-atom geometry, which reproduced ORCA's
        264 and 402 exactly but has never been checked against ORCA at this basis. The gate
        therefore requires only that all eight def2-TZVPD runs report the SAME count and that it
        exceeds 402, and it RECORDS that count. If it is not 682 the prediction was wrong, which is
        reported as such and changes no quantity; the basis is set by the keyword, not by this
        document's count of it. The point charges contribute no basis functions and no electrons;
        if either count moves, the charges are no longer the electron-free objects the rest of the
        record describes.

GATE 7  EVERY FIGURE IN THE RESULT DOCUMENT MATCHES THE RUN AT FULL PRECISION, each one recomputed
        from the committed outputs by the reader and compared against the document as written.

A FAILED GATE IS COMMITTED AS A FAILED GATE. DIAGNOSTIC 26's GATE 7 is the precedent.


D27.4  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE IT RUNS

For each vector v, with all three ddE values from ORCA:

    gap_SD(v) = | ddE(def2-SVPD, v) - ddE(def2-SVP,  v) |      double  -> diffuse double
    gap_DT(v) = | ddE(def2-TZVPD, v) - ddE(def2-SVPD, v) |     diffuse double -> diffuse triple

The tolerance is TOL = 1.0 kcal/mol. It is not invented here: it is the basis-agreement figure
already committed as criterion D5.3(d) in STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC5_20261026.txt, applied to the next step
of the same sequence.

  CONVERGED AT def2-SVPD        if gap_DT(v) <= TOL for all three vectors, AND gap_DT(v) < gap_SD(v)
                                for all three. The sequence is then shrinking and def2-SVPD is the
                                production basis. Nothing in the record needs re-running on account
                                of the basis.

  NOT CONVERGED                 if gap_DT(v) > TOL for any vector. def2-SVPD is then not sufficient
                                at that vector, and every committed def2-SVPD quantity at or above
                                that Sum|q| is provisional until re-run. This includes the walled
                                and pseudopotential arms of DIAGNOSTIC 23 and DIAGNOSTIC 26 and the
                                d16 ladder. That consequence is accepted in advance.

  NOT CONVERGING                if gap_DT(v) >= gap_SD(v) for any vector, whatever the absolute
                                size. A sequence whose steps are not shrinking is not converging to
                                anything readable, and no quantity at that vector may be quoted at
                                any of the three bases.

On the third vector specifically, where the two bases already disagree by 13.5154 kcal/mol, the
arbitration is stated now:

  def2-SVP IS THE ARTEFACT      if | ddE(def2-TZVPD) - ddE(def2-SVPD) | <= TOL
  def2-SVPD IS THE ARTEFACT     if | ddE(def2-TZVPD) - ddE(def2-SVP)  | <= TOL
  BOTH ARE ARTEFACTS            if neither holds. The 5.6433e vector is then withdrawn from every
                                quantity until a larger basis is run, and the ladder above
                                Sum|q| = 2.88 e has no admissible basis in this record.

If the three vectors do not give the same verdict, the result is reported per vector and no single
verdict is formed - the same rule DIAGNOSTIC 26 followed when its three sets disagreed.


D27.5  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. It is three charge vectors at one frame, one grid and one pair of geometries. It is not a
   statement about other frames, other arrangements or relaxed geometries.
2. It cannot separate basis-set incompleteness from density spill-out onto the point charges.
   DIAGNOSTIC 26 already found that separation set-dependent, which is why this runs.
3. It says nothing about whether def2-TZVPD is itself converged. A three-point sequence can show a
   step shrinking; it cannot prove a limit. If the answer is CONVERGED, the honest claim is that
   the def2-SVPD -> def2-TZVPD step is below TOL, not that the basis-set limit is reached.
4. It does not re-run DIAGNOSTIC 24's Gaussian-smeared models, so DIAGNOSTIC 24 remains
   EXPLORATORY and unquotable as a whole.
5. It is in vacuo, as every Stage 6 arm is. It says nothing about a solvated barrier.
6. It does not touch the charge representation, the sphere parameters, the grid floors, the
   optimiser or the relaxation.
7. It cannot license a barrier. ddE is a difference of differences; the absolute barrier at any of
   these bases carries B3LYP's own 3 to 5 kcal/mol error on this reaction, which is larger than
   anything measured here.
