THE CONTRADICTION

STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt specifies arrangements that cannot be built from the inputs the same file provides.

It says, under THE GRID (line 61):

   "The A matrix for this frame is not rebuilt; the arrangements are placed by the rule below from the development A
   matrix's ranking of the corresponding sites, and the frozen effects Stage 6 needs are computed directly from the
   single points it runs (below), not from an A column."

and then, under THE ARRANGEMENTS:

   "S6-2   two spheres: the most barrier-lowering admitted site of this grid, then the next that is at least 3.25 A
   from it ..."
   "S6-5, S6-10, S6-20  the committed optimiser's own best design at n = 5, 10 and 20 on this grid
   (cs_opt_milp.py, code tier1-realism 148683d)"

Both of those need an A column for THIS grid:
  - "the most barrier-lowering admitted site" per unit charge is exactly a_if = V_TS,f(r_i) - V_R,f(r_i), the A
    matrix (cs5_amatrix.py; Sokalski 1985 Eq. 19);
  - cs_opt_milp.py's interface is "solve <sites.tsv> <L.tsv> <A.tsv>": it cannot run without A.

The development A matrix cannot stand in for it. It is built on the development grid, whose sites are a different
set; at the NEB band geometries, not the package's stationary points; and in the development alignment's coordinate
frame, not frame 42436's. Borrowing its ranking by proximity would need an approximate transform between two
different site sets, which is not a rule that can be pre-registered.

Nothing had been generated or run when this was found, so no Stage 6 input, output or number is affected.

A6.1 THE STEP THAT IS ADDED

The A column of frame 42436 is built on the Stage 6 grid with the committed cs5_amatrix.py (chorismate-thesis-code,
tier1-realism, phase2.2/; sha256 at the end), which is the machinery Stage 5 production uses, run here for one frame:

   python3 cs5_amatrix.py prepare  <aligned_dir> <sites.tsv> <OUT> [HPC_DIR]     (PC; writes the inputs, committed)
   qsub OUT/stage5_a.pbs                                                          (hpc1, one job)
   python3 cs5_amatrix.py assemble <aligned_dir> <sites.tsv> <WORK> <OUT_RESULTS>  (PC, on the fetched outputs)

It computes its own in vacuo B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP densities of the bare substrate at this frame's R, TS and P and runs
orca_vpot at every grid site, with that script's own checks (a-d). Its output is written to
cs_stage6/A_f42436.tsv (and AP_f42436.tsv, which Stage 6 does not use), and its sha256 is recorded with the
arrangements.

Consequences, stated rather than left implicit:
1. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt's sentence "The A matrix for this frame is not rebuilt" is WITHDRAWN. The clause that follows
   it - placing the arrangements from the development A matrix's ranking - is withdrawn with it, because it was the
   part that could not be done.
2. The same file's "On the PC; no HPC, no new single points" (line 58) remains true OF THE GRID, which is still built
   on the PC from committed files with no new single points. It was never true of the stage as a whole, and this
   amendment makes that explicit: Stage 6 now has two HPC steps, this A-matrix job and then the relaxations.
3. Everything the criteria says about the arrangements now works as written, including the optimiser's own designs at
   n = 5, 10 and 20, with the A column this step produces.
4. The frozen effects Stage 6 measures are still computed from the single points it runs, not from the A column. A is
   used ONLY to place the arrangements. That part of the withdrawn sentence was correct and is kept.

A6.2 WHY THIS RATHER THAN A RULE THAT NEEDS NO A MATRIX

The alternative was to place the arrangements by a purely geometric rule - the admitted site nearest O3, then
successive sites respecting the 3.25 A spacing - which needs no A column and no HPC job.

Claude's assessment, and the reason for the choice: G0 will be applied to designs the optimiser produces, and the
relationship between how hard a design pulls and how much effect it loses on relaxation need not be the same for an
optimiser-placed design as for a hand-ruled one, because the optimiser exploits the grid in ways a geometric rule
does not. A threshold fitted to rule-placed arrangements risks not transferring to the designs it will be used on.
The geometric rule is cheaper and avoids an HPC dependency, but it saves that cost in the one place where it matters
most. Jessica agreed on 21 October 2026.

A6.3 WHAT THIS COSTS

One PBS job for one frame. For scale: the development A matrix covered 2769 sites across 30 frames in a single job
(cs_stage5/exploratory/dev_A/, hpc1 job 420253); this is the same work for one frame and one grid. It is requested as
cs5_amatrix.py's own job script writes it, and it does not compete with the SP package's 16-core, 64-250 GB jobs.

A6.3b TWO THINGS ABOUT THE REUSED SCRIPT, RECORDED SO NEITHER IS MISREAD

1. CHECK (b) OF cs5_amatrix.py IS VACUOUS HERE, AND ITS PASS MEANS NOTHING FOR STAGE 6. That check is
   "rigid-motion invariance: the alignment frame with the largest rotation is also run in its own (original)
   coordinates, with the sites taken back by its inverse transform; max |V_original - V_aligned| <= 1e-4 Eh/e". With
   one frame and no alignment the transform is the identity, so the "original" and "aligned" coordinates are the same
   file and the check compares a calculation with itself: prepare reports "rotation 0.0 deg" and one of the 16 runs
   (inv_42436_R) is a duplicate that cannot fail. A_REPORT.txt will nevertheless print check (b) as passed. It
   carries no information about this frame, and no claim about rigid-motion invariance is made from Stage 6. The
   other checks do their work: (a) normal termination, SCF convergence, 118 electrons, echoed coordinates and one
   vpot value per site; (c) the finite-difference test of a at three sites with +-0.05 e, which is 12 of the 16 runs
   and is the substantive check that A is right; (d) the inputs unchanged since prepare. Stage 5 production will
   exercise (b) properly, having many frames with real rotations.
2. THE JOB IS NAMED cm_s5a, NOT A STAGE 6 NAME, because the committed Stage 5 script writes its own job script and
   this amendment does not fork it for a name. Its output path IS redirected to the Stage 6 folder, by cs5_amatrix.py
   prepare's own HPC_DIR argument, so nothing is written into cs_stage5/. In qstat this job appears as cm_s5a: it is
   Stage 6's A-matrix job and not the start of Stage 5 production.

A6.4 ORDER OF WORK FOR STAGE 6, AS AMENDED

1. On the PC: extract the 24 substrate atoms of frame 42436's R, TS and P from the committed archives (cs6_prepare.py
   extract, with the checks STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt lists); build the grid and L with the committed cs5_grid.py; write
   the A-matrix job inputs with the committed cs5_amatrix.py prepare. Commit all of it.
2. On hpc1: the one A-matrix job.
3. On the PC: assemble A; place the seven arrangements of STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt with the committed cs_opt_milp.py where
   the rule calls for it; write the relaxation job inputs. Commit all of it, for checking before anything is
   submitted.
4. On hpc1: the relaxation jobs.
5. On the PC: cs6_analyse.py - G_add, Loss, the Spearman test, and G0 and kappa or the fallback. Commit.
