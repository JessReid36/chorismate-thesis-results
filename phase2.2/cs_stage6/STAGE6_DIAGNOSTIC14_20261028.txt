Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 14: does the per-site artefact bound a many-charge design?
Written 28 October 2026, after DIAGNOSTIC 7, 12 and 13 were collected and committed, and before
any input is generated. Decided by Jessica on 28 October 2026.

THIS IS A DIAGNOSTIC AND NOT A METHOD CHANGE. No criterion, threshold, tolerance, arrangement or
reported number changes. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt, PHASE22_PLAN.txt and every
STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC*, STAGE6_AMENDMENT* and STAGE6_ERRATUM* file are untouched and stand as
committed. DIAGNOSTIC7_RESULT.txt, DIAGNOSTIC12_RESULT.txt and DIAGNOSTIC13_RESULT.txt in
particular stand exactly as committed; this diagnostic adds a measurement none of them made.


D14.1  WHY, AND THE THREE PLACES IN THE RECORD THAT ASK FOR IT

DIAGNOSTIC12_RESULT.txt, first limitation:
   "Additivity. Every run here carries ONE charge; a design carries several, and nothing here
    says the artefact adds."

DIAGNOSTIC13_RESULT.txt, first limitation:
   "Every run carries ONE charge. A design of twenty-six charges at 0.25 e is not twenty-six
    independent probes ... Nothing here supports a claim that weak charges are safe in a
    many-charge design."

DIAGNOSTIC7_RESULT.txt makes it the binding question. At matched total field, sum |q| = 2.8800 e:

   S6-20 at lambda 0.50      largest |q| 0.2750 e   CLEAN at def2-SVP   CLEAN at def2-SVPD
   S6-10 at lambda 0.657534  largest |q| 0.3616 e   CLEAN at def2-SVP   STRAINED at def2-SVPD

Same total field. Both per-site magnitudes far below the 0.65 e upper bound ERRATUM3 E3.1 records.
Different verdict. D7 reading 3 separately found that Delta, the inter-basis energy difference, IS
a function of total field (MATCHED). So the energy behaves as a function of an aggregate and the
wavefunction health does not - which is what DIAGNOSTIC 12 means by NO DESCRIPTOR IS ADEQUATE.

ddE additivity is measured: Finding 2 gives 3.1 per cent at n = 2 and 6.1 per cent at n = 3, and
phase2b_charge_design/07_additivity's 108 runs are all CLEAN under D6's rule (ERRATUM3 E3.4).
d_shield additivity has been measured at no n at all.

WHAT IT DECIDES. DIAGNOSTIC 12 established that no screen can be built from the quantities a grid
has, and that the charge model must change. What is not established is whether the per-site
readings of D12 and D13 at least BOUND a design. If they do, a design is no worse than the sum of
its charges and those readings are usable as upper bounds. If they do not, nothing measured one
charge at a time says anything about a design, and every frozen Stage 6 number on a many-charge
arrangement is unsupported.

D14.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

d_shield(S) = ddE(bare, S) - ddE(wall, S), each ddE corrected by the zero-charge wall term as
Stage 4c Amendment 1 requires.

   Dsum(S) = sum over the members of S of | d_shield({i}) |    each measured alone, here
   Dset(S) = | d_shield(S) |                                   the set measured together
   tol(S)  = max( 0.10 x Dsum(S), 0.05 kcal/mol )

tol is DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's own tolerance on d_shield, carried over unchanged. No new tolerance is
introduced by this diagnostic.

WHY A BAND ON THE RATIO IS NOT USED, recorded so the choice is visible. d_shield is a difference of
differences, so its non-additivity is identically the difference of its two legs' non-additivities,
and |delta_bare - delta_wall| <= |delta_bare| + |delta_wall| always. Any criterion comparing those
two is satisfied by the triangle inequality and can never fire. Dset and Dsum come from separate
calculations and no inequality relates them.

READING 1, once per arm, over the readable ratio points n2, n3 and n4:

   BOUNDED       Dset <= Dsum + tol at every readable ratio point. The per-site sum is a
                 conservative bound on a design, so D12's and D13's per-site readings DO bound a
                 design and may be quoted as upper bounds.
   NOT BOUNDED   Dset > Dsum + tol at any readable ratio point. Nothing measured one charge at a
                 time bounds a design, and no per-site reading may be quoted as an upper bound on
                 one.
   NOT READABLE  any run in a subset is STRAINED or COLLAPSED by D6.3's rule at the basis being
                 read. That subset returns nothing. A ratio of two unwell calculations is what
                 ERRATUM3 E3.2 withdrew DIAGNOSTIC 3 for and it will not be computed here. If
                 fewer than two ratio points are readable in an arm, that arm returns NO VERDICT.

Dset/Dsum is reported at every readable point whatever the verdict, because its trend in n is the
quantity a later diagnostic would need.

READING 2, across the arms, over the ratio points readable in both:

   CONSTANT        both arms return the same reading-1 verdict.
   TURNS ON        arm A BOUNDED and arm B NOT BOUNDED. Cooperativity appears between total field
                   2.19 and 2.88 e. This would explain D7's failure at matched per-site magnitude
                   and would say that a per-site cap can never be sufficient however small the
                   charges.
   TURNS OFF       arm A NOT BOUNDED and arm B BOUNDED. Unexpected; reported as measured.
   NOT COMPARABLE  fewer than two ratio points readable in both arms.

CONTROLS, read before any ratio. Every run is classified by D6.3's rule, imported from
cs6_audit.py unchanged, against the committed DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt control medians for its
basis-set size, and the classification is read before any energy, as D7.3 and ERRATUM3 E3.4
require. n6neg must be CLEAN at both bases at both arms with |d_shield| <= 0.05 kcal/mol: if the
negative set is not inert, the sign asymmetry both sign arms report is wrong and this diagnostic
stops without a verdict. The three n10 reproduction checks must pass.


D14.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

One arrangement, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry. Two scalings, so nothing about
total fields outside 2.19 to 2.88 e - in particular nothing about lambda = 1, where D7 found both
bases unwell. Positives nested first, so no mixed-sign subset below n10 is measured, and n6neg is
a health control and not an additivity measurement. The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside
their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7), so the walled arm is a contrast and not a truth: this
measures whether the CONTRAST is bounded, which is the question asked, but it cannot say the
walled number is right. In vacuo. Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain unmeasurable until
the OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict recorded in STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028.md is
decided. And nothing about additivity ACROSS arrangements: whether S6-20's twenty charges behave
like S6-10's ten needs S6-20's own series.


D14.5  DIRECTION

None. No decision, threshold, tolerance, criterion, arrangement or reported quantity changes.
Stage 6's method remains as STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt fixes it. What changes is whether the per-site
readings already committed may be quoted as bounds on a design, and D14.3 fixes which answer means
which before the runs go in.
