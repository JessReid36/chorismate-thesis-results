STAGE 6 - PROBE 2, PRE-REGISTERED                                             27 October 2026

DOES A DESIGNED CHARGE PUSH THE DIANION FROM BOUND TO METASTABLE?

Written and committed before the runs. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. No continuum, per
STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027.


P2.1  WHY

Diagnostic 2 measured the vertical detachment energy of the BARE substrate at the reactant geometry
and committed it on 22 October: -0.3167 eV at def2-SVP, +0.5123 eV at ma-def2-SVP, +0.5013 eV at
def2-SVPD. At the basis Stage 6 runs in, the bare dianion is bound.

Nobody has measured it with a designed charge present. Dreuw & Cederbaum (cr0104227, held) give the
reason to:

   "From a certain distance on, when the electrostatic repulsion between the monoanion and the point
    charge exceeds the binding energy of the anionic electron, the monoanion always detaches. Hence,
    at short distances the monoanion-point charge system represents a resonance state that can in
    principle not be correctly computed with bound state methods"

The designs carry negative charges 2.8 to 3.0 A from a dianion: S6-2 one at -1.00 e, S6-20 ten
totalling -3.00 e. If a designed charge drives the VDE to zero or below, every ddE measured at that
condition is a bound-state calculation of a resonance and is not a barrier effect.

A correlation in Diagnostic 10's data is consistent with this and is the reason the probe exists,
but it is three designs with the negative charge confounded against the sphere count, so it decides
nothing on its own:

   design  negative charges  sum q-    HOMO(R) vac   response vs classical
   S6-C          0            0.00       +1.2269          -
   S6-P          0            0.00       -2.3674       +0.37 kcal/mol
   S6-2          1           -1.00       -0.6098       +8.19
   S6-20        10           -3.00       -0.5199      +20.71


P2.2  WHAT IS RUN

Eight single points, reactant geometry only, ma-def2-SVP, in vacuo. Four conditions, each with its
own dianion and monoanion run so the probe is self-contained and does not wait on Diagnostic 11.

   ctl        no charge
   dref_p1    q = +1 at site 1690's committed position (2.960 A)
   dref_m1    q = -1 at the same position
   d250_m1    q = -1 at 2.500 A on the same axis - deliberately closer than any committed design

VDE = E(monoanion, charge -1, multiplicity 2) - E(dianion, charge -2, multiplicity 1), both at the
same geometry WITH THE SAME EXTERNAL CHARGE PRESENT. Positive means bound. The input form copies
the committed, already-run phase2.2/cs_stage6/qscan/VDE/C3_mono_R/job.inp exactly.

The axis and the dref position are identical to Diagnostic 11's, so the two are directly
comparable; dref is site 1690, which is S6-P's position.


P2.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

GATE 0.  Every input must match the committed MANIFEST.sha256 byte for byte.

GATE 1.  The control dianion must reproduce Diagnostic 1's committed -836.227913439 Eh to 1e-6.

GATE 2.  The control VDE must reproduce Diagnostic 2's committed +0.5123 eV to 0.01 eV. If it does
not, this probe is not measuring the same quantity D2 measured and its charged conditions cannot be
compared with it; nothing further is reported.

THE READING, per condition:

   VDE > 0     bound. A bound-state treatment is defensible at that charge and separation.
   VDE <= 0    METASTABLE. The system is Dreuw & Cederbaum's resonance, and any ddE measured there
               is not a barrier effect and must not be reported as one.

   If every condition is bound, the hazard is not realised at one charge and these separations.
   That is explicitly NOT a clearance for the many-charge designs: S6-20's ten negative charges
   totalling -3.00 e are not tested here and cannot be inferred from one charge at -1.00 e.

   If any condition is metastable, the designs carrying negative charges at or inside that
   separation are implicated, and the grid's admitted negative charges need their own bound before
   any further design work.

THE HOMO IS NOT THE TEST. It is printed as data. STAGE6_PROBE1_ERRATUM1_20261027 records why, and
Diagnostic 2 withdrew a finding over it.


P2.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

The transition-state geometry: this and D2's VDE are both at the reactant, so neither says whether
boundedness changes along the reaction coordinate. Multi-charge arrangements. Relaxed geometries.
And the basis dependence Dreuw & Cederbaum warn of - they show a VDE's SIGN flipping between a
non-diffuse DZP basis and the same basis with diffuse functions added, and this probe runs one
basis only. D2 already covers that for the bare substrate across three bases; it is not repeated
here with a charge present, and that gap is stated rather than closed.
