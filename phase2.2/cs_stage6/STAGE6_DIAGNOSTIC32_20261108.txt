D32.1  WHY

DIAGNOSTIC 31 found that a single +-1.00 e point charge already puts the calculation outside the
reliable regime. Its committed result records, for the one-sphere unit-charge design A/n01, a
maximum Loewdin charge rising +0.7784 e in one state and +0.8650 e in the other against D_LW, and
in the same breath an unstable SCF solution with three negative eigenvalues. Every unit-charge
design failed, at every n. The usable envelope it found is in partial charges: q <= 0.75 e to
n = 3, q <= 0.50 e to n = 5, q <= 0.25 e to n = 20.

The second one-sphere unit charge, B/n01_q1.00 on a weaker site, rises +0.799 e [RECOMPUTED here
from the committed outputs by the committed classifier; the d31 result prints verdicts rather than
rises for Arm B, so this figure is not quoted from it].

IT ALSO SHOWED WHAT KIND OF FAILURE IT IS. Across the whole grid the Loewdin rise is either about
0.02 to 0.09 e or about 0.8 to 1.9 e, with nothing in between. It is a switch, not a gradient, and
the switch is thrown by the magnitude on one sphere rather than by the total: twenty charges of
0.25 e are reliable while one of 1.00 e is not.

AND THE LITERATURE NAMED THE MECHANISM. Behrens, p. 28, on point charges with a larger basis:
"With larger basis functions, electron density spilling can become a problem, where the density is
overpolarized due a strong interaction with the pure Coulomb charge with no Pauli repulsion. In
context of minimal basis semi-empirical methods, however, this is not an issue that needs to be
addressed at this point." Their basis was minimal, so they could set it aside. Ours is diffuse,
which is why a single unit charge is enough to show it here.

A BARE POINT CHARGE HAS NO SIZE. Nothing stops density falling onto it. A real anion has a filled
shell that pushes electrons away, and that is the term the model is missing.

THE MEASUREMENT THAT MOTIVATES THIS ONE IS ALREADY IN THE RECORD. DIAGNOSTIC 23 found the argon
ECP wall cut the Loewdin relocation from +0.875 e to +0.093 e at the strongest field then
available, and argon was chosen because its repulsive wall matches the validated Amber nitrogen
Lennard-Jones wall more closely than neon or helium at the 10, 1 and 0.1 kcal/mol contours. If
that factor of nine carries over, the unit charge may be usable after all. If it does not, the
DIAGNOSTIC 31 envelope is what the method supports and the optimiser works in partial charges.


D32.2  WHAT IS RUN

Sixty-six single points, in two PBS jobs, CAM-B3LYP / def2-SVPD, STABPerform on every one, with
the same keyword line DIAGNOSTIC 31 used. A walled arm carries its charges on its Ar> lines rather
than in a pc.pc, so its geometry is inline: the ECP centres sit in the same coordinate block as the
substrate.

  JOB A, the +-1 question.      24 runs, budgeted 14.5 h
      A/n01 .. A/n20            the optimiser's own unit designs, n = 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20
      B/n01_q1.00 .. n20_q1.00  the q = 1.00 column of the ladder
  JOB B, the rest of the ladder and the controls.  42 runs, budgeted 10.5 h
      B at q = 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, n = 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20
      wall0/n01, wall0/n05, wall0/n20   the ECP centres present with ZERO charge

Job A alone answers the question this diagnostic exists for, so it runs first and can be read
before Job B finishes. Both budgets come from DIAGNOSTIC 31's own measured per-condition times
scaled by the wall's measured overhead, which is 13 per cent on the one committed pair where both
exist (2 min 34 s walled against 2 min 16 s bare). Walltime 24:00:00 each; both restartable.

THE PAIRING IS THE DESIGN. Every condition reuses DIAGNOSTIC 31's committed sites and charges
exactly, read out of its pc.pc files, with one argon centre placed at each charge. The substrate
geometry is copied line for line from the committed substrate.xyz. The ECP parameter block is
lifted verbatim from the committed DIAGNOSTIC 23 input and is identical at every centre, a full
argon core, N_core 18. So the only difference from DIAGNOSTIC 31 is the presence of the wall, and
the reading is the change the wall makes, condition by condition.

THE WALL ADDS NO ELECTRONS AND NO BASIS FUNCTIONS. A committed walled arm reports NEL 118 and 402
basis functions, the same as the bare arm. The comparison is therefore like for like in the
electronic structure, and only the potential is added. A gate requires both numbers.

NO CONTROLS ARE RUN. DIAGNOSTIC 31's charge-free ctl_R and ctl_TS are the same functional, basis
and geometry, and are re-read from the committed tree so that every rise here is measured on the
same baseline as every rise there. A gate requires them present and records their sha256.


D32.3  THE DIPOLE LEG IS WITHHELD, AND WHY

ORCA counts an ECP centre as a nucleus. On a committed walled arm the nuclear contribution to the
dipole moves from -3.4653/-2.1928/+0.5580 to -6.5258/-5.4665/+9.1994 while the electronic
contribution barely changes, 1.2415 to 1.2177, and the twenty centres' own charges account for
about 27.0 D of the 27.21 D ORCA reports. The dipole of a walled arm is bookkeeping, not physics.

So the dipole ratio is RECORDED AND REPORTED FOR EVERY CONDITION AND GATES NOTHING. Relocation is
judged on the Loewdin leg alone, against D_LW. This is stated here rather than discovered later,
and it is the leg that carried DIAGNOSTIC 31's result in any case.

The gap and the stability analysis are read as in DIAGNOSTIC 31, including the level-shift rule: a
condition whose gap cannot be read is never called RELIABLE.


D32.4  THE DECISION RULE, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS READ

Each condition is judged in both states, exactly as D31.3 judged its bare twin, except that
relocation uses the Loewdin leg only:

  RELIABLE      no relocation, no collapsed gap, a stable solution, converged, gap readable
  UNRELIABLE    any of those fires
  INCONCLUSIVE  nothing fires but a level shift made the gap unreadable

Then, against the committed DIAGNOSTIC 31 verdict for the same (n, q):

  RESCUED             d31 UNRELIABLE and d32 RELIABLE
  STILL UNRELIABLE    both UNRELIABLE
  BROKEN BY THE WALL  d31 RELIABLE and d32 UNRELIABLE
  UNCHANGED           both RELIABLE
  INCONCLUSIVE        either side INCONCLUSIVE; no pairing claim is made for that condition

THE PRIMARY QUESTION is whether q = 1.00 is rescued, and the single most informative condition is
B/n01_q1.00: one sphere, one unit charge, the point at which DIAGNOSTIC 31 already failed. The
primary verdict is formed from the six q = 1.00 conditions and the six Arm A conditions together:

  THE WALL RESCUES THE UNIT CHARGE       every one of those twelve is RESCUED or UNCHANGED
  THE WALL RESCUES IT UP TO SOME n       they are RESCUED for n up to some value and not above
  THE WALL DOES NOT RESCUE IT            none of the twelve is RESCUED

REPORTED BESIDE IT, NOT GATED: the Loewdin rise under the wall against the rise without it, for
every paired condition, so the factor the wall achieves can be compared with DIAGNOSTIC 23's nine.


D32.5  THE WALL0 CONTROLS, AND WHAT THEY CAN INVALIDATE

wall0/n01, wall0/n05 and wall0/n20 place the argon centres with zero charge, at the same sites.
They separate the wall's own effect on the density from the charge's.

  If a wall0 control shows a Loewdin rise reaching D_LW, THE WALL ITSELF PERTURBS THE DENSITY at
  that n, the paired comparison at that n is confounded, and no rescue is claimed for it. That
  consequence is accepted in advance.

For every condition with a matching wall0 at the same n, the rise over the wall0 baseline is also
reported, which is the charge's own contribution with the wall's removed. The primary figure
remains the rise over the bare control, because that is the reliability question.


D32.6  WHEN TO STOP TREATING THIS AS THE ANSWER

G1  A WALL0 CONTROL MOVES. The wall is not a neutral addition, and whatever it rescues cannot be
    separated from what it perturbs. The diagnostic reports that and claims no rescue at that n.
G2  NOTHING IS RESCUED. If none of the twelve is RESCUED, including B/n01_q1.00, the wall does not
    solve this. DIAGNOSTIC 31's envelope stands, the optimiser works in partial charges with a
    per-sphere ceiling, and the next question is whether a different wall or a different charge
    representation does better - not another ECP species chosen the same way.
G3  SOMETHING RELIABLE IS BROKEN. If any condition reliable without the wall becomes unreliable
    with it, the wall is not a pure improvement. The result is reported per condition and no
    blanket recommendation to wall everything is made.
G4  MORE THAN A QUARTER DO NOT CONVERGE. The finding is then about the protocol with ECP centres
    at these field strengths, not about the designs.

D32.8  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. IT IS ONE ECP SPECIES. Argon was chosen in DIAGNOSTIC 23 by matching the Amber nitrogen
   Lennard-Jones wall. A different species would give a different wall, and nothing here searches
   over species.
2. IT DOES NOT VALIDATE THE WALL AGAINST ANYTHING PHYSICAL. A full-core ECP at a point charge is a
   model of a filled shell, not a measured ion. That the relocation stops does not establish that
   the resulting barrier is right.
3. NO BASIS CHECK. DIAGNOSTIC 30 found def2-SVPD not converged at Sum|q| = 5.6433 e under
   CAM-B3LYP, and no def2-TZVPD arm is run here. The strong-field conditions inherit that doubt;
   B/n01_q1.00, at Sum|q| = 1.00 e, does not.
4. THE DIPOLE IS UNUSABLE HERE, so one of the two relocation legs is unavailable throughout, and a
   condition that would have failed on the dipole alone cannot be detected.
5. ONE FRAME, ONE GRID, IN VACUO, FIXED GEOMETRIES, NO CONTINUUM.
6. IT SETS NO BUDGET AND NO CAP. Whatever it finds, DIAGNOSTIC 29 still rules out a limit written
   as a function of the charges, and the withdrawn Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 stays withdrawn.
