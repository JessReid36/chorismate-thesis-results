2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE ELECTRON AND NOTHING ELSE: no substrate, no negative charge. Charges of +0.25 e at positive
sites of B_n11_q0.25, taken as lines of the committed point-charge file of the design in D50
(d50/CAMTZX_B_n11_q0.25_X2A_di_R/pc.pc). Six systems:

   system   charges of +0.25 e at            distances between them, A     the limit, eV
   s1       1124                             -                             0.8504
   pc       1073, 1052                       3.28                          2.0967
   pf       1124, 1052                       6.17                          1.5712
   t3       1124, 1073, 1107                 3.29 to 3.41                  3.3705
   f4       1124, 1073, 1107, 1052           3.28 to 6.17                  4.2747
   x6       all six                          3.28 to 11.54                 5.0261

THE FUNCTIONS stand at ALL SIX positive sites in every run, charged or not, as Jessica agreed for
the runs of the dianion: the six ghost atoms of the committed input of the design in D50, each
0.01 A beyond its charge (D47's rule). Set X2: the def2-TZVPD functions of hydrogen with an s and
a p function added at each of 0.035 and 0.012. Set X3: the same with 0.004 as well. With a
functional, auxiliary treatment A (ORCA's AutoAux on each ghost atom), as D51's rule gives.

THE LEVELS.
   HF    Hartree-Fock. Exact for one electron in the functions it is given; it uses no grid.
   CAM   CAM-B3LYP, the working functional, without its dispersion term, as in D49 to D51.
   WXV   wB97X-V.     WMV   wB97M-V.     Two functionals with full exact exchange at long
         range, where CAM-B3LYP has 65 per cent. Both ran on the dianion in D37, and the word
         for each is that of D37's committed inputs. Each carries the VV10 term, which ORCA
         adds after the SCF from the density; it is part of the functional and stays in B.
         THE DRAFT OF THIS DESIGN SAID THAT THIS FAMILY WAS NEW TO THE RECORD. THAT WAS WRONG:
         D37 and D40 ran these two, and D28, D35 and D40 ran wB97X-D3. None of those runs
         carried the stability analysis.
   RDS   revDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021, by the committed line of keywords of level RDTZ (D44) with UKS
         added, and its line "%mp2 RI true end". It carries the D4 term and an MP2 step, and
         whether ORCA runs either for one electron on ghost atoms alone is not known.

THE RUNGS. i12, i20, i40: IntAcc 12, 20 and 40 for the grid of the functional, as in D51. g2:
ORCA's default grid. z1: the default grid with IntAcc 12 asked for hydrogen alone
("SpecialGridAtoms 1", "SpecialGridIntAcc 12.0").

THE RUNS, 104 in one job:
   Hartree-Fock: 2 sets x 6 systems                                              12
   CAM on i12, i20 and i40: 2 sets x 6 systems x 3 rungs, and g2 and z1 at
      (X2, s1)                                                                   38
   WXV, WMV and RDS: set X2 on i12 and i20, set X3 on i20, 6 systems each        54

Every input is D49's committed one-electron input, formed through the committed
cs6_d50_prepare.py, with its one ghost atom replaced by the six, the functions of the set under
each, and for a functional the line of keywords of its level and the block of its rung
(d52/LEVELS.tsv). cs6_d52_prepare.py forms them and refuses unless the charges are at the sites
of the committed table (d43/SITES.tsv) and each ghost atom is 0.01 A from its charge.

THE JOB is D51's committed job script with its names and runs changed and two things altered: a
run is stopped after 3600 seconds in place of 900, since these systems have six centres, and NO
RUN IS MADE A SECOND TIME in its folder (D44 NOTE 3): submitted again, the script makes only the
runs that have no output yet. The runs of IntAcc 40 come last. MEASURED: D51's 200 runs of one
centre took 38 min 45 s. ESTIMATE for these 104: 1 to 4 hours on one node. Nothing is run on
Jessica's PC.

3. THE READINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B of a run is minus the energy of the one electron, in eV: ORCA's final energy less the nuclear
repulsion it prints. DERIVED from committed outputs of D50: the nuclear repulsion ORCA prints for
the design, less that of its control, is the repulsion between the charges and the nuclei
(2.2065532 hartree) and holds nothing of the repulsion among the charges (0.0404 hartree). With
ghost atoms alone it should be zero, and the result prints the largest value found.

THE LIMIT of a system is minus the lowest level of the one electron in the field of its charges,
in a large even-tempered basis of s, p and d functions at every charge. It was computed by Claude
in a second program before this was written (section 7) and is the reference of every rule below.
For one centre it is 13.6057 q^2.

THE COUNT of a run is the last N(Total) ORCA prints: the electrons its grid adds up to.

R52.1   THE RUNS: how many are measured at each level and rung, the IntAcc ORCA prints, and every
        run that is not measured, with ORCA's own words. A rung is used only if ORCA prints the
        IntAcc asked for; otherwise the run is NOT AS ASKED.
R52.2   ORCA AGAINST THE SECOND PROGRAM. ORCA's Hartree-Fock B against the lowest level in the
        same functions computed beforehand. AGREES if within 0.001 eV. It tests that the two
        programs were given the same system.
R52.3   THE FUNCTIONS. Hartree-Fock against the limit: ADEQUATE if within 0.05 eV.
R52.4   THE GRID OF THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL, as in D51. THE TOP is rung i40 if it is measured,
        otherwise i20; THE NEXT is the rung below it. CONVERGED if both are measured and agree
        to 0.005 eV; NOT CONVERGED if they do not; not decided otherwise. THE LOWEST RUNG THAT
        SERVES: the first whose B, and that of every rung above it up to the top, lies within
        0.005 eV of the top one. The count is printed beside every B.
R52.5   THE FUNCTIONS FOR THE FUNCTIONAL. Where the grid is CONVERGED in both sets: CAM in set
        X2 minus CAM in set X3, each on its top rung. HOLDS if within 0.05 eV.
R52.6   RULE A. A set SERVES at a system if the two programs AGREE there, its functions are
        ADEQUATE and its grid is CONVERGED; set X2 must also HOLD what set X3 holds. It DOES
        NOT SERVE if one of these fails; it is not decided if one is not measured.
           RULE A GIVES SET X2 if X2 serves at all six systems; SET X3 if X2 does not serve at
           one of them and X3 serves at all six; NONE if neither does; NOT DECIDED otherwise.
           With the set it gives the IntAcc: the highest of the lowest rungs that serve over
           the six systems.
R52.7   THE CANDIDATES. For each: the runs measured; its grid, IntAcc 20 minus IntAcc 12 in set
        X2 (CONVERGED within 0.005 eV); its functions, X2 minus X3 at IntAcc 20 (HOLDS within
        0.05 eV).
R52.8   EVERY FUNCTIONAL AGAINST THE LIMIT: B in set X3 at IntAcc 20 minus the limit, at each
        system, for CAM, WXV, WMV and RDS. Above zero means that the functional holds the
        electron at the charges more strongly than it is held. THE LARGEST DISTANCE of a
        functional is the largest of the six in size; it is formed only if all six are measured.
R52.9   RULE B. THE SECOND LEVEL is the candidate whose largest distance is smallest, if it is
        measured at all six systems and its largest distance is smaller than CAM's. NONE if no
        candidate is, or none lies nearer the limit than CAM. NOT DECIDED if CAM's own largest
        distance is not formed.
        RULE C. THE LIMIT L of D51's pre-registration is 3 times the larger of CAM's largest
        distance over the six systems and D51's 0.0631 eV. If CAM's is not formed, L rests on
        D51's number alone and the result says so. In the diagnostic of the dianion a run
        that takes electrons with a gain not larger than L is NOT SETTLED AT THIS LEVEL ALONE.
        D51's words "a few times" are fixed here as three.
R52.10  THE GRID GIVEN TO HYDROGEN ALONE. At (X2, s1): the energies of g2, i12 and z1. In these
        runs every atom is a ghost hydrogen. THE SAME AS IntAcc 12 FOR EVERY ATOM if z1 gives
        i12's energy to 0.000001 hartree; THE SAME AS THE DEFAULT GRID if it gives g2's;
        NEITHER otherwise; NOT DECIDED if a run is missing or g2 and i12 do not differ.

The tolerances. 0.05 and 0.005 eV are D51's. 0.001 eV between the two programs is a fiftieth of
the first: by section 7 the functions themselves fall short of the limit by up to 0.0026 eV, so
a wrong position or a wrong function would show.

WHY THE FUNCTIONALS ARE COMPARED IN SET X3 AT IntAcc 20. It is the larger set, and in D51 one
charge from 0.25 e up at IntAcc 20 lies within 0.003 eV of IntAcc 40. CAM is run at IntAcc 40 as
well and R52.8 prints it beside, so that the choice can be seen to matter or not.

G52.2  The files of d52/SOURCES.sha256 (15) are the ones listed.

G52.4  The sentences quoted from AMENDMENT 10, D50 and D51 are in those documents and in this

5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TWO PROGRAMS DO NOT AGREE at a system. Nothing else is read as settled there. The cause is
found first (a position, a function, a unit) and the next step is a dated document on it. RULE A
may still name a set; the dianion is not pre-registered with charges together before that
document exists.

RULE A GIVES SET X2 OR SET X3, WITH AN IntAcc. The diagnostic of the dianion places that set
with treatment A at all six positive sites and gives the ghost atoms that IntAcc. Its
pre-registration still reads the count of every run, as D51's pre-registration requires.
RULE A GIVES NONE OR NOT DECIDED. The dianion is not pre-registered with charges together until
a dated document has said what is mended. NONE with Hartree-Fock ADEQUATE means the grid or the
functional's use of the functions; with Hartree-Fock NOT ADEQUATE, the functions.

RULE B GIVES A CANDIDATE. The diagnostic of the dianion runs it beside the working functional,
on arrangements its own pre-registration fixes by rule, with Hartree-Fock beside both as a
bracket and not as a judge. That the candidate can be run on the dianion with the stability
analysis is not shown here and is for that pre-registration to test first.
RULE B GIVES NONE. There is no second level by this rule. A capture with a gain not larger than
L is then reported NOT SETTLED AT THIS LEVEL ALONE and stays so until a dated amendment names
what judges it.
A CANDIDATE THAT ORCA REFUSED FOR ONE ELECTRON is not ranked by RULE B. Nothing is said of it
for the dianion.

RULE C GIVES L, whatever else happens: at least 0.1893 eV. D50's capture had a gain of 0.0898 eV
and is inside it. That is already AMENDMENT 10's word for B_n11_q0.25 (NOT SETTLED AT A SECOND
LEVEL) and changes nothing committed.

THE GRID FOR HYDROGEN ALONE IS THE SAME AS IntAcc 12 FOR EVERY ATOM. The diagnostic of the
dianion may give its IntAcc to hydrogen alone, which leaves the grid of the heavier atoms as
committed. Any other word: it gives the IntAcc to every atom.

THE WALL. Not run here. If, in the diagnostic of the dianion, bare charges together hold, the
wall is left and a comparative run may follow later, as Jessica said. If they take electrons, the
wall is the fallback of her order of 17 November 2026, as a test arm under AMENDMENT 10, with one
electron at walled charges run first.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D52.1  One electron in the field of bare charges says what the functions, the grid and a
       functional do for one electron. In a run of the dianion the electron that may leave is
       one of 118, and the substrate and the negative charges are present. A functional that
       lies near the limit here is not thereby right there.
D52.2  The limit is computed, not measured, and by Claude. It rests on a basis shown to be
       converged by repeating it in a larger one (section 7), and for one centre on 13.6057 q^2.
       R52.2 tests the same program against ORCA in the functions of the runs, not in that
       basis.
D52.3  The six systems are subsets of the positive sites of one old design. They are used
       because D50 measured that design; nothing here is a statement about it or about any
       design to come.
D52.4  The candidates are compared at one grid and in one set. A candidate may need another
       grid than CAM-B3LYP; R52.7 shows what IntAcc 12 against 20 does and nothing above 20.
D52.5  Nothing here is walled, and no charge is other than +0.25 e.
D52.6  It does not test D50's finding on the dianion and does not say whether that capture is
       real. It measures how far the working functional can be trusted with one electron held
       by several charges, which bears on how that finding is to be judged.
