D31.1  THE QUESTION, AND WHY IT IS NOT A SCAN IN ONE NUMBER

The question is: how many charges, and how large, can a design carry before the calculation stops
being trustworthy.

IT CANNOT BE ANSWERED BY REFINING ONE TOTAL. DIAGNOSTIC 29 found that no function of the charges
orders density relocation: transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_R and lam075_TS carry identical charge
vectors and only one of them relocates. Bisecting Sigma|q| between two arrangements would locate
where those two arrangements break and would not transfer to a third.

AND q IS NOT FREELY CHOOSABLE AT A GIVEN SITE. Stage 5 built the grid on charge-dependent resting
distances: a sized, charged sphere rests at a distance that depends on its charge, so the +1
floors are nearer the substrate than the small-charge floors. The consequence is recorded in the
grid itself - a site admits q in [q_min_pos, +1], so a close-in site admits only LARGE positive
charges, and the cation layer admits no negative charge at all. Of the ten best-ranked sites, five
admit nothing at the sign that would lower the barrier and four admit only +1.00 e. Scaling a
design down therefore places spheres below their own floors, and an unreliability found that way
could be the violation rather than the field.

So the scan is two-dimensional, it respects the grid's bounds at every point, and it is built from
the committed optimiser rather than from a rule invented here.


D31.2  WHAT IS RUN

Sixty-two single points. One keyword line, used by every one of them:

    ! CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
    %maxcore 3000
    %pal nprocs 8 end
    %scf
      MaxIter 300
      STABPerform true
    end
    %pointcharges "pc.pc"                 (omitted for a control)
    * xyzfile -2 1 substrate.xyz

CAM-B3LYP and def2-SVPD because DIAGNOSTIC 30 validated that pair: at Sigma|q| = 1.3000 e and
2.8800 e the basis is converged, the solution is stable, the density does not relocate and the gap
is clean. STABPerform ON EVERY RUN because DIAGNOSTIC 30 showed it returns a bit-identical energy
where the solution is stable - all six stable conditions agreed to the last digit - and the
correct solution where it is not. It therefore costs nothing and removes a failure mode.

ARM A, THE DESIGNS THE OPTIMISER ACTUALLY PRODUCES.  n in {1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20}, 12 runs.
  Sites: the committed ranking - per_site_best over each site's own admissible ranges, then
  greedy_sites at the committed r_min. Charges: per_site_best's own choice at each site, with no
  budget imposed, so each sphere carries the best charge its site admits.
  EVERY ONE OF THOSE IS +-1.00 e. Arm A is therefore the unit-charge family, and Sigma|q| = n:
  1, 2, 3, 5, 10 and 20 e. The budget is deliberately not imposed, because the question is where
  reliability ends and a budget would cap every design at the same total.

ARM B, THE MAGNITUDE DIMENSION WHERE IT PHYSICALLY EXISTS.  n x q, 48 runs.
  The same n values, crossed with q in {0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}, on the 1438 sites that admit
  every value of that ladder at the sign per_site_best chose for them. All n spheres carry the
  same magnitude, so Sigma|q| = n*q and max|q| = q exactly.
  THE COST OF THAT RESTRICTION IS STATED IN ADVANCE: the best site in the restricted pool scores
  -5.490 kcal/mol alone against -6.115 for the best site overall. Arm B's designs are weaker than
  the optimiser's best by that much, and D31.6 names what to do if that turns out to matter.

CONTROLS. ctl_R and ctl_TS, charge-free, shared by both arms and by every condition.

THE PIPELINE IS VALIDATED AGAINST COMMITTED DESIGNS. Run on the committed grid it reproduces
  S6-2  sites [1690, 678]       q +1.0000, -1.0000                  score -11.21837
  S6-3  sites [1690, 678, 540]  q +1.0000, -0.7625, -0.6500         score -12.85731
which are the committed arrangements in relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt, to every printed digit. A gate
requires that agreement before any input is accepted.

CROSS-COMPARABILITY, WHICH IS THE POINT OF THE DESIGN. Held identical across all 62: the geometry
of each state, byte for byte; the keyword line; the basis; the functional; the controls. Varying:
only n and q. Guaranteed by construction and gated: the site sets are nested, so the n-sphere set
is a prefix of every larger one in the same arm; the sign at a site never changes with q; the
coordinates in a pc.pc never change with q; R and TS carry byte-identical charges.

ORDER OF THE RUN. Ascending in Sigma|q|. The informative low-field points land first, and the
points that may not converge at all are last. Budgeted 18.3 h against a 24:00:00 walltime, from
DIAGNOSTIC 30's measured timings, with everything above Sigma|q| = 5.6433 e budgeted rather than
measured because nothing above it has ever been run. run.pbs is restartable.


D31.3  WHAT MAKES A CONDITION RELIABLE, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS READ

Each condition is read in both states, against the controls, by the committed classifier:

  GAP        flagged if the classifier's gap criterion fires (gap below GAP_ABS and below R_GAP
             times its control's gap)
  RELOCATION flagged if the Loewdin rise reaches D_LW, or the dipole ratio reaches R_DIP
  STABILITY  flagged if the run's stability analysis reports an instability
  CONVERGED  flagged if the SCF did not converge within 300 cycles

  RELIABLE      no flag fires in either state, and the gap was readable in both
  UNRELIABLE    any flag fires, naming which
  INCONCLUSIVE  no flag fires but the gap could not be read in one or both states

THE GAP IS WITHHELD WHERE IT CANNOT BE READ. A run whose SCF engaged TRAH with a level shift
prints shifted orbital energies, so its LUMO-HOMO difference is the shift floor and not the gap.
Detection is by "Leaving SCF to start the TRAH-SCF procedure", "(TRAH MAcro)" or a level-shift
line, and NOT by a bare search for TRAH, which matches the author credits in every ORCA output.
A condition is NEVER called RELIABLE on a withheld gap: it is INCONCLUSIVE. This is expected to
affect the strongest fields, where DIAGNOSTIC 30 already saw it at Sigma|q| = 5.6433 e.

NOT CONVERGING IS A RESULT, NOT A BROKEN JOB. At Sigma|q| = 20 e the field is three and a half
times the strongest ever run here. A condition that does not converge in 300 cycles is recorded
UNRELIABLE (did not converge) and the run is kept.


D31.4  THE TWO ENVELOPES, AND WHAT COUNTS AS HAVING FOUND ONE

ARM A, THE ENVELOPE IN n AT UNIT CHARGE.
  The envelope is the largest n such that every n' <= n in the arm is RELIABLE.
  If some n is RELIABLE while a smaller one is not, NO ENVELOPE IS FORMED and the arm is reported
  per n. That is an admissible outcome and not a failure.

ARM B, THE ENVELOPE IN q AT EACH n.
  For each n, the envelope is the largest q such that every q' <= q at that n is RELIABLE.
  Non-monotone in q at any n: that n is reported per point and no envelope is formed for it.

NEITHER IS A PREDICTOR. Both are empirical: the largest tested design that passed. DIAGNOSTIC 29
rules out reading either as a rule that a new arrangement must obey.


D31.5  THE ISO-TOTAL TEST, WHICH COSTS NOTHING EXTRA

Ten values of Sigma|q| are reached by more than one (n, q) split:

  Sigma|q| = 0.50   B/n01_q0.50, B/n02_q0.25
  Sigma|q| = 0.75   B/n01_q0.75, B/n03_q0.25
  Sigma|q| = 1.00   A/n01, B/n01_q1.00, B/n02_q0.50
  Sigma|q| = 1.50   B/n02_q0.75, B/n03_q0.50
  Sigma|q| = 2.00   A/n02, B/n02_q1.00
  Sigma|q| = 2.50   B/n05_q0.50, B/n10_q0.25
  Sigma|q| = 3.00   A/n03, B/n03_q1.00
  Sigma|q| = 5.00   A/n05, B/n05_q1.00, B/n10_q0.50, B/n20_q0.25
  Sigma|q| = 10.00  A/n10, B/n10_q1.00, B/n20_q0.50
  Sigma|q| = 20.00  A/n20, B/n20_q1.00

If Sigma|q| governed reliability, the members of each family would agree. DIAGNOSTIC 29 says it
does not, so they are expected to disagree. Whichever way it comes out:
  THEY DISAGREE   DIAGNOSTIC 29 is confirmed on a purpose-built family, and the envelope must be
                  stated in (n, q) and never as a total.
  THEY AGREE      that is new and it does not overturn DIAGNOSTIC 29, whose counterexample is a
                  single charge vector in two geometries and is not a statement about totals. It
                  would be reported as agreement on these ten families and nothing more.
This is recorded and reported; it gates nothing.


D31.6  WHEN TO ABANDON THIS DESIGN, DECIDED NOW RATHER THAN AFTERWARDS

F1  NOTHING IN ARM B BREAKS. If no Arm B condition is UNRELIABLE, including n=20 at q=1.00, then
    the restricted pool was too weak to reach the boundary and the magnitude question is
    unanswered on it. The reassessment is then to run the magnitude ladder on the strongest sites
    by scaling a full design, accepting that the scaled-down charges sit below their own floors,
    and to say so wherever the result is used. That is the alternative D31.1 rejected, and this is
    the condition under which it becomes the better of the two.

F2  EVERYTHING IN ARM B BREAKS. If every Arm B condition is UNRELIABLE, including n=1 at q=0.25 -
    a quarter of one charge, weaker than anything in the record that has ever misbehaved - the
    fault is in the setup and not in the physics. Nothing is concluded; the inputs and the reader
    are diagnosed first.

F3  THE PROTOCOL CANNOT REACH THE DESIGN SPACE. If more than a quarter of the 62 runs fail to
    converge, the finding is about the protocol at these field strengths and not about the
    designs. The envelopes are reported only over the conditions that converged, and the
    non-convergence is reported as the primary result.

F4  THE TWO ARMS DISAGREE AT THE SAME TOTAL. This is NOT a failure and triggers nothing. It is
    what D31.5 expects, and it is the answer rather than a problem with it.

D31.8  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. IT IS ONE BASIS. DIAGNOSTIC 30 found def2-SVPD converged at 1.3000 e and 2.8800 e under
   CAM-B3LYP and NOT converged at 5.6433 e, with gap_DT 10.00 kcal/mol. Every condition here above
   about 3 e inherits that doubt, and no def2-TZVPD arm is run. A condition called RELIABLE here
   is reliable at def2-SVPD and no more than that.
2. ONE FRAME, ONE GRID, IN VACUO, FIXED GEOMETRIES, NO CONTINUUM. No barrier reported from this
   diagnostic is a prediction of a physical barrier.
3. IT GIVES NO RULE. The envelopes are the largest tested designs that passed, on these two site
   pools. DIAGNOSTIC 29 rules out turning either into a constraint a new design must satisfy.
4. ARM B IS NOT THE OPTIMISER'S BEST. Its pool is restricted to sites admitting the whole ladder,
   which costs 0.625 kcal/mol at the best single site.
5. IT DOES NOT REINSTATE A BUDGET. The withdrawn Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 stays withdrawn whatever
   this finds, and nothing here licenses writing a cap into the optimiser.
6. THE RELOCATION AND GAP CRITERIA ARE THE COMMITTED CLASSIFIER'S. Their own scope notes apply,
   including that the dipole criterion is unsafe where ECP centres are present - none are here.
