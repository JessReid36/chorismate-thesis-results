N1.1  WHY THIS NOTE

D50 has 106 runs in three jobs. Its result reads 104 of them. The other two were not measured:
the cluster killed job G2 at its limit of 24 hours while its 12th run of 13 was still going,
and the 13th never started. The job was not submitted again. D50 was collected as it stood.

The result and the files committed with it show that two runs are missing and what the reader
made of them. They do not say that the collection was made that way by choice, on whose advice
and when, or that the unfinished output had been looked at the evening before. This note says
those things.

N1.6  THE CHOICE: COLLECTED AS IT STOOD, NOT SUBMITTED AGAIN

The plain collection, H1, stopped on 23 November 2026 at 08:30:42 and printed: "STOPPED: these
jobs did not run to their end: G2. To finish them, run block RS: it submits again each job
that was cut short, and a job skips every run that ended normally." and, further on: "If they
are NOT going to be run again, tell Claude: the collection can be made as it stands."

Claude advised the second, and not block RS. By its work log it first named that way on 22
November 2026 at about 14:36, after the W of 14:31, when all that was known was that 11 of 13
runs had ended and the job was still going. It advised it outright at about 18:10, after the
look of N1.4 and before the job was cut, and again when H1 stopped. Jessica ran the block for
it, H1A, at 08:34:47. H1A is H1 with the script's "collect anyway".

The reasons, as given then:
   Block RS would start the same slow run again, for up to another 24 hours. (By N1.3 that
   run had by the end gone 20 hours 34 minutes without ending.)
   Treatment J is the auxiliary basis left as it is. The rule of R50.3 turns to it only if
   treatments A and E are not adequate, and the design's four runs with A and E had ended.

What the choice rested on that came from the look: that run 12 was still going and was slow.
The same could have been read from the committed log once the job was cut, but the advice was
given the evening before, from the look.

What was NOT known when the choice was made: any result of job K1, and so whether treatment J
is adequate; any energy or word of any run.

NOT LOOKED INTO THEN, and noted now. The cut run left a file of orbitals, job.gbw, in its
folder on hpc1 (it is in HPC_MANIFEST.sha256), and its input does not tell ORCA to ignore
such a file. ORCA then starts from it. D44's committed outputs show that on hpc1: of the 13
runs that its RDTZ job made on its second submission, the four that had been started before
each print "The File: job.gbw exists" and "INITIAL GUESS: MOREAD", and the nine that had not
print "INITIAL GUESS: MODEL POTENTIAL". Every output of D50 prints "INITIAL GUESS: HCORE"
(the 80 of one electron) or "INITIAL GUESS: MODEL POTENTIAL" (the 25 of the dianion). So "the
same slow run again" was not right: submitted again, run 12 would have started from where
the cut run stood and not from the starting guess its first attempt began with. That was not
a reason for the choice. It is a reason why a run made that way would have had to be
described as such.

The two runs can still be made, under a dated document of their own. This note does not
propose it and does not rule it out.
