N1.1  READING 2's TOLERANCE WAS POOLED ACROSS THE TWO BASES

D20.3 defines

   tol = max( 0.10 x |largest correction entering this reading| , 0.05 kcal/mol )

and cs6_d20.py, as committed at a506e35, computed ONE tol from the largest correction anywhere
in the reading. Because def2-SVPD produced a correction of 9.5558 kcal/mol, that gave

   tol = max(0.10 x 9.5558, 0.05) = 0.9556 kcal/mol

and applied it to def2-SVP as well. def2-SVP's own correction is 0.4092 kcal/mol, which is 38%
of DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's budget, and it was therefore reported as falling UNDER tolerance purely
because a different basis produced a larger number.

THAT CONTRADICTS THE PRE-REGISTRATION'S OWN RULE. D20.1 and D20.3 both state that the two bases
are reported separately and never pooled, and D20.2's tables keep them apart throughout. A
tolerance computed across both is a pooling of them. The correct reading is per basis:

   tol(def2-SVP)  = max(0.10 x 0.4092, 0.05) = 0.0500 kcal/mol
   tol(def2-SVPD) = max(0.10 x 9.5558, 0.05) = 0.9556 kcal/mol

THE CORRECTED READING 2, generated by the corrected code from the same committed outputs:

   READING 2, what does the stable solution do to the barrier?
      basis      lambda      ddE(first)    ddE(stable)     correction  % budget vs its tol
      def2-SVP   0.75          -17.0581       -17.4673        -0.4092      38.4 exceeds
      def2-SVPD  0.75          -10.2346        -0.6788         9.5558     895.6 exceeds
      def2-SVPD  1.00           -6.1422        -2.8258         3.3164     310.8 exceeds
      tol(def2-SVP) = max(0.10 x its own largest |correction|, 0.05) = 0.0500 kcal/mol
      tol(def2-SVPD) = max(0.10 x its own largest |correction|, 0.05) = 0.9556 kcal/mol
      The tolerance is PER BASIS, never pooled across them (D20.1, D20.3; NOTE 1).
      VERDICT: CORRECTION MATERIAL

All three corrections exceed their own basis's tolerance. READING 2's verdict is CORRECTION
MATERIAL before and after, and the two corrections that exceed the 1.067 budget - def2-SVPD at
lambda 0.75 and 1.00 - are the same two. What changes is that def2-SVP's 0.4092 kcal/mol is now
correctly reported as exceeding its tolerance rather than falling under one borrowed from
another basis.

WHAT IS NOT AFFECTED. GATE 1, GATE 3, READING 1 and READING 3 do not use tol. Neither does
D20.3's consequence clause, which is stated against the 1.067 budget and not against tol.
