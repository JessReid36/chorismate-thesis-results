STAGE 6  DIAGNOSTIC 28  ERRATUM 1                                           5 November 2026
THE READER LEFT ITS LOEWDIN AND DIPOLE FIELDS UNPOPULATED

DIAGNOSTIC 28's result document and its pre-registration are NOT edited by this erratum. No figure
in either changes. What changes is the reader, for its next use.

E28.1  THE DEFECT

phase2.2/cs6_d28.py as committed today
   sha256 67a9e6b2f6050cdf9b10d0bf7662fa48202e7d64e2998231185a96497a495838
builds its values file with

   "maxlw_dip": dict(("|".join(map(str, k)), None) for k in R),

which writes a dictionary of None. The Loewdin maximum and the dipole are present in every job.out
and the reader simply does not extract them. DIAGNOSTIC 28's pre-registration listed both under
"RECORDED, NOT GATED" in D28.4, so this is a failure to record something the document promised to
record, not a wrong number.

E28.2  WHAT IT DID AND DID NOT AFFECT

AFFECTED: nothing in STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC28_RESULT_20261105.txt. That document reports the primary,
mechanism and secondary readings, all of which are orbital energies and gaps, and none of which
passes through the unpopulated field. The result stands as committed.

NOT AVAILABLE: the Loewdin and dipole figures D28.4 said would be recorded. They were obtained by
hand from the same committed outputs afterwards and are reported in an uncommitted note
(D28_FINDINGS_AND_WITHDRAWAL_20261105.md, which is not pre-registered and not quotable).

E28.3  THE FIX

The reader now obtains both fields through the COMMITTED classifier's own read(), imported by
explicit path - phase2.2/cs6_audit.py, sha256 1dda394a134b3c72... - rather than by a second
implementation of the same extraction. cs6_d26.py already loads that classifier the same way. The
reader's own orbital and TRAH logic is unchanged, because those are what it exists to do and the
classifier does not do them.

E28.4  WHY THIS IS RECORDED RATHER THAN QUIETLY PATCHED

The reader is committed and was used to produce a committed result. A reader that silently gains a
capability between one use and the next is not reproducible. DIAGNOSTIC 29 depends on exactly these
two fields, so the fix and the defect are both on the record before that diagnostic reads anything.
