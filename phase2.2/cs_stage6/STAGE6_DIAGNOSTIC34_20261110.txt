STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 34
The production barriers at the corrected level of theory, and whether the committed
designs were ever measured on reliable densities.

Pre-registered 10 November 2026, before any D34 output exists and before any of the
three readable criterion legs has been evaluated on the committed frozen outputs.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every barrier this project quotes comes from the frozen half, and every one of them
is at a functional D28 retired and a basis every diagnostic from D30 onward has
abandoned. The committed inputs all carry

   ! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF QMMM EnGrad

D33 stage A measured what the functional costs on the bare substrate at these same
geometries. MEASURED, and the only figures its result states:

   in vacuo barrier   26.8248 kcal/mol at CAM-B3LYP/def2-SVPD, against 18.4610
                      committed   (STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC33_STAGEA_RESULT_20261109.txt)

DERIVED here by subtraction from that line and from D27's committed basis series
(STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC27_RESULT_20261105.txt R27.1: def2-SVP 18.461014, def2-SVPD
18.332256), and stated in neither result:

   the whole rise          26.8248 - 18.461014  = +8.3638
   the basis alone      18.332256 - 18.461014  = -0.1288
   the functional alone    26.8248 - 18.332256  = +8.4925

So the rise is the functional's, and D34 inherits that as its reference.

The committed lowerings were measured against the 18.4610 reference. What they are
against the corrected one is not known, and cannot be obtained by arithmetic: ddE is
a difference of two QM/MM energies, and both move.

Second, and separately: the reliability criterion D31, D32 and D33 use did not exist
when the frozen half was run. No committed document applies it to S6-P, S6-2, S6-3,
S6-5, S6-10, S6-20 or the four ADD-* arrangements. D33 stage B then measured that a
design inside D31's envelope can still fail it (bare_n03_q0.75, UNRELIABLE), so
whether these ten were ever on reliable densities is an open question about numbers
already written down.

3. STAGE A. THE THREE READABLE LEGS, ON THE COMMITTED OUTPUTS, NO COMPUTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage A runs nothing. It applies the committed classifier to the 22 outputs already
in frozen/sp_outputs.tar.xz, using phase2.2/cs6_audit.py's own thresholds:

   D_LW    = 0.25     Loewdin rise against the control at the same leg
   R_DIP   = 2.0      dipole magnitude ratio against the control at the same leg
   GAP_ABS = 1.0 eV   HOMO-LUMO gap floor
   R_GAP   = 0.5      gap ratio against the control at the same leg
   NEL, NOCC = 118, 59

Energies are read with the committed cs6_analyse.py read_run convention - bare,
(MM) and (QM/MM) - and not with a reader written for this diagnostic.

THE FOURTH LEG IS UNREADABLE AND IS NOT SUBSTITUTED. No frozen output carries a
stability analysis, so stage A cannot evaluate the stability leg and does not guess
at it. It follows that stage A can never return RELIABLE: that verdict requires
four legs. Stage A's vocabulary is fixed here as

   FLAGGED                 at least one of the three readable legs fires
   NOT FLAGGED (3 LEGS)    none of the three fires; the stability leg is unknown

and no document may upgrade "NOT FLAGGED (3 LEGS)" to RELIABLE.

STAGE A IS POST HOC AND IS LABELLED SO WHEREVER IT IS REPORTED. The criterion
postdates these runs by two weeks; they were neither designed nor selected under it.
What stage A can do is say whether a criterion the project now applies to every new
design would have fired on the designs whose barriers it already quotes.

R34.1  For each of the ten designs, at each leg, the Loewdin rise, the dipole ratio
       and the gap, against S6-C at the same leg, with the verdict in the stage A
       vocabulary above.
R34.2  The count of designs FLAGGED, and on which legs.
R34.3  If any design is FLAGGED, its committed ddE(frozen) is thereafter reported
       with that flag attached. Nothing is withdrawn by stage A: a flag is a
       statement about the density, and withdrawal of a number requires its own
       dated erratum.

4. STAGE B. 44 RUNS, TWO ARMS, ONE VARIABLE EACH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ARM 1, the lift, 22 runs. Every committed input is copied and exactly two lines
change:

   the method line
      ! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF QMMM EnGrad
   ->  ! CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF QMMM EnGrad
   and, for the control alone, the same pair without QMMM.

   the iteration cap
      %scf MaxIter 250 end  ->  %scf MaxIter 300 end

Both method lines occur in the committed tree and the prepare script must accept
both and still refuse to proceed if neither matches a given input exactly once.
The cap is raised because an iteration cap cannot change a converged result - it can
only truncate a run that would otherwise have finished - and 300 is what D33 used.
Everything else is copied byte for byte: combined.xyz, site.ORCAFF.prms, the %qmmm
block, QMAtoms {0:23}, the charge and multiplicity -2 1, %maxcore, %pal, run names
and directory layout.

Arm 1 therefore differs from the committed runs in the functional and the basis and
in nothing that bears on the answer. In particular arm 1 carries NO stability
analysis, which is what the committed runs also carried, so arm 1 against the
committed ddE is a one-variable comparison.

ARM 2, the classified arm, 22 runs. Arm 1 plus one line:

   %scf
     MaxIter 300
     STABPerform true
   end

This is the Amendment 5 setting and is what every D31, D32 and D33 run used. It
makes the fourth leg readable and makes the reported energy the corrected solution
rather than the first one.

WHETHER ORCA 6.0.1 ACCEPTS STABPerform INSIDE A QM/MM JOB IS NOT ESTABLISHED HERE.
No committed run combines them. Arm 2 is therefore ordered after arm 1 in the job
script, so a rejection costs nothing already measured. If ORCA refuses the input or
the stability analysis does not appear, arm 2 is reported as a failure with ORCA's
own words, its outputs are not committed, a note about the failure is, and a
stability-carrying frozen arm needs its own amendment. Arm 1 stands either way.

ORDER. Arm 1 first, ascending in Sigma|q|, so the seven designs inside D30's
confirmed basis window land before the three outside it; then arm 2 in the same
order. The control runs first in each arm, because it is the gate on everything
else.

COST, measured from the committed outputs' own TOTAL RUN TIME: the 22 frozen runs
have median 5.0 min and maximum 11.3 min at the old level; D33's 16 charged runs at
CAM-B3LYP/def2-SVPD have median 5.7 min and maximum 33.8 min. 44 runs is therefore
expected to be a few hours. Walltime 24:00:00, which routes to day. No -q.

7. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D34.1  Nothing here escapes r_safe. Every sphere in every one of these arrangements
       sits at 2.85-2.96 A, inside D11's measured 3.50 A, and the route (a)/(b)
       decision is open. A corrected barrier for a design inside r_safe is a
       corrected number for an uninterpretable design, and D11's wording stands:
       "No design measured inside r_safe is interpretable."
D34.2  Three of the ten designs are outside the basis window D30 confirmed, and
       S6-20 at Sigma|q| = 5.7600 e is past the 5.6433 e vector at which D30 found
       def2-SVPD not sufficient under CAM-B3LYP. Their corrected barriers are
       reported and are not quotable.
D34.3  These ten arrangements were placed by the OLD A matrix. D33 stage A measured
       that the corrected a_i are a different matrix - Pearson 0.977520, rank
       0.863979, median |a_new/a_old| 1.1212, largest single change -1.8590 at site
       1554 - so a corrected barrier for an old design is not the barrier of the
       design a corrected objective would have chosen. D34 re-reads the record; it
       does not design anything.
D34.4  D33 stage B measured that the reliability envelope does not transfer across
       arrangements. A verdict here is a verdict for that arrangement and for no
       other, and none of these verdicts may be read as a rule about (n, q).
D34.5  Frozen geometries, one frame (42436), in vacuo apart from the MM Lennard-
       Jones term, no continuum, no sampling. No activation free energy follows.
D34.6  The Sigma qi = 0 decision is untaken and D34 does not take it. Of the ten,
       only S6-2 has Sigma q = 0.0000 exactly; the other nine would be voided by it.
       That is recorded as a consequence, not an argument either way.
D34.7  No budget is reinstated. D29 and D31 rule out a cap written as a function of
       the charges and the withdrawn Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 stays withdrawn.
D34.8  Nothing here is shaped toward a lower barrier. Arm 1 is expected to RAISE
       every barrier, because the reference it is measured against rose by 8.3638,
       and if the lowerings shrink that is the result.
