D26.1  WHY, AND WHY NOW

THE OPEN LIMITATION. DIAGNOSTIC 5 established that def2-SVP and def2-SVPD disagree, and D5.4
recorded that without def2-TZVPD neither is shown converged. That is still open. D5.3(d)'s criterion
is |ddE(def2-SVPD) - ddE(def2-SVP)| <= 1.0 kcal/mol, and the measured gap is 0.5183 kcal/mol at
sum|q| = 1.3000 e, which passes, against 1.0847 at 2.8800 e, which fails. The boundary is not
localised between them.

TWO EXPLANATIONS, AND THE PROJECT HAS EVIDENCE FOR ONE AND HAS NOT EXCLUDED THE OTHER.

   (a) SPILL-OUT. Diffuse functions let electron density drift toward a positive point charge that
       presents no repulsion to electrons. Behrens' dissertation names exactly this mechanism on its
       printed page 28: "With larger basis functions, electron density spilling can become a problem,
       where the density is overpolarized due a strong interaction with the pure Coulomb charge with
       no Pauli repulsion", citing Laino et al. 2005, and dismisses it for that work because
       minimal-basis semi-empirical methods cannot exhibit it. This project's DFT can. If this is the
       whole story, def2-SVP is the better production basis on physical grounds and def2-TZVPD is not
       needed to say so.

   (b) REAL INCOMPLETENESS. def2-SVP is simply too small, and part of what def2-SVPD adds is genuine
       polarisation. DIAGNOSTIC 24's Reading 5 states the risk in its own words: "a basis too small to
       respond to the embedding is exactly a basis that might also be too small to respond to real
       polarisation."

THE DISCRIMINATING OBSERVATION, which is what makes this diagnostic possible at all. This project's
Lennard-Jones wall is CLASSICAL. It acts between a sphere and the substrate's nuclei and never
enters the electronic Hamiltonian. It prevents geometric collapse and does nothing to prevent
electronic spill-out. An ECP centre at the charge is a QUANTUM wall and does act on electrons, which
is why Marefat Khah et al. proposed it and why DIAGNOSTICS 11, 18 and 23 carry Ne, He and Ar arms.

So: if the gap is spill-out, a quantum wall on the charges should move the def2-SVPD answer TOWARD
def2-SVP. If the gap is real incompleteness, the wall should not close it, and def2-TZVPD becomes
mandatory before any barrier is quoted.

WHY NOW. D26.3's GATE 2 establishes that the arms needed for this comparison are already committed,
so the question can be asked for no HPC cost. It was not asked earlier because the identity of
DIAGNOSTIC 23's and DIAGNOSTIC 24's charge vectors had not been checked.

D26.3  THE GATES, AND THE TWO THAT HAVE ALREADY RUN

GATE 1, THE SIGN CONVENTION. RUN, AND IT PASSES. Getting this backwards inverts the entire reading,
so it is gated rather than assumed. d_shield is ddE(bare) - ddE(wall), confirmed on eight rows from
three independent committed places: DIAGNOSTIC 11's standoff ladder (five rows), its ma/svp
comparison table (two rows), and DIAGNOSTIC 18 Reading 2's explicitly labelled row. The tolerance is
1.05e-04 kcal/mol, which is the precision of a four-decimal print, since the difference of two such
values carries up to 1e-04 of rounding. Therefore ddE(wall) = ddE(bare) - d_shield.

GATE 2, VECTOR IDENTITY. RUN, AND IT PASSES. Both files were compared site by site rather than
inferred from both being described as "S6-10 at 1.30 e". d24/pc_1.3000e_n10.pc and
d23/ar_d16_S6-10_wall_F1_R/job.inp carry the identical ten-site vector - same charges, same
coordinates, every site agreeing to better than 1e-06, and 0.00e+00 at the transition state. The He
arms match likewise. Every walled arm declares def2-SVPD on its keyword line. The wall0 arms differ
from the vector by exactly the largest charge magnitude in it, which is the expected signature of a
zeroed-charge control and not a mismatch.

GATE 3, CONTROL REPRODUCTION. NOT YET RUN. The def2-SVP and def2-SVPD charge-free controls must
reproduce their committed values to 1e-06 Eh, as every Stage 6 diagnostic requires.

GATE 4, READABILITY. NOT YET RUN. Every arm must be CLEAN or POLARISED under D6.3. A STRAINED or
COLLAPSED arm makes its ddE unquotable; that condition is then dropped and its name recorded, per
AMENDMENT 1's A1.3. DIAGNOSTIC 23 already reports two of its seven conditions as NOT READABLE for
this reason, so this gate is not a formality.

GATE 5, NO ANSWER IN THE QUESTION. The reader must refuse to proceed if this document contains any
f_closed value, any of the four verdict words of D26.4 attached to a figure, or any arithmetic over
A, B and C. A pre-registration that contains its own answer is not one.


D26.4  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE IT IS COMPUTED

The reading is formed per ECP set and the verdict is taken on the MEDIAN of the readable sets, with
every set's value reported individually.

   f_closed >= 0.70          SPILL-OUT. The gap is dominated by density drift onto unwalled charges.
                             def2-SVP is supported as the production basis on physical grounds and
                             not only by DIAGNOSTIC 24 Reading 5's robustness argument. def2-TZVPD
                             remains desirable and stops being the blocking item.

   0.30 <= f_closed < 0.70   MIXED. Both mechanisms contribute. def2-TZVPD is required to apportion
                             them, and the domain statement must say that it is unapportioned.

   0 <= f_closed < 0.30      NOT SPILL-OUT. The wall does not close the gap, so def2-SVP's internal
                             consistency is not evidence that it is right. def2-TZVPD becomes
                             MANDATORY before any barrier is quoted.

   f_closed < 0              The wall moves def2-SVPD AWAY from def2-SVP. Reported as measured. It
                             would falsify the spill-out reading outright, and it is the outcome
                             that most needs to be reportable, so it is named here.

IF THE THREE SETS STRADDLE A BOUNDARY, the verdict is SET-DEPENDENT and no single verdict above is
formed. DIAGNOSTIC 23's finding that three committed verdicts reversed on a 0.0068 to 0.0119
kcal/mol margin is the precedent for naming this outcome in advance rather than discovering it.

PRE-REGISTERED EXPECTATION, recorded so that it can fail. MIXED, leaning spill-out. The basis for
the expectation is that the committed d_shield magnitudes at def2-SVPD are 0.17 to 0.44 kcal/mol
against a bare-basis gap of 0.5183 kcal/mol at this field strength - the same order, but not
obviously larger. This is an expectation and not a result, and the arithmetic that would settle it
has deliberately not been done.


D26.5  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE, WHATEVER IT RETURNS

1. IT CANNOT ESTABLISH BASIS CONVERGENCE. Only a higher-zeta basis can. A high f_closed says the GAP
   is explained; it does not say def2-SVP is converged. D5.4's requirement stands either way.
2. THE ECP SETS ARE ONE LINEAGE. Ne, He and Ar come from one paper by one group fitted by one recipe,
   as ECP_CONVENTION_20261102.md records. Three sets bound the sensitivity from below, not from
   above, and this diagnostic inherits that bound exactly as DIAGNOSTIC 23 did.
3. ONE FRAME, 42436, ONE CHARGE VECTOR, at sum|q| = 1.3000 e. That is the one field strength inside
   D5.3(d)'s passing region, which makes it the cleanest place to ask the question and also the least
   demanding. The 2.8800 e and 5.6433 e cells have no matching DIAGNOSTIC 23 arm, so extending the
   test to them is not free and is not claimed here.
4. FROZEN GEOMETRIES, IN VACUO, and the spheres do not move between the two states.
5. THE MULTIREFERENCE QUESTION IS UNTOUCHED. DIAGNOSTIC 19's Reading 3 returned DISAGREE and still
   does, and a basis-set reading says nothing about it.
6. IT LICENSES NO BARRIER. The catalytic verdict remains SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.5's chain.
7. ITS BARE PAIR COMES FROM AN EXPLORATORY DIAGNOSTIC. DIAGNOSTIC 24 was run before a
   pre-registration existed and its own record states that nothing in it may be quoted as a Stage 6
   result in its present form. This diagnostic therefore cannot be quoted in the thesis until
   DIAGNOSTIC 24's cells are re-registered and re-run. It is run now because it is free and because
   it tells the project whether the def2-TZVPD campaign is needed; it is not a substitute for that
   re-registration.
