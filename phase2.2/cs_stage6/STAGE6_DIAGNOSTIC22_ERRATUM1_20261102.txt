Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 22 ERRATUM 1 (2 November 2026).

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC22_20261101.txt is not edited. Committed BEFORE DIAGNOSTIC 22's result is read,
so no criterion is re-read after its data arrived. No gate, threshold or verdict rule is changed,
and the thirty runs are unchanged and remain correct.


E22.1  WHAT IS WRONG

D22 took its nine conditions from DIAGNOSTIC 21 without re-deriving them, and inherited the error
recorded in STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC21_ERRATUM1_20261102.txt. Two of the nine carry no readable Dset:

   S6-2_D16    DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 1: NOT READABLE   bare S6-2_bare_F1_R STRAINED, gap 0.567 eV
   S6-5_D17    DIAGNOSTIC 17 READING 2: NOT READABLE   bare S6-5_bare_M_R  STRAINED, gap 0.880 eV

   [both measured, from the committed DIAGNOSTIC16_RESULT.txt and DIAGNOSTIC17_RESULT.txt]

Two statements in D22 are therefore wrong as written.

   D22.1's leg table reads "bare | VERIFIED STABLE | DIAGNOSTIC 21, nine conditions, 18 runs".
   The stability is verified for nine; the readable d_shield exists for seven.

   D22.4's ALL ARMS VERIFIED clause reads "Then every readable d_shield in this project rests on
   verified-stable solutions in ALL FOUR LEGS ... and the quantity the thesis quotes is
   stability-verified end to end." That is true of the SEVEN readable conditions and vacuous for
   the other two.


E22.2  WHY STABILITY CANNOT REPAIR IT

The two excluded conditions fail a DIFFERENT test from the one D22 applies. D6.3 classifies an
arm STRAINED on an electronic-health indicator - here a HOMO-LUMO gap of 0.567 and 0.880 eV
against the control's own gap. D22 measures whether the SCF solution is a minimum with respect to
orbital rotation. A solution can be a perfectly good minimum and still sit on a collapsing gap.

So for these two, ALL SIX ARMS STABLE would be a true statement about their wavefunctions and
would NOT make their d_shield quotable. Nothing D22 can return changes that.


E22.3  WHAT THE READING REPORTS INSTEAD

The verdict rule of D22.4 is NOT changed: ALL ARMS VERIFIED still requires all thirty stable and
all six arms of all nine conditions stable, exactly as pre-registered. What changes is what is
printed beside it, so that the result does not assert something false:

   (a) READING 2's per-condition verdict column, for S6-2_D16 and S6-5_D17 only, reads
       ARMS STABLE, NO READABLE Dset
       instead of ARMS VERIFIED, with the reason named on the line.

   (b) The sentence printed when the verdict is ALL ARMS VERIFIED names the seven conditions
       that have a readable d_shield, and states separately that the other two have stable arms
       and no readable Dset.

   (c) READING 4, if it fires for either of those two, states that the correction applies to a
       quantity that is already not readable, so it bounds nothing that is quoted.

cs6_d22.py is corrected to print (a), (b) and (c) and is committed BEFORE the reading runs, so
DIAGNOSTIC22_RESULT.txt is produced by committed code. The correction touches only printed text
and the per-condition label; no gate, no tolerance, no arithmetic and no verdict rule is altered.


E22.4  WHAT STANDS

The thirty runs, the three gates, and all four readings stand as pre-registered. The measured
d_shield formula in D22.1, reproduced against two committed Dset values before the pre-registration
was written, stands. The scope note distinguishing the zero-charge wall's two uses stands.

D22's purpose is narrowed, not withdrawn: it establishes that the walled and wall-at-zero legs of
the SEVEN readable d_shield values rest on verified-stable solutions, and it measures the same
arms for two further conditions whose Dset is not readable for an unrelated reason.
