STAGE 6 - ERRATUM 6, 26 October 2026

Follows STAGE6_ERRATUM5_20261026.txt. Pre-registrations and committed results are never edited;
this records a confound in Erratum 5's central negative result and a defect in the indicator D6.3
reads for one class of run. Both were found by reading the committed ORCA outputs directly while
diagnosing Diagnostic 8.


E6.1  ERRATUM 5 E5.3 COMPARED TWO DIFFERENT PHYSICAL MODELS

E5.3 sets the Stage 4c arrangements E5 and E7 beside the Stage 6 design S6-5 and observes that all
three carry sum|q| = 3.000 at max|q| = 1.00, that E5 and E7 are STRAINED and S6-5 is CLEAN, and
therefore that no global scalar on the charge vector orders the def2-SVP verdicts. It states that
"E5 and S6-5 differ in arrangement, not amount."

THAT STATEMENT IS WRONG, AND THE ERROR IS NOT SMALL.

Read from the committed input of phase2.2/cs_stage4c/L0_E5_R, which also appears in full in that
run's own output echo, E5's three charges are placed as:

   Ne>  1.000000  47.48070500  24.05965300  54.92320200  NewECP
     N_core 10 ... lmax f ...

These are not point charges. ORCA's own words for them, printed in the same output, are

   > coreless ECP center with (optional) point charge
     Group 1, Type Ne ECP (replacing 10 core electrons, lmax=3)

The ECP's projectors supply short-range repulsion at the charge: A PAULI WALL. Every Stage 6 design,
by contrast, places its charges through %pointcharges "<file>.pc" - bare charges with no wall.

So E5 and E7 differ from S6-5 in the one variable Finding 4 identifies as the defect, and the one
Erratum 4 E4.6 records B1 adding. The comparison in E5.3 varies the Pauli wall and the arrangement
together. It is not a controlled comparison and it cannot carry the weight E5.3 puts on it.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CHANGE. The charge magnitudes in E5.3 are correct: +1, -1, +1 for E5 and
-1, +1, -1 for E7, now confirmed from the primary input rather than inferred, and E5's peak field of
2.920 V/A at a closest approach of 2.581 A is reproduced to three decimals by cs9_audit.py on a
different code path from y03's. What is withdrawn is the inference. E5.3's conclusion - that no
global scalar orders the verdicts - is NOT restored to doubt in the direction of rescuing sum|q|:
Diagnostic 9 tests peak local field within one model class, on 225 runs rather than eight, and
falsifies it. The correction is that E5.3 reached a right-shaped conclusion from a comparison that
was not entitled to deliver it.

E5.3's withdrawal of the recommendation sum|q| <= 3.0 e stands, on D9's evidence rather than on
E5.3's.


E6.2  FOR ONE CLASS OF RUN, D6.3's LOEWDIN CRITERION READS THE DESIGN'S OWN CHARGE BACK

ORCA lists a coordinate-block charge centre in its LOEWDIN ATOMIC CHARGES table under the element
label Q, and prints the centre's CHARGE on that line. From L0_E5_R:

   23 O :   -0.263337
   24 Q :    1.000000
   25 Q :   -1.000000
   26 Q :    1.000000

cs6_audit.read() matches that label like any other element, and Q is not in its METALS exclusion
list. So for any run whose charges are coordinate-block centres, "the largest Loewdin charge on a
light atom" is the largest magnitude of the design's own charges. D6.3's MOVED test then fires on
any design carrying |q| >= about 0.64 e, with no reference to the electron density whatever.

The same runs' dipoles are contaminated for the same reason: the ECP centres are part of the QM
system, so their charges enter ORCA's printed dipole. L0_E5_R reports 57.93 D against a charge-free
control median of 8.16 D, and R_DIP fires on that too.

SCOPE, MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED. This affects ONLY runs whose charges sit in the coordinate
block: 101 runs, all in cs_stage4b and cs_stage4c. Runs that place charges through a .pc file are
NOT affected - ORCA does not list those in the Loewdin table at all, confirmed over every
point-charge-file single point in the corpus: 24 Loewdin entries each, none of them Q. EVERY STAGE 6
DESIGN USES A .pc FILE. D6's verdicts on the Stage 6 designs, and Finding 4 with them, are untouched
by this erratum.

WHAT IT CHANGES WHERE IT APPLIES. D9 reports both readings side by side and changes no rule: D6.3 as
written, and D6.3 with the Q centres taken out of the Loewdin table and the dipole test suspended
for those runs. Taking them out is what D6.3's own phrase "on a light atom" says - a Q centre is not
an atom - and suspending the dipole comparison is the only honest option when the quantity printed
is not the electron density's dipole. 49 of the 63 affected runs change verdict. Every COLLAPSED
among them becomes STRAINED: with a Pauli wall and the charge centres excluded, the density does not
move, and what is left is an unwell SCF. On this record no ECP-shielded charge collapses.

The healthy/sick split is invariant under the correction, since it moves runs only between CLEAN and
POLARISED and between STRAINED and COLLAPSED, so Diagnostic 9's field test is unaffected either way.


E6.3  WHAT THE CORPUS TURNS OUT TO CONTAIN THAT NOTHING HAS READ

cs_stage4b and cs_stage4c hold a matched series that isolates the Pauli wall: the Z_* and *_q0 runs
place the same ECP centres with every charge EXACTLY zero. The wall is present and there is no
charge. Set against the charge-free controls at the same basis, those runs are reported in
DIAGNOSTIC9_RESULT.txt reading (h), and on this record the wall alone is inert in all four
indicators. That is an observation on runs that already exist and not a designed experiment, and it
is qualified as such there.
