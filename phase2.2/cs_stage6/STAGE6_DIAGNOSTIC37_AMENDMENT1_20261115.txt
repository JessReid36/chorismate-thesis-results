--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1.1  WHAT HAPPENED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D37 ran on 11 November 2026 and is committed at results 64ca690. Five of its six
candidates were measured. The sixth, revDSD-PBEP86-D4, was not: ORCA 6.0.1 stopped all
four of its runs before any calculation, in these words (d37/revDSD_SV_R/job.out):

                                 INPUT ERROR
            UNRECOGNIZED OR DUPLICATED KEYWORD(S) IN SIMPLE INPUT LINE
              RI-REVDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021

D37's pre-registration had said its keywords were checked against the manual and not
against a run, and that a rejected line "needs its own dated amendment. It is not swapped
silently." This is that amendment.

THE ERROR WAS IN THE PRE-REGISTRATION, NOT IN THE FUNCTIONAL. The manual's sentence about
the RI- prefix was read as applying to every keyword of its table. ORCA 6.0.1 does not
accept it on this one. The same prefix on PWPB95 was accepted, and that run did use RI
(d37/PWPB95_TZ_R/job.out, "RI-MP2 ENERGY", 1664 correlation-fitting functions).

D37's four failed runs stay in the record as they are. They are results.

A1.2  WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10 single points on the same committed geometries, d33a/sp_42436_{R,TS}/substrate.xyz,
charge -2, multiplicity 1, in vacuo, in a new directory d37a1.

THE CONTROL, 2 runs, FIRST. D28's CAM-B3LYP input at def2-SVPD, byte for byte, as in D37.

TWO ARMS, 4 runs each: def2-SVPD and def2-TZVPD, reactant and transition state. Both
carry the same first line, which is D37's rejected line with the prefix removed and
nothing else changed (X is the basis):

      ! revDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021 X def2/J X/C RIJCOSX TightSCF

   ARM RI adds one line to the input, directly above the geometry line:
      %mp2 RI true end
   It asks for the RI approximation in the second-order part by the %mp2 block instead
   of by the prefix. PWPB95 in D37 used RI, so this is the arm that treats revDSD as D37
   treated its other double hybrid.

   ARM K adds nothing. It is the control input with its first line replaced, which is
   exactly what D37 pre-registered for every candidate. What ORCA then does in the
   second-order part, RI or not, is read from the output and reported; it is not assumed.

WHY TWO ARMS. One manual-based keyword has already failed. The two arms ask for the same
functional by two routes, so that a second rejection of one route does not leave the
candidate unmeasured again. If both run, their difference is the effect of the RI
approximation on this barrier and is reported.

CHECKED IN THE ORCA 6.0 MANUAL ONLINE, 11 November 2026, AND NOT AGAINST A RUN: its keyword
table lists revDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021 without a prefix, one of its worked examples uses that
keyword with a /C set and no prefix, and its list of %mp2 keywords includes RI, default
false. def2-SVPD/C and def2-TZVPD/C are known to exist in ORCA 6.0.1: D37's PWPB95 runs
used them.

ORDER. The control; arm RI then arm K at def2-SVPD; arm RI then arm K at def2-TZVPD. A run
that fails does not stop the runs after it. 8 cores, 48 GB, walltime 24:00:00, no -q, job
name cm_d37a1, its own directory and lock. How long a second-order part without RI takes
at def2-TZVPD is not known.

A1.3  READINGS AND THE RULE FOR WHICH ARM IS ENTERED, FIXED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Energies and orbital energies are read exactly as D37 reads them (last FINAL SINGLE POINT
ENERGY; cs6_d28.py read_out under D28's rule).

R-A1.1  The control: its two energies and its barrier beside D28's.
R-A1.2  For each arm that ran: whether its outputs say RI was used in the second-order
        part, its barrier at both basis sets, the shift between them, and the highest
        occupied orbital at def2-SVPD, reactant, with D37's criterion (BOUND below 0.0 eV)
        and its distance from -0.5123 eV. As in D37, a double hybrid's orbital energies
        are those of its hybrid SCF step.
R-A1.3  If both arms ran: arm RI minus arm K, for the barrier at each basis set.
R-A1.4  THE ENTRY FOR revDSD-PBEP86-D4 in D37's readings R37.2 to R37.6 is
           arm RI, if all four of its runs pass the gates and its outputs say RI was used;
           otherwise arm K, if all four of its runs pass the gates, labelled with what its
              outputs say about RI;
           otherwise NOT MEASURED.
        The choice is made by this rule and not by the numbers.
R-A1.5  D37's table of the two tests, as its result document prints it, with the entry
        of R-A1.4 added as a ninth row. D37's own rows are read from d37.tsv and are not
        recomputed or changed.
R-A1.6  Test 2 for the entry: its def2-TZVPD barrier minus D36's DLPNO-CCSD(T)/def2-TZVPD
        barrier. D36's result is committed and prints 23.8603 kcal/mol. The reader takes
        that number from the result document and requires it to agree with the barrier
        recomputed from d36a2/B_{R,TS}/job.out, which is where D36's result says its
        coupled-cluster arm is read from. D37's own reader looks under d36, where that
        arm has no output; that is why D37's R37.5 could not have been evaluated by it,
        and it is not relied on here.
