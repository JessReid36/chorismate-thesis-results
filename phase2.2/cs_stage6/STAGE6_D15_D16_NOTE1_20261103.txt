N1.1  WHAT DIAGNOSTIC 23 ESTABLISHED ABOUT THESE THREE READINGS

DIAGNOSTIC 23 ran the He and Ar ecp-all parameter sets of the same paper through both arms of the
seven readable conditions, 50 single points, against a pre-registration committed before they
existed. Three of the four readings that compare arrangements REVERSE under the He set, which
EXPERIMENT 0 validated against its own published fitting target to 1.381e-06 Eh:

   reading                              committed (Ne)         under He              margin was
   DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 1              FALLS WITH n           NOT MONOTONIC         0.0068
   DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 3              COOPERATIVITY FALLS    FLAT                  0.0070
   DIAGNOSTIC 15 READING 1              NOT BOUNDED            BOUNDED               0.0119
   DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 2              CONSISTENT             CONSISTENT            0.0721 -> 0.0126

Recomputed from the He d_shield values, independently of DIAGNOSTIC 23's own reader:

   D16 R1  Ne  Dset 0.4379 0.4311 0.2430 0.1702  steps +0.0068 +0.1881 +0.0728  FALLS WITH n
   D16 R1  He  Dset 0.2668 0.2710 0.1449 0.1057  steps -0.0042 +0.1261 +0.0392  NOT MONOTONIC
   D15 R1  Ne  Dset 0.4140 - Dsum 0.3521 = +0.0619                              NOT BOUNDED
   D15 R1  He  Dset 0.2413 - Dsum 0.3521 = -0.1108                              BOUNDED
   D16 R3  Ne  excess +0.1071 and +0.1641   difference -0.0570                   COOPERATIVITY FALLS
   D16 R3  He  excess -0.0062 and -0.0086   difference +0.0024                   FLAT

Every number above is computed from the energies, not from the four-decimal figures beside it.
Doing it the other way gives 0.0127 rather than 0.0126 for DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 2, a response
ratio of 10.34 rather than 10.314, and the two differences in the table above as -0.0571 rather
than -0.0570 and +0.0023 rather than +0.0024. All four stood in earlier drafts of these documents
and are recorded here because that shortcut has now produced a wrong figure five times in this
project. cs6_y53.py GATE 6 re-derives every figure in these five documents, in the draft's new
text and in the commit message from the pushed outputs, and refuses to write any it cannot
account for; the last two were found by that gate and not by reading.

STAGE6_RECORD_CORRECTIONS_20261102.md C1 recorded that these three rest on margins of 0.0068,
0.0070 and 0.0119 kcal/mol while the term subtracted to obtain their Dset values is 0.18 to 0.72,
and said they should be reported as properties of this parameter set until this experiment had
run. It has run. They are.


N1.2  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY ABOUT THE COMMITTED RESULTS

NOTHING IN DIAGNOSTIC 15's OR DIAGNOSTIC 16's RESULT FILES IS WRONG. Each verdict is correct for
the parameter set, basis, geometry and tolerance it was computed under, and each result file
states all four. What changes is the SCOPE of the three readings above. They may not be quoted as
properties of the designed arrangements. Each is a property of

   the Marefat Khah et al. Ne ecp-all parameter set,
   at def2-SVPD, in vacuo, on frame 42436.

DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 2 may be quoted, with its margin stated as 0.0126 under the alternative set
rather than the committed 0.0721.

Of the two consequences, the second is the larger. Under He both of DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 3's
excesses go NEGATIVE: the collective Dset becomes smaller than the sum of its single-site parts.
Under Ne the collective exceeded the parts, and that excess was this project's evidence of
cooperativity between charges. The cooperativity DIAGNOSTIC 12 and DIAGNOSTIC 14 reported is
therefore not robust to the parameter set, and no claim of cooperativity may rest on it.


N1.3  WHAT IS UNAFFECTED

- EVERY BARE-ARM QUANTITY. The barrier lowerings of 4.9965 to 10.6714 kcal/mol and the frozen
  barriers of 7.6609 to 13.3357 are bare-arm quantities. No bare arm was run in DIAGNOSTIC 23 and
  none is touched by this note.
- THE READABILITY WINDOW. D6.3's classification of every arm, D17's per-site ceiling of 0.5389 to
  0.6500 e, and the two NOT READABLE exclusions are unaffected. All seven conditions were readable
  for both alternative sets, so no condition was dropped and the comparison is on equal footing.
- STABILITY. DIAGNOSTICS 20, 21 and 22 measured solutions, not parameter sensitivity.
- THE PROJECTOR'S INERTNESS. All 440 centres across DIAGNOSTIC 23's 48 walled arms report nbasis
  402 and NEL 118, equal to the charge-free control, and are labelled Q in the Loewdin block. The
  centre contributes no basis function and no electron with He and Ar parameters as it did with Ne.


N1.4  WHAT THIS IS NOT EVIDENCE FOR

He and Ar are not independent of Ne: one paper, one group, one fitting recipe, one ansatz. This
bounds the sensitivity FROM BELOW. A parameter set from outside that lineage could move these
quantities further, and ECP_ASSESSMENT_20261102.md Section 3 records that the reference library
contains no such set. Nothing here says the wall is wrong, or right: d_shield has no third arm,
and the comparison the remedy literature uses exists in this record for one site, one basis pair
and one integer charge only.
