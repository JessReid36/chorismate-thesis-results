STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 38
Do embedding point charges reach the correlated step? A mechanism probe, not a barrier.

Pre-registered 14 November 2026, before any D38 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D36 measured the functional error on the BARE barrier: CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD sits
+1.8400 kcal/mol above DLPNO-CCSD(T)/def2-TZVPD at frame 42436. What the record quotes
is not a bare barrier but ddE, the change a charge arrangement produces, and D34's three
quotable values are -3.3985, -4.1567 and -5.3701 kcal/mol. The bare error is therefore
34 to 54 per cent of the signal, and whether that matters depends entirely on whether
the error cancels between the bare and the charged system. NOTHING IN THIS RECORD
MEASURES THAT. D36 never ran a charged system.

The test that would measure it is a coupled-cluster calculation with the arrangement's
charges present. It is affordable for a reason worth stating: the ORCA 6.0 manual says
of an external point-charge file that it "adds the point charge contribution to the
one-electron matrix and the nuclear repulsion", so the charges add no electrons and no
basis functions and the correlation problem is the same size as the bare one. D36's two
legs took 4.1 and 6.7 hours.

   source: https://www.faccts.de/docs/orca/6.0/manual/contents/detailed/coords.html
   read 14 November 2026

BUT THE PRODUCTION DESIGNS DO NOT USE AN EXTERNAL POINT-CHARGE FILE. Checked while
writing this: D34's inputs use the QM/MM module with an ORCAFF force field, and
cs6_analyse.py states that ddE is taken on the QM/MM total because "the score this is
measured against contains L, the sphere's Lennard-Jones energy at TS minus at R, so the
measurement must contain it too". The Lennard-Jones term is pure molecular mechanics: it
depends on the geometry and the force field and not at all on the QM method, so it
cancels exactly when two methods' ddE are compared. What a coupled-cluster run must
reproduce is therefore the embedding electrostatics, not the whole QM/MM energy.

WHETHER DLPNO-CCSD(T) HONOURS EMBEDDING CHARGES IS NOT ESTABLISHED. Checked while
writing this: the manual's point-charge page states no restriction to any method; the
multiscale overview says "Use all kinds of available electronic structure methods as QM
method"; the QM/MM page's only explicit DLPNO mention is for MOL-CRYSTAL-QMMM, a
different scheme. I could not find a positive statement that the combination works, and
I could not find one that it does not. 653 committed inputs in this project use
%pointcharges - qscale, standoff, sitescan - and none of them uses a correlated method.

THE FAILURE MODE THAT MATTERS IS NOT AN ERROR MESSAGE. It is that ORCA accepts the
keyword, applies the charges in the SCF, and the correlated step does not see them. The
output would look entirely normal and the number would be a bare correlation energy on
a shifted reference. That would pass every gate written so far. D38 exists to make that
failure visible before a week of node time is spent on it.

2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Five single points, all on the committed bare substrate of frame 42436's REACTANT,
d33a/sp_42436_R/substrate.xyz, QM charge -2, multiplicity 1, frozen geometry.

   ! DLPNO-CCSD(T) def2-SVPD AutoAux TightSCF TightPNO

   run   embedding charge                                     source
   N     none. no %pointcharges line at all                   the common reference
   B0     0.000000 e at 44.20782400 19.21545700 38.95945100   inertness check for B
   B1    +1.000000 e at the same position                     ADD-s1690_qp10000
   A0     0.000000 e at 45.44147300 23.87423600 30.61699600   inertness check for A
   A1    -1.000000 e at the same position                     ADD-s678_qm10000

BOTH ARRANGEMENTS ARE ALREADY IN THE FIGURES, which is why these two and not others.
Checked while writing this, in the committed figure data:

   ADD-s1690_qp10000   F6, F7, T3, T4, T5, controls.tsv, derived.tsv
   ADD-s678_qm10000    F6, F7, F9, T3, T5, controls.tsv

So whatever D38 and the test it licenses measure can be carried into figures that already
exist, rather than needing a new one.

THE BASIS IS def2-SVPD AND NOT def2-SVP, DELIBERATELY. def2-SVPD is the production basis
and it carries diffuse functions. That matters here and not only for consistency: D34
flagged 7 of 10 arrangements UNRELIABLE at CAM-B3LYP/def2-SVPD while stage A at
B3LYP/def2-SVP flagged 0 of 10, and def2-SVP has no diffuse functions to see whatever
def2-SVPD is seeing. A probe at def2-SVP could not show it either.

BOTH SIGNS ARE RUN, AND BOTH ARE REAL. A1 is the largest quotable arrangement; B1 is the
committed positive arrangement at the same magnitude. Neither charge nor position is
invented: both are read out of their design's own site.ORCAFF.prms and combined.xyz, and
G38.6 requires the derivation to be shown and to agree with what the design's name
encodes.

ORDER. N first, then B0, B1, then A0, A1. The reference and each inertness check come
before the run that depends on them, and the B arm comes before the A arm because section
3 is the reason: B1 is the arrangement the record says is electronically bound and A1 the
one it says is not, so the interpretable probe runs first.

RESOURCES. One job, the five runs sequentially, 16 cores, 120 GB, walltime 24:00:00,
which routes to day. No -q. The job runs in node-local /scratch and clears each run's
intermediates before starting the next, so the footprint is one run at a time: this is a
shared cluster and D36 at a larger basis peaked at 74 GB. D36's three guards carry over -
free space checked before the run, the PNO storage mode written to /home within minutes,
and output copied back every ten minutes rather than only at the end.

3. WHAT THE RECORD ALREADY SAYS ABOUT THESE TWO ARRANGEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Checked while writing this, and it bears on how a failure in either arm must be read.

STAGE6_FINDINGS_20261022.md Finding 8 measured the dianion's highest occupied orbital at
the reactant geometry with each design's charges present, at def2-SVP:

   S6-C, bare                                    +2.278 eV   not bound
   S6-P / ADD-s1690 (+1 at site 1690)            -0.243 eV   BOUND
   ADD-s678 (-1.00)                              +5.205 eV   not bound

and its own words for what that means: "A positive HOMO means the highest electrons are
not bound; the calculation only looks well-behaved because def2-SVP has no diffuse
functions to let an electron leave." It concludes "Only the single positive sphere at
Arg90's own position binds the dianion."

The vertical detachment energies point the same way. STAGE6_PROBE1_ERRATUM1_20261027.txt
puts the bare substrate at +0.5013 eV at def2-SVPD, bound. Probe 2 at site 1690 measured
+3.4366 eV for +1, MORE bound than bare, and -2.1005 eV for -1 at the same position,
METASTABLE.

SO THE TWO PROBES ARE NOT EQUALLY SAFE, AND B1 IS THE SAFE ONE. B1's charge is the only
one in the record that makes this dianion more bound. A1's sign is the one Probe 2 found
makes it metastable, and A1's own arrangement had the highest positive HOMO of the ten at
def2-SVP. def2-SVPD, unlike def2-SVP, does have the diffuse functions to let an electron
leave, so A1's reference may not be a bound state at all here. That is a measurement this
probe will make and not an assumption it rests on.

WHAT FOLLOWS FOR THE GATES is G38.10, the interpretability contingency, written below
before any output exists. A coupled-cluster calculation on an unbound reference is the
case the record already quotes Dreuw and Cederbaum on, and G38.10 gives their words.

A CORRECTION TO SOMETHING I SAID BEFORE WRITING THIS. Looking at the first ten rows of
figures/T5_positive_charge_vs_verdict.tsv I told Jessica that D34's reliability screen
eliminates exactly the designs carrying positive charge. Within those ten that is true:
7 of 7 with positive charge are UNRELIABLE and 3 of 3 without are RELIABLE. ACROSS THE
WHOLE TABLE IT IS FALSE. Of 46 rows, 43 carry positive charge and 17 of those are
RELIABLE, including wall_n02_q1.00 and wall_n03_q1.00 at a full +1.0000 e, which is the
wall rescuing unit charge at small n exactly as F9's node N8 already records. The table's own
caption says it: "The designs differ in site, sign pattern, n and representation as well
as magnitude; this table is a tabulation, not a test." The sign of the charge does not set
the verdict, and D38 does not assume it does.

4. READINGS, FIXED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R38.1  E(0), the Hartree-Fock reference, for each of the five runs, and the shifts
       A1 minus N and B1 minus N. This says whether the charge reached the SCF.
R38.2  E(CORR)(corrected) for each run, and the shifts A1 minus N and B1 minus N. THIS
       IS WHAT THE DIAGNOSTIC EXISTS TO ANSWER: whether the charge reached the
       correlation treatment or only the reference.
R38.3  E(CCSD(T)) for each run and the shifts, with the triples correction separated.
R38.4  The T1 diagnostic for each run. A first look at whether an embedding charge
       degrades the single-reference quality and whether the sign of the charge matters.
R38.5  The highest occupied orbital for each run, by the committed cs10_cpcm.py
       convention, beside D36's measured HF values of -1.9636 eV at the reactant.
R38.6  Whether ORCA reports how many point charges it read, and what it reports. If it
       prints no such count, that is stated rather than assumed either way.
R38.7  The highest occupied orbital of each run at def2-SVPD, against Finding 8's
       def2-SVP values of +2.278, -0.243 and +5.205 eV and against D36's measured HF
       value of -1.9636 eV for the bare substrate at def2-TZVPD. This is the first
       measurement of these arrangements at a basis with diffuse functions, and it is
       what decides G38.10 for each arm.

5. GATES. ANY FAILURE STOPS THE DIAGNOSTIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G38.1  INERTNESS. A0 and B0 must each reproduce N's E(0), E(CORR)(corrected) and
       E(CCSD(T)) to within 1e-8 Eh. A point charge of zero magnitude cannot change a
       converged energy. If one does, the file is being misparsed and nothing else here
       is readable.
G38.2  THE CHARGE REACHES THE SCF. |E(0)[A1] - E(0)[N]| must exceed 1e-6 Eh, and the
       same for B1. If it does not, the point-charge file is not being read at all.
G38.3  THE DECISIVE GATE. |E(CORR)(corrected)[A1] - E(CORR)(corrected)[N]| must exceed
       1e-6 Eh, and the same for B1. If E(0) moves and E(CORR) does not, the correlated
       step is blind to the embedding charges, the route is void, and no coupled-cluster
       ddE can be computed this way. A PASS HERE IS THE ONLY THING THAT LICENSES THE
       EXPENSIVE TEST.
G38.4  Every run terminates normally, converges its SCF, reports 118 electrons, and
       prints a CCSD(T) energy. A run that terminates normally without one is a failure.
G38.5  Every geometry is byte-identical by sha256 to d33a/sp_42436_R/substrate.xyz.
G38.6  Each charge and position is derived from its design's committed site.ORCAFF.prms
       and combined.xyz, the derivation is printed, and the charge equals what the
       design's own name encodes: qm06500 is -0.6500, qm07625 is -0.7625, qm10000 is
       -1.0000, qp10000 is +1.0000. Nothing is invented.
G38.7  The five inputs are identical except for the presence and the filename of the
       %pointcharges line. The method line, basis, auxiliary, PNO thresholds, QM charge
       and multiplicity are the same in all five.
G38.8  The QM charge stays -2 and the multiplicity 1 in all five. The embedding charge
       is never folded into the QM charge.
G38.9  No file under d28, d33a, d34, d36 or d36a2 is modified by this diagnostic.
G38.10 THE INTERPRETABILITY CONTINGENCY, written before any output exists so that it
       cannot be reached for afterwards. An arm whose reference is not a bound state is
       not evidence about anything, because the record's own citation of Dreuw and
       Cederbaum says a resonance "can in principle not be correctly computed with bound
       state methods". So: an arm whose SCF fails to converge, or whose highest occupied
       orbital at def2-SVPD is at or above zero, is reported NOT INTERPRETABLE, and the
       mechanism verdict rests on whichever arm is interpretable.
          - If B1 is interpretable, G38.2 and G38.3 are decided on B1.
          - If B1 is not interpretable and A1 is, they are decided on A1, and that is
            stated prominently because it is the weaker evidence.
          - IF NEITHER ARM IS INTERPRETABLE, D38 HAS NO VERDICT. The expensive test is
            not licensed, and the open question becomes whether a coupled-cluster
            treatment of these arrangements is available at all at a diffuse basis.
       This contingency relaxes nothing: G38.1's inertness requirement and G38.4 to
       G38.9 apply to every arm regardless, and an arm reported NOT INTERPRETABLE is
       still reported, with its numbers, rather than dropped.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D38.1  IT IS A MECHANISM TEST AND NOT A BARRIER. One geometry, the reactant only, no
       transition state, no ddE. It produces no barrier of any kind and none may be
       quoted from it.
D38.2  It does not establish that %pointcharges reproduces the QM/MM module's
       electrostatics. The charges here are read from the force field the QM/MM runs
       used, but whether the two routes give the same QM energy is a separate control
       at density functional level and belongs to the real test, not here.
D38.3  A pass licenses building the real test. It licenses nothing else.
D38.4  IT DOES NOT REVISIT ANY D34 VERDICT. ADD-s1690_qp10000 is UNRELIABLE at
       CAM-B3LYP/def2-SVPD and remains so. It is used here because it is the committed
       positive arrangement at the same magnitude as A1, not because its verdict is in
       question.
D38.5  T1 and the highest occupied orbital at one geometry with one charge are a first
       look. They are not a reference-quality verdict, not a multireference check, and
       not evidence about any other arrangement.
D38.6  No design conclusion, no reliability claim, nothing about r_safe, nothing about
       any barrier lowering, and nothing about which designs survive.
D38.7  IT DOES NOT TEST WHETHER THE SIGN OF A CHARGE SETS D34's VERDICT. Section 3
       records that it does not, across the record's own 46 classified rows. Two
       arrangements of opposite sign cannot test that and are not run for that purpose;
       they are run because one is bound and one may not be, and because both are
       already in the figures.
D38.8  Nothing here is shaped toward an outcome. If the correlated step turns out blind
       to the embedding charges, that is the result, it is reported as plainly as the
       opposite, and it closes this route rather than being worked around.
