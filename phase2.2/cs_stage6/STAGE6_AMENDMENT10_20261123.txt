A10.1  WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS

THE DESIGN. D43's result names B_n11_q0.25 a SURVIVOR at the working level. AMENDMENT 9, A9.1,
gives D44's words for it: PASSES at CAMTZ, RDSV and RDTZ, OPEN at PWSV and PWTZ, "THE
CROSS-CHECK IS INCOMPLETE". A9.5, item 8: "the two designs that did not fail carry one positive
charge of 0.50 e (B_n02_q0.50) and six of 0.25 e (B_n11_q0.25)".

WHAT THE BASIS SET OF THAT CROSS-CHECK CAN SHOW. In D43 and D44 the charges are bare points and
no basis function stands at a charge. D48 put the def2-TZVPD functions of hydrogen at every
positive charge, and D49, its calibration run again, measured what those functions hold: for
one electron at a bare charge of 0.25 e, -0.6113 eV at Hartree-Fock and -0.5983 eV with the
functional, where exact is 0.8504 eV. D49's result: "These functions do not hold an electron at
a charge of that size." and, of D48's test of this design with them, "By the pre-registration:
NOT A TEST OF CAPTURE."

WHAT D50 MEASURED. D50 put a larger set of functions there (set X2), which D51 has since found
adequate from 0.25 e up. Its result, R50.5, for the design with auxiliary treatment A: at R it
"TOOK ELECTRONS ONTO THE FUNCTIONS", with +0.5205 e left the atoms, a gain of -0.0898 eV and
"dipole 2.38x control"; "the ghost centre that gained most is at site 1073: -0.1593 e".
Treatment E reads the same. At TS it "DID NOT TAKE" (+0.0392 e). One charge of +0.25 e alone,
at site 1124, "DID NOT TAKE" at R and at TS with all three treatments (+0.0021 e at R). "Runs
with charges: 12; took electrons 2, did not take 8, not decided 2." Of the design: "ddE with
the functions, treatment A: -7.5715 kcal/mol; without functions (committed runs): -9.5106
kcal/mol; difference +1.9392".
   DERIVED from d50_dianion.tsv, the ghost rows of that run minus those of its control: the
functions at sites 1124, 1073, 1107 and 1052 gained 0.1404, 0.1593, 0.1582 and 0.0633 e; those
at 1359 and 1005 gained none (+0.0007 and +0.0000). The first four lie 3.28 to 6.17 A from one
another.

WHAT D50 HAD FIXED FOR THAT OUTCOME, in its pre-registration: "the one design of this size that
passes A9.4 does not hold when the basis can hold an electron at its charges, and what fails is
charges together. The next diagnostic then puts charges together first, the search is not run
at 0.25 e before a rule for arrangements exists, and S is not that rule".

WHAT D51 ADDS. D50's own rule for its functions gave "THE FUNCTIONS FOR THE CAPTURE TEST: NONE."
D51 measured why, and its rule gives "THE FUNCTIONS FOR THE CAPTURE TEST: SET X2, with auxiliary
treatment A." and "THE GRID FOR A GHOST ATOM: rung i12 (IntAcc 12.000)". D51 also reports:
"From 0.25 e up, where the grid is converged, the functional lies at most +0.0631 eV from exact
(0.40 e)." Its pre-registration: "a run that takes electrons with a gain in energy not larger
than a few times that error is not settled at this level alone." The design's gain at R,
0.0898 eV, is 1.4 times 0.0631 eV.

THE WALL. D43's pre-registration names two routes: "(a) bare charges moved out to 3.50 A or
more, or (b) the present distances with a charge behind a core potential, the wall, which is
NOT ADOPTED and NOT CALIBRATED." It records the order Jessica set on 17 November 2026: route (a)
"is tried first, and of the wall she wrote "i agree the wall as a fall back"." and "D43 is the

"Before any walled run it needs a dated amendment for route (b), a parameter set that is
validated, which the argon set in use is not, and a pre-registration." A9.5, item 4: "THE WALL
IS NOT ADOPTED."

The parameter sets are one published family, Marefat Khah et al.'s sets for He, Ne and Ar
(D23's pre-registration: "one paper, one group, one fitting recipe"). EXPERIMENT 0 tested
whether each reproduces its own published fitting target. Its table:

   He   2s2p3s3p     within 6 digits 1.381e-06    7.136e-02    VALIDATED
   Ne   3s3p3d4s4p4d within 6 digits 7.670e-07    1.495e-01    VALIDATED
   Ar   4s4p4d5s5p5d NONE STATED    3.067e-03    7.954e-01    NOT VALIDATED

Of the neon set D13's pre-registration says: "these are Marefat Khah's published Ne parameters
used outside their octet assumption; the caveat is carried, not resolved, and the walled arm is

COUNTED by a10/count_walled_inputs.py, committed beside this amendment: 680 of the 2425 ORCA

A10.2  WHAT THAT MEANS FOR A9.4, READ ON COMMITTED WORDS

A9.4 (i) names a design a survivor of a search only if, among other things, it "is RELIABLE on
four legs" at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD. That test is run with bare charges and no functions at
them. By D49 it cannot show an electron leaving for a charge of 0.25 e, because the basis has
nowhere to put it. By D50 the one design of six charges of 0.25 e that passes it gives up half
an electron at R once the basis has. So passing A9.4 (i) is not enough to say that a design
holds.

THIS IS A READING. What the word SURVIVOR should ask in addition is not decided here (A10.5).


A10.3  WHAT JESSICA DECIDED

1. B_n11_q0.25 IS NOT CARRIED FORWARD AS A DESIGN THAT HOLDS. D43's and D44's words about it
   stand as committed. From now on no ddE of it is quoted as a result at any level, and where
   it is reported D50's words are reported beside it. It is not called refuted either: by D51
   its gain at R is 1.4 times the functional's own error for one electron, and whether a
   second level of theory finds the same has not been measured. Its standing is: DOES NOT HOLD

2. CHARGES TOGETHER ARE MEASURED NEXT, as D50's pre-registration fixed. No search is
   pre-registered at 0.25 e before that result is committed. For bare charges the diagnostics
   that measure it use what D51's rule gives: set X2 with auxiliary treatment A at each

3. THE WALL IS BROUGHT FORWARD TO BE TESTED, NOT ADOPTED. She asked on 23 November 2026: "and if

   proposed a small walled arm beside the bare arrangements of the next diagnostic. She
   answered: "okay yes and also adress those other issues you listed ie the poorly designed
   test and none issue". Those other issues are what D51 has since measured.
      D43's pre-registration names three things owed before any walled run. This amendment is
   the first of them, FOR A TEST ARM AND FOR NOTHING ELSE. The arm is not route (b) as D43
   words it, a wall at "the present distances": it stands at the sites of arrangements that
   are also measured bare.
      (a) Arrangements that are measured bare under step 2a of A10.4 may also be run walled,
          in the same diagnostic or in one that follows it: every charge of the arrangement
          placed as a coreless core potential that carries the charge, with functions beside

   charges of +0.25 e at                    bare     He wall   Ne wall
   one site                                 0.8504   0.4718    0.5174

So the four sites that gained charge in D50 hold one electron five times as strongly together
as one of them does alone, and behind either wall they still hold it more than three times as
strongly as one bare charge, which D50 found did not take electrons. CLAUDE THEREFORE EXPECTS
THE WALL TO WEAKEN THE PULL OF CHARGES TOGETHER AND NOT TO REMOVE IT. That is an expectation
from one electron and positive charges alone. Whether the dianion gives up an electron depends
as well on the negative charges and on what all of them do to the dianion, which this does not
compute. The test arm is brought forward with that expectation known, because the record holds
no measurement either way.

Known too, from a10/x2_behind_wall.py (COMPUTED BY CLAUDE, the same program): what set X2 holds
of those levels when it stands where a dianion run has it, 0.01 A beyond each charge. Bare, it

0.0576 eV (Ne), more than the 0.05 eV that D51's rule allows, and at the four by 0.0038 eV
(He) and 0.0185 eV (Ne). So which functions serve behind a wall has to be measured, for one
electron, before any walled run of the dianion is read. That is why A10.3, item 3 (a), lets
one electron be run first.
