E7.1  RETRACTION: THERE WAS NO FABRICATED QUOTATION

WORKLOG_20261026.md states:

   "A fabricated quotation was caught and discarded. A sentence was offered as verbatim Dittner
    section 8.3 - '...these are not specifically favored by additional objective function
    ingredients'. The phrase 'specifically favored' occurs nowhere in the extracted thesis text,
    including after collapsing whitespace and ligatures."

STAGE6_ASSESSMENT2_20261026.md section 12 quotes the passage in full and states of it:

   "It does not exist."

BOTH STATEMENTS ARE WITHDRAWN. THE QUOTATION IS REAL.

It is in the Dittner dissertation, in the Prospects chapter, and reads:

   "of the search space used in this Thesis. However, these are not speci<fi>cally favored
    by additional objective function ingredients. In other words, no fundamental new"

where <fi> is the typographic ligature U+FB01. A search for the eight ASCII characters
"specifically" returns zero matches in the extracted text; a search for the same word set with the
ligature returns one. The claim that ligatures had been collapsed before concluding the quotation
did not exist was FALSE WHEN IT WAS WRITTEN. No such normalisation had been performed.

What this means, stated plainly rather than softened. An earlier session quoted the source
correctly. A later session - the one that wrote Erratum 5, Assessment 2 and the 26 October work log
- accused it of the single failure HANDOVER_20261026.md section 10 names as the worst available
here, and committed that accusation to the permanent record, on the strength of a typographic
ligature in a PDF text extraction. The accusation is withdrawn in full and without qualification.

Two things are NOT withdrawn, because they were independently correct. The substantive reading of
the surrounding passage stands: Dittner does propose regularisation penalties on charge values as
FUTURE WORK in the Prospects chapter, for parsimony and interpretability, and states that simpler
solutions "are not specifically favored by additional objective function ingredients" in the work as
performed. And nothing in Stage 6 ever rested on the quotation, in either direction.


E7.2  WHY IT HAPPENED, AND THE FOUR WAYS THIS LIBRARY DEFEATS A NAIVE SEARCH

The failure was not carelessness about chemistry. It was trusting a negative result from a string
search over PDF-extracted text. Four distinct mechanisms in chorismate-refs produce a false negative,
all four of them confirmed by direct inspection:

  LIGATURES         The Dittner dissertation sets fi, ff, ffi as single code points U+FB01, U+FB00,
                    U+FB03. "specifically", "effect" and "difficult" are not searchable as ASCII.
  OCR SUBSTITUTION  b508181e (Claeyssens) renders "mol-1" as "mol21" and "=" as "5", so its
                    "average barrier height of 16.2 kcal mol-1" is stored as "16.2 kcal mol21".
                    1.471933 (Galbraith) renders the minus sign as the digit 2, so -0.378 reads
                    as 20.378 and -6.1965 as 26.1965. ANY NUMBER TAKEN FROM THAT FILE MUST BE
                    RE-CHECKED AGAINST THE PDF BEFORE IT ENTERS A DOCUMENT.
  TWO-COLUMN TEXT   Most of these PDFs extract with both columns on one physical line. A sentence's
                    second half can be hundreds of lines from its first, with unrelated text
                    between. A contiguous-substring search cannot match such a sentence at all.
  SOFT HYPHENATION  A word split across a line break ("regulari-" / "zation") matches neither half.

Three further quotations were reported unverifiable on 26 October for reasons two and three. Two of
the three were subsequently found and are genuine. The lesson recorded here is narrow and should be
applied literally: IN THIS PROJECT, A FAILED TEXT SEARCH IS NOT EVIDENCE OF ABSENCE. It is evidence
that the search failed.

cs_citecheck.py is committed alongside this erratum so that the check is mechanical and repeatable
rather than a matter of care. It normalises ligatures, combining marks and soft hyphenation, splits
elided quotations at the ellipsis and verifies each fragment separately, falls back from exact
substring matching to n-gram coverage so that two-column breakage does not produce a false negative,
and reports anything it cannot match as UNMATCHED - never as absent, and never as fabricated.

E7.4  THE DIELECTRIC CAVEAT: SOURCE WITHDRAWN, DIRECTION PRESERVED

Assessment 2 section 10 attributes to Burschowsky et al. (FEBS Open Bio 2017, doi
10.1002/2211-5463.12224) the figures 36.6 kJ/mol at eps = 1, about 18 at converged eps, against an
experimental 27.2, and the conclusion "In this exact enzyme, vacuum overshoots by 35 per cent". That
paper is not in chorismate-refs and those numbers appear in no paper that is. ALL FOUR FIGURES AND
THE 35 PER CENT ARE WITHDRAWN.

The direction of the error is independently supported and may be retained, without a number:
Warshel et al. (cr0503106) state that microscopic studies "frequently overestimate the electrostatic
effect, because microscopic studies usually drastically underestimate the dielectric effect for
charge-" separation, and the Menshutkin study (jacs.9b13029) quantifies screening in the same
direction. So: a vacuum point-charge model is expected to OVERSTATE the catalytic effect a given
charge set would deliver in a protein. The magnitude of that overstatement is not established here.

E7.7  THERE IS NO NORM BUDGET IN THE GOCAT LITERATURE. THE Sq^2 CONSTRAINT IS THIS PROJECT'S OWN

A full reading of both Kiel dissertations and both journal papers establishes the complete GOCAT
constraint set: a fixed number of charges N_Ch; per-charge box bounds q_i in [-1, +1] e; a minimum
INTER-CHARGE separation r_min, 1 A by default and varied to 0.1 A and 3.0 A in specific studies;
charges pinned to a common van der Waals surface or to a 7.5 A sphere; and a total-charge equality,
sum q_i = 0, in the neutral runs. There is no minimum charge-to-substrate distance; the surface does
that work.

NO L1 BUDGET, NO L2 BUDGET, NO LASSO, NO RIDGE PENALTY AND NO SPARSITY TERM IS IMPOSED ON THE DESIGN
CHARGE VECTOR ANYWHERE IN THAT LITERATURE. A mechanical sweep for lasso, elastic net, sparsity,
cardinality, Tikhonov, budget, l1 and l2 returns nothing; the one genuine occurrence of "Ridge"
concerns RESP charge FITTING, not design; and the only regularisation passage is the Prospects one
quoted in E7.1, which proposes it as future work and states it was not done.

CONSEQUENCE FOR STAGE 7: the Sq^2 <= B budget is this project's own contribution and must be
presented as such. It must never be attributed to Dittner or Hartke. Two honest hooks exist for
MOTIVATING it, neither of which is a precedent: the saturation result, where the thesis reports "a
convergence of the final maximal catalytic effect" and N_Ch = 10 to 20 changes nothing; and the
Prospects passage. A third, in the opposite direction, deserves equal prominence: Behrens reports
that "fewer than 12 charges are not enough to reproduce the reference fit" precisely because
|q| <= 1 e, which is the mirror of this project's own finding that 18 of the 20 top-ranked sites
admit only q = 1.00.


E7.8  THE ECP CENTRES ARE A PUBLISHED PSEUDOPOTENTIAL, USED OUTSIDE ITS VALIDATED DOMAIN

Extending Erratum 6 E6.1. The Ne> centres in cs_stage4b and cs_stage4c are, parameter for parameter,
the "Ne ecp-all" of Marefat Khah et al. (acs.jctc.9b01162) - the same paper Finding 4 rests on. Its
supporting information gives ncore = 10, lmax = 3, s-d 54.5100 / 2 / 2.0475, p-d 1.46500 / 2 /
0.44815, d-f -0.8380 / 2 / 0.49205 and the null reference 0.0000 / 2 / 1.0000. L0_E5_R/job.inp

IT IS ALSO AN EXTRAPOLATION BEYOND WHAT THOSE AUTHORS TESTED, AND THEY NAMED THE ASSUMPTION BEING

exactly such an acceptor. Stage 7 should state this as a limitation of the ECP-shielded arrangements
rather than leave it implicit.


E7.9  THE REGIME STAGE 6 OCCUPIES IS NOT COVERED BY THE CHARGE-DESIGN LITERATURE

Two findings combine, and the combination matters more than either.

First, a compact basis does not prevent electron spill-out; it conceals it. Nabo et al. (1.4962367)
explain a transition visible at small basis and lost at larger basis by noting that compact sets "do
not allow the wave function to spread out", and Marefat Khah report that "the amount of ESO varies a
lot with the inclusion of diffuse functions". EVERY COMMITTED STAGE 6 DESIGN WAS EVALUATED AT
def2-SVP. A CLEAN verdict at def2-SVP is therefore not evidence that spill-out is absent.

Second, the GOCAT work escapes the problem by construction rather than by remedy. Behrens states
that "With larger basis functions, electron density spilling can become a problem, where the density
is overpolarized due a strong interaction with the pure Coulomb charge with no Pauli repulsion", and
immediately that for "minimal basis semi-empirical methods, however, this is not an issue that needs
to be addressed at this point". Neither GOCAT paper mentions spill-out at all.

So a charge design evaluated at B3LYP/def2-SVP sits in a regime the published charge-design
literature does not cover: too large a basis for GOCAT's escape, too compact a basis for the
QM/MM spill-out literature's diagnostics to fire. That is simultaneously a genuine novelty claim for
this thesis and a genuine risk to its numbers, and Stage 7 should make both statements, together.

One encouraging precedent in the same reading: Coulomb collapse is a named GOCAT failure mode -
"by using a vdW surface for the point charge entities, Coulomb implosion is an issue" - and the
remedy proposed is "adding Lennard-Jones potentials to the respective" entities. This project's LJ
spheres therefore have precedent.


E7.10  D6.3's INDICATORS ARE NOT THE LITERATURE'S INDICATORS

Across the six spill-out and embedding papers, the documented signatures are: a virtual orbital
localising on an environment site; the LUMO escaping into the environment; excitation energies
collapsing; oscillator strength vanishing. NO PAPER IN THIS LIBRARY REPORTS SCF CONVERGENCE
BEHAVIOUR OR POPULATION ANALYSIS AS A SPILL-OUT SIGNATURE, and none gives a distance or a
charge-magnitude threshold for its onset.

D6.3's four indicators - SCF cycle ratio, HOMO-LUMO gap, Loewdin charge and dipole - therefore have
no literature backing as diagnostics for the effect they were built to detect. This does not make
D6, D8 or D9 wrong about what they measured; it bears on what those measurements can be said to
mean. It also removes a route to the budget: no charge-magnitude or distance threshold can be
borrowed from this literature, which independently reinforces Diagnostic 9's negative result.

ORCA's own release note adds a further reason to distrust one of them: version 6.0's SCF convergers
"are more robust now and on average take about 20% fewer cycles than before to reach convergence",
so a cycle count is a property of the converger version and not of the wavefunction.

A replacement that IS anchored. Galbraith & Schaefer (1.471933) distinguish a genuinely bound anion
from a basis-set artefact by ORBITAL COMPOSITION, not by orbital energy: for unbound Ne- "this basis
function was essentially the sole contributor to the highest occupied orbital", whereas for bound
F- "the diffuse functions contributed a negligible amount". They also warn that a positive HOMO
energy is not the test, since "the physical significance of the orbitals obtained from density
functional methods is questionable". Their conclusion is explicitly scoped to electrons "bound as
strongly as in F2" and they decline to extend it to loosely bound cases. That paper concerns
MONOANIONS ONLY and must not be cited for a dianion claim; for dianions, Dreuw & Cederbaum
(cr0104227) show the sign of a vertical detachment energy flipping between a non-diffuse DZP basis
and the same basis with diffuse functions added.


E7.11  POTENTIAL ENERGIES AND FREE ENERGIES ARE BEING COMPARED ACROSS THE WRITE-UP

Both Mulholland-group chorismate papers report POTENTIAL energy barriers and say so: "in this study
are electronic energies and the experimental values" / "are free energies; however, entropic effects
are relatively small". The experimental anchors are ddG = 15.4 and ddH = 12.7 kcal/mol for BsCM, and
24.5 / 20.7 in water, with a catalytic effect of about 9.1 kcal/mol - the last of which now has
three independent sources in the library (Claeyssens 9.1; Ranaghan 9.4 calculated against 9.1
experimental; Warshel's Table 1 at 9.0 and Table 3 at 9.1 observed).

Any comparison of a computed barrier with an experimental one must state which quantity is which.
Two further traps in the same paper: B313759G gives the experimental solution barrier as 25.4 in its
abstract and 24.5 everywhere else - cite 24.5; and its Table 3 "gas phase" values are single points
on enzyme-path geometries, with the footnote "Single point calculations on geometries taken from the
6-31G(d)/CHARMM22 enzyme path", so they are not gas-phase optimised barriers.

Finally, the two published transition-state geometries disagree by more than the grid spacing this
project uses: Ranaghan puts the TS at C-O 2.28 and C-C 2.58 A, Claeyssens at 2.02 and 2.63 A. Neither
should be treated as the transition-state geometry.
