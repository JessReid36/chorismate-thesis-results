Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 3, CLOSING NOTE. Written 22 October 2026, after DIAGNOSTIC3_RESULT.txt.

This note does NOT change DIAGNOSTIC3_RESULT.txt's verdict, which is INCONCLUSIVE and stands as committed. It
records what the clean arm of that diagnostic shows, why the other arm is not being repaired, and the three things
the runs established. It changes no method and no reported number.

N3.1 WHY THE VERDICT IS INCONCLUSIVE, AND WHY THAT IS MY DOING

D3.3's ghost control reads "If |ddE(ghost)| exceeds 0.30 kcal/mol AT EITHER BASIS ... the result is then reported
as inconclusive". The measured ghost terms are

   def2-SVP    ghost Na  -0.8302    ghost K  -0.9461      (264 -> 279 and 288 basis functions)
   def2-SVPD   ghost Na  +0.0629    ghost K  +0.0569      (402 -> 420 and 429 basis functions)

so def2-SVP fails it by a factor of three and def2-SVPD passes it by a factor of five. Written as an "either
basis" test it fails on def2-SVP and carries def2-SVPD down with it, even though D3.3's substantive comparisons
were all specified "at def2-SVPD". That is a defect in how I wrote the criterion, not in the runs, and the verdict
stands as written because a pre-registered rule that turns out to be clumsy is not re-read after the data arrives.

N3.2 THE FIRST FINDING, WHICH IS NOT ABOUT PAULI REPULSION AT ALL

The ghost terms are a direct measurement of each basis's incompleteness at this site. Placing 15 to 27 EMPTY basis
functions next to the substrate - no nucleus, no electrons, no charge - moves the barrier by nearly a kcal/mol at
def2-SVP and by 0.06 kcal/mol at def2-SVPD. def2-SVP is about FIFTEEN TIMES more sensitive to basis functions
placed at a design site than def2-SVPD is.

This bears directly on how DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt should be read. def2-SVP was the only one of the five conditions
that stayed internally consistent all the way to q = 1.00 e, and it would have been easy to treat that as evidence
that def2-SVP is the reliable condition. It is not evidence of that. A basis that gains 0.9 kcal/mol of
differential energy from empty functions placed near the substrate is demonstrably incomplete there, and its
smooth behaviour at large q is the absence of the freedom needed to show the effect rather than the absence of the
effect. Anything in this project that depends on the substrate's RESPONSE should be read at def2-SVPD or better.
Rappoport and Furche, J. Chem. Phys. 133, 134105 (held as 1.3484283.pdf), built def2-SVPD for response properties
and recommend it as "sufficiently accurate for polarizability estimates and for exploratory calculations", with
TZVPD and TZVPPD "recommended for accurate routine calculations".

N3.3 THE SECOND FINDING: AT def2-SVPD THE POINT CHARGE IS AN ADEQUATE SURROGATE AT +1 e

At the basis where the ghost control passes by a factor of five, so that the counterpoise correction is 0.06
kcal/mol and the comparison is clean:

   bare +1 point charge                    -7.5135
   Na+ , counterpoise-corrected            -7.4534      0.8 per cent from the point charge
   K+  , counterpoise-corrected            -8.1184      8.1 per cent from the point charge

Both inside D3.3's 10 per cent yardstick. Replacing the point charge with a real ion, which has electrons and
therefore has the Pauli repulsion our Lennard-Jones walls do not supply, changes the differential stabilisation by
under a tenth. Pauli repulsion is a small effect at this site at this magnitude.

A LIMIT ON HOW FAR THAT MAY BE CARRIED. The two ionic radii are about 1.02 A (Na+) and 1.38 A (K+), and the
difference between their effects is 8.9 per cent. A guanidinium carbon's van der Waals radius is about 1.70 A,
outside the range tested, and the dependence over the range tested is weak but not zero. This note therefore does
NOT establish that a point charge can represent a guanidinium, and nothing downstream may claim it does.

N3.4 THE THIRD FINDING, AND THE ONE THAT DECIDES STAGE 7

DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt's coefficients at def2-SVPD, fitted where every condition is internally consistent
(q <= 0.50 e), are a = -7.4233 and b = -1.532, so a + b predicts ddE at q = +1.00 e of -8.955 kcal/mol. What is
actually measured at +1 e, by three different representations of the same +1 monopole:

   bare point charge                -7.5135      16.1 per cent short of the prediction
   Na+ , counterpoise-corrected     -7.4534      16.8 per cent short
   K+  , counterpoise-corrected     -8.1184       9.3 per cent short

A REAL ION SATURATES AS MUCH AS A BARE POINT CHARGE DOES. The softening of the response above about 0.5 e is
therefore a property of the chorismate dianion's own response and not an artefact of a charge model that lacks a
wall for electrons. That is the opposite of what I expected when D3 was written, and it is the reason D3 was run
rather than argued.

The consequence for Stage 7 is direct: a per-site charge bound below 1 e is an honest constraint reflecting where
the substrate stops responding quadratically, and NOT a device for avoiding a regime in which our own model
misbehaves. STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC3_20261022.txt D3.3 stated in advance that if the point charge turned out adequate
then "the measured charge bound would stand as a property of the substrate's response rather than as a defect of
the charge model". On the clean arm, that is what happened.

N3.5 WHY THE PLANNED AMENDMENT IS NOT BEING RUN

The obvious repair for N3.1 is to move the ghost into the reference so that every model carries identical basis
functions and superposition cancels exactly rather than being corrected for - eight further single points, with
M2 to M5 reused. It is not being run, for a reason that is itself one of the findings: its only purpose would be
to make the def2-SVP arm rigorous, and N3.2 shows def2-SVP to be the deficient basis at this site by a factor of
fifteen. Making a deficient arm rigorous answers nothing. The eight runs go instead to
STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC2_AMENDMENT1_20261022.txt, which locates where the saturation N3.4 found actually begins.

N3.6 WHAT REMAINS UNTESTED

Negative charge, and any magnitude other than +1 e, neither of which an ion can vary. The relaxed geometry, where
Finding 4 says the Lennard-Jones wall's entire value lies and where a real ion would move while a point charge
would be approached. A group of guanidinium's size, per N3.3. The published ECP cure, whose parameters remain held
and unused for the reason D3.2 records. One site, one frame.
