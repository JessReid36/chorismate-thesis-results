WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

   Nothing was computed with ORCA, so this cannot test any hypothesis the
   committed files do not already contain. The field is computed in vacuo with
   no screening, from point charges at the committed sphere centres, at the
   REACTANT geometry; ddE is a TS-minus-R differential, so the two are not
   like for like. Field at a bond midpoint is not the whole of the differential
   stabilisation of a pericyclic reaction, in which charge redistributes over
   the entire substrate. Two frames, one basis, a handful of arrangements, and
   the Stage 4c block is the only one that breaks the Stage 6 designs' shared
   budget, so it carries the whole weight of any cross-block comparison.
   NOTHING HERE IS A PREDICTOR. Two electrostatic predictors have already been
   proposed and tested and failed in this project (ERRATUM3 E3.5, and sum q.V_R
   against the D6 table). A third must be tested against the whole corpus, after
   cs6_audit.py's walker and charge detection are fixed together, before it is
   believed.
