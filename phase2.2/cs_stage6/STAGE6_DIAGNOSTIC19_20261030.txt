Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 19 (30 October 2026).
Committed before any of its runs exist. Nothing of this diagnostic has been computed.

This diagnostic answers the question DIAGNOSTIC 8 and DIAGNOSTIC 9 both deferred, in the same
words, on 26 October 2026: "whether those two unanchored criteria should be replaced by SCF
stability analysis and FOD is the next question and is not this one." It is also the follow-up
DIAGNOSTIC 18.4 named in advance for the outcome D18 returned.


D19.1  THE QUESTION

At full field the substrate's HOMO-LUMO gap closes to 0.60 eV and the SCF takes 2.8 times the
control's cycles. Is that near-degeneracy multireference in character, or is it a small gap that
single-reference B3LYP can still be trusted with?

DIAGNOSTIC 18 settled what it is NOT. Twenty Pauli walls at the charge sites moved the gap by
-0.92% at R and +0.06% at TS while doing 0.0789 kcal/mol of measurable energetic work, and
neither arm showed any density displacement (Loewdin excess 0.068 against D_LW 0.25, dipole
1.13x against R_DIP 2.0). The gap closure is not produced by density falling onto the bare
charges and is not removed by representing Pauli repulsion. D18.4 stated, before that result
was known, that this establishes only that the near-degeneracy is not a representation
artefact, and that the follow-up is a multireference diagnostic rather than another
representation.

It matters because of one number. At lambda = 1.00 the design's barrier change is
ddE(bare) = -24.6898 kcal/mol, 23 times DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's budget of 1.067. If the wavefunction
there is multireference, that number is not one this method may report, and no amount of work
on the charge representation changes it. If it is not, then D6.3's STRAINED flag is
over-conservative at this basis and readability at full strength has to be revisited.

   NO FOD OR STABILITY DATA EXISTS. Every committed archive was scanned for a fractional
   occupation or stability calculation on this system and none was found. This is new work.


D19.2  WHAT IS MEASURED

Two independent indicators on the BARE arm of S6-20, on the committed lambda ladder at the
geometry everything else uses. The walled arm is not run: D18 measured that the wall changes
neither the gap nor the density here.

   A.  FOD, the fractional occupation number weighted density.

       ORCA's `!FOD`, which is TPSS / def2-TZVP at an electronic temperature of 5000 K. This is
       DELIBERATELY NOT THE PRODUCTION METHOD. The question is whether B3LYP/def2-SVP is out of
       its depth, and a method cannot be asked to certify itself.

          0.00     0.0000     R, TS      the control, and the anchor of the ladder
          0.25     1.4400     R, TS
          0.50     2.8800     R, TS
          0.75     4.3200     R, TS
          1.00     5.7600     R, TS

   B.  SCF stability analysis, at the production method.

       B3LYP / def2-SVP with `%scf STABPerform true end`, which tests the restricted solution
       for an instability toward an unrestricted one. This is the reading that speaks about the
       production method itself rather than about the system.

          lambda 0.00   R, TS      the control
          lambda 1.00   R, TS
                        4

                                             TOTAL 14

   The charge sets are the committed ones, taken from the design and not retyped. Every
   lambda's charges must reproduce field/def2-SVP/lam<n> byte for byte, both states (GATE 2),
   so that this ladder is the same ladder the gap was measured on.

   N_FOD is not size-consistent. Every system here carries the same 118 electrons, because a
   point charge adds none, so the ladder is internally comparable without normalisation. This
   is stated so that a later comparison against a system of a different size is a stop and not
   an adjustment.

GATE 1. The lambda = 0.00 stability runs must report a STABLE restricted solution. A charge-free
        closed-shell substrate at this geometry that reported an instability would mean the
        indicator is not measuring what it is meant to, and nothing below would be readable.
GATE 2. Every lambda's charges reproduce the committed field tree byte for byte, both states.
GATE 3. Every run at lambda > 0 must report ORCA reading exactly 20 point charges. The FOD
        tutorial does not document point charges; if ORCA silently ignores them the ladder
        would be flat for the wrong reason, and this gate is what catches that.


D19.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS COMPUTED

   THE THRESHOLDS BELOW ARE CONVENTIONS FIXED HERE, NOT VALUES TAKEN FROM THE LITERATURE.
   All 77 papers in chorismate-refs/ were searched in full text; 76 are machine-readable
   (ja9621760.pdf is a scanned image and yielded no text) and NONE of them mentions FOD,
   fractional occupation, N_FOD or finite-temperature DFT. The project's own rule is that a
   methodological standard needs three or more independent sources, and it has none, so no
   published N_FOD threshold is used and none is implied. Every criterion below is internal:
   the system compared against itself at zero field. The full ladder is reported whatever the
   verdict, so that any threshold obtained later can be applied to it without re-running.

READING 1, does FOD rise with the field?

   R_FOD = N_FOD(lambda = 1.00) / N_FOD(lambda = 0.00), computed per state.

   RISES WITH FIELD    R_FOD >= 2.0 in both states.
   FLAT                R_FOD <= 1.25 in both states.
   WEAK OR SPLIT       anything else, including the two states disagreeing. Both are reported
                       and no verdict on the mechanism is drawn.

   The five-point ladder and whether it is monotonic in lambda are reported in every case.

READING 2, is the production wavefunction stable at full field?

   UNSTABLE AT FULL FIELD   at least one lambda = 1.00 run reports an instability of the
                            restricted solution.
   STABLE AT FULL FIELD     neither does.

   GATE 1 must have passed. If ORCA's automatic restart fires on any run, that run's energy is
   not the production energy, is not compared with one, and is used for nothing but this
   verdict.

READING 3, do the two indicators agree?

   AGREE, MULTIREFERENCE        Reading 1 RISES and Reading 2 UNSTABLE.
   AGREE, SINGLE REFERENCE      Reading 1 FLAT and Reading 2 STABLE.
   DISAGREE                     anything else. Both are reported, neither is preferred, and the
                                question stays open.

   DISAGREE is named here so that it cannot be resolved afterwards by choosing whichever
   indicator gave the more convenient answer.


D19.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

THE LARGEST LIMITATION, AND IT IS THE INSTRUMENT'S OWN. ORCA's documentation states that FOD
"is not a fully reliable diagnostic for multi-reference character" and recommends it as a first
indication to be combined with more sophisticated diagnostics. This diagnostic therefore cannot
establish multireference character. At most it can show that the field drives the system toward
it, or fail to. A positive result here licenses a CASSCF or NEVPT2 calculation; it does not
substitute for one.

The two indicators are not independent of method. FOD is read at TPSS/def2-TZVP and the
stability analysis at B3LYP/def2-SVP, deliberately, and they are therefore answering two
different questions: whether the SYSTEM has multireference character, and whether the
PRODUCTION calculation is sitting on an unstable solution. READING 3 treats their disagreement
as a result rather than as something to be averaged.

Nothing here tells us what the correct barrier is for any design. A wavefunction found to be
unstable does not come with a better number attached.

The FOD runs are at def2-TZVP, which is neither of the two production bases. No D6.3 class is
assigned to them and none of their energies enters any Dset, d_shield or barrier. The three
bases are not pooled and this is a fourth.

One design, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, in vacuo, no continuum
(STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). The bare arm only. Nothing about def2-SVPD, where density
displacement and gap closure occur together. Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain
unmeasurable until the OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028
is decided.


D19.5  DIRECTION

If READING 3 returns AGREE, MULTIREFERENCE, then the designs that deliver large barrier
reductions drive the substrate into a regime single-reference DFT cannot evaluate. DFT
evaluation at full strength is closed, the -24.6898 kcal/mol is withdrawn as unreportable, and
the thesis's claim is a charge-design method with a characterised domain of validity and a
mechanistically identified boundary. That is a result, not a failure, and it is reached without
running E2 or E4.

If it returns AGREE, SINGLE REFERENCE, then the small gap is not multireference character,
D6.3's gap criterion is over-conservative at def2-SVP, and readability at lambda = 1.00 must be
reopened - which would make the full-strength designs evaluable after all and change what the
thesis can claim.

If it returns DISAGREE, the question is not settled and the next step is the CASSCF or NEVPT2
calculation that D19.4 says this diagnostic cannot replace.

D19.3 fixes which answer means which before anything is computed.
