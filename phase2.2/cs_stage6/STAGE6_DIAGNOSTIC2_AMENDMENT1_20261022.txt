Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 2, AMENDMENT 1: two further charge magnitudes, to locate where the response stops being
quadratic. Written 22 October 2026, after DIAGNOSTIC3_NOTE1_20261022.txt and before Stage 7's charge bound is set.

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC2_20261022.txt is not edited. Its conditions, its site, its geometries, its reported quantities
and all of D2.3's criteria stand exactly as committed, and DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt stands as the result of what it
pre-registered. This amendment adds two magnitudes in one of its five conditions and states, before the runs, how
the bound is read off. It changes no method, no threshold, no tolerance and no reported number.

A1.1 WHY

Diagnostic 2 measured q = 0.05, 0.25, 0.50 and 1.00 e. At def2-SVPD the quadratic model fitted on q <= 0.50 e
reproduces the measurements there to better than one per cent:

   with a = -7.4233 and b = -1.532 fitted on q <= 0.50 e, so that the prediction is a*q + b*q^2:

      q        predicted    measured     S(q)
      0.05      -0.3750      -0.3749     0.03 per cent
      0.25      -1.9516      -1.9531     0.08 per cent
      0.50      -4.0946      -4.1183     0.58 per cent
      1.00      -8.9553      -7.5135    16.10 per cent

and DIAGNOSTIC3_NOTE1_20261022.txt N3.4 established that the 16 per cent shortfall at 1.00 e is reproduced by real
Na+ and K+ ions in place of the point charge, so it is the substrate's own response saturating rather than a defect
of the charge model.

The bound therefore lies between 0.50 and 1.00 e and nothing in hand says where. Stage 7's per-site cap would
otherwise have to be set at 0.50 e for no better reason than that it is the largest magnitude that happened to be
measured, which is the same kind of inherited number as the [-1, +1] bound this whole sequence replaced.

A1.2 WHAT IS RUN

Two further magnitudes, both signs, both committed geometries, at def2-SVPD in vacuo only:

   q = +-0.65 e and +-0.80 e        8 single points

def2-SVPD only, and that is a decision with a measured reason rather than an economy: N3.2 measured def2-SVP to be
about fifteen times more sensitive to basis functions placed at a design site than def2-SVPD is, so def2-SVP
cannot show saturation of the response whether or not it is there, and its agreement with the quadratic model at
1.00 e (0.2 per cent) is the absence of the necessary freedom and not the absence of the effect. ma-def2-SVP is
excluded because DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt found it the one condition whose response is not even monotone at 1.00 e.
The two CPCM conditions are excluded because PHASE22_PLAN.txt section 2.2 keeps the design environment free of a
continuum and because TIER2_KNOWN_ISSUES.md A5's cavity question is unresolved for charges this close.

Everything else is Diagnostic 2's: site 1690, bare point charges, the committed frame_42436 geometries byte for
byte, cs5_amatrix.py's point-charge file format, and the committed bare references at def2-SVPD, which are NOT
re-run but read out of the committed qscan outputs archive so that a and b are the published ones.

A1.3 HOW THE BOUND IS READ OFF, STATED BEFORE THE RUNS

a and b are taken from DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt's def2-SVPD block and are NOT refitted with the new points -
refitting would let the data choose the model it is then judged against. Precisely which numbers, because the
report offers two and only one of them is right for this purpose:

   a  is the printed a_est, odd/q at q = 0.05 e, which is -7.4233.
   b  is the printed EVEN value at q = 0.50 e, -0.3830, divided by q^2, giving -1.532. It is NOT the report's own
      printed b of -0.7367, which is even/q^2 evaluated at q = 1.00 e - that is inside the region whose validity
      this amendment exists to establish, so using it would judge the saturation against a coefficient the
      saturation had already corrupted.

For each magnitude,

   S(q)  =  | ddE_measured(+q)  -  (a q + b q^2) |  /  | a q + b q^2 |

the residual beyond quadratic, as a fraction. Then

   q_quad  =  the largest magnitude measured at which S(q) <= 10 per cent

and the odd and even parts are reported at every magnitude as Diagnostic 2 reported them, so that the separation
into first order and polarisation can be followed through the knee rather than only summarised.

   q_quad >= 0.80 e   the response is quadratic essentially to the old bound, the saturation sets in only in the
                      last fifth, and Stage 7's cap is 0.80 e.
   q_quad = 0.65 e    Stage 7's cap is 0.65 e.
   q_quad = 0.50 e    the saturation begins immediately above the highest well-behaved point already measured,
                      and Stage 7's cap is 0.50 e.
   q_quad < 0.50 e    impossible unless something is wrong, since S(0.50) is already measured at 0.58 per cent;
                      if it happens, the new runs disagree with the committed ones at a magnitude both cover and
                      the discrepancy must be found before anything is read.

The 10 per cent is Diagnostic 2's own q* convention, used here for the same purpose and not re-tuned. Note that
q_quad is a bound on the QUADRATIC model's validity, which is the model Stage 7 reports with; the stricter q* of
D2.3, which bounds where the LINEAR term alone is within 10 per cent of the whole, is a different and smaller
number and remains 0.25 e at this condition.

A1.4 WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

Other sites, and the bound is certainly site- and distance-dependent: site 1690 sits 2.96 A from the nearest
substrate atom and a site further out sees a weaker perturbation at the same charge. Other frames. Negative
charge beyond the signs run here. Whether the saturation has the same cause as the sign inversion ma-def2-SVP
showed at 1.00 e, which remains unexplained. And nothing about the relaxed geometry.

DIRECTION

None. The outcome sets one number, Stage 7's per-site charge cap, and A1.3 fixes how that number is read before
the runs go in.
