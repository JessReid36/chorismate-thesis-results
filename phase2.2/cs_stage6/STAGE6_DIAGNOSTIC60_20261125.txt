2. WHAT IS RUN

ON hpc1, ONE JOB, V1, of 4 single points, each followed by orca_vpot at the 2626 sites of
d33a/points.xyz with the run's own SCF density, in this order: WXV_R, WXV_TS, CAM_R, CAM_TS.
Each input and geometry is the committed one byte for byte: WXV_R from d37/wB97XV_SV_R; WXV_TS
from d37/wB97XV_SV_TS; CAM_R from d33a/sp_42436_R; CAM_TS from d33a/sp_42436_TS. Each run has a
cap of 1800 s, D56's at def2-SVPD; a run that fails does not stop the ones after it. The job
script is D58's committed run_E1.pbs with its names, runs and caps changed.

The runs of wB97X-V are D37's inputs, so their energies can be set against D37's; the runs of
CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ are D33 stage A's, so their energies and potentials can be set against D33's.
Neither set of inputs asks for a stability analysis, as neither did when it was first run.

ON THE PC, AT THE FIRST HARVEST STEP, cs6_d60.py reads the outputs, forms the map, and makes
three searches with D56's committed program, cs6_d56_prepare.search, unchanged: first with the
working map A_f42436_d33a.tsv, as a check that the program and the PC give D56's 27 designs
again; then with the map at wB97X-V, if it is used; then with the committed map at
B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, A_f42436.tsv. Each search is D56's: the same 1488 sites that admit a
charge, the same cap of 0.25 e, spacing of 3.25 A, sum of charges zero or more, 3 designs at
each of n = 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 20, and the same limit of 3600 s on each solve. The maps
are passed through the program's argument a_col, whose text says it is meant for rehearsals:
"a_col replaces the committed coefficients ONLY in rehearsals, with invented numbers." Here it
carries measured maps, and that is stated now. Then the random designs of R60.4 are drawn and
scored.


3. THE READINGS, FIXED NOW

R60.1 THE RUNS AND THE CONTROL. For each run: whether it ended normally, its final energy, and
whether orca_vpot gave 2626 potentials at the committed points (cs5_amatrix.vpot checks the
points). CAM_R and CAM_TS are set against D33's committed d33a/sp_42436_R and _TS: the
difference of energy, and the largest difference of potential over the 2626 sites. THE CONTROL
HOLDS if both energies are within 1e-6 Eh and every potential within 1e-4 Eh/e, D33's committed
limit ("must give max |dV| <= 1e-4 Eh/e, the committed limit."); THE CONTROL DOES NOT HOLD
otherwise; THE CONTROL IS NOT FORMED if either run lacks an energy or its potentials. WXV_R and
WXV_TS are set against D37's committed energies: REPRODUCES D37 if both are within 1e-6 Eh; DOES
NOT REPRODUCE D37 otherwise; NOT FORMED if either lacks an energy. THE MAP AT wB97X-V IS USED
only if THE CONTROL HOLDS, the runs of wB97X-V REPRODUCE D37, and both have their potentials;
otherwise it is NOT USED, with the reason, it is not written, and R60.2 and R60.3 are read for
the B3LYP map alone.

R60.2 THE MAP AT wB97X-V: a_i = (V_TS(r_i) - V_R(r_i)) x 627.5094740631 kcal/mol per Eh
(cs5_amatrix.H), at the 2626 sites, written as A_f42436_d60_wB97XV.tsv in the form of
A_f42436_d33a.tsv (three lines of comment, then one value to six decimals per site, in the order
of sites_f42436.tsv). The map at wB97X-V and the B3LYP map are each set against the working map
over the 1488 sites that admit a charge: Pearson's and Spearman's correlation, the mean and the
largest absolute difference.

R60.3 THE SEARCHES SIDE BY SIDE. THE CHECK: the search with the working map must give D56's 27
designs of d56/DESIGNS.tsv, by name, sites and charges (gate GV.6). Then, for the map at wB97X-V
and for the B3LYP map, its 27 designs are printed beside D56's in d60_designs.tsv, each with its
sites, its charges and its first-order score under all three maps. A DESIGN IS THE SAME as one
of D56's when it holds the same sites with the same charges, each charge to four decimals, as
A9.3 item 5 has it; nothing nearer than that counts as the same. At each n: how many of D56's
designs at that n are among the map's designs at that n, and the word THE SAME DESIGNS if all
three are, PARTLY THE SAME if one or two are, DIFFERENT DESIGNS if none is; beside it, reported
and deciding nothing, how many of D56's designs at that n hold the same sites as one of the
map's whatever the charges, the share of charges (site and value) the two best designs at that n
have in common, the map's best design's score on its own map, D56's best design's score on that
map, and the map's best design's score on the working map. The totals over the nine numbers of
charges are given. If a search at another map is not proven optimal within the limit, THE SEARCH
STOPPED is said for that map with the program's message, and the rest of the reading stands.

R60.4 THE RANDOM DESIGNS, decision 10's null distribution, at first order on the working map and
under D56's rules. THE SITES: "Sites are added at random, respecting the spacing." A random
order of the 1488 sites that admit a charge is drawn and each site in turn is taken unless it
lies within 3.25 A of one already taken, until n are taken; the pairs that are too close are
those the committed cs_opt_milp.close_pairs gives, the pairs D56's search forbids. KIND (i):
each charge is 0.25 e in size, its sign drawn with equal chance where its site admits both signs
and the admitted sign otherwise; if the charges sum below zero, the design (sites and signs) is
drawn again, and the share accepted is reported. KIND (ii): "The optimiser chooses only the
charges on random sites, which tests whether choosing the sites matters." The best charges are
those that minimise the sum of a_i q_i, each q_i within its site's admitted range cut to 0.25 e,
with the charges summing to zero or more: each charge is set to the end of its range that lowers
the sum, then, if the charges sum below zero, they are raised in order of their coefficient, the
smallest first, until the sum is zero. That is exact for this problem, and it is checked against
HiGHS (scipy linprog) on the first 100 designs at each n (gate GV.7). Where it leaves a charge
smaller than 0.25 e or zero, as none of D56's designs holds, the design is kept and their number
is reported; it makes kind (ii) no easier for D56's designs. 10 000 DESIGNS OF EACH KIND AT EACH
n ("10 000 of each kind per n at first order."), drawn from numpy's PCG64 generator with seed
20261125, n in increasing order, kind (i) before kind (ii).

For each of D56's 27 designs, "Each design's percentile in (i) and (ii) is reported." here as
the share of random designs of its n, of each kind, whose first-order score is above the
design's, that is, whose lowering is smaller; with the median and the lowest score of each kind
at that n. The number of D56's designs that beat 99 % or more of each kind is given as a count;
no threshold decides anything.

THE TABLES, beside the result: d60.tsv (the runs), d60_designs.tsv (the three searches),
d60_null.tsv (each of D56's designs against the random designs), and A_f42436_d60_wB97XV.tsv if
the map is used.

5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

It is first order, on one frame, in vacuo. A design that a map places is placed by that map, not
measured at that functional; a design found again at another map is not confirmed by that, and
one not found again is not refuted by that. A random design is scored, not measured; the 50 full
single points of decision 10 are not run, as item 8 is at first order. The B3LYP map is at
def2-SVP, the working map and the map at wB97X-V at def2-SVPD, so the B3LYP comparison mixes the
functional and the basis set; the comparison at wB97X-V changes the functional alone. The
percentiles depend on the sampling rule, which is the one stated here. The search with the
working map made again is a check of the program and the PC, not of the map. Nothing here names,
keeps or drops a design, and no word of D56 changes.
