E5.1  THE 11 PER CENT AND 19-25 PER CENT UNCERTAINTIES ARE NOT RECORDED WHERE ERRATUM 3 SAYS THEY ARE

STAGE6_ERRATUM3_20261026.txt E3.4 states that the CLEAN designs are

   "quotable, subject to the basis (11 per cent) and environment (19 to 25 per cent) uncertainties
    already recorded in STAGE6_ASSESSMENT_20261022.md"

THEY ARE NOT RECORDED THERE. The full text of STAGE6_ASSESSMENT_20261022.md contains no 11 per cent figure
and no 19 to 25 per cent figure, and neither does STAGE6_FINDINGS_20261022.md. The percentages those two
files do contain are, in the assessment, 1.1 / 3 / 12.9 / 19.9 / 20 / 23.2 / 29 / 118 / 134 / 136 / 165 /
169, and in the findings 3.1 / 6.1 / 10 / 15 / 16 / 21 / 26. The nearest thing to an "11" in the
assessment is "-11 kcal/mol per unit of total absolute charge", which is a rate and not a percentage.

The figures themselves are sound. They are DERIVATIONS from the a_est table of
qscan/DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt, which prints, at site 1690:

   C1  def2-SVP      in vacuo        a_est = -6.6760
   C2  def2-SVP      CPCM eps=4.0    a_est = -7.9408
   C3  ma-def2-SVP   in vacuo        a_est = -7.1956
   C4  ma-def2-SVP   CPCM eps=4.0    a_est = -9.0086
   C5  def2-SVPD     in vacuo        a_est = -7.4233

   basis, in vacuo,            C1 -> C5   (7.4233 - 6.6760)/6.6760 = 11.19 per cent
   environment at def2-SVP,    C1 -> C2   (7.9408 - 6.6760)/6.6760 = 18.95 per cent
   environment at ma-def2-SVP, C3 -> C4   (9.0086 - 7.1956)/7.1956 = 25.20 per cent

CITE THEM AS DERIVED FROM qscan/DIAGNOSTIC2_RESULT.txt, never as recorded in the assessment. The clause
"already recorded in STAGE6_ASSESSMENT_20261022.md" is withdrawn.

Three things must travel with them.

   (a) D2.3's own printed comparison is 7.8 per cent (C1 against C3), and the QUALIFIED verdict rests on
       7.8 against decision O6's 15 per cent. The 11.19 per cent figure is a wider comparison that D2 does
       not print. Both clear 15 per cent so no verdict moves, but a document quoting 11 per cent as "the
       basis uncertainty" is not quoting the figure the verdict used.

   (b) The environment range is measured at def2-SVP and ma-def2-SVP only. There is no def2-SVPD plus CPCM
       condition in the C1-C5 set, so basis and environment cannot be separated at C5 - the basis whose
       VDE says the dianion is bound. The basis spread UNDER a continuum, C2 -> C4, is 13.45 per cent,
       wider than the 11.19 quoted.

   (c) claude/HANDOVER_20261026.md section 6 states both figures with no source at all. That is an
       omission rather than a misattribution and is corrected here too.


E5.2  A PER-SITE CHARGE CAP CANNOT BE SET INDEPENDENTLY OF THE GRID, SO IT IS A STAGE 5 QUANTITY

This item sits ALONGSIDE Erratum 4 E4.3 and does not contradict it. E4.3 withdraws DIAGNOSTIC 4's argument
that a per-site cap "would not bind on a Stage 7 design at all", and concludes the cap may be needed after
all. Nothing here disputes that. What is added is WHERE such a cap can be set.

relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt lists the twenty best single sites with the charges each admits. EIGHTEEN OF THE
TWENTY ADMIT "[1.00, 1.00]" - lower bound equal to upper bound. One admits [0.75, 1.00] and one
[0.25, 1.00]. At those eighteen the charge is not a decision variable; the optimiser cannot choose it.
This is the ranking-block form of the fact E4.3 already cites from OPTIMISER_CODE_README.md, that "1064
sites admit only |q| = 1".

Two consequences.

   (a) A per-site cap below 1.00 e makes those eighteen sites INFEASIBLE. It does not restrain them, it
       removes the twenty best sites from the grid.

   (b) STAGE5_GRID_DEV records the floors as a function of q, made monotone in q. Changing the admitted
       charge therefore moves the layer the sites sit on. The cap and the grid are not independent.

So a per-site cap is an input to the grid, not a constraint that can be bolted onto a design stage built
on that grid. If Stage 7 is to carry one, Stage 5 has to be amended first and the grid rebuilt under it.
Which value, and whether one is needed at all, is E4.3's question and is not answered here.

ONE FURTHER OBSERVATION, which is why E3.1's requirement is not sufficient. E3.1 requires any cap to be
stated as a magnitude AND a distance. The committed data additionally require a CHARGE COUNT: at def2-SVPD
a single charge is CLEAN at 0.65 e (qp0650_R) while twenty charges are COLLAPSED at a largest magnitude of
0.4125 e (the lambda = 0.75 field runs). A per-site number alone cannot express that.


E5.3  NO GLOBAL SCALAR ON THE CHARGES ORDERS THE def2-SVP VERDICTS ONCE ERRATUM 4 E4.5's RUNS ARE INCLUDED

Erratum 4 E4.5 establishes that four Stage 4c runs at three point charges are STRAINED at def2-SVP, and
that the def2-SVP onset is therefore three charges and not ten. STAGE4C_CRITERIA.txt defines those
arrangements: "E5 three sites by |a|, charges opposing a, x1" and "E7 E5 reversed". So each carries three
charges of unit magnitude.

The charges are confirmed without a new measurement. The three sphere positions in
cs_stage4c/L1_E5_R/combined.xyz agree to all eight decimals with the Ne> centres Erratum 4 E4.6 quotes
verbatim from cs_stage4c/L0_E5_R/job.out.xz, which carry q = +1.000000, -1.000000, +1.000000. E7 is the
same three sites reversed, so its magnitudes are identical.

Placing those beside the Stage 6 designs, with the def2-SVP verdicts as DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt and E4.5
give them:

   design     n   sum|q|   max|q|   sum q^2   verdict
   S6-P       1    1.000     1.00     1.000   CLEAN
   S6-2       2    2.000     1.00     2.000   CLEAN
   S6-3       3    2.413     1.00     2.004   CLEAN
   S6-5       5    3.000     1.00     2.010   CLEAN
   E5         3    3.000     1.00     3.000   STRAINED
   E7         3    3.000     1.00     3.000   STRAINED
   S6-10     10    4.380     0.55     2.023   STRAINED
   S6-20     20    5.760     0.55     2.048   STRAINED

   sum|q|   DOES NOT SEPARATE. CLEAN reaches 3.000 and STRAINED starts at 3.000.
   max|q|   RUNS BACKWARDS. Every CLEAN design carries a +1.00 e charge; the two STRAINED Stage 6
            designs top out at 0.55 e.
   n        DOES NOT SEPARATE. E5 is STRAINED at three charges, S6-5 is CLEAN at five.
   sum q^2  separates only in a window of 0.013 e^2, from 2.010 to 2.023. All six Stage 6 designs were
            built to saturate B = 2.0 e^2 and therefore cluster there by construction; a difference of six
            tenths of one per cent in sum q^2 cannot flip a wavefunction verdict. That separation is the
            budget showing through, not the physics.

S6-5 AND E5 CARRY THE SAME TOTAL CHARGE AND THE SAME LARGEST SINGLE CHARGE, ON THE SAME BASIS, AND GET
OPPOSITE VERDICTS. They differ in how the charge is arranged, not how much of it there is.

CONSEQUENCE FOR STAGE 7. A budget expressed as a bound on sum|q|, on max|q|, on n or on sum q^2 cannot be
justified from this evidence. Any such bound in Stage 7 must either be derived from something other than
the D6 verdicts, or be stated on its face as unsupported by them. This is recorded as a negative result,
and it joins the two electrostatic predictors already proposed, tested and failed in this project
(ERRATUM3 E3.5, and the sum q.V_R proposal tested against the D6 table).

WHAT IS NOT ESTABLISHED AND IS NOT ASSERTED. The Stage 6 designs are frame 42436 and the Stage 4c
arrangements are frame 41786, so frame is confounded with arrangement in the one comparison that carries
the weight. Whether any quantity orders the verdicts is left open, and must be settled against the whole
corpus after cs6_audit.py's walker and charge detection are fixed together, which is Erratum 4's open
item 1. Y03_FIELD_CALIBRATION_RESULT.txt section 8 supplies the quantities and fits nothing.

E5.5  DIAGNOSTIC 4's PREDICTION MIXES TWO CONDITIONS, AND TWO TOTAL-FIELD BOUNDS ARE IN CIRCULATION A
      FACTOR OF TWO APART

DIAGNOSTIC4_RESULT.txt reading 1 states "lambda_quad = 0.25, so the total-field bound is sum |q| <= 1.440
e. Stage 7 imposes that in addition to the per-site cap of 0.80 e." The 0.80 e cap is withdrawn by E3.1.
The 1.440 e bound is NOT withdrawn: E3.3 re-reads what lambda_quad MEANS but leaves reading 1's
instruction standing. Against it, E3.3 and D5.3(d) give a defensible domain of lambda <= 0.50, i.e.
sum |q| = 2.880 e. Stage 7 cannot carry both.

Before either is imposed, one thing must be checked. D4.3's predicted column is

   predicted(lambda) = lambda * sum a q + lambda^2 * b * sum q^2

with sum a q from ARRANGEMENTS.txt, which is the def2-SVP A matrix, and b = -1.5320 from C5, which is
def2-SVPD. The one prediction is then scored against both bases. Scaling sum a q by the measured C5/C1
ratio at site 1690 (7.4233/6.6760 = 1.1119) and keeping D4's own definition of S as
|measured - predicted| / |predicted| gives, for def2-SVPD:

   lambda   S as committed    S like-for-like
   0.25          9.5%              1.1%
   0.50         10.3%              0.0%
   0.75         37.6%             43.3%
   1.00         72.9%             75.3%

E3.3 records that lambda_quad came out 0.25 rather than 0.50 "because def2-SVPD's S at lambda = 0.50 is
10.3 per cent against a pre-registered threshold of 10.0", a near miss of three tenths of a point that
"moves the implied bound by a factor of two". On a like-for-like prediction that near miss disappears.

THIS IS AN ESTIMATE, NOT A RESULT. It scales a twenty-site sum by a one-site ratio. Settling it needs the
def2-SVPD A column for those twenty sites, which does not exist. D4.3 is not at fault: it followed its own
pre-registration, which chose a def2-SVP a. NOTHING IN D4 IS WITHDRAWN. But lambda_quad = 0.25 must not be
imposed as a bound until this is resolved, and the collision with 2.880 e must be settled explicitly.

D4 reading 3, "S6-20 DOES NOT SURVIVE", is unaffected: it compares two measured numbers and involves no
prediction. D5.3(d) is likewise unaffected.

CORRECTIONS TO MY OWN STATEMENTS MADE EARLIER IN THIS SESSION

Recorded because they were stated and were wrong.

   I recommended a budget of sum|q| <= 3.0 e, on the ground that sum|q| ordered the def2-SVP verdicts.
   E5.3 withdraws that recommendation. The ordering was an artefact of looking only at six designs that
   all saturate the same budget, against a D6 table that Erratum 4 E4.5 shows omits 20 per cent of the
   record in the direction that matters.

   I drafted an item asserting that sum q^2 <= 2.00 is inert once a per-site cap is in force, computing
   that the most concentrated admissible design under a 0.65 e cap reaches only sum q^2 = 1.768. That
   calculation assumed charges may be chosen freely down to zero on the admitted sites. Erratum 4 E4.3
   records from OPTIMISER_CODE_README.md that 1228 of the 2769 development sites cannot carry zero charge
   and 1064 admit only |q| = 1, so a sphere costs the budget at least its floor squared. The item is wrong
   and is not in this erratum.

   I described sum q^2 <= 2.00 as simply uncalibrated. Erratum 4 E4.3 states it correctly:
   STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt does fix B = 2.0 e^2, for the S6-2 and S6-3 arrangements, while calling it a
   code-test value in the same breath.

   I wrote that a Pauli wall for electrons is the untested fix. Erratum 4 E4.6 establishes that it is not
   untested: STAGE4B_CRITERIA.txt pre-registered criterion B1, and B1 FAILED. The wall recovers 3.09 eV of
   gap at identical coordinates and takes the SCF from 26 cycles to 14, so the mechanism claim is right
   and measured, but b remained 32.6 per cent apart between bases against a 10 per cent criterion. A CLEAN
   verdict is necessary and not sufficient.
