==============================================================================
THE VERDICT
==============================================================================

   THE EMBEDDING CHARGE REACHES THE CORRELATION TREATMENT.
   On B1 the Hartree-Fock reference moves by -0.204109274 Eh and the corrected
   correlation energy moves by -0.004901707 Eh, which is 4902 times the
   threshold. The silent failure this diagnostic was built to catch - a
   charge entering the SCF and not the correlated step - did not happen.

   WHAT THAT LICENSES, AND ONLY THAT. The coupled-cluster test of ddE
   with the arrangement's charges present may be built. D38.3 says a pass
   licenses the expensive test and licenses nothing else. No barrier, no
   ddE, no design conclusion and no production level of theory follows
   from this document.

   NO BARRIER (D38.1). One geometry, the reactant only. No transition state was
