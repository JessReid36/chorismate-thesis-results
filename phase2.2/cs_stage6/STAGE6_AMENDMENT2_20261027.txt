A2.1  WHAT CHANGES

From this date, every Stage 6 single point that evaluates a designed arrangement is run IN VACUO.
No continuum appears alongside designed charges again without a further amendment.

This supersedes, for Stage 6 only, the sequencing proposed in SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md section 4
item 1 - "Retain CPCM eps = 4 while the charge-placement machinery is developed". That proposal was
sound on what was known on 29 September. Diagnostic 10 supplies what was missing.


A2.2  WHY - THREE INDEPENDENT GROUNDS

Each is from the full text of a paper held in chorismate-refs, verified by grep.

1. IT IS THE WRONG KIND OF FIELD.  Fried & Boxer (annurev-biochem-061516-044432): a preorganised
   active site creates a field "with a particular orientation with respect to its substrate because
   the enzyme's field is controlled by the protein's folded structure, not by the dipole(s) of its
   substrate (as in a solvent reaction field). This highlights the difference between a field
   created by preorganization versus reorganization." A continuum's field is slaved to the
   substrate's own dipole; a designed field is not. Warshel (cr0503106) makes it exact - for an
   environment "randomly oriented toward the TS in the absence of charge (as is the case in water),
   then the second term is zero", the second term being the preorganisation contribution. Five
   independent sources (Warshel; Fried & Boxer; Hennefarth & Alexandrova, nihms-1716744; Sokalski,
   0304-5102; Dittner & Hartke, acs.jctc.8b00151).

2. THE GEOMETRY HAS NO PRECEDENT.  Barone & Cossi's definition of the method (jp9716997): "the
   solute molecule, possibly supplemented by some solvent molecules belonging to the first solvation
   shell, is placed in a cavity surrounded by a polarizable continuum." Explicit charges belong
   INSIDE the cavity. Four independent sources use that topology. Diagnostic 10 measured, from the
   committed outputs, that ORCA builds the cavity on the 24 QM atoms alone - cavity volume
   1694.8824 A^3 and 2094 surface points, identical with 0, 1 and 20 external charges - so Stage 6's
   charges sat 1.0 to 1.7 A OUTSIDE it, in the dielectric. The mechanism is documented
   (Garcia-Rates & Neese, jcc.26139: the cavity is built by "scaling the vdW radius of each atom",
   Bondi radii, f = 1.2). Whether placing external charges outside it is legitimate is documented
   nowhere: the strings "inside the cavity", "outside the cavity" and "dielectric boundary" occur in
   none of the 77 papers held. SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md section 6's "Nothing found in the held
   literature" still stands, now precisely.

   For chorismate mutase specifically the field puts the charge in the solute: Li & Chan 2025
   (ct4c01698) place Arg90 inside the QM region with no continuum anywhere; Claeyssens
   (B313759G) treat Arg90 as an MM residue inside an explicitly solvated protein and use continuum
   models only for the uncatalysed solution reference.

3. IT IS NOT A SMALL CONSTANT.  Diagnostic 10 measured the continuum's differential contribution
   (TS minus R) as -0.62 kcal/mol for the bare substrate but -6.6, +5.9 and +20.2 kcal/mol for
   S6-P, S6-2 and S6-20 relative to that control. The designed charges do not move between R and
   TS; only the substrate, and with it the cavity, does. Claeyssens' Table 4 gives the
   chorismate-specific caution: PCM 53.4 against IPCM 36.0 kcal/mol for the solution barrier at
   identical RHF/6-31+G(d) - "Different continuum solvent models give very different estimates of
   these relatively subtle effects." Himo (jacs.7b02671) supplies the rule: "if the obtained
   energies in the cluster model are very sensitive to the choice of the dielectric constant, then
   conclusions involving these energies must be considered as weak", and calls eps = 4 "somewhat
   arbitrary".

   CAVEAT ON GROUND 3. The -6.6 / +5.9 / +20.2 figures are the CPCM dielectric-energy differential
   read from the outputs. They are not an exact decomposition of the total: the solute energy also
   changes, and the dielectric differential matches the total CPCM shift closely for S6-P and S6-20
   but not for S6-2. Ground 3 is therefore corroborative. Grounds 1 and 2 carry the amendment.


A2.3  WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

- It is NOT claimed that combining a continuum with designed charges is formally double-counting.
  No source in the library says so, and this amendment does not say so either.
- It is NOT claimed that Diagnostic 10's CPCM numbers are wrong as measurements. They reproduce
  Diagnostic 2's independently generated C4 condition to 3.4e-10 Eh. They stand as measurements of
  what that condition gives. They are simply not a design evaluation.
- Diagnostic 10's verdict, NOT RECOVERED, is unaffected: S6-20's sign reverses in the CONTINUUM-FREE
  ma-def2-SVP arm too (+4.71 here, +4.57 in D1), and the ranking inverts there as well.


A2.4  WHAT THIS LEAVES OPEN

Binding the dianion. The reason CPCM was retained on 29 September was that it binds a species whose
HOMO is +1.2269 eV in vacuo, and that reason has not gone away - it has lost its remedy. Removing
the continuum does not restore the gas-phase reference's innocence; it returns the problem.

Two things bear on it, both verified. Mulholland (b508181e), on this reaction: "We use the gas-phase
profiles as a convenient and meaningful reference. The difference between the gas-phase and QM/MM
energy gives the stabilization of the reacting system by the protein environment." Li & Chan 2025
do the same independently. So a gas-phase reference is defensible here. Against that, Diagnostic 10
showed the cancellation that justifies it fails once designed charges are present.

STAGE6_PROBE1_20261027.txt begins on this, cheaply, by asking whether an explicit first shell binds
the density. If it does not, the evaluation moves to Phase 1's full QM/MM, which is a decision for
the supervisor and is recorded here as such.
