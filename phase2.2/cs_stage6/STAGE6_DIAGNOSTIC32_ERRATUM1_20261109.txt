E1.1  WHAT THE RESULT SAYS

R32.1 reports the primary verdict

   THE WALL RESCUES IT UP TO SOME n: rescued or unchanged at n = [1, 2, 3],
   still unreliable at n = [5, 10, 20]

and marks nine of the thirty conditions STILL UNRELIABLE.


E1.2  WHAT ACTUALLY FIRED

Every one of those nine was flagged on ONE criterion, the wavefunction stability analysis, and
on nothing else. Of the five criteria the reader applies:

   relocation      all nine have a Loewdin rise BELOW the 0.25 e threshold. The largest is
                   A/n20 at +0.2441 e, which is 0.0059 e under the line. The single exception
                   in the grid is B/n20_q1.00 at +0.8203 e, which IS over and stays flagged.
   gap             fired on none of them
   convergence     GATE RUNS records 0 of 66 runs not converged
   termination     GATE RUNS records all 66 terminated normally
   stability       fired on all nine

So relocation - the quantity the wall was added to change - passes at every n except the one
condition named above.


E1.3  WHY THAT CRITERION READS THE WRONG SOLUTION

phase2.2/cs6_d32.py judges on stability["first"], the verdict of the FIRST stability analysis.
Read from the committed outputs with the reader's own markers:

   of the 66 d32 runs, 21 reported an unstable first solution
   all 21 restarted and reconverged
   0 of the 66 end with an unstable verdict

   of the 62 d31 runs, 34 reported an unstable first solution
   all 34 restarted and reconverged
   0 of the 62 end with an unstable verdict

And in all 128 runs across the two diagnostics there is exactly one LOEWDIN ATOMIC CHARGES
block and exactly one FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY, and both appear AFTER the last stability
block. Every quantity that reached the result - the Loewdin relocation, the gap, the energy,
the dipole - was therefore computed from the reconverged, stable solution. The unstable
solution's numbers were never reported.

The criterion is inherited, and the reason it was once correct has lapsed. DIAGNOSTIC 20's
pre-registration states it in terms, at line 84:

   "The verdict of a run is the FIRST stability analysis, which by GATE 3 is the production
   solution. A second analysis, where ORCA reconverged, is reported with it."

and phase2.2/cs6_d19.py prints the same justification. That was true when it was written: the
production head did not carry STABPerform, so the first analysis WAS the solution the numbers
came from. DIAGNOSTIC 31 put STABPerform on by default. From that point the production
solution is the reconverged one, and the first verdict no longer describes it. The rule was
carried into cs6_d31.py and cs6_d32.py with the code; its justification was not re-examined.


E1.4  WHAT A CORRECTED READING GIVES, AND WHY IT IS NOT A VERDICT

The reader was re-run on the committed tree with one line changed - blocks[-1] in place of
blocks[0] in stability(), nothing else - and gave:

   nine conditions move from UNRELIABLE to INCONCLUSIVE, not to RELIABLE:
     A/n05  A/n10  A/n20  B/n05_q1.00  B/n10_q0.50  B/n10_q0.75  B/n10_q1.00
     B/n20_q0.50  B/n20_q0.75
   the primary becomes "rescued or unchanged at n = [1, 2, 3], still unreliable at n = [20]"
   no DIAGNOSTIC 31 verdict changes: 0 of 30 conditions, and the iso-total families are
     unchanged, because every d31 condition is independently flagged on relocation

They become INCONCLUSIVE rather than RELIABLE because all nine required level shifting or
TRAH to converge. A shifted run's HOMO-LUMO gap reads as the shift floor, so the gap leg is
unreadable. With relocation passing and the gap unreadable there is nothing left to certify in
either direction. The three rescued conditions on each arm are the contrast: n = 1, 2 and 3
are neither shifted nor restarted in either state, so both legs are readable.

THIS RECOMPUTATION IS POST HOC AND IS NOT A REPLACEMENT VERDICT. It was computed after the
result was committed and after its outcome was known, so it cannot be dressed as a
pre-registered re-reading, and it is not offered as one. DIAGNOSTIC 32's committed verdict
stands as the diagnostic's verdict. What this erratum establishes is narrower and is stated
plainly: the STILL UNRELIABLE verdict at n = 5 and n = 10 rests on a criterion that reads a
solution no reported number came from, and it should not be quoted as evidence that the wall
fails there.

Nothing here reinstates a budget or a cap. DIAGNOSTIC 29 still rules out a limit written as a
function of the charges and the withdrawn Sigma q^2 <= 0.58 e^2 stays withdrawn.


E1.5  WAS THE INSTABILITY THE WALL'S DOING

No, and the depths say so. Lowest eigenvalue of the stability matrix on the first pass, from
the committed outputs:

   wall-only controls, argon centres at zero charge, TS   -0.000157  -0.000158  -0.001973 Eh
   n = 1, 2, 3 with a full unit charge                     all positive, so stable
   n = 5                                                   -0.011955 to -0.019596 Eh
   n = 10 and n = 20                                       -0.124114 to -0.173439 Eh

The charged conditions reach 88 times deeper than the wall ever does, and the depth increases
monotonically with n. The wall-only arms also did not move density on the relocation leg -
rises of +0.0004, +0.0063 and -0.0069 e - which is why D32.6 G1 did not fire.
