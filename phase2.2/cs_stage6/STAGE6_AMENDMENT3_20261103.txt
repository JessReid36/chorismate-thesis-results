Stage 6, AMENDMENT 3. 3 November 2026.
STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is unchanged and is not edited. This amendment adds ONE arrangement to the list
of designs that are relaxed, and changes nothing else: not the protocol, not the quantities, not the
survival criteria, not the tolerance, not its floor, not the failure definitions, and not how G0 and
kappa are fixed. Every one of those is used exactly as the criteria state them.

WHY

The criteria name S6-5, S6-10 and S6-20 - the committed optimiser's own best designs at those
counts - as the designs Stage 6 relaxes, and their inputs were committed on 26 October in
phase2.2/cs_stage6/relax/. Read out of those inputs' own site.ORCAFF.prms files, they carry

   S6-5    5 spheres   sum|q| = 3.0000 e   max|q| = 1.0000
   S6-10  10 spheres   sum|q| = 4.3800 e   max|q| = 0.5500
   S6-20  20 spheres   sum|q| = 5.7600 e   max|q| = 0.5500

Three diagnostics have since measured where those fields sit.

   DIAGNOSTIC 20 ran a field ladder on S6-20 and its READING 1 verdict is "ONSET AT lambda 0.75",
   with lambda 0.50 STABLE at both states. D20 prints lambda, not a charge. On S6-20's own
   committed field of 5.7600 e those two rungs are sum|q| = 2.88 e and 4.32 e, DERIVED HERE by
   multiplication and not quoted from D20. S6-10 at 4.3800 e and S6-20 at 5.7600 e are at or above
   the onset; S6-5 at 3.0000 e is inside the unmeasured 2.88 to 4.32 e window.

   DIAGNOSTIC 17 classified its own designs at full field NOT READABLE under D6.3 - CLEAN,
   COLLAPSED, STRAINED at n = 10 (sum|q| = 4.2913 e) and at n = 20 (5.6433 e). Those are different
   arrangements from the three above, with a different per-site profile, but of comparable total
   field.

   DIAGNOSTICS 15, 16 and 17 established the readable window, and every condition the Results draft
   reports sits at sum|q| = 1.3000, 1.0777, 1.6166 or 2.1900 e. NONE of those has relaxation inputs.

So the relaxation as committed would relax arrangements whose single points are outside the domain
the project can read, and the arrangements inside that domain cannot be relaxed at all. This
amendment adds one of the latter.

THE SELECTION RULE, FIXED HERE AND APPLIED MECHANICALLY

Among the conditions of CHARGE_VECTORS_SUMMARY_20261103.tsv, take those that are
   (a) readable under D6.3 (readable_D6_3 = yes),
   (b) at n > 5, and
   (c) at sum|q| at or below 2.88 e, which is S6-20's committed field times DIAGNOSTIC 20's last
       fully stable rung, lambda 0.50,
and choose the one with the LARGEST sum|q|. TWO conditions tie there, both at 2.1900 e - S6-10 at
2.1900 e and S6-20 at 2.1900 e - so the tie-break is fixed here as well: at equal total field, take
the LARGER max|q|, as the stricter test of DIAGNOSTIC 17's per-site ceiling of 0.5389 to 0.6500 e.
That selects S6-10 at 2.1900 e with max|q| 0.2750 e over S6-20 at 2.1900 e with max|q| 0.2091 e.

cs6_y57.py re-derives the whole selection from the committed files and refuses to build if the rule
does not pick the arrangement these inputs carry, or if the tie-break leaves two conditions level.
No arrangement is chosen or rejected by an effect it produces: the rule uses n, the total field, the
largest per-site charge and a readability verdict, and none of those is an effect. The tie was found
by the gate, not anticipated by me, and the tie-break was fixed before the inputs were built.

The rule selects S6-10 at sum|q| = 2.1900 e - the arrangement committed as n10_bare_A, DIAGNOSTIC
15's arm A, at max|q| = 0.2750 e. Its inputs are added as relax/n10_A/.

AND IT IS NOT A NEW DESIGN. It is the criteria's own S6-10 AT HALF FIELD: the same ten sites, the
same Amber N spheres, the same two starting geometries, with every charge multiplied by exactly 0.5.
That is checked rather than asserted - each of the ten new charges equals the template's charge times
0.5 to within 1e-12 AND equals the charge in this arrangement's own committed pc.pc, and the ten
sphere positions in the committed inputs are where that pc.pc puts them to within 1e-9 A. So
combined.xyz and job.inp of all four jobs are the committed files BYTE FOR BYTE, and the only
difference anywhere in the four inputs is ten numbers in one column of site.ORCAFF.prms.

DIRECTION

This is the LESS permissive choice, and it is the only direction worth stating here: the selected
arrangement's committed frozen effect is smaller in magnitude than those of the three the criteria
name, so the amendment moves the relaxation away from the largest effects and not towards them.
Nothing here is chosen by the barrier any design reaches.

WHAT IS RUN

Four jobs for the added arrangement, byte-for-byte on the criteria's protocol:
   relax/n10_A/R_sp    def2-SVP QM/MM EnGrad single point at the fixed reactant geometry
   relax/n10_A/TS_sp   the same at the fixed transition state
   relax/n10_A/R_opt   Opt, substrate active, spheres frozen, Cartesian, from the same start
   relax/n10_A/TS_opt  OptTS from the same start, frequencies to follow
and the two already committed and not yet run:
   relax/S6-C/R_opt    relax/S6-C/TS_opt     the no-sphere control, from the same start
The control's frozen single points are already among the 22 of frozen/FROZEN_REPORT.txt.

ONE CLARIFICATION THE CRITERIA NEED FOR THIS ARRANGEMENT

ddE(frozen) for the Loss comparison is the NEW R_sp and TS_sp pair, at def2-SVP with the spheres in
the force field. It is not the -10.6714 kcal/mol the Results draft reports for this condition: that
figure is at def2-SVPD with bare point charges and no Lennard-Jones term, which is a different level
and a different representation. Loss is a difference within one representation, so both of its terms
come from the same four new jobs. The draft's figure is unaffected and is not restated here.

WHAT THIS AMENDMENT CANNOT SETTLE

1. One frame, 42436, as the criteria already state.
2. One arrangement. A single relaxed design cannot calibrate G0 and kappa; the criteria's fit needs
   the arrangements that meet both survival criteria, and this adds one candidate to that set.
3. This is not the catalytic verdict. The criteria say so and this amendment does not change it: the
   verdict is SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.5's chain - an optimised transition state with one counted
   imaginary mode, an IRC in both directions, the ends minimised, and B against the reference by the
   same chain in the same medium. Nothing here is quoted as a barrier.
4. The spheres are frozen, which the criteria already name as a limitation rather than a choice.
