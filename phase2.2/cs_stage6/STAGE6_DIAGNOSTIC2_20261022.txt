Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 2: the charge-magnitude scan. Does the linear coefficient the optimiser uses survive a change
of basis and of environment, and where does the linear model stop being valid? Written 22 October 2026, after
DIAGNOSTIC1_RESULT.txt returned NOT USABLE AS THEY STAND and before any relaxation input is regenerated.

THIS IS A DIAGNOSTIC AND NOT A METHOD CHANGE. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt fixes B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP in vacuo for everything
Stage 6 reports and that stays fixed. PHASE22_PLAN.txt section 2.2's decision to run no continuum alongside the
charges stays in force. No number these runs produce replaces a reported number, enters a fit, or changes a
criterion, tolerance or threshold. They exist to say whether a reported number can be believed, and to measure a
quantity the pre-registration already names as neglected.

D2.1 WHY

This measurement is not new. s11_probe_scan_result.txt ends:

    STILL PENDING: the charge-magnitude scan at 3.2 A (q = 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5), which
    fits ddE(q) = a*q + b*q^2 and so measures the linear coefficient the MILP uses and the
    differential polarisation it neglects. PBS 414212.

and the companion table in OUTPUT_EXCERPTS_20260929.md headed "Charge-magnitude scaling: does the even part go as
q^2?" is empty. The scan was designed on 30 September 2026 and never produced a result. Three things since have
made it the decisive measurement rather than a loose end.

First, PHASE22_PLAN.txt lists as surrogate level 2 "LINEAR electrostatic response, neglecting the measured b*q^2
polarisation term". The term is neglected by design and its size at the designs' own geometry has never been
measured.

Second, the only measurement of it anywhere - phase2b_charge_design/06b_inner_shell/, 29 September 2026 - was made
at def2-SVPD with CPCM epsilon = 4, that is with diffuse functions AND the dianion bound. It found the odd part
recovered to 1.1 per cent of q*Dv at 3.0 A and to 20 per cent at 2.5 A, with the even part 12.9 and 23.2 per cent.
Every Stage 6 design places its closest sphere at 2.85 to 2.96 A, between those two rows. The production pipeline
then moved to def2-SVP in vacuo, where neither quantity has been measured.

Third, s11_probe_scan_result.txt's own finding 1 - "NO ELECTRONIC OVER-POLARISATION ANYWHERE FROM 1.5 TO 10 A, AT
FIXED GEOMETRY" - was obtained at "! B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVP def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF", in vacuo. That is the one
condition in which the effect cannot appear: no diffuse functions, around a density whose highest electrons are
not bound and therefore have nowhere to go. Two measurements in the repository disagree and the pipeline inherited
the reassuring one. DIAGNOSTIC1_RESULT.txt has now shown a 118 per cent movement with a sign reversal.

D2.2 WHAT IS RUN

One site, both geometries, both signs, four magnitudes, five conditions. 93 single points of about five minutes.

   SITE        1690, which is S6-P's site and the top-ranked site of the committed grid, at
               44.207824 19.215457 38.959451. Its committed a is -6.67782 kcal/mol per e (A_f42436.tsv via
               relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt). A_REPORT.txt's finite-difference check (c) validated a at sites 1678,
               1954 and 1612 at +-0.05 e and NOT at 1690, so this also extends that validation to the site every
               design uses.

   GEOMETRIES  frame_42436/reactant_qm.xyz and transition_state_qm.xyz, the committed Stage 6 geometries, byte for
               byte, with their recorded sha256 checked.

   CHARGES     q = +-0.05, +-0.25, +-0.50, +-1.00 e. Both signs at every magnitude, because that is what separates
               the response into its parts: odd(q) = [ddE(+q) - ddE(-q)]/2 is the first order the MILP uses and
               even(q) = [ddE(+q) + ddE(-q)]/2 is the polarisation it neglects. +-0.05 is A_REPORT.txt's own
               finite-difference step; +-1.00 is the charge bound the grid admits; nothing outside [-1, +1] is run
               because the optimiser cannot use it.

   A BARE POINT CHARGE, not an LJ sphere, in every run. This is forced and not a convenience. STAGE6_FINDINGS
   _20261022.md Finding 4 measured the LJ sphere against the bare charge at this very site and found they differ by
   exactly L, 0.0000 kcal/mol, with identical electronic deviation - necessarily, since the Lennard-Jones term
   never enters the QM Hamiltonian. So the electronic response is the same object either way, L is a classical
   number already committed in L_f42436.tsv, and a bare charge is what cs5_amatrix.py itself uses for the A matrix
   and its finite-difference check. It is also what makes the continuum conditions runnable at all: ORCA 6.0.1
   refuses CPCM together with its QM/MM module but permits CPCM with %pointcharges, which is what the
   29 September runs used.

   CONDITIONS  a 2 x 2 in basis and environment, plus one convergence point:

      C1   def2-SVP      in vacuo          the production condition. STAGE5_CRITERIA.txt section 6's own level,
                                           and its keyword line must equal cs5_amatrix.py's HEAD byte for byte.
      C2   def2-SVP      CPCM epsilon 4    isolates the continuum at fixed basis
      C3   ma-def2-SVP   in vacuo          DIAGNOSTIC 1's condition
      C4   ma-def2-SVP   CPCM epsilon 4    isolates the basis at fixed continuum; diffuse AND bound, the condition
                                           in which the linear response has ever been found accurate
      C5   def2-SVPD     in vacuo          the convergence point, and CHARGE_MODEL_DECISIONS.md D1's own
                                           outstanding guard: "the polarisation term b is the quantity most
                                           affected by an unbound dianion, so WP7 repeats two sites at def2-SVPD"

   The 2 x 2 is the point. Basis and environment have never been varied independently in this project, and
   Diagnostic 1 changed only the basis while the one favourable measurement changed both.

   ALSO, three runs that cost nothing and settle a separate question: the VERTICAL DETACHMENT ENERGY of the bare
   dianion at the reactant geometry, as E(monoanion, charge -1, doublet, same geometry) - E(dianion), at def2-SVP,
   ma-def2-SVP and def2-SVPD in vacuo. Finding 8 reads a positive HOMO as meaning the highest electrons are
   unbound. Two papers held in chorismate-refs say that inference is not valid for DFT orbitals: Rienstra-Kiracofe
   et al., Chem. Rev. 2002, 102, 231, p. 246, attributes positive anion HOMOs to self-interaction error in inexact
   functionals and notes hybrids "often produce negative HOMO energies for even anions"; Galbraith & Schaefer,
   JCP 1996, 105, 862, p. 863, states that "the physical significance of the orbitals obtained from density
   functional methods is questionable". The VDE is the quantity that settles it, and no paper held computes it, or
   the HOMO, for the chorismate dianion in any basis.

D2.3 WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

The quantities are, per condition: a_est, the slope of the odd part as q -> 0, taken as odd(0.05)/0.05; b, the
curvature from the even part; and q*, the largest magnitude at which |even(q)| stays under 10 per cent of
|odd(q)|.

  THE A MATRIX DOES NOT REPRODUCE ITS OWN RESPONSE - tested first, and the most serious outcome - if a_est at C1
  disagrees with the committed a of -6.67782 by more than A_REPORT.txt's finite-difference tolerance. C1 is the
  level and the site that produced that number, and analyse separately requires C1's two bare energies to
  reproduce A_REPORT.txt's committed in vacuo values to below 1e-6 Eh, so a disagreement here is not a basis or
  environment question at all: either the A matrix's construction or its row order is wrong at this site. That
  would have to be found before any comparison below means anything, and it would put every design in Stage 6 and
  every number downstream of the A matrix in question. It is tested first for that reason.

  THE A MATRIX IS SOUND if a_est at C1 agrees with the committed a of -6.67782 within A_REPORT.txt's own
  finite-difference tolerance, 0.01 kcal/mol per e plus 1 per cent of |a| (that is, within 0.0768), AND a_est at
  C3 agrees with C1 within the same tolerance. The linear coefficient is then basis-independent, Stage 5's A
  column stands untouched, and the whole of Diagnostic 1 is a statement about charge MAGNITUDE and not about the
  surrogate's coefficients. The way forward is then the formulation: a minimum sphere-to-substrate distance, a
  total-field budget sum_i |q_i| <= B1 of the kind s14_design_milp.py already implements, and a per-site cap at q*.

  THE A MATRIX IS BASIS-SENSITIVE AND MUST BE RE-DERIVED if a_est at C3 differs from C1 by more than decision O6's
  15 per cent method-and-basis figure. Stage 5's A column, every design built on it, and every number downstream
  of it would then have to be rebuilt at a basis that is stated and justified. This is the expensive branch and it
  must be identified rather than avoided.

  QUALIFIED, in between: a_est moves by more than the finite-difference tolerance but less than 15 per cent. The
  coefficients are then usable with the movement stated as a method uncertainty.

  SEPARATELY, ON THE ENVIRONMENT: if b at C4 is smaller in magnitude than half of b at C3, binding the density
  materially improves the linearity of the response, and the design environment becomes a live question on grounds
  of model validity rather than of results. PHASE22_PLAN.txt section 2.2's double-counting argument would then have
  to be weighed against it explicitly, with the measured differential continuum effect of 0.431 kcal/mol
  (SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md) as the cost. If instead b at C4 and C3 agree within half, the continuum does not
  fix the response, section 2.2 stands untouched, and no part of this diagnostic argues for reopening it.

  ON CONVERGENCE: if b at C5 (def2-SVPD) agrees with C3 (ma-def2-SVP) within 25 per cent, the minimal augmentation
  is converged enough to be read as an answer. If it does not, neither diffuse basis is an arbiter and the
  bracketing in the assessment stands as the honest conclusion.

  ON BINDING: a NEGATIVE vertical detachment energy means the dianion is genuinely metastable to electron loss at
  that condition, and Finding 8's reading is vindicated on the right quantity. A POSITIVE VDE means the species is
  bound despite the positive HOMO, Finding 8's second half must be withdrawn as an artefact of reading Koopmans'
  theorem off a DFT orbital, and "the designs make it worse" ceases to be a finding.

No threshold here is new. The 0.01 + 1 per cent is A_REPORT.txt's existing finite-difference tolerance, the 15 per
cent is decision O6's existing method-and-basis figure, and the 10 per cent defining q* is a stated convention for
reporting rather than a criterion anything passes or fails. Nothing is accepted or rejected on the strength of them.

D2.4 WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

The geometries, which are frame 42436's committed stationary points throughout; Finding 7's 33.8 kcal/mol descent
is untouched by anything here. Additivity, which Finding 2 measured at def2-SVP only and which the four ADD-* runs
would have to repeat. More than one site: site 1690 is where every design puts its largest positive charge, and a
coefficient that survives there may still fail elsewhere on the grid. More than one frame. And whether any
admissible charge distribution can lower this barrier usefully, which is the open question of the project and is
not what a scan at one site can answer.

DIRECTION

None. No decision, threshold, tolerance, criterion, arrangement or reported quantity changes, and the stage's
method remains B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP in vacuo. What changes is whether the A matrix is known to be sound, what the
largest usable per-site charge is, and whether the environment question is reopened - and D2.3 fixes which before
the runs go in.
