The result is not edited. This records what it got wrong, how, and what follows.


E2.1  THE STATEMENT

R29.4 says, under THE SET STILL CONTAINS NO UNIT CHARGE:

   "Every design carrying a +-1.00 e charge - S6-P, S6-2, S6-3, S6-5, E5, E7 - was run only at
    def2-SVP, the basis DIAGNOSTIC 27 showed to be blind to relocation for want of diffuse
    functions. Nothing in the record measures relocation for a +-1 e design."

The first clause of R29.4 is true as written: D29's set, defined by the pre-registration as 402 or
682 basis functions, contains no charge above 0.6500 e. Everything after it is false.

132 conditions carrying a charge of magnitude 1.000 e exist on the dianion, in vacuo, in a
diffuse basis, each with both a Loewdin and a dipole block. They sit at ma-def2-SVP, which is
328 basis functions against plain def2-SVP's 264. They are in cpcm, qscale, sitescan, standoff, distributed
{'cpcm': 4, 'qscale': 16, 'sitescan': 96, 'standoff': 16}. 130 of them place a single charge; 2 place two
(cpcm/S6-2_R_vac, cpcm/S6-2_TS_vac), carrying Sum|q| = 2.0000 e at unit magnitude.

Charge-free ma-def2-SVP controls on the dianion exist in cpcm, qscale, sitescan, standoff, water_probe, 9 of them, in
both states. So the relocation criterion is applicable to these conditions as it stands.


E2.2  THE CAUSE

ma-def2-SVP IS A DIFFUSE BASIS. It is def2-SVP minimally augmented: one diffuse shell on each
heavy atom, which is where the 64 extra functions come from. Calling it blind "for want of diffuse
functions" is wrong about the basis, not merely about the coverage.

The pre-registration defined the set as "a diffuse basis (402 or 682 basis functions)". That
definition came from a survey of the tree which had already been narrowed by the directory glob
Erratum 1 records, so ma-def2-SVP never appeared in it and the restriction was written as though
the only diffuse bases in the record were def2-SVPD and def2-TZVPD. The reading is sound within
its own declared set; the account of why the set excludes unit charges is not.

A SECOND NAME-BASED FILTER, RECORDED BECAUSE IT IS THE SAME ERROR TWICE. In counting these
conditions the first attempt identified the reaction state by requiring the directory name to END
in _R or _TS. That dropped the cpcm vacuum arms, whose names end in _vac - and those are the only
two unit-magnitude conditions at n = 2 in any diffuse basis, the single most relevant conditions
to the question the paragraph was about. Erratum 1 recorded a glob over directory names; this is a
suffix test on directory names. Both failed silently and both changed what was concluded.


E2.3  WHAT IT AFFECTS

D29's VERDICT IS UNAFFECTED. It rests on transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_R and lam075_TS, both inside
D29's own set, whose charge vectors are identical and whose relocation verdicts differ. Adding
conditions cannot undo a pair that already settles it: no function of the charges can order
relocation. Every figure in R29.1, R29.2 and R29.3 stands.

WHAT CHANGES IS THE LIMITATION. R29.4's claim that nothing in the record measures relocation for a
+-1 e design is withdrawn. The correct statement is:

   D29's set contains no unit charge, because its pre-registered basis restriction excludes
   ma-def2-SVP. 132 unit-magnitude conditions on the dianion do exist in that basis, with
   same-basis controls, and the relocation criterion has not been applied to them. 130 of them
   are a single charge and 2 are two. No condition with unit magnitudes at n >= 3 exists in
   any diffuse basis.

So the unit-charge question is answerable from runs already on disk, for n = 1 and for one n = 2
design, and is not answerable at all for n >= 3 without new runs. Reading them needs its own
pre-registration; nothing about their relocation has been computed, and only the availability of
their inputs and controls has been counted.
