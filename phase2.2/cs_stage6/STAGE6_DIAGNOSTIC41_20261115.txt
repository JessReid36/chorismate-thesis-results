STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 41
The correlated barrier at the production basis: DLPNO-CCSD(T)/def2-SVPD on frame 42436.

Pre-registered 15 November 2026, before any D41 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D36 measured the correlated barrier of the bare substrate at def2-TZVPD: 23.8603
kcal/mol. Every design in the record is evaluated at def2-SVPD. So the benchmark and the
production numbers are at different basis sets, and D36 says what it could not do about
that: "D36 DOES NOT RUN CCSD(T) AT def2-SVPD AND CANNOT MEASURE THE METHOD'S OWN BASIS
SHIFT." What it offered in place of a measurement is the shift the functionals show,
which for the eight functionals now measured at both basis sets runs from -0.8624 to
-1.3028 kcal/mol (def2-TZVPD minus def2-SVPD; D37's table).

Half of the missing measurement already exists. D38's run N is the bare substrate at
frame 42436's reactant at DLPNO-CCSD(T)/def2-SVPD, committed:

   d38/N    E(0) -831.831909980   E(CCSD(T)) -834.533005232   T1 0.016726026

D41 runs the other half, the transition state, with the same input.

It is also a leg that the coupled-cluster test of ddE, which D38 licensed, cannot do
without: that test needs the charge-free barrier at the basis the charged runs use.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
One single point.

   run   geometry                             input
   T     d33a/sp_42436_TS/substrate.xyz       d38/N/job.inp, byte for byte

   ! DLPNO-CCSD(T) def2-SVPD AutoAux TightSCF TightPNO
   %maxcore 6000, %pal nprocs 16, %scf MaxIter 300, charge -2, multiplicity 1

No charges, no MM region, no wall. The reactant leg is NOT run again: it is
d38/N/job.out as committed, and its sha256 is written beside the inputs
(d41/REACTANT_LEG.sha256) so that the reader can require that very file.

RESOURCES. One job, cm_d41, 16 cores, 120 GB, walltime 24:00:00, no -q. The job script is
D39's, cut down to one closed-shell run: it works in node-local scratch, copies the
output home, and samples the scratch in use every 60 s while ORCA is running. D38's
result Erratum 1 bounds a reactant run at this basis between 31 and 43 GB. The transition
state is not measured; at def2-TZVPD it needed more than the reactant (80 GB against 74,
D36). So 100 GB is asked for and 60 GB is the least a scratch location may offer. That
figure is not defended as sufficient. If the run fails on a write, that is a measurement
of the footprint and not a failure of the method. D38's five runs took between 1 h 05 and
1 h 14 each.

4. GATES. ANY FAILURE STOPS THE DIAGNOSTIC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G41.1  T/substrate.xyz is byte-identical by sha256 to d33a/sp_42436_TS/substrate.xyz, and
       d38/N/substrate.xyz to d33a/sp_42436_R/substrate.xyz.
G41.2  T/job.inp is byte-identical by sha256 to d38/N/job.inp.
G41.3  THE REACTANT LEG. d38/N/job.out has the sha256 recorded in d41/REACTANT_LEG.sha256
       and prints E(CCSD(T)) = -834.533005232.
G41.4  The run terminates normally, converges its SCF, reports 118 electrons and prints a
       CCSD(T) energy, and its last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY is that energy. A label
       that ORCA prints more than once must print one value.
G41.5  In both legs, E(0) plus E(CORR)(corrected) equals E(CCSD), and E(CCSD) plus the
       triples correction equals E(CCSD(T)), each to within 2e-9 Eh.
G41.6  No committed file under d33a, d36, d36a2 or d38 is modified.

THERE IS NO INDEPENDENT CONTROL FOR THE TRANSITION STATE AT THIS BASIS, as D36 said of
its own transition-state leg. What stands behind the number is this: the input is D38's
run N; D38 ran the same method line and settings four more times, twice with a charge of
zero, and those two reproduced run N to 1e-9 Eh; and D36's G36.8 measured that where the
integrals are stored does not move a converged energy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D41.1  IT IS NOT A CONVERGED BARRIER. def2-SVPD is a smaller basis than D36's, which D36
       already calls modest for CCSD(T). The number is for comparison at the production
       basis and must not be quoted as the in vacuo barrier.
D41.2  It is not the experimental number. E7.11 applies to every sentence that puts it
       near 24.5 or 20.7 kcal/mol, which are a free energy and an enthalpy in water.
D41.3  One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo. D35 measured the barrier moving by +1.4992
       to +3.6023 kcal/mol between two frames.
D41.4  DLPNO is an approximation; TightPNO is requested and its residual error is not
       measured. The two legs were run in different jobs.
D41.5  Two basis sets do not make an extrapolation, and none is attempted.
D41.6  No charges, no design, no reliability claim, nothing about r_safe.
D41.7  It adopts no production level of theory. That is an amendment and a decision for
       Jessica.
D41.8  Nothing here is shaped toward an outcome. If the functionals' order at def2-SVPD
       differs from their order at def2-TZVPD, or CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ is not the closest of
       D36's three at this basis, that is the result.
