STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 61: D59's test of the functions at the positive charges of the ten
survivors, made again with each ghost centre 0.01 A beyond its charge. PRE-REGISTRATION.

25 November 2026. Claude drafted this text; it takes effect when Jessica commits it, with D59
NOTE 1, the inputs of d61/ and the programs cs6_d61_prepare.py, cs6_d61.py and
cs6_d61_harvest.sh, before any job of it is submitted, and before D59's outputs are collected.
It is hers to change before she commits it.

It changes no word of D56 or of D59, names no design a result, and adopts no level. Nothing
committed is edited by it.


1. WHY

AMENDMENT 11, A11.5 item 5: "THE SURVIVORS, at most the ten with the largest lowering, ARE RUN
WITH FUNCTIONS AT THEIR CHARGES AND BEHIND THE HELIUM WALL under a pre-registration of their
own, and what is found is reported beside each." D59 was that test for the ten survivors A13.4
item 1 (b) names. Its 60 runs with the charges and the functions together stopped in ORCA's
start-up, because its program put each ghost centre on its point charge (D59 NOTE 1). This
diagnostic makes those runs again, with their controls, as D53's inputs place the ghost centres.


2. WHAT IS RUN

THE DESIGNS: D59's ten, by the rule of A11.5 item 5 applied by cs6_d59_prepare.py to the
committed d56_survivors.tsv: S_n10_k1, S_n10_k3, S_n10_k2, S_n08_k1, S_n08_k3, S_n08_k2,
S_n06_k1, S_n06_k2, S_n06_k3, S_n05_k2.

THE RUNS: for each design and state (R, TS), the dianion, frozen, in vacuo, at CAM
(CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD), WXV (wB97X-V/def2-TZVPD) and HF (Hartree-Fock/def2-TZVPD): F, the
design's charges as D56's committed pc.pc holds them and the functions of D50 as a ghost
hydrogen near each positive charge; GF, the same functions at the same centres and no charge.
120 runs: 40 at HF, 40 at CAM, 40 at WXV. Each input is D59's committed input of the same name
with only the lines of the ghost centres changed (checked by Claude for all 120: the rest of
each input, and each pc.pc, are D59's byte for byte). N and GN are not made here: they carry no
ghost atom, ended normally in D59, and are read from d59/ as D59's pre-registration reads them
("N: the same charges and no functions (the partner)." and "GN: neither (the plain control, one
for each level and state).").

WHERE THE GHOST CENTRES STAND: 0.01 A beyond each positive charge, on the line from the nearest
atom of the substrate at R or TS, rounded to six decimals, the rule of the committed
cs6_d48_prepare.py ("the ghost centre sits this far beyond the charge, on the line from the
nearest atom"), by which D50, D53 and D54 placed theirs. cs6_d61_prepare.py re-forms by this
rule every ghost centre of D53's committed inputs with functions that stands at a positive
charge, 144 of them, and stops unless each is D53's to the six decimals written; it stops if any
ghost centre of d61/ lies outside 0.005 to 0.02 A from its charge, as cs6_d53_prepare.py did, or
within 0.005 A of any point charge. The centres are listed in d61/GHOSTS.tsv. DIRECTION: none;
the functions stand 0.01 A from where D59's words put them.

THE JOBS AND THE ORDER: 13 jobs; the caps are D59's ("10200 s for F and GF, 9000 s for N and GN,
4500 s at Hartree-Fock"), and every job ends within 24 hours at its caps. A run that reaches its
cap is stopped and not made again. The first job, J01, is submitted alone. Its first run is
HF_F_S_n05_k2_R, a run with the charges and the functions together. A block that reads only (P6)
shows whether that run has passed ORCA's start-up into its SCF (its output holds SCF SETTINGS,
which ORCA writes when its start-up is done, and no error termination); it reads none of its
numbers. Only then are the other 12 jobs submitted (P7). If it has stopped in start-up, nothing
more is submitted and a dated note says why.


3. THE READINGS, FIXED NOW

R61.1 IS D59's R59.1, WORD FOR WORD: the three paragraphs of section 3 of D59's pre-registration
under R59.1, read by D53's committed functions as D59's committed reader reads them, with one
change of where: F and GF are the runs of d61/, and N and GN the runs of d59/. So, as there,
"each design has its own control with functions GF, because its functions stand at its own
positive charges." THE GAIN is "(F minus GF) minus (N minus GN), formed when all four are
measured and, at CAM and WXV, the electrons each grid integrates lie within 0.02 e of 118." and
the two levels together give, for each design, "TAKES ELECTRONS WITH FUNCTIONS AT ITS POSITIVE
CHARGES if at R or at TS its word is TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS;" "HOLDS WITH FUNCTIONS AT
ITS POSITIVE CHARGES if its word is HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS at both states;" or "otherwise NOT
SETTLED WITH FUNCTIONS, with its words printed."

THE TABLES, beside the result: d61.tsv (every run of d61/ and every run of d59/ used, with its
status) and d61_functions.tsv (the columns of D59's committed reader's d59_functions.tsv).


4. THE GATES

If one fails, the reader writes nothing and the harvest commits nothing. GI.1 the
pre-registration and D59 NOTE 1 are the committed ones; GI.2 the programs are the committed
ones, among them D53's and D56's reading functions and cs6_d59_prepare.py; GI.3 every input of
d61/ is the file the committed cs6_d61_prepare.py forms from the committed files, D59's inputs
of N and GN are the files the committed cs6_d59_prepare.py forms, and the ten designs are A11.5
item 5's; GI.4 every output read echoes the committed input beside it; GI.5 every measured run
reports the basis functions its input asks for (682, and 17 more for each ghost atom); GI.6 the
committed d52_rules.tsv holds the rules D59 names; GI.7 D56's reading functions give D56's
committed word SURVIVOR for each of the ten; GI.8 D59's result is committed, with the output of
every run N and GN. D61 is therefore collected after D59.


5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW

As D59's section 5: "Nothing found here names, keeps or drops a design, and none is rescued;
D56's words stand." A design that TAKES ELECTRONS WITH FUNCTIONS AT ITS POSITIVE CHARGES is
reported as such, and whether it may then be quoted is for the amendment A11.5 item 5 names. No
run is made again in its folder.


6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

As D59's section 6: "One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, charges of 0.25 e that stand for no
atom. Functions at the positive charges only cannot show an electron going to a negative
charge." D59's own runs with functions are not read: its runs F stopped at start-up, and its
runs GF had their ghost centres at the charges' positions.
