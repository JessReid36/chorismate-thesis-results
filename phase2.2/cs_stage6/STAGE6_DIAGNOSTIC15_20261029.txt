D15.1  THE QUESTION

Does the sum of the per-site d_shield, each charge measured alone, bound the d_shield of the whole
ten-charge design?

This is DIAGNOSTIC 14.5's question and DIAGNOSTIC 14 did not answer it. D14's Reading 1 covered
positive-only subsets, and its n6neg control stopped the diagnostic before even those were read.
That stop was right, and not only procedurally: S6-10 is a mixed-sign design, so additivity
measured on positive subsets alone would not license quoting a per-site reading as an upper bound
on it. AMENDMENT 1 A1.4 then measured the six negatives one at a time and found them neither
inert nor confined to one site - d_shield from -0.0197 to -0.0863 kcal/mol each at def2-SVPD,
summing to within 0.7% of the six together. The premise D14 was built on, that the artefact is a
positive-charge phenomenon, does not hold.

DIAGNOSTIC 14 is not reopened and is not re-read. It stands at NO VERDICT. This diagnostic asks
the mixed-sign question D14.4 explicitly excluded, and it is a new diagnostic for that reason.

D15.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS COMPUTED

d_shield(S) = ddE(bare, S) - ddE(wall, S), each ddE corrected by the zero-charge wall term as
STAGE4C_AMENDMENT1 requires. Classification is D6.3 with AMENDMENT 1 A1.2's dipole correction.
Nothing here is new; no tolerance changes.

   Dsum = sum over the TEN sites of | d_shield({i}) |     each measured alone
   Dset = | d_shield(S6-10) |                             the ten together
   tol  = max( 0.10 x Dsum, 0.05 kcal/mol )               DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's, unchanged

READING 1, at each readable condition:

   NO EFFECT TO BOUND   Dsum <= tol and Dset <= tol. There is no artefact at this condition for a
                        bound to be about, and no verdict is given. This precondition is
                        AMENDMENT 2's lesson applied in advance: where everything lies under the
                        0.05 floor, a BOUNDED verdict is arithmetic about noise.
   BOUNDED              Dset <= Dsum + tol. The per-site readings of DIAGNOSTIC 12 and 13 may be
                        quoted as upper bounds on THIS design at THIS condition.
   NOT BOUNDED          Dset > Dsum + tol. They may not, and nothing measured one charge at a time
                        bounds this design.
   NOT READABLE         any of the eleven runs is STRAINED or COLLAPSED by D6.3.

Dset/Dsum is reported at every readable condition whatever the verdict.

READING 2, the sign decomposition. Dpos over the four positives and Dneg over the six negatives
are reported beside Dsum, with Dneg/Dsum. NO CRITERION is placed on them. D14 assumed the
negatives were silent and A1.4 showed they are not; the honest replacement for that assumption is
the measured split, not a new threshold invented now.

READING 3, the nested positive subsets n2, n3 and n4, where readable, under the same rule as
Reading 1. These are DIAGNOSTIC 14's own ratio points, and they are reported here as a NARROWER
statement about positive-only subsets. They are not an answer to D15.1 and may not be quoted as
one, for the reason given in that section.


D15.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

One arrangement, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry. Two conditions, and nothing at arm
B def2-SVPD where the design's own bare run is STRAINED. Nothing about total fields outside 2.19
and 2.88 e, and in particular nothing at lambda = 1. Nothing about any other arrangement: whether
S6-20's twenty charges behave like S6-10's ten needs S6-20's own series. Nothing about WHY the
negatives contribute at def2-SVPD and not at def2-SVP; A1.4 located that effect and did not
explain it. The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption
(ERRATUM7), so the walled arm is a contrast and not a truth: this measures whether the CONTRAST
is bounded. In vacuo, no continuum (STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa,
which remain unmeasurable until the OptTS / coordsys cartesian conflict of
STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028.txt is decided.

A BOUNDED answer licenses quoting per-site readings as upper bounds on THIS design at THAT
condition. It is not a property of the grid, of the charge model, or of designs in general.


D15.5  DIRECTION

DIAGNOSTIC 12 said no descriptor is adequate and the charge model must change. DIAGNOSTIC 13 said
a single per-site cap cannot be stated and a per-site screen is what the grid cannot carry. Both
readings are per-site. Whether either may be quoted against a real, mixed-sign design has never
been established, and D14 stopped before establishing it. That is what this diagnostic settles,
for one design at two conditions, and D15.3 fixes which answer means which before anything is
computed.
