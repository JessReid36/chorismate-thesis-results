Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 21 ERRATUM 1 (2 November 2026).

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC21_20261031.txt is not edited. This erratum records an error in how its set was
DESCRIBED. No criterion, gate, threshold or verdict is changed, and no measured number is
replaced.


E21.1  WHAT IS WRONG

D21.2 states, of its nine conditions:

   "These nine are every readable WHOLE-DESIGN condition at def2-SVPD in the record."

That is false for two of the nine. Their Dset is reported NOT READABLE by the diagnostics that
produced them:

   S6-2_D16    DIAGNOSTIC 16 READING 1 reports S6-2 at 1.3000 e as NOT READABLE
               its bare arm S6-2_bare_F1_R is STRAINED under D6.3, gap 0.567 eV
   S6-5_D17    DIAGNOSTIC 17 READING 2 reports S6-5 at 1.6166 e as NOT READABLE
               its bare arm S6-5_bare_M_R  is STRAINED under D6.3, gap 0.880 eV

   [both measured, read from the committed DIAGNOSTIC16_RESULT.txt and DIAGNOSTIC17_RESULT.txt]

In both cases the unreadable arm is the BARE arm at R - the very arm D21 measured. D21.2's
"THE BARE ARM ONLY" therefore does not rescue the description.

This project holds SEVEN readable whole-design Dset values at def2-SVPD, not nine:

   condition      sum|q|   Dset     source
   S6-2_D17       1.0777   0.3468   DIAGNOSTIC 17
   S6-3_D16       1.3000   0.4379   DIAGNOSTIC 16
   S6-5_D16       1.3000   0.4311   DIAGNOSTIC 16
   S6-10_D16      1.3000   0.2430   DIAGNOSTIC 16
   S6-10_D15      2.1900   0.4140   DIAGNOSTIC 15 / 14 arm A
   S6-20_D16F1    1.3000   0.1702   DIAGNOSTIC 16
   S6-20_D16F2    2.1900   0.2919   DIAGNOSTIC 16


E21.2  HOW IT HAPPENED

The set was assembled from the designs that had committed def2-SVPD energies, and "readable" was
taken to mean "has a committed result" rather than "has a Dset its own diagnostic reported". The
runs' existence was checked; their results' readability was not. WORKING_RULES rail 2 requires
the record to be checked before a diagnostic is designed, and this is a failure of that rail, not
of the runs.


E21.3  WHAT STANDS AND WHAT DOES NOT

STANDS, unchanged:
   - All eighteen stability measurements. They measured what they measured, on the arms named,
     and GATE 1, GATE 2 and GATE 3 all passed as reported.
   - READING 1's verdict ALL STABLE, over those eighteen runs.
   - READING 2 (NOTHING TO CORRECT) and READING 3 (ONSET TRANSFERS) as reported.
   - The measured stability region: stable to sum|q| = 2.88 e, unstable from 4.32 e, 2.88 to
     4.32 e unmeasured.

DOES NOT STAND:
   - The description of the nine as "every readable WHOLE-DESIGN condition".
   - D21.1's sentence "If these designs are stable, the window claim is measured rather than
     inferred and every Dset inside it stands as reported." Two of the nine carry no Dset, so
     that sentence is true of seven conditions and vacuous for two. The window claim itself is
     unaffected: it rests on the seven, all of which are stable.

NOT A CORRECTION, BUT WORTH RECORDING. D21's post-hoc reading found S6-2_D16 to have the
thinnest stability margin of all eighteen runs, E(0) = +0.00137 Eh. It is also one of the two
conditions whose bare arm is STRAINED, at a 0.567 eV gap. A near-zero stability eigenvalue and a
collapsing HOMO-LUMO gap are the same electronic marginality seen through two different
indicators, and they agree on which design is marginal. [measured, both from committed files]


E21.4  WHAT THIS CHANGES DOWNSTREAM

DIAGNOSTIC 22 inherited this set from D21 without re-deriving it and carries the same error. It
is corrected by STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC22_ERRATUM1_20261102.txt, which is committed before D22's
result is read.

Nothing else in the record depends on the count of nine.
