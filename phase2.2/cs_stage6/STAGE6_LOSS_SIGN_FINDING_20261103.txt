This file records that Stage 6's tolerance - the one number the stage exists to apply - cannot fail
as the criteria write it, and that the criteria contain three readings of Loss which do not agree in
sign. It proposes no correction. Correcting a pre-registered formula is an amendment and is
Jessica's to make; this finding exists so that the choice is made deliberately and on paper rather
than inside a reader.

Found on 3 November 2026 while building cs6_relaxed.py, before any relaxation had finished, so no
measured number is affected and nothing is withdrawn.


L1  THE TWO LINES

STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt line 109, under WHAT IS MEASURED PER ARRANGEMENT:

   Loss          ddE(frozen) - ddE(relaxed), with the control's own change subtracted so that Loss
                 is the design's share and not the substrate's descent from a geometry that is not
                 its own minimum;

STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt line 139, under THE TOLERANCE, AND WHERE IT COMES FROM:

   LOSS MUST BE BELOW 15 % OF |ddE(frozen)|.

And the intent the same section states, in its first sentence: "No published source states a
tolerance of the form 'at least X per cent of a predicted effect must survive a fuller treatment'".


L2  THREE READINGS, AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME NUMBER

Write B for a barrier, D for the design, 0 for the control, and note that ddE is negative when a
design lowers the barrier.

   (1) the expression as line 109 writes it
          Loss = ddE(frozen) - ddE(relaxed)

   (2) the clause inside the same line: the design's own barrier change with the control's own
       change subtracted
          [B_relaxed(D) - B_frozen(D)] - [B_relaxed(0) - B_frozen(0)]
             = ddE(relaxed) - ddE(frozen)
             = -(1), identically, for any eight energies

   (3) the magnitude of effect that did not survive
          |ddE(frozen)| - |ddE(relaxed)|
             = (2), whenever both ddE are negative

(2) and (3) agree with each other and are POSITIVE exactly when a design loses effect, which is
what a tolerance of the form "at least 85 % must survive" has to mean. (1) is their negative.

So line 109's formula and line 109's own clause are each other's negative, and only one of them can
be what line 139 tests.


L3  WHAT THAT COSTS, ON FOUR WORKED CASES

|ddE(frozen)| = 20 kcal/mol throughout, so line 139 allows at most 3.00. Criterion (i) also
requires ddE(relaxed) to be negative. Computed, not argued:

   case                      ddE(frozen)  ddE(relaxed)   (1)      (2)=(3)   verdict by (1)  by (3)
   keeps a tenth of it           -20.00       -2.00     -18.00    +18.00       PASS          FAIL
   keeps nine tenths of it       -20.00      -18.00      -2.00     +2.00       PASS          PASS
   effect grows by half          -20.00      -30.00     +10.00    -10.00       FAIL          PASS
   effect reverses sign          -20.00       +5.00     -25.00    +15.00       FAIL          FAIL

Read literally, the tolerance PASSES a design that kept a tenth of its predicted effect and FAILS
one whose effect grew by half. Under reading (1) a design can only fail the tolerance by getting
BETTER, so for any design that keeps the right sign and loses magnitude - which is the entire case
Stage 6 was built to detect - the tolerance cannot fail.

What does still work: criterion (i)'s separate requirement that ddE(relaxed) is still negative
catches the fourth case, where the effect reverses. The gap is precisely the third row of
STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt's own motivation, Dittner's 2.8-80 % carry-over: right sign, most of the
magnitude gone.


L4  WHAT THIS IS NOT

It is not a measurement, and it changes no committed number. The frozen half is untouched: nothing
in frozen/FROZEN_REPORT.txt or frozen.tsv involves Loss.

It is not a claim about which reading is intended. Two of the three agree with each other and with
the stated intent, and that is as far as the evidence goes; the pre-registration says what it says.

It is not evidence about n10_A or any other arrangement. No relaxation had produced a barrier when
this was found.

It is not a reason to relax the tolerance. The 15 % figure, its provenance in decision O6, and the
3.2 kcal/mol floor clause are all untouched here; only the sign of the quantity compared against
them is at issue.


L5  HOW THE READER BEHAVES UNTIL IT IS DECIDED

cs6_relaxed.py computes and prints all three readings, checks that (1) and (2) really are each
other's negative to 1e-9 and stops if they are not, and reports criterion (i) TWICE - once under
reading (1) and once under reading (3) - naming the disagreement when they differ. It does not
choose. Its relaxed.tsv carries both verdicts in separate columns, effect_as_written and
effect_lost_reading, so nothing downstream can silently adopt one.

Its selftest drives the four cases of L3 plus one under the 3.2 floor and requires the two verdicts
to differ on exactly two of the five, so the defect is a tested property of the reader rather than a
remark in a document.


L6  WHAT DECIDING IT WOULD TAKE

A dated amendment that states which quantity line 139's "LOSS" names, leaves line 109's prose in
place unedited, and says whether any committed figure elsewhere in the project was computed with the
other sign. One search has been done for that last point and found nothing, because no Loss has ever
been computed: STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028.txt R4 records that Loss "cannot be produced by the
protocol as written" and was unmeasurable until AMENDMENT 4. That is not a proof of absence - it is
one search of the Stage 6 directory and the frozen report - and an amendment should say where else
it looked.

Not decided here.
