D22.1  THE QUESTION

The quantity this thesis quotes is d_shield, and its reported form is Dset = |d_shield|. For
the nine readable whole-design conditions it is formed exactly as cs6_d17.py and
cs6_additivity.py form it: every energy referenced to the charge-free control at the same
basis, and the WALLED ARM ONLY additionally corrected by its zero-charge wall term, which
cs6_additivity.py labels "Stage 4c Amendment 1: ddE_corr = ddE - ddE(zero charge)".

   z(st)      =  E(wall0, st)  -  E(ctl, st)
   ddE(bare)  =  [ (E(bare,TS) - E(ctl,TS))         - (E(bare,R) - E(ctl,R))         ] x H
   ddE(wall)  =  [ (E(wall,TS) - E(ctl,TS) - z(TS)) - (E(wall,R) - E(ctl,R) - z(R)) ] x H
   d_shield   =  ddE(bare)  -  ddE(wall)

That formula has been reproduced from raw committed energies against two independently
committed Dset values before this document was written: S6-20 at n=20 gives 0.2919 and S6-10
gives 0.4140, both matching DIAGNOSTIC 16's committed result to four decimals. [measured]

d_shield therefore rests on FOUR distinct solutions per state, not three, because the
wall-at-zero energy is INSIDE the number through z and is not merely a gate on it:

   leg            status                      measured by
   control        VERIFIED STABLE             DIAGNOSTIC 20, def2-SVPD ctl_R and ctl_TS
   bare           VERIFIED STABLE             DIAGNOSTIC 21, nine conditions, 18 runs
   walled         UNVERIFIED                  this diagnostic
   wall-at-zero   UNVERIFIED                  this diagnostic

Two of the four legs under the number the thesis quotes are sitting on solutions nobody has
verified are minima.

   A note on scope, because the project uses the zero-charge wall in two different ways and
   they must not be confused. In cs11_standoff.py and cs12_sites.py the zero-charge arm is an
   INERTNESS VETO: C-A1.1 requires |ddE(wall, q=0)| <= WALL0_KCAL and VOIDS the walled result
   at any rung that fails. In cs6_d17.py and cs6_additivity.py, which are the modules that
   produce the nine readable conditions and therefore the subject of this diagnostic, it is a
   SUBTRACTED CORRECTION as written above. This diagnostic concerns the second use. Verifying
   these arms does not revisit the first.

DIAGNOSTIC 20 measured a wall once: def2-SVP, 20 Ne> centres, lambda = 1.00.

   bare   UNSTABLE in both states (DIAGNOSTIC 19, not re-run there)
   wall   R   UNSTABLE  E(0..2) -0.01146856  -0.00812353  +0.00251640
   wall   TS  UNSTABLE  E(0..2) -0.03474872  -0.01510996  +0.00240551

That is one design at one basis at a field where the bare arm was already unstable, and D20.4
says so in its own words: "The numbers here are for S6-20 at these lambdas and do not transfer
to DIAGNOSTIC 16's or 17's designs without measuring them." THIS DIAGNOSTIC MEASURES THEM.

The expectation going in is that every arm here is stable. Every one of these nine conditions
sits at sum|q| <= 2.19 e, inside the measured stable region, their bare arms are stable, and
where a wall was measured it did not change stability. An expectation is not a measurement.
Nothing below assumes it, and D22.4 is written so that the opposite result is readable.

One thing is worth stating because it bounds what the wall can be doing. In all thirty source
runs the basis count is 402 and NEL is 118 - identical to the bare def2-SVPD control. The Ne>
centres are coreless (N_core 10) and carry no basis set, so they contribute no electrons and no
basis functions. The wall acts through the ECP projector alone. [measured, from the committed
job.out of all thirty sources]

WHY 1e-6 Eh AND NOT TIGHTER. This project now has two independent measurements of how closely
these runs reproduce at all. DIAGNOSTIC 21's GATE 2 found |diff| <= 3.07e-12 Eh over eighteen
runs. And the two replicate wall-at-zero runs above - byte-identical job.inp, same ORCA 6.0.1,
same 8 MPI ranks, same 15 SCF cycles - differ by 7.958e-12 Eh at R and 2.194e-11 Eh at TS. That
is 35 and 96 units in the last place of a float64 near 836, and it comes from parallel reduction
ordering, not from physics. [both measured]

That floor has since been measured across the whole committed record rather than inferred from
two pairs. Of 937 committed single-solution production runs, 14 groups - 24 pairs - have a
byte-identical COMPLETE input set, counting every file in the run directory that is not a known
ORCA output, so that an unrecognised file makes two runs look different rather than falsely
identical. Those 24 pairs span def2-SVP and def2-SVPD, controls and designs, and seven trees
(additivity, transfer, d16, d17, d18, negatives, qscale/sitescan/standoff). Their energy
differences run from exactly zero - 8 of the 24 are bit-identical - to a maximum of

   2.194e-11 Eh  =  1.4e-08 kcal/mol  =  96 ULP          [measured, 24 pairs]

Fingerprinting job.inp alone would have been wrong and the data said so: an input that
references an external geometry is byte-identical across runs with different geometries, which
produced apparent differences of 1e3 Eh before the fingerprint was corrected.

So 2.2e-11 Eh is the tightest any energy gate in this project could legitimately be set.
GATE_EH at 1e-6 Eh is 4.6e4 times looser than that, and D11.3's 1.067 kcal/mol budget is eight
orders of magnitude above it, so neither is anywhere near the floor. No gate in this project has
been set near it, and this one is not either.

every one of the thirty runs, and any failure stops the run before anything is submitted.

   GATE 3(a) REDUCTION. Reversing the one substitution must reproduce the committed source job.inp
       BYTE FOR BYTE. Not equivalent, not agreeing in the head, not matching after whitespace
       normalisation - identical bytes.

   GATE 3(b) SELF-CONTAINMENT. The source input must contain no `xyzfile` and no `%pointcharges`, so
       that job.inp alone is the complete input and copying it copies everything. A walled
       input that silently referenced an external geometry would be run here against whatever
       file happened to be beside it. All thirty have been checked and none references an
       external file. [measured]

   GATE 3(c) GEOMETRY. The 24 substrate atoms in the source's inline `* xyz -2 1` block must match
       the committed frame_42436 geometry for that state, element by element, to 1e-8 A.
       d_shield compares a bare and a walled arm AT THE SAME GEOMETRY; a walled arm standing on

       would carry that error forward rather than catch it. All thirty have been checked and all
       match. [measured]

D22.3  WHAT IS READ

READING 1. The verdict and the three lowest eigenvalues of the stability matrix, for all thirty
runs, labelled by condition and arm.

READING 2. THE d_shield ARM AUDIT. For each of the nine conditions, six arms:

   bare R, bare TS        DIAGNOSTIC 21, committed, READ AND NOT RE-RUN
   walled R, walled TS    this diagnostic
   wall0 R, wall0 TS      this diagnostic

A condition is ARMS VERIFIED when all six report STABLE. The control leg, which enters every
term of the formula in D22.1, is verified separately and not re-run: it is GATE 1. So ARMS
VERIFIED plus GATE 1 is the full set of solutions d_shield stands on.

This is the reading the diagnostic exists for: it is the difference between "the bare leg is
stable" and "the number we quote is stable in every leg".

READING 3. THE REPLICATE. n10_wall0 and S6-10_wall0 have byte-identical inputs, so they are the
same calculation submitted twice. Two questions, both pre-registered:

   (a) do they return the same verdict?
   (b) is |dE(0)| <= max(1e-5 x |E(0)|, 5e-8 Eh)?

The tolerance is relative with an absolute floor, per the project's rule that tolerances are
relative. The floor is the print resolution of E(0), which ORCA gives to eight decimals. It is
NOT derived from the 1e-11 Eh energy floor above, and must not be read as a prediction:
WHETHER A STABILITY EIGENVALUE REPRODUCES AS TIGHTLY AS A TOTAL ENERGY IS NOT KNOWN TO THIS
PROJECT, and this reading is how it finds out. If the replicate disagrees, the finding is about
the reproducibility of the eigenvalue - a property of the diagnostic - and not about the
chemistry.

READING 4. CONDITIONAL, and empty unless READING 1 finds an unstable arm. GATE 2 is what
licenses this comparison. For each unstable arm, the correction to that arm's own energy

   correction  =  E(reconverged stable)  -  E(first converged)

is substituted into the slot of D22.1's formula that the arm occupies, and the resulting
d_shield is reported beside the committed one. Which slot depends on the arm, and both routes
are pre-registered here so neither is chosen after the fact:

   an unstable WALLED arm        changes E(wall, st), so it moves ddE(wall) only
   an unstable WALL-AT-ZERO arm  changes E(wall0, st), so it moves z(st), which moves
                                 ddE(wall) only - and it moves EVERY condition that shares
                                 that wall-at-zero arm, per D22.2's sharing table

Each affected condition's change in d_shield is stated against the DIAGNOSTIC 11.3 budget of
1.067 kcal/mol. The bare arm is not re-run here, so its committed energy is used unchanged;
DIAGNOSTIC 21 verified it stable and that verdict is reused, not revisited. If every arm is
stable, READING 4 says so and reports nothing else.


D22.4  CRITERIA, FIXED BEFORE THE RUNS EXIST

Every verdict this diagnostic can return is named here. The reader emits one verdict per
reading and nothing else, and the line it prints is one of these words.

READING 1 returns ALL STABLE or SOME UNSTABLE
   ALL STABLE      every one of the thirty arms reports a stable restricted solution.
   SOME UNSTABLE   at least one does not. The result names which, by arm and state.

READING 2 returns ALL ARMS VERIFIED or ARMS NOT VERIFIED
   ALL ARMS VERIFIED
      All thirty stable, and all six arms of all nine conditions stable. Then every readable
      d_shield in this project rests on verified-stable solutions in ALL FOUR LEGS - control,
      bare, walled and wall-at-zero - and the quantity the thesis quotes is stability-verified
      end to end.
   ARMS NOT VERIFIED
      At least one arm unstable. The result marks every affected condition NOT VERIFIED and
      reports READING 4 for it. No d_shield of an affected condition may be quoted without
      that correction attached.

READING 3 returns REPLICATE AGREES or REPLICATE DISAGREES
   By READING 3's test, reported either way. A disagreement is a finding about the
   reproducibility of a stability eigenvalue, not about the chemistry.

READING 4 returns NOTHING TO CORRECT, CORRECTION WITHIN BUDGET, or CORRECTION MATERIAL
   NOTHING TO CORRECT        no arm is unstable, so the reading is empty.
   CORRECTION WITHIN BUDGET  every affected condition's change in d_shield is at or under
                             D11.3's 1.067 kcal/mol.
   CORRECTION MATERIAL       at least one exceeds it. Those conditions' d_shield may not be
                             quoted without the correction attached.

Whatever is returned stands. If the walled arms prove unstable where the bare arms were not,
that is a finding about the wall representation and it enters the record as it comes. This
diagnostic is not permitted to prefer the outcome that keeps d_shield quotable.


D22.5  WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

It is a restricted-to-restricted stability test. A STABLE verdict means the solution is a
minimum with respect to the orbital rotations ORCA's stability analysis spans. It does NOT mean
the system is single-reference. DIAGNOSTIC 19's READING 3 returned DISAGREE and that stands
untouched by anything here.

ORCA's restart is a bounded heuristic. Any correction in READING 4 is a LOWER BOUND on the
correction, not the correction. (DIAGNOSTIC 20.4, carried forward.)

It measures nine conditions at def2-SVPD and nothing else. It says nothing about other designs,
other bases, or sum|q| above 2.19 e. The region from 2.88 e to 4.32 e remains unmeasured and
everything from 4.32 e up remains unstable and provisional.

It does not revisit the Ne parameter borrowing recorded in ERRATUM 7. The wall remains a
contrast, not a truth, and verifying that its solutions are minima does not make its parameters
appropriate.

It cannot distinguish a wall that is physically wrong from a wall that is physically right.
Every arm here could be stable and the wall could still be the wrong representation of Pauli
repulsion. Stability is a necessary condition on the arithmetic, not evidence for the model.
