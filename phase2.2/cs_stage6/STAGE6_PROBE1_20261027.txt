STAGE 6 - PROBE 1, PRE-REGISTERED                                             27 October 2026

WHAT DOES AN EXPLICIT WATER SHELL COST, AND DOES IT BIND THE DIANION?

A probe, not a diagnostic. It has no verdict rule because it decides nothing on its own.


P1.1  WHY

STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027 removes CPCM. The reason CPCM was retained on 29 September - that it
binds a dianion whose HOMO is +1.2269 eV in vacuo - has lost its remedy, not its force. Before
committing to an expensive environment, two planning questions are worth a few hours:

   1. Does a small explicit first shell bind the density at all?
   2. What does a ma-def2-SVP single point cost at 24, 42, 60 and 84 QM atoms?

Question 1 is the one that matters. Question 2 sizes Phase A's environment step.


P1.2  WHAT IS RUN

The BARE SUBSTRATE ONLY. No designed charges, no arrangements, no ddE. There is deliberately nothing
here that could be read as a result about a design.

   w00_R    24 atoms    the in-vacuo control
   w06_R    42 atoms
   w12_R    60 atoms
   w20_R    84 atoms
   w06_TS   42 atoms    the same R-frame waters at the TS geometry

THE SHELL, by rule. The solvated frame is read from the committed archive
05_qmmm/24_sp_barriers/f42436_r8_a1/hpc_text_outputs_2_r_irc.tar.xz, member .../r_irc/final.xyz,
55680 atoms. The substrate is located inside it by exact coordinate match against
frame_42436/reactant_qm.xyz - BUILD_RECORD.txt records that the two agree to 0.0e+00 A and the code
re-checks it rather than trusting the record. A water is an oxygen outside the substrate carrying
exactly two hydrogens within 1.2 A. Waters are ranked by the shortest oxygen-to-substrate distance
and the first N taken.

N = 6 is not a free choice: ranks 1 to 6 lie between 2.592 and 2.949 A and rank 7 is at 4.192 A, a
gap of 1.243 A. Six is the hydrogen-bonded first shell as the distance distribution defines it.
12 and 20 test convergence beyond it.

The SAME water positions are used at R and at TS. A designed charge does not move with the
substrate and neither does this shell, which keeps the environment preorganised rather than
reorganising. The substrate moves 0.225 A on average and 0.615 A at most between R and TS, so the
R-frame shell is geometrically sound at the TS; the generator refuses if any water comes within
1.5 A of a substrate atom.


P1.3  WHAT THIS IS NOT

The real first shell around the substrate in the enzyme is mostly PROTEIN. Within 3.5 A of the
substrate at frame 42436 there are 63 non-water atoms and 17 water atoms. Substrate-plus-waters in
vacuo is therefore a DEVICE FOR BINDING THE ELECTRON DENSITY, not a model of the active site. It is
consistent with SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md section 4 item 2, where the designed charges replace the
protein's MM charges and the waters are retained - but on its own, without charges, it models
nothing and no energy from it should be quoted as a barrier.


P1.4  WHAT IS REPORTED, AND WHAT IS NOT

Reported: wall time, SCF cycle count, and the HOMO of each run.

NOT reported: any ddE, any barrier, any comparison between arrangements. The measure step does not
compute them.

The reading, such as it is: in vacuo the bare dianion's HOMO is +1.2269 eV (Diagnostic 10's
control). A shell that brings it below zero binds the density by a physical mechanism. If the three
shell sizes disagree with each other, the shell is not converged and the environment question goes
to Phase 1's full QM/MM - which is a decision for the supervisor and not something this probe can
or should settle.
