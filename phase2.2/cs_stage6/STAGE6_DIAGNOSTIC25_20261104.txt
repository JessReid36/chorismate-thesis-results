D25.1  WHY, AND WHY NOW

THE ARGUMENT, which is the finding's section 3 and is not restated in full. The objective is
d_f = sum_i a_if q_i + sum_i L_if z_i with a_if = V_TS,f(r_i) - V_R,f(r_i). a is a DIFFERENCE. A site
reaches a large favourable a either because V_TS is strongly negative there, so a positive charge
stabilises the transition state, or because V_R is strongly positive there, so a positive charge
destabilises the reactant and the barrier falls by raising its foot. a is identical in the two cases.
Only the difference is ever formed, so the information needed to tell them apart is discarded before
the solver runs, and none of cs_opt_milp.py's four rules - cardinality, budget, spacing, distinctness
- references the reactant term.

WHAT MAKES IT URGENT RATHER THAN TIDY. Three things, none of them available when the finding was
written on 3 November.

   (a) The relaxation of AMENDMENT 3's arrangement, run 3-4 November, returned both survival criteria
       failing, with its relaxed reactant having moved its centroid 8.8567 A - from 2.61 A to 10.25 A
       from the centre of a sphere cage 15.90 A across - while its transition state moved 0.6536 A.
       (Its rotation is not quoted here: the figure available for it comes from cs6_relaxed.py,
       whose own erratum is outstanding, and unlike the centroid it has not been independently
       re-derived.) The relaxed reactant and the relaxed transition state are therefore no
       longer the same complex. A field that offers the reactant somewhere better to sit is exactly
       what the blindness above permits, and this is the first time the question could be asked of a
       measurement rather than of the code.

   (b) V_R DOES NOT CHANGE SIGN ANYWHERE ON THE GRID. Measured from the committed
       absolute_potentials/V_f42436.tsv and recorded in ERRATUM 1 E1.3: across all 2626 sites V_R
       runs from -164.3739 to -71.6115 kcal/mol per e, while a spans only -6.8436 to +8.3853. So the
       SIGN of the reactant term is governed by the design's net charge, placement modulating the
       magnitude within about a factor of two, and the discarded term is roughly an order of
       magnitude larger per unit charge than the term that is kept.

   (c) cs_opt_milp.py ENFORCES NO CHARGE NEUTRALITY. Its rule set is the four above; net charge is
       reported (its line 812) and never constrained. GOCAT does constrain it. Dittner & Hartke,
       J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2018, Sec. 3.3, added the constraint "to erase this artificial
       freedom" and to "eliminate the redundant total GOCAT charge"; Dittner & Hartke, J. Chem. Phys.
       152, 114106 (2020), p. 114106-6, state that every model there "always enforced charge
       neutrality of the total GOCAT, sum_i q_i = 0". Both read from the full papers in
       chorismate-refs (acs.jctc.8b00151.pdf, 1.5142839.pdf). Taken with (b), net charge is precisely
       the lever that moves the reactant, and this project has left it free where its source method
       closed it.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR THIS ENZYME IN PARTICULAR, which is the finding's section 5: Szefczyk, Mulholland,
Ranaghan & Sokalski, J. Am. Chem. Soc. 2004, 126, 16148 (chorismate-refs/ja049376t.pdf) place
chorismate mutase's catalysis in differential transition-state stabilisation relative to the bound
substrate. A design reaching its barrier reduction by raising the reactant would be off-mechanism for
the enzyme being mimicked, however good its d.

D25.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE IT RUNS

The project carries one fraction, fixed once and used in both places it is needed (decision O6, and
STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt's tolerance): FIFTEEN PER CENT. It is used again here rather than a new number.

   OUTCOME A - THE BLINDNESS NEVER BIT.
   For every design, |dE_R| is below 15 % of |d_elec|. The predicted effects are predominantly
   transition-state side, on-mechanism against Szefczyk 2004, and the only consequence is a sentence
   in the write-up recording that the check was made and what it found. No constraint is added.

   OUTCOME B - OFF-MECHANISM CATALYSIS.
   One or more designs have dE_R > 0 contributing more than 15 % of |d_elec|. Because V_R is negative
   everywhere (D25.1 b), dE_R > 0 means negative net charge raising the reactant's energy: the
   barrier falls partly by lifting its foot. Those designs' barrier reductions do not mean what the
   write-up says they mean, and that is reported per design and carried into the short list as a
   stated limitation. It is NOT grounds for deleting a design: the number stands, its interpretation
   changes.

   OUTCOME C - THE RELOCATION RISK.
   One or more designs have dE_R < 0 with |dE_R| exceeding |d_elec|. The field is lowering the
   reactant's energy by more than it changes the barrier, so the reactant sits in a well the design
   built for it and has an incentive to find the deepest part of it. This is the mechanism the
   4 November relaxation exhibited, and if the arrangement that relocated is in this outcome, that is
   a prediction retrodicted rather than a coincidence. It would make the relocation a property of the
   objective and not of that one arrangement.

   OUTCOMES B AND C CAN BOTH OCCUR, on different designs, and are reported separately.

   THE REMEDY, AND WHICH ONE.
   If the Spearman correlation between q_net and dE_R excludes zero and is strong, the reactant term
   is governed by net charge and the remedy is the constraint GOCAT already uses, sum_i q_i = 0, which
   is one linear row and costs the MILP nothing structurally. If the correlation does not exclude
   zero, placement dominates net charge and the remedy is the explicit row sum_i V_R(r_i) q_i <= 0
   proposed in the finding's section 7. In both cases the constraint is linear in q, so the certified
   gap of zero is preserved.

   ADDING EITHER CONSTRAINT IS NOT DONE BY THIS DIAGNOSTIC AND IS NOT AUTHORISED BY IT. It changes
   what the optimiser optimises, which is a Stage 5 decision requiring its own dated amendment, a
   re-solve, and a statement of which committed designs the amended optimiser would no longer
   produce. This diagnostic only says which remedy the evidence points at.

DIRECTION. Nothing here can favour a lower barrier: the diagnostic computes a decomposition of
numbers already committed, adds no design, removes no design, and changes no barrier. Both remedies
it may point at are RESTRICTIONS on the feasible set, so each can only raise the optimiser's
achievable d, never lower it. If this diagnostic leads anywhere, it leads towards worse scores and
more defensible ones.


D25.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

1. It is FIRST ORDER ONLY, built from the frozen densities of the bare substrate, exactly as a is.
   It says nothing about polarisation, which decision O1 excludes from the objective and which the
   pool's re-scoring with full single points carries. A design with dE_R <= 0 at first order could
   still destabilise the reactant once polarised. The finding's own section 7 states this and it is
   restated here rather than quietly dropped: the diagnostic bounds the problem, it does not close
   it.
2. ONE FRAME, 42436, as every Stage 5 and Stage 6 statement about this project is. V_R is committed
   for 30 frames in the exploratory development set, but at a different grid (ERRATUM 1 E1.1 c), so
   a multi-frame version of this diagnostic is not free and is not proposed here.
3. It is NOT a measure of whether any design catalyses anything. The catalytic verdict remains
   SP_AMENDMENT2.txt A2.5's chain, and no barrier is quoted by this diagnostic.
4. It cannot explain the 4 November relocation on its own. A first-order term at the frozen geometry
   and a relaxed reactant 8.8567 A away are different quantities; outcome C would make the first
   consistent with the second, not equivalent to it.
5. It says nothing about the Lennard-Jones term L, which is not a potential and is not decomposed
   here.
