Both availability counts in the pre-registration are wrong, and why that mattered
5 November 2026

Correcting STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC29_20261105.txt

The pre-registration is not edited. This records what it got wrong, how, and what followed.


E1.1  THE TWO STATEMENTS

D29.1 says the availability of the input fields was counted, and reports

   110 conditions in a diffuse basis carry both a Loewdin and a dipole block
    16 same-basis charge-free controls exist

The correct counts are 182 and 26.


E1.2  THE CAUSE

One line. The code that produced both figures, and the first version of the reader, enumerated
candidate directories with a glob over directory names rooted at the d-series:

   T.glob("d*/**/")              and, for the count, d1[4-9] and d2*

cs_stage6 has 22 top-level directories. Thirteen hold charge files. Seven of those begin with
"d". The glob therefore excluded, silently and with no error,

   transfer         12 conditions      additivity      36 conditions
   negatives        12 conditions      negatives_armA  12 conditions

and the charge-free controls under additivity, negatives and negatives_armA, which are six of
the ten controls the count missed. The other four - d20/def2-SVPD/bare_lam000_R and _TS, and
d23/ctl_R and ctl_TS - lie inside the d-namespace and were missed for a second reason: the count
of 16 was taken over a list of eight directory roots written out by hand, which does not include
d23, and keyed on the name ctl_, which the zero-field lambda arm does not carry. 72 of 182
conditions - two in five - were never looked at. Every excluded
directory is a committed diagnostic: transfer and additivity are named in the DIAGNOSTIC 20, 21
and 22 pre-registrations, negatives carries DIAGNOSTIC14_AMENDMENT1_RESULT.txt and
negatives_armA carries DIAGNOSTIC15_RESULT.txt.

A glob over names is not a search of the record. It encodes an assumption about how directories
happen to be called, it fails silently when that assumption is wrong, and nothing in the counts
it produces shows that anything is missing. The project's own rule covers this: a failed search
is not absence.


E1.3  WHAT IT AFFECTED

THIS ONE CHANGED THE ANSWER. It is not a bookkeeping correction.

On the restricted set of 110, the highest Sigma |q| among conditions that did not relocate was
2.8800 e and the lowest among those that did was 4.2913 e, so Sigma |q| appeared to SEPARATE with
a relative margin of 0.49; Sigma q^2 appeared to separate as well. A reading had been written to
that effect.

On the full set of 182, the excluded transfer directory contains

   transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_R    MOVED,           Loewdin rise +0.3181
   transfer/def2-SVPD/lam075_TS   did not move,    Loewdin rise +0.1136

whose charge vectors are identical. C and B collapse onto the same value, 3.2850 e in Sigma |q|
and 1.1381625 e^2 in Sigma q^2, and no scalar separates. The result is the opposite of what the
restricted set showed, and it rests on a pair of runs the glob had excluded.

So the apparent separation was an artefact of which directories the reader could see.

HOW IT WAS CAUGHT. Before the reading was committed, the figures were recounted independently,
from the committed outputs, by code written from scratch against cs6_audit.py's own definitions
and without reading cs6_d29.py. That recount returned 182 conditions, 170 in the primary set, 35
arrangements and 15 moved, against 110, 98, 25 and 12, and reported C equal to B for both
scalars. The discrepancy was the finding; the pair above was then checked by hand against the raw
job.out and pc.pc files, which is what E1.2 and the result quote.


E1.4  THE FIX

phase2.2/cs6_d29.py now enumerates candidates from the whole tree, by what a directory contains
rather than what it is called: every directory holding a pc.pc or a job.out, anywhere under
cs_stage6. The committed reader carries the comment recording why.

The controls are likewise identified by content - charges absent from both pc.pc and job.inp -
and not by name, except that a control must additionally be named ctl_ or be the zero-field arm
of a lambda series. All 26 are reported with their per-key spread, which is 0.000000 in both the
maximum Loewdin charge and the dipole for every one of the 12 keys, so no choice among the eight
duplicate B3LYP def2-SVPD controls changes any figure.


E1.5  WHY THIS IS RECORDED RATHER THAN QUIETLY FIXED

A pre-registration's availability counts are what a later reader uses to check that the set was
the set it claims. Both were wrong, the error reversed the diagnostic's verdict, and the
corrected reading contradicts a reading that had already been drafted. None of that is visible
from the corrected files alone.
