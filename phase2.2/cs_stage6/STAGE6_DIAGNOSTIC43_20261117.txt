STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 43
Bare charges at or beyond r_safe, on the committed grid: do they pass the four legs, does
the dianion stay bound, and what do designs placed there do to the barrier?

Pre-registered 17 November 2026, before any D43 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.
No committed file used the number 43 when this was written.
It is read under AMENDMENT 5 (stability) and AMENDMENT 6 (binding), and it is the first
search diagnostic under AMENDMENT 7.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every design the record has measured sits inside r_safe. D11 measured r_safe = 3.50 A, at
B3LYP-D3BJ/ma-def2-SVP, along one axis from one site with one unit charge, and fixed in
advance what that would mean: "No design measured inside r_safe is interpretable." D33
says of its own designs "NOTHING HERE ESCAPES r_safe", and D42 of its conditions "EVERY
ONE OF THE 13 CONDITIONS SITS INSIDE r_safe."

At the corrected level, CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD, the record now holds two tests, and
inside r_safe they pull against each other. A design carrying a positive unit charge is
UNRELIABLE at every n tested (D31). The five arrangements that passed the four legs and
had a quotable lowering are METASTABLE at both states (D40). Ten of D31's thirteen
RELIABLE conditions pass both tests (D42), and all ten are inside r_safe.

No arrangement has been measured at this level with all of its charges at or beyond
3.50 A. What the record holds there is D12, at B3LYP-D3BJ/ma-def2-SVP: of its 36 sites,
four lie at or beyond 3.50 A from the nearest atom, and a bare charge of +1 fails D11's
tolerance at three of them.

   site    D12's d_near   ddE bare   ddE wall   d_shield   D12's verdict
   396        3.676       +10.0884    +2.0228    +8.0656      FAIL
   1043       3.720        -0.6715    -0.6089    -0.0627      pass
   397        3.732        +6.2942    +1.8235    +4.4707      FAIL
   4          3.732        -0.0075    -1.8457    +1.8382      FAIL

The consolidated write-up, WRITEUP_CONSOLIDATED_20261111.md in its revision of 16
November 2026, names three hypotheses in its section 11 that this bears on. They are
quoted here as committed in results c21e34be.

   H3  "Outside r_safe a bare charge is reliable at every magnitude up to one." To be
       given up if "a unit charge at 3.50 A or beyond fires any leg of the criterion at
       CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD."
   H2  "Relocation is onto the positive charges: an arrangement with no positive charge
       does not relocate, at any magnitude up to one." To be given up if "any
       arrangement with no positive charge fires a leg of the criterion at
       CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD with the stability analysis".
   H9  that "a RELIABLE arrangement whose charges sum to zero or more keeps it bound",
       the dianion. D42 did not falsify it on 8 conditions inside r_safe. The write-up
       says what would still do so: "a RELIABLE arrangement whose charges sum to zero or
       more that is METASTABLE at either state, at another site, outside r_safe, or
       among designs an optimiser places".

The record leaves one decision open between two routes: (a) bare charges moved out to
3.50 A or more, or (b) the present distances with a charge behind a core potential, the
wall, which is NOT ADOPTED and NOT CALIBRATED. On 17 November 2026 Jessica decided the
order: route (a) is tried first, and of the wall she wrote "i agree the wall as a fall
back". D43 is the first step of route (a). It does not take route (a): that needs the
grid rebuilt, and D43 uses the committed grid as it stands.

It can do so because the committed grid already reaches past 3.50 A. Computed by
cs6_d43_prepare.py from sites_f42436.tsv and the two committed geometries: 616 of the
2626 sites lie at or beyond 3.50 A from every substrate atom at both states. The
farthest lies at 4.023 A and 3 lie beyond 4.00 A. 371 of the 616 admit both +1.00 e and
-1.00 e. So "at or beyond r_safe" means, on this grid, a shell between 3.50 and about
4.0 A.

2. THE CONVENTIONS, ALL TAKEN FROM THE RECORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OUTER SITE. A site of sites_f42436.tsv whose distance to every one of the 24 substrate
atoms, hydrogens included, is at least 3.50 A at the reactant and at the transition
state, with the geometries of d33a/sp_42436_R and d33a/sp_42436_TS.

THE FOUR LEGS. The committed classifier phase2.2/cs6_audit.py at its committed
thresholds, D_LW 0.25 e, R_DIP 2.0, GAP_ABS 1.0 eV and R_GAP 0.5, read at read time and
not transcribed, on each dianion run against d31's charge-free control at the same
state, with the rules D33.3 and AMENDMENT 5 settled:
   - stability is judged on the LAST analysis of the run. A first solution that was
     unstable and then corrected is recorded and is not a flag.
   - the gap is withheld in a run that engaged TRAH or a level shift, by the markers of
     cs6_d33b.py.
   - the dipole leg is applied: there is no ECP centre in any run here.
A state is FLAGGED if the Loewdin rise reaches D_LW, or the dipole ratio reaches R_DIP,
or the gap is below GAP_ABS and below R_GAP of the control's, or the last stability
analysis is UNSTABLE, or the SCF did not converge (D31.3: "NOT CONVERGING IS A RESULT,
NOT A BROKEN JOB.").

   RELIABLE       no flag at either state, and at both the gap was readable and the
                  stability analysis was read
   UNRELIABLE     a flag at either state; the result names each
   INCONCLUSIVE   no flag, and a gap withheld or a stability analysis not read at one
                  state or both
   NOT CLASSIFIED no flag, and a state NOT MEASURED

THE FIFTH LEG, by AMENDMENT 6. VDE = E(monoanion) - E(dianion) at the same state with
the same point charges, both energies from D43's own runs, each the last FINAL SINGLE
POINT ENERGY of its output, at 27.211386245988 eV per Eh.

   BOUND        VDE above zero, and the monoanion's last stability analysis STABLE
   METASTABLE   VDE at or below zero
   "ABOVE ZERO, MONOANION NOT STABLE" is reported with its number and is not BOUND.

A condition PASSES THE FIFTH LEG if it is BOUND at both states.

ddE = (E(TS) - E(TS, control)) - (E(R) - E(R, control)) on the dianion, with d31/ctl_R
and d31/ctl_TS as the control, at 627.5094740631 kcal/mol per Eh. Negative lowers the
barrier.

THE FIRST-ORDER VALUE of a condition is the sum over its charges of a_i q_i, with a_i the
row of A_f42436_d33a.tsv for the site: the matrix D33 stage A measured at this level.
The committed score adds L_i, the classical Lennard-Jones term of a sphere at the site.
The charges of D43 are bare point charges and carry no such term in the calculation; the
score is listed because the record's ratios were formed with it.
