STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 42
Is the dianion bound with the charges of Diagnostic 31's RELIABLE conditions? The first
test of the hypothesis that binding is lost because the net charge is negative.

Pre-registered 15 November 2026, before any D42 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.
No committed file used the number 42 when this was written.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D40 measured the detachment energy with charges present, at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD and
at both states, for the five arrangements whose lowering the record held quotable. The
bare dianion is BOUND, +1.2486 eV at the reactant and +0.7681 eV at the transition state.
With the charges of each of the five it is METASTABLE at both states, and Probe 2's
sentence, "Any ddE measured at such a condition is not a barrier effect and must not be
reported as one.", reaches all five numbers. D40 "withdraws nothing and changes no
verdict".

D40 does not say why. The record holds two things that point at the sign of the charge.
Probe 2, at B3LYP-D3BJ/ma-def2-SVP and the reactant, found that one charge of +1.0 e
raises the detachment energy to +3.4366 eV and one of -1.0 e at the same position takes
it to -2.1005 eV. And the charges of all five of D40's arrangements sum to less than
zero: -0.6500, -0.7625, -1.0000, -0.5000 and -0.2500 e, as their committed charge files
hold them.

The consolidated write-up, WRITEUP_CONSOLIDATED_20261111.md in its revision of the
evening of 15 November 2026, states this as hypothesis H9 in its section 11: "a RELIABLE
arrangement whose charges sum to zero or more keeps it bound", to be given up if "a
RELIABLE arrangement whose charges sum to zero or more is METASTABLE at
CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD at either state." The write-up says of H9 that it is post hoc as
a reading of D40. D42 is its first test on data that did not exist when it was written.

It matters because of what the record now holds. A design carrying a positive unit
charge is UNRELIABLE at every n tested (D31), and in D34 every arrangement that carries
a positive charge is UNRELIABLE. The five arrangements that passed the four legs and had
a quotable lowering all carry a net negative charge, and D40 found them METASTABLE. No
arrangement measured at the corrected level has passed both tests. D31's arm B holds 13
conditions that passed the first, in partial charges, and no detachment energy has been
computed for any of them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE CONVENTION, TAKEN FROM THE RECORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D40's, unchanged, which is Probe 2's. VDE = E(monoanion) - E(dianion) at the same
geometry with the same external charges. The monoanion is charge -1, multiplicity 2. The
conversion is 27.211386245988 eV per Eh. Each energy is the last FINAL SINGLE POINT
ENERGY of its output. The dianion half of every difference is the committed output of
the D31 run the monoanion input was copied from; it is not run again.

   BOUND at this condition       VDE above zero
   METASTABLE at this condition  VDE at or below zero

No third word is introduced. One qualification is fixed here because it can only work
against a BOUND reading: a monoanion solution that is not the lowest one makes the
detachment energy too HIGH. So the word BOUND is given at a state only if the monoanion's
last stability analysis is STABLE. If the value is above zero and the last analysis is
not STABLE, the state is reported as "ABOVE ZERO, MONOANION NOT STABLE" with its number,
and is not counted as BOUND anywhere below. A value at or below zero is METASTABLE
whatever the analysis says, because a lower monoanion solution would only lower it.

4. READINGS, FIXED IN ADVANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
R42.1  The control's energy beside D40's -836.26897688 Eh, and the detachment energy it
       gives against the committed dianion d31/ctl_R beside D40's +1.2486 eV.
R42.2  THE TABLE. For each of the 13 conditions at each state: n, q, the sum of its
       charges, its class, the detachment energy, the word of section 2, <S**2> of the
       monoanion, whether the stability analysis found the first solution stable and
       whether the last is, whether a level shift or TRAH was engaged, and the number of
       point charges ORCA says it read.
R42.3  THE TEST OF H9, on the 8 conditions of class H.
       A condition KEEPS THE DIANION BOUND if it is BOUND at both states.
       H9 IS FALSIFIED if any condition of class H is METASTABLE at either state. The
       result names every such condition and state.
       H9 IS NOT FALSIFIED BY THESE 8 CONDITIONS if all 8 keep the dianion bound.
       THE TEST IS INCOMPLETE if no condition of class H is METASTABLE at either state
       and at least one of the 16 runs is NOT MEASURED or is "ABOVE ZERO, MONOANION NOT
       STABLE". The result names them. A falsification by any measured state stands
       whether or not the test is complete.
       n20_q0.25 carries 5.00 e in all, and D31's own first limitation says that every
       condition above about 3 e inherits D30's doubt about def2-SVPD. The count of
       class H is therefore given twice, with n20_q0.25 and without it.
R42.4  THE 5 CONDITIONS OF CLASS N. The word at each state, in the same table. Reported.
       H9 neither predicts nor forbids either word for them.
R42.5  For each condition and state, the detachment energy minus the bare substrate's at
       the same state, D40's +1.2486 eV and +0.7681 eV, set beside the sum of the
       charges. Reported, not interpreted. Nothing is fitted.
R42.6  THE BARRIER CHANGE BESIDE THE TWO TESTS. D31's result reports verdicts and no
       ddE. For each condition, ddE is computed from D31's committed dianion outputs:
       (E(TS) - E(TS, control)) - (E(R) - E(R, control)), with d31/ctl_R and d31/ctl_TS
       as the control, at 627.5094740631 kcal/mol per Eh. Then, by the rule of D40's
       R40.7: if either state of a condition is METASTABLE, Probe 2's sentence applies to
       that condition's ddE and the result says so; if both states are BOUND, the result
       says that this objection does not reach that ddE at this level and basis. The
       conditions that are RELIABLE on four legs in D31 and BOUND at both states here are
       listed together with their ddE. If there are none, the result says so in those
       words. Section 6 says what such a list does and does not license.

6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D42.1  IT WITHDRAWS NOTHING AND CHANGES NO VERDICT. D31's verdicts are reliability
       verdicts and stay as committed. What becomes of the five numbers D40 reaches is
       still a dated erratum and a decision for Jessica, whatever D42 finds.
D42.2  It does not make binding a leg of the reliability criterion, and it does not take
       the decision on a neutrality constraint for the optimiser. Each is an amendment.
D42.3  NOT FALSIFIED IS NOT ESTABLISHED. Class H is 8 conditions on one site pool. Its
       three conditions with n = 1 are one positive charge at one site at three
       magnitudes; its three with n = 2 add one negative charge at one further site. The
       sign of the sum is confounded with n and with site throughout, exactly as the
       write-up says of D40's five. D42 can falsify H9. It cannot turn it into a rule,
       and D29 found that no function of the charges orders relocation.
D42.4  A detachment energy computed with unrestricted Kohn-Sham is an energy difference
       between two self-consistent solutions. Spin contamination is reported and not
       corrected. Where a detachment energy is negative its sign is the reading and its
       size is not (D40.4).
D42.5  One basis, def2-SVPD, and n20_q0.25 lies above the total at which D30 found that
       basis converged.
D42.6  Vertical, not adiabatic. One frame. In vacuo.
D42.7  EVERY ONE OF THE 13 CONDITIONS SITS INSIDE r_safe. Computed from the committed
       pc.pc and substrate.xyz while writing this: in every condition, at both states,
       the nearest charge lies 3.083 to 3.211 A from a substrate atom, and r_safe is
       3.50 A. D11: "No design measured inside r_safe is interpretable." D42 says nothing
       about that, and a condition that passes both tests here is still inside it.
D42.8  A ddE LISTED UNDER R42.6 IS NOT MADE QUOTABLE BY D42. D31's own limitations stand:
       "No barrier reported from this diagnostic is a prediction of a physical barrier",
       and arm B "IS NOT THE OPTIMISER'S BEST". Its sites come from the ranking that stood
       when D31 was pre-registered, on 6 November 2026, which is before D33 stage A
       measured the corrected matrix.
       Whether any number of R42.6 may be quoted, and as what, is a decision for Jessica
       and a dated document of its own.
D42.9  It adopts no level of theory and says nothing about D39.
D42.10 NOTHING HERE IS SHAPED. H9 was written after D40, by the author of this document,
       and a result that falsifies it is reported as plainly as one that does not, in
       the same table, by rules fixed here before any output exists. Nothing in the
       reading is turned toward a lower barrier: the one asymmetry, in section 2, can
       only take a BOUND away.
