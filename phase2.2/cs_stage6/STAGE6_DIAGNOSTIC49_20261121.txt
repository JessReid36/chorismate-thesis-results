STAGE 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 49 - PRE-REGISTRATION
The calibration of DIAGNOSTIC 48, run again so that ORCA ends normally
================================================================================
Written 21 November 2026, after D48's result (results commit 31966d77) and before any run of D49.
On the calibration of D48, Claude offered: "Rerun the 20 with the dipole step switched off, in a new
folder under its own short pre-registration." Jessica, 21 November 2026: "yes lets do a clean
version". No committed file used the number 49 for a diagnostic when this was written.

It adopts no rule, opens or closes no size, no distance and no design, and edits nothing of D48.
D48's own reading of its calibration stays what its result says: "Calibration measured: 0 of 20."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D48's part C was to measure what the functions at a charge can bind: one electron, the def2-TZVPD
functions of a hydrogen atom with no nucleus (a ghost atom), and a bare point charge q 0.01 A from
their centre, at ten sizes and two levels. All twenty runs converged their SCF and wrote a final
energy. ORCA then stopped in its property step: the dipole moment it prints by default has a
nuclear part, there is no nucleus, and the outputs end "ORCA finished by error termination in
PROPERTIES". D48's committed reader counts a run that does not end normally NOT MEASURED, and D48's
pre-registration had fixed what follows: "NOT MEASURED at a size: S' is not formed for the
arrangements that hold that size, and the outcomes above are read on S alone."

Submitting the same job again would run the same inputs to the same end. D48's inputs are committed
and are not edited. D49 writes new inputs from them, in a new folder, and runs those.

What the calibration is for. D48 defined S' as S with the last term of S, 13.605693122994 x q^2,
replaced by what these functions bind, and said of it: "S' PREDICTS NOTHING. It is set beside S".
D48's result leaves three things for it:
   (a) S did not separate what D48's runs did. Runs took electrons at S up to +0.60 eV and did not
       at S down to +0.37 eV. Whether the functions account for any of that is what S' shows.
   (b) Site 1166 carries the smallest charges of part A (0.40 to 0.60 e), where these functions
       are expected to bind least well.
   (c) D48, section 6, on the design B_n11_q0.25: "part C says how much that test is worth."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D48's twenty systems: HF and CAM, each at ten sizes
(0.25, 0.40, 0.45, 0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75 and 1.00 e). Each is run in two
variants: 40 runs, one job (K1, PBS name cm_d49_k1), on one core each, each stopped if it has not
ended after 900 seconds. The job script is D48's committed run_E1.pbs with its names and its runs
changed and nothing else. The inputs are written by cs6_d49_prepare.py from D48's committed inputs.

VARIANT N, the D48 input with the dipole moment switched off. Three lines are added to the committed
input of D48 and nothing else changes: same electron, same functions, same charge, same place. For
CAM at 0.55 e the input is

      ! UKS CAM-B3LYP def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
      %maxcore 3000
      %scf
        MaxIter 300
        Guess HCore
      end
      %elprop
        Dipole false
      end
      %pointcharges "pc.pc"
      * xyz -1 2
      H: 0.010000 0.000000 0.000000
      *

with D48's point-charge file beside it.

VARIANT Z, a nucleus in place of the point charge. No point charge and no ghost atom: one hydrogen
atom whose nuclear charge is set to q on its coordinate line, neutral by ORCA's count, so one
electron. The functions are the same nine. The charge sits AT their centre and not 0.01 A from it,
and ORCA has a nucleus for its property step. For HF at 0.25 e the input is

      ! UHF def2-TZVPD TightSCF
      %maxcore 3000
      %scf
        MaxIter 300
        Guess HCore
      end
      * xyz 0 2
      H 0.000000 0.000000 0.000000 Z = 0.250000
      *

Neither variant has run in ORCA in this record, and neither could be tried where this was written.
ORCA's manual describes both keywords (the Dipole keyword of the elprop block; "Z = ..." after an
atom's coordinates, with values that need not be whole numbers). That is a pointer and not part of
this record; whether the runs end normally is what the job shows. Two variants are run so that one
failing does not leave the calibration unmeasured again.

HOW FAR APART THE TWO SHOULD BE, AN ESTIMATE. Moving the charge 0.01 A off the centre of a
hydrogen-like 1s electron raises the energy by about (2/3) q^4 d^2 hartree with d in bohr: 0.0065 eV
at 1.00 e, 0.0016 eV at 0.70 e, 0.0004 eV at 0.50 e. So Z should lie below N by no more than that.

HOW LONG, AN ESTIMATE. D48's twenty runs took under four minutes together on hpc1 (its committed
log). 40 runs: under ten minutes of one node.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE READINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
27.211386245988 eV per hartree, 13.605693122994 eV per Rydberg, 0.5291772083 A per bohr, as D48.

A RUN IS MEASURED if the committed reader of outputs, cs6_d40.py, reads it as D48's reader read
D48's calibration: it ends normally, its SCF converged, it reports one electron and one final
energy, and (variant N) ORCA read one point charge or (variant Z) none. Otherwise it is NOT
MEASURED, or DID NOT CONVERGE.

B(q), at HF and at CAM, is minus the energy of the one electron, in eV. At each size and level it
is taken from variant N where that run is measured; otherwise from variant Z where that is
measured; otherwise it is NOT MEASURED. The result says at each size which variant it is from.

S' of each of D48's 44 runs with a charge is S with 13.605693122994 x q_i^2 replaced by B(CAM) at
q_i, the smallest over the positive charges, as D48 defines it. S is formed again from the
committed files by D48's formula and must be the S that d48.tsv holds (gate G49.7). What each run
did, and how much electron charge left the 24 atoms, are D48's (d48.tsv) and are not read again.
S' is formed only where B(CAM) is measured at each positive size the arrangement holds.

R49.1  Each of the 40 runs: its status and its energy.
R49.2  THE CALIBRATION. At each size: 13.6057 q^2, B(HF), B(CAM), the variant each is from, exact
       minus HF (what the functions lose), CAM minus HF (what the functional adds), exact minus CAM
       (what S' adds to S). Each size and level at which B is at or below zero, named.
       Beside it, for comparison: the energies of the two variants and the final energy that D48's
       own output holds, with Z minus N and N minus D48's. D48's outputs did not end normally;
       their energies are set beside and are not measured values of D48.
R49.3  S' beside S for each of D48's 44 runs, with what the run did and the charge that left.
R49.4  WHETHER S' SEPARATES what D48's runs did, as D48 defines it (the largest value at which a run
       TOOK ELECTRONS below the smallest at which a run DID NOT TAKE), with the two numbers and, if
       it does not, the runs between them; and the same for S over the same runs. Runs that D48
       marks A LEG ONLY are set aside, as in D48; its result has none.
R49.5  HOW WELL S AND S' ORDER THE CHARGE THAT LEFT. Spearman's rank correlation between the
       estimate and the electron charge that left the 24 atoms, for S and for S', over the runs of
       part A (one positive charge) and over all the runs. -1 is a perfect order. No threshold
       enters it.
R49.6  WHAT THE TEST OF B_n11_q0.25 WITH FUNCTIONS WAS WORTH, from B(CAM) at 0.25 e:
          NOT A TEST OF CAPTURE   B at or below zero: the functions hold no electron at 0.25 e
          A WEAK TEST             B above zero and below half of 13.6057 x 0.25^2
          A TEST AS IT STANDS     B at half of it or more
          NOT KNOWN               B not measured

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
G49.1  Every file of d49 is the one listed in d49/INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 (63 files) and is the
       file cs6_d49_prepare.py forms again from the committed files of D48. No run folder holds a
       geometry file, and no folder of variant Z a point-charge file.
G49.2  The files of d49/SOURCES.sha256 (68) and of d48/SOURCES.sha256 are the ones listed.
G49.3  No file under d31, d33a, d37, d40, d43, d44, d47 or d48, and no table or document of D48,
       differs from the committed one. The pre-registration given to the reader is the committed
       one.
G49.4  The sentences this document quotes from D48 are in D48's documents.
G49.5  Every output that exists prints, as the input it ran, the committed input beside it.
G49.6  Every measured run reports nine basis functions.
G49.7  S of each of D48's 44 runs, formed again here, is the S that d48.tsv holds (to 0.00006 eV).
A run that is not measured is not a gate.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D49 adopts nothing. D48's result, AMENDMENT 9 and A9.4 stand whatever it shows.
   BOTH VARIANTS NOT MEASURED at a size: B is not measured there and S' is not formed for the
      arrangements that hold it. If that is every size, D49 says only that. The final energies in
      D48's outputs may then be read in a dated note, labelled as read after the fact from outputs
      that did not end normally.
   B TAKEN FROM VARIANT Z at a size: the result says so. Where both variants are measured, Z minus N
      says how much that matters; more than 0.02 eV at any size and the two are not treated as one
      calibration: S' from Z is then labelled as the charge at the centre.
   N DIFFERS FROM THE FINAL ENERGY IN D48'S OUTPUT by more than 0.000001 hartree at a size: the
      result says so. The inputs differ by the dipole switch alone, and no difference is expected.
   S' SEPARATES what D48's runs did, where S did not: the functions and the functional account for
      what S left unordered. It is then S', and not S, that describes what a run with these
      functions shows. A threshold on either is proposed only in a pre-registration of its own,
      which says which of the two it tests and tests it on conditions not yet run.
   S' DOES NOT SEPARATE them: what S left unordered is not the functions alone. The runs between
      the two numbers are listed, and R49.5 says whether S' orders the runs better than S or not.
   THE PAIR +0.60/-0.60 e AT THE TRANSITION STATE, the one prediction of S that failed: its S' is
      reported. D48 found that the detachment energy does not account for it. If its S' lies at or
      above the smallest S' at which a run did not take, the calibration does not account for it
      either, and the cause stays open: S is not put forward for arrangements, as D48 says.
   B_n11_q0.25: NOT A TEST OF CAPTURE or A WEAK TEST: its run with functions showed nothing, or
      little, beyond A9.4. The design stands where A9.4 leaves it, no better and no worse.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D49.1  S' is formed after D48's result was known. It predicts nothing and D49 tests nothing. A
       separation found now is a description of 44 runs, not a rule.
D49.2  B is what these nine functions bind. It says what D48's runs with functions could show. It
       does not say what a complete basis would bind, which is 13.6057 q^2 and is what S uses.
D49.3  Variant Z has the charge at the centre of the functions and not 0.01 A from it.
D49.4  One electron and a bare charge: no substrate, no other charge, nothing relaxed.
D49.5  The final energies in D48's outputs are set beside for comparison. D48's reading of its
       calibration stays NOT MEASURED.
D49.6  Neither input has run in ORCA before in this record. If neither ends normally, D49 measures
       nothing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WHAT WAS KNOWN WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN, AND WHAT CLAUDE EXPECTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D48's result was known in full. Its table shows, read after the fact, that the electron charge
that left the atoms rises as S falls, and that three of the four sites of part A lie near one
curve while site 1166 lies apart near the threshold. The rehearsal of this reader formed R49.5 for
S on that table, so those two numbers are known: -0.94 over part A and -0.93 over all the runs.

Three of D48's twenty final energies were seen, in a printout Jessica made on hpc1 on 21 November
2026 at 17:42 to find why the job had failed: CAM at 0.25 e, +0.0219876991 hartree; HF at 0.25 e,
+0.0224665888; HF at 1.00 e, -0.4997055052. They are not measured values of D48. So B at 0.25 e is
expected at or below zero, and B at 1.00 e within 0.01 eV of exact. The other seventeen have not
been read by Claude: the rehearsal of this reader was made on invented energies, and the column of
D48's energies was not looked at.

Claude expects, and states it so that it can be held against the result:
   Variant Z ends normally. Variant N: likely, not sure.
   B below 13.6057 q^2 at every size, by a few tenths of an eV at 0.40 to 0.50 e and by little from
      0.60 e up. S' is then above S everywhere, most at the small sizes.
   S' orders the runs of part A better than S (R49.5), site 1166 falling in with the others.
   The largest S' at which a run took electrons lies above +0.5 eV: the runs take a quarter of an
      electron while the estimate is still well above zero.
   The pair at the transition state is not accounted for by the calibration.
   B_n11_q0.25: NOT A TEST OF CAPTURE.
