N2.1  WHAT F5 SAYS

D44's pre-registration, section 6: "F5  THE RESTRICTED SOLUTION OF THE BARE SUBSTRATE IS
UNSTABLE AT A DOUBLE HYBRID, that is, C_ctlS finds a lower solution. Then the charge-free
barriers of D37 and of D37 Amendment 1 at that level, and the comparisons of D36 and D41 that
rest on them, were made on a solution the analysis does not find to be the lowest. D44 reports
it and reads the level all the same, by section 2, which was fixed before the result was
known. What it means for those comparisons is not decided here."

The pre-registration expected it. Section 8: "EXPECTED, NOT KNOWN: that with more exact
exchange the restricted solution at the transition state may be UNSTABLE at the double
hybrids, for the designs and perhaps for the bare substrate, in the way that breaks the
pairing of spins and moves no charge."

A RESTRICTED SOLUTION is one in which every orbital holds a pair of electrons. THE STABILITY
ANALYSIS asks whether the self-consistent step could reach a lower energy by giving that
pairing up. At a double hybrid the energy has two parts: the self-consistent step, and a
perturbative term added on top of it. The analysis looks at the first part only.

So, measured, at revDSD-PBEP86-D4 in both basis sets:

   1. The restricted solution is unstable in the self-consistent step: slightly at the
      reactant, strongly at the transition state.
   2. The solution the search goes to keeps the charge in place: no flag on the Loewdin rise
      or on the dipole. It has given up the pairing of spins: <S**2> is 0.02 and 0.01 at the
      reactant and 0.90 at the transition state, where a paired solution has 0.
   3. ITS FINAL ENERGY IS HIGHER THAN THE RESTRICTED ONE at all four, not lower: every differ
      above is positive. With the perturbative term added, the restricted solution is the
      lower of the two solutions found, at both states and in both basis sets.

N2.4  WHAT IS NOT KNOWN

   1. Whether the restricted solution is unstable at PWPB95-D3BJ. ORCA did not run the
      analysis there (D44, F4).
   2. Whether the Hartree-Fock solution under the coupled-cluster benchmark of D36 and D41 is
      stable. No document of the record has asked. What the record holds is the T1 diagnostic
      of those runs: D41's result prints 0.016726026 (reactant) and 0.019922376 (transition
      state) at def2-SVPD, and 0.015649748 and 0.018809319 at def2-TZVPD.
   3. Whether a third solution lies lower than both of the two found. The search goes where
      the analysis points and reports where it ends; it does not show that nothing else
      exists.
   4. What the unpaired solution means physically at the transition state. D44 did not ask
      and this note does not say.
