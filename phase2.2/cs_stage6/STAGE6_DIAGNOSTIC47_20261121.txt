STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 47
A preliminary: when does a bare positive charge take electrons from the substrate - its size, its
place, its distance, and the reach of the basis set?

Pre-registered 21 November 2026, before any D47 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.
No committed file used the number 47 when this was written. A measurement with this number was built
on 20 November 2026 (bundle cs_d47_20261120) and WITHDRAWN on 21 November before anything of it was
committed; section 1 says why. This document replaces it and takes nothing from it on trust.
It is read under AMENDMENT 5 (stability) and AMENDMENT 9. It rebuilds no grid, runs no search, and
opens or closes no size and no distance. It decides what the next measurement should be.

1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D44 ran the six survivors of D43 in the larger basis set: "Of the 6 designs. Pass the cross-check:
0. Do not pass: 4. Incomplete: 2." At CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD four are UNRELIABLE and two are
RELIABLE (d44.tsv). AMENDMENT 9 puts next: "The safe surface is measured, the grid is rebuilt and a
search is run on it, each under its own pre-registration."

A measurement of that surface was built on 20 November: 382 runs, most of them single charges of
0.25 e walked in by chemical group. On 21 November Jessica asked for it to be assessed before it
ran: "okay critically assess the whole approach. think how we might address design to adress the
issues before we run such a long job." And: "i do think do the walk out from almost each atom might
be necessary becuase its neighboring atoms might impact its personal distance. but perhaps we do
preliminary tests first". After the assessment: "ok go build". This is that preliminary.

WHAT THE ASSESSMENT RESTS ON, AND WHAT KIND OF EVIDENCE IT IS. On 21 November, after D44's official
result, Claude read the Loewdin charges in D44's COMMITTED outputs at CAMTZ against the committed
control. It is a reading after the fact, made with no reader committed beforehand. R47.8 makes the
same reading again under this pre-registration. What it showed, to three decimals:

   design        most negative change of a Loewdin charge, e   four legs at CAMTZ (d44.tsv)
                 at R          at TS
   B_n02_q0.50   O3  -0.032    O3  -0.052    RELIABLE
   B_n02_q0.75   O3  -0.888    O3  -0.163    UNRELIABLE
   B_n02_q1.00   O3  -1.726    O3  -1.642    UNRELIABLE
   B_n03_q0.75   O3  -1.704    O3  -1.643    UNRELIABLE
   B_n05_q0.50   O3  -1.712    O3  -1.670    UNRELIABLE
   B_n11_q0.25   O3  -0.044    O3  -0.079    RELIABLE

   In all twelve runs the atom is O3, the ether oxygen, which is the substrate atom nearest the
   positive charge at site 1124. In the four designs D44 finds UNRELIABLE its charge falls by
   0.89 to 1.73 e at R and by 0.16 to 1.67 e at TS; in the two it finds RELIABLE, by 0.03 to 0.08
   e. Every one of the six designs holds a positive charge at site 1124. So D44's four failures
   are, on this reading, one event at one site, and D44 does not say what a positive charge does
   anywhere else.

AN IDEA THAT WOULD EXPLAIN IT, NOT YET TESTED. A bare positive point charge q binds one electron by
13.6 q^2 eV if the basis set can describe an electron sitting at the charge. The dianion holds its
least bound electron by about 1 eV (the committed bare detachment energies are +1.2486 eV at R and
+0.7681 eV at TS at the working level). If that is what happens, then:
   - the SIZE of a positive charge, and the pull of other positive charges near it, decide whether
     electrons leave the substrate;
   - DISTANCE helps mainly because an atom-centred basis set cannot put an electron far from the
     atoms, so a distance found safe in one basis set is safe in that basis set;
   - a single positive charge may hold where the same charge among others does not.
   A crude form of the estimate S of section 2, with the monoanion's potential taken from Loewdin
   charges, put the four flagged designs between -2.2 and -9.6 eV and the two others between -0.1
   and +0.5 eV. That was computed after the fact on six designs that share one site. It is the
   reason for this preliminary and is evidence of nothing.

If the idea is right, walking a charge out from every atom would map the reach of def2-TZVPD at
great cost. If it is wrong, that walk is the measurement to make, as Jessica says. So before either:

   PART A   Is D44's failure the doing of ONE positive charge, and from what size?
   PART B   Is site 1124 a special case?
   PART C   Does distance rescue a charge that is flagged where it stands?
   PART D1  If the basis set is given functions AT the charge, does a charge that distance had
            rescued take electrons after all? And does S say beforehand which charges do?
   PART D2  Do the two designs that pass in def2-TZVPD still pass one basis step up?
   PART E   The potentials of the dianion and the monoanion at every committed site, in both basis
            sets, from which S and the first-order coefficients are formed.

EACH JOB BUT Q1 RUNS A PLAIN CONTROL FIRST, a committed charge-free dianion input of CAMSV or CAMTZ

THE LEGS, on each run with a charge. The committed classifier phase2.2/cs6_audit.py at its committed
thresholds, D_LW 0.25 e, R_DIP 2.0, GAP_ABS 1.0 eV and R_GAP 0.5, read at read time, against the

withheld in a run that engaged TRAH or a level shift, or whose control did. A run whose SCF did not
converge is flagged (D31.3).

   FLAGGED        the Loewdin rise reaches D_LW, or the dipole ratio reaches R_DIP, or the gap is
                  below GAP_ABS and below R_GAP of the control's, or the last stability analysis is
                  UNSTABLE, or the SCF did not converge
   NOT FLAGGED    none of those, the gap readable and the stability analysis read
   HELD           none of those, and the gap withheld or an analysis not read
   NOT READ       the run's job or its control cannot be used
   NOT MEASURED   no readable output

   For a condition run at BOTH states at a level (part A at CAMSV and CAMTZ, part D2 at CAMQZ), the
   four-leg word of D43 and D44: UNRELIABLE if either run is FLAGGED; else NOT CLASSIFIED if either
   is NOT MEASURED or NOT READ; else INCONCLUSIVE if either is HELD; else RELIABLE.

WHERE THE CHARGE WENT, on each run with a charge: the Loewdin charge of each of the 24 substrate
atoms minus the control's; the atom with the most negative change, that change, and that atom's
distance to the nearest positive point charge; and the sum over the 24 atoms minus the control's.
Reported; it is not a leg and changes no word.

AT CAMTZG ONLY, ONE MORE READING, NEW IN THIS RECORD. The legs were written for electrons that stay
on the substrate's atoms. With functions at the charge an electron can leave the atoms altogether,
and the legs may or may not catch that: the sum of the 24 atoms' Loewdin charges then rises above
the control's by what has left. So, for a CAMTZG run with a charge:
   LEFT THE ATOMS   that sum is above its control's by D_LW (0.25 e) or more. The committed
                    threshold, used here on a quantity it was not written for.
   TOOK ELECTRONS   the SCF converged and either a leg fires or the run LEFT THE ATOMS
   DID NOT TAKE     the run is NOT FLAGGED and did not leave the atoms
   not decided      anything else (HELD without leaving the atoms, SCF not converged, NOT READ,
                    NOT MEASURED)
   This reading is used in R47.6 and section 6 only. It is not part of A9.4 and classifies no
   design.

ddE at a level = (E(TS) - E(TS, control)) - (E(R) - E(R, control)), at 627.5094740631 kcal/mol per
Eh, where both states are run. THE AGREEMENT of two levels is D44's: STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, "LOSS MUST
BE BELOW 15 % OF |ddE(frozen)|." WITHIN if the difference is below 15 % of the reference ddE in
size; OUTSIDE if it is 15 % or more; NOT ASSESSABLE if the reference ddE is below 3.2 kcal/mol in
size; NOT FORMED if either is not formed.

THE MAPS. orca_vpot, as D33a ran it, at the 2626 committed sites and the three points of part C, on
the charge-free dianion and monoanion of CAMSV and CAMTZ at R and TS. A map is used only if its run
gives back the committed energy within 1e-6 Eh (and, for the dianion, its last stability analysis
is STABLE) and its points are those of d47/points.xyz. V is in Eh per e. From the maps:

   a   = (V_dianion,TS - V_dianion,R) x 627.5094740631        the first-order coefficient, as D33a
   v   = (V_monoanion - V_dianion) x 27.211386245988          eV per e: the first-order change of the
                                                              detachment energy per unit charge there
   and, for a positive charge q_i at point i among charges q_j, at a level and state,
   S_i = VDE_bare + SUM_j q_j v_j - 27.211386245988 x [ V_monoanion(i) + SUM_(j not i) q_j / r_ij ]
         - 13.605693122994 x q_i^2                            eV, r_ij in bohr at 0.5291772083 A
   where VDE_bare is the committed detachment energy of the bare substrate at that level and state.
   The first two terms are what holds the electron on the dianion with the charges present, to
   first order. The bracket is the potential the monoanion and the other charges make at charge i.
   The last term is the energy with which a point charge q_i binds one electron in a complete basis.
   S of an arrangement is the smallest S_i over its positive charges. The size at which S of one
   positive charge alone is zero is the positive root of the same expression set to zero.

   S IS AN ESTIMATE AND DECIDES NOTHING HERE. It is one electron, nothing relaxed, first order, and
   it treats the charge as a nucleus. It is written down now so that it can be wrong: at each CAMTZG
   run, S formed from the CAMTZ maps PREDICTS
      DID NOT TAKE     if S is +0.5 eV or more at that state
      TOOK ELECTRONS   if S is -0.5 eV or less
      nothing          between them, or where a map is missing
   and the result sets each prediction beside what the run did.

6. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D47 opens and closes nothing. r_safe, AMENDMENT 9 and D44's result stand whatever it shows. A9.4
stays the test of every design: "A design is a SURVIVOR OF THE SEARCH, and nothing more, only if it
passes the five legs at the working level AND, at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD, is RELIABLE on four
legs, passes the fifth leg, and has a ddE that is WITHIN the tolerance of its ddE at the working
level."

PART A.
   A size is UNRELIABLE at CAMTZ and every smaller size is RELIABLE: at site 1124 one positive
      charge of that size is enough, and D44's failures at that size and above are not only the
      doing of charges together.
   Every size is RELIABLE at CAMTZ: one charge alone does not do it at site 1124; D44's failures
      need the other charges, and what is measured next must be arrangements.
   The pattern is neither (a smaller size UNRELIABLE and a larger one RELIABLE, or words that are
      not RELIABLE or UNRELIABLE): nothing is concluded from part A.
PART B, read at the size or sizes that part A finds FLAGGED at the reactant at CAMTZ.
   FLAGGED at all three other sites too: site 1124 is not a special case at that size.
   NOT FLAGGED at one or more: where the charge sits matters at that size, beyond its distance (all
      four sites lie between 3.5 and 4.0 A from their nearest atom). A measurement site by site, or
      atom by atom as Jessica proposes, is then the next step for that size.
PARTS C AND D1 TOGETHER, for +1.00 e.
   NOT FLAGGED at 9.00 A at CAMTZ and TOOK ELECTRONS there at CAMTZG: distance took the charge out
      of the reach of def2-TZVPD and did nothing else. A safe distance is then a statement about a
      basis set, and no walk out is made for a size that behaves so.
   NOT FLAGGED at 9.00 A at CAMTZ and DID NOT TAKE at CAMTZG: distance protects that charge even
      when the basis set can put an electron at it. S is then wrong there, and the walk out, atom
      by atom, is the next step.
   FLAGGED at 9.00 A at CAMTZ: distance does not rescue +1.00 e within 9 A even in def2-TZVPD.
   Anything else: not decided.
PART D1, THE PREDICTIONS.
   Every prediction made is borne out: S may be put forward, in a pre-registration of its own, as a
      rule to be tested on more sites and on arrangements. Nothing is built on it before that.
   One is not borne out: S is not put forward as a rule. The result says which.
PART D2.
   Both designs RELIABLE at CAMQZ with ddE WITHIN of the working level's: they hold one step up.
   Either UNRELIABLE, or OUTSIDE: passing in def2-TZVPD is not the end of the matter for that
      design. If that is so for both, no design of this record is shown to hold beyond def2-TZVPD,
      and the wall, which the record keeps as the fall-back, comes to Jessica for decision.
   Otherwise: not decided.
THE MAPS decide nothing. They are the material for whatever comes next.

7. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D47.1  It is a preliminary on one frame, with few conditions. It fixes no distance, no size and no
       rule. It names no design.
D47.2  Parts B and C, and part D1 but for +0.50 e, are run at the reactant only. NOT FLAGGED
       at the reactant says nothing of the transition state, where the bare substrate holds its
       electron less strongly.
