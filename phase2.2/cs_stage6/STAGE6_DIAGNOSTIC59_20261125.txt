WHERE THE FUNCTIONS STAND. Jessica, 25 November, asked whether the functions stand at the
positive charges only, as D50, D53 and D54 placed them and as A10.5 item 2 has it ("That a run
with functions at the positive charges should be part of it follows from A10.2."), or at every
charge: "1. positive charges only." So a ghost atom with the functions stands at each positive
charge of a design, and none at a negative charge. DIRECTION: functions at every charge would be
the stricter test, as they give an electron more places to go; this is the less strict of the
two.

THE FUNCTIONS, D53's test with its forms copied. For each design and state (R, TS), the dianion,
frozen, in vacuo:
   - F: the design's charges, as D56's committed pc.pc holds them, and the functions of D50 (set
     X2, treatment A) as a ghost hydrogen at each positive charge, D53's committed ghost block
     word for word.
   - N: the same charges and no functions (the partner). GF: the functions and no charge (the
     control with functions). GN: neither (the plain control, one for each level and state).
   - At CAM, CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-TZVPD, and WXV, wB97X-V/def2-TZVPD: F, N, GF and GN. At HF,
     Hartree-Fock/def2-TZVPD: F, GF and GN, the bracket. The heads of the inputs are D53's
     committed heads at each level, with hydrogen given IntAcc 12 at CAM and WXV.

3. THE READINGS, FIXED NOW

R59.1 THE FUNCTIONS, for each design and state, read by D53's committed functions
(cs6_d53.read_di, legs, took, control_holds and together, with cs6_d40, cs6_d33b and cs6_audit)
exactly as D53's R53.3 to R53.7 read its arrangements, with this one change: each design has its
own control with functions GF, because its functions stand at its own positive charges. GF is
read against GN by the three legs that do not divide the density among atoms (it HOLDS, DOES NOT
HOLD, or is not decided). F is read against its GF by D50's reading: "TOOK ELECTRONS the SCF
converged and either a leg fires or the run LEFT THE ATOMS"; LEFT THE ATOMS when the Loewdin
charges of the 24 atoms sum to D_LW, 0.25 e, or more above the control's. N is read against GN
by the four legs and reported. THE GAIN, in eV, is (F minus GF) minus (N minus GN), formed when
all four are measured and, at CAM and WXV, the electrons each grid integrates lie within 0.02 e
of 118. A run that TOOK ELECTRONS is marked by its gain against L = 0.3127 eV at CAM ("RULE C
GIVES: L = 0.3127 eV.") and L2 = 0.1151 eV at WXV, as D53 fixed them, because "a run that takes
electrons with a gain in energy not larger than a few times that error is not settled at this
level alone."

The two levels together, by D53's R53.6 and in its order: NOT DECIDED (the second level is not
read; or at CAM or WXV the F run is not decided, its GF does not HOLD, a count does not hold, or
a needed gain is not formed); TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS; TAKEN AT THE WORKING FUNCTIONAL
ONLY; TAKEN AT THE SECOND LEVEL ONLY; HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS. The second level is read for a
design when WXV's GN and that design's WXV GF are measured and STABLE on their last analysis at
both states. Hartree-Fock's F against its GF is reported beside and changes no word.

FOR EACH DESIGN: TAKES ELECTRONS WITH FUNCTIONS AT ITS POSITIVE CHARGES if at R or at TS its
word is TAKES ELECTRONS AT BOTH LEVELS; HOLDS WITH FUNCTIONS AT ITS POSITIVE CHARGES if its word
is HOLDS AT BOTH LEVELS at both states; otherwise NOT SETTLED WITH FUNCTIONS, with its words
printed.

R59.2 THE HELIUM WALL, for each design: each run read by D56's committed functions against D56's
controls of the same level, its four legs, fifth leg, ddE and the agreement of the two levels
formed as D56 formed them, with the change D32 made before D56 and D57 read walls the same way:
"So the dipole ratio is RECORDED AND REPORTED FOR EVERY CONDITION AND GATES NOTHING." The word
of A9.4 (i) is printed as BEHIND THE HELIUM WALL, beside D56's committed word. ddE is also given
against the wall-alone control (ZHe), the effect of the charges with the walls in place. ORCA
prints each walled centre in the Loewdin block under the symbol Q; the committed cs6_audit
leaves those rows out of the largest Loewdin charge, and cs6_d53 compares the 24 atoms only.

R59.3 THE WALL ALONE. For each ZHe pair, the Loewdin rise over the plain control and the change
of the barrier the centres make with no charge on them. A wall-alone control whose Loewdin rise
reaches D_LW is reported as a wall that perturbs the density by itself.

5. WHAT EACH OUTCOME SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW

Nothing found here names, keeps or drops a design, and none is rescued; D56's words stand. "The
word SURVIVOR is read on bare charges, as D56's pre-registration fixed it." A design that TAKES
ELECTRONS WITH FUNCTIONS AT ITS POSITIVE CHARGES is reported as such, and whether it may then be
quoted is for the amendment A11.5 item 5 names. A walled word is beside the bare one and nothing
more. No run is made again in its folder.


6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

One frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, charges of 0.25 e that stand for no atom. Functions at
the positive charges only cannot show an electron going to a negative charge. The helium wall on
0.25 e is a model beside the bare one; whether it is nearer real ions is D57's question at +1 e
and one site, not this one's.
