Stage 6 - the eight additivity single points. Added 21 October 2026, after phase2.2/cs_stage6/relax/ was committed
(results 307e129, walltimes patched in ff8fdc5) and after its 14 frozen single points were submitted, and before any
relaxation. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is unchanged and needs no amendment: this file records that the first generation of
the inputs FAILED TO IMPLEMENT one of the measurements the criteria already lists, and what was added to implement it.

WHAT WAS MISSING

STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, WHAT IS MEASURED PER ARRANGEMENT, lists:

   "first-order additivity at fixed geometry: the sum of the one-sphere effects against the measured effect of S6-2
   and S6-3 (single points only, no relaxation)."

The inputs as first generated contain no one-sphere LJ-sphere run. S6-P is the only one-sphere arrangement and it is
a BARE point charge with all four Lennard-Jones fields zeroed, so it is not the one-sphere effect of a sphere and
cannot stand in for one. The quantity was therefore not computable from the committed inputs.

WHAT WAS ADDED, AND WHY IT IS NOT A NEW CHOICE

One LJ sphere at each distinct (site, charge) pair that S6-2 and S6-3 already fix, at R and at TS, single points
only, as the criteria says:

   folder                    site    charge      used by        why it is this charge
   ADD-s1690_qp10000         1690    +1.0000     S6-2, S6-3     both designs put this charge at this site
   ADD-s678_qm10000           678    -1.0000     S6-2           S6-2's second sphere
   ADD-s678_qm07625           678    -0.7625     S6-3           S6-3 re-optimised the charge at the same site
   ADD-s540_qm06500           540    -0.6500     S6-3           S6-3's third sphere

Four pairs, two geometries, eight single points of about five minutes each. Site 678 appears twice and the two are
NOT interchangeable: the electrostatic part of the score is linear in q by construction, a_i q_i, and whether the
measured effect is also linear in q is part of what this test checks, so each sphere is run at the charge its design
gives it rather than at a reference charge and scaled.

Nothing is chosen here. Every site and every charge is one the pre-registered placement rule already produced and
ARRANGEMENTS.txt already records. No arrangement is added, none is changed, and nothing about S6-2 or S6-3 moves.

WHAT WAS NOT TOUCHED

ARRANGEMENTS.txt is NOT regenerated or replaced, for the reason STAGE6_ERRATUM2.txt E6.6 and E6.7 give: re-running
cs_opt_milp.py returns the identical designs but can record a different strategy and certified gap, because its
fallback is decided by a 120 s wall-clock probe. On the generation that produced these eight folders, every one of
the 110 committed files outside the record was reproduced byte for byte, and S6-10's line again came back as
"strategy lp, certified gap 0.21594" against the committed "strategy full, certified gap 0.00000" - the same design,
the weaker claim. The committed record stands and these runs were copied in beside it; INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 gained
the new files' entries with every existing entry unchanged and re-verified against disk.

The 14 frozen single points already submitted are unaffected and were not resubmitted. They remain correct and
sufficient for ddE(frozen) and for G_add: ORCA's EnGrad on these QM/MM jobs writes the energy and the gradient of
exactly the 24 QM atoms, checked against WP4's committed armb_freecart/job.engrad, and the g(0) and E(0) those two
quantities need come from S6-C's pair.

DIRECTION

None. No decision, threshold, tolerance, criterion, arrangement or reported quantity changes. A measurement the
criteria already required becomes computable, and the omission is recorded rather than quietly repaired.
