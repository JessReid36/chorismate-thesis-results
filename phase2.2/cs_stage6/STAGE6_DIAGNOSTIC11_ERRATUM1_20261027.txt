The verdict stands: r_safe = 3.50 A. This corrects one control that was mis-specified, records a
margin that is too thin to lean on, and says what r_safe is and is not. DIAGNOSTIC11_RESULT.txt is
not edited. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited.


E11.1  CONTROL A3.4 WAS MIS-SPECIFIED, AND IT FAILED

As pre-registered: "A charge at 15 A must agree with its classical estimate to 0.1 kcal/mol." It
did not - measured -0.2860, classical -0.1848, diff -0.1012 - and the result reports FAIL.

The oracle is not what failed. The measured/classical ratio at 15 A is 1.548, which sits with the
plateau the same run gives at 3.50, 4.50 and 6.00 A (1.771, 1.792, 1.693). The quantity behaves
consistently from 3.5 A to 15 A.

The criterion was wrong. "Classical" is the designed charge acting on the control's FROZEN Loewdin
density, so it omits the substrate's polarisation by construction - and that polarisation does not
vanish with distance, it is a roughly constant ENHANCEMENT FACTOR of 1.7 to 1.9 at every separation
tested. Requiring measured to equal classical therefore tested "the classical reference is exact",
which it never is, and not "the oracle returns nothing when nothing is there", which is what A3.4
was for.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED. A3.4 is NOT re-scored as a pass. The restatement below was written after
seeing the number, and a control rewritten after the fact is not a control. It is recorded as a
control that failed as written, with its diagnosis, and the far-field question is carried forward
unanswered.

FOR THE NEXT DIAGNOSTIC THAT NEEDS ONE, pre-registered here before any such run exists: the
far-field check is that ddE(measured)/ddE(classical) at the far rung lies within 20 per cent of the
mean of that ratio over the two outermost converged rungs. It tests the oracle against its own
plateau, not against a reference that is known to be incomplete.


E11.2  C-A1.1 PASSED AT dref BY 0.018 kcal/mol, WHICH IS NOT A MARGIN

The wall-only control at dref gave +0.2817 kcal/mol against a 0.30 tolerance. It passed, and the
walled dref result is reported as valid. But 0.018 kcal/mol of margin is not a margin, and the
walled dref figure of -7.8221 carries roughly +0.28 of wall contribution inside it.

Any use of the dref walled number must state that. The clean rungs for the walled arm are d350 and
outwards, where the wall contributes +0.0407, -0.0013 and -0.0001.

At d250 the wall contributes +1.3105 and is NOT inert; that rung is voided in the result, correctly.
Below about 3.5 A neither a bare point charge nor this ECP centre is a clean instrument.


E11.3  WHAT r_safe IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

r_safe = 3.50 A BOUNDS THE VALIDITY OF A MODEL. IT IS NOT A STATEMENT ABOUT WHERE A CATALYTIC
CHARGE MAY SIT.

This needs saying because the result's verdict - "the grid must be rebuilt with this stand-off" -
can be read as the second, and real active sites do not obey it. Arg90's guanidinium carbon sits
3.19 A from the substrate at the reactant and 3.27 A at the transition state in this project's own
frame 42436 (charges_map/BUILD_RECORD.txt), INSIDE r_safe, and it is the catalytic residue
Burschowsky's crystallography identifies. A real guanidinium has electrons and Pauli repulsion; a
bare point charge has neither. What fails at 2.96 A is the representation, not the chemistry.

TWO ROUTES SATISFY THE UNDERLYING REQUIREMENT, AND ONLY ONE IS PRE-REGISTERED.

  (a) Keep bare point charges and move the admitted surface out to >= 3.50 A. This is the branch
      D11.3 specified and it is what the result says. Cost: the classical estimate falls from
      -4.23 kcal/mol at 2.96 A to -3.16 at 3.50 A, about 25 per cent less field per charge.

  (b) Keep the distances and change the charge model to one that has Pauli repulsion. D11's own
      data supports this: subtracting each rung's wall-only contribution gives a single consistent
      enhancement over the classical estimate at EVERY rung including 2.50 A (see E11.4). This
      route was NOT specified by D11.3 - the branch that contemplated changing the charge model was
      the one where bare and walled never converge, which is not what happened.

ROUTE (b) IS NOT ADOPTED HERE. Taking it requires its own dated amendment, and it requires the wall
to be calibrated first: STAGE6_ERRATUM7 records that these Ne ECP parameters are Marefat Khah's,
used outside the octet assumption they were published under, so the +1.31 kcal/mol the wall
contributes at 2.50 A is an uncalibrated model term and not a measured steric effect.

The choice between (a) and (b) is a scientific decision, not a correction, and it is recorded here
as open.


E11.4  POST HOC, NOT PRE-REGISTERED

Subtracting each rung's own wall-only contribution from its walled value:

   rung    d / A    wall-corrected    / classical
   d250     2.50        -10.512          1.877
   dref     2.96         -8.104          1.918
   d350     3.50         -6.034          1.911
   d450     4.50         -3.606          1.801
   d600     5.90         -1.967          1.693

A single enhancement factor of 1.7 to 1.9 over the frozen-density estimate at every separation. The
bare charge matches it from 3.50 A out and departs from it inside that, most severely at dref,
which is S6-P's committed position.

THE DIRECTION IS WORTH RECORDING. At dref the bare charge gives -3.858 and the wall-corrected value
is -8.104: the artefact SUPPRESSES the apparent catalytic effect by about 4.2 kcal/mol. The
wall-corrected value agrees to within 0.9 with Diagnostic 2's independent small-q extrapolation of
-7.20 for the same site. On this evidence Stage 6 has been UNDER-estimating what a charge at that
site does.

This is one charge at one site, the correction is post hoc, and the subtraction assumes the wall's
contribution is additive, which is not established. It is not a result and nothing is built on it.
