N1.5  READING 5 (NEW). THE SENSITIVITY TO THE CHARGE MODEL LIVES ENTIRELY IN THE DIFFUSE BASIS.

Spread of ddE across the four charge models (max minus min), per basis, per vector, in kcal/mol:

  vector            def2-SVP    def2-SVPD      ratio
  1.3000e_n10         0.0010       0.0383       38.3x
  2.8800e_n10         0.0013       0.0383       29.5x
  5.6433e_n20         0.0191       2.9002      151.8x

All six figures are far above the numerical floor of 1.4e-08 kcal/mol
(NUMERICAL_FLOOR_20261101.md), so all six are real.

def2-SVP does not care whether a design site is a mathematical point or a Gaussian of width 1
Angstrom. Over the whole in-domain range it answers the same to about one thousandth of a kcal/mol,
and even at 5.6433 e to two hundredths. That is the expected behaviour of a density that does not
reach the site: the two embeddings differ only where there is nothing to differ about.

def2-SVPD does care, by one and a half to two orders of magnitude more, and out of domain it swings
by 2.9 kcal/mol - larger than many of the barrier changes this project reports.

THE ARGUMENT THIS SUPPLIES. D5 established that the two bases DISAGREE and left open which is closer
to the truth; D5.4 records that without def2-TZVPD neither is shown to be converged, and that
limitation still stands. Reading 5 does not close it, but it adds something D5 could not see: of the
two bases, only one gives an answer that depends on how the embedding is drawn. A basis whose ddE
moves by 2.9 kcal/mol according to whether a point charge is blurred over 1 Angstrom is reporting a
property of the basis and not of the chemistry. That is an argument for def2-SVP as the production
basis which does not depend on knowing which basis is right, and it is independent of, and consistent
with, the spill-out reading of D24.6 Reading 2.

WHAT READING 5 DOES NOT SAY. It does not say def2-SVP is correct. A representation-insensitive
answer can still be a converged-to-the-wrong-thing answer, and a basis too small to respond to the
embedding is exactly a basis that might also be too small to respond to real polarisation. The
honest statement is narrower: def2-SVP's numbers are robust to the charge representation and
def2-SVPD's are not, and robustness is a necessary but not sufficient condition for trusting them.
Settling correctness still needs def2-TZVPD, or the walled arms of DIAGNOSTIC 23, or both.
