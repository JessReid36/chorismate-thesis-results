D10.0  WHY, AND THE PREDICTION THAT WAS MADE BEFORE ANY OF IT RAN

SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md, written 29 September 2026, measured that the bare chorismate dianion is
electronically unbound in vacuum at both def2-SVP and def2-SVPD, and bound by CPCM at eps = 4 and at
eps = 78.4. It then recommended retaining the continuum while the charge-placement machinery was
developed, and gave the reason:

   "Removing the continuum alone is well founded by GOCAT practice, but cannot be done in isolation:
    the bare dianion is unbound in vacuum, so designed charges would then be doing two jobs at once -
    binding the excess electron density and catalysing the reaction. The optimiser cannot distinguish
    them and would likely spend charges on the former."

Stage 6 ran in vacuo. On 22 October DIAGNOSTIC1_RESULT.txt returned NOT USABLE AS THEY STAND: moving
from def2-SVP to ma-def2-SVP moves ddE(frozen) by 49.7, 96.7 and 118.4 per cent for S6-P, S6-2 and
S6-20 against a 15 per cent yardstick, and S6-20's sign reverses, from -24.83 to +4.57 kcal/mol.

That is the predicted failure, measured. The prediction and the measurement were made independently,
four weeks apart. A third line agrees: STAGE6_ERRATUM7_20261026.txt E7.9 establishes from the held
literature that a compact basis conceals electron spill-out rather than preventing it, which is
exactly why def2-SVP looked well-behaved and ma-def2-SVP does not.

What none of the three establishes is whether the designs catalyse AT ALL once the density is bound,
or whether their apparent effect was always the charges holding the density in. That is the question
here, and it is the question Stage 7 cannot be written without.


D10.1  WHAT IS RUN, AND WHY IT IS ONLY SIXTEEN SINGLE POINTS

The four Diagnostic 1 arrangements, at both committed geometries, in two conditions:

   S6-C    the bare dianion, the reference every effect is measured against
   S6-P    one bare +1 at Arg90's position, the only arrangement D1 found still strongly catalytic
   S6-2    the smallest pair, +1 and -1
   S6-20   the largest design, whose sign reversed

   vac     B3LYP-D3BJ / ma-def2-SVP, no continuum
   cpcm    the same, plus CPCM eps = 4.0

NO GEOMETRY MOVES. These are single points on D1's committed R and TS. Nothing relaxes, nothing is
re-optimised, and no new geometry enters the record.

HOW CPCM IS AVAILABLE AT ALL. ORCA refuses to combine CPCM with its QMMM module, and D1's design
runs are QMMM jobs because the spheres carry Lennard-Jones parameters. Finding 4 is the way through
and it is exact: the Lennard-Jones term never enters the QM Hamiltonian, and at a fixed geometry the
difference between an LJ sphere and a bare point charge is exactly L. So at FIXED GEOMETRY a design
is re-expressible through %pointcharges, which CPCM does support - the qscan's C4 condition is
already ma-def2-SVP with CPCM and %pointcharges, 18 committed runs. The offset is sum L, which
ARRANGEMENTS.txt states per site: +0.56294 for S6-P, +0.61564 for S6-2 and -0.13622 for S6-20.

THAT OFFSET IS NOT ASSUMED. It is gate 2 below.

Every input is generated by cs10_cpcm.py from committed files only: the substrate geometry and the
sphere positions from each DIFF-* combined.xyz, the sphere charges from its site.ORCAFF.prms, and
both cross-checked against ARRANGEMENTS.txt, which is the authority for the charges and for L. The
generator refuses if a charge disagrees by more than 1e-6 e or a position by more than 1e-4 A.


D10.2  THE TWO GATES, WHICH RUN BEFORE ANY READING

GATE 1. S6-C carries no spheres, so its %pointcharges job IS the calculation Diagnostic 1 already
ran. Its two energies must reproduce D1's committed -836.227913439 and -836.199397816 Eh to within
1e-6 Eh. If they do not, the two are not the same calculation and nothing else is interpretable.

GATE 2. For each design, ddE measured here in vacuum must equal D1's committed ddE at ma-def2-SVP
MINUS sum L, to within 0.05 kcal/mol. This is Finding 4 used as a test rather than as an assumption:
it is what makes the re-expression of a QMMM design as point charges checkable rather than asserted.

Either gate failing stops the reading. The script writes the gate table and nothing else.


D10.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

The quantity is ddE(frozen) in the cpcm condition for S6-P, S6-2 and S6-20, each against the S6-C of
the same condition, compared with the COMMITTED def2-SVP values -7.6757, -12.3835 and -24.8261.
The yardstick is decision O6's existing 15 per cent, used as a yardstick and not as a criterion.

  RECOVERED if every sign matches the committed def2-SVP value and the largest move is within 15 per
  cent. The vacuum unboundedness was then the artefact Diagnostic 1 detected, the frozen numbers are
  usable WITH the continuum and not without it, and Stage 7 proceeds by restoring CPCM eps = 4 to the
  frozen half and saying so on its face.

  PARTIALLY RECOVERED if every sign matches but a move exceeds 15 per cent. The ordering survives and
  the magnitudes are continuum-dependent; they would be reported with that stated, and the budget and
  scoring work would have to be redone in the bound condition before any of it informs Stage 7.

  NOT RECOVERED if any sign still reverses. Binding the density does not restore the design numbers.
  The designs' apparent catalysis in vacuo was then substantially the charges holding the density in,
  the frozen half must be rebuilt rather than re-interpreted, and the optimiser's objective itself is
  implicated, because the scores it minimised were computed in the condition that fails.

A fourth outcome is possible and is not a verdict: if the HOMO at cpcm is not negative, the continuum
has not bound the species and the test has not been performed. That is reported as such.


D10.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

The geometries. D1's R and TS are stationary points of a different Hamiltonian and nothing here
relaxes them; a RECOVERED verdict would license the frozen numbers in the condition tested and in no
other, and certainly not in vacuo, which is the condition Stage 6 actually used.

Whether a continuum is the RIGHT environment. SOLVATION_DECISION_NOTE.md section 3 argues from held
papers that a continuum responds isotropically and instantaneously and therefore cannot represent the
solvent screening that matters, and that the small continuum effect on the bare barrier "is not
evidence that water is harmless to a charge design - it is evidence that a continuum cannot see the
effect that matters". Nothing here touches that argument. A continuum is used as an INSTRUMENT to
bind the density and separate two effects, not as a claim about the physical environment.

The wavefunction-health verdicts. Diagnostics 6, 8 and 9 classify runs and are untouched.

The budget. Diagnostic 9's falsification of the peak-field hypothesis rests on the committed record
and on no literature value, and is unaffected whichever way this goes.
