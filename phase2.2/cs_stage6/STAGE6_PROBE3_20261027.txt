STAGE 6 - PROBE 3, PRE-REGISTERED                                             27 October 2026

ARE THE COMMITTED DESIGNS ELECTRONICALLY BOUND?

Written and committed before the runs. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. No continuum.


P3.1  WHY

Probe 2 measured the vertical detachment energy with one designed charge present:

   ctl       no charge                VDE +0.5123 eV   bound
   dref_p1   +1 at 2.960 A            VDE +3.4366 eV   bound
   dref_m1   -1 at 2.960 A            VDE -2.1005 eV   METASTABLE
   d250_m1   -1 at 2.500 A            VDE -2.3338 eV   METASTABLE

A positive charge makes the dianion more bound. A negative one at these separations unbinds it,
which is Dreuw & Cederbaum's case: "at short distances the monoanion-point charge system represents
a resonance state that can in principle not be correctly computed with bound state methods".

Probe 2 was pre-registered as NOT a clearance for the many-charge designs, and this is the
measurement it deferred. Where the committed designs actually put negative charge, derived from
relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt and the committed geometry:

   S6-P    none
   S6-2    -1.00 e at 3.485 A
   S6-20   -2.70 e across eight sites between 3.388 and 3.485 A

Diagnostic 11 separately found that all three sit inside r_safe = 3.50 A and that their ddE values
are therefore uninterpretable as design evaluations. Probe 3 asks a DIFFERENT question with a
different answer available: whether they are bound states at all. A design that is not a bound
state has no barrier to compute, and that would be a stronger statement than uninterpretability.


P3.2  WHAT IS RUN

Four single points. The dianion runs already exist and are committed - Diagnostic 10's
cpcm/S6-{C,P,2,20}_R_vac - so only the monoanion partners are new. Each monoanion input is copied
byte for byte from its committed dianion partner, substrate.xyz and pc.pc unchanged, differing only
in the charge line: -1 2 instead of -2 1. Reactant geometry, ma-def2-SVP, in vacuo.

VDE = E(monoanion) - E(dianion). Positive means bound.


P3.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

GATE 0.  Every input must match the committed MANIFEST.sha256 byte for byte.
GATE 1.  The S6-C dianion energy, taken from D10's committed output, must equal Diagnostic 1's
         -836.227913439 Eh to 1e-6.
GATE 2.  The S6-C VDE must reproduce Diagnostic 2's committed +0.5123 eV to 0.01 eV.

THE READING, per design:

   VDE > 0     bound. Its ddE is a barrier effect, subject to Diagnostic 11's separate finding
               about r_safe, which this probe does not touch.
   VDE <= 0    METASTABLE. Its ddE is not a barrier: it is a bound-state calculation of a
               resonance. Its Diagnostic 1 and Diagnostic 10 values are withdrawn as barrier
               effects - not as wrong measurements, but as measurements of something else - and
               any conclusion built on them, including the sign reversal and the ranking
               inversion, is withdrawn with them.

IF ANY DESIGN IS METASTABLE, A CONSTRAINT IS IMPLIED THAT THE OPTIMISER DOES NOT HAVE. Its budget
is sum q_i^2 <= B, which is blind to sign: it cannot distinguish two unit charges of one sign from
two of the other. A bound on negative charge near a dianion is a physical constraint and the grid's
admitted negative ranges would need one before any further design work.

PRE-REGISTERED AS NOT A THRESHOLD. Four arrangements say which of those four are bound. They do not
locate how much negative charge is too much, and no threshold is to be read off them.


P3.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

The transition-state geometry: this and both earlier VDEs are at the reactant, so none says whether
boundedness changes along the reaction coordinate. A design bound at R and metastable at the TS
would be worse than one metastable at both, and that is not tested. The basis dependence Dreuw &
Cederbaum warn of, since this runs one basis - Diagnostic 2 covers it for the bare substrate across
three and it is not repeated here with charges present. And it does not revisit r_safe.
