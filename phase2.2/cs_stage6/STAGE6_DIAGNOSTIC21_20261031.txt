D21.1  THE QUESTION

Are the designs that carry every readable result in this project sitting on stable solutions?

DIAGNOSTIC 20 found that the restricted solution the production runs converge to is a SADDLE
POINT from sum |q| = 4.32 e upward, at both bases, and that the stable solution moves the barrier
by up to 9.5558 kcal/mol - nine times DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's budget. It measured stability on ONE
arrangement, S6-20's, along one ladder:

   stable, measured      up to sum |q| = 2.88 e   (both bases, both states)
   unstable, measured    from sum |q| = 4.32 e
   unmeasured            2.88 to 4.32 e

Every readable whole-design result this project holds sits at or below 2.19 e, which is inside
that stable region. D20.4 states, however, that its findings "do not transfer to DIAGNOSTIC 16's
or 17's designs without measuring them". So the claim that the readable results rest on genuine
minima is at present an INFERENCE from one arrangement's onset, not a measurement on the designs
that carry them. This diagnostic measures them.

   LAMBDA IS NOT COMPARABLE ACROSS DESIGNS AND IS NOT USED HERE AS IF IT WERE. It scales each
   design's own charge vector, so D16's lambda 0.65 (S6-2, two charges, 1.3 e) and the field
   tree's lambda 0.65 (S6-20, twenty charges, 3.74 e) are different fields. Every statement in
   this diagnostic is in sum |q|.

It matters because the readable window is the thesis's contribution. If these designs are stable,
the window claim is measured rather than inferred and every Dset inside it stands as reported. If
any is not, the number that design carries is provisional and the window has to be restated.

D21.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE ANYTHING IS COMPUTED

The verdict of a run is its FIRST stability analysis, which by GATE 2 is the production solution.

READING 1, are the readable designs stable?

   ALL STABLE        all eighteen runs report a stable restricted solution.
   SOME UNSTABLE     any run does not. Every one is named, with its three lowest eigenvalues and
                     its sum |q|.

   The three lowest eigenvalues of every run are reported whatever the verdict.

READING 2, what does any instability cost?

   For each design where either state is unstable, with ddE against the committed def2-SVPD
   charge-free control:

      ddE(first)   from the first converged solution, which is the production solution
      ddE(stable)  from the reconverged solution
      correction = ddE(stable) - ddE(first)

      tol = max( 0.10 x |largest correction entering this reading| , 0.05 kcal/mol ),
      DIAGNOSTIC 11.3's tolerance. ONE BASIS APPEARS HERE, so there is nothing to pool; this is
      stated because DIAGNOSTIC 20 NOTE 1 records a reading that pooled two.

   NOTHING TO CORRECT      no design is unstable.
   CORRECTION NEGLIGIBLE   every |correction| <= tol.
   CORRECTION MATERIAL     any |correction| > tol, each reported in kcal/mol and as a percentage
                           of the 1.067 budget.

   THE CONSEQUENCE IS STATED NOW. If any correction exceeds 1.067 kcal/mol, the Dset and the
   barrier that design carries are PROVISIONAL until recomputed on a stable solution, and the
   readable window cannot be claimed on that design's number. Which designs those would be is
   named in the result, not left to be worked out later.

READING 3, does DIAGNOSTIC 20's onset transfer across arrangements?

   D20 measured one arrangement and found it stable to 2.88 e. Every design here is at or below
   2.19 e, so the ladder predicts all of them stable.

   ONSET TRANSFERS            READING 1 is ALL STABLE. One arrangement's onset field predicted
                              nine others at four different charge counts, and the window claim
                              becomes measured rather than inferred.
   ONSET DOES NOT TRANSFER    any design below 2.88 e is unstable. The onset is a property of the
                              arrangement and not of the field alone, D20's boundary cannot be
                              read as a field threshold, and every design in the record needs its
                              own measurement before its number is quoted.

   ONSET DOES NOT TRANSFER is named here so that it cannot afterwards be presented as a detail.
   It would be the more important of the two outcomes.


D21.4  WHAT THIS DOES NOT TEST

STABILITY IS NECESSARY AND NOT SUFFICIENT. A stable restricted solution is the lowest
single-reference solution ORCA's rotations could find; it does not make the system
single-reference. DIAGNOSTIC 19's READING 3 returned DISAGREE on whether the near-degeneracy is
multireference and that remains open at every field, including these. The CASSCF or NEVPT2
calculation D19.4 named is not replaced by anything here, and ALL STABLE would not license any
claim about multireference character.

ORCA's restart is a bounded heuristic. A solution reported stable is stable against the rotations
it tried, so any correction in READING 2 is a LOWER BOUND.

The bare arm only, def2-SVPD only, one frame, one geometry pair, fixed geometry, in vacuo, no
continuum (STAGE6_AMENDMENT2_20261027). Nothing about the walled arms, nothing about d_shield
itself - which is a difference of two arms, only one of which is measured here - and nothing
about the single-site runs, of which the record holds many.

Nothing about Loss, G0 or kappa, which remain unmeasurable until the OptTS / coordsys cartesian
conflict of STAGE6_RELAXATION_STATE_20261028 is decided.


D21.5  DIRECTION

If READING 3 returns ONSET TRANSFERS, then the readable window rests on measured minima and the
thesis's central claim is as strong as the record can make it: a charge-design method with a
domain of validity bounded by two independent markers, readability at about 1.6 e and stability
at 4.32 e, each measured and mutually consistent, with the failure beyond characterised.

If it returns ONSET DOES NOT TRANSFER, the boundary D20 drew is not a field threshold and the
write-up cannot use it as one. Every quoted design would then need its own stability measurement,
which is bounded work but changes what may be said in the interim: nothing, about any design not
yet measured.
