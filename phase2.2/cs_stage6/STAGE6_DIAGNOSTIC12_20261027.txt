D12.1  WHY, AND WHY NOT THE DIAGNOSTIC THAT WAS FIRST PROPOSED

Diagnostic 11 measured r_safe = 3.50 A along ONE axis, from ONE atom, through ONE committed site.
The grid rebuild needs more than that: it needs a rule that decides, for each of 2626 candidate
sites, whether a bare point charge there is a faithful model of a catalytic group.

The diagnostic first proposed was a floor TABLE in the shape of phase2.2/cs_stage5/floors.tsv -
one row per atom, one column per charge level - of which D11's 3.50 A would be a single cell.
That object was abandoned before anything was written, because three measurements show it cannot
be filled. All three are computed from committed files at the committed reactant geometry, over
the 2463 grid sites that admit q = +1:

   (a) C4, C8 and C9 are NEVER the nearest atom to any admissible site. C5 and C7 are nearest to
       fewer than ten each. No charge position on the grid is owned by those atoms, so no
       measurement could ever fill their rows.

   (b) Nearest-atom distance does not determine the environment. Holding it inside a 0.2 A band,
       the whole-molecule term sum_j 1/d_j^2 still varies by 57 to 88 per cent of its median.
       Two sites at the same nearest-atom distance can sit in environments differing by nearly
       a factor of two.

   (c) The owning atom is not stable along the reaction coordinate. For 199 sites (8.1 per cent)
       the nearest atom differs between R and TS - H3->O3, H1->O3, O1->O2, H5->O6, O5->H4 among
       them. A charge would change rows mid-reaction.

The CLASSICAL floors are unaffected by any of this, because a Lennard-Jones floor is a PAIRWISE
criterion enforced against all 24 atoms simultaneously; it never asserts that a charge belongs to
one atom. That was audited here and holds: every one of the 2626 committed sites clears every
atom's floor at the charge level it is admitted for, zero violations. The column 'on_atom' was
found to mean "the atom whose floor binds", not "the nearest atom" - site 1690 is labelled H3
while O3 lies 0.25 A nearer, because the site clears O3's floor (2.960 against 2.745) and sits
exactly on H3's (3.211). The audit is recorded in the work log, not re-derived here.

Overpolarisation is a response of the whole electron density and has no pairwise decomposition,
so it cannot be hung on a per-atom row. What follows measures the same physical question in a
form that can be answered: the artefact is measured at REAL grid sites, and the question is which
geometric property of a site, if any, predicts it.

This keeps the per-atom intuition as the hypothesis under test rather than discarding it. If
nearest-atom distance alone predicts the artefact, then a single distance floor IS a valid screen
and D11's 3.50 A generalises beyond its axis. The measurement decides.

   Candidates are the 2463 sites with q_min_pos != none, i.e. every site the grid admits a +1

D12.3  WHAT WOULD COUNT AS WHICH ANSWER, STATED BEFORE THEY RUN

GATE 0. Every input file must match the committed MANIFEST.sha256 byte for byte.

GATE 1. The ma-def2-SVP control must reproduce Diagnostic 1's committed -836.227913439 and
-836.199397816 Eh to 1e-6.

GATE 2. Site 1690 must reproduce what is already committed for that position, to 0.01 kcal/mol:
ddE bare -3.8579 (Diagnostic 10's S6-P, -3.857852, and Diagnostic 11's dref) and ddE wall
-7.8221 (Diagnostic 11's dref wall). If either fails, the harness has drifted and nothing below
is read.

GATE 3. phase2.2/cs_stage6/sites_f42436.tsv must have sha256
d2a750077d79e34dfecf448c30c2899ae0cea98f1edc5de3d10361b7c357cfc7, the value
phase2.2/cs_stage6/relax/ARRANGEMENTS.txt records for it. The site coordinates are the input to
this diagnostic and are checked against an independent committed record, not trusted.

C-A12.1, THE WALL MUST BE INERT AT EACH SITE. An ECP centre carrying no charge must give
|ddE| <= 0.30 kcal/mol at that site. Diagnostic 11 found the wall NOT inert at its d250 rung
(+1.311) and voided that rung, so this is known to fire and is checked per site, not once. A site
that fails has its WALLED result voided and is reported as voided; it contributes to no fit and
is not reinterpreted.

THE OBSERVABLE. For each site s,

   ddE(arm, s) = [ (E_TS(arm,s) - E_R(arm,s)) - (E_TS(ctl) - E_R(ctl)) ] x 627.5094740631

   d_shield(s) = ddE(bare, s) - ddE(wall, s)

   PASS(s)  iff  |d_shield(s)| <= max( 0.10 x |ddE(bare,s)| , 0.05 kcal/mol )

The tolerance is Diagnostic 11's, unchanged and for its stated reason: it is RELATIVE, because an
absolute tolerance passes automatically wherever the quantities are small and would certify almost
any site. The 0.05 term is a numerical floor, not the test.

THE DESCRIPTORS. Exactly these five, computed at the R geometry from committed coordinates. No
sixth is added after the numbers are seen.

   X1  d_nearest        distance to the nearest substrate atom
   X2  sum 1/d^2        over all 24 atoms
   X3  sum_O 1/d^2      over the six oxygens only
   X4  d_O              distance to the nearest oxygen
   X5  |ddE classical|  the same charge acting on the control's frozen Loewdin density

ADEQUACY, STATED BEFORE THE RUNS. A descriptor X is ADEQUATE iff BOTH:

   (i)  |Spearman rho( X , |d_shield| )| >= 0.80 over the non-voided sites, AND
   (ii) some threshold on X separates the PASS sites from the FAIL sites with ZERO
        misclassifications.

   (ii) is the operative test. (i) alone would let a descriptor that correlates well in the bulk
   but misorders sites near the boundary license a screen that admits artefact-ridden sites, which
   is exactly the failure this diagnostic exists to prevent.

   All five are reported whether adequate or not. If more than one is adequate the screen uses X1,
   because it is what the grid already applies and the simplest to carry; failing X1, the adequate
   descriptor built from the fewest atoms. This tie-break is fixed here so that no descriptor is
   chosen by looking at which fitted best.

THE READINGS.

   X1 ADEQUATE                  A single distance floor is a valid screen. The per-atom table was
                                unnecessary, D11's r_safe generalises beyond its axis, and the
                                rebuild applies the threshold this measures to all 2626 sites.

   X1 NOT adequate, another is  Nearest-atom distance is abandoned as the admission criterion.

FAR-FIELD CONTROL. At both far-field positions |d_shield| must be <= 0.05 kcal/mol, and ff1's bare
arm must reproduce D11's committed -0.2860 to 0.01. This control is on d_shield - bare against
walled - and NOT on agreement with the classical estimate. STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC11_ERRATUM1 E11.1
records that D11's A3.4 was mis-specified precisely because it required measured ~ classical while
the classical reference uses the frozen density and omits a polarisation enhancement present at
every distance. That failure mode cannot recur here, because the classical reference does not
enter this control.

C-A12.2, THE SIGN ARM. Dreuw & Cederbaum (cr0104227) warn that a monoanion-plus-point-charge at
short range is a resonance "that can in principle not be correctly computed with bound state
methods", and D11's C-A1.4 found its artefact specific to POSITIVE charges. The ten anion-layer
sites are run at q = -1 as well as +1, same positions, so the comparison is within-site. Reported,
not scored: ten sites in one layer cannot separate sign from position.


D12.4  WHAT THIS CANNOT SETTLE

Additivity. Every run here carries ONE charge; a design carries several, and nothing here says the
artefact adds. That is the next diagnostic, not this one.

The Ne ECP parameters remain used outside their published octet assumption (ERRATUM7). The walled
arm is a contrast against which the bare arm is read, not a truth the bare arm is scored against.

The environment. These are in vacuo. STAGE6_PROBE1 and the explicit-water cost measurement bear on
that separately, and Probe 3 established that all four committed designs are electronically bound
at the reactant geometry, so the resonance hazard is not what is being measured here.

The product geometry. The grid's clearances are minima over R, TS and P - that was established
here and is why the descriptors are recomputed per geometry rather than read from the d_ columns -
but only R and TS are run, because ddE is defined on those two.

Charge magnitudes other than +1 and -1. The grid admits +0.25, +0.50 and +0.75 at some sites and
the floors shrink as |q| grows; whether the artefact does the same is not tested. The screen this
produces is therefore a screen AT |q| = 1, the worst case the grid admits, and is applied to
smaller charges only as an upper bound.

Whether any site that passes is catalytic. A faithful charge model is a precondition for reading a
design, not a design.
