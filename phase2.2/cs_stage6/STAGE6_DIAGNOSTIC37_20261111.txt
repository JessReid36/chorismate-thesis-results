STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 37, PRE-REGISTERED 11 NOVEMBER 2026
Six candidate functionals on the record's own two tests: the bound orbital and the barrier.

This file is pre-registered. It is never edited. Changes go into a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The production level of theory was never adopted by an amendment (D36, section 1). At
def2-SVPD and def2-TZVPD the record holds three functionals on this dianion at frame
42436, and two tests of them.

   TEST 1, D28. The highest occupied orbital of the bare dianion, def2-SVPD, reactant,
   against the -0.5123 eV that Probe 3's detachment energy implies:
      B3LYP +1.2307 eV    CAM-B3LYP -0.5918 eV    wB97X-D3 -1.3424 eV

   TEST 2, D36, running. The in vacuo barrier at def2-TZVPD against DLPNO-CCSD(T):
      B3LYP 17.304571    CAM-B3LYP 25.7003    wB97X-D3 27.9689    benchmark: not yet known

If D36 lands away from CAM-B3LYP, the record offers nothing else to turn to: the choice
would be among three functionals, one of which fails test 1. D37 puts six more through
the same two tests, on the same two geometries, so that the choice of a level, when
Jessica makes it, is made among nine and not among three.

WRITTEN BEFORE D36's RESULT. Checked while writing this: the results repository is at
9812caeb and the code repository at f22924e; d36 holds its 6 inputs and no output. No
D36 number was known when the candidates and the readings below were fixed, and that is
deliberate. A candidate list drawn up after the benchmark is known could be drawn up to
suit it.

WHY THESE SIX, AND WHAT THAT REASON IS WORTH. They were named in the session of 11
November 2026 from three benchmark papers:

   Goerigk, Grimme and co-workers, Phys. Chem. Chem. Phys. 2017, the GMTKN55 database,
      pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2017/cp/c7cp04913g
   "Some Observations on the Performance of the Most Recent Exchange-Correlation
      Functionals for the Large and Chemically Diverse GMTKN55 Benchmark",
      arXiv:1905.06172
   Karton and Goerigk, J. Comput. Chem. 2015, 36, 622, doi:10.1002/jcc.23837, on the
      barriers of pericyclic reactions

NONE OF THE THREE IS HELD IN chorismate-refs, and they were read as abstracts and
extracts found online, not in full. By this project's rule they are therefore NOT
EVIDENCE IN THIS RECORD, and nothing in D37 rests on them. A candidate is judged here by
this record's own two tests and by nothing else. They are named so that the origin of
the list is on file and so that Jessica can obtain them.

   two range-separated hybrids       wB97M-V, wB97X-V
   two global hybrid meta-GGAs       PW6B95-D3BJ, M06-2X-D3(0)
   two double hybrids                PWPB95-D3BJ, revDSD-PBEP86-D4

2. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
28 single points on the committed bare substrate of frame 42436,
d33a/sp_42436_{R,TS}/substrate.xyz, charge -2, multiplicity 1, frozen geometries, in
vacuo. No charges, no MM region, no wall.

THE CONTROL, 4 runs, FIRST. D28's own CAM-B3LYP inputs, copied byte for byte, at both
basis sets:
      ! CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-SVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
      ! CAM-B3LYP D3BJ def2-TZVPD def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
   The second is also D36's arm C. Their geometries are identical to d33a's by sha256,
   checked while writing this, so these are repeats of committed calculations and must
   return the committed numbers. See G37.4.

THE SIX CANDIDATES, 24 runs: each at def2-SVPD and at def2-TZVPD, reactant and
transition state. def2-SVPD is the basis of test 1 and of every diagnostic since D30;
def2-TZVPD is the basis of test 2.

EVERY CANDIDATE INPUT IS THE CONTROL INPUT OF THE SAME BASIS WITH ITS FIRST LINE
REPLACED. Below the first line the files are identical byte for byte: %maxcore 3000,
8 cores, %scf MaxIter 300, and the same substrate.xyz. The first lines, with X standing
for def2-SVPD or def2-TZVPD:

      ! wB97M-V X def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
      ! wB97X-V X def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
      ! PW6B95 D3BJ X def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
      ! M062X D3ZERO X def2/J RIJCOSX TightSCF
      ! RI-PWPB95 D3BJ X def2/J X/C RIJCOSX TightSCF
      ! RI-revDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021 X def2/J X/C RIJCOSX TightSCF

   What the first lines share with the control's: the basis, def2/J, RIJCOSX and
   TightSCF. What differs is the functional, its dispersion correction, and, for the two
   double hybrids, the correlation-fitting set.

   DISPERSION. Each functional carries the correction it is defined with: the VV10
   non-local term inside wB97M-V and wB97X-V, which ORCA applies after the SCF by default
   for these two keywords; D3 with Becke-Johnson damping for PW6B95 and PWPB95, as the
   committed B3LYP and CAM-B3LYP arms carry; D3 with zero damping for M06-2X; D4 inside
   the revDSD keyword. The corrections are therefore not the same across the nine
   functionals and cannot be made the same. This is stated as a limit in D37.6.

   THE DOUBLE HYBRIDS. THIS RECORD HAS NO DOUBLE-HYBRID PRECEDENT. The RI- prefix asks
   ORCA to use the RI approximation for the second-order part, and that part needs a
   correlation-fitting set. D36 could not establish whether a /C set exists for these
   basis sets and used AutoAux. Checked for this document in the ORCA 6.0 manual online
   (faccts.de/docs/orca/6.0/manual, 11 November 2026): its table of AuxC sets lists
   def2-SVPD/C and def2-TZVPD/C, its keyword table lists PWPB95 and
   revDSD-PBEP86-D4/2021 and says of that table "Add the prefix RI- or DLPNO- to use the
   respective approximation for the MP2 part." The named sets are used, and the Coulomb
   fitting stays def2/J as in every other arm. Frozen core is ORCA's default and is not
   overridden.

   THE INTEGRATION GRID is ORCA's default, as in every committed arm, so that the first
   line differs in the method alone. See D37.5.

   THE KEYWORDS HAVE BEEN CHECKED AGAINST THE MANUAL AND NOT AGAINST A RUN. ORCA is not
   available where this was written. If ORCA 6.0.1 rejects a line, that candidate is
   reported as a failure with ORCA's own words, the other candidates stand, and a
   corrected line needs its own dated amendment. It is not swapped silently.

ORDER. The four control runs; then all six candidates at def2-SVPD; then all six at
def2-TZVPD; reactant before transition state throughout. Every keyword this record has
never run is therefore first tried in a run of a few minutes. A run that fails does not
stop the runs after it.

RESOURCES. 8 cores, 48 GB, walltime 24:00:00, which routes to day. No -q. Its own
directory, its own lock and its own job name, cm_d37, so it neither waits for D36 nor
touches it. For scale: D28's CAM-B3LYP controls took 3 to 4 minutes at def2-SVPD and 9
to 10 minutes at def2-TZVPD on 8 cores (read from their outputs). What the meta-GGA and
double-hybrid runs take is not known.

5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D37.1  It does not adopt a production level of theory. That is an amendment and a
       decision for Jessica. D37 supplies evidence for it and nothing more.
D37.2  Six functionals are not the field. No range-separated double hybrid, no
       optimally tuned functional and no functional outside the three papers of section
       1 is tested. A candidate absent from this list has not been found wanting.
D37.3  Test 1 is one orbital at one geometry against one number, and carries every limit
       D28 states for it. Passing it does not make a functional right for this dianion.
D37.4  Test 2 inherits every limit of D36: one correlated treatment, one modest basis,
       one frozen frame, in vacuo. Nearest is nearest there, and nothing more.
D37.5  THE GRID IS NOT TESTED. Four of the six candidates are meta-GGAs or are built on
       one (wB97M-V, PW6B95, M06-2X, PWPB95), and meta-GGAs can be sensitive to the
       integration grid. D37 keeps ORCA's default grid so that its inputs differ from
       the control's in the method alone. If one of the four leads, a grid check needs
       its own amendment before that functional is relied on.
D37.6  THE NINE FUNCTIONALS DO NOT SHARE A DISPERSION CORRECTION. See section 2. For a
       rearrangement within one molecule the difference between corrections is expected
       to be small against the differences between functionals. That expectation is not
       measured here.
D37.7  THE DOUBLE HYBRIDS ARE THE LEAST CERTAIN ARM. Their second-order part converges
       more slowly with basis set than a hybrid does, so R37.4 is their only handle on
       basis-set error here, and def2-TZVPD is modest for them. Their orbital energies
       are not comparable in meaning with a hybrid's (R37.2). They are run with ORCA
       6.0.1's built-in parameters, its default frozen core and RI, as they are.
D37.8  NO CHARGES, NO DESIGN, NO RELIABILITY CLAIM. Nothing here bears on relocation, on
       r_safe, or on which designs survive. A functional that did well on both tests
       would still need D31 to D34 measured at it before any design number at that level
       could be quoted.
D37.9  Nothing here is shaped toward a lower barrier. No outcome is preferred. The
       candidates were fixed before D36's result was known, on published rankings of
       accuracy and not on where their barriers were expected to fall.
