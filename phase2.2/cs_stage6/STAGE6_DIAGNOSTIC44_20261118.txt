STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 44
The cross-check of AMENDMENT 7: the six survivors of D43 at five further levels. Do they
pass the four legs there, does the dianion stay bound, and does the barrier change agree?

Pre-registered 18 November 2026, before any D44 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.
A search of the tracked file names and of the text files of both repositories found none
that uses the number 44.
It is read under AMENDMENT 5 (stability), AMENDMENT 6 (binding) and AMENDMENT 7, and it is
the pre-registration A7.3 requires of the cross-check.

1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D43's result, committed in results 36116c36, lists six survivors of the search: designs
of bare charges at or beyond 3.50 A that are RELIABLE on four legs and BOUND at both
states at the working level, CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD. Its R43.8 prints them:

   B_n02_q0.50    2   +0.00   1.00       -5.4190        -5.2489
   B_n02_q0.75    2   +0.00   1.50       -8.3173        -7.8733
   B_n02_q1.00    2   +0.00   2.00      -11.7348       -10.4978

   B_n11_q0.25   11   +0.25   2.75       -9.6049        -7.9140

and says of them: "each ddE above is a working-level number and is quoted as no more than
that. A survivor goes to the cross-check of AMENDMENT 7, under a pre-registration written
before any cross-check output exists (F5)."

AMENDMENT 7, A7.2: "No ddE of a survivor is quoted as more than a working-level number
until it has been through A7.3." A7.3 names five levels, says what is measured at each,
and leaves four things to this document: "What is added to a method line for the point

On 17 November 2026, with D43's result reported to her, Jessica was asked whether the
cross-check should be built first, and wrote "ok proceed".

Where the survivors sit against the limits at the working level is known, from D43's
result, and is why the larger basis and the other functionals are a test and not a
formality. The designs with the largest lowering are the ones nearest the limits:
   - B_n03_q0.75: its gap falls to 1.6625 eV at the reactant, where the flag needs it
     below 1.0 eV and the control's is 6.3277; its Loewdin rise is +0.1717 e at the
     transition state, where the flag is at 0.25.
   - B_n02_q1.00: its detachment energy at the reactant is +0.2272 eV. AMENDMENT 6 sets
     no margin, so it is BOUND.
   - ddE exceeds the first-order value most for B_n03_q0.75 and B_n05_q0.50, by 3.3579
     and 3.8747 kcal/mol.

2. THE CONVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIVE LEVELS are A7.3's, each with the method line of the committed input A7.3 names.
The key is the name used in the run directories.

   key     level                          method line of              double hybrid
   CAMTZ   CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ / def2-TZVPD    d37/CAM_TZ_R/job.inp          no
   PWSV    PWPB95-D3BJ / def2-SVPD        d37/PWPB95_SV_R/job.inp       yes
   PWTZ    PWPB95-D3BJ / def2-TZVPD       d37/PWPB95_TZ_R/job.inp       yes
   RDSV    revDSD-PBEP86-D4 / def2-SVPD   d37a1/revDSD_RI_SV_R/job.inp  yes
   RDTZ    revDSD-PBEP86-D4 / def2-TZVPD  d37a1/revDSD_RI_TZ_R/job.inp  yes

   The %mp2 block of the two revDSD inputs, "%mp2 RI true end", is kept as committed.
   A double hybrid's energy is not the energy of its self-consistent solution: it adds a
   perturbative term built on that solution.

WHAT IS ADDED TO A METHOD LINE. Two kinds of input are used, and no keyword appears in
either that a committed input of this stage does not already hold.

   WITH THE STABILITY ANALYSIS. The committed charged input of the working level,
   d31/B/n01_q0.25_R/job.inp, with the method line of the level in place of its own and
   the %mp2 block where the level has one. What that input adds to a method line is the
   %maxcore and %pal lines, the %scf block with MaxIter 300 and STABPerform true, and the
   %pointcharges line. ORCA's defaults govern the analysis, as at the working level:
   where it finds a solution unstable it re-converges from it and analyses again. D43's
   outputs print it so: "The stability analysis indicates that the wavefunction is
   unstable" and then "Restart requested: Orbitals will be transformed and reconvergence
   attempted".

   WITHOUT IT. The committed charge-free input of the level, every line, with the
   %pointcharges line put before the geometry line. With no charges it is the committed
   input byte for byte.

   THE MONOANION is always the first kind with the charge-and-multiplicity line "-1 2" in
   place of "-2 1". AMENDMENT 6: "The monoanion is charge -1, multiplicity 2, and carries
   the stability analysis."

   A CONTROL is the same input without the %pointcharges line.

AT CAMTZ, a hybrid, every run is of the first kind, as at the working level. The dianion
run is called di and the monoanion run mo.

AT A DOUBLE HYBRID THE DIANION IS RUN TWICE at each state.
   di   without the analysis. ITS ENERGY IS THE ONE USED, for ddE and for the detachment
        energy. It is on the restricted solution, in which every orbital holds a pair of
        electrons.
   ds   with the analysis: THE STABILITY SEARCH on the same dianion. Its energy is never
        used.
   mo   the monoanion, with the analysis.
   WHY. The record's comparisons of these functionals with a correlated barrier (D36,
   D41, D37 Amendment 1), which are the reason A7.3 names them, were made on the
   restricted solution without the analysis. ORCA follows an unstable solution to one
   that is no longer restricted, and a perturbative term built on that is another method
   than the one A7.3 names; no document of the record has measured it against anything.
   So the energy stays on the restricted solution, and the analysis is run beside it to
   answer the question the stability leg exists for: whether a lower self-consistent
   solution with the charge moved was hiding under the one that is used.

WHICH OF THE FOUR LEGS CAN BE READ AT A DOUBLE HYBRID. All four.
   The Loewdin rise, the dipole ratio and the gap are read on di, against the level's
   control di. They are of the self-consistent solution, which is the one the
   perturbative term is built on.
   The stability leg is read on ds:
      NOTHING LOWER            its first analysis is STABLE. No flag. Its final energy
                               must then be di's within 1e-6 Eh; if it is not, the search
                               did not start from the solution whose energy is used, and
                               the leg is not read.
      LOWER, CHARGE IN PLACE   its first analysis is UNSTABLE, ORCA re-converged, and the
                               last analysis is STABLE. The Loewdin rise, the dipole ratio
                               and the gap are read again, on the solution it ended on,
                               against the same control. None is flagged. NOT A FLAG: the
                               lower solution differs in how the spins are paired and not
                               in where the charge is, and it is reported with its <S**2>.
      CHARGE MOVED             the same, and one of the three is flagged on the solution
                               it ended on. A FLAG.
      UNSTABLE AT THE END      the last analysis is UNSTABLE. A FLAG, as at any level.
   This is the rule of AMENDMENT 5 carried to a method with two steps. At the working
   level a first solution that was unstable and then corrected is not a flag, and the
   three legs are read on the corrected solution, because it is the one the run ends on.
   Here they are read on both.

THE FOUR LEGS. The committed classifier phase2.2/cs6_audit.py at its committed thresholds,
D_LW 0.25 e, R_DIP 2.0, GAP_ABS 1.0 eV and R_GAP 0.5, read at read time and not
transcribed, on each dianion against the charge-free control of the same level and state,
with the rules D33.3 and AMENDMENT 5 settled: stability on the LAST analysis; the gap
withheld in a run that engaged TRAH or a level shift, or whose control did. A state is
FLAGGED if the Loewdin rise reaches D_LW, or the dipole ratio reaches R_DIP, or the gap is
below GAP_ABS and below R_GAP of the control's, or the last stability analysis is
UNSTABLE, or the SCF of di did not converge (D31.3: "NOT CONVERGING IS A RESULT, NOT A
BROKEN JOB."), or, at a double hybrid, the search says CHARGE MOVED.

   RELIABLE       no flag at either state, and at both the gap was readable and the
                  stability analysis, or the search, was read
   UNRELIABLE     a flag at either state; the result names each
   INCONCLUSIVE   no flag, and at one state or both a gap withheld, or a stability
                  analysis or search not read, or a search that did not start from the
                  solution whose energy is used
   NOT CLASSIFIED no flag, and a state NOT MEASURED or a control that cannot be used

A CONTROL THAT CANNOT BE USED. At CAMTZ: the control di is not measured, or its stability
analysis was not read, or its last analysis is UNSTABLE. At a double hybrid: the control
di is not measured, or does not reproduce the committed energy of the same input within
1e-6 Eh (section 3). The legs that need the control, and ddE, are then not formed at that
level.

THE FIFTH LEG, by AMENDMENT 6, at each level. VDE = E(mo) - E(di) at the same state with
the same point charges, each the last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY of its output, at
27.211386245988 eV per Eh.

   BOUND        VDE above zero, and the monoanion's last stability analysis STABLE
   METASTABLE   VDE at or below zero
   "ABOVE ZERO, MONOANION NOT STABLE" is reported with its number and is not BOUND.

A design PASSES THE FIFTH LEG at a level if it is BOUND there at both states.

ddE at a level = (E(TS) - E(TS, control)) - (E(R) - E(R, control)), on di and the level's
control di, at 627.5094740631 kcal/mol per Eh. Negative lowers the barrier.

THE WORKING-LEVEL ddE of a design is recomputed by the reader from D43's committed outputs
and must be the number d43.tsv prints.

THE TOLERANCE. The record carries one constant for the uncertainty of method and basis.
STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, decided by Jessica on 21 October 2026 for another use: "LOSS MUST BE
BELOW 15 % OF |ddE(frozen)|." and "15 % is not a new constant: it is the fraction decision
O6 already requires for the method-and-basis uncertainty". It is used here and no new one
is made. At a level, with differ = ddE - working:

   WITHIN           |differ| is below 15 % of |working|
   OUTSIDE          |differ| is 15 % of |working| or more, IN EITHER DIRECTION: a ddE that
                    is more negative than the working level's by that much is OUTSIDE just
                    as one that is less negative is
   NOT ASSESSABLE   |working| is below 3.2 kcal/mol, the floor the same document sets
                    because a ratio means nothing on a small number. All six working
                    values are above it; the smallest is 5.4190.
   NOT FORMED       a state of di is not read, or the level's control cannot be used

   The tolerance is relative, as WORKING_RULES.md guard rail 4 requires. The centre is the
   working level because the question A7.2 asks is whether the working-level number may be
   quoted as more than that.

AT A LEVEL a design
   PASSES   if it is RELIABLE on four legs, passes the fifth leg and its ddE is WITHIN
   FAILS    if it is UNRELIABLE, or METASTABLE at either state, or its ddE is OUTSIDE
   is OPEN  otherwise: not established either way

A DESIGN
   PASSES THE CROSS-CHECK          if it passes at all five levels
   DOES NOT PASS THE CROSS-CHECK   if it fails at any level
   otherwise THE CROSS-CHECK IS INCOMPLETE for it

A7.3: "All five are run for every survivor and all five are reported, whichever way they
come out. None is dropped."

3. WHAT IS RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
196 single points on frame 42436, with bare point charges: no wall, no Lennard-Jones term,
no continuum. The geometries are d33a/sp_42436_R and d33a/sp_42436_TS, which are also the
geometries of the committed inputs A7.3 names. Each design's point-charge file is D43's
committed file, byte for byte.

   level   runs   controls                              each of the six designs
   CAMTZ    28    C_ctl (di), C_bare (mo)               di, mo
   PWSV     42    C_ctl (di), C_ctlS (ds), C_bare (mo)  di, ds, mo
   PWTZ     42    the same                              the same
   RDSV     42    the same                              the same
   RDTZ     42    the same                              the same
   every one at the reactant and at the transition state

A run directory is <level>_<design>_<di|ds|mo>_<R|TS>, and a control's is
<level>_<C_ctl|C_ctlS|C_bare>_<R|TS>.

THE CONTROLS.
   C_ctl    the charge-free dianion. At a double hybrid it is the committed input of the
            level byte for byte, and its committed energy is the one it must reproduce:

            level   committed input at R and TS   E at R, Eh       E at TS, Eh    barrier
            PWSV    d37/PWPB95_SV_{R,TS}          -835.98233074   -835.94694777   22.2031
            PWTZ    d37/PWPB95_TZ_{R,TS}          -836.98868516   -836.95467654   21.3407
            RDSV    d37a1/revDSD_RI_SV_{R,TS}     -834.95568796   -834.92088148   21.8414
            RDTZ    d37a1/revDSD_RI_TZ_{R,TS}     -836.09265712   -836.05840150   21.4957

            At CAMTZ it carries the analysis, so it is not the committed input. The
            committed energies of d37/CAM_TZ_{R,TS}, -837.20938385 and -837.16842778 Eh, a
            barrier of 25.7003 kcal/mol, are printed beside it. Whether it reproduces them
            is a reading: its own stability is the condition for using it.
   C_ctlS   the stability search on the charge-free dianion, at the double hybrids.
   C_bare   the bare monoanion, which gives the bare substrate's detachment energy at the
            level.

ORDER, in each job: the controls; then the six designs in D43's order, each as di R,
di TS, (ds R, ds TS,) mo R, mo TS. A run that fails does not stop the runs after it.

FIVE JOBS, one for each level, side by side: cm_d44_ct (CAMTZ), cm_d44_ps (PWSV),
cm_d44_pt (PWTZ), cm_d44_rs (RDSV) and cm_d44_rt (RDTZ). Each has 8 cores, 48 GB,
walltime 24:00:00, no -q, its own job script run_<level>.pbs and its own log.

HOW LONG. Estimated, not measured. From the committed PBS logs (derived): the ten
committed charge-free runs A7.3 names, which carry no analysis, took 2.3 to 2.8 minutes
at def2-SVPD and 5.0 to 6.8 at def2-TZVPD; D37's two runs at the working level without
the analysis took 2.3 and 3.0; D43's 12 dianion runs of the designs, with the analysis,
took 5.4 minutes on average and its 12 monoanion runs 11.3. ASSUMING the analysis
multiplies a run by 2.04 and a monoanion by a further 2.10 at every level, as those
figures give at the working level, the five jobs take about 9.9 hours (CAMTZ), 4.3
(PWSV), 9.5 (PWTZ), 4.7 (RDSV) and 10.7 (RDTZ). NO RUN OF THE RECORD CARRIES THE ANALYSIS
AT def2-TZVPD OR AT A DOUBLE HYBRID, so that assumption is untested and a job may reach
its walltime. Every job script is restartable: submitted again, it skips every run that
ended normally. The harvest refuses a job whose log does not reach its last line unless
it is told to go on.

WHAT HAS NO PRECEDENT. Among the inputs under phase2.2/cs_stage6 that git tracks, none
carries the stability analysis at def2-TZVPD or at a double hybrid, and the only monoanion
at any of the five levels is d40/A_PWPB95, at PWPB95-D3BJ/def2-SVPD without the analysis.
Inputs inside archives were not searched. Whether ORCA 6.0.1 runs the analysis at a double
hybrid is therefore not known. The di runs do not depend on it. If the ds and mo runs of a
double hybrid fail, that is F4.

       UNRELIABLE or not BOUND at that level, as D33's and D43's results did.

6. WHAT FOLLOWS FROM EACH OUTCOME, DECIDED NOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
F1  A DESIGN PASSES THE CROSS-CHECK. Its six values of ddE, the working level's and the
    five, may be quoted together, each with its level, and R44.6 prints their range. That
    is all D44 gives it. A7.4: "WHAT A SURVIVOR THAT PASSES THE CROSS-CHECK MAY BE CALLED.
    That is a dated document of its own and a decision for Jessica."
F2  A DESIGN DOES NOT PASS. The result names the level and the reason. The design stays a
    survivor of the search at the working level and nothing more, and no ddE of it is
    quoted as more than a working-level number. No level is dropped and none is added to
    find one at which it passes: A7.3 allows a sixth "only by a dated amendment written
    before any output at it exists".
F3  THE CROSS-CHECK IS INCOMPLETE FOR A DESIGN. The cause is diagnosed first. A job that
    was cut short is submitted again as it is. Anything else that would change an input
    is a dated amendment, written before the run is repeated.
F4  A LEVEL DID NOT RUN. If none of the charged runs of a level is measured, or none of
    the ds and mo runs of a double hybrid is, as when ORCA does not run the analysis
    there, that is the primary result for the level. Its di runs, if they ran, still give
    a ddE, which is printed; the stability leg and the fifth leg are then not established
    there and every design is INCOMPLETE. What is added to the method line for the
    analysis at a double hybrid is then the subject of a dated amendment, and A7.3
    stands: "None is dropped."
F5  THE RESTRICTED SOLUTION OF THE BARE SUBSTRATE IS UNSTABLE AT A DOUBLE HYBRID, that
    is, C_ctlS finds a lower solution. Then the charge-free barriers of D37 and of D37
    Amendment 1 at that level, and the comparisons of D36 and D41 that rest on them, were
    made on a solution the analysis does not find to be the lowest. D44 reports it and
    reads the level all the same, by section 2, which was fixed before the result was
    known. What it means for those comparisons is not decided here.
F6  A DESIGN FAILS AT CAMTZ ALONE. CAMTZ differs from the working level in the basis and
    in nothing else, so that failure is the basis's. D43.7 said of its own designs: "Every
    total is inside the 2.88 e at which D30 found def2-SVPD converged, and D30 found that
    on another arrangement." Whether the working level's basis is then enough for a search
    is not decided here.
F7  THE PRODUCTION LEVEL OF THEORY is not decided by D44 under any outcome (A7.4).

7. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D44.1  IT ADOPTS NO PRODUCTION LEVEL OF THEORY and does not say which benchmark is nearer
       the truth. It names no design.
D44.2  ONE FRAME, FROZEN GEOMETRIES, IN VACUO, NO CONTINUUM. D31.8 said it of its own
       numbers and it holds of these: "No barrier reported from this diagnostic is a
       prediction of a physical barrier."
D44.3  THE CHARGES ARE BARE POINT CHARGES. They stand for no residue. The refined
       representation, the wall, is not in any run here.
D44.4  THREE FUNCTIONALS IN TWO BASIS SETS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT TESTS. The working level
       and CAMTZ share a functional. A7.4 says of whether a functional's error on the
       barrier cancels in ddE: "Nothing in the record measures it. Agreement of three
       functionals is not that measurement."
D44.5  THE TOLERANCE WAS NOT MADE FOR THIS. It is the record's one constant for method
       and basis, fixed on 21 October 2026 for the loss of a design on relaxation. It was
       taken up here with D43's six numbers known and no number of D44 in existence. Other
       forms were thought of before it was found in the record, an absolute figure and
       other percentages; none was set against any number, and the record's constant was
       taken because it is the record's.
D44.6  AT A DOUBLE HYBRID the energy is on the restricted solution whether or not the
       search finds that solution stable. The three legs read a self-consistent density
       and not one relaxed with the perturbative term. The monoanion is an unrestricted
       reference: its spin contamination is reported and not corrected, and a
       contaminated reference can raise its perturbative energy and so the detachment
       energy. No threshold on <S**2> is set, because the record holds none.
D44.7  THE SIX DESIGNS SHARE TWO SITES and were placed by a greedy rule on one grid. D43.6
       holds: they are not an optimisation.
D44.8  THE DETACHMENT ENERGIES are vertical and in vacuo. Where one is negative its sign
       is the reading and its size is not (D40.4).
D44.9  It does not re-measure r_safe, does not take route (a) and does not adopt the wall.
D44.10 NOTHING HERE IS SHAPED. The tolerance is symmetric. Every level is reported
       whichever way it comes out. The rule for the stability search at a double hybrid
       was written before any double hybrid had been run with the analysis in this
       project, and section 8 says what was expected of it.
