STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 40
Detachment energies at the density-functional level: eight functionals on the bare
substrate, and the five arrangements whose lowering is quotable.

Pre-registered 15 November 2026, before any D40 output exists.
This document is never edited. Changes go in a dated amendment or erratum.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The record's test of whether the dianion keeps its second electron is the vertical
detachment energy, and not the sign of an orbital energy. Diagnostic 2 said so, Probe 1's
Erratum 1 withdrew a column for reading the orbital instead, and Probe 2's result heads
itself "THE HOMO IS NOT THE TEST". Diagnostic 39's pre-registration says it again: "The
observable this project actually uses for boundedness is not the orbital energy."

Two things in the record rest on the orbital all the same, because the observable has
never been computed where it is needed.

FIRST, THE CHOICE OF FUNCTIONAL. D28 and D37 read each functional's highest occupied
orbital against one number, -0.5123 eV, which is minus a detachment energy computed with
B3LYP-D3BJ at ma-def2-SVP. Checked while writing this: every committed input at charge -1
and multiplicity 2 is B3LYP-D3BJ (one at def2-SVP, one at def2-SVPD, nine at
ma-def2-SVP), apart from the three DLPNO-CCSD(T) inputs of D39, which have no output yet.
CAM-B3LYP, which D30 to D34 all use, has no detachment energy of its own, and neither has
any other functional of D28 or D37. So each functional's orbital is being compared with
another functional's energy difference at another basis.

SECOND, THE QUOTABLE NUMBERS. Five lowerings are quotable at CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD:

   D34, R34.9            ADD-s540_qm06500    -3.3985   one sphere of -0.6500 e
                         ADD-s678_qm07625    -4.1567   one sphere of -0.7625 e
                         ADD-s678_qm10000    -5.3701   one sphere of -1.0000 e
   D33 stage B, with     bare_n05_q0.50     -14.3612   five point charges, 0.50 e each
   its Erratum 2         bare_n11_q0.25     -13.0007   eleven point charges, 0.25 e each

None of the five has a detachment energy at any level. What the record does hold is
Probe 2, at B3LYP-D3BJ/ma-def2-SVP and at the reactant: one charge of -1.0 e at 2.960 A
gives -2.1005 eV, METASTABLE, and Probe 2's verdict on such a condition is "Any ddE
measured at such a condition is not a barrier effect and must not be reported as one."
That charge sits at another site than D34's three spheres, so it does not decide them.
Probe 3 found S6-P, S6-2 and S6-20 bound. The four-leg reliability criterion has no leg
for this: D34's three are RELIABLE on four legs and that says nothing about binding.

D40 computes the observable in both places. D39 is computing it at the coupled-cluster
level for the bare substrate and two point-charge arrangements; D40 is the
density-functional side of the same question and is read beside D39 when D39 reports.

2. THE CONVENTION, TAKEN FROM THE RECORD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Probe 2's result: "VDE = E(monoanion) - E(dianion) at the committed reactant geometry,
same external charge in both. Positive means bound." The monoanion is charge -1,
multiplicity 2. The conversion is the probes' own, 27.211386245988 eV per Eh
(vde_designs/cs11_design_vde.py). Energies are the last FINAL SINGLE POINT ENERGY; for a
QM/MM run the energy is read the way cs6_analyse.py reads it for ddE, the QM/MM total,
and the Lennard-Jones term is the same in the monoanion and the dianion because nothing
but the QM charge differs.

   BOUND at this condition       VDE above zero
   METASTABLE at this condition  VDE at or below zero

These are Probe 2's two words and no third is introduced.

WHY PART B CARRIES THE STABILITY ANALYSIS AND PART A DOES NOT. Neither is a choice made

monoanion solution that is not the lowest one makes the detachment energy too HIGH, so an
undetected instability favours BOUND. Part B's runs are analysed for it. Part A's are
not, and section 6 says how that limits them. A_CAM and B_bare_R are the same species at
the same level, without and with the analysis, and their difference is a reading.

R40.4  PART B. For the bare substrate and each of the five arrangements, at each state:

       level shift or TRAH was engaged.
R40.5  A_CAM against B_bare_R: the two monoanion energies and their difference.
R40.6  For each arrangement, the change in detachment energy from the bare substrate at
       the same state, in eV. Reported, not interpreted.
R40.7  WHAT THE RULE OF THE RECORD THEN REACHES. A lowering is a difference between a
       transition state and a reactant. If either state of an arrangement is METASTABLE,
       Probe 2's sentence applies to that arrangement's ddE, and the result names the
       number it reaches. If both states are BOUND, the result says that this objection
       does not reach the number at this level and basis. D40 does no more than that:
       section 6.

6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D40.1  IT WITHDRAWS NOTHING AND CHANGES NO VERDICT. D34's and D33's verdicts are
       reliability verdicts and stay as committed. If R40.7 reaches a quotable number,
       what becomes of that number is a dated erratum and a decision for Jessica. D34's
       result says the same of its own numbers: "withdrawal of a committed number needs
       its own dated erratum".
D40.2  It does not make binding a leg of the reliability criterion. That is an amendment.
D40.3  A detachment energy computed with unrestricted Kohn-Sham is an energy difference
       between two self-consistent solutions. Part A's monoanions are not analysed for
       stability, so each of part A's values is an upper bound in this sense: a lower
       monoanion solution, if one exists, would lower it. Spin contamination is reported
       and not corrected; Probe 3's committed S6-2 monoanion shows <S**2> of 1.243299.
D40.4  A METASTABLE species has no bound state to converge to. In a finite basis its
       energy depends on the basis, which is what Dreuw and Cederbaum warn of in the
       record's citation. So where a detachment energy is negative, its sign is the
       reading and its size is not.
D40.5  One basis, def2-SVPD. Probe 1 measured a change of sign in the bare detachment
       energy between def2-SVP and def2-SVPD.
D40.6  Vertical, not adiabatic: both species at the dianion's geometry.
D40.7  One frame. The transition-state values of part B are the first at that geometry
       in the record at any level, and have nothing to reproduce.
D40.8  All five arrangements sit inside r_safe. D40 says nothing about that.
D40.9  It does not test the functionals against the benchmark: R40.3 waits for D39.
D40.10 It adopts no level of theory.
D40.11 NOTHING HERE IS SHAPED. The five arrangements are the record's only quotable
       lowerings. A METASTABLE result for any of them is reported as plainly as BOUND, in
       the same table, by a rule fixed here before any output exists.
