STAGE 6 - AMENDMENT 8: how the level of theory for reporting is chosen, fixed before the
cross-check is read and before any coupled-cluster barrier change exists
18 November 2026. Proposed on 18 November 2026 in answer to Jessica's question of that day, how
a level is selected after a benchmark, and agreed by her the same day: "okay yes you can
proceed". It takes effect when she commits it.

Nothing already committed is edited by this amendment and no committed verdict is rewritten by
it. STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is not edited. AMENDMENTS 1 to 7 stand. IT DOES NOT ADOPT A LEVEL OF
THEORY. It fixes the rule by which one is chosen, so that the rule cannot be written later
with the numbers it is applied to in hand.


A8.1  WHAT THE RECORD HOLDS

No amendment has adopted a level of theory. AMENDMENT 7: "IT DOES NOT ADOPT A PRODUCTION LEVEL
OF THEORY." Its A7.4 leaves the decision open: "THE PRODUCTION LEVEL OF THEORY. That decision
stays where D36.7 and D41.7 leave it." DIAGNOSTIC 44, F7: "THE PRODUCTION LEVEL OF THEORY is
not decided by D44 under any outcome (A7.4)."

Jessica, 18 November 2026: "we will need to select one or two to proceed".

What the record can compare a functional with is the barrier of the bare substrate: D36's
DLPNO-CCSD(T) barrier at def2-TZVPD and D41's at def2-SVPD. What a design is judged on is not
that barrier. It is ddE, the change of the barrier when the charges are present. A7.4 says of
that: "WHETHER A FUNCTIONAL'S ERROR ON THE BARRIER CANCELS IN ddE. Nothing in the record
measures it. Agreement of three functionals is not that measurement." D38 ran the benchmark
method with charges present at the reactant only, and says of itself: "No transition state was
run, so no barrier and no ddE exists in this document at any level."

So the record holds no number by which a level could be chosen for ddE. This amendment says
what that number is, how it is measured, and what is done with it.


A8.2  THE WORDS

THE SEARCH LEVEL is the working level of A7.2, CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVPD with the stability
analysis. It is not changed.

THE REPORT LEVEL is the one level at which the five legs and the ddE of a design are quoted as
the result of this stage. It is what the documents of the record call a production level of
theory. It need not be the search level.

THE CANDIDATES are the six levels at which D43's survivors are measured: the working level,
and the five of A7.3. No other is a candidate unless a dated amendment adds it before any
output at it exists, as A7.3 requires of a sixth cross-check level.


A8.3  THE REFERENCE

THE REFERENCE DESIGNS are two of D43's six survivors, fixed by rule and not by choice: the one
whose working-level ddE is smallest in size and the one whose is largest. By R43.8 of D43's
result they are

   B_n02_q0.50    2 charges    ddE  -5.4190 kcal/mol at the working level
   B_n05_q0.50    5 charges    ddE -15.3124 kcal/mol at the working level

B_n05_q0.50 is THE CHECK DESIGN: of the two the one with the larger lowering, and of the six
the one whose ddE exceeds its first-order value by most (3.8747 kcal/mol, DIAGNOSTIC 44,
section 1).

THE REFERENCE METHOD is the benchmark's: DLPNO-CCSD(T) with TightPNO, by the method line of
d38/N/job.inp at def2-SVPD and of d36a2/B_R/job.inp at def2-TZVPD, with the design's committed
point-charge file of D43 added by the %pointcharges line that D38's charged inputs carry. The
charge-free legs are the committed ones, d38/N and d41/T at def2-SVPD and d36a2/B_R and B_TS at
def2-TZVPD, and are not run again.

THE REFERENCE VALUE of a reference design, ddE_ref, is its ddE by that method at def2-TZVPD:

   ddE = (E(TS, charges) - E(TS, charge-free)) - (E(R, charges) - E(R, charge-free))

THE REFERENCE IS CHECKED BEFORE IT IS USED, on three things that DIAGNOSTIC 45 measures for the
bare barrier and that DIAGNOSTIC 46 measures for ddE:

   c_T      the triples. ddE of the check design at def2-SVPD with DLPNO-CCSD(T1), the
            iterative treatment, minus the same with the benchmark's non-iterative one.
   c_PNO    the PNO threshold. Half of: ddE of the check design at def2-SVPD at the
            benchmark's TCutPNO of 1e-7 minus the same at 1e-6. That half is what the
            two-point formula of Altun, Neese and Bistoni (J. Chem. Theory Comput. 2020, 16,
            6142) adds to the 1e-7 value.
   c_basis  the basis set. For each reference design, its ddE at def2-TZVPD minus its ddE at
            def2-SVPD, both by the reference method.

   The charge-free legs of c_T and of c_PNO are D45's runs T1_R, T1_TS, P6_R and P6_TS.

THE REFERENCE IS USABLE if every run it needs is measured, |ddE_ref| of both reference designs
is 3.2 kcal/mol or more, and

   for the check design    |c_T| + |c_PNO| + |c_basis|  is below 15 % of |ddE_ref|
   for the other design    |c_basis|                    is below 15 % of |ddE_ref|

The reason is plain arithmetic: a reference whose own measured sensitivity is as large as the
tolerance cannot tell a level that is inside the tolerance from one that is not. The three are
added as sizes, so nothing is assumed to cancel. c_T and c_PNO are measured on one design, the
check design, and that is a limit of this rule, said here.

All of it is measured by DIAGNOSTIC 46, under a pre-registration written before any of its
outputs exists. That pre-registration fixes its gates and its readings. It may not change the
two designs, the method lines, the three checks or the rule above.

WHY TWO DESIGNS AND NOT SIX. Cost, on a machine that is shared. One charge-free leg of the
benchmark at def2-TZVPD took 4 h 08 min (reactant) and 6 h 42 min (transition state) on 16
cores with 240 GB of memory asked for (D36 Amendment 2), and at def2-SVPD between 1 h 05 and
1 h 14 on 16 cores (D38, D41). Two designs are twelve new runs with the checks; six would be
twenty-eight. What two designs buy is a test at both ends of the range of working-level
lowering. What they do not buy is said in A8.7.


A8.4  THE RULE

FOR EACH CANDIDATE LEVEL, three things are asked.

(a) THE FIVE LEGS ARE RUN AND PASSED THERE BY BOTH REFERENCE DESIGNS. At the working level that
    is D43's committed result: RELIABLE on four legs, and BOUND at both states. At a level of
    A7.3 it is D44's result for that level: RELIABLE on four legs and passing the fifth leg.
    A LEVEL AT WHICH A LEG CANNOT BE RUN DOES NOT MEET (a). It can meet it only after a dated
    amendment has said how that leg is run there, and it has been run, and it is passed.

(b) THE ddE AGREES WITH THE REFERENCE FOR BOTH REFERENCE DESIGNS. With differ = ddE at the
    level minus ddE_ref:

       WITHIN           |differ| is below 15 % of |ddE_ref|
       OUTSIDE          |differ| is 15 % of |ddE_ref| or more, IN EITHER DIRECTION
       NOT FORMED       the ddE at the level is not measured, or the reference is not usable

    Every candidate, in either basis set, is compared with the same number, the reference at
    def2-TZVPD, because the question is whether the level's ddE is right and not whether it
    matches a small-basis number. The tolerance is relative, as WORKING_RULES.md guard rail 4
    requires, and is centred on the reference. 15 % and 3.2 kcal/mol are the record's one pair
    of constants for the uncertainty of method and basis, from STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt, as in D44:
    "LOSS MUST BE BELOW 15 % OF |ddE(frozen)|." No new constant is made.

(c) FOR A CANDIDATE AT def2-SVPD, THE LEGS HOLD IN THE LARGER BASIS: both reference designs
    also meet (a) at the same functional at def2-TZVPD. D44's F6 leaves open "Whether the
    working level's basis is then enough for a search"; a level whose verdicts on the legs
    change with the basis set is not one to report at.

A LEVEL QUALIFIES if it meets (a) and (b), and (c) where (c) applies.

THE REPORT LEVEL IS THE FIRST LEVEL THAT QUALIFIES in this order of preference, fixed now:

   1. CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ / def2-SVPD, the working level
   2. CAM-B3LYP-D3BJ / def2-TZVPD
   3. a double hybrid at def2-SVPD
   4. a double hybrid at def2-TZVPD

   Where both double hybrids qualify in one basis set, the one whose larger |differ| / |ddE_ref|
   of the two reference designs is the smaller.

   WHY THIS ORDER. The working level first, because every design in the record already has its
   numbers there, so adopting it moves nothing that is measured. A hybrid before a double
   hybrid, because at a hybrid the legs are read on the density that gives the energy, and at
   a double hybrid they are not (D44.6). The smaller basis before the larger. COST DOES NOT
   SEPARATE THE SIX BY MUCH AND IS NOT CLAIMED TO: the committed charge-free reactant runs took
   between 2 min 19 s and 6 min 36 s each on 8 cores (d37/CAM_SV_R, d37/CAM_TZ_R,
   d37/PWPB95_SV_R, d37/PWPB95_TZ_R, d37a1/revDSD_RI_SV_R, d37a1/revDSD_RI_TZ_R).

   THE ORDER DOES NOT LOWER THE BAR. A level that is preferred still has to qualify, and every
   level's differ is printed whether it qualifies or not. A7.3 stands: "None is dropped."

IF NO LEVEL QUALIFIES, none is adopted. Each design is then reported as its values at every
level at which it was measured, with the reference values of the two reference designs beside
them, and no single number is quoted for it. What is done next is a dated amendment.

IF THE REFERENCE IS NOT USABLE, the rule is not applied and none is adopted. What is done next
is a dated amendment, written with the three checks in view and saying so.


A8.5  WHAT ADOPTS A LEVEL

This amendment does not. A level is adopted by a dated amendment that applies A8.4 to the
committed results of D43, D44 and D46, prints every number the rule uses, and is Jessica's
decision. That amendment may not change the rule. If she decides against what the rule gives,
that is hers to decide, and the amendment says that the rule gave one thing and she decided
another, and why.

Once a level is adopted, every design that is reported is measured there on all five legs, and
its numbers at the other levels are still reported beside it.


A8.6  THE ORDER OF EVENTS

1. This amendment is committed before any output of D44 is read. D44's harvest, which runs its
   reader, is held until then.
2. DIAGNOSTIC 45, pre-registered with this amendment, measures for the bare barrier the three
   approximations of the benchmark that the record says are not measured.
3. DIAGNOSTIC 46 is pre-registered before any of its outputs exists and is submitted only
   after D45's result is committed, and only if that result reads its PNO series as
   CONVERGING (D45, F1 and F2). Any other word there means a dated amendment first.
4. The adopting amendment of A8.5 follows the results of D44 and D46.


A8.7  WHAT THIS DOES NOT DECIDE, AND WHAT THE RULE CANNOT SHOW

1. IT ADOPTS NO LEVEL.
2. WHAT A SURVIVOR THAT PASSES THE CROSS-CHECK MAY BE CALLED. A7.4: "That is a dated document
   of its own and a decision for Jessica."
3. TWO DESIGNS OF ONE FAMILY. The reference designs share sites 557 and 1124 with each other
   and with every survivor, were placed by one greedy rule on one grid (D43.6), and stand on
   one frame with frozen geometries, in vacuo. A level that qualifies has been shown to follow
   the reference for these two designs. That it follows it for another design is an inference,
   and the four survivors between the two are not measured at the reference.
4. THE REFERENCE IS NOT THE TRUTH. DLPNO-CCSD(T) in a triple-zeta basis is the best number
   this project can make for ddE, and the three checks measure its sensitivity, not its error.
5. NOTHING ABOUT r_safe, the wall, route (a), or DIAGNOSTIC 39.
6. THE BARE BARRIER. D45 measures the benchmark's approximations for it. No number of D45
   enters A8.4; D45 bears on this amendment only through step 3 of A8.6.


A8.8  WHAT WAS KNOWN WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN

Known: every number of D43's result, among them the six working-level values of ddE, so the two
reference designs were known by name when the rule that picks them was written. The rule,
smallest and largest, was the first one proposed and no other was tried against the numbers.
Known: the results of D36, D37, D37 Amendment 1, D38 and D41.
Known of D44, whose five jobs were submitted on 18 November 2026 and four of which were still
running when this was written: how many of its outputs ended normally at each level, by a count
on hpc1; and, from the job log of PWPB95-D3BJ/def2-SVPD and the last lines of its outputs, that
its 14 runs without the stability analysis ended normally and its 28 with it ended with ORCA's
words "Error: Invalid or unknown value for Exchange in DFT XC-Kernel. Please try using LIBXC
instead!". That is D44's outcome F4 for that level. So it was known, as (a) was written, that
PWPB95-D3BJ/def2-SVPD does not meet (a) as things stand, and expected that def2-TZVPD will be
the same. (a) is not written for that reason: it is AMENDMENT 6's five legs, required at the
level that is reported.
NOT KNOWN: the energy of any run of D44. None has been read, by the reader or by eye. No
coupled-cluster ddE exists. No output of D45 or D46 exists.
Changed after Jessica agreed, and before she committed: (c) was added; the checks of the
reference were written out in full; and "the cheapest level that qualifies", as it was put to
her, became the order of preference of A8.4, because the committed run times do not separate
the six by much. She was told of all three when this was delivered.
