Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 6, NOTE 1 (2026-10-30).

Two defects in how cs6_audit.read() read every committed output, and what fixing them changes.
Measured by cs6_audit_diff.py, committed alongside, against the repository at this commit. No
pre-registration, criterion or threshold is altered. D6.3's five rules and their constants are
untouched.


N1.1  THE TWO DEFECTS

(a) The Loewdin test could read a design's own input charge back out.

    ORCA prints an inline ECP centre (Ne>) inside LOEWDIN ATOMIC CHARGES under the symbol Q,
    carrying the charge ASSIGNED to it, not a Loewdin population. cs6_audit.read() filtered only
    METALS = {Na, K, Mg, Ca, Li, Zn, Fe, Cl, Br, I}, which does not contain Q, and did not
    truncate the list. Those rows therefore entered max|Loewdin|.

    A walled run whose largest charge exceeds its control median by D_LW = 0.25 e then fires
    D6.3's MOVED test on its own input, with nothing having happened to the density. At
    def2-SVPD the control median is 0.339 e, so the trip point is 0.589 e.

(b) The gap printed for a TRAH-SCF run was the level-shifted one.

    With CNVShift on, the ORBITAL ENERGIES table prints shifted orbital energies, so LUMO - HOMO
    reads as the shift floor of 0.1 Eh (2.7211 eV) rather than the converged gap. TRAH reports
    the gap itself, and it is far smaller - under 0.5 eV in the affected Stage 6 runs.


N1.2  WHAT FIXING THEM CHANGES

    outputs classified                     2605
    of which walled, A1.2 correction used  520
    verdicts changed                       140
    moved toward COLLAPSED                 0

    Every change is POLARISED -> CLEAN. A1.3 makes both readable, so no reading is withheld or
    granted that was not before, and no Dset, no d_shield and no diagnostic verdict changes.

    The walled runs are scored here with AMENDMENT 1 A1.2's dipole correction applied, because
    that is what DIAGNOSTICS 14 to 17 do. It matters: on the printed dipole the 2x rule fires on
    most walled runs and masks the Loewdin defect, and the diff then reports a far smaller
    number than the diagnostics themselves would see. A re-run that reports single digits has
    been run without the correction and is answering a different question.

    MOVED TOWARD COLLAPSED is the only count that decides whether this is a note or an erratum.
    D6.3 is MOVED and SICK -> COLLAPSED, SICK -> STRAINED, MOVED -> POLARISED. The fix only ever
    removes a MOVED flag, and the diagnostics' thresholds are fixed text parsed from the
    committed DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt, so they cannot shift underneath it. A run can therefore
    only move POLARISED -> CLEAN or COLLAPSED -> STRAINED. The second would change a published
    class; it does not occur, because no walled run in any committed diagnostic is COLLAPSED.


N1.3  WHY THIS WAS WORTH FIXING ANYWAY

    Defect (a) is one charge-scale step from mislabelling a design. The def2-SVPD trip point is
    0.589 e. DIAGNOSTIC 17's walled runs carry 0.538860 e - a margin of 0.0501 e - and four of
    them are already SICK on gap and cycles. At the 0.65 e that ERRATUM 3 E3.1 admits, the
    excess is 0.311 e, MOVED fires automatically, and all four read COLLAPSED on the strength of
    their own input charge. COLLAPSED is documented as density captured by a bare positive
    charge, which is precisely backwards for a walled run.

    Defect (b) hides evidence. The record shows the project's strongest-field runs at 2.7211 eV
    when their converged gaps are below 0.5 eV. Those gaps bear directly on whether the
    high-field failure is a property of the representation or of the system.


N1.4  WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

    That the audit is now correct in every respect - only that these two readings were wrong and
    are now right. The corpus was read twice and compared; nothing else was re-derived.

    cs11_standoff, cs12_sites, cs13_qscale and cs_crosscheck already truncate their Loewdin list
    with lw[:24] and never had defect (a). cs6_transfer and cs6_additivity inherit cs6_audit's
    reading through AUD.read() and are fixed by this change.
