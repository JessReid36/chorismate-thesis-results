Stage 6, DIAGNOSTIC 23, ERRATUM 1 (3 November 2026).
Issued after the diagnostic's fifty runs were read. The pre-registration is not edited.


E23.1  D23.1 ASSERTS A BOUND ON THE PROPAGATION FACTOR THAT ITS OWN DATA DISPROVES

DIAGNOSTIC 23's pre-registration, D23.1, states:

   "If a parameter change moves them by the same amount, d_shield does not move at all. The
    propagation factor therefore lies between 0 and 1 and is undetermined."

The second sentence is withdrawn. The factor is undetermined, which is why the diagnostic was
run; it does not lie between 0 and 1. Measured, over the seven readable conditions:

   He   -9.314, +0.235, +0.294, +0.413, +0.517, +0.704, +2.279
   Ar   -1.542, -0.453, +0.088, +0.139, +0.155, +0.245, +0.613

Four of the fourteen values fall outside [0, 1], two of them by a wide margin.


E23.2  WHY THE STEP WAS INVALID

Writing the quantity as the project forms it,

   d_shield = ddE(bare) - ddE(wall, raw) + ddE(wall0)
   f        = dd / dz   =  1 - d(wall,raw) / dz

f lies in [0, 1] if and only if d(wall,raw)/dz lies in [0, 1] - that is, if the charged walled
arm responds to a parameter change IN THE SAME DIRECTION AS, AND NO MORE STRONGLY THAN, the
zero-charge arm. D23.1 argued from the two terms carrying opposite signs in d_shield to a bound,
and that argument needs the magnitudes ordered as well as the signs. Neither condition holds:

   condition          dz         d(wall,raw)   ratio      f
   S6-2 (D17), He    -0.0145       -0.1500     +10.314   -9.314
   S6-3 (D16), He    +0.0751       -0.0960      -1.279   +2.279
   S6-2 (D17), Ar    +0.4687       +0.6811      +1.453   -0.453

At S6-2 under He the zero-charge arm moves by -0.0145 kcal/mol while the charged arm moves ten
times as far, -0.1500. At S6-3 the two move in OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS.

Every ratio and factor above is computed from the energies. Dividing the four-decimal
figures printed in the first two columns gives 10.34 for the first row rather than 10.314,
and that artefact is noted here because an earlier draft of this erratum carried it.


E23.3  WHAT THAT MEANS, WHICH IS NOT A BOOKKEEPING MATTER

The pseudopotential's response to a change of its own parameters DEPENDS ON THE CHARGE SITTING ON
THE CENTRE, and strongly. STAGE6_ERRATUM7_20261026.txt E7.8 recorded that the published set is
used outside the domain its authors validated, the set having been fitted on a neutral atom
carrying no net charge. EXPERIMENT 0 (ECP_CONVENTION_20261102.md) established that the operator
ORCA is given is the authors' operator. This erratum records the remaining half: placing that
operator on a charged centre is not a neutral act, and the record now measures how far from
neutral it is.

It also strengthens, rather than weakens, DSHIELD_DECOMPOSITION_ERRATUM1_20261102.md E1.3's
withdrawal of a zero-charge-only sweep. That design was withdrawn because it would measure dz and
assume f. The measurement shows dz bounds dd neither in magnitude nor in sign, so such a sweep
could not have been salvaged by any assumption about f.


E23.4  WHAT IS NOT CORRECTED

No gate, tolerance, reading, criterion or verdict of DIAGNOSTIC 23 changes. D23.4 READING 3
reports f per condition with its sign and attaches no criterion to it, and D23.5's criteria are
functions of dd and of the recomputed verdicts, not of f. The diagnostic's verdict stands as the
result file prints it. What is withdrawn is a sentence of the pre-registration's own reasoning in
D23.1, and the same sentence wherever else this project states it:

   DSHIELD_DECOMPOSITION_ERRATUM2_20261103.md          E1.3's copy of the bound
   writeup/RESULTS_charge_representation_domain_
     DRAFT_ERRATUM2_20261103.md                        Section 6's copy of the bound
   claude/ECP_ASSESSMENT_20261102.md Section 1 item 4 and Section 6.3 state the same bound; that
     document belongs to another session and the correction is recorded here rather than in it.
