N1.3  WHY THE ESTIMATE WAS WRONG

THE ERROR IS CLAUDE'S. The pre-registration gives "about 10 to 30 minutes each, as D56's took on
the PC". That is not what D56's search took: 10 to 30 minutes was Claude's estimate for D56's
search, made before it ran, and D56's search took 1 h 36 min, which Claude told Jessica on 24
November. The pre-registration repeated the estimate and not the measured time, and the reader
and the harvest repeated the pre-registration. From D56's measured time the three searches
would have been put at up to 4 h 48 min (derived: three times 1 h 36 min); the whole of H1 took
3 h 38 min 19 s.


N1.4  WHAT IT CHANGES

Nothing in D60's result. The cost was an estimate and not a rule: no gate, test or reading of
D60 depends on how long a step took. For what follows: AMENDMENT 13, A13.4 items 7 and 8, put
the second search and its random designs on the PC. On the PC a search takes hours, not minutes,
and an estimate for them is to start from the times measured here. Whether they are run on hpc1
instead is Jessica's decision, and would need an amendment; this note does not make it.
