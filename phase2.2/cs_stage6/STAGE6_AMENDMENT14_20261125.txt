A14.1  WHY

AMENDMENT 13, A13.4 item 7: "The record holds the map of the search at a second functional:
A_f42436.tsv, at B3LYP-D3BJ/def2-SVP, beside A_f42436_d33a.tsv at the working level." and "The
two functionals differ in basis set too, and that is said with the comparison." B3LYP-D3BJ is
not this work's method: DIAGNOSTIC 28's result placed the bare substrate's highest occupied
orbital at +1.2307 eV with B3LYP and -0.5918 eV with CAM-B3LYP and said: "Probe 3's measured
detachment energy is +0.5123 eV, so for the exact functional this orbital should sit near
-0.5123 eV." Jessica asked on 25 November: "why b3lyp? we know that level of theory is not good
enough right?" A map at another functional costs two calculations, at R and TS, and the
potentials at the 2626 sites, as DIAGNOSTIC 33, stage A, measured the working map.
