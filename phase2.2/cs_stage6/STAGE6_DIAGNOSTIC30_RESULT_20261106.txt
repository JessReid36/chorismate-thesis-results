R30.3  WHAT FOLLOWS, BY THE PRE-REGISTERED RULES AND NOTHING ELSE

  ARM A. def2-SVPD is not sufficient under CAM-B3LYP at 5.6433e_n20.
  By D30.3, every CAM-B3LYP quantity at or above that Sum|q| is provisional until re-run.

  ARM B. At least one barrier moves by more than TOL: 5.6433e_n20.
  By D30.4, every CAM-B3LYP barrier at or above that vector is provisional until the
  reconverged solution is used.

  D30.6's six limitations stand as written. In particular this says nothing about +-1 e
  designs and nothing about density relocation, and it does not by itself make CAM-B3LYP
  the production functional.
