Stage 6 - DIAGNOSTIC 1: how the eight single points were actually obtained. Written 22 October 2026, the same day.

This records an HPC-operations fact, not a scientific one. No decision, threshold, tolerance, criterion, arrangement,
input file or reported quantity changes, and STAGE6_CRITERIA.txt is untouched. It exists because seven of sixteen
submissions were lost to one bad compute node, and a record that showed only the eight that worked would be a record
of a cleaner run than the one that happened.

N2  WHAT THE FAILURE WAS, AND WHAT IT WAS NOT

Every failure is on comp036 and every success is not. PBS exit -2 is a job-execution failure: the job never reached
the submission script, used 00:00:00 of walltime and wrote no output at all - not a job.out, and not even the
job.pbs.out that PBS itself creates. A job that dies this way is indistinguishable, from the file system alone, from
a job that has not started.

It is NOT an input problem, and the evidence is in the table rather than in an argument:

  - DIFF-S6-2/R_sp failed on comp036 while its own TS twin ran on comp055 in the same submission batch, and the two
    differ only in geometry;
  - DIFF-S6-C/TS_sp and DIFF-S6-P/TS_sp were checked against their R twins with diff after they had failed twice:
    job.inp is byte-identical and job.pbs byte-identical apart from the job name and the directory;
  - all four designs eventually ran to normal termination from the SAME unmodified files;
  - the first three runs that succeeded already established that ma-def2-SVP with def2/J is accepted, that the
    20-sphere QM/MM job works at the new basis, and that job.engrad comes out at the expected size.

The scheduler kept choosing comp036 because a node on which nothing ever runs is always the idlest node available,
so the fault is self-reinforcing: each resubmission was sent to the same broken node first.

An earlier hypothesis - that the plain-QM control was the odd one out because it alone has no %qmmm block - was
WRONG and is recorded as wrong. Both S6-P jobs and S6-2/R are QM/MM and were among the failures.

N3  WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT

The last two were resubmitted with the host named on the qsub command line:

   qsub -l select=1:ncpus=8:mem=24gb:host=comp043 job.pbs

This is a RESOURCE REQUEST and nothing else, of the same kind as the walltime corrected in STAGE6_ERRATUM2 and the
memory corrected in SP_AMENDMENT3, and no pre-registration fixes where a job runs. It was passed on the command line
specifically so that the committed job.pbs files were NOT edited: all 30 files under phase2.2/cs_stage6/diffuse/
still match INPUT_CHECKSUMS.sha256 as x97j committed them, which cs6_diagnostic1.py's own run re-verified. The
cores, the memory and the walltime asked for are unchanged.

N4  WHAT THIS MEANS BEYOND THIS DIAGNOSTIC

comp036 should be reported to the HPC administrators with job IDs 421260, 421262, 421263, 421264, 421265, 421268
and 421270.

The wider risk is not this stage. These were five-minute jobs in the short queue and their absence was obvious
within the hour, and the x97k bundle refused to measure anything while any output was missing rather than
measuring the six it had. A week-queue job that dies this way looks exactly like a week-queue job that has not
started yet, and the SP package has many of those queued. Anything that concludes a job "has not started" from the
absence of its output is unsafe on this cluster; the plain qstat -u 18660916 record, with Exit_status from
qstat -xf, is the only evidence of what happened to a job.
