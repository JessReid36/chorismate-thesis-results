STAGE 6, DIAGNOSTIC 34 - ERRATUM 1
Stage A's vocabulary has no term for a dark gap leg, and two of the ten designs need one.

Written 10 November 2026, BEFORE any stage A verdict is committed and before any
stage B output exists. The pre-registration is not edited; this records the defect
and the narrower reading that replaces it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE DEFECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section 3 of STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC34_20261110.txt fixes stage A's vocabulary as

   FLAGGED                 at least one of the three readable legs fires
   NOT FLAGGED (3 LEGS)    none of the three fires; the stability leg is unknown

and names the three legs as relocation, dipole and gap. It does not state the
convention DIAGNOSTIC 31, DIAGNOSTIC 32 and DIAGNOSTIC 33 all use: in a run that
converged under a level shift, the ORBITAL ENERGIES table prints the SHIFTED
orbital energies, so LUMO - HOMO is the shift floor and not the converged gap, and
the gap leg is treated as UNREADABLE rather than as passing. The committed
cs6_audit.py carries the same note against its own gap field.

That convention is not optional here. Two of the ten designs converged under a
level shift at the committed level of theory, so for them the gap leg is dark, and
"none of the three fires" would be literally true while reading as though three
legs had been checked.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW IT WAS FOUND, AND WHY THIS IS NOT A VERDICT CHOSEN AFTER THE FACT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It was found while rehearsing cs6_d34.py against the committed frozen outputs,
which means the stage A numbers had been seen in rehearsal before this erratum was
written. That is stated plainly rather than hidden, and the following two facts are
what keep this from being a reading chosen to suit an outcome.

First, the change can only ever make a verdict NARROWER. It adds a term for a case
in which a leg could not be evaluated; it moves nothing from FLAGGED to not
flagged, it moves no threshold, and it cannot admit a design that any reading in
the pre-registration would have rejected.

Second, it applies by the run's own printed output - whether ORCA reports a level
shift - and not by the design, the arrangement or the number the design produced.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE READING THAT REPLACES IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage A's vocabulary is, from here:

   FLAGGED                      at least one leg that could be evaluated fires
   NOT FLAGGED (3 LEGS)         relocation, dipole and gap all evaluated, none
                                fires; the stability leg is unknown
   NOT FLAGGED (2 LEGS, GAP     relocation and dipole evaluated, neither fires;
   DARK)                        the run converged under a level shift so the gap
                                leg is unreadable, and the stability leg is
                                unknown as before

The level shift is detected by the markers the committed cs6_d33b.py uses, which
are the markers DIAGNOSTIC 31 and DIAGNOSTIC 32 used:

   "Leaving SCF to start the TRAH-SCF procedure"
   "(TRAH MAcro)"
   level shift set to

No verdict in any of the three terms is RELIABLE, for the reason the pre-
registration gives: no frozen output carries a stability analysis, so the fourth
leg cannot be evaluated at all. R34.1 reports which legs were evaluated for each
design, and the result states each design's shift state beside its verdict.

Stage A remains POST HOC and remains labelled so.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHAT IS NOT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Nothing else. The thresholds are the committed ones, the legs are the committed
ones, stage B's two arms are unchanged, R34.4 to R34.10 are unchanged, every gate
G34.1 to G34.8 is unchanged, and every limitation D34.1 to D34.8 stands. Arm 2's
four-leg verdicts are unaffected: they are the committed classifier's own
UNRELIABLE / INCONCLUSIVE / RELIABLE, in which a dark gap leg already produces
INCONCLUSIVE, which is why stage B needed no term added.
