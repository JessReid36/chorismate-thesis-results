D9.0  THE DISCLOSURE THAT HAS TO COME FIRST, AND IT IS STRONGER THAN D6.0's

D6.0 disclosed that its four thresholds were chosen after the Stage 6 outputs had been inspected.
D9 inherits that unchanged and adds a second disclosure which is larger:

   D9 IS NOT A BLIND TEST AND IT IS NOT A PRIOR PREDICTION. Its charge detector, its model classes
   and its readings were all developed by reading this corpus directly, in the same session that
   wrote this file. The ECP centres, the Q label in the Loewdin table and the zero-charge wall-only
   series were all found before this document existed. D9 IS A CORRECTED MEASUREMENT, NOT A TEST.

Calling it a pre-registration therefore buys one thing only, and the claim is limited to that one
thing: the procedure is fixed in writing before the result is generated, so the result is
reproducible and the procedure is checkable against it. It buys nothing about priority, and no
reading in D9 may be quoted as a prediction that was confirmed.

Two readings are exceptions in the other direction and are weaker still, not stronger: reading (i)
applies a criterion that D8.4(h) fixed BEFORE any of this was known, and reading (h) was not
designed at all - the series it reads was run by this project long before any of these diagnostics
and is simply read as it stands.


D9.1  WHY IT EXISTS

STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC8_ERRATUM1_20261026.txt withdraws D8's stage 3 and its readings (b), (g) and (h):
D8's charge detector matched nothing in this corpus, 101 charged runs entered its control pool, and
its own specificity check caught that at 49 of 469 against D6.4(a)'s requirement of zero. D9 is the
same audit with the charges read from what ORCA actually prints.


D9.2  WHAT IS READ, AND FROM WHERE

NO CALCULATION IS PERFORMED AND NO NETWORK IS USED. Pure standard library; safe on a login node.

cs6_audit.read() and all six of its thresholds are IMPORTED from cs6_audit.py and used unchanged.
D6.3's rule is applied unchanged. verdict() is reproduced rather than imported, being a closure, and
the gates in D9.3 are what establish the reproduction is faithful.

Charges are taken from two committed sources and NEVER inferred:

  (i)  THE RUN'S OWN INPUT ECHO. ORCA echoes the whole input back, one line per '|  N> ' prefix, so
       this works for an archived run exactly as for a loose one, with no external file. A centre
       written 'Ne>  q  x  y  z' carries charge q. The coordinate block prints the same centre with
       its position only, which is why a reader looking there finds no charge.
  (ii) A COMMITTED .pc FILE, attributed only on an unambiguous match: the exact path the run's own
       %pointcharges directive names, relative to that run's directory. A weaker tier - a basename
       unique across the repository - is recorded separately and excluded from reading (i).
       A charge vector attributed to the wrong run is worse than no charge vector.

Where both the echo and the coordinate block list the centres, their positions must agree to 1e-3 A
or no field is computed for that run and the disagreement is reported instead.

WHETHER A CENTRE CARRIES A PAULI WALL IS TAKEN FROM WHAT ORCA DID, NOT FROM THE INPUT SYNTAX. ORCA
prints, per centre, 'Atom  NNNe>  ECP group =>  N', and names the group 'Ne ECP (replacing 10 core
electrons, lmax=3)'. An audit that parsed the input instead would call a %basis-declared ECP run
bare, because the same ECP can be declared inline after the centre or in a %basis block; ten runs in
cs_stage4b do it the second way.

QM/MM runs are identified by their own directive, '! QMMM' or '%qmmm'. D6.1 excludes them by the
proxy "more than 200 point charges"; detecting the directive is what that sentence means, and it
moves 120 runs out of the design table that the proxy left in.

The Loewdin indicator is read twice, for the reason E6.2 gives: once exactly as cs6_audit.read()
reads it, and once with the point-charge centres ORCA labels Q removed, which is what D6.3's phrase
"on a light atom" says. Where the two differ, BOTH are printed. Neither is suppressed.


D9.3  THE TWO GATES, WHICH RUN BEFORE ANY READING

  GATE 1. Stage 1 is D6 as published. The entire text of DIAGNOSTIC6_RESULT.txt is rebuilt from it
  and compared with the committed file BYTE FOR BYTE.

  GATE 2. Stage 2 is D8's stage 2. Its rows in D8 tables (a) and (d), and stage 1's, must appear
  LINE FOR LINE in the committed DIAGNOSTIC8_RESULT.txt - that is, exactly the part of D8 that
  STAGE6_DIAGNOSTIC8_ERRATUM1_20261026.txt leaves standing.

Either gate failing stops the script with the offending line printed and no result written. Passing
both means D9 agrees with everything D6 and D8 are still entitled to say, and differs from them only
where the erratum withdrew something.

  stage 1  D6 as published.                                                              gate 1

           ECP-shielded charges kept apart from bare ones everywhere.

  Model classes, never pooled:
     nothing      no charges and nothing added.
     wall only    ECP centres with every charge EXACTLY zero. The wall and no charge.
     wall+charge  ECP centres carrying charge.

     QM/MM        an MM environment, not a design.

  (a) the corpus at each stage, and how its charges are carried.
  (b) every run D6 and D8 filed as a control that carries charges, named, with its charges.
  (c) the control medians at each stage AND THE SPECIFICITY CHECK. D6.4(a) governs: zero is the
      only value that licenses the rest of the table, and if stage 3 is not zero it is said on the

  (d) verdicts on charged single points by basis and model class, on both Loewdin readings.
  (e) every verdict that changed against D8 stage 2.
  (f) where the charge vectors come from, by tier.
  (g) the charge vectors, one row per distinct signature - the exact vector and the exact final
      geometry to four decimals, matched by sha256, at one basis and in one class.
  (h) WHAT THE PAULI WALL ALONE DOES. The wall-only series against the charge-free controls at the
      same basis, in all four indicators, with the charged rows beside them. Observational.
  (i) THE PEAK-FIELD HYPOTHESIS, on D8.4(h)'s criterion unchanged - healthy is CLEAN or POLARISED,
      sick is STRAINED or COLLAPSED, and it survives only if max(healthy) < min(sick) - with one
      restriction D8 did not have: THE TEST IS RUN WITHIN ONE MODEL CLASS, because comparing a bare
      charge with a shielded one varies the wall and the field together, which is the confound
      STAGE6_ERRATUM6_20261026.txt records in Erratum 5 E5.3. Run per basis, because a threshold
      needing a different value at each basis would not be a property of the design. Surviving at
      some bases and failing at others IS failure and is to be reported as failure.
  (j) the optimisations and the QM/MM runs, apart.


D9.5  WHAT THIS CANNOT TEST

Nothing is computed. D6.0's post-hoc disclosure is inherited and D9.0 adds a larger one. The
thresholds are NOT re-derived here: changing the corpus and the rule in one step would make the
comparison uninterpretable, and SCF stability analysis and FOD remain the next question.

A CLEAN verdict is necessary and not sufficient. Erratum 4 E4.6 records B1 recovering the gap from
0.550 to 3.637 eV with a Pauli wall while b still stood 32.6 per cent from target against a 10 per
cent criterion.

Reading (h) is observational. The wall-only and charged series were run at different times for
different purposes and are matched only in basis and geometry. It can show that the wall coincides
with no change in the indicators; it cannot show that the wall is therefore free in a design.

Charge magnitudes exist only where the record carries them. For the runs whose .pc file was never
committed there is no vector and none is inferred, and the size of that group is reported, because
an untestable majority is itself a finding about the record.

A falsified scalar is not a licence to adopt the next one. If reading (i) fails, what follows is not
a sixth candidate but the question of whether the validity domain can be written as a constraint on
the design at all.
