1. WHY

AMENDMENT 13, A13.4 item 9: "A short test of the new forms of input on hpc1, which reads no

The packages that follow need forms of input that have not run on hpc1 at the method of D56: a
helium wall on a positive charge beside a bare negative one, as A12.4 item 5 places it; the

3. THE READING, FIXED NOW

For each run the reader cs6_forms1.py gives a status: NOT RUN (no output); REFUSED (an output,
and no SCF begun); ACCEPTED AND ENDED NORMALLY; ACCEPTED, STOPPED AT ITS CAP (the job log gives
exit 124); ACCEPTED, DID NOT END NORMALLY. Then it checks, from the output, what the form was
written to do:
   - the number of electrons: 118 with the dianion alone, a wall or a ghost; 128 with Na+; 136
     with K+; 117 for the control with one electron removed;
   - the number of point charges read: 1 in SV_wallpos_R, 2 in SV_engrad_R, none elsewhere;
   - the centre that carries a core potential: He> in SV_wallpos_R and TZ_wallHe_R, Ne> in
     TZ_wallNe_R, Na> in the two runs of the capping block, none elsewhere;
   - the basis functions: the substrate's own (402 at def2-SVPD, 682 at def2-TZVPD, as D56's
     gate G56.6 has them) where no functions were added, and more than that with an ion or a
     ghost;
   - SV_engrad_R: job.engrad holds 24 atoms and 72 gradient values. job.pcgrad is reported and
     decides nothing;
   - SV_mono_R: its final energy is within 1e-6 Eh of D56's committed SV_C_bare_R energy in
     d56.tsv, and vpot.out holds the five points with a potential at each.

THE WORD of each run: WORKS if it ended normally and every check holds; ACCEPTED, NOT FINISHED
if the SCF began, it did not end normally and the checks that come before the SCF hold (not
available to SV_engrad_R and SV_mono_R, whose checks need the end: for them it is NOT SETTLED);
CHECK FAILED if a check does not hold; REFUSED; NOT SETTLED if it did not run.

THE GATES. GF.1 the pre-registration given is this committed document; GF.2 the two programs are
the committed ones; GF.3 every input of forms1/ is the file the committed cs6_forms1_prepare.py
forms from the committed files; GF.4 every output that exists echoes the committed input beside
it. If a gate fails the reader writes nothing.


4. WHAT EACH WORD SENDS THE NEXT STEP TO, DECIDED NOW

WORKS: the form is used, as tested, in the packages that follow. ACCEPTED, NOT FINISHED: the
form may be used, and the package that uses it sets its cap with this run's time in view and
says so. REFUSED, CHECK FAILED or NOT SETTLED: the form is not used in any later package until a
dated note says how it is mended and a run shows it. In every case the result is committed, and
nothing of it is quoted as a number of a design.


5. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

WORKS says that ORCA did with the input what the input asked. It does not say that a wall
behaves like a real ion: that is D57's measurement. The energies of these runs are those of one
probe at one site of one frame; apart from the check of SV_mono_R against D56, none is read and
none is quoted.


6. WHAT WAS KNOWN WHEN THIS WAS WRITTEN, WHAT CLAUDE EXPECTS, AND THE COST

Known: every committed result to D56, D56 NOTE 1 and AMENDMENT 13. CLAUDE EXPECTS every form to
work, and is least sure of three things: how long the walls take to converge at def2-TZVPD;
whether ORCA writes job.pcgrad in the form the reader looks for, which decides nothing; and
whether orca_vpot reads the density of an open-shell run as it read D33's closed-shell ones.
COST, AN ESTIMATE: three jobs that run side by side; at their caps they would take 3.2, 2.5 and
3.3 hours; Claude's estimates have been too low. DIRECTION: none on any barrier.
