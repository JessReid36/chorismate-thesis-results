WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY (section 6 of the pre-registration, in short; its own words govern)
================================================================================================

   One electron and a bare charge say what the functions hold. They do not say what a run of the
   dianion does.
   Part G is one site and one design, at R and TS, one frame, frozen geometries, in vacuo, bare
   point charges. It is not the test of the inner sites, of the chemical groups or of charges
   placed together; that is the next diagnostic.
   A run that DID NOT TAKE with these functions is a test of capture at 0.25 e only if R50.3
   finds the set adequate with the treatment that run used.
   S is an estimate. D48 found that S does not separate what its runs did, and D50 adopts
   nothing: no size, no distance and no design is opened or closed here.
   The Loewdin charge of a diffuse function is a way of dividing the density, not a count of
   electrons that moved; the control with the same functions is what each run is read against.
