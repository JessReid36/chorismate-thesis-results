Stage 4 of the charged-sphere development: spill-out check, def2-SVPD against def2-SVP (pre-registered,
7 October 2026). Inputs: cs4_prepare.py from committed files; analysis: cs4_analyse.py.

Why. Electron density can spread towards a nearby positive charge, more so with diffuse basis functions (Laio et al.
2002; Senn & Thiel 2009; Behrens' thesis section 3.6.1 citing Laino et al. 2005). The project uses def2-SVP; a
dianion would usually get diffuse functions, so the check also asks whether the sphere results hold with them.
Negative spheres push density away and are not tested.

Runs. Same method as Stage 3 / WP4 (B3LYP-D3BJ, def2/J, RIJCOSX, TightSCF, QM/MM, Amber N sphere), basis changed:
  o1_p1_svpd  Stage 3 p1's def2-SVP minimum (q +0.5, nearest O2 2.674 A) re-optimised with def2-SVPD
  o2_q1_svpd  WP4 armb_anch's def2-SVP minimum (q +1, nearest O2 2.711 A) re-optimised with def2-SVPD
              (both anchored as before: C5-C8 and the sphere held; starting from the SVP minimum measures how that
              minimum moves, not whether another binding mode is found)
  sp_*        p1's converged geometry (sphere 2.674 A from O2) at q = 0, +-0.5, +-1, in def2-SVP and def2-SVPD,
              Hirshfeld charges printed

S4.1 PASS: in both re-optimisations, converged, same nearest oxygen, contact distance within 0.05 A of the def2-SVP
     value, closest atom >= 2.0 A.
S4.2 PASS: the polarisation term at contact, b (from symmetric sums, exact through q^4), changes by less than 10%
     between def2-SVP and def2-SVPD.
Informative: the change in the first-order term a (a different density has a different potential), the q = 0 -> +1
change of the Hirshfeld and Mulliken charge on O2 in each basis, and the def2-SVP q = +0.5 single point against
Stage 3 p1's final energy (same geometry and method).
If either criterion fails: spill-out matters at contact distance, and the remedies are a larger sphere or smeared
charges (whether ORCA supports the latter is to be checked), before positive spheres at contact are used.
